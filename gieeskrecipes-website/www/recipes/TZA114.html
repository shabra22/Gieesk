🥕 Tanzanian Carrot and Pea Curry

Mchuzi wa Karoti na Mbaazi

Tanzanian Mchuzi wa Karoti na Mbaazi — carrots and peas in a spiced tomato curry. Colourful, sweet, and fast to make — the vegetable curry that children love most and that brings colour and sweetness to the Tanzanian table.

Carrot and pea curry is the most child-friendly Tanzanian vegetable dish — the natural sweetness of the carrots and peas, the mild spicing, and the beautiful colour make it a favourite across all ages.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan

  • 300g carrots — peeled and sliced
  • 200g fresh or frozen peas
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, spices. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add carrots and water. Cover and cook 10 minutes until nearly tender.
  5. Add peas. Cook 5 more minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Carrots take longer than peas — add them first.
  • Fresh peas need 5 minutes; frozen only 2—3 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding peas and carrots together — peas overcook.

The Science

The orange colour of carrots (beta-carotene) and the green of peas (chlorophyll) are preserved by brief cooking. Extended cooking converts chlorophyll to pheophytin — producing the dull grey-green of overcooked peas.

Cultural Note

Carrot and pea curry is Tanzania's most colourful everyday vegetable dish — the vibrant orange and green that brightens every plate.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Rice, Chapati (TZA004)
