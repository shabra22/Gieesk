🍍 Tanzanian Pineapple Ginger Juice

Juisi ya Nanasi na Tangawizi

Tanzanian Pineapple Ginger Juice — fresh pineapple blended and strained with fresh ginger and lime, served over ice. The juice that hits every note simultaneously — sweet, tart, spicy, and deeply refreshing. One of Tanzania's most popular juice bar combinations.

Pineapple and ginger is one of the great flavour partnerships in tropical juice making — the pineapple's sweetness and bromelain depth balanced by the ginger's warming spice. Tanzania produces excellent pineapples in the coastal and highland regions, and this juice makes the most of them.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Fine sieve or muslin cloth

  • ½ large ripe pineapple — peeled, cored, and cubed
  • 3cm fresh ginger — peeled and roughly chopped
  • 3 cups cold water
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1—2 tbsp honey (to taste)
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Blend pineapple and ginger with 1 cup cold water until smooth.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing the pulp firmly.
  3. Add remaining cold water, lime juice, and honey.
  4. Stir to combine.
  5. Serve over ice.

Chef Tips

  • The ginger heat increases over time — if making ahead, use less ginger than you think.
  • Press the sieve pulp firmly — the most concentrated juice is in the pulp.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not straining — fibrous texture.
  • Too much ginger if making ahead.

The Science

Pineapple contains bromelain — a proteolytic enzyme that gives fresh pineapple juice a distinctive slightly astringent quality and helps break down proteins. Combined with gingerols from the ginger, the drink has a powerful dual anti-inflammatory effect.

Health Benefits

  • Bromelain from pineapple aids digestion.
  • Very high vitamin C.

Cultural Note

Pineapple ginger juice is the juice bar staple of Tanzania's urban health-conscious community — the combination that orders itself, that every vendor has, and that never disappoints.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days — ginger flavour intensifies.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a standalone, With breakfast, After exercise
