🫓 Ethiopian Shiro Firfir

ሽሮ ፍርፍር — Shiro Firfir

Ethiopian Shiro Firfir — torn day-old injera tossed with Shiro Wat sauce, oil, and onion. The most common fasting firfir — the quick, filling breakfast made from leftover shiro and yesterday's injera.

Shiro Firfir is the most-eaten fasting breakfast in Ethiopia — the leftover shiro from the previous day's fasting meal tossed with day-old injera pieces. Quick, filling, and completely satisfying.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 3 pieces day-old injera — torn
  • 1 cup Shiro Wat — warmed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add berbere. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add warmed shiro.
  4. Add torn injera. Toss to coat every piece.
  5. Cook 3—4 minutes. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Same technique as ETH032 — day-old injera absorbs better.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Fresh injera.
  • Cold shiro.

Cultural Note

Shiro Firfir is the most common fasting breakfast in Ethiopia — the quick, no-waste morning meal that sustains millions of Orthodox Christians through their morning prayers and early work.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a standalone fasting breakfast
