🌯 Kenyan Chicken Wrap

Wrap ya Kuku

Kenyan Chicken Wrap — spiced grilled chicken, kachumbari, sliced avocado, and garlic mayo wrapped in a warm chapati or flour tortilla. The portable Nairobi lunch — combining the traditional chapati with modern wrap culture.

The Kenyan chicken wrap is the natural meeting point between the chapati tradition and urban café culture. The chapati makes a superior wrap — thicker, more flavourful, and more structurally sound than a plain tortilla. Filled with spiced grilled chicken, fresh kachumbari, and avocado, it is the perfect portable Nairobi lunch.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan or grill, Knife

  • 2 chapatis or large flour tortillas
  • 300g chicken breast — spiced and grilled
  • 2 tbsp kachumbari
  • 1 ripe avocado — sliced
  • 2 tbsp garlic mayo
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • salt

Method

  1. Season chicken with paprika, cumin, and salt. Grill until cooked through. Slice thinly.
  2. Warm chapatis briefly in a dry pan.
  3. Spread garlic mayo over each chapati.
  4. Layer: lettuce, chicken strips, kachumbari, avocado.
  5. Roll firmly, tucking in the sides.
  6. Cut diagonally and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Warm the chapati before assembling — it is more pliable and less likely to crack.
  • Do not overfill — it becomes impossible to roll.
  • The chapati is what makes this Kenyan — use it instead of tortilla where possible.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold chapati — cracks when rolling.
  • Over-filling — structural failure.

The Science

Warming the chapati reactivates the gluten network, making it more flexible and elastic — this is why warm chapati rolls without cracking while a cold one splits.

Cultural Note

The Kenyan chicken wrap perfectly illustrates how Nairobi's café culture takes global food formats and transforms them with local ingredients into something distinctly and confidently Kenyan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Warm briefly in a pan.

Serve with: Masala Fries, Fresh Juice, Coleslaw
