🥜 Swahili Peanut Soup

Supu ya Karanga ya Kiswahili

Tanzanian Swahili Peanut Soup — a thick, creamy peanut and tomato soup with Zanzibar spices, fresh coriander, and a coconut milk finish. Rich, nourishing, and deeply satisfying — the coastal marriage of inland peanut tradition and coastal coconut.

Peanut soup with coconut milk is the coastal Tanzanian hybrid — the inland groundnut tradition meets the coastal coconut pantry. The result is richer than either alone — the peanut and coconut fats combine to produce a soup of extraordinary body.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender

  • ½ cup raw peanuts — roasted and ground (or 4 tbsp peanut butter)
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
  3. Add water. Simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Whisk peanut paste with ½ cup warm water. Add to soup.
  5. Simmer 12 minutes until thick and well flavoured.
  6. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 5 minutes.
  7. Blend partially for a creamier texture if desired.
  8. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Whisk peanut paste with warm water first.
  • Never boil after coconut milk is added.
  • Partial blending produces the best texture.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding dry peanut paste — lumps.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

Cultural Note

Peanut soup with coconut milk is the taste of the meeting between inland and coast — the two great Tanzanian flavour traditions combining into one remarkable bowl.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently with a splash of water.
