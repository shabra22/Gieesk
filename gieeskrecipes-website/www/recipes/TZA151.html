🥭 Zanzibar Mango Lassi

Sharubati ya Embe

Zanzibar Mango Lassi — ripe Zanzibar Dodo mango blended with natural yogurt, honey, and cardamom. A refreshing, cooling drink that captures the island's most famous fruit. Simple, tropical, and completely Zanzibari.

Zanzibar's Dodo mango is among the finest mangoes in the world — intensely sweet, fibreless, and deeply fragrant. Blended with local yogurt and cardamom, it produces a lassi of extraordinary quality.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 2 large ripe Zanzibar mangoes — peeled and diced
  • 1 cup natural yogurt
  • 1 cup cold water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • Ice

Method

  1. Blend mango, yogurt, water, honey, and cardamom until completely smooth.
  2. Taste — adjust honey.
  3. Serve over ice.

Chef Tips

  • The ripest, most fragrant mango is the only choice.
  • Cardamom is the Zanzibar signature.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-ripe mango.
  • Too much water — dilutes flavour.

The Science

Mango contains amylase enzymes that partially break down starch in the yogurt — slightly thickening the drink naturally while the lactic acid in yogurt partially preserves the mango colour.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar mango lassi is the island's most refreshing drink — the famous Dodo mango blended with the morning's fresh yogurt, drunk in the shade as the Indian Ocean breeze cools the stone walls of the old town.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.
