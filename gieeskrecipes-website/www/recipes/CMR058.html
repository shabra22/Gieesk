🌿 Sauce de bocko (Baobab Leaf Sauce with Meat)

Sauce de bocko

Meat cooked and fried, then simmered with onions, tomatoes, garlic, pepper and salt for 10 minutes, cooking water and dalang added, and dried baobab-leaf powder cooked 10–15 minutes.

65 minTotal time
20 minPrep
45 minCook
6Servings
300Calories
MediumDifficulty

p. 68 of the October 2023 recipe book. The meat is cooked then fried; onions, tomatoes, garlic, pepper and salt go in and simmer 10 minutes; the cooking water and dalang are added; dried baobab-leaf powder is added and cooked 10–15 minutes. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Frying pan

  • Dried baobab-leaf powder — quantity not printed
  • Meat — quantity not printed
  • Water, oil, onion, garlic, ginger, salt, chilli, dalang — quantities not printed
  • Fresh tomatoes — in the method, not in the list; quantity not printed

Method

  1. Cook the meat, then fry it.
  2. Add the onions, tomatoes, garlic, pepper and salt and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Add the cooking water and the dalang.
  4. Add the baobab-leaf powder and cook 10–15 minutes.
  5. Serve with couscous.

Chef Tips

  • No doneness cue is printed beyond the times.
  • The tomatoes appear in the method but not in the ingredient list.
  • Quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the powder all at once so it clumps.
  • Leaving out the tomatoes because the list omits them.
  • Over-reducing the sauce.
