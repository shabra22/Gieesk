🥜 Arachides enrobées (Coated Peanut Croquettes)

Peanuts / arachides enrobées

Raw peanuts coated nine to ten times alternately in an egg-sugar-milk liquid and sifted flour, then fried on low heat, turning, until golden.

p. 48 of the October 2023 recipe book. Eggs and sugar are beaten; milk, salt, lemon zest or flavouring and 20 ml of oil are added to make the ‘jus’. The peanuts are coated with half a spoon of jus and a ladle of sifted flour, and the jus-flour step repeated 9–10 times. They are fried in portions on low heat, turned regularly, and removed when the croquettes take a golden colour. The list prints 1 litre of oil while the method uses 20 ml in the batter; the frying oil quantity is not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Sieve, Frying pan

  • 400 g wheat flour
  • 200 g raw peanuts
  • 50 g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1 litre vegetable oil (the method uses 20 ml in the batter; frying quantity not printed)
  • Lemon zest or flavouring — quantity not printed
  • 10 g salt

Method

  1. Beat the eggs and sugar. Add the milk, salt, zest or flavouring and 20 ml of oil to make the jus.
  2. Coat the peanuts with half a spoon of jus, then a ladle of sifted flour.
  3. Repeat the jus-flour coating 9–10 times.
  4. Fry in portions on low heat, turning regularly.
  5. Remove when the croquettes take a golden colour.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is the golden colour.
  • Fry on low heat and turn regularly.
  • The frying time and temperature are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying on high heat so the coating browns before it cooks.
  • Coating fewer than the printed 9–10 times.
  • Crowding the pan.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The page says ‘also called peanuts’ and describes them as peanut croquettes.
