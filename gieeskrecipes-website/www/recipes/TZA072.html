🦑 Tanzanian Calamari

Pweza Mdogo wa Kukaanga

Tanzanian Calamari — fresh calamari rings in a spiced flour and semolina batter, deep-fried until golden and crispy in 2—3 minutes. Served with pili pili mayo and lemon. The most popular seafood starter at Tanzanian coastal restaurants.

Fresh calamari from the Indian Ocean, battered and fried in 3 minutes. The semolina in the batter gives a far crunchier coating than plain flour alone.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan

  • 500g fresh calamari — cleaned and sliced into rings
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup fine semolina
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Pili pili mayo (4 tbsp mayo + 1 tbsp pili pili sauce)
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Pat calamari rings completely dry.
  2. Mix flour, semolina, and all spices.
  3. Toss calamari in seasoned flour. Shake off excess.
  4. Heat oil to 180°C.
  5. Fry in small batches 2—3 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve immediately with pili pili mayo and lemon.

Chef Tips

  • 2—3 minutes maximum.
  • Small batches.
  • Completely dry before battering.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking — rubbery.
  • Crowding the fryer.

The Science

Semolina has larger particles than flour — creating more air pockets in the coating, producing a lighter, crunchier crust that stays crispy longer.

Cultural Note

Calamari at a Zanzibar beach restaurant — cold drink in hand, the Indian Ocean in front — is one of the finest simple pleasures the island offers.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Pili Pili Mayo, Lemon
