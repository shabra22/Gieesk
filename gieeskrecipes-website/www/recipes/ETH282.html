🍗 Ethiopian Berbere Grilled Chicken

ቤርበሬ ዶሮ ጥብስ — Berbere Doro Tibs

Ethiopian Berbere Grilled Chicken — whole chicken joints marinated in berbere, kibbeh, garlic, lemon, and yogurt for 4 hours, then oven-grilled until the berbere crust caramelises magnificently. The modern Ethiopian grilled chicken — a berbere-marinade technique that works for any cooking format.

Berbere grilled chicken is the modern Ethiopian home cook's answer to oven roasting — the berbere marinade produces a crust of extraordinary colour and complexity, and the yogurt tenderises the meat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Oven at 200°C, Baking tray with rack

  • 1 whole chicken — jointed into 8 pieces

Berbere Marinade

  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh — melted
  • 1 tbsp yogurt
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients.
  2. Score chicken pieces deeply. Rub marinade into every surface and score.
  3. Marinate 4 hours minimum — overnight is better.
  4. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  5. Place on a rack over a baking tray.
  6. Roast 22—25 minutes until internal temperature 75°C.
  7. Grill (broil) last 3 minutes for the crust.

Chef Tips

  • Scoring is essential — berbere marinade must reach the bone.
  • The yogurt in the marinade tenderises through lactic acid.
  • The kibbeh carries the fat-soluble berbere compounds deep into the meat.
  • Rack cooking allows air circulation — crispier skin.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not scoring.
  • Insufficient marination time.

The Science

See ETH066 for berbere chicken science. The yogurt's lactic acid at 4+ hours of marination partially denatures the muscle protein surface — creating a more tender texture. The berbere's carotenoids and Maillard products during roasting create the distinctive red-brown caramelised crust.

Cultural Note

Berbere grilled chicken is the modern Ethiopian home cook's adaptation — taking the ancient berbere tradition and applying it to the globally familiar roast chicken format, producing a result that is unmistakably Ethiopian.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Oven at 180°C.

Serve with: With injera (ETH001) or rice, With silsi (ETH146), With ayib (ETH096)
