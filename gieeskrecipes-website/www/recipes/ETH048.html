🥩 Ethiopian Dry Beef (Quanta)

ቋንጣ — Quanta

Ethiopian Quanta — beef marinated in berbere and mitmita, sun-dried for 2 days until preserved, then deep-fried in kibbeh until crispy. Ethiopia's ancient beef preservation technique — intensely spiced, chewy, extraordinarily flavourful dried beef that keeps for weeks.

Quanta is Ethiopia's preserved beef tradition — strips of beef marinated in berbere and mitmita, dried in the highland sun for 2 days, then deep-fried in kibbeh until crispy and concentrated. The result is one of the most intensely flavoured beef preparations in the world — fiery, chewy, complex.

Ingredients

Equipment: Drying rack in full sun, Heavy pan for frying

  • 1 kg lean beef — sliced into thin strips 2—3cm wide
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 1 tsp mitmita
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp kibbeh — for the marinade

Frying: 3 tbsp kibbeh

    Method

    1. Mix berbere, mitmita, salt, and 1 tbsp kibbeh.
    2. Coat beef strips thoroughly.
    3. Hang on a rack in direct sun for 2 days. Bring in at night.
    4. The strips are ready when dry, stiff, and dark.
    5. To cook: fry dried strips in hot kibbeh for 3—4 minutes per side until very crispy.
    6. Eat as-is or use in Quanta Firfir.

    Chef Tips

    • The drying must be complete — undried quanta does not keep.
    • The frying in kibbeh at the end adds the final layer of flavour.
    • Quanta keeps for weeks in an airtight container.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Insufficient drying.
    • Thick cuts — do not dry evenly.

    The Science

    See TZA075 and ETH015 for drying science. The berbere's acids and the salt act as additional preservatives alongside the dehydration — the combination of acid, salt, and moisture removal creates a multi-barrier preservation system.

    Cultural Note

    Quanta is Ethiopia's ancient survival food — prepared before the long highland rainy season when travel is impossible, carried by traders and pilgrims across Ethiopia's mountains. Every Ethiopian grandmother knows how to make it; every Ethiopian child grows up eating it.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Airtight container 3—4 weeks room temperature. Freeze 6 months.

    Reheating: Briefly in hot kibbeh.

    Serve with: As a snack, In Quanta Firfir (ETH049), With injera
