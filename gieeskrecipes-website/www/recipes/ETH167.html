🥟 Ethiopian Potato Sambusa

ድንች ሳምቡሳ — Dinich Sambusa

Ethiopian Potato Sambusa — the thin crispy sambusa pastry filled with spiced mashed potato, green peas, jalapeño, and coriander. The vegan-friendly sambusa — during fasting periods when lentils are available but meat is not, potato sambusa is the most commonly found.

Potato sambusa is the fasting period street vendor's most important offering — the potato filling is filling, inexpensive, and completely vegan. The berbere-spiced potato and pea filling has more body and flavour than it sounds.

Ingredients

Equipment: Rolling pin, Deep pan

    Sambusa Pastry (same as ETH161)

    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • ½ tsp salt
    • ½ cup water
    • 2 tbsp oil

    Potato Filling

    • 3 medium potatoes — boiled and roughly mashed
    • ½ cup green peas — cooked
    • 1 onion — diced and fried golden
    • 1 green jalapeño — finely minced
    • 1 tsp cumin
    • ½ tsp turmeric
    • ½ tsp berbere
    • ¼ cup fresh coriander
    • 1 tsp salt
    • Oil for frying

    Method

    1. Make potato filling: mix mashed potato, peas, fried onion, jalapeño, spices, and coriander. Season. Cool.
    2. Make pastry as per ETH161.
    3. Shape, fill, and fry as per ETH161 method.

    Chef Tips

    • The potato must be mashed dry — watery potato breaks the pastry.
    • Same sealing, same frying technique as ETH161.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Wet potato filling — same result as wet lentil: broken sambusa.

    The Science

    See ETH161 for sambusa frying science.

    Cultural Note

    Potato sambusa is the fasting street food that keeps vendors in business during the 200+ annual Orthodox fasting days — the entirely plant-based sambusa that satisfies the hunger of the fasting Orthodox majority.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately. Freeze uncooked.

    Reheating: Oven at 190°C.

    Serve with: With Silsi (ETH146), With Green Chilli Paste (ETH149)
