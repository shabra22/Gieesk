🍽️ Ethiopian Fish and Seafood Platter

አሣ ድስት — Asa Dist

Ethiopian Fish Platter — Asa Wat (ETH081), Fried Nile Perch (ETH082), Grilled Spiced Fish (ETH085), Fish Tibs (ETH083), and Fresh Salad (ETH099) assembled on a communal injera. The complete Ethiopian fish feast for fasting periods or lakeside celebrations.

The Ethiopian fish platter is the fasting-period equivalent of the great meat celebration feast — all the significant fish preparations arranged on a large injera, demonstrating that fasting food in Ethiopia can be as magnificent and varied as any other meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple pans and grill

    Assemble from individual recipes:

    • Asa Wat — the fish wat centrepiece
    • Fried Nile Perch — crispy berbere-coated
    • Grilled Spiced Fish — charcoal-grilled whole
    • Asa Tibs — fish sautéed in kibbeh
    • Selata — fresh salad
    • Ayib — fresh cheese
    • Large injera as the base

    Method

    1. Make Asa Wat 30 minutes ahead — it keeps warm.
    2. Prepare marinade for grilled fish — 30 minutes before grilling.
    3. Grill fish over charcoal while Asa Wat warms.
    4. Fry Nile perch batches.
    5. Last: make Asa Tibs — 12 minutes.
    6. Make fresh salad immediately before serving.
    7. Assemble on large injera: wat at centre, fried fish beside, grilled fish opposite, tibs, salad, ayib mound.

    Chef Tips

    • Asa Tibs must be last — serves hot.
    • Grilled fish is the visual centrepiece — place it where it can be seen.
    • All fish is eaten with injera torn around the platter.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Old fish — the platter's quality depends entirely on freshness.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian fish feast is the fasting period's great equaliser — the monks on Lake Tana's island monasteries eat this feast on fish-permitted fasting days, as do the city families observing Lent and the 200+ other fasting days. It is proof that restriction can produce abundance.
