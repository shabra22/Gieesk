🍗 Tanzanian Chicken and Potato Stew

Mchuzi wa Kuku na Viazi

Mchuzi wa Kuku na Viazi — chicken and potato stew. Bone-in chicken and potato chunks simmered in a fragrant tomato sauce until both are perfectly tender. The affordable, complete, everyday Tanzanian family stew.

Chicken and potato stew is the weekday workhorse of Tanzanian family cooking — the stew that appears when resources are limited but the family needs a complete, satisfying meal. Chicken extends further with potatoes alongside, and the potatoes absorb all the flavours of the stew.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 400g potatoes — cubed
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1.5 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown chicken. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  4. Return chicken. Add water. Simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes. Simmer 20 more minutes until tender.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Bone-in chicken gives much more flavour.
  • Add potatoes when chicken is half-cooked.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boneless chicken — reduced flavour.
  • Potatoes added too early.

Cultural Note

Chicken and potato stew is the daily meal of Tanzania's working families — reliable, affordable, and deeply satisfying. It appears on every mama lishe menu and in every family pot across the country.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.
