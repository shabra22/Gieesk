🐟 Kenyan Grilled Salmon

Samoni wa Kuchoma

Kenyan Grilled Salmon — Norwegian or Kenyan farmed salmon fillets marinated in lemon, garlic, and fresh coriander, pan-grilled to a golden crust with a blushing centre. Served with a passion fruit beurre blanc — a uniquely Kenyan flavour pairing.

Salmon has become a staple of Kenya's hotel and restaurant menus — imported from Norway but given a Kenyan identity through its preparation. The passion fruit beurre blanc is the uniquely Kenyan contribution — its tropical acidity paired with butter and salmon is an extraordinary combination.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick or cast iron pan, Small saucepan for sauce

  • 4 salmon fillets (180g each) — skin on

Marinade

  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp coriander
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Passion Fruit Beurre Blanc

  • 3 passion fruits — juice only
  • 2 tbsp white wine or stock
  • 1 shallot — finely minced
  • 100g cold butter — cubed
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Marinate salmon for 30 minutes.
  2. Make beurre blanc: reduce passion fruit, wine, and shallot by two-thirds over medium heat. Remove from heat. Whisk in cold butter piece by piece until emulsified. Add honey and season.
  3. Pat salmon skin dry.
  4. Heat pan over high heat. Add oil.
  5. Place salmon skin-side down. Press lightly for 30 seconds.
  6. Cook without moving 4—5 minutes until skin is crispy and opaque halfway up the fillet.
  7. Flip. Cook 2—3 minutes for medium (still blushing in the centre).
  8. Rest 2 minutes. Plate and spoon beurre blanc alongside.

Chef Tips

  • Start skin-side down and do not move — the skin needs uninterrupted contact to crisp.
  • Beurre blanc must be made off direct heat — the cold butter should melt slowly into the reduction.
  • A blushing centre is the goal — overcooked salmon is dry.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Moving the salmon before the skin crisps.
  • Overcooked centre.
  • Beurre blanc over heat — breaks into greasy puddle.

The Science

The crispy salmon skin is achieved by removing all surface moisture (patting dry) before placing in a very hot pan. The Maillard reaction on the dry fish skin creates the characteristic golden, crispy surface. Beurre blanc is a warm emulsion — the cold butter pieces gradually melt and emulsify the fat into the acidic reduction.

  • Exceptionally high in omega-3 fatty acids.

Cultural Note

Grilled salmon with passion fruit beurre blanc is one of the most successful examples of Kenyan culinary fusion — a globally beloved fish made uniquely Kenyan through the extraordinary marriage with passion fruit.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Warm gently in oven — do not microwave.

Serve with: Herb Rice (KEN276), Buttered Vegetables (KEN278), Tropical Lemonade (KEN245)
