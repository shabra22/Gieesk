🍅 Ethiopian Tomato Salad — Classic

ቲማቲም ሰላጣ — Timatim Selata (Kilasik)

Ethiopian Classic Tomato Salad — very ripe tomatoes diced with onion, green jalapeño, and coriander, dressed with lemon and oil. This extended version includes roasted green pepper and a touch of cumin for depth beyond the simpler ETH119.

The classic tomato salad (distinct from the simpler ETH119) adds roasted green pepper and cumin — two elements that lift it from a fresh salad to something with more cooked depth. The roasted pepper skin adds a charred note.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Open flame or grill for pepper

  • 4 very ripe tomatoes — diced small
  • 1 green pepper — roasted, peeled, finely diced
  • ½ red onion — very finely diced
  • 2 jalapeños — finely sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Roast green pepper directly over gas flame or under grill until charred all over.
  2. Place in a sealed bag 5 minutes. Peel, deseed, and finely dice.
  3. Combine tomatoes, roasted pepper, onion, and jalapeño.
  4. Dress with lemon, oil, cumin, and salt.
  5. Add coriander. Toss gently.
  6. Rest 5 minutes. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • The roasted pepper is the upgrading element — the charred note adds depth.
  • Rest 5 minutes — salt draws out the tomato juices.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the roasted pepper — the simpler version is ETH119.

The Science

Roasting the green pepper triggers Maillard reactions on its skin and partially caramelises its sugars — producing complex flavour compounds (pyrazines, furans) that add a smoky, complex note absent from raw peppers.

Cultural Note

The classic Ethiopian tomato salad with roasted pepper is the marker of a cook who goes further — who takes the time to roast the pepper rather than adding it raw. It is attention to detail expressed as food.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Alongside all grilled and fried dishes, On the full mesob platter
