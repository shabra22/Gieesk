🦐 Tanzanian Coconut Prawns

Kamba wa Nazi

Tanzanian Kamba wa Nazi — large prawns sautéed in foaming coconut oil and butter with garlic, cardamom, and fresh coriander. The simplest and most elegant Zanzibar prawn preparation — when the prawns are this fresh, nothing more is needed.

Kamba wa Nazi is simplicity elevated — large fresh prawns, coconut oil, garlic, and the lightest Zanzibar spice touch. This is the restaurant starter that makes visitors understand why Zanzibar seafood is world-class.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large wide pan, Tongs

  • 500g large prawns — peeled, deveined, tails on
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves — thinly sliced
  • 2 cardamom pods — bruised
  • 1 red chilli — sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Pat prawns completely dry.
  2. Heat coconut oil and butter over very high heat until foaming.
  3. Add prawns in a single layer. Cook without touching 90 seconds.
  4. Add garlic, cardamom, and chilli. Flip prawns.
  5. Cook 60—90 seconds on the other side.
  6. Add lemon juice and coriander. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Maximum heat.
  • Single layer only.
  • Entire cook under 4 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Crowding the pan.
  • Wet prawns.

The Science

Coconut oil has a higher smoke point than olive oil — it achieves the rapid high-temperature sear needed for prawns without burning.

Cultural Note

Kamba wa Nazi is Zanzibar hospitality at its purest — the best ingredient, the simplest treatment, the greatest result.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Must be eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Chapati (TZA004), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Fresh Lime
