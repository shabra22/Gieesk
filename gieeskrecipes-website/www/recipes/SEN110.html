🍵 Kinkéliba Tisane

Kinkéliba / quinquéliba

A big handful of kinkéliba leaves added to a litre of boiling water, covered and boiled about 15 minutes, left to infuse and sweetened to taste — or cooled with a few drops of lemon.

Amina Savoura’s ‘Kinkéliba en tisane’, published on Afrik on 27 January 2013, is the cleanest kinkéliba method found. A litre of water is brought to the boil and the kinkéliba added; the pot is covered and boiled about 15 minutes; it is left to infuse and served sweetened to taste. For variety it can also be infused with a few leaves of lemongrass or mint, or finished with a few drops of lemon and drunk cold. The method is a covered boil — a decoction — despite the word ‘infuser’. THE ONLY MEASURE FOR THE LEAVES IS ‘UNE GROSSE POIGNÉE’ (a big handful), a vernacular volume not converted to grams; the lemon, lemongrass or mint carries no quantity. The one peer-reviewed study of Dakar preparation (Moussa et al. 2017) confirms it is a decoction of dry Combretum micranthum leaves but, in the part of it that could be read, gives no leaf quantity, water volume or time.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot with a lid

  • 1 litre water
  • 1 big handful kinkéliba leaves (une grosse poignée) — not weighed in the source
  • Lemon (optional), or lemongrass or mint leaves — quantity not printed
  • Sugar, to taste

Method

  1. Bring the water to the boil and add the kinkéliba leaves.
  2. Cover and boil for about 15 minutes.
  3. Leave to infuse. Serve, sweetened to taste.
  4. For variety, infuse it with a few lemongrass or mint leaves, or add a few drops of lemon and drink it cold.

Chef Tips

  • The only cue is the timer, ‘environ’ 15 minutes; the infusion has no endpoint.
  • It is a decoction — a covered boil.
  • Add the lemon after cooking, not in the pot.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Treating the handful as a precise measure.
  • Uncovering the pot, which loses aroma.
  • Overboiling past the 15 minutes.

Health Benefits

  • Kinkéliba is ‘réputé pour ses nombreuses vertus, en particulier digestive’ (the page’s wording); this is tradition, not a medical claim.

Cultural Note

‘Utilisée dans la plupart des pays du Sahel … la préparation de boisson à base de Kinkéliba donne souvent lieu à de véritables cérémonials.’

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed.

Reheating: Served hot, or cold with lemon.

Serve with: Meals, as a digestive, Sweet pastries
