🍮 Ethiopian Bread Pudding

ዳቦ ፈርፈር — Dabo Ferfar

Ethiopian Dabo Ferfar — stale Difo Dabo or Kita torn into pieces, soaked in eggs, honey, and cardamom-spiced milk, then baked. The Ethiopian zero-waste sweet — using stale celebration bread to create a spiced bread pudding.

Dabo Ferfar is the Ethiopian cook's solution to stale celebration bread — the leftover Difo Dabo or Himbasha that has hardened is soaked in an egg-honey mixture and baked into a warm, fragrant pudding. Zero waste, complete deliciousness.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking dish, Oven at 175°C

  • 4 cups stale Ethiopian bread (Difo Dabo or Kita) — torn into pieces
  • 2 eggs
  • 1.5 cups milk (or plant milk for fasting)
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp butter (or oil)
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Beat eggs with milk, honey, cardamom, cinnamon, and salt.
  2. Add torn bread pieces. Toss to coat.
  3. Soak 15 minutes.
  4. Grease a baking dish with butter.
  5. Pour bread mixture in. Press down gently.
  6. Bake at 175°C for 20—25 minutes until golden on top and set.
  7. Serve warm with a drizzle of honey.

Chef Tips

  • Stale bread works better than fresh — it absorbs the custard without becoming mushy.
  • Allow the full 15 minutes of soaking — the bread must hydrate completely.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Fresh bread — becomes too soft.
  • Insufficient soaking.

The Science

Stale bread (partially retrogradated starch) absorbs the egg-milk custard more slowly and evenly than fresh bread — fresh bread absorbs so rapidly that it collapses into a paste. The retrogradated starch in stale bread maintains some structure during soaking, then softens as it bakes.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian bread pudding is the final chapter of Ethiopian celebration bread — when the Difo Dabo of the wedding has gone stale, it becomes Dabo Ferfar, the family's next sweet. Nothing from a celebration is wasted.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C.

Serve with: With honey drizzle, As a dessert, With chai
