🍝 Kenyan Vegetable Pasta

Pasta ya Mboga ya Kenya

Kenyan Vegetable Pasta — penne or rigatoni tossed with roasted cherry tomatoes, courgette, spinach, garlic, olive oil, and parmesan in a light tomato-cream sauce. The vegetarian restaurant pasta that does not compromise.

Vegetable pasta on a Kenyan restaurant menu is the dish that has improved dramatically as the country's food culture has evolved — roasted rather than boiled vegetables, a proper tomato-cream sauce, and quality parmesan make this genuinely satisfying.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large wide pan

  • 400g penne or rigatoni
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes — halved
  • 2 courgettes — diced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 5 garlic cloves — sliced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup cream
  • ½ cup parmesan — grated
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ cup pasta water — reserved
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Fresh basil or coriander

Method

  1. Roast cherry tomatoes at 200°C with olive oil and salt for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook pasta al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.
  3. Sauté courgette in olive oil and butter until golden — 4 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and paprika. Cook 1 minute.
  5. Add roasted tomatoes and cream. Simmer 3 minutes.
  6. Add pasta and pasta water. Toss.
  7. Add spinach. Toss until just wilted.
  8. Add parmesan. Toss off heat.
  9. Garnish with fresh herbs.

Chef Tips

  • Roasting the tomatoes first concentrates their flavour dramatically.
  • Add spinach only at the end — it wilts in seconds.
  • Reserve pasta water before draining.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not roasting the tomatoes.
  • Overcooking the spinach.
  • Discarding pasta water.

The Science

Roasting tomatoes at 200°C drives off 60—70% of their water content, concentrating sugars and acids while promoting Maillard browning of the sugars — producing intensely flavoured roasted tomatoes with far more depth than raw or boiled tomatoes.

Cultural Note

Vegetable pasta demonstrates that Nairobi's restaurant scene now serves genuinely excellent vegetarian options — not an afterthought but a properly conceived, beautifully executed dish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for 2 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop with splash of water.
