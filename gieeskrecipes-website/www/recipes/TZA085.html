🐟 Tanzanian Grilled Kingfish

Ngisi Mkubwa wa Kuchoma

Tanzanian Grilled Kingfish — thick kingfish steaks marinated in lemon, garlic, and spices, grilled over charcoal until lightly charred and moist. Kingfish is the Indian Ocean's finest grilling fish — firm, flavourful, and magnificent over charcoal.

Kingfish (Spanish mackerel) is the most prized grilling fish of the Tanzanian coast — its firm, oily flesh holds together perfectly over charcoal and develops a magnificent char without drying out.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill, Spatula

  • 4 kingfish steaks (200g each)
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Coat kingfish steaks thoroughly. Marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Grill over medium-high coals for 6—8 minutes per side.
  3. Do not move until fish releases from grill cleanly.
  4. Garnish with coriander. Serve with lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Kingfish steaks are thicker — adjust time.
  • Oily flesh is more forgiving.
  • Oil the grill before adding.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Moving too early — sticks.
  • Under-cooking thick steaks.

The Science

Kingfish has higher fat content than tilapia — intramuscular fat melts during grilling, naturally basting the fish from within.

Cultural Note

Grilled kingfish is the prized catch of the Tanzanian coast — the fish fishermen bring home for their families on important days.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly on grill.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Kachumbari, Pili Pili
