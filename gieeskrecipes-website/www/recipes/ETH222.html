🍈 Ethiopian Papaya Juice

ፓፓያ ጭማቂ — Papaya Chumaki

Ethiopian Papaya Juice — ripe papaya blended with water or milk, honey, and a squeeze of lime. The Rift Valley's most available fruit made into a silky, tropical drink. Often layered with avocado juice in Addis Ababa's famous multi-layer juice presentations.

Ethiopian papaya juice is the morning drink of the Rift Valley — the fruit grows abundantly and is available year-round. Its digestive enzyme (papain) makes it particularly beneficial after heavy Ethiopian meals.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 1 medium ripe papaya — peeled, seeded, cubed
  • 1 cup water or milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Ice

Method

  1. Blend papaya, water or milk, honey, and lime juice until smooth.
  2. Add ice. Blend briefly.
  3. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Lime juice brightens the papaya's somewhat flat flavour.
  • Ripe papaya — orange flesh, yields to gentle pressure.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unripe papaya — bitter and unpleasant.

The Science

Papaya contains papain — a protein-digesting enzyme that survives into the juice. Papain has documented digestive benefits, which is why papaya juice is traditionally recommended after heavy meals in cultures where it grows.

Health Benefits

  • Papain digestive enzyme.
  • Very high vitamin C.
  • Beta-carotene from orange flesh.
  • Excellent after heavy Ethiopian meals.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian papaya juice is the fruit of the Rift Valley in a glass — the abundant papaya harvest of Ethiopia's warm lowlands, blended with lime and honey, is the simple, complete expression of a country that grows fruit of extraordinary quality.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: At juice bars, As a morning digestive drink, Layered with avocado (ETH221)
