🥬 Creamed Collard Greens with Peanut Butter and Chile

Gombo vert / feuilles de chou à l’arachide

Collard greens boiled five minutes and pureed, then folded into a dried-shrimp, chile, onion and tomato base and finished with natural peanut butter, red palm oil and caramelised onion rings.

45mTotal time
20mPrep
25mCook

MediumDifficulty

Thiam’s creamed collards are a Senegalese-inspired side. The greens are boiled about 5 minutes in a little salted water, drained over a bowl reserving 1 cup of the liquid, and blended smooth. In a separate step the dried shrimp, three-quarters of the chiles, half the chopped onion and the fresh tomato are pureed; the remaining onion and tomato paste cook 6 to 8 minutes in palm oil, the shrimp mixture goes in for about 5 minutes, and off the heat the greens puree and the peanut butter are mashed in. A last garnish of onion rings, cooked about 5 minutes until soft and slightly caramelised, and the remaining chile tops the dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large saucepan, Blender, 10-inch skillet

  • 2 lb collard or turnip green leaves, roughly chopped (12 cups)
  • 1 cup dried shrimp
  • 4 Scotch bonnet or habanero chiles, stemmed, seeded and finely chopped
  • 1 1/2 medium yellow onions (1 roughly chopped; 1/2 sliced into 1/4-inch-thick rings)
  • 1 vine-ripe tomato, cored, seeded and roughly chopped
  • 4 tablespoons red palm oil or vegetable oil (2 in step 2, 2 in step 3)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
  • 2 cups water — appears only in the method
  • Kosher salt — quantity not printed
  • Freshly ground black pepper — quantity not printed

Method

    Greens

    1. Boil 2 cups of water in a large saucepan and season with salt (the amount is not printed). Add the greens, cover and cook about 5 minutes, until tender and wilted. Drain over a bowl, reserving 1 cup of the cooking liquid.
    2. Blend the greens with the reserved liquid until smooth and transfer the puree to a bowl.

    Base

    1. Clean the blender. Puree the dried shrimp, three-quarters of the chiles, half the chopped onion and the fresh tomato until smooth.
    2. In a large saucepan over medium-high heat, warm 2 tablespoons of the palm oil. Add the remaining chopped onion and the tomato paste and cook, stirring constantly, 6 to 8 minutes, until soft and lightly caramelised.
    3. Add the shrimp mixture, reduce the heat to medium and cook, stirring, about 5 minutes, until lightly caramelised.
    4. Off the heat, stir in the greens puree and the peanut butter, mashing until evenly combined. Season with salt and pepper and transfer to a serving dish.

    Garnish

    1. In a 10-inch skillet over high heat, warm the remaining 2 tablespoons of palm oil. Cook the onion rings, stirring, about 5 minutes, until soft and slightly caramelised. Arrange over the greens and top with the remaining chile.

    Chef Tips

    • Printed cues: greens ‘tender and wilted’; base onions ‘soft and lightly caramelized’; shrimp mixture ‘lightly caramelized’; onion rings ‘soft and slightly caramelized’.
    • Only medium-high, medium and high heat are printed; no temperatures.
    • Dried shrimp are dried, not fermented, in this recipe.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Leaving the greens unblended; they should be a puree.
    • Cooking the tomato paste on a heat too high to stir constantly.
    • Using sweetened peanut butter.
