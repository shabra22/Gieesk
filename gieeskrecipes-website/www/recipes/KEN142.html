🍠 Kenyan Sweet Potato Pudding

Pudini ya Viazi Vitamu

Kenyan Sweet Potato Pudding — mashed orange sweet potato blended with coconut milk, eggs, brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg, then baked until set into a soft, fragrant, naturally sweet pudding. A beautifully simple Kenyan dessert that lets the sweet potato shine.

Sweet potato pudding is one of the most satisfying ways to finish a Kenyan meal — the natural sweetness and vibrant orange colour of Kenyan sweet potatoes need very little enhancement. Coconut milk adds richness, warm spices add depth, and the baked result is a pudding that is simultaneously homely and special. It appears at family celebrations and everyday tables alike.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large mixing bowl, Baking dish, Oven

  • 600g orange-fleshed sweet potatoes — peeled, boiled, and mashed
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 large eggs — beaten
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp butter — melted

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease a baking dish.
  2. Mash the boiled sweet potatoes until completely smooth — no lumps.
  3. Whisk coconut milk, eggs, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, vanilla, salt, and melted butter together.
  4. Combine with the mashed sweet potato. Mix until smooth.
  5. Pour into the greased baking dish.
  6. Bake for 35—40 minutes until the top is set and lightly golden.
  7. Allow to cool for 10 minutes before serving.
  8. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • The smoother the mashed sweet potato, the silkier the pudding — use a food processor for the smoothest result.
  • Brown sugar adds a caramel depth that white sugar does not.
  • Do not overbake — the pudding should be set but still have a slight jiggle in the centre.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lumpy sweet potato — creates a textured rather than smooth pudding.
  • Overbaking — becomes dry and grainy.

The Science

Eggs in the pudding provide structure through protein coagulation during baking. The high beta-carotene content of orange sweet potatoes is fat-soluble — the coconut milk in the recipe actually enhances its absorption, making this dessert more nutritious than it appears.

Health Benefits

  • Exceptionally rich in beta-carotene.
  • Good source of vitamins A and C.
  • Natural sweetness reduces need for added sugar.

Cultural Note

Sweet potato pudding represents the Kenyan kitchen's instinct to find sweetness and comfort in the everyday ingredients of the homestead garden — transforming a simple root vegetable into something celebratory.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Warm in oven at 160°C for 10 minutes or microwave briefly.

Serve with: Whipped cream, Vanilla ice cream, Kenyan Masala Chai, Fresh fruit
