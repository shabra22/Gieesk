🌶️ Basbaas

Basbaas

Somalia's essential hot sauce, blended green chilies, garlic, and cilantro brightened with lime, a condiment that appears on nearly every Somali table alongside rice, meat, and sambuusa.

Basbaas varies by household, some versions leaning more herbal with cilantro, others sharper with extra garlic or lime, but its role never changes - a bright, fiery counterpoint meant to cut through the richness of Somali meat and rice dishes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor or blender

  • 6-8 green chilies, stemmed
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • A large handful fresh cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Combine the chilies, garlic, cilantro, lime juice, oil, cumin, and a pinch of salt in a food processor.
  2. Blend until you have a smooth, vibrant green sauce, adding a splash of water if needed to loosen.
  3. Taste and adjust salt and lime to preference.
  4. Transfer to a jar and let sit for at least 15 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld.

Chef Tips

  • Adjust the number of chilies to control the heat level - this sauce is meant to have real kick, but intensity is flexible.
  • A generous amount of fresh cilantro gives the sauce its characteristic bright, herbal quality.
  • This sauce keeps well and is worth making in a larger batch to have on hand.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-blending until completely liquid, losing some pleasant texture.
  • Skimping on the lime juice, which is essential for brightness and balance.
  • Making it too far ahead without refrigeration, which can dull the fresh, vibrant flavor.

Health Benefits

  • Chilies and cilantro provide vitamin C, and the sauce is naturally vegan and low in calories.

Cultural Note

Basbaas is a near-universal condiment on Somali tables, its bright heat considered essential for balancing the richness of rice and meat dishes at nearly every meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate in an airtight jar for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Sambuusa, Suqaar, Bariis Iskukaris
