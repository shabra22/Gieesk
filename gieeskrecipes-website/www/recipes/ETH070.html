🫘 Ethiopian Chicken with Lentils

ዶሮ ምስር — Doro Misir

Ethiopian Doro Misir — chicken simmered in a berbere sauce with red lentils added in the final 25 minutes. The lentils dissolve into the chicken sauce, thickening it naturally and adding plant protein. The complete family meal — chicken and lentil together.

Doro Misir is the practical family wat — chicken extended with red lentils produces a dish that is simultaneously more nutritious and more economical than pure chicken wat. The lentils absorb the berbere and create a thick, complete sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 700g bone-in chicken
  • ¾ cup red lentils — washed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 15 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Toast 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Add chicken. Coat in sauce.
  4. Add water. Simmer covered 35 minutes.
  5. Add lentils. Simmer 25—30 more minutes.
  6. Lentils should dissolve and thicken the sauce.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add lentils after the chicken is half-cooked.
  • Red lentils dissolve — they thicken the sauce naturally.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding lentils too early — they disintegrate before chicken is cooked.

Cultural Note

Doro Misir is the Ethiopian cook's smart answer to feeding a large family well — the combination of chicken and lentils produces more than the sum of their parts, both nutritionally and in flavour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Gomen (ETH086)
