🐖 Kondre de porc (Pork and Green Plantain Stew)

Kondré de porc

Pork cubes fried in red palm oil with a crushed paste of tomato, herbs, pèbè, chillies, garlic and ginger, then cooked 1 h 30 with unripe plantains and ginger leaves.

A French-language kondré from Afrik. Tomatoes, parsley, basil, pèbè, chillies, garlic, ginger, one onion and peppercorns are crushed together. The second onion is sliced, the plantains soaked in water and the pork cubed. The sliced onion and pork are fried in hot red palm oil; the crushed condiments, the ginger leaves (odzom) and the plantains are added, the pot is covered generously with water and simmered 1 h 30 on low heat. The page says to serve hot ‘sans complément’ — on its own.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar or blender

  • 500 g pork
  • 4 tomatoes
  • Basil — quantity not printed
  • A few peppercorns
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 50 g ginger
  • 2 tablespoons red palm oil
  • 2.5 kg unripe or barely yellowing plantains
  • Parsley — quantity not printed
  • Chillies — quantity not printed
  • 10 g bongolo (bongo)
  • 2 pèbès
  • 4 ginger leaves (odzom)
  • 2 onions
  • 2 Maggi cubes

Method

  1. Crush the tomatoes, parsley, basil, pèbè, chillies, garlic, ginger, one onion and the peppercorns.
  2. Slice the second onion, soak the plantains in water and cube the pork.
  3. Fry the sliced onion and the pork in the hot red palm oil.
  4. Add the crushed condiments, the ginger leaves and the plantains. Cover generously with water and simmer on low heat 1 h 30.
  5. Serve hot, without any accompaniment.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives a time (1 h 30) but no doneness cue.
  • The Maggi cubes are listed but the method never says when to add them — add with the condiments.
  • The plantain-to-pork ratio is as printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the soak for the plantains.
  • Using ripe plantains — the page specifies unripe or barely yellowing.
  • Letting the pot run dry; the page covers the ingredients generously with water.
