🧊 Tanzanian Cold Brew Tangawizi

Tangawizi ya Baridi

Tanzanian Cold Brew Tangawizi — fresh ginger and tea cold-brewed for 12 hours, strained, and served over ice with lime and a sprig of fresh mint. The modern café evolution of Tanzania's most beloved drink — all the ginger character, none of the heat, perfectly cold.

Cold brew tangawizi is what Tanzania's modern café culture has done with the most beloved drink in the country — the ginger tea reimagined as a cold brew. The 12-hour cold extraction produces a smoother, less bitter ginger note than hot brewing, while concentrating the aromatic compounds in a completely new way.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pitcher or jar, Fine sieve or muslin cloth

  • 8cm fresh ginger — peeled and thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp loose black tea (or 6 tea bags)
  • 6 cups cold filtered water
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime — juice
  • Ice cubes
  • Fresh mint for garnish

Method

  1. Combine ginger slices and tea in a large pitcher.
  2. Pour cold filtered water over.
  3. Refrigerate for 12 hours — do not rush.
  4. Strain through a fine sieve. Discard ginger and tea.
  5. Add honey and lime juice. Stir until honey dissolves.
  6. Taste — adjust honey and lime.
  7. Serve over ice with fresh mint.

Chef Tips

  • 12 hours minimum — cold brewing is gentle and requires time.
  • Do not heat — cold brew is specifically defined by the absence of heat.
  • Ginger slices rather than chunks — more surface area for extraction.
  • Strain through muslin for the clearest result.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-brewing — weak flavour.
  • Over-brewing past 16 hours — becomes bitter.

The Science

Cold brewing extracts the same volatile aromatic compounds as hot brewing but in different proportions — the cold water selectively extracts the brighter, fruitier aromatic compounds (geraniol, linalool) while leaving behind more of the bitter tannins and harsh pungent compounds extracted at high temperatures. The result is a smoother, more complex ginger beverage.

Cultural Note

Cold brew tangawizi is Tanzania's most beloved drink reimagined for the modern café context — the same ginger, the same tea, the same honey and lime, but transformed by time and cold into something that feels entirely contemporary.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

Serve with: As a café drink, With Tanzanian avocado toast (TZA281), With shakshuka (TZA285)
