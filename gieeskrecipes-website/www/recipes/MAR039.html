🫘 Hommos (Moroccan Pressure-Cooked Chickpeas)

Hommos — cumin chickpeas (الحمص)

Chickpeas pressure-cooked with garlic, cumin, saffron and a sliver of preserved lemon: the Moroccan snack, scooped up with bread.

Despite the name, this is not the dip. Hommos is the Arabic word for chickpea, and in Morocco the whole cooked chickpeas are eaten warm from the pot. Soaked chickpeas, garlic, preserved lemon, cumin, saffron, olive oil, butter and water go into a pressure cooker for about an hour on medium heat. The author says they should keep some firmness, but you can cook them as soft as you like.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker

  • 1 1/2 cups (250 grams) dry chickpeas
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/8 piece of a preserved lemon
  • 2 Tbsp cumin
  • pinch of saffron threads
  • 2-3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 2 1/2 cups water

Method

  1. Soak the chickpeas at least 8 hours, ideally longer.
  2. Put everything in the pressure cooker and cook over medium heat for about 1 hour.
  3. Serve warm, scooped up with bread.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: still some firmness, but cook as long as you like for the softness you prefer.
  • The soak is time only.
  • Serve with extra cumin and salt.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not soaking.
  • Opening the cooker too soon.
  • Expecting hummus dip; this is whole chickpeas.

Health Benefits

  • Chickpeas supply protein and fibre.
  • Olive oil and a little butter keep the dish simple.

Cultural Note

The author writes that, unlike in Middle Eastern countries, Moroccans don't eat hummus; hommos is the Arabic word for chickpea, and they are always scooped up with bread.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: Khobz
