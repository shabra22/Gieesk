🥜 Kenyan Peanut Porridge

Uji wa Karanga

Kenyan Peanut Porridge — finger millet or sorghum porridge enriched with freshly ground roasted groundnuts, creating a thick, protein-rich, deeply nutty and satisfying breakfast. The high-protein Kenyan breakfast that sustains children and athletes alike.

Uji wa karanga takes the nutritional powerhouse of millet porridge and amplifies it with the protein and healthy fats of roasted groundnuts. The result is richer, creamier, and more satisfying than plain uji — a breakfast that provides sustained energy for hours. It is particularly popular for children and in athletic training communities.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Wooden spoon, Blender or mortar for grinding groundnuts

  • ¾ cup finger millet flour
  • ¼ cup roasted groundnuts — finely ground to a paste
  • 4 cups water
  • 2—3 tbsp sugar or honey
  • ½ cup milk — optional
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Roast groundnuts in a dry pan until golden and fragrant. Cool, then grind to a smooth paste or fine powder.
  2. Mix millet flour and ground groundnuts together with 1 cup cold water to a smooth slurry.
  3. Bring remaining 3 cups of water to a boil.
  4. Pour the slurry into the hot water in a steady stream, stirring constantly.
  5. Cook for 8—10 minutes, stirring continuously, until thick.
  6. Add sugar and salt. Stir.
  7. Finish with milk if using. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Freshly roasted and ground groundnuts give incomparably better flavour than peanut butter — roast the nuts yourself.
  • Grind the groundnuts to a very fine powder or paste — coarse pieces remain gritty in the porridge.
  • The groundnut paste makes the porridge significantly richer — it thickens faster than plain uji.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using shop-bought peanut butter — too much added salt and sugar; fresh-ground is far better.
  • Coarsely ground groundnuts — gritty texture in the porridge.
  • Not stirring enough — the enriched porridge sticks more readily than plain uji.

The Science

Groundnut proteins and fats dramatically change the porridge texture — the fat coats the starch granules and creates a creamier, more emulsified texture. The protein (25% of groundnut composition) also slows starch absorption, lowering the overall glycaemic index of the meal.

Health Benefits

  • Significantly higher protein than plain uji — 9g per serving.
  • Groundnuts add healthy monounsaturated fats.
  • Rich in niacin, folate, and magnesium from groundnuts.
  • Excellent for growing children and athletes.
  • Sustained energy from protein and fat combination.

Cultural Note

Peanut porridge is one of Kenya's most beloved breakfast enrichments — the intuitive combination of two highly nutritious local ingredients into a breakfast of exceptional nourishment.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best consumed fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water, stirring.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast, With banana, With roasted sweet potato
