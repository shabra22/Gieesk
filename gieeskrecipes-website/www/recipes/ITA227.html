🍞 Casatiello

Casatiello

A dramatic Neapolitan Easter bread ringed with whole eggs baked directly into the dough, generously studded throughout with cured meats and cheese for a rich, savory holiday centerpiece.

Casatiello's whole eggs, nestled into the dough and secured with strips of dough forming a cross over each one, are said to symbolize resurrection for the Easter holiday, and the bread's dense richness reflects its role as a meat-and-cheese-forward centerpiece after the fasting of Lent.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large mixing bowl, Ring-shaped bundt pan, Kitchen twine or extra dough for securing eggs

  • 500 g (4 cups) bread flour
  • 10 g (3 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 250 ml (1 cup) warm water
  • 100 g (⅓ cup) lard or olive oil, softened
  • 10 g (1¾ tsp) salt
  • 150 g (5.3 oz) salami, diced
  • 150 g (5.3 oz) pancetta or ham, diced
  • 150 g (1½ cups) mixed cheese (pecorino and provolone), diced
  • 6 whole raw eggs, in their shells, for baking into the dough
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, salt, and softened lard, then add the yeast mixture, kneading into a smooth, elastic dough.
  3. Let rise, covered, in a warm place for about 1.5–2 hours, until doubled.
  4. Punch down the dough and knead in the diced salami, pancetta, cheese, and a generous amount of black pepper.
  5. Shape the dough into a ring and place in a well-oiled bundt or ring pan.
  6. Let rise again for about 45 minutes.
  7. Nestle the whole, unpeeled raw eggs into the top of the dough at intervals, pressing them in gently.
  8. Cross two thin strips of reserved dough over each egg to secure it in place.
  9. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 45–50 minutes until deeply golden, tenting with foil if browning too quickly.
  10. Cool before slicing — the eggs cook through fully during baking.

Chef Tips

  • The whole eggs bake and cook fully inside their shells as part of the bread — they're meant to be peeled and eaten as part of each slice.
  • Crossing dough strips over each egg helps keep them securely in place through the final rise and bake.
  • A generous amount of black pepper is traditional and helps balance the richness of the meats and cheese.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not securing the eggs well enough, causing them to shift or fall out during baking.
  • Skimping on the meat and cheese filling, which is meant to be generous and is central to the bread's character.
  • Underbaking, leaving the dense, enriched dough undercooked in the center.

Cultural Note

Casatiello is a defining feature of the Neapolitan Easter table, its whole baked-in eggs traditionally understood to symbolize resurrection and rebirth for the holiday.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: A glass of wine, As part of an Easter breakfast spread
