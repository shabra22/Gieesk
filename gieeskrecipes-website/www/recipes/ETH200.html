🍈 Ethiopian Tropical Fruit Platter

ፍሬ ሳህን — Fre Sahin

Ethiopian Tropical Fruit Platter — a generous arrangement of Ethiopia's finest fruits: mango, papaya, banana, avocado, guava, passion fruit, and watermelon, dressed with a honey-lemon-cardamom syrup. Ethiopia's remarkable fruit diversity on one beautiful plate.

Ethiopia's fruit diversity is extraordinary — from the mango of the lowlands to the highland strawberries of Jimma, from the papaya of the Rift Valley to the avocado of the highland gardens. The fruit platter celebrates this diversity with a simple honey-cardamom dressing that enhances without overpowering.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large serving plate, Small bowl for dressing

    Selection of Ethiopian fruits (use what is available):

    • 1 ripe mango — sliced
    • ½ papaya — seeded, sliced
    • 2 ripe bananas — sliced
    • 1 avocado — sliced
    • 200g watermelon — cubed
    • 2 guavas — halved
    • 2 passion fruits — halved

    Honey-Cardamom Dressing

    • 2 tbsp Ethiopian honey
    • 2 tbsp lemon juice
    • ½ tsp ground cardamom
    • 1 tbsp rose water (optional)

    Method

    1. Mix honey, lemon juice, and cardamom for the dressing.
    2. Prepare all fruits — peel and slice where needed.
    3. Arrange on a large plate artfully — no rules, only abundance.
    4. Drizzle honey-cardamom dressing over.
    5. Place passion fruit halves for guests to squeeze themselves.
    6. Serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • The avocado is typically not served in sweet fruit platters internationally — in Ethiopia it absolutely is.
    • Passion fruit is squeezed over everything by guests — provide half fruits not pulp.
    • Ethiopian mango (Tommy Atkins or Kent) at peak ripeness requires no dressing — but the honey-cardamom elevates everything.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Preparing too far ahead — avocado and banana discolour.

    The Science

    The lemon juice in the dressing inhibits enzymatic browning on avocado and banana. The honey's hygroscopic properties draw the fruit's own juices to the surface, creating a natural additional dressing.

    Health Benefits

    • Extraordinary vitamin C from guava and passion fruit.
    • Beta-carotene from mango and papaya.
    • Healthy fats from avocado.
    • Very high antioxidant diversity from multiple fruits.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian fruit platter is the dessert that requires no cooking and no complexity — just the extraordinary diversity of Ethiopian fruits arranged with care and dressed with the country's finest honey. It is Ethiopia's agricultural abundance made edible.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not applicable.

    Serve with: As the dessert course, At celebrations, As a light conclusion to rich Ethiopian meals
