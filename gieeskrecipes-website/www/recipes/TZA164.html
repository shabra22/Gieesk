🍦 Tanzanian Coconut Ice Cream

Aiskrimu ya Nazi

Tanzanian Coconut Ice Cream — dairy-free ice cream made from thick Zanzibar coconut milk, cardamom, vanilla, and a touch of lime zest. Sold at Forodhani Gardens and every beach restaurant — pure coconut, pure Zanzibar.

Coconut ice cream is Zanzibar's modern celebration dessert — completely dairy-free, made from the island's own coconut milk, fragrant with cardamom, and often studded with pieces of fresh coconut. At Forodhani, it is scooped from large freezer bowls by vendors who have been making it the same way for decades.

Ingredients

Equipment: Ice cream machine or loaf tin for no-churn method

  • 800ml thick coconut milk (from 2 cans or fresh)
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Zest of 1 lime
  • Pinch of salt

Optional: ½ cup fresh grated coconut mixed in

    Method

    1. Whisk coconut milk, sugar, cardamom, vanilla, lime zest, and salt until sugar dissolves.
    2. Churn method: pour into ice cream machine. Churn 20—25 minutes. Freeze 4 hours.
    3. No-churn method: whip in a cold bowl for 3 minutes. Freeze 1 hour. Whip again. Repeat 3 times. Freeze solid.
    4. Serve in small bowls or cones. Garnish with toasted coconut.

    Chef Tips

    • Thicker coconut milk produces a creamier result — use the cream from the top of the can.
    • The no-churn method works well — just requires patience.
    • Lime zest is the brightening note.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Thin coconut milk — icy result.
    • Skipping the no-churn re-whipping.

    The Science

    Coconut milk's high fat content (20—25%) allows it to freeze into a smooth, creamy solid without the dairy proteins that normal ice cream uses. The fat crystals structure provides the smooth texture; the sugar lowers the freezing point, preventing it from becoming completely rock-hard.

    Cultural Note

    Zanzibar coconut ice cream at Forodhani Gardens — scooped into a cone, eaten at the water's edge as the Indian Ocean breeze cools the evening — is one of the simplest and most perfect food experiences the island offers.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Freeze up to 2 weeks.

    Reheating: Soften 5 minutes at room temperature.

    Serve with: As a standalone dessert, With fresh mango, Alongside kashata
