🥞 Tanzanian Coconut Pancakes

Panceki ya Nazi

Tanzanian Coconut Pancakes — light, fluffy pancakes made with coconut milk and a touch of cardamom, served with fresh tropical fruit and honey. The modern Zanzibar breakfast that combines Western technique with coastal ingredients.

Coconut pancakes are the modern Zanzibar breakfast at hotels and guesthouses — the Western pancake technique applied to coastal ingredients. The coconut milk produces a softer, more tender pancake than plain milk, and the cardamom announces their Zanzibari origin.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan or griddle

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp butter — melted

Serve with: fresh mango, pineapple, banana, honey

    Method

    1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, cardamom, and salt.
    2. Whisk coconut milk, egg, and melted butter.
    3. Combine wet and dry. Mix until just combined — lumps are fine.
    4. Rest 5 minutes.
    5. Cook in a lightly buttered pan over medium heat: 2—3 minutes per side.
    6. Serve with fresh tropical fruit and honey.

    Chef Tips

    • Do not over-mix — lumpy batter makes fluffier pancakes.
    • Medium heat only — too hot burns exterior before inside cooks.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Over-mixing — tough, flat pancakes.
    • Too high heat.

    The Science

    Over-mixing develops gluten, creating a tight network that traps CO2 bubbles less efficiently. A lumpy batter has minimal gluten development — the CO2 bubbles from baking powder expand freely, producing a light, fluffy result.

    Cultural Note

    Coconut pancakes are the Zanzibar hotel breakfast — the first food international visitors eat on the island, and often the food they remember most fondly because nothing else tastes quite like coconut milk pancakes eaten in Zanzibar.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten fresh.

    Reheating: Briefly in pan.
