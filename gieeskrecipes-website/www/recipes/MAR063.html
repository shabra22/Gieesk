🍪 Baked Harcha (Semolina Cookies with Anise, Raisins and Sesame)

Harcha dyal ferran / harcha au four

Small, crumbly baked semolina cookies flavoured with anise, sesame and raisins, rolled in sugar and semolina.

55mTotal time
20mPrep
25mCook
30Servings
110Calories
EasyDifficulty

These are a baked relative of the stovetop harcha bread. The moist dough, almost too wet to shape, rests about 10 minutes until it can be rolled into 2-inch balls; they are rolled in coarse semolina and sugar and baked at 170°C for about 25 minutes until golden.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking trays, Oven

  • 2 cups fine semolina
  • 1/2 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons sesame seeds
  • 1 teaspoon anise seeds
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 2/3 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup milk, approx.

Garnish

  • 4 tablespoons semolina (coarse or fine)
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 338°F (170°C).
  2. Mix the dry ingredients, seeds and raisins; stir in the vanilla, eggs, melted butter and milk. The dough should be almost too moist to shape.
  3. Rest for about 10 minutes, or until you can easily roll balls from the dough.
  4. Roll into balls about 2 inches (5 cm), roll in the semolina and sugar garnish, and space two to three inches apart on trays.
  5. Bake about 25 minutes, or until golden.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: golden after about 25 minutes.
  • A 2021 reader comment found baking powder had been printed as 6 teaspoons; the author confirmed 3 teaspoons and updated the page.
  • These are often sold larger in snack shops, says the author.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using 6 teaspoons baking powder (the old misprint).
  • Skipping the rest so the dough cannot be rolled.
  • Overbaking.

Cultural Note

The author notes that classic harcha is a stovetop semolina flatbread and that these baked cookies are often sold in snack shops in a larger size.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps several days in a tin.

Reheating: Not reheated.

Serve with: Mint tea
