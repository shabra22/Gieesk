🫓 Warqa (Homemade Pastry Leaves)

Warqa / ouarka (الورقة)

Paper-thin pastry leaves made by dabbing soft dough onto a hot pan; the wrapper for bastilla and briouats.

13h 20mTotal time
1hPrep
20mCook
12Servings
80Calories
ExpertDifficulty

The dough is made in a food processor from high-gluten flour, fine semolina, salt, vinegar or lemon juice, olive oil and water, then refrigerated overnight. The leaf is cooked for about 2 minutes on the hot pan or until it turns completely white and the edges begin to curl but it is still supple. Leaves are brushed with oil. They keep refrigerated up to 3 days or frozen up to a month.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Large non-stick skillet or griddle

  • 1 3/4 cup (226 grams) high-gluten flour
  • 1/4 cup (40 grams) fine semolina flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon cider vinegar or fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 3/4 cup (177 ml) water
  • Vegetable oil for brushing the cooked leaves (no quantity printed)

Method

  1. In a food processor, with the machine running, add 1 cup water and the vinegar. Process for 30 seconds.
  2. Slowly pour in 3/4 cup more water and process another 30 to 45 seconds.
  3. Refrigerate the dough overnight.
  4. Cook each leaf on a hot pan for about 2 minutes, until it turns completely white and the edges begin to curl but it is still supple.
  5. Brush the cooked leaves with oil. Keep refrigerated up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: completely white, edges curling, still supple.
  • The page says not to worry about lacy edges or tiny cracks in the centre.
  • The water amounts differ between the ingredient list and the method; see sources.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking until brittle.
  • Skipping the overnight rest.
  • Stacking leaves unoiled.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month.

Reheating: Not reheated; used as a wrapper.

Serve with: Chicken bastilla
