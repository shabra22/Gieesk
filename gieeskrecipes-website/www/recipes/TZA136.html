🐙 Zanzibar Octopus Salad

Saladi ya Pweza

Zanzibar Saladi ya Pweza — tenderised octopus sliced and tossed with tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, coriander, and a lime-garlic-chilli dressing. The elegant Zanzibar octopus salad — a lighter, fresher preparation than the curry, showcasing the tidal flat catch at its most refined.

Octopus salad is Zanzibar's most refreshing seafood preparation — the tenderised octopus is sliced and served cold or at room temperature in a vibrant lime dressing. The same octopus that goes into the rich curry here reveals a completely different character: clean, light, and briny.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Knife and board

  • 600g fresh octopus — tenderised and simmered until just tender
  • 2 medium tomatoes — diced
  • ½ cucumber — diced
  • ¼ red onion — very thinly sliced
  • 1 green chilli — sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander

Lime-Garlic Dressing

  • 3 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove — minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Tenderise octopus (freeze-thaw or pound). Simmer in plain water 25—30 minutes until tender. Cool completely.
  2. Slice cooled octopus into 2cm pieces.
  3. Whisk all dressing ingredients together.
  4. Combine octopus, tomato, cucumber, red onion, and chilli.
  5. Pour dressing over. Toss gently.
  6. Scatter coriander.
  7. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

Chef Tips

  • Cool octopus completely before slicing.
  • Do not overdress — the octopus flavour should dominate.
  • Add dressing just before serving.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Warm octopus — absorbs dressing unevenly.
  • Over-dressing.

Cultural Note

Octopus salad is Zanzibar's most elegant seafood preparation — the same catch that the women bring in from the tidal flats, prepared with the lightness and restraint that shows its quality most clearly.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best served within 2 hours.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a starter, Chapati (TZA004), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002)
