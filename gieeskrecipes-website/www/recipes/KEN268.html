🥗 Kenyan Tropical Chicken Salad

Saladi ya Kuku ya Tropiki

Kenyan Tropical Chicken Salad — grilled spiced chicken over mixed greens with mango, avocado, papaya, macadamia nuts, and a passion fruit-lime vinaigrette. The signature hotel salad that puts Kenya's tropical produce on display.

The tropical chicken salad is the signature salad of Kenya's finest resort and hotel restaurants — a generous, colourful bowl that showcases the extraordinary quality of Kenya's tropical produce while delivering a complete, balanced meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill or pan, Bowl, Whisk

  • 4 chicken breasts — grilled with paprika, garlic, and lemon
  • 4 cups mixed salad greens
  • 1 large ripe mango — sliced
  • 2 ripe avocados — sliced
  • ½ papaya — sliced
  • ¼ red onion — thinly sliced
  • ½ cup macadamia nuts — toasted and roughly chopped

Passion Fruit-Lime Vinaigrette

  • 4 passion fruits — juice only
  • 3 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Grill chicken with spices. Rest and slice thinly.
  2. Make vinaigrette: whisk passion fruit, lime, honey, and salt. Whisk in olive oil.
  3. Arrange greens on plates.
  4. Top with mango, avocado, papaya, and red onion.
  5. Lay sliced chicken across the salad.
  6. Drizzle generously with vinaigrette.
  7. Top with toasted macadamia nuts.

Chef Tips

  • Toast macadamia nuts in a dry pan — they go from raw to burnt very quickly.
  • Dress generously — this salad needs the vinaigrette.
  • Arrange for visual impact — this is a restaurant-level presentation salad.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-dressing.
  • Burning the macadamia nuts.
  • Poor visual arrangement — this salad should be beautiful.

The Science

Macadamia nuts have the highest fat content of any nut (76%) — when toasted, this fat reaches temperatures well above the boiling point of water, creating rapid Maillard browning on the nut surface and producing rich, deeply toasted flavour compounds.

Cultural Note

The tropical chicken salad is Kenya's culinary calling card at its finest — a salad that could only exist in a country with these specific ingredients, prepared with confidence and pride in what the Kenyan landscape produces.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Assemble and dress immediately before serving.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a main course salad, With fresh juice or wine
