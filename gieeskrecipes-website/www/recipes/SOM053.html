🍗 Suqaar Digaag

Suqaar Digaag

Cubed chicken leg with potato and carrot cut into sticks, seasoned with xawaash, turmeric and paprika — the quick version of suqaar, and what a Somali restaurant puts on the platter next to the rice.

Suqaar is a shape rather than a recipe: small cubes, cooked fast. The chicken version is the weeknight one, and the order is the opposite of what you might expect — the potatoes and carrots go in first and cook for eight or ten minutes, and the chicken goes in near the end, because it needs almost no time and dries out the moment it gets more.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan or wok with a lid

  • 680 g (1 1/2 lb) skinless boneless chicken legs, cubed
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) canola oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 potato, cut into sticks
  • 1 carrot, cut into sticks
  • 1 tsp xawaash spice blend
  • 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) water
  • 100 g (1 cup) sweet peppers, sliced
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp green chilli sauce, to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and add the sliced onion, then the potato and carrot sticks.
  2. Stir well, cover and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until the vegetables are nearly tender.
  3. Add the xawaash, turmeric, paprika and salt, and stir well.
  4. Add the cubed chicken, stir, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes.
  5. Add the water, cover, and cook for 5 minutes.
  6. Add the sliced sweet peppers and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, so they keep some bite.
  7. Finish with the black pepper and the chilli sauce, and serve with rice or anjero.

Chef Tips

  • Dark meat. Chicken leg stays moist through this; breast will work but dries out within a minute of being done.
  • Vegetables first, chicken late. The cubes need about eight minutes in total and the potatoes need ten on their own.
  • Soak the cut potato sticks in cold water and drain them before frying, to wash off the surface starch.
  • Deboning chicken legs yourself is cheaper than buying them boneless, and the bones freeze for fuud ari.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Putting the chicken in at the start with the vegetables, so it is overcooked by the time the potatoes are done.
  • Cutting the potato into cubes rather than sticks, which changes how fast it cooks and how it sits on the plate.
  • Adding the sweet peppers early, where they go soft and lose their colour.
  • Using breast meat and then cooking it to the same schedule as leg.

Health Benefits

  • Chicken leg is a good source of protein, niacin and selenium.
  • Carrot and sweet pepper add vitamin A and vitamin C.
  • A complete meal in one pan with vegetables already in it.

Cultural Note

Somali restaurants serve food on a shared platter — a mound of rice with two different meats beside it — and suqaar digaag is one of the standards in that rotation. It is the dish that gets ordered when nobody can agree on goat or fish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat quickly in a hot pan. Long reheating dries the chicken.

Serve with: Bariis Iskukaris, Canjeero, Basbaas
