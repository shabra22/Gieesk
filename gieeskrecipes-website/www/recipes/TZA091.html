🫘 Tanzanian Coconut Beans

Maharage ya Nazi

Tanzanian Maharage ya Nazi — kidney or red beans slow-cooked until completely tender, then simmered in a rich coconut milk and tomato sauce fragrant with Zanzibar spices and coriander. The most beloved bean dish of the Tanzanian coast — rich, creamy, and deeply satisfying.

Maharage ya Nazi is the coastal version of bean stew — the coconut milk transforms everyday beans into something extraordinary. The combination of the creamy beans and the rich coconut sauce, served over wali wa Nazi, is one of the most complete and satisfying plant-based meals in East African cooking.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Zanzibar and the Swahili coast — the defining coastal bean dish

Maharage ya Nazi is one of the most distinctive dishes of the Tanzanian coast — the combination of slow-cooked beans with thick coconut milk produces a richness and depth that no other bean preparation can match. It is the dish that makes visitors to Zanzibar understand why coconut milk is used in everything on the coast — because it makes everything better. Served at every celebration, every family meal, and every mama lishe on the coast.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 400g dried kidney or red beans — soaked overnight
  • 400ml thick coconut milk
  • 2 large onions — diced
  • 4 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander

Method

  1. Cook soaked beans in fresh water for 60—75 minutes until completely tender. Reserve 1 cup cooking liquid.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onions until deeply golden — 10 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and all spices. Stir 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes until very thick.
  5. Add cooked beans and reserved liquid.
  6. Simmer 15 minutes until sauce coats the beans.
  7. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 10 minutes.
  8. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Beans must be completely tender before adding to the sauce.
  • Add coconut milk only at the end — never boil it hard.
  • The reserved bean cooking liquid adds flavour — use it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooked beans.
  • Boiling coconut milk hard.
  • Discarding bean liquid.

The Science

Bean cooking liquid contains dissolved plant proteins and starch — adding it to the sauce provides natural body and thickening. The coconut milk fat coats the bean surface during the final simmer, creating the characteristic glossy, creamy result.

Cultural Note

Maharage ya Nazi is the dish that defines the Tanzanian coast's relationship with coconut — the same ingredient that perfumes the rice and bastes the fish also transforms the humble bean into a feast. It is the most requested dish by Tanzanian diaspora worldwide.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004)
