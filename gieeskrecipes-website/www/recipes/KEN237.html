🧁 Kenyan Vanilla Cupcakes

Keki Ndogo za Vanilla

Kenyan Vanilla Cupcakes — moist, tender vanilla cupcakes frosted with a passion fruit-infused buttercream. The bakery staple of Nairobi — individually portioned celebration cakes that are as beautiful as they are delicious, with a Kenyan twist in the passion fruit frosting.

Vanilla cupcakes with passion fruit frosting have become a Nairobi bakery signature — the cupcake itself is a moist, classic vanilla sponge, but the frosting incorporates fresh passion fruit juice for a tropical twist that is unmistakably Kenyan.

Ingredients

Equipment: Cupcake tin, 12 cupcake liners, Electric beater, Piping bag

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 115g butter — softened
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup milk

Passion Fruit Buttercream

  • 150g butter — softened
  • 3 cups icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp passion fruit juice — strained
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Line tin with cupcake liners.
  2. Beat butter and sugar until pale — 4 minutes.
  3. Add eggs one at a time. Add vanilla.
  4. Alternate adding flour and milk in three additions.
  5. Fill liners ⅔ full. Bake 18—20 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool completely before frosting.
  7. Make buttercream: beat butter until pale. Add icing sugar and passion fruit juice alternately. Beat until fluffy.
  8. Pipe onto cupcakes.

Chef Tips

  • Fill liners only ⅔ full — they rise significantly.
  • Completely cool before frosting — warm cupcakes melt the buttercream.
  • Passion fruit juice must be strained — seeds in the buttercream are unpleasant.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-filling liners.
  • Frosting warm cupcakes.

The Science

The ⅔ fill rule accounts for the CO2 expansion from baking powder during baking — cupcakes rise to form a dome above the liner. Over-filling causes overflow; under-filling produces flat-topped, dense cupcakes.

Health Benefits

  • Passion fruit adds vitamin C to the frosting.

Cultural Note

Vanilla cupcakes with passion fruit frosting have become synonymous with Nairobi's bakery culture — the treat that appears at office birthdays, children's parties, and café display cases across the city.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Room temperature 3 days. Refrigerate frosted 4 days.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Iced Kenyan Coffee, Festival Fruit Punch, Fresh Juice
