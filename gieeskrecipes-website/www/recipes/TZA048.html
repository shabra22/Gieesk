🐐 Tanzanian Goat Coconut Curry

Mchuzi wa Mbuzi wa Nazi

Mchuzi wa Mbuzi wa Nazi — Tanzanian goat coconut curry. Young goat slow-cooked until falling-tender in a deeply spiced coconut milk and tomato sauce. The most luxurious goat preparation on the Tanzanian coast — served at weddings and major celebrations.

Goat coconut curry is the Swahili coast's most celebratory goat dish — the combination of slowly cooked young goat and rich coconut milk produces a sauce of extraordinary depth. It is served at weddings and major celebrations where only the best food will do.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot

  • 1 kg young goat — bone-in
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 400ml thick coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown goat. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until very golden — 10 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, all spices. Stir 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 10 minutes until very thick.
  5. Return goat. Add water. Simmer covered 70—80 minutes until very tender.
  6. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 12 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Young goat (mbuzi mdogo) is non-negotiable for this dish.
  • The tomato base must be very thick before adding water.
  • Coconut milk goes in only after the goat is fully tender.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Old goat — too tough even after long cooking.
  • Early coconut milk.

The Science

Young goat contains less mature collagen than older animals — it converts to gelatin faster and more completely, producing the characteristically silky, rich sauce.

Health Benefits

  • Goat is leaner than beef or lamb.
  • Coconut milk provides MCTs.
  • Rich in iron and zinc.

Cultural Note

Goat coconut curry is the coastal Tanzanian dish that signals the highest level of hospitality — when a host serves this, they are saying: you matter, this occasion matters, and nothing has been spared in preparing for you.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004)
