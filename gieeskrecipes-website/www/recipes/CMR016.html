🍲 Sese Plantains (Goat or Beef with Plantain Rounds and Kale)

Sese plantains

Goat or beef shank cooked until tender, then green plantain rounds simmered about 20 minutes with Maggi, crayfish and palm oil, and kale added for the last 10.

A one-pot plantain stew the author says is frequently served in Cameroon households and across West Africa. The meat and onion are cooked until tender. The green plantain rounds go in with Maggi, crayfish and palm oil and simmer about 20 minutes, and the chopped kale is added for another 10 minutes. Salt and pepper to taste.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 2 pounds goat meat or beef shank
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 pounds green plantains
  • 1/2 cup crayfish
  • 1/2 cup palm oil
  • 2 cubes Maggi (1 tablespoon bouillon)
  • 1 dash cayenne pepper, optional
  • 1 bunch kale, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Cook the meat with the chopped onion until tender. The time is not printed.
  2. Add the plantain rounds with the Maggi, crayfish and palm oil. Simmer about 20 minutes.
  3. Add the chopped kale and cook an additional 10 minutes. Season with salt, pepper and the optional cayenne.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cues are ‘until tender’ for the meat and the stated 20 and 10 minutes.
  • Add liquid as needed — the page does not give a water quantity.
  • Kale goes in last so it keeps its colour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the kale at the start.
  • Cutting the plantain rounds unevenly.
  • Skipping the tenderness check on the meat before the plantains go in.
