🧈 Ethiopian Spiced Butter — Extended

ቅቤ ነጭ ምርጥ — Kibbeh Mirt (Premium)

Ethiopian Niter Kibbeh — the premium extended version of the spiced clarified butter with additional aromatics. While ETH092 covers the standard recipe, this Niter Kibbeh includes the expanded aromatic profile used by experienced Ethiopian cooks for the most important celebration dishes.

Niter Kibbeh differs from basic Kibbeh in its additional spice complexity — more aromatics, including korerima (Ethiopian cardamom), koseret (an Ethiopian aromatic herb), and sacred basil (besobela). These additions produce a kibbeh of greater aromatic depth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan, Fine cheesecloth, Clean glass jar

  • 500g unsalted butter
  • 1 small onion — diced
  • 5 garlic cloves — halved
  • 3 tbsp fresh ginger — sliced
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom (or 3 green pods)
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp dried rue (tena adam)

Premium additions

  • 1 tsp korerima (Ethiopian black cardamom) — or additional green cardamom
  • 1 tsp dried koseret (Ethiopian herb) — or dried thyme as substitute
  • 1 tsp dried sacred basil (besobela) — or regular basil
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Melt butter in a heavy saucepan over the lowest possible heat.
  2. Add all aromatics and spices.
  3. Gently simmer on lowest heat — the butter must not fry the spices, only gently infuse.
  4. Cook for 25—30 minutes until foam subsides and the butter is golden and fragrant.
  5. The milk solids should be light gold at the bottom — not dark.
  6. Strain through cheesecloth into a clean jar.
  7. Discard all solids.

Chef Tips

  • Korerima (Ethiopian black cardamom) is dramatically different from green cardamom — smoky, camphor-like, and very complex. Find it at Ethiopian grocery shops.
  • Koseret gives Ethiopian kibbeh its distinctive character unavailable elsewhere.
  • The lowest possible heat for the entire infusion — burning is the only way to fail.
  • Premium kibbeh is best made in a large batch and used over months.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat — darkens milk solids, produces burnt flavour.
  • Insufficient straining — remaining solids cause faster rancidity.

The Science

See ETH092 for the base science. The additional korerima (containing eucalyptol and camphor compounds) and koseret (containing ursolic acid and flavonoids) add both aromatic complexity and additional antimicrobial compounds to the already-stable clarified butter.

Cultural Note

Niter Kibbeh (as opposed to plain Kibbeh) is the mark of an accomplished Ethiopian cook — the additional herbs and the precise technique required to make it properly represent the depth of Ethiopian culinary knowledge. The smell of Niter Kibbeh in a kitchen announces that something extraordinary is being prepared.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Sealed jar: 3 months room temperature, 6 months refrigerated, 1 year frozen.

Reheating: Melt gently.

Serve with: In celebration Doro Wat (ETH061), On kitfo (ETH029), As a table condiment on genfo (ETH009)
