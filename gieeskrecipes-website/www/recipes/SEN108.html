☕ Café Touba, AeroPress with Toasted Selim

Café Touba

15 g of medium-fine ground coffee, one Selim pepper pod toasted in a dry pan until it smells, ground and used by the half teaspoon in an AeroPress topped up with 200 °F water and steeped 2 to 3 minutes.

Julia Bobak’s Home Grounds recipe, published 16 August 2022 and last updated 27 June 2025, yields one 8-ounce drink. The spices — one Selim pepper pod, and optionally 1 teaspoon grated nutmeg, 1 teaspoon coriander seeds and 1 cardamom pod — are toasted in a dry pan over medium heat ‘until they release their aroma’, stirring occasionally to avoid burnt spots, then ground in a spice grinder or mortar to about the size of the coffee grounds. 15 g of medium-fine ground coffee goes in an AeroPress with a fine metal or paper filter, with only 1/2 teaspoon of the ground spice mix; water of at least 16 ounces is boiled, the chamber is topped with 200 °F water and steeped 2 to 3 minutes. The traditional version uses dark-roasted Robusta; the author prefers a medium-dark Arabica espresso blend. THE PAGE GIVES NO PEPPER-TO-COFFEE RATIO beyond this half teaspoon against 15 g.

Ingredients

Equipment: Dry pan, Spice grinder or mortar, AeroPress with fine metal or paper filter, Kettle

  • 15 grams coffee beans, ground medium-fine
  • 1 Selim pepper pod
  • 1 teaspoon grated nutmeg (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander seeds (optional)
  • 1 cardamom pod (optional)
  • Water, enough to fill the AeroPress chamber at 200 °F, from at least 16 ounces boiled

Method

  1. Toast the Selim pod and any optional spices in a dry pan over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until they release their aroma, avoiding burnt spots.
  2. Grind the toasted spices in a spice grinder or mortar to about the size of the coffee grounds.
  3. Bring at least 16 ounces of water to a boil. Put 15 g of medium-fine ground coffee and 1/2 teaspoon of the ground spice mix in the AeroPress.
  4. Top it off with enough 200 °F water to fill the chamber and steep for 2 to 3 minutes. Press and drink.

Chef Tips

  • The one observable cue is the aroma of the toasting spices, not burnt.
  • The page prints no roasting temperature for the beans or the spices.
  • Traditional café Touba uses dark-roasted Robusta.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the spices.
  • Grinding the spices much coarser than the coffee.
  • Using the whole spice mix instead of the half teaspoon the brew step uses.

Cultural Note

A Western home-brew adaptation of a Mouride coffee. The pepper is the Selim pod (Xylopia aethiopica), djar in Wolof. The brotherhood tradition and the Grand Magal pilgrimage are documented in other sources, none of which is cited here.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Not applicable.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Sweet beignets, Bread
