🥗 Salade Merk Hzina (Moroccan Chopped Salad)

Merk hzina — "sad sauce" salad

A kitchen-sink chopped salad of cherry tomatoes, sweet peppers, celery, capers and chopped preserved lemon peel with a sharp red-wine-vinegar dressing.

10mTotal time
10mPrep
6Servings
70Calories
EasyDifficulty

Halve the cherry tomatoes, dice the peppers and celery, toss with preserved lemon peel, capers, vinegar, lemon juice, olive oil and flaky salt, and add the celery leaves. Serve immediately. Brosh says the name is Judeo-Arabic for 'sad sauce', and that in her family it was a salad enjoyed at the end of the week or the day before going to the souk to restock.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl

  • 1 lb. cherry tomatoes
  • 1 lb. of sweet bell peppers
  • 2 stalks of celery, plus a handful of celery leaves
  • 1 tablespoon preserved lemon peel, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons capers
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  • ½ fresh lemon, juiced
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 pinches Maldon or other flaky salt

Method

  1. Halve the cherry tomatoes and dice the bell peppers and celery.
  2. In a large bowl toss with the preserved lemon peel, capers, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, olive oil and salt.
  3. Add the reserved celery leaves and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Serve immediately.
  • Use up whatever fresh vegetables are on hand, as the name suggests.
  • Rinse capers if very salty.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dressing it early so it waters.
  • Oversalting with capers and lemon.
  • Skipping the celery leaves.

Health Benefits

  • All raw vegetables with a light dressing.
  • Capers and preserved lemon bring sodium, so season carefully.

Cultural Note

Brosh explains that 'merk hzina' is Judeo-Arabic for 'sad sauce', a kitchen-sink salad enjoyed at the week's end, or the day before heading to the souk to restock; the recipe's journey is listed as Mogador, Morocco and Mascara, Algeria.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; 1 day.

Reheating: Not reheated.

Serve with: Khobz
