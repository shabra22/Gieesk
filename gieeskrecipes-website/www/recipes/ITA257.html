🐟 Stocco alla Mammolese

Stocco alla Mammolese

Mammola's celebrated stockfish preparation, simmered slowly with potatoes, tomatoes, and olives, the small Calabrian town so devoted to the dish it hosts an annual festival in its honor.

Despite Calabria's coastline, the town of Mammola sits inland in the mountains, and its deep culinary attachment to stockfish reflects centuries of trade routes bringing the preserved fish far from the sea to this unlikely landlocked stronghold of stockfish cuisine.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl for soaking, Heavy-bottomed pan with lid

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) dried stockfish, soaked and rehydrated
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 500 g (1 lb) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 80 g (1/2 cup) black olives, pitted
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 1 dried chili, crumbled
  • 100 ml (scant 1/2 cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Soak the stockfish in cold water for 2-3 days, changing the water several times daily, until fully softened.
  2. Drain, remove any skin and bones, and cut into large pieces.
  3. Warm the olive oil in a heavy pan and saute the onion and garlic until softened.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, olives, capers, and chili, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the potatoes and enough water to almost cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until the potatoes begin to soften.
  6. Gently add the stockfish and simmer for another 20 minutes, until everything is tender.
  7. Finish with fresh parsley and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Stockfish requires a genuinely long soak to rehydrate properly - plan accordingly, unlike quicker-soaking salt cod.
  • Let the potatoes get a head start cooking before adding the more delicate stockfish.
  • This dish is traditionally celebrated each year at a dedicated festival in Mammola.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not soaking the stockfish long enough, leaving it tough and underhydrated.
  • Adding the fish too early, causing it to overcook and fall apart before the potatoes are done.
  • Using a thin, underseasoned sauce that does not stand up to the rich stockfish.

Cultural Note

Mammola's devotion to stockfish is so strong that this small inland Calabrian town hosts an annual festival dedicated entirely to the dish, despite being far from the coast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust Calabrian red wine
