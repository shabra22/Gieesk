🥣 Egyptian Tehina Sauce

Salsat el-tehina — صوص الطحينة

Tahini loosened with warm water until it reaches the consistency of béchamel and turns pale, then seasoned with vinegar, lime, cumin, coriander and garlic.

10mTotal time
10mPrep
8Servings
150Calories
EasyDifficulty

One cup of tehina paste is stirred with warm water, starting with half a cup, adding more and stirring each time until the sauce reaches the consistency of béchamel and its colour becomes lighter. Vinegar, oil, lime juice, spices, garlic and optional parsley are stirred in.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Whisk or spoon

  • 1 cup tehina paste
  • 3/4 – 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or regular oil
  • 1 tablespoon white distilled vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup parsley, finally chopped (optional)

Garnish

  • Parsley leaves
  • Dash of paprika or Aleppo pepper
  • Drizzle of olive oil

Method

  1. Put the tahini paste in a bowl and add warm water, starting with ½ cup and stirring in more each time, until the sauce reaches the consistency of béchamel sauce and its colour becomes lighter.
  2. Stir in the vinegar, oil, lime juice, salt, spices, garlic and parsley.
  3. Garnish with parsley, paprika and a drizzle of olive oil.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: consistency of béchamel; colour lighter. Warm water makes the sauce combine smoothly.
  • Page storage: airtight in the fridge for almost a month; bring to room temperature before serving. Storing the tahini paste itself in a dark, dry spot is advised.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all the water at once, which can split the paste.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour calls tehina 'our Egyptian ketchup-like condiment'.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight in the fridge for almost a month (page); make up to a week in advance.

Reheating: Bring to room temperature and stir; loosen with warm water.

Serve with: Ta'meya, Hawawshi
