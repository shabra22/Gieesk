🍕 Sfincione Palermitano

Sfincione

Palermo's own thick, spongy street-food pizza, its dough topped generously with a rich onion and tomato sauce, anchovies, and caciocavallo cheese, baked in a rectangular pan.

Sfincione's thick, airy crumb sets it apart clearly from Neapolitan-style pizza, and it's traditionally sold from street vendors throughout Palermo, especially around Christmas, cut into generous squares rather than wedges.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rectangular baking pan

  • 500 g (4 cups) bread flour
  • 10 g (3 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 320 ml (1 1/3 cups) warm water
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for the pan
  • 10 g (1 3/4 tsp) salt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 6 anchovy fillets, chopped
  • 150 g (1 1/2 cups) caciocavallo or provolone, diced
  • 80 g (3/4 cup) breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour and salt, then add the yeast mixture and olive oil, mixing into a soft, slightly sticky dough.
  3. Knead for 8-10 minutes, then let rise, covered, for about 1.5-2 hours until doubled.
  4. Meanwhile, warm a little olive oil and saute the onions until soft and golden.
  5. Add the crushed tomatoes and anchovies, and simmer for 15-20 minutes until thickened.
  6. Press the risen dough into a generously oiled rectangular baking pan, dimpling the surface with your fingers.
  7. Spread the onion-tomato sauce evenly over the dough and scatter with diced caciocavallo.
  8. Top with breadcrumbs and a sprinkle of oregano.
  9. Bake at 220C (425F) for 25-30 minutes until deeply golden.
  10. Let cool slightly before cutting into squares to serve.

Chef Tips

  • The dough should be pressed thick, not stretched thin like Neapolitan pizza - sfincione signature thick, spongy crumb is central to its character.
  • Sauteing the onions until genuinely soft and sweet builds essential depth for the sauce.
  • This is traditionally sold and eaten in squares rather than triangular slices.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stretching the dough too thin, missing sfincione signature thick, airy texture.
  • Undercooking the onions, which should be soft and sweet rather than raw and sharp.
  • Skipping the breadcrumb topping, which is a traditional textural element of the dish.

Health Benefits

  • A good source of calcium from the cheese, with lycopene from the tomato sauce.

Cultural Note

Sfincione is a beloved Palermo street food, traditionally sold especially around the Christmas season, with its thick, spongy crust setting it distinctly apart from Neapolitan pizza traditions.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot oven; avoid the microwave, which leaves the crust soggy.

Serve with: A simple side salad
