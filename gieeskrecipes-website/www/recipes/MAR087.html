🍓 Strawberry Rose Milkshake

Milkshake bil fraise w maward — strawberry rose milk

Strawberries blended with milk and rose water into a simple Moroccan-style milkshake.

5mTotal time
5mPrep
4Servings
130Calories
EasyDifficulty

Hulled strawberries, milk and rose water are blended until well blended, with sugar or honey if wanted. The author notes that Moroccan milkshakes are flavoured with rose water or orange blossom water.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 12 oz. hulled strawberries
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 teaspoons rose water
  • sugar or honey (optional)

Method

  1. Put the strawberries, milk and rose water in a blender.
  2. Blend until well blended; sweeten with sugar or honey if wanted.
  3. Serve cold.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no time and no doneness cue beyond "well blended".

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much rose water, which tastes perfumed.

Health Benefits

  • Strawberries add vitamin C; milk adds calcium.

Cultural Note

The author writes that milkshakes are common in Morocco, often flavoured with nuts or fruits, almond and apple being the most common, and always with either rose water or orange blossom water.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink fresh.

Reheating: Serve cold.
