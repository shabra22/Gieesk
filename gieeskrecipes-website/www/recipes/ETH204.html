🍪 Ethiopian Dabo Kolo Sweet

ጣፋጭ ዳቦ ቆሎ — Tafach Dabo Kolo

Ethiopian Sweet Dabo Kolo — the sweeter, richer dessert version of the traditional crunchy Dabo Kolo (ETH162). Made with more honey, less flour, and the addition of sesame seeds. Served as a dessert sweet with coffee rather than a snack.

The sweet Dabo Kolo for dessert is richer and more indulgent than the snack version — more honey, sesame seeds for texture, and the full Ethiopian spice profile of cardamom and cinnamon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Oven at 170°C

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp fenugreek
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp water

Method

  1. See ETH162 for base method — add egg, more honey, and sesame seeds.
  2. Mix all ingredients to a firm dough.
  3. Roll into ropes. Cut into 1cm pieces.
  4. Spread on baking sheet.
  5. Bake at 170°C 22—25 minutes until golden and completely crisp.
  6. Cool completely.

Chef Tips

  • Richer than standard Dabo Kolo — must still be completely crisp.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underbaking.

Cultural Note

Sweet Dabo Kolo is the dessert table version of the snack basket version — the same form, elevated by more honey and sesame into something that earns its place after a meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container — 3 weeks.

Reheating: Not needed.

Serve with: With Ethiopian coffee as a dessert, At celebrations
