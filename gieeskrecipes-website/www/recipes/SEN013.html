🍗 Chicken Yassa

Yassa ginaar — poulet yassa

Grilled first, then finished in two pounds of onions caramelised until they are a uniform light brown — with nokos, lime and Dijon.

3h 10mTotal time
25mPrep
45mCook
6Servings
520Calories
MediumDifficulty

The version that grills the chicken before it meets the onions, which is the step most printings skip. Six bone-in chicken legs marinate at least two hours in thyme, scallions, about a quarter cup of lime juice, white vinegar and a tablespoon of oil, then grill six to seven minutes a side until almost cooked through. Two pounds of yellow onions in thick strips go into three tablespoons of oil over medium-high, left one to two minutes without stirring, then stirred only occasionally for ten to twelve minutes until they take on colour. Scotch bonnet, bay leaf and Dijon follow for five minutes, until the pepper smells fragrant and the onions are a uniform light brown.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill, Large heavy pot, Blender, for the nokos, Platter and foil

  • 6 bone-in, skinless whole chicken legs, cut apart into thighs and drumsticks
  • 2 tbsp fresh thyme, finely chopped
  • 4 scallions, finely chopped
  • 3/4 cup lime juice — about 6 to 7 limes
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/4 cup peanut or vegetable oil — 1 tbsp for the marinade, 3 tbsp for cooking
  • 2 lb yellow onions, cut in thick strips
  • 1 whole scotch bonnet pepper
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp nokos
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup green olives, rinsed

To serve

  • Spring vegetable fonio pilaf

Method

  1. Combine the chicken, thyme, scallions, about 1/4 cup of the lime juice, the vinegar and 1 tablespoon of oil.
  2. Refrigerate covered at least 2 hours, or overnight. Discard the leftover marinade.
  3. Heat a grill until hot. Grill the chicken 6 to 7 minutes on each side, until almost cooked through.
  4. Transfer to a platter and cover loosely with foil.
  5. In a large pot heat the remaining 3 tablespoons of oil over medium-high. Add the onions and cook 1 to 2 minutes without stirring.
  6. Stir once, then stir only occasionally for 10 to 12 minutes, until the onions take on colour.
  7. Stir in the scotch bonnet, bay leaf and mustard. Cook and stir about 5 minutes, until the pepper smells fragrant and the onions are a uniform light brown. Add 1 to 2 tablespoons of water only if needed to prevent scorching.
  8. Add the remaining lime juice to taste, then the salt and pepper.
  9. Add the nokos, the grilled chicken, the juices accumulated on the platter and 1 cup of water.
  10. Stir well and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  11. Add the olives.
  12. Remove the bay leaf and scotch bonnet if you wish, and serve the chicken and sauce over the pilaf.

Chef Tips

  • A uniform light brown is the onion cue, and it takes fifteen minutes of near-neglect to get there. One to two minutes untouched, then stirred only occasionally — stirring constantly steams them instead of colouring them.
  • Grill the chicken before it goes in the pot. Almost cooked through, not fully — it finishes in the sauce and will dry out otherwise.
  • Split the lime juice. A quarter cup into the marinade, the rest added to taste at the end, so you get both the marinating acid and the fresh sharpness.
  • Until the pepper smells fragrant is a real cue and worth waiting for — it is when the scotch bonnet's aroma turns from raw and green to round.
  • Two pounds of onions for six chicken legs looks absurd and is correct. They collapse to a fraction of their volume and they are the sauce.
  • Water only if it is scorching, one or two tablespoons. Adding more turns a caramelising pan into a braise.
  • Nokos is this author's own pounded green seasoning — bay, jalapeño, green pepper, peppercorns, garlic and ginger. It is at Nokos, Green Seasoning Paste and it is not the same thing as nokoss, the Wolof onion-and-nététou base at Nokoss.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stirring the onions constantly.
  • Grilling the chicken all the way through.
  • All the lime juice into the marinade.
  • Adding water to the onions early.
  • Confusing nokos with nokoss.

Health Benefits

  • Two pounds of onions across six servings supplies substantial quercetin and prebiotic fructans.
  • Lime juice at this quantity is a real source of vitamin C.
  • Skinless chicken legs keep the fat moderate for the richness.
  • Gluten-free and dairy-free.

Cultural Note

Yassa came north out of the Casamance and became one of the two dishes everyone names when they name Senegalese food. Dijon mustard in it is not a fusion flourish but a hundred and fifty years of French grocery in Dakar, absorbed and made local — which is most of what a national cuisine is.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 4 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen. It improves overnight.

Reheating: Gentle heat, covered. Add a splash of water if the onions have tightened.

Serve with: Spring Vegetable Fonio Pilaf, Nokos, Attaya
