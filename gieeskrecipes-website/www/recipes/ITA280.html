🍪 Torta Sbrisolona

Sbrisolona

Mantua's deliberately crumbly almond and cornmeal cake, its name literally meaning 'the crumbly one,' meant to be broken apart by hand at the table rather than neatly sliced.

Sbrisolona's coarse, sandy texture is entirely intentional, achieved by keeping the butter cold and only barely working the dough together, and tradition dictates it should be cracked apart with a firm tap rather than cut with a knife.

Ingredients

Equipment: Round baking pan, Mixing bowl

  • 150 g (1 1/8 cups) 00 flour
  • 150 g (1 cup) coarse cornmeal
  • 150 g (1 1/2 cups) almond flour
  • 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
  • 150 g (2/3 cup) cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 80 g (2/3 cup) whole almonds, roughly chopped
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method

  1. Combine the flour, cornmeal, almond flour, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Work in the cold butter with your fingertips or a pastry cutter until the mixture resembles coarse, sandy crumbs.
  3. Add the egg yolks, chopped almonds, and lemon zest, mixing just until everything comes together loosely - the mixture should remain crumbly and not form a smooth dough.
  4. Crumble the mixture loosely and evenly into a buttered round baking pan, without pressing it down firmly.
  5. Bake at 170C (340F) for 25-30 minutes until golden.
  6. Cool completely in the pan.
  7. Dust with powdered sugar and serve by breaking into rustic pieces rather than slicing neatly.

Chef Tips

  • Keep the butter cold and handle the dough minimally - the deliberately crumbly, sandy texture is the entire point of this cake.
  • Do not press the mixture into the pan firmly; a loose, uneven crumble bakes into the correct texture.
  • Traditionally, this cake is cracked apart by hand or with a light tap, never sliced neatly with a knife.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overworking the dough until it becomes smooth, losing the essential crumbly texture.
  • Pressing the mixture too firmly into the pan, resulting in a denser cake than intended.
  • Trying to slice it neatly with a knife, when the tradition is to break it apart by hand.

Cultural Note

Sbrisolona is a beloved specialty of Mantua, its deliberately rustic, crumbly texture and name reflecting a proud tradition of imperfection done exactly right.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store covered at room temperature for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: A glass of sweet wine, Espresso
