🍯 Sirop de tamarin (Tamarind Syrup)

Sirop de tamarin

A kilogram of tamarind soaked in 1½ litres of water 10 minutes, macerated, filtered and boiled 20 minutes with a kilogram of sugar; diluted at 1 volume of syrup to 5 volumes of water.

p. 75 of the October 2023 recipe book: 1 kg tamarind fruit, 1 kg sugar and 1.5 litres of water. The fruit is soaked in the 1½ litres of drinking water 10 minutes, macerated, filtered, the sugar added and the syrup boiled 20 minutes, then cooled and kept cool. To drink: ‘take one volume of syrup for 5 volumes of water’. The book again calls it a ‘laxative par excellence’ (unsourced). The diagram says ‘Cuisson (65°)’ and shows no sugar, in conflict with the text; recorded, not reconciled. The syrup is described as unpasteurised.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Fine cloth or sieve, Bottles

  • 1 kg tamarind fruit
  • 1 kg sugar
  • 1.5 litres water

Method

  1. Soak the fruit in 1½ litres of drinking water for 10 minutes.
  2. Macerate and filter.
  3. Add the sugar and boil 20 minutes.
  4. Cool and keep cool.
  5. To serve, dilute at 1 volume of syrup to 5 volumes of water.

Chef Tips

  • The printed dilution is 1 volume of syrup to 5 volumes of water.
  • The diagram’s 65° and missing sugar conflict with the text.
  • The syrup is unpasteurised, so keep it cold.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Drinking it undiluted.
  • Forgetting the sugar in the boil.
  • Storing warm.

Health Benefits

  • The book claims a laxative effect; it cites no evidence.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep cool (‘conserver au frais’). No shelf life is printed.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Served cold, diluted
