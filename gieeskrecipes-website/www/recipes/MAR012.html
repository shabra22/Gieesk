🍳 Kefta Mkawra (Kefta Tagine with Eggs)

Kefta mkawra — meatballs and eggs in tomato sauce (كفتة مكورة)

Little beef or lamb meatballs simmered in a cumin-and-paprika tomato sauce, finished with eggs cracked on top and cooked until set.

1h 5mTotal time
20mPrep
45mCook
4Servings
450Calories
EasyDifficulty

A weeknight tagine: the meatballs are seasoned with garlic, onion, salt, paprika and parsley; the sauce is built with olive oil, onion, three large tomatoes, turmeric, spicy paprika (sudaniya in Morocco), cumin and garlic. Once the meatballs are cooked through, three eggs are cracked on top and the tagine is covered to set them. Mouttaki cooks hers until the yolk is hard; many prefer it runny.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine or wide lidded skillet, Heat diffuser for an electric range

    Meatballs

    • 1 lb ground lamb or beef
    • 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
    • 1/2 onion finely diced
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
    • Small handful chopped Italian parsley

    Sauce

    • 2-3 Tablespoons olive oil
    • 1/2 onion finely diced
    • 3 large tomatoes
    • 1 teaspoon turmeric
    • 2 teaspoons spicy paprika (sudaniya in Morocco)
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    • 1 1/2 tsp cumin
    • 1 teaspoon chopped Italian parsley
    • 1 teaspoon garlic crushed
    • 3 eggs

    Method

    1. Mix the meatball ingredients and shape into small balls.
    2. Warm the olive oil in the tagine on medium heat (use a diffuser on an electric range). Add the onion, grated or chopped tomatoes, turmeric, paprika, salt, cumin, parsley and garlic and cook the sauce briefly.
    3. Nestle in the meatballs, cover the tagine and continue to cook over low to medium heat. Check after 30 minutes.
    4. Once the meatballs are cooked through, crack the 3 eggs on top of the meatballs and sauce.
    5. Cover again so the eggs can cook through. The author cooks hers until the yolk is hard; some like the yolk still runny.

    Chef Tips

    • Ground beef is traditional, says the author. Lamb works too.
    • Sudaniya is the Moroccan name for spicy paprika; use a hot paprika if you want that heat.
    • Serve straight from the tagine with bread for scooping.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Cooking the meatballs too hard so they dry out.
    • Adding the eggs before the meatballs are cooked through.
    • Forgetting the diffuser on an electric range.

    Health Benefits

    • Eggs and meat add complete protein; tomatoes add lycopene.
    • A one-pan meal with no flour or sugar.

    Cultural Note

    The author says she learned the dish in a rental in Essaouira and that ground beef is the traditional meat.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best fresh; keeps a day in the fridge, eggs and all.

    Reheating: Warm gently covered.

    Serve with: Khobz, Mint tea
