🧀 Cheese Briouats (Adapted)

Briouat bi jben (بريوات بالجبن)

Crisp filo triangles filled with feta, mozzarella, egg and garlic, brushed with butter and baked golden.

50mTotal time
30mPrep
20mCook
15Servings
190Calories
EasyDifficulty

MarocMama notes that Moroccan versions traditionally use processed cheese (Laughing Cow) bulked with boiled egg, and that feta is expensive in Morocco; this version uses feta and mozzarella with beaten eggs and garlic for a US kitchen. The filo is cut in half by the width, a tablespoon of filling goes along the bottom, and the pastries are folded and baked at 375°F for 15 to 20 minutes until golden and starting to flake.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking sheet, Pastry brush

  • 1 package of filo dough
  • 8 oz Greek Feta
  • 8 oz shredded mozzarella
  • 2 eggs beaten
  • 1 tsp crushed garlic
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • Melted butter to brush between layers (no quantity printed)
  • 1 egg yolk beaten for brushing on top before baking

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix the feta, mozzarella, beaten eggs, garlic and pepper.
  3. Cut the filo dough in half by the width, brush the layers with melted butter, and place a tablespoon of filling along the bottom.
  4. Fold into triangles, brush the tops with the egg yolk, and bake 15 to 20 minutes until the pastry is golden brown and starting to flake.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: golden brown and starting to flake.
  • Keep unused filo covered with a damp cloth.
  • Jben, Morocco's best-loved cheese, is described by the author as the traditional filling alternative.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overfilling.
  • Letting filo dry out.
  • Not brushing with butter between layers.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 2 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Re-crisp in a 350°F oven.

Serve with: Mint tea
