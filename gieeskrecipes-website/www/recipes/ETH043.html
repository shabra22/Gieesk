🥔 Ethiopian Beef with Potatoes Wat

ሥጋ ከድንች ወጥ — Siga ke-Dinich Wat

Ethiopian Beef and Potato Wat — beef cooked in a Key Wat-style berbere sauce with potato chunks added in the final 20 minutes. The budget-extending daily wat — the potatoes absorb the berbere sauce completely and become deeply flavourful.

Siga ke-Dinich Wat is the practical everyday version of Key Wat — the addition of potatoes extends a smaller quantity of beef into a full family meal while the potatoes absorb the berbere sauce and become magnificent in their own right.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 500g beef — stewing cuts
  • 400g potatoes — peeled and cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 15 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Toast 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger. Add beef. Coat in sauce.
  4. Add water. Simmer covered 45 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes. Simmer 20 more minutes.
  6. Potatoes should be completely tender and orange from the berbere.

Chef Tips

  • Add potatoes last — they cook faster than beef.
  • The berbere potatoes are often the most popular element of this dish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding potatoes too early.

The Science

Potato starch absorbs the berbere-infused cooking liquid — drawing the carotenoids from the berbere into the potato flesh, producing the characteristic orange-stained potato that is deeply flavoured throughout.

Cultural Note

Siga ke-Dinich Wat is the honest everyday cooking of Ethiopian families — making the most of what is available, ensuring every ingredient is used to maximum effect.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Gomen (ETH086)
