🥤 Ethiopian Coffee Milkshake

ቡና ሚልክሼክ — Buna Milkshake

Ethiopian Coffee Milkshake — strong Yirgacheffe or Harrar espresso blended with vanilla ice cream, cardamom, and Ethiopian honey. The modern café treat — the birthplace-of-coffee nation's contribution to the global coffee shake tradition.

Ethiopian coffee milkshake takes the extraordinary quality of Ethiopian arabica (Yirgacheffe or Harrar) and the cardamom tradition of Ethiopian spiced drinks into the global milkshake format — producing something extraordinary.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 2 shots strong Ethiopian espresso — chilled
  • 3 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp Ethiopian honey
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • Ice

Method

  1. Chill espresso first.
  2. Blend espresso, ice cream, milk, honey, and cardamom.
  3. Add ice if needed.
  4. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Yirgacheffe espresso produces a floral, fruity shake; Harrar produces a chocolatey, winey shake.
  • Chill the espresso — hot espresso melts the ice cream too quickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hot espresso — liquid too thin.

The Science

Ethiopian arabica's high aromatic compound concentration (particularly floral terpenes from Yirgacheffe) survives the blending process well because the fat in the ice cream encapsulates the volatile aromatics, preserving them.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian coffee milkshake is the Addis Ababa youth café's most photographed drink — the global milkshake format made from the world's finest coffee, in the country where coffee was born.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a dessert drink, At Addis Ababa youth cafés
