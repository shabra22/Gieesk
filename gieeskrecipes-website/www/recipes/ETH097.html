🐟 Ethiopian Lake Hawassa Fish

ሐዋሳ አሣ — Hawassa Asa

Lake Hawassa Fish — the Rift Valley tilapia preparation of Hawassa city, the most famous fish restaurant destination in Ethiopia. Fresh tilapia pan-fried with berbere and finished in a light tomato-herb sauce. The dish that brings Ethiopians and tourists alike to Hawassa's lakeside restaurants.

Hawassa fish is the dish that built Hawassa's reputation as Ethiopia's premier fish restaurant destination — the lakeside restaurants of Hawassa, where tilapia is pulled from the lake and prepared within hours, serve what many consider the finest freshwater fish dish in Ethiopia.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pan

  • 4 whole tilapia (300g each) — cleaned, scaled, scored
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil

Sauce: 3 tomatoes — diced, 1 onion, 2 jalapeños, ¼ cup coriander

    Method

    1. Marinate tilapia in berbere, lemon, garlic, and salt for 20 minutes.
    2. Pan-fry in oil over medium-high heat: 5—6 minutes per side.
    3. Remove fish.
    4. In same pan: cook onion, add tomatoes and jalapeños. Cook 5 minutes.
    5. Return fish. Spoon sauce over. Cook 3 minutes.
    6. Scatter coriander.

    Chef Tips

    • Whole fish — skin and bones carry enormous flavour.
    • The scored skin caramelises with berbere.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Broken fish — handle minimally.

    Cultural Note

    Hawassa fish is a pilgrimage for Ethiopian food lovers — the lakeside restaurants of Hawassa, where pelicans stand on the restaurant walls and hippos surface nearby, serve the freshest fish in Ethiopia. The combination of fresh catch, berbere, and the Rift Valley setting is one of Ethiopia's great dining experiences.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Fresh salad, Lime wedges
