🍅 Kenyan Tomato Basil Soup

Supu ya Nyanya na Basil

Kenyan Tomato Basil Soup — vine-ripened tomatoes and garlic roasted until caramelised, blended with fresh basil, vegetable stock, and olive oil into a silky, intensely flavoured soup. Kenya's tomatoes make this soup far superior to the tinned version.

Tomato basil soup made with freshly roasted Kenyan tomatoes has an intensity that tinned tomato soup cannot approach. Kenya produces excellent tomatoes year-round and the roasted version — caramelised and concentrated in the oven — produces a soup of extraordinary depth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking tray, Blender, Large pot

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes — halved
  • 1 head garlic — unpeeled
  • 2 medium onions — halved
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 cup fresh basil — packed
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Cream or coconut cream — to swirl, optional

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C.
  2. Place tomatoes cut-side up, onion halves, and garlic head on a baking tray. Drizzle with olive oil and salt.
  3. Roast for 25—30 minutes until caramelised and slightly charred at the edges.
  4. Squeeze roasted garlic from skin.
  5. Transfer everything to a blender with stock and basil.
  6. Blend until smooth.
  7. Strain for an ultra-smooth restaurant finish.
  8. Warm gently. Add sugar, salt, and pepper to taste.
  9. Serve with a cream swirl and fresh basil.

Chef Tips

  • The charred edges are not burnt — they are flavour. Do not discard them.
  • Fresh basil goes in at the blending stage — not during roasting.
  • The sugar balances the tomato acidity — taste before deciding how much.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-roasting — pale, weak flavour.
  • Cooking the basil — it turns black and bitter.

The Science

Roasting concentrates tomato flavours by evaporating 60—70% of their water while simultaneously caramelising natural sugars and creating Maillard compounds from the amino acids and sugars. This produces a soup with 3—4x the flavour intensity of raw or boiled tomato soup.

Cultural Note

Tomato basil soup made with Kenya's excellent fresh tomatoes demonstrates what can be achieved when good local ingredients are treated with proper culinary technique — it bears no resemblance to the tinned version.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Garlic Bread (KEN279), Club Sandwich, As a soup course
