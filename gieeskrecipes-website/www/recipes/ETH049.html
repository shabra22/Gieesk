🫓 Ethiopian Dried Beef Firfir

ቋንጣ ፍርፍር — Quanta Firfir

Ethiopian Quanta Firfir — torn day-old injera tossed with crispy fried quanta (dried beef), kibbeh, and berbere. The most intensely flavoured firfir — the quanta adds extraordinary depth, chewiness, and concentrated beef flavour to the injera scramble.

Quanta Firfir is the firfir that experienced Ethiopian food lovers crave most — the combination of the tangy injera with the intensely spiced, crispy quanta creates a breakfast or lunch of extraordinary complexity and satisfaction.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 3 pieces day-old injera — torn
  • 100g quanta — fried and broken into pieces
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 small onion — diced
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Fry quanta in kibbeh until very crispy. Remove. Break into pieces.
  2. In the same kibbeh: cook onion until golden.
  3. Add berbere. Stir 30 seconds.
  4. Add torn injera. Toss to coat.
  5. Add quanta pieces. Toss to combine.
  6. Cook 3 minutes. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The quanta must be very crispy before adding to the injera.
  • The residual quanta flavour in the kibbeh is the secret.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Soft quanta — fry more.
  • Not using the quanta-flavoured kibbeh.

The Science

Frying quanta in kibbeh extracts the Maillard compounds from the dried beef surface into the fat — the kibbeh then carries these compounds throughout the firfir as it coats the injera pieces.

Cultural Note

Quanta Firfir is the breakfast of Ethiopian connoisseurs — the combination of three preservation traditions (fermented injera, dried quanta, kibbeh-clarified butter) into a single dish of extraordinary depth.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Ayib (ETH096), As a complete breakfast
