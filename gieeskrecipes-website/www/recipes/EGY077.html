🫘 Ful Medames (Egyptian Stewed Fava Beans, Slow-Cooker)

Ful medames — فول مدمس

Dried fava beans stewed slowly with a little rice, red lentils, tomato and garlic until completely soft, finished with baking powder and served with oil, lemon and cumin.

6h 5mTotal time
5mPrep
4hCook

330Calories
EasyDifficulty

The beans, rice and lentils are washed and set in a slow cooker with the tomato and garlic, covered with a couple of inches of boiled water and a sheet of foil under the lid. They cook on low 4 to 5 hours; after about 3 hours the lid and foil come off, the baking powder goes in and the pot cooks another hour or two until the beans are fully cooked and soft. An Instant Pot method is also given.

Ingredients

Equipment: Slow cooker (or Instant Pot), Foil

  • 2 ½ cups dry fava beans 1 pound
  • 1 ½ Tablespoons dry rice any kind, uncooked
  • 2 Tablespoons dry red lentils uncooked
  • 1 Roma tomato quartered
  • 3 large garlic cloves
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 ½-2 liter boiled water

Method

  1. Wash the beans, rice and lentils and put them in the slow cooker with the tomato and garlic.
  2. Pour in boiled water to a couple of inches above the level of the beans. Put foil under the lid.
  3. Cook on low for 4 to 5 hours. Check after two hours that there is plenty of water.
  4. After 3 hours remove the lid and foil and stir in the baking powder. Cook another hour or two, until it is fully cooked and soft.
  5. Instant Pot: pressure cook on high 30 minutes, quick release, add the baking powder, pressure cook 30 minutes more, natural release 20 minutes, then quick release.
  6. Serve in bowls with olive oil, lemon and cumin to taste.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: beans fully cooked and soft; plenty of water above the beans. Soaking is NO CUE, TIME ONLY: the card has no soak, and the tips say she soaks just a couple of hours, not more.
  • The Servings line on the page reads "4 pounds", a misprint; four servings is our reading.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the pot run low on water.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim calls ful and ta'ameya national dishes alongside koshari, with ful the main breakfast; the beans are traditionally eaten with the skin on and small fava beans are traditional. Nermine Mansour's page says it is traditionally cooked in a pear-shaped copper pot called a dammasa or kedra.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 4 days (our estimate); it thickens as it cools.

Reheating: Warm with a splash of water.

Serve with: Aish baladi, Ta'meya, Pickles
