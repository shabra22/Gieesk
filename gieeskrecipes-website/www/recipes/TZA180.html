🍟 Tanzanian Masala Chips

Chipsi Masala

Tanzanian Chipsi Masala — thick-cut potato chips deep-fried until crispy and golden, then immediately tossed in a Tanzanian masala spice mix while piping hot. The spiced chip that is served with every Tanzanian meal and sold at every chipsi stand.

Chipsi masala is not just chips with spice — it is a Tanzanian institution. The masala spice toss happens immediately out of the fryer; the heat blooms the spices, the oil from the chips carries the aromatics throughout, and the result is a chip that is simultaneously crispy, spiced, and deeply satisfying. Every chipsi vendor has their own masala mix ratio.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Paper towels, Large bowl

  • 4 large potatoes — peeled and cut into thick chips
  • Oil for deep frying

Chipsi Masala Spice Mix

  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Optional: 1 tsp chaat masala

    Method

    1. Cut potatoes into chunky chips. Soak in cold water 20 minutes. Dry thoroughly.
    2. Mix all masala spice ingredients together.
    3. Heat oil to 160°C. First fry chips for 6—8 minutes until cooked but not brown. Drain.
    4. Raise oil to 185°C. Second fry chips 3—4 minutes until deeply golden and crispy.
    5. Drain immediately.
    6. While piping hot, toss chips vigorously in a large bowl with the masala spice mix.
    7. The heat blooms the spices — serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • Double-frying is the secret to maximum crispiness.
    • The masala goes on immediately from the fryer — heat is essential to bloom the spices.
    • Every vendor's masala mix is their secret — adjust ratios to your preference.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Single-fry — less crispy.
    • Adding masala to cold chips — spices do not bloom.

    The Science

    See TZA073 for double-frying science. The masala spices contain volatile aromatic compounds that are released when heated. Adding them to hot chips causes an immediate release of these compounds into the oil coating on the chip surface — distributing the aroma uniformly.

    Cultural Note

    Chipsi masala is the street food lingua franca of urban Tanzania — ordered in every city, every town, every food court. It is the side dish that accompanies everything and the snack that is complete by itself. The masala mix is each vendor's identity.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Oven at 200°C for 5 minutes.

    Serve with: Ketchup, Pili Pili (TZA117), With chips mayai (TZA171), With mishkaki (TZA172)
