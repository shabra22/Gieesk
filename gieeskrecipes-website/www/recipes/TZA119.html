🥑 Tanzanian Avocado Salsa

Salsa ya Parachichi

Tanzanian Salsa ya Parachichi — ripe avocado mashed with tomato, onion, lemon, and coriander. The Tanzanian avocado condiment that accompanies nyama choma, pilau, and grilled fish — creamy, fresh, and completely irresistible.

Tanzania produces excellent avocados — and Tanzanian avocado salsa is simpler and more honest than the Mexican guacamole it resembles. The tomato and coriander make it Tanzanian; the lemon keeps it bright.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Fork

  • 2 large ripe avocados
  • 1 medium tomato — finely diced
  • ¼ red onion — finely minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • 2 tbsp lemon or lime juice
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Halve avocados. Remove stone. Scoop flesh into a bowl.
  2. Add lemon juice immediately — prevents browning.
  3. Mash roughly with a fork — leave some texture.
  4. Add tomato, onion, coriander, salt, and pepper.
  5. Fold gently to combine.
  6. Taste and adjust. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Add lemon juice immediately to prevent browning.
  • Leave texture — not completely smooth.
  • Serve within 30 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Waiting to add lemon — browning.
  • Over-mashing — loses character.

Cultural Note

Avocado salsa represents Tanzania's modern food culture at its most confident — the same incredible avocados that the country exports to European supermarkets eaten fresh and simply at home.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Nyama Choma, Grilled Fish, Pilau, Chips
