✝️ Lalibela Pilgrimage Food

ላሊበላ ምግብ — Lalibela Mgib

Lalibela Pilgrimage Food — the food of pilgrims to Ethiopia's most sacred site. The churches of Lalibela are cut from living rock and pilgrims come from across Ethiopia. The pilgrimage food is the complete fasting platter (beyaynetu) — entirely plant-based, carried to the site, and eaten in the spiritual atmosphere of the rock-hewn churches.

The Lalibela pilgrimage food is the fasting platter — pilgrims come to Lalibela during Ethiopian Christmas (Genna) after fasting, and the plant-based food shared among pilgrims from across Ethiopia represents the unity of the Orthodox tradition.

Method

  2. Pack for travel to the pilgrimage site.
  3. Eat communally with other pilgrims.

Chef Tips

  • Pilgrimage food must be all fasting dishes — no meat or dairy during the pilgrimage fasting period.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Cultural Note

Lalibela during Ethiopian Christmas is one of the most extraordinary experiences on earth — thousands of pilgrims in white robes, the rock-hewn churches lit by candles, the smell of incense, and the shared fasting food. The beyaynetu shared among pilgrims from different regions of Ethiopia is the most powerful expression of Ethiopian national food identity.
