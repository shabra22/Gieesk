🐟 Pokomo Fish Curry

Mchuzi wa Samaki wa Wapokomo

Pokomo Fish Curry — Tana River freshwater fish simmered in a fragrant tomato and onion curry sauce with mild coastal spices. The fish curry of the Pokomo people — shaped by river, forest, and coastal proximity.

The Pokomo fish curry reflects a community at the intersection of inland river life and coastal influence. The Tana River provides the fish; the proximity to the coast introduces the curry spices.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Tana River County — Pokomo heartland along the Tana River

The Pokomo are a Bantu community inhabiting the Tana River Delta. They are an agricultural and fishing community — the Tana River provides freshwater fish central to the Pokomo diet. Pokomo fish curry uses freshwater fish prepared with curry spices influenced by coastal proximity while maintaining distinctly Pokomo characteristics.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan, Wooden spoon

  • 600g freshwater fish fillets — tilapia or catfish
  • 2 medium onions — chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1½ tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup water
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lime juice

Method

  1. Season fish with salt and turmeric.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, curry powder, turmeric, and cumin. Cook for 6—8 minutes.
  4. Add water. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Add fish. Spoon sauce over gently.
  6. Cover and simmer 8—12 minutes until fish is cooked.
  7. Add lime and coriander. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Freshwater fish has a slightly earthier flavour that works beautifully with the spice blend.
  • Catfish from the Tana River is the traditional choice.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the fish.
  • Too thin sauce before adding fish.

The Science

Freshwater fish generally have a higher fat content than lean saltwater fish, contributing to a richer, more satisfying curry.

Cultural Note

The Pokomo represent one of Kenya's most distinctive communities — a river people whose entire culture is shaped by the Tana River and its delta.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Very gently over low heat.

Serve with: Ugali, Rice, Wild Greens from the Tana River area
