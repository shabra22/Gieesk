🍗 Involtini di Pollo alla Siciliana

Involtini di Pollo

Thin chicken cutlets rolled around a breadcrumb, cheese, and prosciutto filling, skewered together and baked until golden, echoing the same Sicilian filling used in stuffed sardines.

These rolled chicken cutlets borrow the classic Sicilian breadcrumb-pine-nut-raisin filling used in sarde a beccafico, adapted here for chicken, and are traditionally threaded onto skewers with bay leaves between each roll.

Ingredients

Equipment: Meat mallet, Skewers, Baking dish

  • 8 thin chicken breast cutlets
  • 100 g (1 cup) breadcrumbs, toasted
  • 60 g (¾ cup) grated pecorino or caciocavallo
  • 4 slices prosciutto, chopped
  • 30 g (¼ cup) pine nuts, toasted
  • 30 g (¼ cup) raisins, soaked
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Bay leaves
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Gently pound the chicken cutlets to an even thinness if needed.
  2. Combine the breadcrumbs, cheese, chopped prosciutto, pine nuts, drained raisins, and lemon zest with a drizzle of olive oil into a moist filling.
  3. Lay each cutlet flat, season lightly, and spread a spoonful of filling at one end.
  4. Roll each cutlet up tightly and thread 2-3 rolls onto a skewer, alternating with bay leaves.
  5. Arrange the skewers in a baking dish and drizzle generously with olive oil.
  6. Bake at 200C (400F) for 20-25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and lightly golden.
  7. Serve hot with any remaining filling scattered over the top.

Chef Tips

  • Pound the chicken to an even thickness so it rolls neatly and cooks evenly.
  • Threading the rolls onto skewers with bay leaves helps them hold their shape and adds aroma during baking.
  • This dish echoes the classic Sicilian sweet-savory filling found in stuffed sardines, adapted here for a heartier chicken version.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overfilling the cutlets, making them difficult to roll and prone to bursting.
  • Overcooking the chicken, which can dry it out.
  • Skipping the bay leaves between rolls, which contribute meaningful aroma during baking.

Cultural Note

This dish reflects Sicily's fondness for rolled, stuffed preparations, borrowing the same sweet-savory breadcrumb filling found throughout the island's culinary traditions.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently in the oven, covered.

Serve with: A simple green salad, Roasted potatoes
