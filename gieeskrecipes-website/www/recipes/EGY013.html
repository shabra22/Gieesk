🍗 Charkasseya (Circassian Chicken with Walnut Sauce)

Sharkaseya — شركسية

Boiled chicken in a thickened walnut-and-toast sauce with mastic-scented stock, served over buttered rice.

1h 35mTotal time
20mPrep
1h 15mCook
5Servings
610Calories
MediumDifficulty

A whole chicken is boiled with grated onion, mastic, cardamom, chili, lemon juice and stock cubes for about 35 minutes over medium heat until tender, and 2 cups of the stock are kept. Blended walnuts and toast are mixed with the stock until smooth and thickened, milk is added and boiled, and fried garlic, coriander and paprika are stirred in before a covered 5-minute simmer. Medium-grain rice is cooked in buttered stock for 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Food processor or blender, Saucepan

  • 1 whole chicken (cleaned)
  • 2L water (for boiling)
  • 1 white onion (grated)
  • Pinch of cardamom powder and chili powder
  • 2 mastic tears
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 chicken stock cubes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Sauce

  • 200g walnuts
  • 6 slices of white toast (edges removed)
  • ¼ tsp paprika powder
  • 1 clove garlic (crushed)
  • ¼ tsp ground coriander
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp butter

Rice

  • 2 cups medium-grain white rice
  • 3 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Boil the chicken in the water with the onion, cardamom, chili, mastic, lemon juice, stock cubes, salt and pepper over medium heat for 35 minutes or until the chicken is tender. Keep 2 cups of the stock; shred the meat.
  2. Blend the walnuts and toast, then mix into the 2 cups of stock until smooth and thickened. Add the milk and bring to the boil.
  3. In the butter fry the garlic until golden, then the coriander and paprika for 1 to 2 minutes; stir into the sauce and simmer covered for 5 minutes.
  4. Rice: melt the 3 tablespoons of butter, add the rice and 3½ cups of stock, boil on high for 3 minutes, then cook about 20 minutes or until fully cooked.
  5. Serve the shredded chicken on the rice, covered with the walnut sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: chicken tender; sauce smooth and thickened; garlic golden. Page timing is 35 minutes for a whole chicken, which is shorter than the other source's hour, so test with a thermometer or by the leg moving freely.
  • Stir the sauce constantly once milk is added so it does not catch (our tip).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-boiling the sauce so the walnut oil separates.

Health Benefits

  • Walnuts provide healthy fats; chicken is a lean protein.

Cultural Note

The article says charkasseya has Ottoman roots and was adapted to suit the Egyptian palate. The Egypt Independent page, quoting Mona Amer, notes that many Egyptian dishes with protein come from Turkey.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate chicken and sauce separately up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat the sauce gently, adding stock or milk to loosen.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
