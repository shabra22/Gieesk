🍖 Kenyan Mixed Grill Platter

Sahani ya Nyama Mchanganyiko

Kenyan Mixed Grill Platter — a spectacular restaurant-style platter of nyama choma goat ribs, chicken tikka, beef mishkaki, and Kenyan sausage, served with kachumbari, ugali fingers, pili pili sauce, and garlic mayo. The definitive Kenyan restaurant centerpiece.

The mixed grill platter is what every Kenyan restaurant orders when the table wants everything — a generous spread of the best grilled meats, beautifully presented with all the essential accompaniments. Each protein is marinated separately to showcase its best character, then grilled over charcoal and served on a large platter.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill or large griddle pan, Skewers for mishkaki, Large serving platter

    Goat Ribs

    • 400g goat ribs — salted and grilled

    Chicken Tikka

    • 300g chicken thighs — marinated in yogurt, garlic, ginger, turmeric, paprika

    Beef Mishkaki

    • 300g beef sirloin — cubed, marinated in garlic, cumin, lemon

    Kenyan Sausages

    • 4 smokies — grilled

    Accompaniments

    • 2 cups kachumbari
    • 4 ugali fingers — sliced from a firm ugali
    • Pili pili sauce
    • Garlic mayo
    • Fresh lemon wedges
    • Fresh coriander

    Method

    1. Day before: prepare all marinades and marinate each protein separately overnight.
    2. Grill goat ribs over medium-low charcoal for 35—40 minutes, turning regularly.
    3. Thread beef mishkaki on skewers. Grill 8—10 minutes, turning every 2 minutes.
    4. Grill chicken thighs for 6—7 minutes per side until cooked through.
    5. Grill smokies for 5 minutes until charred and hot.
    6. Make firm ugali. Cool and slice into fingers.
    7. Arrange everything on a large platter — meats in sections, sauces in bowls, ugali to one side.
    8. Garnish with fresh coriander and lemon wedges.

    Chef Tips

    • Each protein needs different grill timing — stagger them so everything arrives at the platter simultaneously.
    • Ugali fingers should be firm enough to hold — use less water than usual when making.
    • The platter presentation matters at restaurant level — invest in the arrangement.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • All proteins going on the grill at the same time — different cooking times.
    • Soft ugali that cannot hold its shape as fingers.

    The Science

    Each protein has different optimal cooking temperatures and times. Goat (collagen-rich) needs longer at moderate heat; chicken must reach 74°C; beef mishkaki is at its best at medium-rare (60°C). The Maillard reaction on all three produces different flavour compounds due to different amino acid and sugar compositions.

    Cultural Note

    The Kenyan mixed grill platter is the restaurant dish that brings the entire nyama choma tradition to a formal table setting — everything that makes Kenyan grilling great, arranged with care and presented with pride.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten freshly grilled.

    Reheating: Grill briefly to reheat.

    Serve with: Kachumbari, Ugali, Pili Pili Sauce, Garlic Mayo, Cold Tusker
