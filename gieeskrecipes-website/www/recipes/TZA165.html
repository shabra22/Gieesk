🍛 Swahili Lamb Biriani

Biriani ya Kondoo ya Kiswahili

Swahili Lamb Biriani — the grandest expression of the Swahili biryani tradition using lamb, the Eid sacrifice meat. Overnight-marinated lamb in yogurt and Zanzibar spices, layered with saffron rice and fried onions, sealed and dum-cooked. The most important food of the Tanzanian Islamic calendar.

Lamb biryani for Eid ul-Adha is the Swahili coast's most important ceremonial food — the lamb sacrificed in the name of faith transformed by the island's greatest culinary technique into a dish of profound significance and extraordinary flavour. The dum cooking is performed with the same reverence as the sacrifice itself.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large dum pot, Dough seal, Heat diffuser

  • 1.2 kg lamb — cubed, marinated overnight in yogurt-spice mix
  • 5 cups basmati rice — parboiled 70%
  • 3 onions — fried golden
  • ½ tsp saffron in warm milk
  • 4 tbsp ghee
  • 2 tbsp rose water
  • ½ cup coriander
  • ¼ cup raisins
  • ¼ cup fried cashews

Marinade: 1 cup yogurt, 5 garlic, 2 tbsp ginger, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp salt

    Method

    1. See TZA121 (Zanzibar Biryani) for the full technique — lamb biryani follows the same layering and dum cooking method.
    2. Lamb requires 50—60 minutes pre-cooking to reach 90% doneness.
    3. Seal tightly. Dum 30—35 minutes over lowest heat.
    4. Break seal at the table.

    Chef Tips

    • See TZA121 for all detailed tips.
    • Lamb needs longer pre-cooking than chicken.
    • The rose water is the Eid signature.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Insufficient pre-cooking of lamb.
    • Breaking the seal during cooking.

    Cultural Note

    Swahili lamb biryani for Eid ul-Adha is the most significant food the coast produces — the sacrifice made meaningful through the highest expression of the Swahili culinary tradition. To receive this biryani is to understand what the word 'celebration' means in Zanzibar.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

    Reheating: Covered with splash of water.

    Serve with: Raita, Kachumbari (TZA108), Chatni ya Nazi (TZA118)
