🥣 Shorbet Lesan Asfour (Orzo Soup)

Shorbet lisan asfour — شوربة لسان العصفور

Orzo stirred in butter until golden brown, then simmered ten minutes in chicken stock.

20mTotal time
5mPrep
15mCook
8Servings
300Calories
EasyDifficulty

A very simple Ramadan soup: a pack of orzo (lesan asfour, 500 gm) is stirred in butter until golden brown, chicken stock is added and the pot is left on medium heat for ten minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 1 pack orzo (Lesan asfour) 500gm
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan and stir the orzo in it until it turns golden brown.
  2. Add the chicken stock and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Leave for ten minutes on medium heat, then serve.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: orzo golden brown. 500 gm orzo to 3 cups of stock is very thick as printed; add more stock for a soup (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the orzo catch and darken beyond golden.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; orzo absorbs liquid on standing.

Reheating: Reheat with extra stock.

Serve with: White rice
