🍩 Kac Kac

Kac Kac

Small, diamond-shaped Somali fried dough bites, lightly sweetened and fried until golden and crisp, a favorite everyday snack and a fixture at celebrations.

Kac kac's simple, sturdy dough makes it a practical treat that keeps well for days, and its bite-sized diamond shapes have made it a common sight at Somali gatherings, served alongside tea or eaten on their own as a satisfying, not-too-sweet snack.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Deep frying pan

  • 400 g (3 cups) all-purpose flour
  • 80 g (1/3 cup) sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 150 ml (2/3 cup) warm water, approximately
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus more for frying

Method

  1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, cardamom, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the egg with the oil, then combine with the dry ingredients along with enough warm water to form a firm dough.
  3. Knead for 5-8 minutes until smooth, then rest, covered, for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll the dough out to about half a centimeter thickness.
  5. Cut into diamond or square shapes using a knife.
  6. Heat vegetable oil to 170C (340F).
  7. Fry the pieces in batches for 3-4 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
  8. Drain on paper towels and cool before serving.

Chef Tips

  • The dough should be firm, not sticky, which helps the pieces hold their shape and fry evenly.
  • Frying at a moderate, steady temperature ensures the pieces cook through without burning outside.
  • These keep well for several days in an airtight container, making them a good make-ahead treat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Making the dough too soft, which can cause the pieces to lose their shape during frying.
  • Frying at too high a temperature, browning the outside before the inside cooks through.
  • Cutting the pieces unevenly, resulting in inconsistent cooking.

Cultural Note

Kac kac is a common feature at Somali celebrations and everyday gatherings alike, its sturdy, not-too-sweet character making it a reliably popular snack alongside tea.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Shaah Cadays (spiced tea), Somali coffee
