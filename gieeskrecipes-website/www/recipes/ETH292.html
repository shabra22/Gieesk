The modern Ethiopian vegetarian platter is the beyaynetu presented with restaurant precision — the same ancient dishes, arranged with contemporary care, for an international and local audience that expects both authenticity and beauty.

Equipment: Multiple pots, Large injera plate

Method

  1. Make all wats per individual recipes.
  2. Make salads fresh immediately before serving.
  3. Arrange on large injera with restaurant precision: colours alternating, salads at edges.
  4. Rolled injera alongside.

Chef Tips

  • Restaurant presentation: the beetroot's purple, the Misir's red, the Kik's golden, and the Gomen's green should alternate for visual impact.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Random arrangement — presentation matters in the modern format.
