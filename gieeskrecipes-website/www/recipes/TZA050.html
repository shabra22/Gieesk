🍗 Tanzanian Chicken Wings

Mbawa za Kuku za Tanzania

Tanzanian Chicken Wings — chicken wings marinated in Zanzibar spices and tamarind, baked until crispy, then glazed with a honey-tamarind glaze. The most popular restaurant starter in Dar es Salaam — sweet, sticky, and powerfully spiced.

Tanzanian chicken wings carry the full flavour profile of the coast — tamarind, honey, and Zanzibar spices create a glaze that is simultaneously sweet, tart, and aromatic. At Dar es Salaam's most popular restaurants, these disappear faster than anything else on the menu.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking tray with rack, Oven at 200°C

  • 1 kg chicken wings — split
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt

Glaze

  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Coat wings. Marinate 2 hours.
  2. Place on rack. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  3. Flip. Bake 10 minutes.
  4. Make glaze: simmer all glaze ingredients 3 minutes.
  5. Brush wings with glaze. Bake 5 minutes. Flip. Glaze again. Bake 5 more minutes.
  6. Serve with extra glaze for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • The rack gives crispier skin.
  • Multiple glaze applications build the lacquer.
  • Serve immediately.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Single glaze — insufficient lacquer.
  • No rack — steamed skin.

Cultural Note

Tanzanian tamarind-glazed chicken wings are the social food of Dar es Salaam's restaurant scene — the starter that orders itself and the dish people return specifically to eat.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Oven at 200°C for 8 minutes.

Serve with: Kachumbari, Pili Pili Sauce, Cold Drinks
