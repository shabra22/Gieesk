🫘 Ful with Garlic and Cream (Ramadan Quick Version)

Ful bi-toum wa eshta — فول بالثوم والقشطة

Canned ful medames simmered with crushed garlic, then finished with cream and mashed with a fork until no whole beans are left.

20mTotal time
5mPrep
15mCook
2Servings
260Calories
EasyDifficulty

A can of plain ful medames is emptied into a small pot over a medium flame with crushed garlic. After 10 minutes of simmering, cooking cream or eshta is stirred through and the pot simmers five minutes more. The beans are broken up with a fork until no whole beans are left, and the ful is served with hot baladi bread.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small pot, Fork

  • 1 can of plain ful medames
  • ½ small carton of cooking cream or 1 large spoon of "fresh cream" – eshta
  • 3 cloves of garlic, smushed
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • drizzle of olive oil

Method

  1. Empty the can of ful into a small pot over a medium flame and add the garlic.
  2. After 10 minutes of simmering, stir in the cream.
  3. Simmer another five minutes.
  4. Break the beans apart with a fork until no whole beans are left. Season, drizzle with olive oil and serve with hot baladi bread.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: no whole beans left. Servings are our estimate; the page states no yield.
  • This is a quick modern variation on canned ful, not a dried-bean method.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the cream at the start, which can separate (our caution).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Hot baladi bread
