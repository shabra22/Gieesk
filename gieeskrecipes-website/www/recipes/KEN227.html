🍕 Kenyan Pizza

Pizza ya Kenya

Kenyan Pizza — thin-crust pizza topped with a spiced tomato and onion sauce, Kenyan spiced minced beef or chicken tikka, fresh kachumbari, avocado, and mozzarella. The Nairobi pizza — a global format with unmistakably Kenyan toppings.

Nairobi has a thriving pizza scene, and the most popular Kenyan versions confidently put local ingredients on the pizza without apology — spiced beef, kachumbari, avocado, and pili pili drizzle transform the Italian format into something entirely Kenyan.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Baking tray or pizza stone, Oven (250°C)

    Dough

    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 1 tsp dried yeast
    • ¾ cup warm water
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 tsp sugar
    • ½ tsp salt

    Kenyan tomato sauce

    • 4 medium tomatoes — diced
    • 1 small onion — finely chopped
    • 2 garlic cloves — minced
    • 1 tsp curry powder
    • ½ tsp paprika
    • 1 tsp salt

    Toppings

    • 200g spiced minced beef — cooked
    • 150g mozzarella — grated
    • 2 tbsp kachumbari
    • ½ avocado — sliced
    • 2 tbsp pili pili sauce — for drizzle
    • 2 tbsp fresh coriander

    Method

    1. Make dough: mix yeast, sugar, and warm water. Rest 10 minutes until foamy.
    2. Mix flour and salt. Add yeast mixture and olive oil. Knead 8 minutes until smooth.
    3. Cover and rest 1 hour until doubled.
    4. Make sauce: cook onion, garlic, tomatoes, and spices until thick — 15 minutes.
    5. Preheat oven to 250°C with baking tray inside.
    6. Roll dough thinly to desired size.
    7. Spread sauce, top with cheese and spiced beef.
    8. Bake 10—12 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbling.
    9. Remove from oven. Add kachumbari, avocado, pili pili drizzle, and coriander.

    Chef Tips

    • Preheating the baking tray creates a crispy base.
    • Add kachumbari and avocado after baking — fresh toppings after the oven.
    • A very hot oven (250°C) is essential for pizza.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Low oven temperature — soggy base.
    • Too many toppings — overloaded pizza does not cook evenly.

    The Science

    Preheating the baking tray simulates a pizza stone — the immediate contact with the hot surface drives rapid moisture evaporation from the base, creating the characteristic crispy crust.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate for up to 2 days.

    Reheating: Oven at 200°C for 8 minutes.

    Serve with: Masala Fries, Fresh Juice, Coleslaw
