🐟 Baccalà Mantecato

Baccalà Mantecato

Whipped Venetian salt cod, beaten with olive oil until light and fluffy like a savory mousse, traditionally spread over slices of grilled polenta.

The technique here is almost entirely about the whipping — poached, flaked stockfish beaten patiently with a steady stream of olive oil, similar to making mayonnaise, until it transforms into something impossibly light and creamy despite containing no dairy at all.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl for soaking, Large pot, Stand mixer or wooden spoon

  • 600 g (1.3 lb) dried stockfish or salt cod
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 250 ml (1 cup) extra virgin olive oil, approximately
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and white pepper, to taste
  • Grilled polenta, for serving

Method

  1. Soak the dried stockfish or salt cod in cold water for 2–3 days, changing the water several times daily.
  2. Drain and simmer the fish in fresh water with the garlic for 30–40 minutes until very tender.
  3. Drain thoroughly, remove any skin and bones, and flake the fish while still warm.
  4. Transfer to a stand mixer, or use a wooden spoon and plenty of elbow grease, and begin beating the fish.
  5. With the mixer running on medium speed, drizzle in the olive oil very gradually, similarly to making mayonnaise, until the mixture becomes pale, light, and fluffy.
  6. Continue until you reach a soft, spreadable, mousse-like consistency — this can take 10–15 minutes.
  7. Season with salt, white pepper, and fresh parsley.
  8. Serve at room temperature or lightly chilled, spread generously over slices of grilled polenta.

Chef Tips

  • The fish must be genuinely warm when you begin beating in the oil, which helps it emulsify properly.
  • Add the olive oil very gradually — pouring it in too quickly can prevent the mixture from emulsifying into a light, fluffy texture.
  • A stand mixer makes this process much easier than beating entirely by hand, though both work.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not soaking the fish long enough, leaving it too salty or tough.
  • Adding the oil too quickly, causing the mixture to separate rather than emulsify.
  • Beating an insufficient amount of oil in, resulting in a dense rather than light, whipped texture.

Cultural Note

Baccalà mantecato is a beloved Venetian cicchetti (small plate) staple, commonly found in the city's traditional bacari, or wine bars, spread on crostini or polenta.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Grilled or fried polenta, Crusty bread
