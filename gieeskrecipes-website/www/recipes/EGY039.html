🥣 Shorbet Ades (Egyptian Spiced Lentil Soup)

Shorbet ads — شوربة عدس

Orange lentils simmered with onion, carrot, celery and a curry-cumin spice mix, blended smooth, sieved and served with sumac-toasted bread.

1h 10mTotal time
15mPrep
25mCook
8Servings
250Calories
EasyDifficulty

Orange lentils soak 30 minutes and are rinsed, then simmered with sautéed vegetables, stock and spices on low for 10 minutes. The soup is cooled, blended fine, sieved, reheated and salted, and served with oven-toasted bread seasoned with sumac.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender, Fine sieve, Baking sheet

  • 2 cups orange lentil
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil such as canola oil
  • 1 small onion, coarsely chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled and cubed
  • 1 celery stalk with leaves, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 8 cups broth, chicken, meat or vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon ground coriander
  • ½ teaspoon ginger powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon sweet paprika
  • ¼ cayenne pepper, optional
  • Salt and pepper

Garnish

  • Whipping cream or sour cream, optional
  • Celery leaves, whole or chopped
  • Toasted bread, recipe follows
  • Pita bread or Lavash

Toasted bread

  • 1 teaspoon sumac
  • Salt and pepper
  • Canola oil

Method

  1. Soak the lentils in cold water for 30 minutes, then rinse under running water.
  2. Heat the oil until hot but not smoky and sauté the vegetables a few minutes until they wilt.
  3. Add the lentils and stock and bring to a boil. Add all the spices except the salt and simmer on low for 10 minutes.
  4. Cool the soup, blend it finely, sieve, reheat and add the salt.
  5. Toast the bread with oil, sumac, salt and pepper in a 150° oven for 6 to 8 minutes until crispy and golden brown. Serve with cream, celery leaves and lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: vegetables wilted; bread crispy and golden. The lentil soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. She advises avoiding high heat, which scorches, and making it a day ahead.
  • The cayenne line has no unit on the page ('¼ cayenne pepper, optional'); use a pinch to taste.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Scorching the pot on high heat.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour writes that her mother made it all the time in winter in Alexandria. Zaineldine calls lentil soup a national staple with as many recipes as people who have made it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate); the page suggests making it a day ahead.

Reheating: Reheat gently, thinning with stock.

Serve with: Toasted baladi bread
