🥥 Chicken Thighs with Red Palm and Coconut Rice

Thiebou guinaar — modern twist

Eight bone-in chicken thighs seared skin-side down, then set on rice cooked in coconut milk, stock and red palm oil and covered for 25–30 minutes.

65 minTotal time
15 minPrep
50 minCook
4Servings
760Calories
EasyDifficulty

A one-pot twist on thiebou guinaar. Thighs sear skin-side down about five minutes until the skin is golden, then the other side, and rest on a platter while all but a tablespoon of fat is poured off. Onion cooks about five minutes, garlic and 2 cups of jasmine rice are stirred in, then 1 1/2 cups each of coconut milk and chicken stock and a spoonful of red palm oil. The chicken returns on top, the pot is covered and simmers 25 to 30 minutes. Lime juice and cilantro go on at the table, with sautéed greens.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large cast-iron pot or Dutch oven with a heavy lid, Wooden spoon

  • 8 skin-on, bone-in chicken thighs (about 2 lb total)
  • Fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, coarsely chopped
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 1/2 cups full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp red palm oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp pepper, for the pot
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro, or more to taste
  • Sautéed greens, for serving

Method

  1. Season the chicken. Heat the vegetable oil in the pot over medium-high. Sear the thighs skin-side down, in batches if needed, until the skin is golden — about 5 minutes. Turn once to brown the other side and set on a platter.
  2. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of the rendered fat.
  3. Cook the onion, stirring often, until soft and slightly golden — about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the garlic and rice and stir to coat. Add the coconut milk and stock and bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce to a simmer, stir in the red palm oil, season with 1 teaspoon each of salt and pepper.
  6. Return the chicken and its juices, setting the pieces on the rice. Cover and simmer 25 to 30 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed, the rice tender and the chicken cooked through.
  7. Squeeze the lime over, top with cilantro and serve with sautéed greens.

Chef Tips

  • Skin-side down first, golden before turning — the skin is the point.
  • Pour off the fat but keep one tablespoon for the onions.
  • Do not stir once the chicken is on the rice and the lid is on.
  • Thiam calls it a twist on thiebou guinaar, ‘what he calls the original jambalaya’.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid during the simmer.
  • Leaving all the fat in the pot.

Cultural Note

In the cited remarks Thiam says Senegal’s place on the western coast made it a port of entry for French, Dutch, English and Portuguese mariners, with Portuguese influence in Casamance and French in Saint-Louis. He says he modernises recipes ‘by deconstruction’.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 3 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Steam with a splash of water.

Serve with: Sautéed greens
