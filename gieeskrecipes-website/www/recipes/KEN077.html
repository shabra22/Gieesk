🐟 Kenyan Fish Fingers

Vidole vya Samaki

Kenyan Fish Fingers — tilapia or other white fish cut into strips, seasoned with Kenyan spices, coated in spiced breadcrumbs, and deep-fried until golden and crispy. Beloved by children and adults alike — the Kenyan take on a universal classic.

Fish fingers with a Kenyan twist — the familiar breadcrumbed format elevated with garlic, ginger, lemon, and paprika in the coating. Crispy outside, tender and flaky within, they are the ideal introduction to fish for children and a beloved quick meal for the whole family.

Ingredients

Equipment: 3 shallow bowls (for flour, egg, breadcrumbs), Deep frying pan, Tongs

  • 600g tilapia or white fish fillets — cut into finger-sized strips
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 2 eggs — beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • Vegetable oil — for frying

Method

  1. Mix garlic, lemon juice, paprika, cumin, turmeric, salt, and pepper. Toss the fish strips in this seasoning.
  2. Mix the paprika and cumin into the breadcrumbs for a flavoured coating.
  3. Set up a coating station: flour in bowl 1, beaten egg in bowl 2, spiced breadcrumbs in bowl 3.
  4. Coat each fish strip: flour first, then egg, then breadcrumbs. Press gently to adhere.
  5. Heat oil to 175°C.
  6. Fry in batches for 3—4 minutes until deep golden brown, turning once.
  7. Drain on paper towels. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Season the breadcrumbs as well as the fish — this doubles the flavour impact.
  • Press the breadcrumbs firmly onto the fish — loose coating falls off during frying.
  • Make sure the oil is hot enough before the fish goes in.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet fish — pat completely dry before coating or the crumb does not adhere.
  • Too cool oil — produces soggy, pale fish fingers.
  • Overcrowding — lowers oil temperature.

The Science

The three-stage coating (flour → egg → breadcrumb) creates a moisture barrier that protects the fish during frying. The flour helps the egg adhere; the egg binds the breadcrumbs; the breadcrumbs create the crispy shell through starch gelatinisation and Maillard browning.

Cultural Note

Fish fingers have become a firm favourite in Kenyan urban households — the familiar format with a distinctly Kenyan spice profile makes them irresistible to children and adults alike.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 2 days. Freeze uncooked for up to 2 months.

Reheating: Reheat in oven at 190°C for 8 minutes.

Serve with: Chips (French Fries), Coleslaw, Kachumbari, Tartar Sauce, Pili Pili Sauce, Lemon Wedges
