🍚 Rice Pudding with Mastic and Orange Blossom (Lola’s Rice Pudding)

Roz bi-laban — أرز بلبن

Firm cooked short-grain rice simmered in spiced milk with mastic, sweetened and chilled, then folded with whipped cream and orange blossom water.

Rice boils 15 to 20 minutes on medium-high until it is firm and not mushy. Milk warms with sugar and a cloth bag of cloves and cinnamon, the rice goes in and the pudding is stirred until it thickens, with cornflour optional to speed it up. Crushed mastic is stirred in until it dissolves, the spices come out and orange blossom water goes in; once cool the pudding is folded with whipped cream and served cold.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Cloth spice bag, Mixing bowl

  • 1/2 cup cooked short-grain rice (also known as arborio)
  • 2 1/2 cups full-fat milk.
  • 1 stick cinnamon
  • 2 whole cloves
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cornflour dissolved in 1/2 cup of milk (optional)
  • 5-8 pieces mastic crystals, depends on their size
  • 1 tablespoon orange blossom water
  • 3/4 cup cream
  • 1/4 powdered sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon rose blossom water
  • Pistachios and organic rosebuds for garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the rice 15 to 20 minutes on medium-high heat; it should stay firm and not mushy.
  2. Warm the milk on low heat, add the sugar and a cloth bag of cloves and cinnamon. Add the rice and stir until the pudding thickens; the optional cornflour slurry speeds this up.
  3. Add the crushed mastic and stir a few more minutes, until the mastic dissolves.
  4. Remove the spices, add the orange blossom water and cool completely.
  5. Whip the cream (3 minutes low, then to soft peaks), with the powdered sugar, vanilla and rose water, and chill at least a couple of hours.
  6. Fold the cold cream into the cold pudding and serve cold, garnished.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: rice firm, not mushy; pudding thickens; mastic dissolves. The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The powdered sugar line is missing its unit ('1/4 powdered sugar'); use about ¼ cup.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the rice go mushy.
  • Folding warm pudding into cold cream.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Pistachios, rosebuds (garnish)
