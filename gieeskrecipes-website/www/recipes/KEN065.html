🍗 Kenyan Garlic Herb Chicken

Kuku wa Vitunguu Saumu

Kenyan Garlic Herb Chicken — whole chicken pieces marinated in a generous blend of fresh garlic, lemon, olive oil, coriander, thyme, and paprika, then grilled or roasted until deeply golden and fragrant. Simple, elegant, and universally loved.

Garlic herb chicken is the Kenyan kitchen at its most generous — no complicated technique, just great ingredients treated with care. A robust garlic and herb marinade, plenty of lemon, and a hot grill or oven produces chicken with a deeply golden exterior, fragrant herb-infused skin, and tender, juicy meat that needs no sauce to be completely satisfying.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Grill or oven, Tongs or basting brush

  • 1.2 kg chicken pieces — bone-in, skin-on
  • 6 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger — grated
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme — or 1 tsp dried
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Score the chicken pieces 2—3 times to the bone.
  2. Mix garlic, ginger, lemon juice, oil, coriander, thyme, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper into a marinade.
  3. Coat the chicken thoroughly, ensuring the marinade gets into the scored cuts.
  4. Marinate for at least 1 hour — overnight gives the best result.
  5. Grill method: cook over medium charcoal or gas for 12—15 minutes per side, turning once, until deeply golden and cooked through.
  6. Oven method: roast at 200°C for 30—35 minutes until golden and the juices run clear.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Scoring is essential — without it the marinade only flavours the surface.
  • Do not rush the marinade — the garlic and lemon need time to penetrate.
  • Baste with the remaining marinade (bring to a boil first) during the last 5 minutes for a beautiful glaze.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not scoring the chicken.
  • Cooking over very high heat — burns the garlic coating before the inside is done.

The Science

Lemon juice in the marinade begins denaturing surface proteins and carries the fat-soluble aromatics from the garlic and herbs into the chicken flesh. Oil in the marinade acts as a vehicle for the fat-soluble flavour compounds, distributing them evenly.

Health Benefits

  • High in lean protein.
  • Garlic provides allicin — a natural antimicrobial compound.
  • Herbs add antioxidants and flavour without extra calories.

Cultural Note

Garlic herb chicken reflects the evolution of Kenyan home cooking — drawing on classic Kenyan ingredients (garlic, coriander, lemon) while embracing fresh herb preparations that have become popular in urban Kenyan kitchens.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat in oven at 180°C for 10 minutes.

Serve with: Roasted Sweet Potatoes, Kachumbari, Ugali, Steamed Rice, Grilled Vegetables
