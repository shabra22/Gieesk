🍌 Tanzanian Grilled Banana

Ndizi ya Kuchoma

Tanzanian Ndizi ya Kuchoma — small Tanzanian finger bananas grilled directly over charcoal until the skin blackens and the flesh inside caramelises. The simplest highland street snack — sweet, smoky, and completely natural.

Grilled finger bananas are the snack of Tanzania's highland regions where bananas grow abundantly. Smaller and sweeter than plantains, they need only minutes over charcoal to become magnificently caramelised.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill or jiko

  • 8 small ripe finger bananas or standard bananas

Method

  1. Place whole unpeeled ripe bananas directly on charcoal.
  2. Grill 5—6 minutes until the skin is completely blackened.
  3. Turn. Grill 4—5 minutes more.
  4. Skin should be fully blackened; the banana inside soft and caramelised.
  5. Slit and eat directly from the skin.

Chef Tips

  • Ripe yellow bananas only — green ones remain starchy.
  • The blacker the skin, the better the caramelisation inside.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Green bananas.
  • Not blackening the skin fully.

The Science

See TZA181 for grilled plantain cooking science — the same chemistry applies to bananas, with the added factor that dessert bananas contain more sucrose and fructose than starchy plantains, producing even more rapid and dramatic caramelisation.

Cultural Note

Grilled finger bananas are the highland market snack — sold at the Kilimanjaro foothills markets, the Arusha vegetable stalls, and every highland bus stop. They are the simplest possible street food and among the most satisfying.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a standalone snack
