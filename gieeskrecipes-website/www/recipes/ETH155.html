🫙 Ethiopian Berbere Oil

በርበሬ ዘይት — Berbere Zeyt

Ethiopian Berbere Oil — vegetable oil infused with berbere, garlic, and onion over low heat. The vegan alternative to kibbeh — used during fasting periods to provide the fat-soluble colour and flavour of berbere without dairy.

Berbere oil is the fasting cook's essential tool — it provides the same function as kibbeh (fat that dissolves the berbere's fat-soluble carotenoids and distributes their colour throughout the wat) without any animal products.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small saucepan, Strainer, Clean jar

  • 1 cup neutral vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 2 garlic cloves — sliced
  • 1 small onion — roughly sliced

Method

  1. Combine oil, berbere, garlic, and onion in a small saucepan.
  2. Heat over the lowest possible flame for 12—15 minutes.
  3. The oil should never fry — just gently warm and infuse.
  4. Cool. Strain through a fine sieve.
  5. The resulting red oil is used as the fat base for all fasting wats.

Chef Tips

  • The lowest possible heat — this is infusing, not frying.
  • Strain completely — leave no solids in the oil.
  • The red colour is the quality indicator — deeper red = better berbere.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat — fries the berbere instead of infusing.
  • Not straining.

The Science

Berbere's carotenoids (fat-soluble pigments responsible for the red colour) dissolve readily into oil at gentle temperatures. Unlike water, which cannot dissolve these pigments, oil extracts them completely — producing the characteristic red oil that colours and flavours the entire fasting wat.

Health Benefits

  • Carotenoids from berbere extracted into a bioavailable fat form.
  • Vitamin E from vegetable oil.

Cultural Note

Berbere oil is the invisible hero of Ethiopian fasting cuisine — the vegan fat that makes Misir Wat red, that makes Shiro Wat fragrant, and that makes the entire fasting platter as visually vibrant as its meat-day equivalent.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Sealed jar at room temperature — 3 months. Refrigerate for longer.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As the fat base for all fasting wats, As a condiment drizzled over fasting dishes
