🍲 Kefta Tagine (Meatballs in Saffron-Cilantro Sauce)

Tajine kefta (طاجين الكفتة)

Olive-size lamb or beef meatballs, spiced with paprika, cumin, cinnamon and nutmeg, poached in a saffron, butter and cilantro sauce finished with lemon juice.

1h 35mTotal time
25mPrep
40mCook
5Servings
360Calories
MediumDifficulty

Ground lamb or beef, crème fraîche or suet, spices and herbs are blended to a paste, chilled at least 30 minutes and rolled into 24 olive-size balls. A sauce of grated red onion, butter, saffron water, spices, cilantro and hot water is simmered about 10 minutes, then the meatballs are poached covered for 30 minutes, turned midway, and lemon juice is added at the end.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Wide heavy pan or tagine with lid

    Saffron water

    • 1/2 teaspoon crumbled saffron strands
    • 1 cup hot water (only 1/4 cup used)

    Kefta

    • 1 pound lean ground lamb or beef
    • 3 tablespoons crème fraîche or grated beef suet
    • 2 teaspoons sweet paprika
    • 1 teaspoon ground cumin, preferably Moroccan
    • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
    • 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, preferably Ceylon
    • 1/4 teaspoon grated nutmeg
    • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
    • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
    • 2 tablespoons roughly chopped flat-leaf parsley
    • 2 tablespoons roughly chopped cilantro

    Sauce

    • 1 medium red onion, grated
    • 2 tablespoons (1 oz) unsalted butter
    • 1/4 cup saffron water
    • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
    • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin, preferably Moroccan
    • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
    • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, or to taste
    • 2 to 3 pinches cayenne
    • Pinch of ground turmeric
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
    • 1 cup roughly chopped cilantro
    • 1/2 cup hot water
    • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

    Method

    1. Steep the saffron in the hot water.
    2. Blend the meat, crème fraîche or suet, spices, salt, pepper, parsley and cilantro in a food processor until a paste forms. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
    3. Shape into 24 olive-size balls and set on a plate.
    4. Combine the grated onion, butter, 1/4 cup saffron water, spices, salt, 3/4 cup of the cilantro and the hot water in the pan. Slowly raise the heat, bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low, cover and simmer gently about 10 minutes.
    5. Add the meatballs, cover and poach 30 minutes, turning them midway. Add the lemon juice and the rest of the cilantro to finish.

    Chef Tips

    • The chill and the poach are NO CUE, TIME ONLY (30 minutes each).
    • Testers on the page (single reading) reported 35 to 36 meatballs instead of 24 when rolled smaller, and one found them cooked in under half the time.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Rolling the balls too large for the stated time.
    • Boiling hard so the meatballs break.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

    Reheating: Warm gently in the sauce.

    Serve with: Bread
