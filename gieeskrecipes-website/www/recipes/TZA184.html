🍠 Tanzanian Grilled Sweet Potato

Viazi Vitamu vya Kuchoma

Tanzanian Viazi Vitamu vya Kuchoma — whole sweet potatoes roasted over charcoal until the skin is charred and the flesh inside is sweet, caramelised, and meltingly soft. The nutritious street snack that children and adults buy at every roadside market.

Roasted sweet potato is one of Tanzania's most nutritious street snacks — the natural sweetness of the Tanzanian sweet potato caramelises beautifully over charcoal, and the high beta-carotene content makes it one of the most vitamin-rich street foods available.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill or jiko

  • 4 medium sweet potatoes — unpeeled, washed
  • Salt and chilli — optional

Method

  1. Place whole sweet potatoes directly on charcoal coals or grill rack.
  2. Roast 30—35 minutes, turning every 8—10 minutes.
  3. The skin will blacken and char; the interior should feel soft when pressed.
  4. Test with a skewer — no resistance means done.
  5. Allow to cool slightly. Slit and eat from the skin.

Chef Tips

  • Medium coals — patience produces a more evenly cooked interior.
  • The skin should be completely blackened — the inside will be perfect.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat — exterior burns before inside cooks.

The Science

Sweet potato contains beta-carotene in fat-soluble form — the natural oils in the sweet potato mobilise during roasting, making the beta-carotene more bioavailable than in raw sweet potato. The charring creates pyrazines and caramelisation products that add the characteristic roasted-sweet flavour.

Health Benefits

  • Extremely high in beta-carotene (vitamin A).
  • Good potassium and vitamin C.
  • Natural sweetness — no added sugar needed.

Cultural Note

Roasted sweet potato is the snack of Tanzania's roadside — the woman at the charcoal brazier who has been in the same spot for decades, her sweet potatoes arranged in rows, each one at a different stage of roasting. It is one of Tanzania's most nourishing and most honest street foods.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly on grill.

Serve with: As a standalone, With salt and chilli, With roasted corn (TZA173)
