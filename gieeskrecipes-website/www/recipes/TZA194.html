🥜 Tanzanian Spiced Mixed Nuts

Njugu Mchanganyiko

Tanzanian Njugu Mchanganyiko — a mix of groundnuts, cashews, and sunflower seeds dry-roasted with Tanzanian spices including curry powder, cumin, and chilli. The upmarket street snack sold at urban markets and bus stations across Tanzania.

Spiced mixed nuts are the urban Tanzanian upgrade from plain roasted groundnuts — the addition of cashews and the spice coating makes them more complex and more satisfying. Sold in small packets at petrol stations, offices, and upmarket markets.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide dry pan

  • 1 cup raw groundnuts
  • ½ cup raw cashews
  • ½ cup sunflower seeds
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Heat pan over medium heat.
  2. Add all nuts and seeds. Stir constantly for 8—10 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Add oil. Toss to coat.
  4. Add all spices immediately. Stir vigorously to coat every nut.
  5. Cook 2—3 more minutes until spices are fragrant and coating is set.
  6. Spread on a tray. Cool completely before eating.

Chef Tips

  • Add oil and spices at the same time — the oil helps spices adhere.
  • Cool completely — the spice coating sets as they cool.
  • Work quickly when adding spices — they bloom in seconds.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not cooling properly — spices slide off when warm.
  • Adding spices to a cold pan — they do not bloom.

The Science

See TZA177 for nut roasting science. The oil added with the spices acts as a binding medium — spice compounds are fat-soluble and adhere to the oil-coated nut surface much more effectively than to a dry surface. As the oil cools and partially solidifies, it cements the spices to the nuts.

Cultural Note

Spiced mixed nuts are the snack of Tanzania's urban professional class — the packet on the office desk, the car snack on a long drive, the accompaniment to the cold drink on a hot afternoon.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container 2 weeks.

Reheating: Brief warm in dry pan.

Serve with: Cold drinks, As a standalone snack
