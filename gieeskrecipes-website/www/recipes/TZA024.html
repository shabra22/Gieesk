🐐 Tanzanian Goat Stew

Mchuzi wa Mbuzi

Mchuzi wa Mbuzi — Tanzanian goat stew. Young goat simmered slowly in a rich tomato-onion sauce fragrant with cumin, coriander, and garlic until the meat is falling off the bone. The stew served at every Tanzanian celebration and every important family gathering.

Goat is the most celebratory meat in Tanzania — when a goat is slaughtered, it signals a significant occasion. Mchuzi wa Mbuzi is the slow-cooked stew made from that goat — patient, aromatic, and deeply satisfying. The collagen from goat bones creates a silky sauce that coats ugali perfectly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot with lid

  • 1 kg young goat — bone-in pieces
  • 2 large onions — diced
  • 4 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander

Method

  1. Brown goat pieces in hot oil. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until deeply golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and spices. Stir 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook until completely reduced — 10 minutes.
  5. Return goat. Add water.
  6. Simmer covered on very low heat for 75—90 minutes until meat falls from bone.
  7. Adjust seasoning. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Young goat (mbuzi mdogo) is most tender.
  • Very low heat for the simmer — goat toughens if boiled hard.
  • The longer the simmer, the better.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hard boiling.
  • Young goat is essential — old goat requires 3+ hours.

The Science

Goat contains more collagen than beef at the same cut — particularly around the joints and bone. Extended slow cooking converts this to gelatin, producing an exceptionally rich, silky sauce.

Health Benefits

  • Goat is leaner than beef or lamb.
  • Very high complete protein.
  • Rich in iron and zinc.

Cultural Note

Goat stew is Tanzania's most celebratory everyday meat dish — eaten at naming ceremonies, circumcisions, homecomings, and harvests. The slaughter of a goat for a guest is among the highest expressions of Tanzanian hospitality.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
