🎃 Kenyan Pumpkin Soup

Supu ya Maboga

Kenyan Pumpkin Soup — roasted orange-fleshed pumpkin blended with ginger, garlic, coconut milk, and warm spices into a vividly orange, silky, fragrant soup. The soup that showcases the colour and sweetness of Kenya's finest pumpkins.

Pumpkin soup made from Kenya's orange-fleshed pumpkin varieties is a vivid, naturally sweet, deeply satisfying first course. The addition of coconut milk gives it a coastal character, and the ginger and spices give it warmth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking tray, Large pot, Blender

  • 800g orange-fleshed pumpkin — peeled, seeded, cubed
  • 2 medium onions — roughly chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger — sliced
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil or vegetable oil
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Toasted pumpkin seeds and cream — to garnish

Method

  1. Toss pumpkin, onion, and garlic with oil. Roast at 200°C for 25 minutes until soft and caramelised.
  2. Transfer to a pot with ginger, stock, coconut milk, and spices.
  3. Simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Blend until completely smooth.
  5. Season with salt and sugar.
  6. Serve with a swirl of coconut cream and toasted pumpkin seeds.

Chef Tips

  • Roasting the pumpkin is the key step — boiled pumpkin soup is flat; roasted is extraordinary.
  • Blend while warm for the smoothest result.
  • Toasted pumpkin seeds (KEN156) make the perfect garnish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling instead of roasting the pumpkin.
  • Under-seasoning — pumpkin needs generous salt and spices.

The Science

Roasting caramelises pumpkin's natural sugars, converting them from simple sweetness to complex caramel notes. The coconut milk emulsifies with the pumpkin puree during blending, producing a naturally silky texture without additional cream.

Cultural Note

Pumpkin soup in a Kenyan hotel demonstrates the culinary potential of crops that Kenyan farming communities have grown for generations — the same pumpkin whose leaves become mboga and whose seeds become snacks is also the base of an elegant restaurant soup.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Garlic Bread (KEN279), As a starter course, With a salad
