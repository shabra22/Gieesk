🫓 Tanzanian Fried Cassava Cakes

Mikate ya Muhogo

Tanzanian Mikate ya Muhogo — grated cassava mixed with coconut milk, sugar, and cardamom, formed into small cakes and pan-fried until golden. A naturally gluten-free cassava breakfast cake sold at coastal morning markets.

Cassava cakes are the gluten-free breakfast of the Tanzanian coast — grated fresh cassava combined with coconut milk and a little sugar produces small, golden, naturally sweet cakes that are completely satisfying in the morning.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grater, Wide pan

  • 400g fresh cassava — peeled and grated
  • 100ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp desiccated coconut — optional
  • Oil for pan-frying

Method

  1. Grate cassava finely. Squeeze out as much moisture as possible through a cloth.
  2. Combine grated cassava, coconut milk, sugar, cardamom, salt. Mix well.
  3. The mixture should hold together when pressed — add more coconut milk if too dry.
  4. Form into small patties 1cm thick.
  5. Pan-fry in oil over medium heat 4—5 minutes per side until golden.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Squeezing out moisture is essential — wet cassava produces a heavy, dense cake.
  • Press patties firmly — they must hold their shape.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not squeezing cassava.
  • Patties that are too thick — raw inside.

The Science

Cassava contains linamarin which must be broken down — finely grating ruptures the cell walls, releasing linamarase enzyme which catalyses linamarin breakdown. The subsequent cooking completes the process. The starch in cassava gelatinises during pan-frying, binding the patty into a cohesive structure.

Cultural Note

Cassava cakes are the coastal morning food of the practical Tanzanian cook — made from what grows locally, without any imported ingredients, producing a completely satisfying breakfast from the ground up.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh. Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Pan-fry briefly.

Serve with: Chai, As a standalone, With honey
