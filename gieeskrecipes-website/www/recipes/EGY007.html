🥣 Weeka (Upper Egyptian Okra Soup)

Weeka — ويكة

A thin, silky soup of finely sliced okra simmered in broth with garlic and coriander, finished with a garlic-coriander tasha.

30mTotal time
10mPrep
20mCook
4Servings
150Calories
EasyDifficulty

Okra is sliced thin and simmered in vegetable broth flavoured with garlic, coriander and lemon until fully cooked, 7 to 10 minutes on low heat. A tasha of crushed garlic and ground coriander fried until the garlic is golden is stirred in, the heat is turned off, and the soup is left to infuse. Served with vermicelli rice or baladi bread.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Small frying pan

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, or ghee/butter
  • 1 crushed garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 kg big okra, tops and ends removed and thinly sliced
  • 4 cups vegetable broth, or 2 vegetable bouillon diluted in 4 cups of water
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice, or more to taste
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Tasha

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, ghee or butter
  • 2 teaspoons crushed garlic, crushed
  • 2 tablespoon ground coriander

To serve

  • Vermicelli rice
  • Aish Baladi
  • Lemon wedges, optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil, then cook the garlic until fragrant. Add the coriander for 30 seconds, then the broth, and bring to a simmer over low heat for about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the okra and lemon juice and bring to a boil, then simmer on low for 7 to 10 minutes, until the okra is fully cooked.
  3. Tasha: fry the garlic and coriander in the fat until the garlic turns golden, and stir it into the soup.
  4. Turn the heat off and let the soup infuse; the page prints no infusion time.
  5. Serve with vermicelli rice, baladi bread and lemon wedges.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: okra fully cooked; tasha garlic golden. The page's wording on the first simmer gives two heat settings in one sentence (low and medium-low); we use low.
  • The page misprints 'boil' as 'oil' in the okra step.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the garlic in the tasha, which turns the soup bitter (our caution).

Health Benefits

  • Okra provides fibre and vitamin C; the soup is vegan.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour calls it an ancient vegan okra soup from Upper Egypt, a Coptic fasting meal, and recalls her late father loving it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently on the stove.

Serve with: Rice with vermicelli, Aish baladi
