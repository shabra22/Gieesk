🥩 Kenyan Beef Lasagna

Lasagna ya Nyama ya Kenya

Kenyan Beef Lasagna — layers of fresh pasta sheets, richly spiced Kenyan beef bolognese, and creamy béchamel, baked until bubbling and golden. A restaurant staple that has become a home celebration dish — the family meal that feeds a crowd beautifully.

Lasagna has become one of Kenya's most beloved baked pasta dishes — served at hotel buffets, family celebrations, and restaurant menus across the country. The Kenyan version uses aromatic spices in the bolognese and adds paprika to the béchamel for character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan for bolognese, Saucepan for béchamel, 30x20cm baking dish, Oven (180°C)

  • 12 lasagna sheets (pre-cooked type)

Kenyan Beef Bolognese

  • 600g minced beef
  • 2 large onions — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • 2 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Béchamel

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Pinch of pepper

Topping

  • 200g mozzarella — grated
  • 50g parmesan — grated

Method

  1. Make bolognese: cook onion and garlic in oil. Add beef — brown thoroughly. Add tomato paste, tomatoes, spices, and salt. Simmer 25 minutes until thick.
  2. Make béchamel: melt butter. Add flour — cook 1 minute. Whisk in milk gradually until smooth. Simmer until thick. Season with paprika, nutmeg, and salt.
  3. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  4. Layer in baking dish: béchamel, lasagna sheets, bolognese, béchamel, sheets, bolognese — repeat.
  5. Final layer: béchamel, mozzarella, parmesan.
  6. Bake covered with foil for 30 minutes. Uncover. Bake 15 more until golden and bubbling.
  7. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.

Chef Tips

  • A thick bolognese is essential — wet meat sauce makes the lasagna watery.
  • The béchamel must be well seasoned — it provides the background flavour of every bite.
  • Rest before cutting — this allows the lasagna to set and cuts cleanly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet bolognese.
  • Under-seasoned béchamel.
  • Cutting without resting — collapses.

The Science

The béchamel (made from a roux) contains a starch gel that absorbs excess moisture from the bolognese during baking, preventing the common soggy lasagna problem. The foil for the first 30 minutes creates a steaming environment that cooks the pasta through; removing the foil for the final 15 minutes dries the surface and creates the golden crust.

Cultural Note

Beef lasagna has become a quintessential family celebration dish in urban Kenya — the dish that mothers spend an afternoon making for a family return or graduation dinner.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C covered for 20 minutes.
