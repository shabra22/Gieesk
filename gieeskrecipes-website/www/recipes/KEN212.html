🥥 Kenyan Coconut Celebration Rice

Wali wa Nazi wa Sherehe

Kenyan Coconut Celebration Rice — wali wa Nazi prepared with extra richness for celebrations. More coconut milk, toasted coconut garnish, and fried onions elevate the everyday coastal rice into the fragrant, glistening centrepiece of the celebration table.

Celebration wali wa Nazi is distinguished from everyday coconut rice by its generosity — more coconut milk for a richer result, a toasted coconut garnish, and fried shallots on top. At coastal weddings, Eid celebrations, and birthday feasts, the wali wa Nazi arrives as a fragrant, glistening mountain of rice that signals abundance and joy.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot with tight lid, Small pan for garnish

  • 4 cups basmati rice — washed and soaked 30 minutes
  • 800ml thick coconut milk
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp butter or coconut oil

Celebration garnish

  • ½ cup desiccated coconut — dry-toasted until golden
  • 2 shallots — thinly sliced and fried until crispy
  • ½ tsp rose water — optional

Method

  1. Drain soaked rice.
  2. Combine coconut milk, water, salt, and butter in a large pot. Bring to a boil.
  3. Add rice. Stir once. Return to a boil.
  4. Reduce to the lowest possible heat. Cover tightly.
  5. Cook for 18—20 minutes without lifting the lid.
  6. Remove from heat. Rest covered for 10 minutes.
  7. Fluff gently. Transfer to serving platter.
  8. Top with toasted coconut, crispy shallots, and a dash of rose water if using.

Chef Tips

  • More coconut milk than the everyday version — this is the celebration ratio.
  • The crispy fried shallot garnish is the celebration signature.
  • Never lift the lid during cooking.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid during cooking.
  • Skipping the rest period.

The Science

See KEN021 for the full cooking science. The higher coconut milk ratio creates a more emulsified cooking liquid — the fat coats more of the rice grains, producing a silkier, richer result than the everyday proportion.

Cultural Note

Celebration wali wa Nazi is the rice dish that tells guests they are valued — the extra coconut milk, the toasted garnish, the fragrant rose water are all signals of hospitality and care that the cook is communicating through food.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Steam briefly or microwave with a splash of water.

Serve with: Easter Coconut Fish, Eid Biryani, Grilled Chicken, Coconut Beef Curry
