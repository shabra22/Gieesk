🍲 Bamya (Okra and Lamb Stew)

Bamya — بامية

Lamb boiled with mastic and aromatics, then added to a tomato and baby okra stew and topped with a garlic-coriander taklia at the table.

1h 15mTotal time
15mPrep
1hCook
6Servings
360Calories
MediumDifficulty

Lamb is boiled rapidly for 3 to 4 minutes and the water discarded, then simmered in fresh water with mastic and aromatics for about 20 minutes. The stew starts with onion, garlic and coriander, then jalapeño, frozen baby okra, tomato paste, tomato sauce and broth, and simmers 20 minutes on low before the lamb goes in for another 10 minutes or until the okra is tender. A taklia of garlic and coriander is stirred half into the stew and spooned half over each bowl.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Saucepan, Small frying pan

    Boiled lamb

    • 2 mastic pieces.
    • 1 pound lamb. Note1
    • 1 small onion.
    • 2 garlic clove.
    • 1 small carrot cut into chunks.
    • 1 celery stalk cut into chunks.
    • ¼ cup cilantro chopped.
    • 1 teaspoon 7-spice blend.
    • 2 teaspoon black pepper.
    • ¼ teaspoon cinnamon.
    • ¼ teaspoon ground cardamom.
    • 2 bay leaves.

    Okra stew

    • 3 Tablespoons olive oil.
    • 1 medium (200g) onion, diced.
    • 5 garlic cloves minced (1.5 Tablespoons).
    • ½ Tablespoon freshly ground coriander.
    • 2 jalapeño pepper. leave out or replace by bell pepper if you do not want the heat.
    • 2 Tablespoon fresh cilantro chopped.
    • 1 bag (14oz) frozen baby okra. Note2
    • 4 Tablespoons tomato paste.
    • 1 can (15oz) tomato sauce.
    • 2 cups broth. beef or lamb.
    • 1 teaspoon salt or to taste.
    • ½ teaspoon black pepper.

    Tasha (garlic-coriander topping)

    • 3 Tablespoons olive oil.
    • 5 Garlic cloves minced.
    • 1 Tablespoon freshly ground coriander.
    • ½ cup fresh cilantro chopped.
    • 1 tablespoon red chili pepper diced. Optional for extra heat

    Method

    1. Bring about 4 litres of water to a boil, add the lamb and boil rapidly for 3 to 4 minutes. Discard the water.
    2. Return the lamb to fresh water with the mastic, onion, garlic, carrot, celery, cilantro and spices and simmer until done; the page says it will take about 20 minutes.
    3. Stew: cook the onion, garlic and coriander 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant; add the cilantro for almost another 30 seconds and the jalapeño for a minute.
    4. Add the okra, tomato paste, tomato sauce and broth and simmer on low for 20 minutes.
    5. Add the lamb and simmer another 10 minutes, or until the okra is tender.
    6. Tasha: cook the garlic, coriander, cilantro and chili in olive oil for two to three minutes. Stir half into the stew and simmer 3 to 4 minutes; top each bowl with the rest.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: coriander and garlic fragrant; okra tender. Note 1: beef can replace lamb, cooked as in the fattah recipe. Note 2: frozen or fresh okra works but baby okra is best.
    • The page lists the mastic and jalapeño as shown; omit or swap the jalapeño for bell pepper if you want no heat.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Overcooking the okra, which turns it slimy (our caution).

    Cultural Note

    Amira Ibrahim calls bamya one of Egypt's signature dishes and believes this variant is from Upper Egypt, though she says she is not sure.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

    Reheating: Warm gently on the stove.

    Serve with: White rice, Aish baladi
