🥥 Coconut Rice

Coconut rice — three ways

Published at three scales with three appliances — one cup in a microwave, three in a rice cooker, sixteen in an oven for sixty people.

The same cook gives coconut rice three ways, and the scaling is the real information. One cup of rice in the microwave at fifty per cent power for fifteen to twenty minutes with the vent holes open, then five minutes' rest. Three cups in a rice cooker with a cup and a half of coconut milk and three and a half to four cups of water. Or sixteen cups of washed rice with two tins of coconut milk and about thirty cups of water, baked at 375 degrees for forty-five minutes to an hour across two covered pans — enough for about sixty people at a quarter cup of rice each.

Ingredients

Equipment: Microwave with a vented lid, rice cooker, or oven with two covered pans, Measuring cups, Large pot, for boiling the oven batch's water

    Microwave — 1 cup rice, serves about 4

    • 1 cup white rice
    • 1 tbsp coconut cream mixed with enough coconut milk to replace 1/2 cup of the water
    • 1-1 1/2 cups water

    Rice cooker — 3 cups rice

    • 3 cups rice
    • 1 1/2 cups coconut milk
    • A few tablespoons coconut cream (optional)
    • 3 1/2-4 cups water
    • 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) turmeric (optional)

    Oven — 16 cups rice, about 60 people

    • 16 cups rice, already washed
    • 2 tins coconut milk with cream
    • About 30 cups water
    • 1 1/2 tbsp salt — 1/4 tsp for each cup of rice

    Common

    • Salt, to taste

    Method

      Microwave, 1 cup

      1. Mix 1 tablespoon of the coconut cream with enough coconut milk to replace 1/2 cup of the water, and combine with the rice and the remaining water.
      2. Cook covered at 50 per cent (medium) for about 15 to 20 minutes, with the vent holes in the lid open.
      3. Rest another 5 minutes or so.

      Rice cooker, 3 cups

      1. Combine 3 cups rice, 1 1/2 cups coconut milk, 3 1/2 to 4 cups water, the optional cream and the optional turmeric.
      2. Cook on the standard setting.

      Oven, 16 cups

      1. Allow 1/4 teaspoon of salt for each cup of rice, about 1 1/2 tablespoons in all.
      2. Combine the washed rice, the 2 tins of coconut milk and about 30 cups of boiling water across two covered pans.
      3. Bake at 375 degrees for 45 minutes to an hour.
      4. After about 30 minutes open the oven and carefully tilt and lift the lids.
      5. Fluff and serve.

      Chef Tips

      • The three scales are the real lesson: a microwave for one cup, a rice cooker for three, an oven for sixteen. Coconut milk scorches on a hob, which is why the big batch goes in the oven.
      • Fifty per cent power in the microwave, not full. Coconut milk boils over at full power and the vent holes stay open for the same reason.
      • The oven batch is quarter-cup-per-person party catering — sixteen cups of rice feeds about sixty. That is a genuinely useful figure and almost nobody publishes it.
      • Lift the lids at the thirty-minute mark on the oven batch. Tilt them carefully; the trapped steam is the dangerous part.
      • A quarter teaspoon of salt per cup of rice is the scaling rule, and it works at every size.
      • Turmeric is optional and is for colour. One printing gives half a teaspoon and another half to one teaspoon, so start low.
      • Coconut cream plus coconut milk, not just milk — the cream is what makes it rich. Her companion recipe covers extracting both from a fresh coconut.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Full power in the microwave, which boils it over.
      • Closing the vent holes.
      • Cooking sixteen cups on a hob, where the milk catches.
      • Dropping the lids flat and getting a steam burn at the thirty-minute check.
      • Salting by taste at party scale rather than by the per-cup rule.

      Health Benefits

      • Coconut provides medium-chain fats that are metabolised differently from long-chain.
      • Vegan and gluten-free.
      • Lower in sodium than most savoury rice dishes.
      • Coconut milk adds manganese and copper.

      Cultural Note

      Coconut palms line the Ghanaian coast and coconut rice is the natural result — sweet, rich, and served beside something hot. The jump from a microwave cup to sixteen cups in an oven is also a quiet record of how Ghanaian cooking scales for a party, which is most of the time.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: 3 days refrigerated. Coconut rice firms up considerably.

      Reheating: Steam, or microwave with a splash of water.

      Serve with: Grilled Tilapia, Shito, Fresh Pepper Sauce
