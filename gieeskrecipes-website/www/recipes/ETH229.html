☕ Ethiopian Coffee with Butter

ቅቤ ቡና — Qibe Buna

Ethiopian Coffee with Butter — strong Ethiopian coffee brewed in the jebena and served with a small piece of fresh butter melted in. The highland farming tradition — butter coffee predates the global 'bulletproof coffee' trend by centuries in Ethiopia's Oromo communities.

Coffee with butter is the highland farmer's breakfast coffee — the fat from the butter sustains energy through the morning's physical work. The Oromo communities have been adding butter to coffee for generations, long before the concept became global.

Ingredients

Equipment: Jebena, Cups

  • 2 cups strong Ethiopian jebena coffee
  • 1 tsp fresh unsalted butter per cup
  • Salt to taste (Harrar and some Oromo communities add salt not sugar)

Method

  1. Brew strong Ethiopian coffee as per ETH212.
  2. Pour into cups.
  3. Add a small piece of fresh butter to each cup.
  4. Add salt if following the traditional salted version.
  5. The butter melts as you drink — stir or swirl.

Chef Tips

  • Fresh, high-quality butter is essential — the fat is the point.
  • Traditional versions use salt, not sugar.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Salted butter — too salty.
  • Blending — traditional coffee with butter is not blended.

The Science

The fat in butter dissolves the fat-soluble aromatic compounds in coffee (particularly the cafestol and kahweol diterpenes) — enhancing the coffee's body and producing a smoother, less bitter experience. The fat also slows caffeine absorption, producing more sustained energy release.

Health Benefits

  • Coffee antioxidants.
  • Butter fat supports sustained energy release.
  • The fat-soluble coffee compounds (diterpenes) have documented anti-cancer properties.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian coffee with butter predates the global butter coffee trend by centuries — the Oromo highland farmer who has been starting their day with butter in their jebena coffee since long before the internet had no idea they were ahead of a global trend. This is traditional food knowledge from the world's oldest coffee culture.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: At breakfast, Before highland farming work
