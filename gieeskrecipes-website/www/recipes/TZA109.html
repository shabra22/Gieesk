🍌 Tanzanian Banana Flower Curry

Mchuzi wa Ganda la Ndizi

Tanzanian Banana Flower Curry — the inner petals of the banana flower (inflorescence) cooked in a spiced tomato sauce. A traditional dish of Tanzania's banana-growing highland regions — zero-waste cooking at its most creative.

Banana flower curry is one of Tanzania's most creative zero-waste dishes — the large purple flower at the end of a banana bunch, which falls off as the bananas mature, is harvested and cooked as a vegetable. Its tender inner petals have a mild, artichoke-like flavour.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl with acidulated water, Large pot

  • 1 fresh banana flower — outer purple petals removed, inner pale yellow flower prepared
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Prepare banana flower: remove outer purple petals until you reach the pale inner florets. Chop finely. Immediately submerge in water with lemon juice — prevents browning.
  2. Blanch chopped flower in boiling salted water 5 minutes. Drain.
  3. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  5. Add blanched banana flower. Stir to combine.
  6. Add coconut milk. Simmer 15—18 minutes until tender.
  7. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Work quickly and submerge in acidulated water immediately — banana flower browns on contact with air.
  • Blanching removes initial bitterness.
  • The inner pale florets are the vegetable — the outer purple leaves are discarded.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not using acidulated water — immediate browning.
  • Using outer purple petals — bitter and tough.

The Science

Banana flower contains polyphenol oxidase — the same enzyme that causes apple and banana browning when cut. Lemon juice's citric acid inhibits this enzyme through pH reduction, preventing browning.

Cultural Note

Banana flower curry represents the Tanzanian highland kitchen at its most ingenious — finding a complete meal in what most people walk past on the banana plant.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Rice, Beans
