🫕 Ethiopian Teff Porridge Sweet

ጤፍ ጣፋጭ — Teff Tafach

Ethiopian Sweet Teff Porridge — teff flour cooked into a smooth, slightly thick porridge, sweetened with honey and enriched with a touch of kibbeh. The sweetened version of the morning porridge becomes Ethiopia's most nutritious dessert — served warm in small bowls.

Sweet teff porridge occupies the space between breakfast and dessert in Ethiopian food culture — teff's natural earthiness, sweetened with honey and enriched with kibbeh, produces a warm dessert of remarkable nutritional density.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 1 cup teff flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tbsp Ethiopian honey
  • 1 tbsp kibbeh
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Bring water to a simmer.
  2. Whisk teff flour into simmering water gradually.
  3. Cook over medium heat, stirring continuously, 15—18 minutes until thick.
  4. Remove from heat.
  5. Stir in honey, kibbeh, and cardamom.
  6. Serve warm in small bowls.
  7. Drizzle extra honey if desired.

Chef Tips

  • Teff porridge thickens quickly — work fast.
  • The kibbeh adds richness that transforms it from breakfast to dessert.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not stirring — teff sticks badly.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Sweet teff porridge is the dessert that Ethiopian mothers serve to children and elderly — warm, gentle, deeply nourishing, and completely satisfying. It is the taste of care in Ethiopian food.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten warm and immediately.

Reheating: Gently with added water.

Serve with: As a warm dessert, As a nutritious children's sweet, With extra honey drizzle
