🍌 Kondres (Spicy Stewed Plantains)

Kondre / kondres

Green plantains stewed for an hour or more in a blended tomato, herb, njansa and pebe sauce with beef, oxtail or goat, until the plantains are very tender.

140 minTotal time
20 minPrep
120 minCook
6Servings
620Calories
MediumDifficulty

Adamu writes that kondres originated in western Cameroon among the Bamileke. Meat is boiled with salt and onion in plenty of water until tender (a pressure cooker cuts the time in half) and the stock reserved. Green plantains are peeled, cut into 2–3 pieces each and kept in cool water. Tomatoes, onions, green onions, njansa, peeled pebe, garlic, basil, parsley and celery are blended with a little water if needed. Oil is heated, the blend added with white pepper, curry, the meat and stock, boiled, reduced and simmered about 10 minutes; the plantains and bouillon go in with enough stock or water to cover, simmering an hour or more until very tender.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender

  • 2–3 pounds (1–1.5 kg) stew meat, cut up (beef, oxtail, goat, chicken, pork, etc.)
  • Salt (3/4–1 teaspoon per pound of meat)
  • 1 large onion, coarsely chopped (white or yellow)
  • 3–4 pounds (1.5–2 kg) green plantains
  • 10 njansa
  • 3 large tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup (15 g) parsley
  • 1/2 cup (24 g) basil
  • 1/4 cup (15 g) celery leaves
  • 2 teaspoons (10 g) grated ginger
  • 8 cloves garlic
  • 2 green onions
  • 1 teaspoon (2–3 g) white pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon (1–2 g) curry powder
  • 1 tablespoon (6 g) thyme (listed; no step uses it)
  • 2 pebe (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon (1 g) country onion seeds (optional)
  • 2–4 tablespoons (30–60 ml) vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon (10 g) bouillon powder or 2 Maggi cubes
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Boil the meat with salt and the onion in plenty of water until tender. A pressure cooker can halve the time. Reserve the stock.
  2. Peel the plantains, cut each into 2–3 pieces and cover with cool water.
  3. Blend the tomatoes, onion, green onions, njansa, peeled pebe, garlic, basil, parsley and celery, adding a little water if needed.
  4. Heat the oil. Add the blend, the white pepper, the curry, the meat and the stock. Boil, then reduce and simmer about 10 minutes.
  5. Add the plantains, the bouillon or Maggi and enough stock or water to cover. Simmer an hour or more, until the plantains are very tender.

Chef Tips

  • The printed doneness cue is plantains ‘super tender’.
  • Keep the cut plantains in water until they go in.
  • The page lists thyme but never says when to add it; add with the herbs if you use it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking the plantains in too little liquid.
  • Pulling the pot early — the page says an hour or more.
  • Forgetting to peel the pebe before blending.

Cultural Note

Adamu: ‘Kondres originated in the western part of Cameroon, specifically among the Bamelieke tribe’ (her spelling). Her roundup calls it a popular one-pot meal.
