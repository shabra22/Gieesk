🫘 Koki Beans (Steamed Black-Eyed Pea Pudding)

Koki

Skinless black-eyed peas pureed with palm oil, onion, pepper, water and bouillon, folded with spinach, wrapped in banana leaves and foil and steamed about 1 hour 30 minutes.

240 minTotal time
30 minPrep
90 minCook
6Servings
430Calories
MediumDifficulty

Adamu, who writes that she grew up in Cameroon, calls it koki beans, ekoki, haricots koki and bean cakes. The skinless black-eyed peas are soaked about 2 hours (or up to 24) until fully hydrated. Palm oil is warmed a minute or two until completely melted. The peas, onion, pepper, water and bouillon are pulsed to a puree that is ‘just a little gritty’, the spinach folded in, about 2 cups placed on each leaf, wrapped in foil, and steamed about an hour and a half, then cooled about 10 minutes before serving.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or food processor, Steamer pot

  • 2 cups (280–300 g) skinless black-eyed peas
  • 1/2 medium onion
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet or habanero pepper (optional)
  • 1 cup (236 ml) palm oil
  • 3 1/2 cups (826 ml) water
  • 3 cups (90–100 g) chopped fresh spinach
  • 2 Maggi cubes or 2 teaspoons (7 g) bouillon powder
  • 3 teaspoons (15–20 g) salt, or more to taste
  • Banana leaves and foil — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Soak the black-eyed peas about 2 hours, or up to 24 hours, until fully hydrated.
  2. Warm the palm oil about a minute or two until completely melted.
  3. Pulse the peas with the onion, pepper, water, bouillon and salt to a puree that is just a little gritty, using up all the water.
  4. Fold in the spinach.
  5. Put about 2 cups of the mixture on each banana leaf, wrap in foil and steam about 1 hour 30 minutes, adding water as needed.
  6. Cool about 10 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • The printed texture cue is ‘just a little gritty’ puree.
  • Add water to the steamer as needed during the long steam.
  • No doneness cue is printed beyond the time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-blending to a smooth paste.
  • Letting the steamer boil dry.
  • Skipping the cooling time.
