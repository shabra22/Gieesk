Fiori di Zucca Ripieni e Fritti

Delicate zucchini blossoms stuffed with mozzarella and anchovy, dipped in a light batter, and fried until crisp — a seasonal treat found across central and southern Italy each summer.

Zucchini flowers are famously fragile and short-lived, best used the same day they're picked, and their delicate flavor pairs beautifully with a simple, molten mozzarella filling beneath a light, crisp batter shell.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small bowl for batter, Deep pot for frying

  • 12 fresh zucchini flowers
  • 150 g (5.3 oz) fresh mozzarella, cut into small batons
  • 6 anchovy fillets, halved (optional)
  • 150 g (1⅛ cups) all-purpose flour
  • 200 ml (¾ cup plus 1 tbsp) sparkling water, very cold
  • Pinch of salt
  • Vegetable oil, for frying

Method

  1. Gently clean the zucchini flowers, removing the inner pistil, and check for insects.
  2. Tuck a piece of mozzarella and half an anchovy fillet, if using, into each flower, twisting the tip gently to close.
  3. Whisk the flour, cold sparkling water, and salt together into a light, slightly lumpy batter — do not overmix.
  4. Heat vegetable oil to 180°C (350°F).
  5. Dip each stuffed flower into the batter, coating well, and carefully lower into the hot oil.
  6. Fry in batches for 2–3 minutes until light golden and crisp, turning once.
  7. Drain on paper towels and season lightly with salt.
  8. Serve immediately while hot and crisp.

Chef Tips

  • Very cold sparkling water is key to a light, crisp batter — the cold and carbonation both help minimize gluten development.
  • Don't overmix the batter; a few lumps are perfectly fine and even desirable.
  • Zucchini flowers are extremely delicate and best used the same day they're purchased or picked.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overmixing the batter, which develops too much gluten and results in a heavier, less crisp coating.
  • Overstuffing the flowers, making them difficult to close and prone to leaking filling during frying.
  • Letting the oil temperature drop too low, resulting in greasy rather than crisp results.

Health Benefits

  • A good source of calcium from the mozzarella; best enjoyed as an occasional treat given the frying.

Cultural Note

Fiori di zucca fritti are a beloved summer specialty across central and southern Italy, appearing on trattoria menus whenever fresh zucchini flowers are in season.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately; these do not hold or reheat well.

Reheating: Not recommended; best enjoyed fresh and hot.

Serve with: Lemon wedges, As part of a summer antipasto spread
