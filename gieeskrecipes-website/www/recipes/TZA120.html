🥥 Tanzanian Vegetable Coconut Stew

Mboga za Nazi

Tanzanian Mboga za Nazi — a medley of seasonal vegetables simmered in a rich coconut milk sauce with Zanzibar spices. The coastal complete vegetable dish — where inland Tanzania uses peanuts for richness, the coast uses coconut milk.

Mboga za Nazi is the coastal Tanzanian answer to any question about what to make with mixed vegetables — simmer them in coconut milk with Zanzibar spices and the result will always be magnificent. The coconut milk elevates every vegetable it touches.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 200g sweet potato — cubed
  • 150g green beans — halved
  • 200g eggplant — cubed
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 2 medium carrots — sliced
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and spices. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes until thick.
  4. Add sweet potato and carrot. Stir to coat. Cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add coconut milk. Bring to gentle simmer.
  6. Simmer covered 12 minutes.
  7. Add green beans and eggplant. Simmer 8 minutes.
  8. Add spinach. Stir until wilted — 1 minute.
  9. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Add vegetables in order of cooking time — hardest first.
  • Never boil coconut milk hard.
  • Spinach goes in at the very end — it wilts in seconds.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all vegetables simultaneously — uneven cooking.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

The Science

Each vegetable in this stew reaches optimal doneness at different times. Staggered addition ensures all are perfectly cooked when the dish is served — neither mushy sweet potato nor raw spinach.

Cultural Note

Mboga za Nazi is the Swahili coast's most versatile dish — the vegetables change with the season but the coconut milk-spice base remains constant. It is the dish that proves the Swahili coast philosophy: coconut milk makes everything better.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004)
