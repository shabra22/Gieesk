🥕 Moroccan Carrot Salad

Shlada dyal khizzou (سلطة الجزر)

Carrots boiled until lightly crisp and tender, dressed warm with garlic, coriander, parsley, paprika, cumin and vinegar.

30mTotal time
10mPrep
20mCook
4Servings
110Calories
EasyDifficulty

Benkabbou boils sliced carrots with two unpeeled garlic cloves 10 to 20 minutes depending on size, drains and rinses, then mixes them with a dressing made from the mashed garlic, herbs, spices, white wine vinegar and olive oil. Serve warm or cold. She adds a little pomegranate molasses though it is not traditional.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium pan

  • 500g carrots, peeled and sliced or chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped coriander
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped parlsey (printed "parlsey")
  • 1 teaspoon ground paprika
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons pomegranate molasses (optional)
  • Salt to season

Method

  1. Bring water to the boil, add the carrots and garlic cloves, reduce to medium and gently cook until lightly crisp and tender, about 10 to 20 minutes depending on size.
  2. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain again.
  3. Peel and finely chop the garlic cloves. In a small bowl combine the garlic, herbs, paprika, cumin, vinegar, olive oil, pomegranate molasses and salt; stir until smooth.
  4. Gently combine the carrots with the dressing. Serve warm or cold.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: lightly crisp and tender.
  • The author says pomegranate molasses is not part of the traditional recipe, but she likes it.
  • Cook the garlic with the carrots to mellow it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the carrots to mush.
  • Dressing cold carrots too long ahead.
  • Skipping the rinse.

Cultural Note

The author writes that she has many memories of her mom making this salad.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Serve cold or warm.

Serve with: Khobz
