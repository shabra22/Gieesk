🐟 Fish Tagine with Chermoula and Vegetables

Tajine de poisson à la chermoula

Thick pieces of firm fish marinated in cilantro-garlic chermoula, layered over onion, potato, carrot, tomato and peppers and simmered gently for about an hour.

3h 30mTotal time
30mPrep
1hCook
6Servings
420Calories
EasyDifficulty

A fish tagine is built in layers. Half of the chermoula marinates the fish for at least two hours; the other half seasons the vegetables. Onion goes first on the bottom of the tagine with olive oil, then the vegetables, then the fish, with lemon slices and optional olives and preserved lemon on top. Add a splash of water, bring slowly to a simmer and cook about an hour over medium-low heat. The page says meagre, sea bass, sea bream or conger eel are typical, and that thick fish suit the long cook.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine or wide heavy pot with lid, Heat diffuser if on an electric hob, Mortar or bowl for the chermoula

    Tagine

    • 35 oz. firm thick fish, cut into pieces
    • 1 batch chermoula (see below)
    • 1/3 cup olive oil
    • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
    • 2 large potatoes, cut into 1/4" (0.5 cm) slices
    • 1 or 2 carrots, cut into 1/4" (0.5 cm) sticks
    • 2 tomatoes, sliced
    • 2 bell peppers (any color), thinly sliced, seeds removed
    • fresh lemon slices for garnish
    • 1/2 cup red or green olives (optional)
    • 1 preserved lemon, quartered (optional)
    • 1 or 2 fresh chili peppers, for garnish (optional)

    Seasoning the vegetables

    • 1 teaspoon ginger
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
    • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
    • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric

    Chermoula

    • 2 cups fresh cilantro (about 1 large bunch), finely chopped
    • 4 cloves garlic, very finely chopped or pressed
    • 1 tablespoon cumin
    • 2 teaspoons paprika
    • 1 teaspoon salt
    • 1 teaspoon ginger (optional)
    • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
    • 1/4 teaspoon saffron threads, crumbled (optional)
    • 3 tablespoons vegetable or olive oil
    • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, approx.

    Method

    1. Mix the chermoula. Reserve half, covered, in the fridge.
    2. Coat the fish with the other half, cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours or overnight.
    3. Toss the sliced vegetables with the ginger, salt, pepper and turmeric.
    4. Pour the olive oil in the tagine and layer: onion first, then carrots, potatoes, then tomatoes with the reserved chermoula, then the fish. Top with peppers, lemon slices, olives and preserved lemon if using.
    5. Add 1/4 to 1/2 cup water, cover and place over medium-low to medium heat.
    6. Let it slowly reach a simmer, then simmer over medium-low heat for about 1 hour, or until the fish and vegetables are cooked.
    7. Allow a few minutes for the lemon to heat through, then take off the heat and serve.

    Chef Tips

    • Thin or small fish: cook the vegetables first and add the fish part-way. The source says thick fish suit the full hour.
    • Doneness cue from the source: the fish and vegetables are cooked and the liquid has reduced to a thick sauce.
    • Use a diffuser under a tagine on an electric range so the clay does not crack.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Adding too much water. 1/4 to 1/2 cup is enough; the vegetables and fish release liquid.
    • Skipping the 2-hour marinade; the chermoula needs time to flavour the fish.
    • Using delicate fish for the full hour.

    Cultural Note

    The page says fish tagines are among the most popular ways of cooking fresh fish in Morocco and mentions the northern clay vessel called a tagra.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Keeps 2 days in the fridge; fish is best eaten fresh.

    Reheating: Reheat covered over low heat just until warm to avoid drying the fish.

    Serve with: Khobz, Chermoula
