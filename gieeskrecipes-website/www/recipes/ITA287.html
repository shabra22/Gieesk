Salsiccia e Fagioli

A simple, hearty Tuscan pairing of grilled or pan-seared sausage with cannellini beans stewed in tomato and sage, a reliable trattoria staple found throughout the region.

This dish embodies Tuscan simplicity at its best, needing little more than good sausage and beans, the sage-scented tomato sauce doing most of the work to bring the two together into a genuinely satisfying meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large skillet

  • 8 Italian sausages
  • 400 g (2 cups) dried cannellini beans, soaked overnight (or 2 cans, drained)
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 8–10 fresh sage leaves
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. If using dried beans, simmer the soaked beans in fresh water until tender, about 1 hour, then drain.
  2. Warm half the olive oil in a large pot and sauté the garlic and sage until fragrant.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and cooked beans, and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, warm the remaining oil in a skillet and cook the sausages until browned and cooked through, about 15–18 minutes.
  5. Season the beans to taste.
  6. Serve the sausages alongside a generous portion of the beans.

Chef Tips

  • Cook the garlic gently in the oil to avoid bitterness, which would compound with the beans' mild flavor.
  • Good-quality, well-seasoned sausage makes a real difference in this simple dish.
  • This is an excellent make-ahead dish, as the beans improve with a little resting time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the garlic while infusing the oil, which turns the whole dish bitter.
  • Overcooking the beans until they turn mushy rather than staying intact.
  • Using bland sausage, which is a primary flavor component of this simple pairing.

Cultural Note

Salsiccia e fagioli is a reliable, beloved staple across Tuscan trattorie, exemplifying the region's confidence in simple, well-executed combinations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The beans keep refrigerated for up to 4 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust Tuscan red wine
