🥣 Hssoua Belboula (Cream of Barley Soup with Milk)

Hssoua belboula — creamy barley porridge (حسوة بلبولة)

A thick, comforting porridge-soup of barley grits simmered with olive oil and cumin, finished with milk, butter and evaporated milk.

50mTotal time
10mPrep

6Servings

EasyDifficulty

Barley grits are rinsed until the water runs clear, then simmered with water, olive oil, salt, pepper and cumin for 30 to 40 minutes until thick like porridge and tender. Whole milk goes in for a few minutes, and butter and evaporated milk finish it off the heat. The author says it can be served for breakfast or a light evening meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1.5 cups barley grits, medium caliber
  • 8.5 cups water
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • pepper to taste
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  • 5 to 6 oz evaporated milk

Method

  1. Pick over and rinse the grits until the water is no longer cloudy.
  2. Combine the grits, water, oil, salt, pepper and cumin. Simmer over medium-low heat 30 to 40 minutes, stirring, until thick like porridge and the grains are tender.
  3. Stir in the milk and simmer a few minutes.
  4. Off the heat, stir in the butter and evaporated milk.
  5. Garnish with cumin and olive oil or butter and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: thick like porridge and the grains tender.
  • The author notes Moroccan evaporated milk is sold as Lait Concentré Non-Sucre.
  • Barley grits are also used for breads and couscous.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the rinse, which clouds the soup.
  • Boiling the milk hard.
  • Leaving it unstirred and scorching the base.

Health Benefits

  • Barley is high in soluble fibre.
  • Warm, filling and low cost.

Cultural Note

The author says barley grits are used for sweet or savoury porridge-like soups (hssoua), breads and even couscous, and the soup may be served for breakfast or as part of a light evening meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 2 days in the fridge; thickens a lot.

Reheating: Reheat with extra milk or water, stirring.

Serve with: Khobz
