🥘 Sauce gombo à la viande (Okra Sauce with Meat, Northern Cameroon)

Sauce gombo

Salted meat boiled 30 minutes and fried, onions simmered, okra and water added, then salt and dalang to make the sauce gluey and cooked 10 minutes, plus a further 15–20 minutes.

1h 30mTotal time
20mPrep
1h 10mCook

MediumDifficulty

p. 69 of the October 2023 recipe book. Meat is boiled in salted water for 30 minutes and fried in hot oil. Onions simmer 5 minutes; the remaining crushed condiments go in; fresh okra goes in with water and the pot is brought to the boil. Salt and dalang are added ‘to make the sauce gluey’ and cooked 10 minutes; an optional stock cube; adjust the salt; cook a further 15–20 minutes. The book’s tips say natron makes the sauce gluey and keeps it green, and the meat may be replaced with fish. No quantities are printed; ‘dalang’ is not defined on the page.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Frying pan

  • Fresh okra — quantity not printed
  • Meat — quantity not printed
  • Oil, tomato, onion, garlic, ginger, salt, chilli — quantities not printed
  • Dalang — quantity not printed (undefined on the page)
  • Stock cube (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the salted meat for 30 minutes. Fry it in hot oil.
  2. Add the onions and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Add the remaining crushed condiments.
  4. Add the okra with water and bring to the boil.
  5. Add salt and dalang to make the sauce gluey and cook 10 minutes. Add the optional stock cube.
  6. Adjust the salt and cook a further 15–20 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is a ‘gluey’ sauce.
  • The book’s tip: natron makes the sauce gluey and keeps it green.
  • The meat can be replaced by fish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking the okra so long it turns grey.
  • Skipping the dalang without knowing what it does here.
  • Adding salt before tasting the stock cube.
