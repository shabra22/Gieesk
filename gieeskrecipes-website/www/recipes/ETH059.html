🦵 Ethiopian Lamb Leg Roast

የበግ እግር ሮስት — Ye-Beg Igr Rost

Ethiopian Lamb Leg Roast — a whole lamb leg marinated overnight in berbere, kibbeh, garlic, and ginger, slow-roasted at low heat until perfectly tender, served whole at the celebration table. The Ethiopian Christmas showpiece.

The whole lamb leg roast is the centrepiece of Ethiopian Christmas — the lamb slaughtered before dawn, marinated through the morning, roasted through the afternoon, and brought whole to the table at the Christmas feast. It is the most spectacular Ethiopian celebration dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large roasting pan, Oven at 150°C then 220°C

  • 2 kg bone-in lamb leg
  • 5 tbsp berbere
  • 4 tbsp kibbeh
  • 8 garlic cloves — minced
  • 3 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tsp salt

Method

  1. Pierce lamb leg deeply all over with a knife.
  2. Mix marinade thoroughly. Rub into every crevice.
  3. Marinate overnight (12 hours minimum).
  4. Bring to room temperature 2 hours before roasting.
  5. Roast at 150°C for 90 minutes — low and slow.
  6. Increase to 220°C for 20—25 minutes to caramelise the berbere crust.
  7. Rest 30 minutes before carving.

Chef Tips

  • The overnight marinade is what distinguishes this from a quick roast.
  • 30 minutes of resting is non-negotiable — do not carve early.
  • The berbere crust at high heat becomes magnificently caramelised.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Insufficient marination.
  • Not resting.

Cultural Note

The whole lamb leg at Ethiopian Christmas is the moment that the feast becomes real — the smell of the berbere crust caramelising in the final blast of heat is the smell of Genna itself. Every Ethiopian Orthodox Christian knows this moment.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Covered in oven.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Tikel Gomen (ETH087), Gomen (ETH086), The entire celebration platter
