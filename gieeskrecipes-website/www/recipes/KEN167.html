🧄 Kenyan Garlic Sauce

Mchuzi wa Vitunguu Saumu

Kenyan Garlic Sauce — a thick, creamy, intensely garlicky white sauce made from roasted garlic, lemon, and natural yogurt or mayonnaise. The addictive dipping sauce for chips, grilled chicken, and mishkaki that has become a Kenyan restaurant staple.

Kenyan garlic sauce is the condiment that regular customers know to ask for at their favourite restaurants — a thick, creamy, pungent white sauce that transforms chips, grilled chicken, and mishkaki into something extraordinary. It sits between a garlic aioli and a yogurt-based dip, with a distinctly Kenyan character from the lemon and fresh coriander.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or mortar and pestle, Bowl

  • 8 garlic cloves — roasted or raw
  • 1 cup natural yogurt or mayonnaise (or half each)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — finely chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ tsp chilli flakes — optional

Method

  1. For roasted garlic: wrap unpeeled cloves in foil and roast at 180°C for 20 minutes until soft and golden. Squeeze out roasted garlic.
  2. For raw garlic version: mince garlic very finely or crush in a mortar.
  3. Combine garlic, yogurt/mayonnaise, lemon juice, coriander, salt, pepper, and olive oil.
  4. Blend until smooth or mix thoroughly by hand for a more textured result.
  5. Taste and adjust salt and lemon.
  6. Refrigerate for 30 minutes before serving — the flavour develops.

Chef Tips

  • Roasted garlic gives a sweeter, more mellow sauce — raw garlic is sharper and more pungent. Both are delicious.
  • A combination of yogurt and mayonnaise gives the best texture — yogurt alone is too thin.
  • Resting in the refrigerator for 30 minutes is important — the flavour deepens significantly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much lemon — can curdle the yogurt.
  • Not resting before serving — the flavour is flat when just made.

The Science

Roasting garlic converts its harsh, pungent allicin into sweeter, milder ajoene and vinyldithiin compounds. This is why roasted garlic sauce has a completely different flavour profile from raw garlic sauce — less sharp, more rounded, and more aromatic.

Health Benefits

  • Garlic provides allicin — antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory.
  • Yogurt contributes probiotics and calcium.
  • Very low in carbohydrates.

Cultural Note

Kenyan garlic sauce has become a restaurant institution — the white sauce that regular customers specifically request. Its popularity reflects Kenya's enthusiastic embrace of bold, flavourful condiments.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

Serve with: Chips Masala, Grilled Chicken, Mishkaki, Chips Mayai, Sausage Roll
