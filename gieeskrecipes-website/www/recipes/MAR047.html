🫑 Taktouka (Green Pepper and Tomato Salad)

Taktouka / flifla (طاكطوكة)

Charred green peppers peeled and folded into a slow-cooked tomato and garlic sauce with paprika and cumin.

50mTotal time
20mPrep
30mCook
4Servings
150Calories
EasyDifficulty

Benkabbou chars two green peppers on a high grill, turning every 3 to 4 minutes until scorched and tender. Peeled tomatoes cook covered for 10 minutes with garlic, herbs and spices, the chopped peppers join uncovered for another 10 minutes until the liquids evaporate. She notes taktouka is a very common salad and dip, and says you can leave out the sugar if tomatoes are as sweet as in Morocco.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill or chargrill pan, Casserole or deep skillet

  • 2 large sweet green peppers such as bell peppers
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped garlic, about 2 cloves
  • 4 tablespoons mixture of fresh chopped parsley and coriander
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon cumin
  • Pinch cayenne pepper
  • ½ teaspoon salt or more to taste
  • ½ teaspoon caster sugar

Method

  1. Preheat the grill or pan to high. Grill the peppers, turning every 3 to 4 minutes, until each side is scorched and tender. Set aside to cool.
  2. Peel, deseed and roughly chop the tomatoes into 1.5 cm (1 inch) pieces.
  3. When the peppers reach room temperature, peel, deseed and chop them the same size.
  4. In a casserole heat the olive oil over medium-low heat and add the tomatoes, garlic, spices, herbs, salt and sugar. Cover and cook 10 minutes until the tomatoes have softened, stirring occasionally.
  5. Uncover, add the peppers and mix. Simmer gently for 10 minutes until all the liquids evaporate.
  6. Enjoy warm or cold, as a side or a sandwich filling.

Chef Tips

  • Cues quoted: peppers scorched and tender; tomatoes softened; liquids evaporated.
  • Leave out the sugar if your tomatoes are sweet.
  • Often eaten at room temperature.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not peeling the peppers.
  • Leaving the sauce wet.
  • Adding sugar to already sweet tomatoes.

Cultural Note

The author describes taktouka as a very common Moroccan salad and dip; a reader reply on MarocMama says it is usually eaten at room temperature in Morocco.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Serve cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Khobz
