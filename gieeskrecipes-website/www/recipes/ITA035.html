Orecchiette con le Cime di Rapa

Orecchiette con Cime di Rapa pairs Puglia's iconic 'little ear' pasta with slightly bitter, garlicky broccoli rabe and a whisper of anchovy — a defining dish of Southern Italian cooking.

This dish showcases the Puglian philosophy of letting a handful of excellent ingredients speak for themselves — the pasta and greens are even cooked together in the same pot, their starches and flavors mingling directly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large skillet

  • 400 g (14 oz) orecchiette (fresh or dried)
  • 500 g (18 oz) cime di rapa (broccoli rabe), tough stems trimmed
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 3–4 anchovy fillets
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Add the cime di rapa and blanch for 2 minutes, then remove with a slotted spoon and set aside, keeping the water boiling.
  3. Add the orecchiette to the same pot and cook according to package directions until al dente.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and anchovies, mashing the anchovies into the oil as they cook, about 2 minutes.
  5. Add the red pepper flakes and the blanched cime di rapa, tossing to coat and cooking for 3–4 minutes until tender.
  6. Drain the pasta, reserving some pasta water, and add directly to the skillet.
  7. Toss everything together over medium heat, adding pasta water as needed to bring the dish together.
  8. Taste and adjust salt — the anchovies add significant seasoning — and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The anchovies melt into the oil and become an aromatic base rather than tasting distinctly fishy — don't skip them even if hesitant.
  • Cooking the greens and pasta in the same water saves a pot and lets their flavors mingle.
  • Fresh orecchiette, if available, gives the most authentic chewy-tender texture.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the cime di rapa, which turns it mushy and dull instead of tender with a pleasant bite.
  • Skipping the anchovies, losing a key layer of savory depth.
  • Using standard broccoli as a substitute without adjusting cooking time — cime di rapa has a distinctly more bitter, leafy character.

  • Cime di rapa is rich in vitamins A, C, and K.

Cultural Note

Orecchiette — 'little ears' — is Puglia's signature pasta shape, traditionally hand-formed by pressing dough with a thumb, and this dish is one of the region's most iconic and frequently cooked meals.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently in a skillet with a splash of olive oil or water.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A crisp Puglian white wine, Grated Pecorino (optional, non-traditional)
