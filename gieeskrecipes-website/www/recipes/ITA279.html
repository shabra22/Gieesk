🥖 Cuzzupe Calabresi

Cuzzupe

Calabria's own braided Easter cookies, their dough shaped into decorative rings or braids around whole hard-boiled eggs, a simpler, crunchier cousin to Cuddura cu l'Ova.

While cuddura is a soft yeasted bread, cuzzupe are firmer, cookie-like, and unleavened by comparison, their glossy egg-wash finish and colorful sprinkles making them a favorite for Calabrian children to help decorate each Easter.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Baking sheet

  • 500 g (4 cups) 00 flour
  • 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
  • 150 g (2/3 cup) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 eggs, plus 1 for egg wash
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Hard-boiled eggs, in their shells, for decorating
  • Colorful sprinkles

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar together until light.
  2. Beat in the 3 eggs and lemon zest.
  3. Combine the flour, baking powder, and salt, then fold into the wet ingredients to form a firm, workable dough.
  4. Rest the dough, covered, for 30 minutes.
  5. Divide into portions and shape into ropes, braiding or coiling them into rings or decorative shapes.
  6. Nestle a hard-boiled egg into each shape, securing gently with a bit of extra dough if needed.
  7. Rest for a further 30 minutes.
  8. Brush with beaten egg and scatter with colorful sprinkles.
  9. Bake at 180C (350F) for 20-25 minutes until golden.
  10. Cool before serving.

Chef Tips

  • The dough should be firm enough to hold decorative shapes like braids and rings without spreading.
  • These are traditionally a fun, hands-on project for children to help shape and decorate.
  • Securing the eggs well prevents them from shifting during baking.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Making the dough too soft, causing the decorative shapes to lose definition during baking.
  • Not securing the eggs well, allowing them to roll out of position.
  • Skipping the egg wash, which is what gives the finished cookies their glossy sheen.

Cultural Note

Cuzzupe are a beloved Calabrian Easter tradition, their firm, cookie-like texture and playful decorative shapes making them a favorite for children to help create each spring.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 4 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: As part of an Easter breakfast or holiday table
