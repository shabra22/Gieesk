🍋 Caldou with Lemon

Caldou ou kaldou au citron

Small fish blanched, never fried, in lightly browned onions and a litre of salted water, finished with the juice of four lemons and served with rice.

2hTotal time
1hPrep
1hCook
8Servings
610Calories
EasyDifficulty

The Afrik caldou is one sentence of introduction — ‘a recipe from the Casamance’ — and a short method. A kilo of rice cooks gently in two litres of salted water. A very large onion, sliced, softens in hot groundnut oil; the small fish are added and ‘blanchis, mais pas frits’, then salted, peppered and covered with a litre of salted water. They simmer for a quarter of an hour, the lemon juice goes in, and cooking continues a few minutes more before everything is served with the rice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot for the rice, Wide pot for the fish

  • 1 kg small fish
  • 1 very large onion
  • 4 lemons
  • 1 or 2 local chiles
  • 3 or 4 spoonfuls of peanut oil — spoon size not specified
  • 1 kg rice
  • Salt and pepper — quantities not printed
  • 2 litres salted water for the rice, plus 1 litre salted water for the fish

Method

  1. Bring 2 litres of salted water to a boil in a pot and add the washed rice. Cook over low heat.
  2. Slice the onion and soften it in hot peanut oil.
  3. Lay the fish in the pot and blanch them — do not fry. Salt, pepper, then pour in 1 litre of salted water.
  4. Let simmer a quarter of an hour.
  5. Add the lemon juice.
  6. Continue cooking a few minutes and serve with the rice.

Chef Tips

  • ‘Blanchir, mais pas frire’ is the one cue the source gives — the fish should turn pale in the pan, not take colour.
  • A quarter of an hour at a bare simmer — the printed time — is the whole cooking of the fish.
  • Add the lemon juice after the simmer, not before: acid early toughens the fish.
  • The source gives no browning target for the onion, only ‘hot oil’.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying the fish in the oil.
  • Adding the lemon too early.
  • Boiling rather than simmering.

Cultural Note

Afrik’s one-line introduction places caldou in the Casamance. A third printing, ‘Caldou ou Kaldou au Bissap’ (with hibiscus), is named on the same site and was not read.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 1 day refrigerated.

Reheating: Gently, covered.

Serve with: Steamed white rice
