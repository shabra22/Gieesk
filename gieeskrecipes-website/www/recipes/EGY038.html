🍋 Shorbet Tarbia (Egyptian Lemon-Chicken Soup)

Shorbet tarbiya — شوربة تربية

Shredded chicken in a lemony roux-thickened stock made from a spiced bone-in chicken broth, with optional toasted orzo.

2h 15mTotal time
15mPrep
2hCook
4Servings
240Calories
MediumDifficulty

A bone-in chicken breast is seared until the skin is crisp and golden and simmered 1½ to 2 hours with vegetables, mastic and whole spices until fork-tender and the vegetables collapse. A butter and flour roux is cooked to a golden paste, 2 cups of the broth are whisked in with lemon juice, and the shredded chicken is added.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Saucepan, Strainer

    Soup

    • 2 tablespoons butter
    • 1 tablespoon all purpose flour
    • 2 cups chicken broth (recipe below)
    • Toasted orzo, optional
    • lemon juice, as needed
    • 2 chicken breasts, shredded
    • Salt and pepper to taste

    Broth

    • Whole chicken breast, bone in
    • 1 tablespoon ghee or butter
    • 1 tablespoon unflavored oil, such as canola
    • 1 celery stalk, coarsely chopped
    • 1 medium carrot, coarsely chopped
    • 1 onion, quartered
    • 5-7 gloves garlic, skin on
    • 1 leek, coarsely chopped, optional
    • Handful parsley stalks
    • 3 bay leaves
    • 3 cardamom, bruised
    • 2 mastic resins, optional
    • 1 tablespoon Lebanese 7 spices or Egyptian Boharat
    • 5 allspice whole berries
    • 10 black peppercorns berries
    • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    • Salt & pepper to taste

    Method

    1. Sear the chicken breast in the fat until the skin is crispy and golden.
    2. Add the vegetables, spices and hot water and simmer one and a half to two hours, or until the chicken is fork-tender and the vegetables have collapsed. Strain.
    3. Melt 2 tablespoons butter, stir in the flour until a golden paste forms, and whisk in 2 cups of the broth. Add the juice of half a lemon.
    4. Add the shredded chicken, and toasted orzo if you like, and season.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: skin crispy and golden; chicken fork-tender; roux a golden paste. The ingredient list says 1 tablespoon flour but the method says 2 tablespoons.
    • The author calls it an eggless version of the Mediterranean chicken soup, eclipsed by avgolemono.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Adding the lemon to boiling soup, which can dull its brightness (our tip).

    Cultural Note

    Nermine Mansour ate it at her grandmother Aida's house and calls it an eggless rendition of the Mediterranean chicken soup, eclipsed by avgolemono.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

    Reheating: Reheat gently.

    Serve with: Toasted baladi bread
