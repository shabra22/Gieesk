☕ Sahlab (Egyptian Winter Drink, Powder-Mix Version)

Sahlab — سحلب

A stored dry mix of milk powder, rice flour, cornstarch, coconut and cinnamon; two tablespoons stirred into cold milk and heated until thick and bubbly.

20mTotal time
10mPrep
10mCook
10Servings
210Calories
EasyDifficulty

Full cream milk powder, powdered sugar, coconut flakes, rice flour, cornstarch, cinnamon and toasted sesame are mixed and stored. For one cup, 2 tablespoons of the mix go into a cup of cold milk and are stirred continuously over medium heat until the mixture gets thick and bubbly, then garnished with nuts, cinnamon and raisins.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Airtight jar, Small saucepan

  • 1 cup full cream powder milk
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut flakes
  • 1 cup rice flour
  • 4 tbs cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla powder - optional
  • 2 tsp cinnamon powder
  • 1/2 cup toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients thoroughly and store in an airtight jar.
  2. For one serving, add 2 tablespoons of the mix to a cup of cold milk.
  3. Stir continuously over medium heat until the mixture gets thick and bubbly.
  4. Pour into a cup and garnish with nuts, cinnamon and raisins.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: thick and bubbly. This is a powder-mix version with no orchid (salep) ingredient. The page gives no storage time for the mix.
  • Stirring constantly prevents lumps.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stopping stirring.
