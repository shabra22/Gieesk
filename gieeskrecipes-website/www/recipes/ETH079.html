🥚 Ethiopian Chicken and Egg Stew

ዶሮ እንቁላል — Doro Inqiilal

Ethiopian Chicken and Egg Stew — a simplified everyday Doro Wat with chicken and boiled eggs simmered together in a berbere sauce. The everyday version of the national dish — eggs extending the meal and adding additional complete protein.

Doro Inqiilal is the daily reminder of Doro Wat — a simplified version where the everyday cook uses fewer ingredients but maintains the essential elements: berbere sauce, chicken, and the all-important whole eggs. The eggs absorb the sauce and become as prized as the chicken.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 600g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs — pierced
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 15 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Toast 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Add chicken. Coat in sauce.
  4. Add water. Simmer covered 35 minutes.
  5. Add pierced eggs. Simmer 15 more minutes.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Pierce eggs with a skewer — deeper than you think.
  • Even 15 minutes in the sauce turns the eggs distinctly orange.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not piercing eggs.
  • Short cooking — eggs should be orange throughout.

Cultural Note

Doro Inqiilal keeps the spirit of Doro Wat alive every day — the whole egg in berbere sauce is the most unmistakably Ethiopian element of the national dish, and this everyday version ensures it is accessible whenever needed.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Gomen (ETH086)
