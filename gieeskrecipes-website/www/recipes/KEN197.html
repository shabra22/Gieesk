🎃 Kenyan Pumpkin Porridge

Uji wa Maboga

Kenyan Pumpkin Porridge — pureed pumpkin cooked with millet flour or cornmeal, coconut milk, and a touch of cinnamon into a vibrantly orange, naturally sweet, creamy breakfast porridge. A beautiful, nutritious Kenyan morning meal that celebrates one of the country's most productive crops.

Pumpkin porridge is one of the most colourful and nutritious of all Kenyan breakfasts — the vivid orange of the pumpkin, the natural sweetness, and the depth that coconut milk adds create something that looks and tastes extraordinary for such a simple preparation. It is particularly popular with children and in communities where pumpkins grow abundantly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Blender or potato masher

  • 400g pumpkin — peeled, seeded, and cubed
  • ¼ cup finger millet flour or cornmeal
  • 3 cups water
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar or honey
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Boil pumpkin cubes in water for 12—15 minutes until completely soft.
  2. Blend until smooth or mash thoroughly.
  3. Return to the saucepan. Add coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Mix millet flour or cornmeal with 4 tbsp cold water to a smooth slurry.
  5. Pour slurry into the simmering pumpkin mixture, stirring constantly.
  6. Cook for 6—8 minutes until thick.
  7. Add sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Stir.
  8. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Orange-fleshed pumpkin varieties produce the best colour and sweetness.
  • The millet flour or cornmeal thickens the porridge and adds nutritional depth.
  • Coconut milk elevates this from a simple pumpkin puree into something rich and fragrant.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using pale, watery pumpkin — the orange-fleshed varieties are sweeter and more flavourful.
  • Not cooking the flour slurry sufficiently — raw flour taste remains.

The Science

Pumpkin's orange colour comes from beta-carotene and other carotenoids. These fat-soluble compounds are more bioavailable when consumed with fat — making the coconut milk in this recipe not just a flavour addition but a nutritional enhancer.

Health Benefits

  • Pumpkin is exceptionally rich in beta-carotene — one serving provides 250% of daily vitamin A.
  • Very low in calories.
  • Coconut milk enhances fat-soluble vitamin absorption.
  • Excellent for young children.

Cultural Note

Pumpkin porridge represents the Kenyan kitchen's tradition of using every part of every crop — the pumpkin plant feeds the community through its leaves, seeds, and fruit from morning porridge to evening stew.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat with splash of water or coconut milk.

Reheating: Warm gently on stovetop, stirring.

Serve with: Kenyan Masala Chai, Toasted Coconut, Roasted Groundnuts, Honey drizzle
