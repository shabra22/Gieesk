🍢 Kefta Dyal Gharb (Ground Meat from the Gharb Region)

Kefta dyal l'ham — Gharb-style kefta

Meat and kidney fat ground twice with herbs and spices, shaped into mini-burgers or fingers and grilled over grey-ashed coals; served with a salt, cumin and cayenne mix.

The author cuts a mix of lean and fatty cuts into big cubes, grinds them with kidney fat, adds grated onion, parsley, mint, marjoram, cumin, paprika, salt, cayenne, ras el hanout and coriander, and grinds a second time. The kefta is shaped into 2-inch mini-burgers, 4-inch fingers or long sausages pressed around metal skewers, then grilled once the charcoal is covered with a layer of grey-silver ash. It should be well done but juicy inside, by Moroccan standards.

Ingredients

Equipment: Meat grinder, Metal skewers, Charcoal grill

  • 1.65 lbs meat, mixture of lean and fatty parts such as leg of lamb, flank, middle chest and large chest, cut into big cubes
  • 8.8 oz kidney fat (suet) diced small
  • 1 onion, medium-sized grated or finely chopped

Seasoning

  • 1/4 cup flat leaf parsley, leaves only, finely chopped
  • 3 to 5 mint leaves finely chopped
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground sweet paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cayenne, or to taste
  • 1 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 tsp ground coriander seeds

For serving

  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp cayenne

Method

  1. Grind the meat and fat with a machine. Add the herbs and spices and grind a second time, then mix in the onion. (If freezing, add the grated onion only on the day you thaw and use it.)
  2. Shape into mini-burgers about 2 inches round and no more than 1/3 inch thick; fingers about 4 inches tall and 1/2 inch thick; or squeeze a lump around metal skewers into a long sausage.
  3. Start grilling when the charcoal is covered with a layer of grey-silver ash. Grill each side a few minutes, rotating to the other side.
  4. Mix the salt, cumin and cayenne and serve alongside for dipping. A knob of butter over the hot kefta fingers is the author's father's serving tip.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: well done but juicy inside; some like it with burnt edges, some just medium.
  • The grill is ready when the coals have a layer of grey-silver ash.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Grilling over flames rather than ash-covered coals.
  • Adding the onion before freezing.

Cultural Note

The page ties this style of kefta to the Gharb region in northwest Morocco, as the author frames it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Freeze the mixture without onion, per the page; refrigerate cooked kefta.

Reheating: Reheat until steaming hot.

Serve with: Bread, Salads, such as Zaalouk
