🍌 Banana Fritters (Sweet Potato and Banana)

Banana fritters

A sweet potato blended with water and two ripe bananas, mixed with flour and salt until smooth, and fried by the spoonful in hot oil until golden brown; served hot.

The sweet potato is cut small and blended with the water; the peeled bananas are added and blended smooth; the flour and salt are mixed in until smooth. Oil is heated in a pot until hot, a spoonful of the mixture goes in and fries until golden brown. Served hot as an appetiser or dessert. Times and oil quantity are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Pot

  • 1 large sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Oil for frying — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Cut the sweet potato into small pieces. Blend with the water.
  2. Add the peeled bananas and blend till smooth.
  3. Mix in the flour and salt till smooth.
  4. Heat oil in a pot till hot.
  5. Put spoonfuls of the mixture into the oil and fry till golden brown.
  6. Serve hot as an appetiser or dessert.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cues are ‘till smooth’ and ‘till golden brown’.
  • Oil temperature is not printed — test with a drop of batter.
  • The sweet potato is raw when blended, so give the fritters time to cook through.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying in oil that is not hot enough.
  • Making the batter too thin.
  • Making the fritters too large to cook through.
