🍰 Ethiopian Coffee Cheesecake

ቡና ቺዝኬክ — Buna Cheesecake

Ethiopian Coffee Cheesecake — a baked cheesecake infused with Yirgacheffe espresso, cardamom, and Ethiopian honey, on a Dabo Kolo crumb base. The modern Ethiopian café's proudest dessert — celebrating the birthplace of coffee in the cheesecake format.

Ethiopian coffee cheesecake is the Addis Ababa café's signature dessert — the globally beloved cheesecake format made completely Ethiopian by using Yirgacheffe espresso, Ethiopian mountain honey, and a Dabo Kolo (traditional crunchy bread) crumb base.

Ingredients

Equipment: 20cm springform tin, Oven at 160°C (bain marie)

    Dabo Kolo Crumb Base

    • 150g Dabo Kolo — crushed
    • 50g butter — melted

    Coffee Cheesecake Filling

    • 500g cream cheese — room temperature
    • 3 eggs
    • 100g Ethiopian honey
    • 2 tbsp strong Yirgacheffe espresso
    • 1 tsp ground cardamom
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract

    Method

    1. Mix crushed Dabo Kolo with melted butter. Press into lined springform tin. Chill.
    2. Beat cream cheese until smooth.
    3. Add honey, eggs one at a time, espresso, cardamom, and vanilla.
    4. Beat until completely smooth.
    5. Pour over Dabo Kolo base.
    6. Bake in a bain marie at 160°C for 55—60 minutes.
    7. Centre should be barely set — slight wobble is correct.
    8. Cool in oven 1 hour (door ajar). Refrigerate 4 hours minimum.

    Chef Tips

    • The Dabo Kolo base is the Ethiopian innovation — its cardamom-honey flavour pairs perfectly with the coffee filling.
    • Bain marie is essential — prevents cracking.
    • Yirgacheffe espresso should be strong — 2 tbsp brewed at espresso concentration.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • No bain marie — cracks.
    • Weak coffee — no flavour impact.

    The Science

    Cheesecake sets through egg protein coagulation — at 160°C in a bain marie, the water bath limits maximum temperature to 100°C, ensuring gentle, even setting. The slight wobble at removal means the residual heat will complete the setting during cooling.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian coffee cheesecake is the Addis Ababa café's proudest statement — 'we invented coffee, and this is what we can do with it'. The Dabo Kolo base, the Yirgacheffe espresso filling, the cardamom note — it is completely Ethiopian in its soul.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 2 months.

    Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

    Serve with: With a shot of Yirgacheffe espresso, As the café's signature dessert
