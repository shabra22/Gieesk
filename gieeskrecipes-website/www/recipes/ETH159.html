🌶️ Ethiopian Berbere Mayonnaise

ሜዮኔዝ ቤርቤሬ — Berbere Mayo

Ethiopian Berbere Mayonnaise — commercial or home-made mayonnaise mixed with berbere, a pinch of mitmita, and lemon. The modern condiment that bridges Ethiopian spice tradition with international café culture — used in sandwiches, burgers, and alongside fried foods.

Berbere mayo is what Ethiopian cafés serve with their craft burgers, their fried fish sandwiches, and their chips — the familiar mayo format given an unmistakably Ethiopian character by berbere and mitmita.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl

  • 8 tbsp good quality mayonnaise
  • 1.5 tsp berbere
  • ¼ tsp mitmita
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove — minced
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients.
  2. Mix thoroughly.
  3. Rest 10 minutes — flavours integrate.
  4. Taste and adjust berbere and lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Start with 1 tsp berbere and taste — berbere varies significantly in heat between batches.
  • The mitmita adds a finishing heat punch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not tasting before serving — berbere batches vary.

The Science

The mayonnaise's emulsified fat dissolves the fat-soluble carotenoids and capsaicinoids from the berbere — producing a uniformly spiced, richly coloured condiment where the berbere flavour is distributed throughout every portion.

Cultural Note

Berbere mayo is the symbol of the new Ethiopian café generation — the young Ethiopian who has grown up eating injera and Doro Wat but also burgers and café food, and who brings the berbere tradition to every format they engage with.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On Ethiopian craft burgers, With chips (chipsi), On fish sandwiches, As a dipping sauce
