🍽️ Cameroun Poulet DG: Chicken for the Director General (Yummy Medley)

Poulet DG

Chicken thigh cubes marinated at least 30 minutes in a blended cilantro, celery and Scotch bonnet marinade, broiled, then combined with fried plantain rounds, carrots, bell peppers and a reduced tomato sauce.

1h 30mTotal time

Lois, a non-Cameroonian blogger, learned this from a Cameroonian friend, Joyce, and notes its similarity to a Nigerian dish. The chicken is marinated at least 30 minutes in half the blended marinade. Plantain rounds (1 cm) are fried golden on both sides; the chicken is broiled until golden brown without flipping; the onion is sautéed 5 minutes until translucent; blended tomatoes are reduced on high heat until thick; the reserved marinade is added and simmered 5 minutes. Carrots cook 5 minutes, then the plantains, chicken and bell peppers 5 more minutes. The page notes that unripe plantains darken more than ripe ones.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Broiler pan, Frying pan, Large skillet

    Marinade

    • 2 heaping cups cilantro
    • 2 stalks celery
    • 1 Scotch bonnet habanero pepper
    • 3 shallots or half a red onion
    • A thumb-size nub of ginger
    • 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon
    • 1 teaspoon salt
    • 1 teaspoon white pepper

    Dish

    • 1 1/2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, chopped into 1-inch cubes
    • 3 unripe plantains
    • 1 ripe plantain
    • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
    • 2 carrots, cut into coins
    • 1/2 green, 1/2 yellow, 1/2 orange bell pepper, cut into strips
    • 8 Roma tomatoes
    • 1 onion
    • Salt to taste

    Method

    1. Blend the marinade ingredients. Marinate the chicken at least 30 minutes in half of it; keep the rest.
    2. Fry 1 cm plantain rounds until golden on both sides.
    3. Broil the chicken until golden brown, without flipping. (The broiler temperature is not printed.)
    4. Sauté the onion about 5 minutes on medium heat until translucent. Add the blended tomatoes on high heat and reduce until thick.
    5. Add the reserved marinade and simmer 5 minutes on low-medium heat.
    6. Add the carrots for 5 minutes, then the plantains, chicken and bell peppers for 5 more minutes.

    Chef Tips

    • Printed cues: chicken ‘golden brown’; onion ‘translucent’; tomato ‘reduce until thick’.
    • Unripe plantains darken more than ripe ones when fried.
    • The broiler temperature is not printed.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Skipping the 30-minute marinade.
    • Crowding the plantains in the pan.
    • Cooking the bell peppers too long.
