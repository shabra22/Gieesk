🍠 Swahili Fish Stew with Cassava

Mchuzi wa Samaki na Muhogo

Tanzanian Mchuzi wa Samaki na Muhogo — fish and cassava simmered together in a fragrant coconut milk and spice sauce. The complete one-pot coastal meal — fish for protein, cassava for starch, coconut milk for richness.

Fish and cassava stew is the complete coastal meal — protein, starch, and sauce in one pot. The cassava absorbs the coconut-spice sauce completely, becoming extraordinarily rich and flavourful alongside the fish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g firm fish — cubed
  • 400g cassava — peeled and cubed
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
  3. Add cassava pieces and ½ cup water. Cover and cook 15 minutes until partially tender.
  4. Add coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Add fish. Cover and simmer gently 12—15 minutes.
  6. Cassava should be completely tender; fish should flake easily.
  7. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Add cassava before fish — it takes much longer.
  • Never boil coconut milk hard.
  • Do not stir once fish is added — it breaks apart.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding cassava and fish simultaneously.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

The Science

Cassava starch gelatinises into the coconut milk during cooking, naturally thickening the sauce. This is why no additional thickening is needed — the cassava does the work.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Fish and cassava stew is the complete coastal meal — protein from the sea and starch from the land, united by the coconut milk that makes everything better on the Swahili coast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Gently — cassava can become sticky.

Serve with: As a complete standalone meal, With kachumbari
