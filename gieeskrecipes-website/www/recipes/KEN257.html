🍝 Kenyan Beef Stroganoff

Stroganoff ya Nyama ya Kenya

Kenyan Beef Stroganoff — tender strips of sirloin sautéed with onions and mushrooms in a rich sour cream and mustard sauce with a touch of Kenyan paprika. The hotel comfort classic adapted for Kenyan tastes — served over herb rice or pasta.

Beef stroganoff arrived in Kenya's hotel kitchens during the colonial era and has never left — adapted over decades with Kenyan spices and local beef into something that belongs as much to the Kenyan restaurant repertoire as to its Russian origins.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large wide pan, Tongs

  • 600g beef sirloin — thinly sliced against the grain
  • 200g button mushrooms — sliced
  • 2 medium onions — thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley

Method

  1. Season beef strips with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in pan over high heat. Sear beef in batches — 1—2 minutes. Remove.
  3. Add butter. Cook onions until golden — 6 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and mushrooms. Cook until mushrooms release liquid and brown — 6 minutes.
  5. Add paprika and Worcestershire sauce. Stir.
  6. Add beef stock. Deglaze. Reduce by half.
  7. Remove from heat. Stir in sour cream and mustard.
  8. Return beef. Stir gently — do not boil after adding sour cream.
  9. Scatter parsley. Serve over herb rice or pasta.

Chef Tips

  • Never boil after adding sour cream — it curdles.
  • Sear beef in small batches over high heat — crowding produces grey steamed beef.
  • Slice beef very thin against the grain for maximum tenderness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling after adding sour cream.
  • Crowding the pan during searing.

The Science

Sour cream curdles when heated vigorously because its proteins denature and aggregate at high temperatures. By removing the pan from heat before adding sour cream and reintroducing the beef after stirring, the temperature stays below the curdling point.

Cultural Note

Beef stroganoff in a Kenyan hotel represents the globalisation of cuisine absorbed and adapted by Kenya's hospitality industry — a Russian dish that has been part of the Kenyan restaurant scene for generations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for 3 days — reheat gently without boiling.

Reheating: Very gently on stovetop.
