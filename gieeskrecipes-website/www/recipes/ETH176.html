🍌 Ethiopian Banana Fritters

ሙዝ ጥብስ — Muz Tibs

Ethiopian Banana Fritters — ripe bananas sliced and dipped in a light spiced batter with cardamom and a pinch of cinnamon, then deep-fried until golden. The southern Ethiopian sweet fritter — sold at markets in banana-growing regions, eaten warm.

Banana fritters are the street food of Ethiopia's southern banana-growing regions — where bananas are abundant, street vendors fry them in a light batter fragrant with cardamom. Served warm with a drizzle of honey, they are one of Ethiopia's best sweet street foods.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep pan, Bowl for batter

  • 4 ripe bananas — sliced into rounds

Light Batter

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • ¾ cup water
  • Oil for frying
  • Honey for serving

Method

  1. Make batter: whisk flour, baking powder, cardamom, cinnamon, sugar, salt, and water until smooth.
  2. Heat oil to 175°C.
  3. Dip banana slices in batter. Coat completely.
  4. Fry in batches 2—3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Drain. Drizzle with honey.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Very ripe bananas — the sweetness increases with ripeness.
  • Thin batter — it should just coat, not be thick.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underripe bananas — starchy and flavourless.
  • Thick batter — doughy.

The Science

Ripe bananas contain predominantly simple sugars (fructose, glucose) converted from starch — these caramelise rapidly in the hot oil, browning faster than the batter. The batter's baking powder creates CO2 bubbles that expand and produce a light, crispy exterior.

Cultural Note

Banana fritters are the sweet street food of southern Ethiopia — the abundant banana harvest of the Rift Valley and the southern highlands produces not just the fruit but a tradition of fried, honeyed banana street food that is one of the region's culinary pleasures.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Oven briefly.

Serve with: With honey, As a dessert, As a sweet street snack
