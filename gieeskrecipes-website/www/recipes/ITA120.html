Affogato al Caffè

The simplest of Italian desserts, a scoop of gelato 'drowned' in a fresh shot of hot espresso, the contrast of hot and cold, bitter and sweet, coming together in seconds.

Affogato requires no real recipe so much as good instincts and good ingredients — quality gelato and a properly pulled espresso shot poured over it tableside, so the coffee just begins melting the ice cream as it's served.

Ingredients

Equipment: Espresso machine or stovetop moka pot, Small serving glasses

  • 4 scoops gelato alla crema or vanilla gelato
  • 2 shots freshly brewed espresso, hot
  • A few coffee beans or a dusting of cocoa, for garnish (optional)
  • A splash of amaretto or Frangelico liqueur (optional)

Method

  1. Place two scoops of gelato into each small serving glass just before serving.
  2. Brew the espresso fresh and hot.
  3. Pour the hot espresso directly over the gelato at the table, right before eating.
  4. Add a splash of amaretto or Frangelico if desired.
  5. Serve immediately with a small spoon, eating quickly while the contrast between hot coffee and cold gelato is at its best.

Chef Tips

  • Timing is everything — pour the espresso over the gelato just before serving, ideally at the table, so guests experience the melting contrast firsthand.
  • Good-quality gelato and freshly pulled espresso make all the difference in such a simple dessert.
  • A splash of liqueur turns this into an adult after-dinner treat with very little extra effort.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Pouring the espresso too far ahead of serving, letting the gelato melt completely before it reaches the table.
  • Using pre-made, lukewarm coffee instead of a freshly pulled hot espresso shot.
  • Using low-quality ice cream, which lacks the density and flavor of true gelato.

Cultural Note

Affogato, meaning 'drowned,' is a simple, beloved way to end an Italian meal, often ordered as a lighter alternative to more elaborate desserts.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Not applicable; must be assembled and served immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Nothing more — this is a complete, minimalist dessert
