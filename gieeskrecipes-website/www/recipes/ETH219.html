🥭 Ethiopian Fresh Mango Juice

አንቦ ጭማቂ — Mango Chumaki

Ethiopian Fresh Mango Juice — ripe Ethiopian mangoes blended with a little water or milk, sweetened with honey, and spiked with a pinch of cardamom. Ethiopia's most beloved juice — the Addis Ababa juice bar staple where fresh fruit is blended to order in front of the customer.

Ethiopian fresh mango juice is the centrepiece of one of Africa's most extraordinary juice bar cultures — the juice bars of Addis Ababa blend fresh fruit to order, often layering mango, avocado, and papaya into rainbow drinks. The Ethiopian mango, grown in the Rift Valley lowlands, is among Africa's finest.

Ingredients

Equipment: High-powered blender

  • 4 ripe Ethiopian mangoes — peeled, stoned, cubed
  • ½ cup water or whole milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Add mango cubes to blender.
  2. Add water or milk, honey, and cardamom.
  3. Blend until completely smooth.
  4. Add ice. Blend briefly.
  5. Taste — adjust honey.
  6. Pour into glasses. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The Ethiopian Rift Valley mango is so sweet it barely needs honey — taste first.
  • Cardamom is the Ethiopian signature that separates this from generic mango juice.
  • Milk version is richer; water version is more refreshing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-sweetening — ripe Ethiopian mango is already very sweet.

The Science

Mango's primary flavour compounds are terpenes (including myrcene and linalool) and esters — blending with cold liquid and ice minimises the oxidation of these volatile aromatics, preserving the freshest flavour.

Health Benefits

  • Very high vitamin C.

  • Naturally sweetened — no refined sugar needed.

Cultural Note

The Ethiopian juice bar (Kaldi's and its competitors) is one of Addis Ababa's most distinctive urban institutions — the fresh juice bar where mangoes, avocados, and papayas are blended to order in front of customers, often layered visually. The Addis Ababa fresh juice tradition is one of the city's most beloved food experiences.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable — drink cold.

Serve with: At Addis Ababa juice bars, As a breakfast drink, With Ethiopian pastries
