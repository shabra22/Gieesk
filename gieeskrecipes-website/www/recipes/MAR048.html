🌿 Bakoula (Mallow Salad with Preserved Lemon)

Bakoula / khoubiza (بقولة - خبيزة)

Steamed mallow leaves pressed dry and sautéed with garlic, herbs, cumin, paprika and preserved lemon, served warm or at room temperature.

55mTotal time
20mPrep
35mCook
4Servings
290Calories
EasyDifficulty

Mallow is washed well, chopped and steamed for 15 to 20 minutes until tender, shrinking in bulk and darkening. After the water is pressed out it goes into a skillet where garlic, herbs and spices have been sautéed in olive oil until the herbs wilt and the garlic is fragrant; preserved lemon pulp and lemon juice go in for 5 to 10 minutes. It is garnished with olives and preserved lemon rind.

Ingredients

Equipment: Steamer basket, Large skillet

  • ½ pound mallow leaves and stems (about 8 cups, packed)
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • ½ cup chopped parsley and/or cilantro (I use a mix)
  • 4 cloves garlic, pressed or finely chopped (or more to taste)
  • 1½ teaspoons paprika
  • 1½ teaspoons cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (or more to taste)
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (or to taste; optional)
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice (or to taste)
  • 1 preserved lemon pulp, chopped
  • Preserved lemon rind, for garnish
  • 1 handful red olives, for garnish

Method

  1. Wash the mallow thoroughly in a large bowl or sink of water; rinse and drain well.
  2. Chop the leaves and stems and steam in a basket over simmering water for 15 to 20 minutes, until tender; it will greatly reduce in bulk and turn darker.
  3. Squeeze or press out the extra water.
  4. While it steams, place the olive oil, garlic, herbs and spices in a large skillet and sauté over medium or medium-low heat for several minutes, until the herbs are wilted and the garlic is fragrant. Be careful not to burn the garlic.
  5. Add the mallow, chopped preserved lemon pulp and lemon juice and sauté over medium heat for 5 to 10 minutes, until the flavours are well combined.
  6. Serve warm or at room temperature, garnished with olives and preserved lemon rind.

Chef Tips

  • Cues quoted: tender, reduced in bulk and darker; herbs wilted and garlic fragrant.
  • Morocco World News pressure-cooks chopped bakoula with a cup of water and a quarter cup of oil for up to 30 minutes instead.
  • Wash thoroughly; wild greens carry grit.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the garlic.
  • Not pressing the water out.
  • Using too much salt with preserved lemon.

Cultural Note

The author writes that Moroccans refer to both the mallow plant and the salad as khoubiza or bakkoula, and cites a journal article describing mallow as one of 17 weeds Moroccans include in their cuisine.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature or warm lightly.

Serve with: Khobz, Preserved lemons
