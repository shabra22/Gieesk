🥬 Folong (Hako Diam) sauté

Folong sauté

Fresh folong leaves blanched in natron water, then added to a fried base of onion, smoked fish, tomato and spices for 10–15 minutes; may be made with meat, fish or plain.

p. 62 of the October 2023 recipe book. Fresh folong leaves are blanched in natron water, then added to the fried base for 10–15 minutes. The page says the dish can be made with meat, smoked or fresh fish, or plain. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Frying pan

  • Fresh folong leaves — quantity not printed
  • Smoked fish — quantity not printed
  • Oil, onion, garlic, tomato, bell pepper, leeks, parsley, celery, white pepper, ginger, salt, chilli — quantities not printed
  • Natron — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Blanch the leaves in natron water.
  2. Fry the onion; add the fish, tomato and seasonings.
  3. Add the leaves and cook 10–15 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Printed variants: meat, smoked or fresh fish, or plain.
  • No doneness cue is printed beyond the times.
  • Quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-cooking the leaves.
  • Skipping the blanch.
  • Adding too much natron.
