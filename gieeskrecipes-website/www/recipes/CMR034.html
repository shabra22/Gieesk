🍗 Poulet DG (Foreign Fork)

Poulet DG

Chicken thighs browned but not cooked through, simmered covered 20 minutes in a blended parsley, green onion, ginger and celery sauce with green beans, carrots and red pepper, topped with fried slightly ripe plantains.

60 minTotal time
20 minPrep
40 minCook
5Servings
600Calories
MediumDifficulty

The author says the dish is called Poulet DG in Cameroon and served to important guests and at holidays. The thighs are seasoned and browned on both sides in a pan with oil about 1/8–1/4 inch deep; they should be brown but not cooked all the way through. A blend of parsley, green onion, olive oil, ginger, garlic, celery, white pepper and salt goes in with the bouillon cube and is heated until the cube melts. About 1/4 cup of water, green beans, carrots and red bell pepper cook about 15 minutes until they begin to soften. The chicken goes back in covered for another 20 minutes, and fried plantains, slightly crispy and cooked through, are arranged on top.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender, Frying pan

  • 5 chicken thighs
  • Olive oil for frying — quantity not printed
  • 2 bunches parsley (printed ‘2 bunch’)
  • 8 stalks green onions, chopped
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 2 inches ginger root, grated
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons garlic, minced
  • White pepper, black pepper and salt to taste
  • 1 chicken bouillon cube
  • 1 handful green beans, trimmed
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 carrots, peeled and sliced into rounds
  • 3 slightly ripe plantains, sliced into rounds about 1 inch thick

Method

  1. Season the chicken with black pepper and salt. Coat the bottom of a pan with oil about 1/8–1/4 inch thick.
  2. Fry the chicken on both sides until it turns brown but is not cooked all the way through.
  3. Blend the parsley, green onion, olive oil, ginger, garlic, celery, white pepper and salt. Add it to the pot with the bouillon cube and heat until the cube melts.
  4. Add 1/4 cup water, the green beans, carrots and red pepper and cook about 15 minutes, until they begin to soften.
  5. Return the chicken to the pot, cover and cook for another 20 minutes.
  6. Fry the plantains until slightly crispy and cooked through and arrange on top.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: chicken ‘brown but not cooked all the way through’; vegetables ‘begin to soften’; plantains ‘slightly crispy’.
  • Do not pre-cook the chicken fully; it finishes in the covered pot.
  • The 1/4 cup water is in the method but not in the list.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Browning the chicken all the way through.
  • Forgetting the 1/4 cup water.
  • Frying the plantains too early so they soften.
