🥧 Crostata di Marmellata

Crostata di Marmellata

Italy's classic jam tart, a buttery shortcrust pastry filled generously with fruit preserve and topped with a simple lattice, a homely dessert found in nearly every Italian household.

Crostata is often the very first dessert Italian children learn to bake alongside a parent or grandparent, its forgiving pastry and simple lattice top making it an accessible, endlessly adaptable classic depending on whatever jam is on hand.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Tart pan, about 24cm

  • 300 g (2¼ cups) 00 flour
  • 150 g (⅔ cup) cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 120 g (⅔ cup) granulated sugar
  • 2 egg yolks, plus 1 whole egg
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 350 g (1¼ cups) fruit jam or preserve (such as apricot or sour cherry)

Method

  1. Combine the flour, cold butter, sugar, and salt, working quickly with your fingertips or a pastry cutter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  2. Add the egg yolks and lemon zest, bringing the dough together quickly without overworking it.
  3. Shape into a disc, wrap, and rest in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Roll out two-thirds of the dough and line a tart pan, trimming the edges.
  5. Spread the jam evenly over the base.
  6. Roll out the remaining dough and cut into strips, arranging them in a lattice pattern over the jam.
  7. Brush the lattice lightly with beaten egg.
  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the pastry is golden.
  9. Cool completely before slicing.

Chef Tips

  • Keep the butter cold and handle the dough as little as possible for the most tender, flaky pastry.
  • A good-quality, not overly sweet jam works best, as the pastry itself is already sweetened.
  • This is a wonderfully forgiving dessert — an uneven lattice is entirely part of its homely charm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overworking the pastry dough, which develops gluten and results in a tough rather than tender crust.
  • Using a very runny jam, which can make the tart difficult to slice cleanly.
  • Slicing before the tart has fully cooled, when the jam filling is still too soft.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store covered at room temperature for up to 4 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Espresso, A dusting of powdered sugar
