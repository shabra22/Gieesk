🍗 Ethiopian Spatchcock Chicken

ዶሮ ቀስቅሳ — Doro Qesqesa

Ethiopian Spatchcock Grilled Chicken — a whole chicken butterflied, marinated in berbere and kibbeh for 4 hours, then grilled flat over charcoal until the berbere skin caramelises magnificently. The Ethiopian outdoor grill tradition applied to a whole chicken.

Spatchcock berbere chicken combines two great cooking traditions — the Ethiopian berbere marinade and the charcoal grill. The flattened chicken cooks more evenly and faster, and the charcoal adds a smokiness that the oven cannot produce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Kitchen scissors or knife for spatchcocking, Charcoal grill, Heavy weight for pressing

  • 1 whole chicken — spatchcocked (backbone removed, flattened)
  • 4 tbsp berbere
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 5 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Remove backbone with scissors. Flatten chicken by pressing on breastbone.
  2. Mix marinade. Score chicken deeply. Push marinade under skin and into all surfaces.
  3. Marinate 4 hours minimum.
  4. Grill over medium charcoal: 15—18 minutes per side.
  5. Press with a heavy pan or foil-wrapped brick for the first 10 minutes.
  6. Internal temperature must reach 74°C at the thickest point.
  7. Rest 10 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The pressing weight is important — it ensures contact with the grill and even cooking.
  • Medium charcoal — berbere burns at high heat.
  • Spatchcocking halves the cooking time of a whole chicken.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat.
  • Not checking internal temperature.

The Science

Spatchcocking removes the thermal mass of the backbone cavity — the flattened chicken has a more uniform thickness, meaning all parts reach the safe internal temperature (74°C) at approximately the same time. This eliminates the common problem of overcooked breast and undercooked thigh.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian spatchcock berbere chicken is what the country's modern restaurants serve for outdoor celebrations — the charcoal grill, the berbere marinade, and the whole flattened chicken combine in a dish of pure theatrical impact.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Oven at 180°C.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ayib (ETH096), Fresh salad
