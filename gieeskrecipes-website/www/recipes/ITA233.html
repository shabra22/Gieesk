🥮 Gubana Friulana

Gubana

Friuli's spiral-coiled sweet bread from the Natisone Valleys, filled with a rich blend of walnuts, raisins, pine nuts, and grappa-soaked dried fruit, traditionally sliced and served with a glass of the same grappa.

Gubana shares its rolled, coiled construction with Calabria's pitta 'nchiusa, though its filling, heavy with walnuts and grappa-soaked fruit, is distinctly Friulian, reflecting the Slavic-influenced culinary traditions of the mountainous Natisone Valleys near the Slovenian border.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Round baking pan

  • 500 g (4 cups) 00 flour
  • 7 g (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 150 ml (⅔ cup) warm milk
  • 100 g (½ cup) granulated sugar
  • 100 g (7 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 eggs
  • 250 g (2 cups) walnuts, chopped
  • 100 g (⅔ cup) raisins
  • 50 g (⅓ cup) pine nuts
  • 3 tbsp grappa
  • 80 g (⅓ cup) granulated sugar, for the filling
  • Zest of 1 lemon

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk and let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, sugar, eggs, and yeast mixture into a dough, then gradually work in the softened butter until smooth and elastic.
  3. Let rise, covered, in a warm place for about 2 hours, until doubled.
  4. Soak the raisins in the grappa for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Combine the walnuts, drained raisins, pine nuts, sugar, and lemon zest into a thick filling, adding a splash of the grappa soaking liquid.
  6. Roll the risen dough into a large, thin rectangle.
  7. Spread the filling evenly over the dough, leaving a small border.
  8. Roll up tightly from one long edge into a log, then coil into a spiral and place in a round baking pan.
  9. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 35–40 minutes until deeply golden.
  10. Cool before slicing.

Chef Tips

  • Soaking the raisins in grappa specifically, rather than water, is what gives gubana its distinctive character.
  • Roll the dough thin and the filled log tightly for the most attractive spiral pattern once sliced.
  • This bread is traditionally served with a small glass of the same grappa used in the filling.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the grappa soak, missing an essential part of the traditional flavor.
  • Rolling the dough too thick, resulting in a less distinct spiral once sliced.
  • Underbaking, leaving the enriched, filled dough undercooked at the center.

Cultural Note

Gubana is a treasured specialty of Friuli's Natisone Valleys near the Slovenian border, reflecting the region's blended Slavic and Italian culinary heritage.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 5 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: A glass of grappa, Espresso
