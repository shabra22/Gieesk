🥔 Tanzanian Coconut Potato Curry

Viazi vya Nazi

Tanzanian Viazi vya Nazi — potatoes simmered in a fragrant coconut milk and Zanzibar spice sauce until completely tender and infused with the richness of the coast. The coastal Tanzanian potato dish — coconut milk transforms ordinary potatoes into something extraordinary.

Viazi vya Nazi is what the Swahili coast does with potatoes — simmers them in coconut milk with Zanzibar spices until they absorb the fragrant, rich sauce completely. The result is a potato dish of unusual depth and richness.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g potatoes — peeled and cubed
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, spices. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes until thick.
  4. Add potato cubes. Stir to coat.
  5. Pour in coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Simmer covered 18—20 minutes until potatoes are completely tender.
  7. Some potato starch will naturally thicken the sauce.
  8. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Potatoes naturally thicken the coconut sauce — no flour needed.
  • Do not stir too aggressively once potatoes are nearly tender — they break.
  • Never boil the coconut milk hard.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling coconut milk.
  • Over-stirring with tender potatoes.

The Science

Potato starch dissolves into the coconut milk during cooking — creating a naturally thickened, slightly glossy sauce. The coconut fat simultaneously coats the potato surface, creating the characteristic rich, creamy result.

Cultural Note

Viazi vya Nazi demonstrates the Swahili coast's most consistent cooking philosophy — add coconut milk to anything and make it magnificent. The humble potato becomes a feast food through this simple transformation.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004), Any grilled fish or meat
