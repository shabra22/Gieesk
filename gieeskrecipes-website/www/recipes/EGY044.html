🥗 Salatet Bangar (Egyptian Roasted Beet Salad)

Salatet bangar — سلطة بنجر

Beets wrapped and roasted until easily pierced, cooled, diced and tossed with cumin-garlic lime dressing and pickled red onions.

1h 45mTotal time
15mPrep
1hCook
4Servings
140Calories
EasyDifficulty

Fresh beets are wrapped in parchment and foil and roasted for 1 hour at 375°F, or until easily pierced, cooled completely and diced. They are dressed with olive oil, vinegar, lime, cumin and garlic and topped with quick pickled red onions.

Ingredients

Equipment: Parchment and foil, Baking tray, Small jar

  • 2 fresh beets
  • 2 tablespoons pickled red onions, recipe follows
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon white distilled vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 12 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon minced garlic
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Pickled onions

  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons white distilled vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • ½ teaspoon Himalayan salt
  • 12 teaspoon ground black pepper

Method

  1. Wrap the beets in parchment then foil and roast for 1 hour at 375°F or until you can easily pierce them. Cool completely.
  2. Pickled onions: toss the sliced red onion with the vinegar, lime juice, salt and pepper and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
  3. Dice the beets (¼ inch on the card), toss with the oil, vinegar, lime juice, cumin, garlic, salt and pepper and top with the pickled onions.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: beets easily pierced. The onion rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least 30 minutes) and is a quick onion pickle, not a ferment.
  • The page misprints the cumin as '12 teaspoon' (probably ½ teaspoon) and the pepper in the pickle likewise; and disagrees on oven time and dice size (card ¼ inch, body 1/8 inch).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dicing before the beets cool, which bleeds and bruises them.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate airtight up to 2 days.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Aish baladi
