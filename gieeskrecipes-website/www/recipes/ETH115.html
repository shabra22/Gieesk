🥬 Ethiopian Cabbage Alicha

ጥቅል ጎመን አልጫ — Tikil Gomen Alicha

Ethiopian Cabbage Alicha — plain cabbage cooked with onion, oil, and a pinch of turmeric in the mildest possible Ethiopian vegetable preparation. The truly mild element of the fasting platter — for children, those new to Ethiopian food, and to provide balance.

Cabbage Alicha is the simplest Ethiopian vegetable dish — just cabbage, onion, oil, and a touch of turmeric. Its role on the platter is balance: where everything else has berbere or complexity, this provides pure, gentle simplicity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan

  • ½ head white cabbage — roughly chopped
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic and turmeric. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add cabbage. Toss.
  4. Cook covered 12—15 minutes until tender.
  5. Season.

Chef Tips

  • The simplest possible preparation — do not add more.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding berbere — not alicha anymore.

Cultural Note

Cabbage alicha is the inclusivity dish of the Ethiopian table — mild enough for the youngest child and the most heat-sensitive guest. Its simplicity is a virtue.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: On the fasting platter, For children on the non-fasting table
