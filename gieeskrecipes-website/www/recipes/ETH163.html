🫘 Ethiopian Roasted Chickpeas

ሽምብራ ቆሎ — Shimbra Qolo

Ethiopian Roasted Chickpeas — soaked chickpeas dry-roasted until golden, crunchy, and nutty. Part of the Qolo tradition with barley (ETH018) — eaten at coffee ceremonies, at markets, and throughout fasting periods as the primary protein snack.

Roasted chickpeas are the protein backbone of the Ethiopian snack tradition — eaten alongside or mixed with roasted barley (as part of full Qolo), they provide high-quality plant protein in a completely natural, unprocessed snack.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy dry pan or oven at 200°C

  • 2 cups dried chickpeas — soaked overnight
  • 1 tsp salt

Optional flavourings: berbere or mitmita for spiced version

    Method

    1. Drain soaked chickpeas. Rinse. Spread on a cloth and dry completely (minimum 1 hour).
    2. Pan method: heat dry pan over medium heat. Add chickpeas.
    3. Roast stirring constantly 20—25 minutes until golden-brown and crunchy.
    4. Oven method: toss dried chickpeas with ½ tsp oil. Spread on baking sheet. Roast at 200°C 25—30 minutes, shaking halfway.
    5. Season with salt while still hot.
    6. Cool completely before eating — they crisp further.

    Chef Tips

    • Complete drying before roasting is essential — wet chickpeas steam and become tough.
    • Cool before tasting — they taste soft when hot but crisp fully as they cool.
    • The pan method produces more even results than the oven.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not drying thoroughly.
    • Eating while hot — seem soft.

    The Science

    See ETH138 for roasted chickpea science. The oven method produces a slightly different result from pan-roasting — the dry oven heat dehydrates more evenly from all sides, while pan-roasting creates direct Maillard contact on one side at a time.

    Cultural Note

    Roasted chickpeas are Ethiopia's most democratic snack — available to everyone, from the highland farmer to the city schoolchild. A handful of roasted chickpeas passed between friends is the simplest expression of Ethiopian social sharing.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Airtight container — 2 weeks.

    Reheating: Not needed.

    Serve with: Mixed with roasted barley as Qolo (ETH018), At coffee ceremonies, As a fasting snack
