🐟 Ndomba Oka (Fish Steamed in Banana Leaf)

Ndomba oka

Fresh fish marinated 15 minutes with green condiments, onion, chilli and garlic, wrapped in wilted banana leaves over odzom leaves, boiled about 1 hour on a plate, then finished 30 minutes on embers.

A fish parcel from Afrik. The fish is cleaned and cut; green condiments and onions are chopped over it; chilli, garlic and white pepper are crushed with a little water and added; salt, and marinate about 15 minutes. The banana leaves are passed over a flame and the rib removed. Odzom leaves go on the leaf, then the fish, tied. The parcel sits on a plate in a pot with a little water so it does not sit in the water, closed and boiled about 1 hour, then finished on barbecue embers about 30 minutes ‘so the remaining water evaporates’. Served hot.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid, Plate, Barbecue embers

  • 5 very fresh fish
  • 4 banana leaves, well cut
  • A few odzom leaves
  • Ossim nnam — quantity not printed
  • Messep — quantity not printed
  • A few basil leaves
  • Garlic — quantity not printed
  • Chilli — quantity not printed
  • Onions — in the method, not in the list
  • Salt and white pepper — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Clean and cut the fish. Chop the green condiments and onions over the fish.
  2. Crush the chilli, garlic and white pepper with a little water. Add them with salt and marinate about 15 minutes.
  3. Pass the banana leaves over a flame and remove the central rib.
  4. Lay the odzom leaves on a leaf, then the fish, and tie the parcel.
  5. Stand the parcel on a plate in a pot with a little water, so it is not in the water. Close and boil about 1 hour.
  6. Finish on barbecue embers about 30 minutes so the remaining water evaporates. Serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cue is the parcel’s smell: the odzom has ‘une odeur toute particulière’.
  • Keep the parcel on a plate so it steams above the water.
  • Quantities for several condiments are not printed — season by taste.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the parcel sit in the water.
  • Skipping the embers finish.
  • Not removing the leaf rib, which can tear the parcel.
