Cicoria Ripassata

Boiled chicory greens quickly re-sautéed in garlic and chili-infused olive oil, a beloved simple side dish that pairs the vegetable's earthy bitterness with just enough richness.

The technique of boiling greens first and then 'passing' them a second time through hot oil and garlic (ripassare, literally 'to pass again') is a defining method across Italian home cooking, applied here to Rome's beloved chicory.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large sauté pan

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) chicory or dandelion greens, trimmed
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 dried chilies, crumbled
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the chicory for 8-10 minutes until tender.
  2. Drain well, pressing out excess water.
  3. Warm the olive oil in a large pan and sauté the garlic and chilies until fragrant, taking care not to burn the garlic.
  4. Add the drained chicory and toss to coat, cooking for 5 minutes until well combined and lightly crisped at the edges.
  5. Season with salt and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Pressing out excess water from the boiled greens before the second sauté helps them crisp slightly rather than steam.
  • A generous hand with garlic and chili is traditional and helps balance the greens' natural bitterness.
  • This technique works well with many bitter greens beyond chicory, including cime di rapa or dandelion greens.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not draining the boiled greens well enough, resulting in a watery rather than lightly crisped final dish.
  • Burning the garlic in the hot oil, which turns bitter.
  • Overcooking the greens in the initial boil, losing their texture entirely.

Health Benefits

  • Chicory is rich in fiber and has traditionally been valued for its digestive properties.

Cultural Note

Cicoria ripassata is a staple side dish throughout Lazio and much of central and southern Italy, the boil-then-sauté technique a fundamental method in Italian vegetable cookery.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently in a pan with a little olive oil.

Serve with: Grilled meats or sausages, Crusty bread
