🐟 Thiou Boulettes, Fish Balls in Vegetable Sauce

Thiou boulettes de poisson

White-fish balls bound with cornflour and peanut butter, fried golden, then warmed on a tomato sauce and a pot of potato, sweet potato, carrot and cabbage.

2hTotal time
50mPrep
1h 10mCook
6Servings
430Calories
MediumDifficulty

A home-cook version from La Tendresse en Cuisine: 600 g of white fish (pollock) minced with an egg, onion, garlic, a spoon of peanut butter, harissa standing in for the chile, and a spoon of cornflour, fried until golden all over. The sauce is tomato, concentrate, onion and garlic with the oil from the boulettes; the vegetables cook about an hour, stirred every quarter of an hour, then the sauce is blended to a thick consistency. The fish balls rest on the vegetables ten minutes to warm through.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor or blender, Frying pan, Large pot, Immersion blender or blender for the sauce

    Fish balls

    • 600 g white fish fillets (the cook used pollock)
    • 1 egg
    • 1 small onion
    • 3 garlic cloves
    • 1 tbsp peanut butter
    • 1 level tbsp harissa (used in place of chile)
    • 1/2 tsp pepper
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1 tbsp cornflour
    • Neutral oil for frying — quantity not printed

    Sauce and vegetables

    • 2 large tomatoes
    • 1 heaping tbsp tomato concentrate
    • 1 large onion
    • 3 garlic cloves
    • 1/2 tsp pepper
    • 1 level tsp harissa
    • 3 tbsp of the frying oil from the fish balls
    • 1 large potato
    • 1/2 small white cabbage
    • 1 small carrot
    • 1 sweet potato
    • Salt to taste

    Method

    1. Process the fish with the egg, onion, garlic, peanut butter, harissa, pepper, salt and cornflour into a paste with no visible pieces.
    2. Shape into balls and fry in neutral oil until well golden on every side. Keep the frying oil.
    3. For the sauce, cook the tomatoes, concentrate, onion, garlic, pepper and harissa in 3 tablespoons of the frying oil.
    4. Add the potato, cabbage, carrot and sweet potato with water as needed and cook about 1 hour, stirring every quarter hour, until the vegetables are cooked and tender.
    5. Blend part of the pot to make a thick sauce.
    6. Lay the fish balls on the vegetables and warm them for 10 minutes. Season to taste.

    Chef Tips

    • Process the fish until ‘there are no visible pieces left’ — a smooth paste holds the ball together.
    • Fry ‘until well golden on all sides’; they finish cooking in the sauce afterwards.
    • Keep 3 tablespoons of the frying oil; it carries the fish flavour into the sauce.
    • Harissa is the cook’s substitution for chile, and she says so.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Leaving fish pieces in the mix so the balls break.
    • Adding the balls to the pot earlier than the last 10 minutes.
    • Throwing away the frying oil.

    Cultural Note

    Neither version of thiou boulettes read for this collection ties the dish to Saint-Louis, despite the association made elsewhere; a second printing under a ‘196 flavors’ site byline differs on the fish, the binder and the sauce and is truncated mid-method, so it is not published.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

    Reheating: Gentle heat, covered.

    Serve with: White rice
