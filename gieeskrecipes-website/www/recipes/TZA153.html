🥤 Tanzanian Sugarcane Juice

Juisi ya Miwa

Tanzanian Sugarcane Juice — freshly pressed sugarcane juice with a squeeze of lime and a pinch of ginger. The most refreshing drink in Tanzania — the sugarcane juice pressed on demand by hand or machine at every Zanzibar market corner.

Sugarcane juice is the universal Tanzanian street drink — present at every market, every bus station, every beach. In Zanzibar, the pressed juice is sometimes served with ginger and lime, making it one of the most refreshing drinks in East Africa.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sugarcane press or heavy knife and blender

  • 6—8 sticks fresh sugarcane — peeled and cut
  • 1 lime — juice
  • ½ tsp fresh ginger — grated
  • Ice

Method

  1. If using a press: feed cane through the press. Collect juice.
  2. If using a blender: chop cane finely, blend with a little water, strain through a cloth.
  3. Add lime juice and ginger.
  4. Serve immediately over ice.

Chef Tips

  • Sugarcane juice oxidises quickly — drink within 30 minutes.
  • Lime and ginger are the Zanzibari additions.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not drinking quickly — juice turns sour.

The Science

Fresh sugarcane juice is particularly susceptible to oxidation — the polyphenol oxidase enzyme in the juice rapidly oxidises phenolic compounds when exposed to air. The citric acid from lime partially inhibits this enzyme, extending the juice's freshness.

Cultural Note

Sugarcane juice from a Zanzibar market press — watched over ice with lime and ginger on a hot afternoon — is one of the island's greatest simple pleasures. It is the drink that makes the afternoon bearable.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.
