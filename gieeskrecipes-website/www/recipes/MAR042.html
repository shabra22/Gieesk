🥕 Harira with Carrot-Potato Thickener

Harira (حريرة) — vegetable-thickened

A pressure-cooker harira of chickpeas, beef bones, lentils, herbs and tomatoes, thickened with a puree of boiled carrot, potato and flour.

2h 20mTotal time
30mPrep
1h 20mCook
6Servings
330Calories
MediumDifficulty

This version appears as a recipe credited to a Rutgers linguistic anthropologist in a university alumni magazine. Onion, celery and tomatoes are pureed and pressure-cooked with chickpeas and beef bones for 1 hour; carrots and potatoes are boiled and pureed with flour as the thickener; lentils soaked for 30 minutes join with the herbs for 15 to 20 minutes until soft; the puree and tomato paste go in for the final 5 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker, Separate pot, Blender

  • 2 c. chickpeas, soaked the night before in water
  • 2 beef bones or 1/4 lb. small beef chunks
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 c. chopped Chinese celery or regular celery
  • 4 large tomatoes, peeled and seeds removed
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp. ground ginger
  • I Tbsp. pepper (printed "I Tbsp.")
  • 2 Tbsp. salt
  • 4 c. water
  • 1 large carrot, peeled
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled
  • 1 tsp. lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp. flour
  • 1 c. brown lentils
  • 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp. tomato paste
  • 1 tsp. salt (for boiling the vegetables)

Method

  1. Puree the onion, celery and tomatoes.
  2. Pour into the pressure cooker with the chickpeas, beef bones or chunks, ginger, salt and pepper. Add 4 cups water.
  3. Close the cooker and cook on medium-low heat for 1 hour. Release the pressure.
  4. In a separate pot, boil the carrot and potatoes in water with 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp lemon juice until tender. Drain and puree with the flour.
  5. Soak the lentils in boiling hot water for 30 minutes, rinse, and add to the pressure cooker with the parsley and cilantro.
  6. Cook uncovered 15 to 20 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  7. Add the pureed vegetables and tomato paste, cook another 5 minutes, stirring constantly, and serve warm with bread.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cues: carrot and potato tender; lentils soft.
  • The pepper line is printed with a capital I for the numeral 1 and is read here as 1 Tbsp.
  • This uses a carrot-potato-flour puree rather than the usual flour-and-water tedouira.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Opening the cooker before releasing pressure.
  • Skipping the stirring after the puree is added.
  • Over-reducing the soup.

Cultural Note

The unsigned headnote says harira is often used to break the fast during Ramadan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 2 to 3 days in the fridge, per the page.

Reheating: Reheat gently, loosening with water.

Serve with: Khobz
