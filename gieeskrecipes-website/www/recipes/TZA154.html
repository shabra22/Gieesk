🦐 Coastal Prawn Biryani

Biriani ya Kamba ya Pwani

Coastal Prawn Biryani — a magnificent seafood biryani with prawn shell stock-cooked rice, large sautéed prawns, fried onions, saffron, and the full Zanzibar spice array, sealed dum-style. The grandest seafood celebration rice of the Swahili coast.

Prawn biryani is the Swahili coast's grandest seafood celebration — the technique of biryani applied to the Indian Ocean's finest prawn. The prawn shell stock gives the rice an extraordinary depth, and the dum sealing creates a fragrance that announces a great occasion.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large dum pot, Foil seal, Heat diffuser

  • 600g large prawns — peeled, shells reserved
  • 4 cups basmati rice — parboiled 70%
  • 2 onions — fried deep golden
  • ½ tsp saffron in warm water
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • ¼ cup coriander

Prawn Marinade

  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt

Prawn Stock: shells + 4 cups water + 1 onion + 2 garlic + 1 tsp salt

    Method

    1. Marinate prawns in yogurt-spice mixture 30 minutes.
    2. Make prawn stock: simmer shells in water with onion and garlic 20 minutes. Strain.
    3. Parboil rice in prawn stock to 70%.
    4. Sauté marinated prawns in ghee 3—4 minutes. Set aside.
    5. Layer: half rice, half fried onions, sautéed prawns, remaining rice, saffron water, remaining ghee, remaining onions.
    6. Seal with foil. Dum over lowest heat 20 minutes.
    7. Break seal at table. Garnish with coriander.

    Chef Tips

    • The prawn stock is liquid gold — do not skip.
    • Prawns are pre-cooked — they only need 3—4 minutes. Do not dum raw prawns.
    • Break the seal at the table for the fragrance ceremony.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Undercooking prawns before the dum.
    • Discarding prawn shells.

    Cultural Note

    Prawn biryani is Zanzibar's most spectacular celebration rice — when the dum seal is broken at the table and the fragrance of prawn, saffron, and spice escapes into the room, every person present knows something magnificent has been prepared.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Covered with splash of water.

    Serve with: Raita, Kachumbari (TZA108), Chatni ya Nazi (TZA118)
