Pizza ai Funghi e Salsiccia

A hearty, rustic pizza topped with crumbled Italian sausage and sautéed mushrooms over melted mozzarella, a popular alternative to the classic Margherita in pizzerias across Italy.

The combination of savory sausage and earthy mushrooms makes this one of the most requested non-classic pizzas in Italy, the two toppings complementing each other over a base of good tomato and properly stretched mozzarella.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large mixing bowl, Pizza stone or steel, Pizza peel, Skillet

  • 500 g (4 cups) 00 flour or bread flour
  • 325 ml (1⅓ cups) water, room temperature
  • 10 g (1¾ tsp) fine sea salt
  • 2 g (½ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) canned San Marzano tomatoes, crushed by hand
  • 250 g (9 oz) fresh mozzarella, torn
  • 250 g (9 oz) Italian sausage, casings removed and crumbled
  • 200 g (7 oz) mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the water, then gradually mix in the flour and salt to form a shaggy dough.
  2. Knead for 10 minutes, place in an oiled bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature for 2 hours.
  3. Divide into 4 balls, shape into tight rounds, and refrigerate covered for at least 8 hours, ideally 24.
  4. Bring the dough to room temperature before baking, and preheat the oven with a stone or steel to its highest setting for a full hour.
  5. Cook the crumbled sausage in a skillet until browned, then set aside.
  6. In the same skillet, sauté the mushrooms with garlic and a little rosemary until golden.
  7. Stretch a dough ball by hand into a round and spread with a thin layer of crushed tomatoes.
  8. Dot with mozzarella, then scatter the cooked sausage and mushrooms evenly over the top.
  9. Slide onto the hot stone and bake for 8–12 minutes until the crust is puffed and charred in spots.
  10. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Pre-cooking both the sausage and mushrooms prevents the pizza from becoming watery or underdone in the center.
  • Break the sausage into small, irregular crumbles rather than large chunks for even distribution and cooking.
  • A well-seasoned, fennel-forward Italian sausage makes a noticeable difference in flavor.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding raw sausage directly to the pizza, which won't cook through properly in the short bake time.
  • Using watery, unsautéed mushrooms, which can make the crust soggy.
  • Overloading the pizza, preventing the center from crisping properly.

Cultural Note

Sausage and mushroom pizza is a widely loved combination across Italian pizzerias, a hearty, satisfying alternative that has become a menu staple nationwide.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh; leftover pizza keeps refrigerated for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot skillet or oven; avoid the microwave.

Serve with: A simple arugula salad, A medium-bodied red wine
