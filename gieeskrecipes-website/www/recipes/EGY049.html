🥟 Sambousek (Cheese-Filled Buttery Pastries)

Sambousek bil-gebna — سمبوسك بالجبنة

A butter-and-oil dough chilled at least 30 minutes, filled with feta, Parmesan and egg, crimped and baked until golden.

1h 25mTotal time
30mPrep
25mCook
5Servings
290Calories
EasyDifficulty

Melted butter, oil, hot water and salt are mixed with flour into a dough that chills at least 30 minutes. It is cut into 10 pieces rolled to about 4 inches, filled with a tablespoon of feta, Parmesan, egg and baking powder mixture, folded, sealed and crimped, and baked at 375°F (190°C) for about 25 minutes, until golden brown.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Baking sheet, Rolling pin

    Pastry

    • 1/4 cup or 55g melted butter
    • 1/4 cup or 60ml canola oil
    • 1/4 cup or 60ml hot water
    • Pinch of salt
    • 1 1/2 cups or 355g flour plus extra for rolling out dough

    Filling

    • 5 1/2 oz 156g feta cheese
    • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
    • 1 egg
    • 2 teaspoons baking powder
    • Black pepper

    Method

    1. Mix the butter, oil, hot water and salt, add the flour and knead a few minutes. Chill at least 30 minutes.
    2. Mix the filling by hand; a food processor made it runny.
    3. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
    4. Cut the dough into 10 pieces and roll each to a circle of about 4 inches. Put about 1 tablespoon of filling in each, fold, seal and crimp.
    5. Bake about 25 minutes, or until golden brown.

    Chef Tips

    • Cue: golden brown, light and flaky. The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
    • The yield of 10 pastries is stated through the dough division; servings are our estimate.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Overfilling so the pastries open during baking.

    Reheating: Warm in a moderate oven.

    Serve with: Salad
