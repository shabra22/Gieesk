🍯 Almond Briouats (Honey-Dipped Almond Pastries)

Briouat bil louz (بريوات باللوز)

Triangles of crisp warqa pastry filled with a spiced almond paste, fried until light golden and soaked in warm honey and orange flower water.

3hTotal time
2hPrep
1hCook
50Servings
210Calories
HardDifficulty

Whole almonds are blanched, fried in oil until light to medium golden brown (about 5 minutes per batch), ground and mixed with sugar, cinnamon, salt, mastic, butter and orange flower water. Warqa strips are filled, folded into triangles (four or five flips is enough), sealed with beaten egg yolk and butter, and fried until light golden, usually 5 to 7 minutes. The briouats are dipped in hot, slightly foamy honey scented with orange flower water.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep pot for frying, Food processor, Pastry brush

  • 35 oz whole raw almonds
  • 2 cups vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon, or more to taste
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon mastic gum powder (about 3 grains, crushed)
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/3 cup orange flower water
  • 2.2 lbs warqa
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 egg yolks, lightly beaten
  • 4 cups vegetable oil, for frying
  • 45 oz honey, mild-flavored such as Eucalyptus
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons orange flower water, pure (avoid imitation)

Method

  1. Blanch the almonds: return the water to a boil for one or two minutes, then drain and peel.
  2. Fry the almonds in the oil until light to medium golden brown; each batch takes about 5 minutes provided the oil is not too hot. Drain, cool and grind.
  3. Mix the ground almonds with the sugar, cinnamon, salt, mastic, softened butter and the 1/3 cup orange flower water to a paste.
  4. Cut the warqa into strips (for cherry-sized balls, use a 5 x 30 cm strip), fill and fold into triangles, four or five flips of the triangle are sufficient, and seal the end with egg yolk mixed with melted butter.
  5. Heat the honey with a tablespoon of orange flower water until quite hot and slightly foamy on top, then remove from the heat.
  6. Fry the briouats in the oil until light golden brown, usually 5 to 7 minutes. Dip them in the hot honey and drain.

Chef Tips

  • Cues quoted: almonds light to medium golden brown; briouats light golden brown; honey quite hot and slightly foamy.
  • The ingredient list says 45 oz honey but the method heats 1 kg; reported as printed.
  • Stored at room temperature they keep a month, says the page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Oil that is too hot (almonds burn, briouats darken).
  • Sealing poorly so honey leaks in.
  • Using imitation orange flower water.

Cultural Note

The author writes these pastries are popular at tea time and at Ramadan, served alongside chebakia and sellou.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps about a month at room temperature (per the page).

Reheating: Not reheated.

Serve with: Mint tea, Chebakia
