🥣 Cameroon Okra Egusi Soup

Okra and egusi soup

Meat boiled until tender, a ground-egusi paste cooked 10 minutes, smoked fish, crayfish and seasoning, and chopped okra added last so it stays green and vibrant.

40 minTotal time
10 minPrep
30 minCook
4Servings
420Calories
EasyDifficulty

Precious Nkeih’s ‘the Cameroonian way’ okra egusi soup starts with boiling meat until tender, seasoning halfway with salt and a quarter of the chopped onions. The ground egusi paste is added and cooked 10 minutes, smoked fish another 5 to 10 minutes, then the seasoning cubes, salt and crayfish simmer two minutes. Okra goes in for 2 to 5 minutes; the page says it should still look green and vibrant. Served with cooked garri or any fufu of choice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot

  • 1/2 pound okra (about 1/4 kg), finely chopped or roughly blended
  • 1 cup egusi (melon seeds), ground
  • 1 pound fish, meat or both (smoked fish and beef used)
  • 1 large crayfish seasoning cube (Maggi Crevette) or 3 small seasoning cubes
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup crayfish
  • Salt
  • 1 hot pepper (habanero), optional

Method

  1. Boil the meat until tender. Halfway through, season with salt and a quarter of the chopped onions. (The boiling time is not printed.)
  2. Add the ground egusi paste and cook for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the smoked fish and cook another 5 to 10 minutes.
  4. Add the seasoning cubes, salt and crayfish and simmer for two minutes.
  5. Add the okra and cook 2 to 5 minutes. It should still look green and vibrant.
  6. Serve with cooked garri or any fufu of choice.

Chef Tips

  • The page’s cue for the okra is that it should still look green and vibrant — do not overcook it.
  • Printed cue for the meat: ‘until tender’.
  • Three-quarters of the onion is not used in the printed method; add it as you see fit and note the gap.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the okra.
  • Adding the okra before the egusi has cooked its 10 minutes.
  • Boiling the meat too little before seasoning.
