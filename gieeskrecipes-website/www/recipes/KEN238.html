🥭 Kenyan Mango Cheesecake

Cheesecake ya Embe

Kenyan Mango Cheesecake — a no-bake cream cheese cheesecake infused with fresh mango puree, on a golden biscuit base, topped with mango jelly and fresh mango slices. Kenya's most tropical no-bake dessert — vivid, fragrant, and beautifully golden.

Mango cheesecake is the golden counterpart to the passion fruit version — where passion fruit brings tartness and drama, mango brings sweetness and tropical warmth. The mango is incorporated into both the filling and the topping, creating an intensely mango dessert of extraordinary colour.

Ingredients

Equipment: 20cm springform tin, Electric beater, Blender

    Base

    • 250g digestive biscuits — crushed
    • 120g butter — melted

    Mango Filling

    • 500g cream cheese — room temperature
    • 1 cup fresh mango puree — from 2 large ripe mangoes
    • 200ml whipping cream
    • 4 tbsp sugar
    • 2 tsp gelatin dissolved in 3 tbsp warm water
    • 1 tsp vanilla
    • 1 tbsp lime juice

    Mango Topping

    • 1 cup mango puree
    • 1 tsp agar agar
    • 1 tbsp sugar
    • Fresh mango slices for decoration

    Method

    1. Make base: crush biscuits, mix with melted butter, press into tin. Refrigerate.
    2. Make mango filling: blend mango puree until smooth. Beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
    3. Fold in mango puree, lime juice, dissolved gelatin, and vanilla.
    4. Whip cream to soft peaks. Fold into mango cream cheese mixture.
    5. Pour over base. Refrigerate 4 hours.
    6. Make mango topping: heat mango puree with agar agar and sugar until dissolved. Cool to room temperature.
    7. Pour gently over set cheesecake. Refrigerate 1 hour.
    8. Decorate with fresh mango slices.

    Chef Tips

    • Use the ripest, most fragrant mangoes available — the flavour of the cheesecake is entirely dependent on the mango.
    • Cool the mango topping to room temperature before adding — warm liquid melts the filling.
    • Agar agar for the topping sets firmer than gelatin — gives a beautiful glassy surface.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Under-ripe mango — pale, flavourless cheesecake.
    • Warm topping — melts the filling.

    The Science

    See KEN234 for cheesecake setting science. The mango puree's natural pectins interact with the cream cheese proteins to create additional structure beyond what the gelatin alone provides — explaining why mango cheesecake often sets more firmly than plain cream cheese.

    Cultural Note

    Mango cheesecake represents Nairobi's bakery scene at its most tropical — a dessert that would be impossible to improve upon anywhere in the world except Kenya, where the mangoes grow to extraordinary quality right on the doorstep.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate for up to 4 days.

    Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

    Serve with: Iced Kenyan Coffee, Festival Fruit Punch
