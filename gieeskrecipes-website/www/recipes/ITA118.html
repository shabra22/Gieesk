🍚 Supplì al Telefono

Supplì al Telefono

Rome's answer to arancini, oval rice croquettes hiding a core of melting mozzarella that stretches into long strings when broken open — hence 'telephone wire' in the name.

Unlike Sicilian arancini, supplì are typically simpler, built from tomato-based risotto with just a mozzarella center rather than a full meat filling, and their fried, oval shape is a familiar sight in Roman pizzerias as a starter alongside pizza al taglio.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan for risotto, Deep pot for frying

  • 350 g (1¾ cups) Arborio rice
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 L (4¼ cups) chicken or vegetable stock
  • 100 ml (scant ½ cup) dry white wine
  • 60 g (¾ cup) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 200 g (7 oz) mozzarella, cut into small batons
  • 150 g (1½ cups) breadcrumbs
  • Flour, for dredging
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a wide pan and sauté the onion until softened.
  2. Add the rice and toast for a minute, then add the wine and let it absorb.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, then begin adding the hot stock gradually, stirring frequently, risotto-style, for about 18 minutes until the rice is tender and sticky.
  4. Stir in the Parmigiano, season, and spread onto a tray to cool completely, ideally chilled for at least 2 hours.
  5. Take a portion of the cooled rice in your palm, press a mozzarella baton into the center, and mold the rice around it into an oval shape, sealing well.
  6. Dredge each supplì in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs.
  7. Heat oil to 170°C (340°F) and fry the supplì until deeply golden, about 5–6 minutes, turning as needed.
  8. Drain and let rest briefly before serving hot, when the mozzarella is at its most stretchy.

Chef Tips

  • Make sure the rice is thoroughly chilled before shaping — this is what allows it to hold together properly.
  • Seal the rice completely around the mozzarella so it doesn't leak out during frying.
  • Serve hot and fresh from the fryer for the best "telephone wire" cheese pull when broken open.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Shaping with warm rice, which falls apart rather than holding its oval form.
  • Leaving gaps in the rice coating, allowing the mozzarella to leak into the frying oil.
  • Letting them sit too long before serving, when the cheese pull is at its most dramatic straight from the fryer.

Health Benefits

  • A good source of protein and calcium from the cheese and eggs, best enjoyed as an occasional treat given the frying.

Cultural Note

Supplì are a beloved Roman street food, traditionally sold in pizzerias and takeaway shops across the city as a classic starter or snack.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate uncooked, shaped supplì for up to 1 day, or freeze for up to 1 month.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot oven to re-crisp; avoid the microwave.

Serve with: Pizza al taglio, A simple side salad
