☕ Kaffa Forest Coffee Preparation

ካፋ ቡና — Kaffa Buna

Kaffa Forest Coffee — wild forest-gathered Ethiopian arabica prepared in the traditional Kaffa way: coffee berries roasted in a clay pot with butter and salt, then either chewed whole or the seeds brewed in a clay vessel. The oldest coffee preparation on earth — from the people who first used coffee.

Kaffa forest coffee is the oldest coffee tradition — before the jebena, before the ceremony, before roasting and brewing as we know it, the Kaffa people chewed the whole coffee berry mixed with butter and salt. This preparation remains the most direct connection to the origins of coffee.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Kaffa region — southwestern Ethiopia, the birthplace of Coffea arabica

The Kaffa region (from which the word 'coffee' is etymologically derived) in southwestern Ethiopia is the biological homeland of Coffea arabica — wild coffee grows here in the montane forest as it has for millions of years. The Kaffa people have been using coffee longer than anyone else on earth — they chew the whole coffee berry mixed with butter and salt (chewing the whole fruit rather than brewing it) and have a distinctly different coffee preparation tradition from the rest of Ethiopia. Kaffa forest coffee is the original — gathered from wild trees in the forest, prepared in ways that predate the coffee ceremony.

Ingredients

Equipment: Clay roasting pot or heavy pan, Jebena, Mortar

    Traditional whole-berry preparation

    • 100g fresh coffee berries (or green beans)
    • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
    • Pinch of salt

    Or: for traditional brew

    • 3 tbsp ground coffee from the roasted berries
    • 600ml water

    Method

    1. // Traditional whole-berry (Kaffa original):
    2. Take fresh coffee berries. Roast briefly in clay pot with butter and a pinch of salt.
    3. The berries blister and release their aroma.
    4. Chew one berry at a time — the whole fruit provides caffeine and coffee flavour differently from brewing.
    5. // Traditional Kaffa brew:
    6. Roast berries until medium-dark.
    7. Pound in mortar.
    8. Brew in clay vessel over fire 8 minutes.
    9. Serve with butter and salt.

    Chef Tips

    • The butter and salt are the ancient Kaffa preparation — not sugar.
    • Fresh berries are ideal — dried green beans are the substitute.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Using sugar — historically incorrect Kaffa tradition.

    The Science

    Whole coffee berry consumption provides caffeine from both the bean and the fruit pulp (coffee cherry) — the pulp contains less caffeine but more antioxidants (especially chlorogenic acid) than the bean alone. This is why the Kaffa chewing tradition produces a different caffeine experience than brewed coffee.

    Health Benefits

    • Wild forest coffee has the highest antioxidant diversity of any coffee.
    • The whole berry preparation provides compounds absent from brewed coffee alone.

    Cultural Note

    Kaffa forest coffee is the original coffee experience — the coffee that exists because the Kaffa forest exists, that the Kaffa people have been using since before history was recorded, from trees that grow wild in one of the world's most important biodiversity hotspots. The Kaffa Biosphere Reserve protects both the wild coffee and the culture that first discovered it.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Fresh berries: use immediately. Roasted: use within days.

    Reheating: Not applicable.

    Serve with: With butter, With salt, As the original Kaffa coffee tradition
