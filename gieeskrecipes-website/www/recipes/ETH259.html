🍞 Tigrayan Himbasha

ሕምባሻ — Tigrayan Himbasha

Tigrayan Himbasha — the round, decorated celebration bread of the Tigray region, pan-baked with nigella seeds and fenugreek. Distinct from Amhara Ambasha — the Tigrayan version uses nigella seeds (black cumin) as the signature spice, giving it a distinctive bitter-aromatic character.

Tigrayan Himbasha is the northern version of the Ethiopian celebration bread — pan-baked, circular, decorated with geometric patterns, and fragrant with the nigella seeds that are the Tigrayan signature spice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Large flat pan with lid

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 cup warm water
  • ½ tsp nigella seeds (black cumin)
  • ¼ tsp fenugreek

Method

  1. Dissolve yeast in water with sugar. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Combine flour, salt, nigella seeds, and fenugreek.
  3. Add yeast mixture and oil. Knead 8 minutes.
  4. Rise 90 minutes.
  5. Press into a large round (2cm thick). Score decorative pattern.
  6. Cook in a flat pan covered on medium-low: 20 minutes per side.

Chef Tips

  • Nigella seeds are the Tigrayan Himbasha signature — do not substitute.
  • The decorative pattern is as important as the flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Omitting nigella seeds — makes it Ambasha, not Himbasha.

The Science

See ETH005 for Ambasha science. Nigella seeds contain thymoquinone — an antioxidant that is released during baking.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Tigrayan Himbasha is the bread of the ancient north — pan-baked in the same way in the same mountains that produced the Aksumite civilisation and the first Ethiopian Christians. The nigella seeds that mark it are the northern signature.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Room temperature 4 days.

Reheating: Warm briefly.

Serve with: At Tigrayan celebrations, With honey butter (ETH152), At Meskel and other celebrations
