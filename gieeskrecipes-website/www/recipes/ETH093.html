🌶️ Ethiopian Bird's Eye Chilli Powder

ሚጥሚጣ — Mitmita

Ethiopian Mitmita — a very fine, orange-red powder of bird's eye chillies, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon. Ethiopia's finishing spice — used on kitfo, gored gored, eggs, and anywhere a quick hit of intense heat and fragrance is needed.

Mitmita is the finishing spice of Ethiopian cooking — fiercely hot from bird's eye chillies but fragrant from cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon. It is sprinkled on kitfo and gored gored, stirred into kibbeh, and used wherever a powerful finishing spice is needed. Finer and more aromatic than berbere.

Ingredients

Equipment: Dry pan, Spice grinder

  • 20 dried bird's eye chillies (very hot)
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Toast bird's eye chillies briefly in a dry pan — 1—2 minutes.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Grind chillies to a very fine powder.
  4. Add cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and salt.
  5. Grind together until uniformly fine.
  6. Store in an airtight jar.

Chef Tips

  • Mitmita should be very finely ground — finer than berbere.
  • Very hot — use sparingly if not accustomed to intense heat.
  • The cardamom fragrance should be clearly present.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Coarse grinding — too chunky for the finishing applications.

The Science

Bird's eye chillies contain 100,000—225,000 Scoville Heat Units of capsaicin — among the highest of commonly used dried chillies. The cardamom's 1,8-cineole and the cloves' eugenol are both aromatic volatile compounds that partially offset the pure heat perception by stimulating other receptors simultaneously.

Health Benefits

  • Very high capsaicin — thermogenic and anti-inflammatory.
  • Cardamom supports digestive health.

Cultural Note

Mitmita is Ethiopia's fiercest spice — the one that tests how much heat you can handle. The experienced Ethiopian eater uses it liberally on kitfo; the first-time visitor approaches it with caution. Its orange-red colour and intense fragrance are immediately distinctive.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight jar — 6 months.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On kitfo (ETH029), On gored gored (ETH028), On eggs, Wherever finishing heat is needed
