🥑 Salade d’avocats (Avocado Salad)

Salade d’avocats

Tender avocados gently mixed with oil, vinegar, mustard, onion, tomato and garlic, with optional canned sardines and parsley — eaten for breakfast, often with baguettes.

Submitted to AFS USA’s recipe collection by Joyce (source country Cameroon). The ingredients are mixed gently, taking care not to mash the avocado, which should stay in pieces. The page says the salad is eaten for breakfast and is often accompanied with baguettes. Time is printed as under 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl

  • 5–8 tender avocados
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • Half an onion
  • 1 small tomato
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 clove garlic (printed ‘glove’)
  • Sardines, preferably canned (optional)
  • Some parsley
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix all the ingredients together gently, taking care not to mash the avocado. You want to keep it in pieces.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is to keep the avocado in pieces.
  • Use tender (ripe) avocados.
  • The sardines are optional.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mashing the avocado.
  • Using under-ripe avocados.
  • Over-salting: the page calls for a pinch.
