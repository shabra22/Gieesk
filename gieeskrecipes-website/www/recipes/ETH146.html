🍅 Ethiopian Tomato Chilli Sauce

ሲልሲ — Silsi

Ethiopian Silsi — a smooth, slightly spiced tomato sauce made from fresh tomatoes, onion, garlic, green chilli, and a touch of berbere. The Ethiopian table sauce served alongside tibs, grilled fish, and grilled meats — lighter and fresher than awaze.

Silsi is Ethiopia's fresh tomato table sauce — lighter than awaze, brighter than berbere-based condiments, and providing a fresh acid note alongside richer preparations. It appears on every restaurant table alongside tibs and grilled preparations.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan, Blender

  • 4 ripe tomatoes — quartered
  • 1 onion — roughly chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 green chillies (jalapeño or serrano)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp berbere
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion and garlic until golden — 5 minutes.
  2. Add green chillies and berbere. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes until completely soft.
  4. Cool slightly.
  5. Blend with lemon juice and coriander until smooth.
  6. Season.
  7. Serve at room temperature or slightly warm.

Chef Tips

  • Silsi should be smooth — blend thoroughly.
  • The green chilli (not berbere) provides the primary heat here.
  • The berbere is a background note only — just ½ tsp.
  • Add lemon after blending — preserves the bright colour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much berbere — overpowers the fresh tomato character.
  • Not blending smooth.

The Science

Cooking the tomatoes before blending concentrates the lycopene and breaks down the pectin in the cell walls — producing a smoother texture than raw tomato blending and concentrating the umami compounds (glutamate) released during cooking.

Cultural Note

Silsi is the light condiment of the Ethiopian table — where awaze is intense and kibbeh is rich, silsi is bright and fresh. It provides the tomato-forward note that balances the heavy spice and fat of the great wats.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 1 week. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Alongside Tibs (ETH022), With grilled fish (ETH085), As a dipping sauce with injera
