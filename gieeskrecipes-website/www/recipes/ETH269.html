🏛️ Axum Ancient Feast

አክሱም ምግብ — Axum Mgib

Axum Ancient Feast — the food of Ethiopia's most ancient civilization: Tigrayan Zigni (ETH235), Tihlo (ETH236), Tej (ETH215), Tella (ETH216), and the coffee of the Kaffa forest. The feast that recalls the food of the Aksumite Empire — the civilization that first traded Ethiopian coffee to Arabia.

Axum was the capital of the Aksumite Empire from the 1st to 7th centuries AD — one of the world's great ancient civilizations. The Aksumite trade routes carried Ethiopian coffee, ivory, and spices to Arabia and India. The feast food of this ancient civilization has been reconstructed from Tigrayan tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple pots, Jebena

  • Tigrayan Zigni
  • Tigrayan Tihlo
  • Tej
  • Tella
  • Kaffa Coffee

Method

  1. Days before: brew Tej and Tella.
  2. Day of: make Zigni and Tihlo.
  3. Serve in the ancient Tigrayan tradition.

Chef Tips

  • Axum feast must include Tej — it predates recorded Ethiopian history.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving without Tej — the ancient drink.

Cultural Note

Axum is the oldest city in sub-Saharan Africa and the capital of one of the ancient world's great empires. The food that the Aksumite Empire served at its feasts — tej, tella, zigni — is still prepared by the descendants of those ancient cooks in the same highland landscape.
