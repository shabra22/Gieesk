🌯 Ethiopian Flatbread Wrap

ኩቱ — Kutu (Kita Wrap)

Ethiopian Kita Wrap — a large fresh kita flatbread wrapped around berbere-spiced minced meat (or lentils for fasting), silsi, fresh salad, and berbere mayo. The Ethiopian street wrap — the chapati-wrap format applied to Ethiopian street food culture.

The Ethiopian kita wrap is the modern Ethiopian street food that bridges the traditional flatbread and the contemporary wrap format — kita is used as the wrap instead of a tortilla, and the fillings are entirely Ethiopian in character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan for meat filling

  • 4 large kita flatbreads — fresh and warm

Berbere Meat Filling

  • 200g minced beef — browned with garlic, berbere, and onion

OR lentil filling (for fasting) — see ETH165

    Build

    • 4 tbsp berbere mayo
    • Timatim selata
    • Shredded cabbage or lettuce
    • Fresh jalapeño slices

    Method

    1. Cook meat filling: brown mince with onion, garlic, and berbere until dry. Season.
    2. Warm kita in a dry pan — must be warm to fold without breaking.
    3. Spread berbere mayo on kita.
    4. Add shredded cabbage or lettuce.
    5. Add filling in a line across the centre.
    6. Add salad and jalapeño.
    7. Roll tightly. Wrap bottom in paper or foil.

    Chef Tips

    • Warm kita is essential — cold kita cracks when folded.
    • Wrap tightly — filling must not escape.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Cold kita — cracks.
    • Overfilling — impossible to wrap.

    The Science

    See ETH007 for kita science. Warm kita has a more elastic gluten network than cold kita — the gluten (which becomes more rigid as it cools and partially retrogrades) is still flexible when warm, allowing the flatbread to fold without tearing.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian kita wrap is the urban food culture's adaptation — young Ethiopians who grew up with injera and Doro Wat also grew up with global food culture, and the wrap format applied to Ethiopian flavours is their natural synthesis.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Eat immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: With cold water, As a complete lunch
