Soppressata Calabrese

Calabria's coarse, chili-spiced cured pork salami, thinly sliced and served simply alongside sharp local cheese and crusty bread, showcasing the region's love of bold, fiery cured meats.

Soppressata Calabrese is generously studded with the region's signature dried chili, giving it real heat compared to milder Italian salami from further north, and it's traditionally enjoyed with nothing more elaborate than good bread and cheese to let the meat's spice and character shine.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp knife, Serving platter

  • 200 g (7 oz) soppressata Calabrese, thinly sliced
  • 150 g (5.3 oz) sharp pecorino or provolone cheese, sliced
  • Crusty bread
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • A few olives, for serving

Method

  1. Slice the soppressata thinly, either by hand or with a meat slicer.
  2. Arrange the sliced soppressata on a serving platter alongside the cheese.
  3. Add a few olives to the platter.
  4. Serve with crusty bread and a small dish of olive oil for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • Genuine soppressata Calabrese needs little more than good bread and cheese to shine — resist over-complicating the presentation.
  • Let the meat and cheese come to room temperature before serving for the best flavor.
  • A sharp, well-aged pecorino balances the salami's heat particularly well.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Slicing the salami too thick, which can make the chili heat feel overwhelming in each bite.
  • Pairing it with an overly mild cheese that gets lost against the salami's bold spice.
  • Serving straight from the refrigerator, when it's better enjoyed closer to room temperature.

  • A good source of protein, best enjoyed in moderation given its cured, salt-rich nature.

Cultural Note

Soppressata Calabrese is a proud regional specialty, its generous use of local dried chili distinguishing it clearly from milder salami traditions found elsewhere in Italy.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Whole soppressata keeps in a cool, dry place for months; sliced portions should be refrigerated and used within a week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust Calabrian red wine
