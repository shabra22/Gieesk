🥜 Nnam owondo (Peanut Paste Steamed in Banana Leaves)

Nnam owondo / mets d’arachides

Roasted peanut paste mixed with crayfish soaking water and smoked meat or fish, thickened about 10 minutes until the oil rises, wrapped in banana leaves and steamed 30 minutes.

1hTotal time

A peanut dish Afrik’s sister article calls the totem of the Beti. The peanuts are roasted and crushed (or a ready paste is used). The crayfish are soaked in 300 ml of warm water; when the water boils, half is taken out and kept. The protein and the peanut paste go in, stirred vigorously to avoid lumps, and the reserved water is added gradually; the oil rises to the surface. About 10 minutes of medium heat, then a ball is formed, wrapped in banana leaves, tied, and steamed 30 minutes on low heat in a steamer basket. Served with bâtons de manioc or plantain.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Steamer basket, Banana leaves, String

  • 200 g roasted peanut paste, or 500 g peanuts
  • 1 bowl crayfish
  • 300 ml warm water to soak the crayfish
  • Banana leaves and string
  • Salt
  • Chilli
  • 200 g smoked meat or a fish (the variation line; the main list says only ‘protein of choice’)

Method

  1. Roast and crush the peanuts, or use a ready paste.
  2. Soak the crayfish in warm water. When the water boils, take out half and set it aside.
  3. Add the protein and the peanut paste. Stir vigorously to avoid lumps.
  4. Add the reserved water gradually; the oil rises to the surface. Cook about 10 minutes on medium heat.
  5. Form a ball, wrap it in banana leaves and tie. Steam in a steamer basket 30 minutes on low heat.
  6. Serve with bâtons de manioc or plantain.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: the mixture thickens as it cooks; the oil rises to the surface.
  • Stir vigorously to avoid lumps.
  • The page gives no cue for the steaming stage beyond 30 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all the reserved water at once.
  • Cooking on high heat so the paste catches.
  • Wrapping loosely so the parcel leaks.
