🫘 Ethiopian Brown Lentil Stew

ቡናማ ምስር ወጥ — Bunama Misir Wat

Ethiopian Brown Lentil Stew — whole brown lentils simmered in a berbere sauce. Unlike red lentils which dissolve, brown lentils retain their shape — producing a chunkier, more textured stew. A satisfying variety change from Misir Wat.

Brown lentil stew uses whole lentils where Misir Wat uses red (split) lentils — the result is more textured, requiring longer cooking but providing a different and equally satisfying eating experience.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 2 cups brown or green lentils — washed, soaked 1 hour
  • 3 onions — finely diced
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 18 minutes.
  2. Add oil and berbere. Fry 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
  4. Add drained lentils. Stir to coat.
  5. Add water. Simmer covered 40—45 minutes.
  6. Brown lentils hold their shape — they will not dissolve like red lentils.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Brown lentils need soaking — reduces cooking time.
  • They retain their shape — not as smooth as Misir Wat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Expecting red-lentil-style dissolving — brown lentils stay whole.

The Science

Brown lentils have their seed coat intact — this coat prevents the rapid water absorption and dissolution of red (dehulled) lentils. The seed coat proteins are also more resistant to heat denaturation, maintaining structural integrity through cooking.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Brown lentil stew provides texture variety on the Ethiopian fasting platter — its chunky character a pleasant contrast to the smooth Misir Wat and silky Shiro.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter as variety from Misir Wat
