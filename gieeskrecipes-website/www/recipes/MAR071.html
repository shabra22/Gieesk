🥛 Milk Bastilla (Bastilla bil Halib)

Bastilla bil halib — milk pastilla (باستيلا الحليب)

A custard-like cinnamon milk cream spooned between disks of fried warka, finished with roasted almonds, powdered sugar and cinnamon.

40mTotal time
20mPrep
20mCook
4Servings
360Calories
MediumDifficulty

Milk is brought to a boil with a cinnamon stick and four cloves, which are removed; sugar, cornstarch and orange blossom water thicken it for another 5 minutes until creamy and thick. Warka disks are fried for one minute on both sides until golden. The dessert is served right away while the warka is still warm.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Frying pan

  • 2 cups (1/2 liter) whole milk
  • 3/4 cup (150 grams) granulated white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 cinnamon stick + 4 whole cloves
  • 1 tablespoon orange blossom water
  • Warka sheets (no quantity)
  • Oil for frying (no quantity)
  • Roasted almonds, powdered sugar and cinnamon, to finish (no quantities)

Method

  1. Heat the milk with the cinnamon stick and cloves on medium heat. Once the milk reaches a boil, remove the cinnamon stick and cloves.
  2. Stir in the sugar and cornstarch (mixed with a little cold milk) and the orange blossom water, and continue cooking on medium heat another 5 minutes until the milk mixture becomes creamy and thick.
  3. Fry the warka disks for one minute on both sides until they get a beautiful golden colour.
  4. Layer the cream between the disks, top with roasted almonds, powdered sugar and cinnamon, and serve at once while the warka is still warm.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: milk mixture creamy and thick; warka golden.
  • Serve immediately, as the page insists.
  • Mixing the cornstarch with cold milk first avoids lumps (our tip).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the cream scorch.
  • Frying the warka too early so it goes soft.
  • Skipping the removal of the cloves.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; the warka softens within minutes.

Reheating: Not reheated.

Serve with: Mint tea
