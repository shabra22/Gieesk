🍗 Hamam Mashwi (Grilled Pigeon, Egyptian Style, with Green Wheat Salad)

Hamam mashwi — حمام مشوي

Pigeons or doves oiled and rubbed with an Egyptian spice mix, grilled pink, and served over a warm freekeh salad with red pepper and parsley.

3h 40mTotal time
20mPrep
20mCook
4Servings
540Calories
MediumDifficulty

Birds are coated with olive oil and a rub of cinnamon, cayenne, cumin, coriander, pepper, cardamom, cloves, oregano and mint, and refrigerated 3 hours or up to overnight. Freekeh is simmered in broth, then rested 30 minutes or so with the heat off. The birds grill breast-up for 10 minutes and breast-down for 3 to 5 minutes, and rest while the freekeh is tossed with shallots, garlic, red pepper, parsley and lemon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill, Saucepan with lid, Skillet

  • 4 to 6 pigeons or 8 to 16 doves, plucked
  • Olive oil to coat them

Egyptian spice rub

  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ground cardamom (optional)
  • 1/4 teaspoon cloves
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried mint
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt

Green wheat salad

  • 2 cups freekeh or bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups pigeon or dove broth or chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup minced shallots
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 tablespoons high-quality olive oil
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon juice to taste

Method

  1. Coat the birds in olive oil, rub with the spices and refrigerate, covered, for 3 hours or up to overnight. Take them out about an hour before cooking.
  2. Bring the broth to a boil, add the freekeh, return to a boil, cover and turn off the heat. It should be ready in 30 minutes or so.
  3. Sauté the shallots for a minute or two, or until they just start to brown; add the garlic and pepper for another minute.
  4. Grill the birds breast-side up for 10 minutes, avoiding burning, then flip and grill breast-side down for 3 to 5 minutes. The author likes them pink inside. Rest the birds.
  5. Toss the freekeh with the pan mixture, oil, parsley, salt, pepper and lemon and serve with the birds.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: shallots start to brown; birds pink inside. The spice rub rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (3 hours to overnight).
  • The author replies in a comment that getting disease from pigeons is largely an urban myth; always source birds from a trusted supplier and cook to a safe temperature for your comfort (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking, which dries lean game birds.

Cultural Note

The author notes that hamam mashi is one of the national dishes of Egypt and that pigeon is sold by street vendors in Cairo; both are his attributed remarks.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked birds up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Freekeh pilaf
