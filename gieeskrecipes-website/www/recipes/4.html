🌮 Birria Tacos

Juicy braised beef birria with rich consommé for dipping. Crispy, cheesy, deeply spiced. Weekend project, weekday reward.

★ 4.8 (389 reviews)
180 minTotal time
20 minPrep
160 minCook
6Servings
490Calories
HardDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 1.5kg beef chuck
  • 4 dried guajillo chillies
  • 2 ancho chillies
  • 4 chipotle in adobo
  • 1 white onion
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • Corn tortillas
  • Oaxaca cheese
  • White onion & coriander to serve

Method

  1. Toast and rehydrate dried chillies. Blend with onion, garlic, chipotles, and spices into a smooth paste.
  2. Marinate beef in the paste overnight.
  3. Brown beef in a heavy pot. Add remaining marinade and 1L water. Simmer covered for 3 hours until shreddable.
  4. Shred beef and strain braising liquid into a bowl (this is the consommé).
  5. Dip tortillas in consommé, fill with beef and cheese, fold and fry in a hot skillet until crispy.
  6. Serve with a bowl of hot consommé for dipping.
