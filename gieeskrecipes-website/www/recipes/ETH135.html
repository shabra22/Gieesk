🫘 Ethiopian Lentil and Vegetable Stew

ምስር አትክልት ወጥ — Misir Atkilit Wat

Ethiopian Lentil and Vegetable Stew — red lentils cooked with diced carrots, potatoes, and tomatoes in a berbere sauce. The combined protein and vegetable one-pot — the lentils dissolve into the sauce while the vegetables retain their texture.

Lentil and vegetable stew is the modern family fasting pot — combining the protein of Misir Wat with the nutrition of mixed vegetables into a single pot that is both filling and complete.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1.5 cups red lentils
  • 2 potatoes — cubed
  • 2 carrots — sliced
  • 3 onions
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions 18 minutes.
  2. Add oil and berbere. Fry 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and tomatoes. Cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add lentils. Add water. Simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer 20 more minutes.
  6. Lentils dissolve; vegetables remain whole.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add vegetables after the lentils have been cooking 20 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding vegetables at the start.

The Science

Adding the root vegetables after partial lentil cooking allows the lentils to dissolve and thicken the sauce before the vegetables go in — if all ingredients are added together, the vegetables break down before the lentils have had time to produce their natural thickening.

Cultural Note

Lentil and vegetable stew is modern Ethiopia's practical fasting answer — one pot, complete nutrition, family-friendly, efficient. The ancient berbere tradition applied to the contemporary family's needs.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), As a complete fasting one-pot meal
