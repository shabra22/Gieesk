🐪 Afar Hilib Ari (Spiced Camel Meat)

ሒልብ አሪ — Hilib Ari

Afar Hilib Ari — camel (or beef) slow-cooked for 3 hours with Afar spices: cumin, coriander, turmeric, and ginger. The nomadic desert feast — camel meat requires long cooking and rewards with extraordinary depth. Made for weddings and significant celebrations.

Hilib Ari is the Afar prestige dish — camel meat, slow-cooked until tender with the Afar spice tradition that emphasises cumin and coriander over berbere. The harsh desert environment has produced a cuisine of remarkable efficiency.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Afar people — the Afar Triangle, one of the world's most extreme environments

The Afar are a nomadic pastoralist people living in one of the world's most extreme environments — the Afar Triangle, which includes the Danakil Depression (the hottest place on earth). Their cuisine is entirely adapted to nomadic life: camel and goat meat, camel milk, dates, and dried foods that can be transported. Hilib Ari (camel meat stew) is the most prestigious Afar dish — camel meat is expensive and valued, prepared for weddings, religious celebrations, and important guests. The Afar use a spice mixture called berbere that differs significantly from Amhara berbere, featuring more cumin and coriander.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot

  • 1.2kg camel meat (or beef as substitute) — cut into large chunks
  • 3 onions — diced
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Brown onions in oil until very golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and pepper.
  3. Add camel meat (or beef). Brown 5 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  5. Add water. Simmer covered 2.5—3 hours (camel) or 90 minutes (beef).
  6. Meat should be very tender. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Camel meat requires much longer cooking than beef.
  • Afar spice: cumin-forward, not berbere-forward.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking camel — very tough if not fully cooked.

The Science

Camel meat has a higher collagen content in its connective tissue than beef — requiring longer braising to convert collagen to gelatine. The resulting sauce is extraordinarily rich when properly cooked.

Health Benefits

  • Camel meat is lower in fat than beef.
  • High protein.

Cultural Note

Afar Hilib Ari is the wedding feast of the desert — prepared when a family wants to honour guests with the most precious protein available in the Afar Triangle. The camel that is slaughtered for a wedding feast represents a significant sacrifice.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Rice (Afar prefer rice with this dish), Flatbread, At Afar celebrations
