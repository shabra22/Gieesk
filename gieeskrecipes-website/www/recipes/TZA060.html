🍖 Tanzanian Lamb Chops

Ghafi za Kondoo

Tanzanian Ghafi za Kondoo — lamb chops marinated in a paste of Zanzibar spices, garlic, and lemon, grilled over charcoal or in a pan until magnificently charred with a blushing medium-rare interior. Tanzania's hotel restaurant luxury lamb dish.

Lamb chops at a Tanzanian hotel restaurant represent the meeting of global fine dining with Zanzibar's extraordinary spice tradition — the marinade is uniquely Tanzanian, the technique is international, and the result is completely world-class.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill pan or charcoal grill, Tongs

  • 6 lamb chops (rack or loin)
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix all marinade into a smooth paste.
  2. Coat chops thoroughly. Marinate 2 hours.
  3. Heat grill pan or charcoal to very high.
  4. Grill chops 3—4 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  5. Rest 4 minutes. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Room temperature chops before grilling.
  • Very high heat — lamb chops must be seared, not steamed.
  • Rest — essential for juicy lamb.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold chops — uneven cooking.
  • Low heat.
  • Not resting.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar-spiced lamb chops represent Tanzania's fine dining ambition — taking the world's great dining tradition and infusing it with the spice heritage of the Indian Ocean's greatest spice island.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Warm briefly in hot pan.
