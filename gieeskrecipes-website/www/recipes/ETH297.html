🌯 Modern Ethiopian Injera Wrap

ዘመናዊ እንጀራ ሲሙ — Modern Injera Wrap

Modern Ethiopian Injera Wrap — fresh injera rolled around berbere-seared meat (or spiced legumes for fasting), ayib, silsi, pickled vegetables, and fresh herbs. The injera-as-wrap format — the ancient bread repurposed as a contemporary street food delivery system.

The modern injera wrap takes injera out of the mesob tradition and into the contemporary street food format — rolled around a filling, wrapped in paper, and eaten on the go. Ethiopian street food culture updated for the 21st century.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan for warming injera

  • 2 large fresh injera
  • 200g berbere-seared beef or chicken tibs
  • 3 tbsp ayib
  • 3 tbsp silsi
  • Pickled vegetables
  • Shredded lettuce or cabbage
  • Fresh jalapeños, coriander

Method

  1. Warm injera briefly in a dry pan — must be warm to roll.
  2. Lay flat. Spread silsi across centre.
  3. Layer shredded lettuce, tibs, ayib, pickled vegetables, jalapeños, coriander.
  4. Roll tightly.
  5. Wrap bottom half in paper or foil.

Chef Tips

  • Warm injera is essential for rolling.
  • Do not overfill — injera tears.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold injera — tears when rolled.
  • Overfilling.

Cultural Note

The modern injera wrap is the Ethiopian answer to the global street food wrap trend — the ancient bread that has been the plate, utensil, and food of Ethiopian civilization for thousands of years, now serving as a wrap. The injera wrap is the ancient and the modern in one hand.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: As a takeaway street lunch
