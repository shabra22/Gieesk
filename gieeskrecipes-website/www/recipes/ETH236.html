🥩 Tigrayan Zigni

ዝግኒ — Zigni

Tigrayan Zigni — the northern highland meat wat made with beef or lamb in a Tigrinya-style berbere sauce. Zigni is the Tigrinya word for the red meat wat — analogous to Key Wat but with korerima and a more northerly spice profile shared with Eritrea.

Zigni is the prestige Tigrayan meat dish — the celebration food of the ancient northern highland culture, made with the korerima and specific spice ratios that define Tigrinya cooking.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 1kg beef or lamb — cubed
  • 4 onions
  • 4 tbsp kibbeh
  • 5 tbsp berbere
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp korerima
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions 20 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Fry 4 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and korerima.
  4. Add meat. Brown 5 minutes.
  5. Add water. Simmer 70—80 minutes.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Korerima is the northern signature.
  • Zigni is always served with taita (thicker injera of Tigray).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Standard berbere instead of Tigrinya proportion.

Cultural Note

Zigni is the food of Tigrinya identity — shared between Ethiopia's Tigray region and Eritrea, it represents the ancient highland culture of the northern mountains.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera or Tigrayan taita, At Tigrayan celebrations
