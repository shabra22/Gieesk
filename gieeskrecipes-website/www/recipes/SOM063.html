Bisteeki Digaag

Chicken breast in a lemon, garlic and olive oil marinade, seared a minute a side, with a quick pan sauce of butter, onion, hot stock and balsamic poured over — the dish for guests who arrive without warning.

Somali hospitality does not allow for saying you have nothing in. This is the answer to an unannounced visit: a marinade mixed in the time it takes the pan to heat, a minute a side on the chicken, and a sauce built in the same pan while the meat rests. Households that take this seriously keep frozen suugo in reserve for the same reason.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Heavy frying pan

    For the chicken

    • 450 g (1 lb) boneless chicken breast, sliced thin
    • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • Juice of 1 lemon
    • 60 ml (1/4 cup) olive oil
    • 1 tbsp vegetable seasoning, or 2 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, to taste

    For the topping

    • 1/4 onion, minced
    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
    • 120 ml (1/2 cup) chicken stock, hot
    • 1/2 yellow or red bell pepper, sliced
    • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
    • Pinch of ground black pepper

    Method

    1. Mix the olive oil, minced garlic, vegetable seasoning, lemon juice and black pepper in a bowl.
    2. Turn the chicken through the marinade in the same bowl, coating both sides, and leave it 20 minutes.
    3. Heat a pan over medium-high and cook the chicken for no more than a minute a side.
    4. Lift the chicken onto a warm plate. In the same pan, melt the butter over medium heat.
    5. Add the minced onion and cook until soft and translucent.
    6. Pour in the hot stock — it must be hot, or it drops the pan temperature and the sauce stalls.
    7. Add the balsamic vinegar, then the sliced bell pepper and the cilantro, and cook for barely a minute.
    8. Finish with black pepper, spoon the sauce over the chicken and serve.

    Chef Tips

    • A minute a side, no more. Chicken breast this thin is done almost on contact and dries out immediately afterwards.
    • Warm the stock before it goes in. Cold liquid into a hot pan stops everything and you lose the fond.
    • Slice the breast thin and even. Thick pieces cannot cook in the time the recipe allows.
    • Everything after the chicken comes out happens in the same pan, in about two minutes. Have the stock hot and the vegetables cut before you start.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Cooking the chicken through to be certain, which is the whole way to ruin it.
    • Adding cold stock, which kills the pan and greys the sauce.
    • Cooking the bell pepper soft — it is there for crunch and colour.
    • Marinating for hours. Twenty minutes is enough, and lemon juice left much longer starts to cure the surface of the meat.

    Health Benefits

    • Chicken breast is very lean and high in protein.
    • Lemon, garlic and olive oil carry the seasoning with little salt.
    • The vegetables are barely cooked, so they keep their vitamin C.

    Cultural Note

    The unannounced guest is a fixture of Somali life, and so is the expectation that they will be fed properly within twenty minutes of sitting down. A dish like this exists because of that expectation, not in spite of it.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

    Reheating: Warm very briefly in a covered pan with a spoon of stock.

    Serve with: Suugo Suqaar, Bariis Shaciiriyo Leh, Sabaayad
