🍤 Ndolé sentimental (Chicken and Prawn Ndolé)

Ndolé sentimental

A smoked free-range chicken, gambas, ground peanuts and dried shrimp simmered with three-times-rinsed ndolé leaves, with chicken and gambas browned separately for the plate.

145 minTotal time
40 minPrep
105 minCook
8Servings
720Calories
HardDifficulty

A festive ndolé from Afrik, attributed on the page to chef Christian Abégan (Le Patrimoine culinaire africain, Michel Lafon). The chicken is cut into 8; peanuts are soaked in 1 litre of water for 20 minutes and pureed; seven onions are pureed with the ginger and garlic; dried shrimp is powdered and the gambas peeled. The leaves are rinsed twice in lukewarm water. Peanut, onion and shrimp purees are combined over low heat with half the chicken, half the gambas and 1 litre of water for 45 minutes; the leaves get a third rinse and are pressed by hand to extract water, then added and cooked and reduced on low heat 30 minutes. The rest of the chicken and gambas are browned with oil, onion and diced tomato for 30 minutes and used to top the plated sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender or food processor, Frying pan

  • 1 large smoked free-range chicken (‘beau gros poulet fermier fumé’)
  • 1 kg gambas
  • 800 g ndolé leaves (or other bitter leaves)
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 8 large onions (printed; the method peels 7 and reserves 1)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 150 g dried shrimp
  • 1 kg skinless white groundnut seeds
  • 50 g ginger
  • Salt and pepper — quantities not printed
  • 3 litres water (printed)

Method

    Prepare

    1. Cut the chicken into 8 pieces. Soak the peanuts in 1 litre of water for 20 minutes, then puree. Peel 7 onions and puree them with the ginger and garlic. Reduce the dried shrimp to a powder and peel the gambas, keeping them cool.
    2. Rinse the leaves twice in lukewarm water.

    Sauce

    1. Combine the peanut puree, onion puree and shrimp over low heat. Add half the chicken, half the gambas and 1 litre of water. Cook 45 minutes.
    2. Rinse the leaves a third time and press by hand to extract as much water as possible. Add them to the sauce and cook and reduce on low heat 30 minutes.

    Finish

    1. Heat the oil with the remaining onion, the diced tomatoes and the remaining chicken and gambas; cook 30 minutes on low heat with a little water. Check the seasoning.
    2. Plate the sauce topped with the browned chicken and golden gambas.

    Chef Tips

    • Printed cues: chicken ‘rissolé’ (browned), gambas ‘dorées’ (golden), leaves ‘réduire’ (reduced).
    • Press the leaves hard after the final rinse — the page does this to take out water and bitterness.
    • The page lists 3 litres of water but the steps account for about 2; keep water on hand and add as needed.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Skipping the three leaf rinses.
    • Adding all the chicken and gambas at once — the method keeps half back for the topping.
    • Following the ‘8 onions’ list and the ‘7 onions’ step without noticing they differ.

    Cultural Note

    Afrik’s page describes this version as especially appreciated by the Douala people of the Littoral; the recipe is attributed to Christian Abégan and the page byline is Amina Savoura. The attribution is the page’s own.
