🍖 Fonio with Grilled Lamb Chops, Asparagus and Mafé Sauce

Fonio, côtelettes d’agneau et sauce mafé

Thyme-and-garlic lamb chops grilled about five minutes a side, asparagus charred three, fonio steamed and a sauce of two cups of peanut butter in a quart of chicken broth.

65 minTotal time
20 minPrep
40 minCook
4Servings

A restaurant plate. The mafé sauce comes first: onion cooks 3 to 5 minutes until soft but not brown, a tablespoon of tomato paste cooks about 5 minutes on low, then two cups of smooth peanut butter and a quart of chicken broth, with a whole Scotch bonnet if wanted, simmer 15 to 20 minutes until oil rises to the surface. Eight chops are rubbed with oil, thyme, garlic, salt and pepper and grilled about 5 minutes a side, rested 5 minutes under foil; asparagus grills about 3 minutes. Fonio is cooked on the stovetop in about 5 minutes covered plus 2 more off the heat, or microwaved 6–8 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill, Large pot, Foil, Platter

    Mafé sauce

    • 1 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil
    • 1 onion, finely chopped
    • 1 tbsp tomato paste
    • 2 cups unsweetened smooth peanut butter
    • 1 quart chicken broth
    • 1 whole Scotch bonnet pepper (optional)

    Lamb and asparagus

    • 8 lamb chops, trimmed of excess fat
    • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
    • 2 tbsp chopped fresh thyme
    • 2 tsp minced garlic
    • 1 tsp salt (method)
    • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
    • 1 pound green asparagus, trimmed
    • 5 cups cooked fonio

    Fonio, stovetop

    • 2 cups water
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 1 cup raw fonio, rinsed and drained well
    • 1 tbsp peanut, vegetable or olive oil (optional)

    Method

    1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high. Cook the onion 3 to 5 minutes, until soft but not brown. Add the tomato paste, reduce to low and stir about 5 minutes, until deep red. Add a few tablespoons of water only if needed to prevent scorching.
    2. Stir in the peanut butter, then the chicken broth. Bring to a boil, add the Scotch bonnet if using, reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring periodically, 15 to 20 minutes, until the oil rises to the surface. Remove from the heat and keep warm.
    3. Heat a grill to high. Rub the chops with the oil, thyme, garlic, 1 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Grill about 5 minutes per side, until nicely browned and medium (adjust for thickness).
    4. Transfer the chops to a platter, cover loosely with foil and rest 5 minutes.
    5. While the lamb rests, season the asparagus with salt and grill about 3 minutes, turning occasionally, until nicely charred.
    6. Fonio: boil the water and salt, add the fonio, stir once, reduce to a simmer, cover tightly and cook about 5 minutes. Turn off the heat and keep covered 2 more minutes, until the water is absorbed. Fluff and mix in the oil if using.
    7. Serve the lamb and fonio with a generous amount of sauce.

    Chef Tips

    • ‘Until the oil rises to the surface’ is the sauce cue.
    • The peanut-to-liquid ratio here (2 cups butter to 1 quart broth) is far thicker than the shank mafé (1 cup to 2 quarts): it is a sauce, not a stew.
    • Rest the chops under foil for 5 minutes before slicing into them.
    • Fonio microwave option: 1 cup fonio, 2 cups water, 1 tsp salt, covered tightly with plastic, high 6–8 minutes.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Overcooking the chops past medium.
    • Scorching the tomato paste.
