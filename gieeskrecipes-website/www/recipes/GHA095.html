🔥 Whole Grilled Tilapia with Grains of Paradise

Whole grilled tilapia

Two tablespoons of ground grains of paradise and two green kpakpo shito, marinated an hour or preferably overnight, grilled twenty-five minutes.

The other documented grilled tilapia, and it goes much further in both directions than the charcoal version — a longer marinade and a longer cook. Two fresh tilapia take a grated white onion, rapeseed oil, five centimetres of ginger, a garlic clove, two deseeded green kpakpo shito chillies, the juice of a lime and two full tablespoons of ground grains of paradise, plus chilli flakes and ground hot pepper. An hour minimum, preferably overnight. Then medium-high for twenty-five minutes, turning and basting halfway, until browned and cooked through.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill or barbecue, Grater, Basting brush, Fish basket

  • 2 fresh tilapia, scaled, gutted and washed
  • 1 white onion, grated
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 5 cm fresh root ginger, grated
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 2 green kpakpo shito chillies, deseeded and diced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp ground grains of paradise
  • 1/2 tsp dried chilli flakes
  • 1/2 tsp ground hot pepper
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

To serve

  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. Combine the grated onion, oil, ginger, garlic, kpakpo shito, lime juice, grains of paradise, chilli flakes, ground hot pepper, salt and black pepper.
  2. Coat the tilapia inside and out.
  3. Marinate at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  4. Heat the grill to medium-high.
  5. Grill 25 minutes, adding 3 to 5 minutes for larger fish, turning and basting halfway.
  6. Cook until browned and cooked through.
  7. Serve with lime wedges.

Chef Tips

  • Two tablespoons of ground grains of paradise for two fish is a lot and it is the signature of this recipe. Grains of paradise are peppery and faintly floral and nothing substitutes well.
  • Overnight is better than the one-hour minimum, by her own framing. With lime juice in the marinade an hour is already doing work.
  • Turn and baste ONCE, halfway. Twenty-five minutes is a long grill and the basting is what keeps it from drying.
  • Browned and cooked through is the cue. Twenty-five minutes for a whole tilapia at medium-high is considerably longer than the other documented version's fifteen.
  • Green kpakpo shito deseeded, which keeps the aroma and loses some heat. Two per fish is generous.
  • A note on the source: its introduction calls this baked tilapia while the method grills it. The method is what to follow.
  • If you want the shorter, hotter charcoal treatment, that is Grilled Tilapia — fifteen minutes marinating and fifteen minutes cooking.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Substituting black pepper for grains of paradise.
  • A short marinade.
  • Turning repeatedly over twenty-five minutes.
  • Skipping the basting.
  • Following the introduction's baking instruction rather than the method's grill.

Health Benefits

  • Tilapia is lean, high in protein and low in mercury.
  • Grains of paradise carry documented antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory compounds.
  • Lime juice adds vitamin C and helps the marinade penetrate.
  • Only a tablespoon of oil for two whole fish.

Cultural Note

Grains of paradise were once traded north across the Sahara and into medieval Europe as a pepper substitute, and a West African cook using two tablespoons of them on a grilled fish is reaching for something far older than the chilli alongside it. The spice is native to this coast and the dish is the better for it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Briefly under a hot grill, or eat cold.

Serve with: Banku, Shito, Lime wedges
