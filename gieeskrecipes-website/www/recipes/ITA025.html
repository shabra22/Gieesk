Pasta alla Norma

Pasta alla Norma layers fried, silky eggplant into a bright tomato sauce, finished with salty ricotta salata and fresh basil — Sicily's tribute to Bellini's opera Norma.

The eggplant is the star here, traditionally fried until deeply golden and creamy inside, then folded through a simple garlic-tomato sauce so its flavor permeates every bite, finished with a generous shower of shaved ricotta salata.

Ingredients

Equipment: Colander, Large skillet, Large pot, Paper towels

  • 400 g (14 oz) rigatoni or penne
  • 2 large eggplants, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 500 g (18 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for frying the eggplant
  • Handful of fresh basil leaves, torn, plus more for garnish
  • 100 g (3.5 oz) ricotta salata, shaved or grated
  • Salt, to taste
  • Vegetable oil, for frying

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant cubes and let sit in a colander for 30 minutes to draw out excess moisture and bitterness. Pat dry thoroughly.
  2. Heat a generous layer of vegetable oil in a skillet and fry the eggplant in batches until deeply golden and tender, about 5–6 minutes per batch. Drain on paper towels.
  3. In a separate pan, heat the olive oil and cook the garlic gently until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and a pinch of salt, simmering for 15–20 minutes until thickened.
  5. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente.
  6. Fold the fried eggplant and torn basil into the tomato sauce.
  7. Drain the pasta and toss with the sauce, adding a splash of pasta water if needed.
  8. Serve topped generously with shaved ricotta salata and extra basil.

Chef Tips

  • Salting the eggplant first is essential — it removes bitterness and excess water, so it fries to a creamy texture rather than turning soggy or greasy.
  • Fry the eggplant in small batches so it browns rather than steams.
  • Ricotta salata is distinct from fresh ricotta — it's pressed, salted, and aged, giving a firm, salty, shaveable texture.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the salting step, leaving the eggplant bitter and prone to soaking up too much oil.
  • Overcrowding the pan while frying, which steams rather than browns the eggplant.
  • Substituting regular ricotta for ricotta salata — they have very different textures and roles in the dish.

Health Benefits

  • Eggplant is rich in fiber and antioxidants, particularly nasunin.
  • Tomatoes provide lycopene, linked to heart health.

Cultural Note

The dish is named in honor of Vincenzo Bellini's opera Norma, reportedly after a writer exclaimed the dish was 'a Norma' — meaning it was a masterpiece worthy of the composer's finest work.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate the sauce (without pasta) for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat the sauce gently in a pan, then toss with freshly cooked pasta.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A crisp Sicilian white wine, A simple side salad
