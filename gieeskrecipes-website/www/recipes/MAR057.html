🥜 Amlou (Almond Dip with Argan Oil and Honey)

Amlou (أملو)

A gritty-smooth spread of roasted almonds, culinary argan oil and honey, eaten with bread at breakfast or snack time.

25mTotal time
10mPrep
15mCook
2Servings
750Calories
EasyDifficulty

Koehler's headnote describes amlou as an almond-based spread local to the Souss region between Agadir and Essaouira and a staple at the Moroccan table. Almonds are baked until deep brown and crunchy, cooled, blended to a gritty paste, and the oil is added gradually until smooth; salt and honey finish it. Refrigerated and covered, it keeps up to one week.

Ingredients

Equipment: Oven, Baking sheet, Food processor

  • 1⅓ cups almonds (8 oz.)
  • Fine salt
  • ½ cup culinary argan oil (see headnote), or walnut or extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp. honey, or to taste

Method

  1. Position a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 350°F.
  2. On an ungreased baking sheet, bake the almonds, shaking the pan occasionally, until deep brown and crunchy, 10 to 15 minutes.
  3. Transfer to a plate and set aside until cool.
  4. In a food processor, blend the almonds to a gritty paste. With the machine running, gradually add the oil and blend until smooth.
  5. Season with salt to taste, scrape into a bowl and stir in the honey.
  6. Refrigerated and tightly covered, amlou keeps up to one week.

Chef Tips

  • Cues quoted: deep brown and crunchy; gritty paste; smooth.
  • Use edible, culinary-grade argan oil, not the cosmetic kind.
  • Serve with bread at breakfast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using cosmetic argan oil.
  • Adding the oil all at once.
  • Under-roasting the almonds.

Health Benefits

  • Almonds and argan oil provide healthy fats.
  • Honey adds sweetness; calorie-dense, so portion small.

Cultural Note

Koehler's headnote says amlou is an almond-based spread local to the southwestern Moroccan region of Souss, between Agadir and Essaouira, and a staple at the Moroccan table.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Up to one week refrigerated and covered.

Reheating: Not heated.

Serve with: Khobz
