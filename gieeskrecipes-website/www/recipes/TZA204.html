🍚 Tanzanian Coconut Rice Pudding

Uji wa Nazi

Tanzanian Uji wa Nazi — short-grain rice simmered slowly in thick coconut milk with cardamom, rose water, and sugar until completely creamy and fragrant. The Swahili coast's most comforting dessert — served warm or chilled, garnished with toasted coconut and fresh mango.

Coconut rice pudding is the coastal Tanzanian dessert that feels like a hug — slow-cooked, gently sweet, perfumed with cardamom and rose water, and topped with the contrast of fresh tropical fruit. It can be breakfast or dessert — on the Swahili coast, the distinction barely matters.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan

  • 1.5 cups short-grain or sushi rice
  • 500ml thick coconut milk
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • ¾ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • Pinch of salt

Garnish: 2 tbsp toasted desiccated coconut, fresh mango slices, a pinch of ground cinnamon

    Method

    1. Combine rice, coconut milk, water, sugar, cardamom, and salt in a heavy saucepan.
    2. Bring to a gentle boil, stirring.
    3. Reduce to lowest heat. Stir every 2—3 minutes.
    4. Cook 28—32 minutes until rice is completely dissolved and the pudding is thick and creamy.
    5. Remove from heat. Stir in rose water.
    6. Serve warm or chill 2 hours.
    7. Garnish with toasted coconut, fresh mango, and a pinch of cinnamon.

    Chef Tips

    • Stir regularly — coconut rice pudding sticks to the base.
    • Short-grain rice dissolves more completely than basmati — producing a creamier result.
    • Rose water goes in off the heat — it is highly volatile and loses its fragrance if cooked.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not stirring — sticks and burns.
    • Adding rose water during cooking.
    • Under-cooking — the rice should be completely soft.

    The Science

    Short-grain rice has a higher amylopectin ratio than long-grain — the amylopectin leaches readily into the coconut milk during cooking, producing the characteristically thick, creamy pudding consistency. The coconut fat also helps create a smooth mouthfeel by coating the gelatinised starch granules.

    Cultural Note

    Coconut rice pudding is the dessert of the Swahili coast's gentlest moments — the warm bowl after a long meal, the chilled portion on a hot afternoon, the sweet that requires nothing more than time and the island's own coconut.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 3 days. Add a splash of coconut milk when reheating.

    Reheating: Gently on stovetop with a splash of coconut milk.

    Serve with: Fresh Mango, Toasted Coconut, As a standalone dessert or breakfast
