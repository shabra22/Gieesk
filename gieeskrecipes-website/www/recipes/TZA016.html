🌽 Tanzanian Maize Porridge

Uji wa Mahindi

Uji wa Mahindi — Tanzanian maize flour porridge, the most widely consumed morning drink/porridge across the country. Slightly sweet, smooth, and filling — the affordable, accessible daily breakfast of millions of Tanzanians.

Maize porridge is Tanzania's most universal breakfast — affordable, familiar, and found everywhere from Dar es Salaam's suburbs to remote village homes. Made thin enough to drink or thick enough to spoon, it is the breakfast that keeps Tanzania going every morning.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Wooden spoon

  • 1 cup fine maize flour
  • 4 cups water
  • 2—3 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • ½ cup milk — optional

Method

  1. Mix maize flour with 1 cup cold water to a smooth slurry.
  2. Bring remaining 3 cups water to a boil.
  3. Pour slurry into boiling water, stirring constantly.
  4. Cook over medium heat for 8—10 minutes until smooth and thick.
  5. Add sugar, salt, and milk if using.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • For a thinner, drinkable porridge use 5 cups water.
  • Add milk for a creamier, more nutritious result.
  • Stir constantly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not making a slurry first.
  • Stopping stirring.

The Science

Fine maize flour contains primarily starch (70—75%). When hydrated and heated, these starch granules swell and gelatinise, thickening the porridge. Fine flour produces a smoother result than coarse flour.

Cultural Note

Uji wa Mahindi is Tanzania in a cup — the breakfast that transcends region, tribe, income, and generation. It is the most widely consumed morning food in the country.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best consumed fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast, With mandazi, With groundnuts
