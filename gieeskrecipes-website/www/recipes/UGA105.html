🥜 Akarafi

Akarafi

Groundnuts, soya, jackfruit seeds and pumpkin seeds roasted together and eaten at tea time — four seeds, one bowl, whatever the garden has.

Akarafi is the Lugbara tea-time snack: roasted groundnuts, soya seeds, jackfruit seeds and pumpkin seeds. It is less a recipe than a category, and that is the interesting thing about it — four quite different seeds, all of them things a household has because they grew them or because they came free with something else, roasted and eaten together. The jackfruit seeds need the fuller treatment in Curry Spiced Jackfruit Seeds before they can join the others.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy cast pan, Wooden spoon, Tray

  • Groundnuts
  • Soya seeds
  • Jackfruit seeds — boiled and peeled first, per Curry Spiced Jackfruit Seeds
  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Salt

No quantities are documented

    Method

    1. Prepare the jackfruit seeds first — boiled, dried and peeled, as in Curry Spiced Jackfruit Seeds.
    2. Roast each kind of seed until browned and fragrant. Roast them separately if their sizes differ much.
    3. Salt them.
    4. Combine and serve at tea time.

    Chef Tips

    • Roast them separately and combine at the end. Groundnuts, soya, jackfruit and pumpkin seeds are wildly different sizes and one pan will burn the small ones before the large ones colour.
    • The jackfruit seeds cannot go straight in — they need boiling, six hours of drying and peeling first, which is the method in Curry Spiced Jackfruit Seeds.
    • The pumpkin seeds take the water-and-salt trick in Toasted Pumpkin Seeds: a tablespoon of water carries the salt onto them and then evaporates.
    • Stir off the heat until they cool, as with any dry-roasted seed. That is when the crunch sets.
    • Four seeds is what the source lists and it is not a fixed set — it is whatever the household has. Simsim would be entirely at home in it.
    • It is a tea-time snack specifically, which places it in the Lugbara day: o'biti at seven to eight in the morning, anya i'di at ten to eleven, nyaka itua at noon to two, asileri ibe'de at eight to nine in the evening.
    • No quantities are documented. Nothing here is estimated.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Roasting all four seeds in one pan.
    • Adding raw jackfruit seeds.
    • Roasting on high heat.
    • Leaving them in the pan off the heat without stirring.
    • Salting after they have cooled, when it will not stick.

    Health Benefits

    • Four seeds together give a wide spread of protein, healthy fats, magnesium, zinc and vitamin E.
    • Soya adds complete protein, which most seeds do not.
    • Dry-roasted with no oil.
    • Contains peanuts and soya.

    Cultural Note

    A snack defined as whatever seeds the household has, roasted, is a good picture of how a subsistence kitchen actually works — not a fixed recipe but a standing habit. That it has a name at all, rather than being called roasted seeds, means it is a thing people expect at a particular time of day.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: 2 weeks in an airtight container once fully cool.

    Reheating: A minute in a dry pan if they soften.

    Serve with: Kuruku Chai, Mukati, Maize Milk
