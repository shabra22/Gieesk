🍆 Mesaka'a (Vegan Egyptian Eggplant and Pepper Bake)

Mesaka'a — مسقعة

Floured eggplant slices fried to an amber colour, layered with quick-fried peppers in a garlicky vinegar-tomato sauce and baked.

1h 10mTotal time
25mPrep
45mCook
4Servings
250Calories
MediumDifficulty

Peeled eggplant slices are floured and fried until a deep amber, peppers are fried no more than two minutes, and a sauce of garlic, white vinegar, tomato juice, paste and cumin simmers about 5 minutes. Layered with the sauce and baked at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes. The author skips salting the eggplant.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Baking dish, Paper towels

  • 2 Eggplants, sliced 1/2inch
  • 2 mild green bell peppers
  • 2 hot green peppers or more
  • ¼ cup all purpose flour
  • ½ cup frying oil, preferably canola
  • 1 cup- 1 1/2 cup garlicky tomato sauce, recipe follows

Garlicky tomato sauce

  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 3 tablespoons white distilled vinegar
  • 1 cup tomato juice, freshly fresh or marinara sauce (see notes)
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • ½ teaspoon sugar, optional
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Chopped parsley

Method

  1. Peel the eggplants lengthwise and slice 1/2 to 1 inch thick. The author does not salt them.
  2. Dust with flour and fry in the oil until the slices have a deep amber colour. Drain on paper.
  3. Fry the peppers for no longer than two minutes, stopping before they lose their green colour. Drain.
  4. Sauce: in 3 tablespoons of the frying oil cook the garlic until fragrant, add the vinegar, then the tomato juice, paste, sugar and cumin. Simmer about 5 minutes on low heat until it thickens.
  5. Layer sauce, eggplant and peppers in a baking dish and bake at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle with parsley.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: eggplant deep amber; peppers still green; sauce thickened. Frying time per batch is NOT PRINTED.
  • The page's body says the sauce simmers 3 to 5 minutes and the bake is 20 minutes (single readings).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying the peppers until they turn dull.
  • Crowding the pan so the eggplant steams.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour describes it as the meatless Coptic Lenten version of Greek moussaka.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat in a moderate oven.

Serve with: Aish baladi
