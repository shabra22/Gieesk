🍰 Kenyan Cheesecake Trio

Cheesecake Tatu

Kenyan Cheesecake Trio — three mini no-bake cheesecakes on individual biscuit bases: passion fruit, mango, and coffee. The hotel dessert menu's most photographed dish — three flavours representing Kenya's greatest produce on one plate.

The cheesecake trio is the hotel restaurant's way of celebrating Kenya's greatest flavours simultaneously — passion fruit, mango, and coffee, each in a mini cheesecake, each representing something extraordinary about what this country produces. Served side by side on one plate, it is both beautiful and delicious.

Ingredients

Equipment: 12 individual ring moulds or ramekins, Electric beater

    Shared Base (for all three)

    • 300g digestive biscuits — crushed
    • 150g butter — melted

    Shared Cream Cheese Base

    • 750g cream cheese — softened
    • 300ml whipping cream
    • 150g icing sugar
    • 3 tsp gelatin dissolved in 3 tbsp water

    Passion Fruit Flavouring

    • ½ cup passion fruit juice

    Mango Flavouring

    • ½ cup mango puree

    Coffee Flavouring

    • 3 tbsp strong Kenyan espresso

    Toppings

    • Passion fruit pulp
    • Mango coulis
    • Cocoa powder

    Method

    1. Divide biscuit mixture into 12 small ring moulds. Press firmly. Refrigerate.
    2. Beat cream cheese and sugar. Whip cream to soft peaks. Fold together with dissolved gelatin.
    3. Divide cream cheese mixture into three equal portions.
    4. Flavour each: passion fruit, mango, or coffee. Mix well.
    5. Spoon into the prepared moulds — 4 of each flavour.
    6. Refrigerate overnight.
    7. To serve: push out of moulds. Top passion fruit with pulp, mango with coulis, coffee with cocoa.
    8. Arrange three on each plate.

    Chef Tips

    • Make the night before — the flavours develop beautifully overnight.
    • Line moulds with acetate strips for perfect release.
    • Arrange on the plate with deliberate colour contrast — orange, yellow, brown.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not enough gelatin — collapses when unmoulded.
    • Poor release — not lining with acetate.

    The Science

    See KEN234 for cheesecake setting science. In individual portions, the gelatin concentration is particularly critical — too little and they collapse; too much and the texture becomes rubbery. The ideal is a dessert that holds its shape while still feeling creamy on the palate.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

    Reheating: Serve cold.

    Serve with: As a hotel dessert plate, With Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249)
