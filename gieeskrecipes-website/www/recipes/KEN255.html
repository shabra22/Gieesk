🐟 Kenyan Herb-Crusted Fish

Samaki wa Mimea

Kenyan Herb-Crusted Fish — fresh fish fillets coated in a vibrant crust of fresh coriander, parsley, garlic, breadcrumbs, and lemon zest, baked until the crust is golden and the fish is perfectly moist. The elegant fish main course of Kenya's finest restaurants.

Herb-crusted fish is the restaurant fish dish that balances the simplicity of good fresh fish with the elegance of a proper crust. The coriander-dominant herb mixture gives it a distinctly Kenyan character, while the technique — pressing the crust firmly and baking at high heat — produces a perfect golden exterior over moist, flaky fish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Baking tray, Oven (200°C)

  • 4 fish fillets (180g each) — snapper, sea bass, or tilapia

Herb Crust

  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup fresh coriander — packed
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

For the fish

  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard — for adhering the crust

Method

  1. Pulse breadcrumbs, herbs, garlic, lemon zest, oil, salt, and pepper in a food processor until bright green and coarse.
  2. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  3. Season fish fillets with salt and paprika.
  4. Brush the top of each fillet with Dijon mustard.
  5. Press herb crust firmly onto the mustard-coated surface.
  6. Place on a baking tray, crust side up.
  7. Bake 14—18 minutes until crust is golden and fish flakes easily.
  8. Do not flip — bake crust-side up throughout.

Chef Tips

  • The Dijon mustard is the glue that holds the crust to the fish.
  • Press the crust firmly — it should compact onto the fish.
  • Do not flip during baking — the crust will fall apart.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the mustard — crust falls off.
  • Flipping during baking.
  • Overcooking — fish should be just flaking.

The Science

The mustard acts as an adhesive due to its emulsified nature — the proteins and fats in mustard bond to both the fish surface and the breadcrumb crust simultaneously. The breadcrumbs dehydrate in the oven heat, browning through the Maillard reaction to create the characteristic golden crust.

Cultural Note

Herb-crusted fish represents Kenya's fine dining scene at its most confident — taking the country's excellent fresh fish and elevating it with a technique that showcases culinary skill without masking the quality of the ingredient.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C — microwave destroys the crust.

Serve with: Herb Rice (KEN276), Buttered Vegetables (KEN278), Tamarind Sauce (KEN163), Lemon wedges
