🌽 Corn Fufu (Adamu)

Fufu corn

Finely ground corn flour whisked into boiling salted water and cooked, stirring often, until the mixture thickens and the cornmeal is tender — shaped into balls to eat with njama njama or sauces.

The fufu that accompanies the author’s njama njama. Water and salt are brought to a boil, the cornmeal whisked in, brought to a boil again, then reduced to low heat and cooked 10–15 minutes, stirring often, until it thickens and the cornmeal is tender. The author shapes it into balls and warns against shaping on plastic wrap — she says she has been told several times not to, for health reasons. A second printing by Alan Bergo uses 1 cup of flour to 1.5 cups of water and a different technique; the two are not reconciled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Whisk or wooden spoon

  • 1 pound finely ground corn flour
  • 4–5 cups water
  • 2 teaspoons salt (optional; the method uses it)

Method

  1. Bring about 4 cups of water and the salt to a boil.
  2. Whisk in the cornmeal and bring to a boil.
  3. Reduce to low heat and cook until the mixture thickens and the cornmeal is tender, stirring often, about 10–15 minutes.
  4. Shape into balls and serve.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is ‘thickens and the cornmeal is tender’.
  • The list gives 4–5 cups of water and the method starts with about 4; add the rest only if needed.
  • Do not shape the fufu on plastic wrap — the author says she was warned against it for health reasons.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dumping in the cornmeal and getting lumps.
  • Cooking on high after the cornmeal goes in.
  • Using plastic wrap to shape it.
