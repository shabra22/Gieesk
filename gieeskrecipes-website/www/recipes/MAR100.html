🐑 Steamed Sheep's Head (Eid al-Adha)

Rass lkbech mbekhr — steamed head (راس الخروف)

A whole sheep's head, charred to remove the fur, cleaned, cut into pieces and steamed over onion with salt, cumin and pepper; served with cumin, salt and salads.

2h 30mTotal time
1hPrep
1h 30mCook
4Servings
320Calories
MediumDifficulty

The head is charred over coals to remove the fur, then cleaned very well and drained. It is cut into 6 or 8 pieces, sprinkled with salt, cumin and pepper and set in a steamer over onion slices, over water in a pressure cooker with an upturned bowl, and steamed over medium heat for an hour and a half.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker with steamer, Metal bowl, Open flame or coals for charring

  • 1 sheep's head.
  • 1 onion cut into slices.
  • 2 tsp salt.
  • 2 tbsp cumin.
  • 1tsp black pepper.

Method

  1. Char the sheep's head over coals to remove the fur, then clean it very well and drain it.
  2. Place a medium metal bowl upside down in a pressure cooker and add enough water. Cut the head into 6 or 8 pieces.
  3. Put the onion and head pieces in the steamer, sprinkle with the salt, cumin and pepper, and set the steamer inside the cooker, keeping the water level below the bottom of the steamer.
  4. Cover the cooker tightly and cook over medium heat for an hour and a half.
  5. Serve the head on a plate with cumin, salt and some salads or steamed vegetables.

Chef Tips

  • The cooking is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (an hour and a half).
  • Keep the water below the steamer base so the meat steams rather than boils.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not cleaning the head thoroughly after charring.
  • Letting the cooker boil dry.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat the day it is cooked.

Reheating: Re-steam briefly.

Serve with: Salads, such as Zaalouk, Cumin and salt
