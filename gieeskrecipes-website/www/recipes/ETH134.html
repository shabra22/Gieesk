🥦 Ethiopian Vegetable Stew with Berbere

አትክልት ወጥ ቤርቤሬ — Atkilit Wat be-Berbere

Ethiopian Spiced Mixed Vegetable Stew — a mix of seasonal vegetables (eggplant, potato, carrot, bell pepper, tomato) cooked in a berbere sauce. The spiced version of the mixed vegetable wat — for those who want all the heat of berbere in their vegetables.

Spiced mixed vegetable wat applies the full berbere tradition to whatever vegetables are seasonally available — eggplant absorbs the sauce beautifully, potatoes thicken it, and the carrots and peppers add colour and nutrition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1 eggplant — cubed
  • 2 potatoes — cubed
  • 2 carrots — sliced
  • 1 bell pepper — diced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Salt eggplant 15 minutes. Rinse. Dry.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add berbere. Fry 2 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes and bell pepper. Cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes and carrots. Add water. Cover. Cook 12 minutes.
  5. Add eggplant. Cover. Cook 10 more minutes.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add eggplant last — it cooks faster than root vegetables.
  • Salt eggplant to prevent it absorbing excessive oil.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding eggplant too early.
  • Not salting eggplant.

The Science

See ETH125 for eggplant science. The combination of vegetables produces a self-thickening sauce — potato starch and dissolved eggplant flesh create a natural thickener without any added starch.

Cultural Note

The spiced mixed vegetable wat adapts Ethiopian berbere cooking to whatever is available — the principle that the berbere tradition can be applied to any vegetable is one of Ethiopian cuisine's great flexibilities.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter
