🌾 Sorgho-tchap (Sorghum and Bean Stew, Northern Cameroon)

Sorgho-tchap

Red sorghum grains cooked about an hour, red or black beans about 1½ hours, then both simmered 5 minutes in the fried onion and spice base.

p. 20 of the October 2023 recipe book: 1 kg of red sorghum and 750 g of red or black beans, with the same seasonings as Corn-tchap. The sorghum is cooked about an hour and the beans about 1 hour 30; both go into the fried base and simmer 5 minutes. The final step as printed mentions a ‘mélange maïs-haricot’, evidently a carry-over from the maize recipe.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar

  • 1 kg red sorghum grains
  • 750 g red or black beans
  • Oil, tomato, bell pepper, white pepper, onion, parsley, celery, basil, garlic, ginger, salt, chilli, stock cube, rock salt — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Cook the sorghum in water with rock salt about an hour. Cool and wash.
  2. Cook the beans in salted water about 1 hour 30. Wash them.
  3. Chop the onion and bell pepper; crush the other condiments.
  4. Roast the onion, add the crushed condiments and simmer. Season with salt and the stock cube.
  5. Add the sorghum and beans and simmer 5 minutes. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Same pattern as Corn-tchap with sorghum in place of maize.
  • The final step on the page mentions a maize-bean mix — a copy error in the source.
  • Seasoning quantities are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Taking the maize-bean wording in the last step literally.
  • Under-cooking the sorghum.
  • Skipping the wash after cooking.
