🌭 Kenyan Sausage Roll

Sausage Roll

Kenyan Sausage Roll — spiced minced beef or sausage wrapped in flaky, golden shortcrust pastry and baked until crisp. A staple of every Kenyan bakery, tea room, and school tuck shop. The most satisfying savoury pastry in the Kenyan snack repertoire.

The Kenyan sausage roll is a fixture of urban life — found in every bakery from Mombasa to Kisumu, wrapped in paper and eaten warm with a cup of tea. The Kenyan version uses well-spiced minced beef or sausage meat with coriander and Kenyan spices, wrapped in a buttery shortcrust that becomes deeply golden in the oven. It is the snack that fuels school breaks and office tea times across the country.

Ingredients

Equipment: Rolling pin, Baking tray, Knife, Pastry brush

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 150g cold butter — cubed
  • 6—8 tbsp cold water
  • 400g minced beef or sausage meat
  • 1 medium onion — finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • ½ cup fresh coriander — chopped
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg — beaten with 1 tbsp water

Method

  1. Make pastry: rub cold butter into flour and salt until it resembles breadcrumbs. Add cold water 1 tbsp at a time until dough just comes together. Wrap and rest in fridge 20 minutes.
  2. Make filling: cook onion in a little oil until soft. Add garlic, minced beef, and all spices. Cook until the beef is browned and the mixture is dry. Add coriander. Cool completely.
  3. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  4. Roll pastry into a rectangle about 3mm thick.
  5. Place a log of filling along the long edge. Roll the pastry over to encase it. Seal the edge with egg wash.
  6. Cut into 8—10 cm lengths. Score the tops lightly.
  7. Brush all over with egg wash.
  8. Bake for 20—25 minutes until deep golden and crispy.
  9. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Cold butter is essential for flaky pastry — warm butter produces crumbly, tough results.
  • The filling must be completely cool and dry — wet filling makes the pastry soggy.
  • Score the tops lightly before baking to allow steam to escape.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Warm butter in the pastry — not flaky.
  • Wet filling — soggy pastry.
  • Not sealing edges properly — filling falls out during baking.

The Science

Rubbing cold butter into flour creates small pockets of fat that melt during baking, releasing steam that separates the pastry into flaky layers. The fat also coats the flour proteins, preventing them from forming long gluten strands — making the pastry tender rather than tough.

Cultural Note

Sausage rolls are a beloved fixture of Kenyan bakeries and tea rooms — the savoury pastry that has been part of urban Kenyan snack culture for generations, always served warm alongside a cup of tea.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Freeze uncooked for 2 months.

Reheating: Oven at 180°C for 10 minutes. Avoid microwave.

Serve with: Kenyan Masala Chai, Tomato Sauce, Pili Pili Sauce, Fresh Juice
