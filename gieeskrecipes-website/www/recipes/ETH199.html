🎂 Ethiopian Honey Cake

ማር ኬክ — Mar Kek

Ethiopian Honey Cake — a light sponge cake made with Ethiopian mountain honey, cardamom, and cinnamon, filled and frosted with honey-whipped cream. The modern Ethiopian celebration cake — served at birthdays, weddings, and New Year celebrations.

Ethiopian honey cake is the modern celebration cake — where traditional Ethiopian sweets are dense and spiced, the honey cake is light and airy but deeply fragrant with the Ethiopian spice tradition. The mountain honey permeates the sponge, and the honey whipped cream frosting is extraordinarily delicious.

Ingredients

Equipment: 2 × 20cm cake tins, Electric mixer, Oven at 170°C

    Honey Sponge

    • 3 eggs — separated
    • 4 tbsp Ethiopian honey
    • 3 tbsp sugar
    • ½ cup all-purpose flour
    • ½ tsp baking powder
    • 1 tsp ground cardamom
    • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
    • Pinch of salt

    Honey Cream Frosting

    • 300ml heavy whipping cream
    • 2 tbsp Ethiopian honey
    • ½ tsp ground cardamom

    Filling: 2 tbsp honey mixed with 1 tbsp warm water (soaking syrup)

      Method

      1. Whisk egg yolks with honey and sugar until pale and thick.
      2. Beat egg whites with a pinch of salt to stiff peaks.
      3. Fold flour, baking powder, cardamom, and cinnamon into yolk mixture.
      4. Fold egg whites into the batter in three additions — gently.
      5. Divide between two greased tins.
      6. Bake at 170°C for 20—25 minutes. Cool completely.
      7. Make frosting: whip cream with honey and cardamom to stiff peaks.
      8. Brush each cake layer with honey soaking syrup.
      9. Stack with honey cream between and on top.
      10. Refrigerate 1 hour before serving.

      Chef Tips

      • Folding egg whites gently is the key to a light sponge — deflating them produces a dense cake.
      • The honey soaking syrup keeps the sponge moist.
      • Ethiopian mountain honey in the whipped cream is transformative — the floral notes are extraordinary.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Over-mixing the egg whites into the batter — deflates them.
      • Serving immediately — the cake needs 1 hour in the fridge to set.

      The Science

      The egg white foam provides the primary leavening in this cake — the mechanical incorporation of air creates a foam matrix that expands during baking. The honey's fructose, being more hygroscopic than sucrose, keeps the sponge moist for longer after baking.

      Cultural Note

      Ethiopian honey cake represents the new Ethiopian celebration culture — modern in format, completely Ethiopian in spirit. The young Ethiopian who grew up eating injera and Doro Wat now also celebrates birthdays with a honey-cardamom cake that tastes like home in a new form.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

      Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

      Serve with: At Ethiopian birthday celebrations, At weddings, At Ethiopian New Year (Enkutatash)
