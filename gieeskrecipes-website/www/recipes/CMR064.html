🍗 Poulet au dalang (Chicken with Dalang)

Poulet au dalang

A hen cut in pieces, marinated 15–20 minutes, fried with onion, then simmered with dalang, a little water, ginger, garlic and salt until tender, adding water until the end.

p. 66 of the October 2023 recipe book. A hen is cut in pieces. Onion is fried in oil and the chicken added; then dalang, a little water, ginger, crushed garlic and salt; it cooks until tender, adding water until the end. The tips: marinate 15–20 minutes, and let the first water evaporate before adding more. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot

  • 1 hen, cut in pieces — weight not printed
  • Oil, onion, garlic, ginger, salt, dalang, chilli, water — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken 15–20 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion in oil. Add the chicken.
  3. Add the dalang, a little water, ginger, crushed garlic and salt.
  4. Cook until tender, adding water until the end. Let the first water evaporate before adding more.

Chef Tips

  • The printed doneness cue is ‘until tender’.
  • Let the first water evaporate before adding more.
  • Quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all the water at once.
  • Skipping the marinade.
  • Stopping before the chicken is tender.
