🧀 Pecorino di Fossa con Miele

Pecorino di Fossa con Miele

A distinctive sheep's milk cheese aged in underground pits, its intense, earthy flavor balanced simply with a drizzle of local honey and perhaps a few walnuts.

Pecorino di fossa gets its name and character from a genuinely unusual aging method — the cheese wheels are wrapped and buried in straw-lined pits for months, a practice with roots tracing back to medieval times when hiding food stores from raiders was a practical necessity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp knife, Serving platter

  • 300 g (10.5 oz) Pecorino di Fossa, at room temperature
  • 3 tbsp chestnut or wildflower honey
  • 40 g (⅓ cup) walnuts, roughly chopped
  • Crusty bread, for serving

Method

  1. Bring the cheese to room temperature before serving to allow its full flavor and aroma to develop.
  2. Slice or crumble the pecorino onto a serving platter.
  3. Drizzle generously with honey.
  4. Scatter with chopped walnuts.
  5. Serve alongside crusty bread.

Chef Tips

  • Let the cheese sit out for at least 20–30 minutes before serving — its aroma and flavor are much duller straight from the refrigerator.
  • A stronger honey, like chestnut, stands up well to the cheese's intense, earthy character.
  • This makes an excellent simple end to a meal, in the way many Italians close dinner with cheese rather than a sweet dessert.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving the cheese too cold, which mutes its distinctive flavor.
  • Pairing it with a very mild, delicate honey that gets lost against the cheese's intensity.
  • Cutting it too far ahead of serving, letting it dry out at the edges.

Health Benefits

  • A good source of calcium and protein; honey provides natural sweetness with some antioxidant compounds.

Cultural Note

Pecorino di Fossa is a protected specialty of the Marche and neighboring Emilia-Romagna, its pit-aging tradition tracing back centuries to when hiding cheese from raiders and tax collectors was a genuine necessity.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store the wrapped cheese refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: A glass of Vin Santo or a robust red wine
