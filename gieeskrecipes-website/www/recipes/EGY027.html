🍤 Breaded Fried Shrimp (Egyptian Ramadan Table)

Gambari maqli — جمبري مقلي

Medium shrimp marinated in lemon, garlic and cumin for one to two hours, then dredged in flour, egg and breadcrumbs and deep-fried until golden.

This Ramadan-series recipe marinates shrimp in lemon juice, garlic and cumin for one to two hours, then dredges them in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs and deep-fries them on high until golden.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Three shallow bowls for dredging

  • 1 kg shrimp, medium size – gam-ba-ri
  • 1 egg, beaten – beid
  • 2 tsp garlic, crushed – tom
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice – a-seer la-moon
  • 2 tbsp cumin – kam-moon
  • 3/4 cup flour – de-'ee'
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs – bo-so-mat
  • oil for frying – zeit
  • salt and pepper – malh and fel-fel

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with the lemon juice, garlic, cumin, salt and pepper and marinate 1 to 2 hours.
  2. Dredge in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs.
  3. Deep-fry in hot oil on high heat until golden.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: golden. The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (1 to 2 hours). The page prints no frying time.
  • Fry in small batches to keep the oil hot (our tip).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the shrimp.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Re-crisp in a hot oven.

Serve with: Sayadiya rice
