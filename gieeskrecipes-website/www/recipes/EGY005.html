🍢 Baked Egyptian Kofta (Oven Kofta with Cold Butter)

Kofta — كفتة

Chilled minced beef or lamb mixed with onion, spices, egg white, cornstarch and grated cold butter, shaped on skewers and baked on a rack until just cooked.

1h 35mTotal time
15mPrep
20mCook
9Servings
220Calories
MediumDifficulty

Minced meat is kept cold, mixed with squeezed onion, cumin, coriander, egg white, cornstarch and shredded cold butter, then chilled for an hour or overnight. The kofta are shaped on skewers using the saved onion water and baked on a rack with a little water in the tray at 200C/392F for 18 to 20 minutes when large, with a check at 16 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Skewers, Baking tray with rack, Oven

  • 500 grams minced beef or lamb has to be chilled
  • 2 medium onion minced Squeezed and its water kept aside
  • ¼ cup shredded chilled unsalted butter
  • ¼ teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper powder
  • ½ teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg white from large egg
  • 1 ½ tablespoon cornstarch

For shaping

  • Onion water from the two onions minced
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon sugar

Method

  1. Mix the cold meat with the spices, baking powder, egg white, cornstarch and squeezed onion solids for about a minute, then add the shredded cold butter and mix for another minute.
  2. Chill for one hour, or overnight.
  3. Mix the saved onion water with the ⅛ teaspoon salt and the sugar. Wet your hands with it and shape the meat onto skewers.
  4. Set the skewers on a rack over a tray with ¼ cup of water. Bake in the middle of a 200C/392F oven for 18 to 20 minutes for large koftas, checking for doneness after 16 minutes. Regular-size koftas take about 10 to 11 minutes.
  5. Optional smoke: heat a piece of charcoal over a flame for 5 minutes, place it in a bowl, cover with the kofta for 2 to 3 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: check doneness after 16 minutes for large koftas. The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • Keep the meat and butter cold so the butter stays in pieces and melts in the oven.
  • Soak wooden skewers in warm water 30 to 50 minutes (single reading).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-mixing after adding the butter.
  • Baking without water in the tray, which dries the kofta.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked kofta up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat in a covered pan or oven.

Serve with: Rice with vermicelli, Egyptian tahini sauce
