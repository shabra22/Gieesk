🥩 Amhara Kitfo Special

አምሓራ ክትፎ — Amhara Kitfo

Amhara Kitfo — finely minced highland beef warmed in kibbeh with more mitmita than the standard recipe. The Amhara highland version of kitfo uses more mitmita and less berbere, reflecting the regional spice preference for the fierce heat of bird's eye chilli over the complex heat of berbere.

Amhara kitfo is hotter than Gurage kitfo — the highland preference for mitmita over berbere produces a fiercer, more immediate heat. Served with the finest Amhara ayib and gomen.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small pan

  • 600g freshest possible beef mince — hand-chopped preferred
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1.5 tsp mitmita
  • ½ tsp berbere
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Warm kibbeh over lowest heat.
  2. Add beef mince. Toss to warm — maximum 2 minutes.
  3. Add mitmita and berbere. Toss.
  4. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Freshness is the absolute requirement.
  • Amhara version: more mitmita than berbere.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not-fresh beef.

Cultural Note

Amhara kitfo is the highland celebration of raw beef — fiercer than the Gurage version, reflecting the Amhara palate's love for mitmita heat.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Amhara Ayib, Gomen (ETH106)
