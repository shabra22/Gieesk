🍜 Tanzanian Coconut Prawn Curry Bowl

Bakuli la Kamba wa Nazi

Tanzanian Prawn Curry Bowl — coconut milk prawn curry over coconut jasmine rice with pickled cucumber, crispy shallots, fresh coriander, and a lime wedge. The modern Tanzanian grain bowl — deconstructed coastal prawn curry assembled with contemporary restaurant presentation.

The prawn curry bowl is Tanzania's answer to the global grain bowl trend — the coastal prawn curry deconstructed and reassembled in the contemporary bowl format. Each component is prepared to its highest standard and arranged deliberately rather than pooled together. The result feels modern and international while tasting purely Tanzanian.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan for curry, Small pan for shallots, Rice cooker or pot

  • 400g large prawns — peeled

Zanzibar Coconut Prawn Curry

  • 300ml coconut milk
  • 2 shallots — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — sliced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste

Bowl Base: 2 cups cooked coconut jasmine rice

    Pickled Cucumber: ½ cucumber, 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sugar, pinch salt

      Crispy Shallots: 2 shallots, thinly sliced, deep fried until golden

        Garnish: coriander, sesame seeds, lime, sliced chilli

          Method

          1. Make pickled cucumber: toss cucumber with vinegar, sugar, and salt. Rest 20 minutes.
          2. Make crispy shallots: deep fry thinly sliced shallots at 160°C until golden. Drain. They crisp as they cool.
          3. Cook coconut jasmine rice with coconut milk and water.
          4. Make prawn curry: sauté shallots and garlic. Add spices, tomatoes, tamarind. Cook 6 minutes. Add coconut milk. Simmer 5 minutes. Add prawns. Cook 4 minutes.
          5. Bowl assembly: rice in base → curry beside → pickled cucumber → crispy shallots scattered over → coriander → sesame seeds → lime wedge → sliced chilli.

          Chef Tips

          • The crispy shallots are the textural contrast that elevates this from good to great.
          • The pickled cucumber cuts through the richness of the coconut curry.
          • Bowl presentation: everything should be visible from above — arrange deliberately.
          • Serve immediately — the crispy shallots soften quickly.

          Common Mistakes to Avoid

          • Soggy shallots — must be added at the last moment.
          • Skipping the pickled cucumber — the acidity is essential.

          The Science

          See TZA084 for prawn curry science. The bowl format's genius is textural contrast — the soft rice, creamy curry sauce, crispy shallots, and crunchy pickled cucumber together provide four different textures in one bowl, creating a more satisfying eating experience than any single texture alone.

          Cultural Note

          The Tanzanian prawn curry bowl is the dish that Tanzania's modern restaurant scene serves to signal its sophistication — the same prawn curry that grandmothers have made for centuries, reframed in the contemporary bowl format, presented with precision and pride.

          Storage & Serving

          Storage: Components separately. Assemble to order.

          Reheating: Reheat curry gently. Warm rice. Add fresh garnishes.
