🥜 Ethiopian Spiced Roasted Peanuts

ሰሊጥ ቆሎ — Groundnut Qolo

Ethiopian Spiced Roasted Peanuts — raw peanuts dry-roasted with salt, mitmita, and berbere. The Ethiopian street vendor staple — sold in paper cones at every market, bus stop, and street corner, eaten as a walking snack throughout the day.

Spiced roasted peanuts are the most portable and affordable protein snack in Ethiopia — the street vendors who sell them from large flat pans on charcoal are a fixture of Ethiopian urban life. The mitmita-berbere spicing makes them distinctly Ethiopian.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy dry pan

  • 2 cups raw peanuts — in shell or shelled
  • 1 tsp coarse salt
  • ¼ tsp mitmita
  • ¼ tsp berbere

Method

  1. Heat a heavy dry pan over medium heat.
  2. Add raw peanuts.
  3. Roast stirring constantly 15—18 minutes until the shells are lightly charred and the peanuts smell roasted.
  4. If shelled: 12—14 minutes until golden.
  5. Remove from heat. Immediately toss with salt, mitmita, and berbere while hot.
  6. Cool. The spices adhere as the peanuts cool.

Chef Tips

  • In-shell peanuts are traditional — the shell chars slightly, adding a smoky note.
  • Add spices while hot — they adhere better to the warm, slightly oily surface.
  • Stir constantly — peanuts burn quickly once they start browning.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not stirring — burns one side.
  • Adding spices when cold — they fall off.

The Science

Raw peanuts contain a high concentration of p-coumaric acid (a weak antioxidant). Roasting converts this to resveratrol — the same powerful antioxidant found in red wine — dramatically increasing the nutritional benefit. The Maillard reactions also produce pyrazines, responsible for the characteristic roasted peanut aroma.

  • Very high in protein and healthy fats.

Cultural Note

Spiced roasted peanuts are the sound and smell of Ethiopian street life — the vendor's pan on glowing charcoal, the scent of roasting nuts drifting through the market, the paper cone cone filled to order. They are the most democratic food in Ethiopia — available to everyone.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container — 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not needed.

Serve with: As a walking street snack, With tej (ETH095) in tej houses, As a market snack
