Addis Ababa Street Food Tour — Street Chechebsa (ETH171), Sambusa (ETH161), Ful (ETH137), Multi-Layer Juice (ETH223), and Ethiopian Coffee (ETH212). The complete Addis Ababa street food experience — the African Union capital's extraordinary food culture from morning to night.

Addis Ababa at 2,355 metres elevation is Africa's highest major city and its most extraordinary food destination — the convergence of all Ethiopian regional food traditions with modern urban innovation.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple pans and blender

  • Street Chechebsa
  • Sambusa — lentil and meat (ETH161, ETH168)
  • Ful Medames
  • Multi-Layer Juice
  • Ethiopian Coffee

Method

  1. Morning: Chechebsa with fried egg.
  2. Mid-morning: fresh juice at a juice bar.
  3. Lunch: Sambusa with silsi.
  4. Afternoon: Ethiopian coffee ceremony.
  5. Evening: Ful with flatbread.

Chef Tips

  • The Addis food day is organized by time — each meal has its moment.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Combining everything at once — the sequence is the experience.

Cultural Note

Addis Ababa is where all of Ethiopia's food traditions converge — the Tigrinya, the Oromo, the Gurage, the Harari, and the Somali-Ethiopian communities all contribute their food culture to the capital's extraordinary street food scene. To eat your way through Addis is to eat through Ethiopia itself.
