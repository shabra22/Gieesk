🥣 Bouillie au natron (Natron Porridge)

Bouillie au natron

The same sweet peanut porridge as the tamarind one, finished with 10 g of soaked natron and its soak-water; the book recommends it for women who have just given birth.

p. 35 of the October 2023 recipe book. 500 g of maize or sorghum flour, 100 g of roasted peanut paste, 250 g of sugar and 10 g of natron. The method follows the tamarind porridge except that the natron is soaked and its soak-water (‘jus de natron’) added at the end. The book recommends it for women who have just given birth and for ‘digestive cleansing’, saying natron ‘cleans the belly efficiently’. THAT HEALTH CLAIM IS THE BOOK’S, UNSUPPORTED BY ANY CITED EVIDENCE, AND THE BOOK STATES NO CAUTION FOR THE NATRON QUANTITY. Soak time and water volume are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Fine sieve or cloth

  • 500 g maize or sorghum flour
  • 100 g roasted peanut paste
  • 250 g sugar
  • 10 g natron
  • Water — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Soak the natron in water.
  2. Knead the peanut paste in clean water, strain and bring the filtrate to the boil.
  3. Mix the flour with water to a smooth paste and pour gradually into the boiling filtrate, stirring 10 minutes.
  4. Add the sugar and cook 10 minutes on low heat.
  5. Add the natron soak-water and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Natron soak time and water volume are not printed.
  • The health claims are the book’s and are unsupported there.
  • The book gives no caution for the natron quantity.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding natron dry.
  • Treating the digestive claim as established.
  • Adding more than the printed 10 g.
