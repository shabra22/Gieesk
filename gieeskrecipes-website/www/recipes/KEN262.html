🍗 Kenyan Chicken Lasagna

Lasagna ya Kuku ya Kenya

Kenyan Chicken Lasagna — layers of pasta with a creamy spiced chicken and mushroom filling and golden béchamel. The lighter, equally beloved alternative to the beef version — particularly popular with families who prefer chicken.

The chicken lasagna version uses a creamy chicken and mushroom filling instead of the tomato-based bolognese, producing a lighter, creamier result that many prefer. The Kenyan spicing of the filling with paprika and coriander gives it local character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan, Saucepan, 30x20cm baking dish, Oven

  • 12 lasagna sheets

Chicken Filling

  • 600g chicken breast — cooked and shredded
  • 200g mushrooms — sliced
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 tbsp coriander
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp salt

Béchamel (same as KEN261)

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 4 cups milk
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 50g parmesan

Method

  1. Cook chicken filling: sauté onion, garlic, and mushrooms in butter until golden. Add shredded chicken, paprika, oregano, cream, and stock. Simmer until thick. Add coriander.
  2. Make béchamel as per KEN261.
  3. Layer and bake as per KEN261 steps 3—7.

Chef Tips

  • Shred chicken finely for even distribution throughout the layers.
  • The chicken filling must be thick and dry before layering.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet chicken filling — soggy lasagna.
  • Not seasoning generously.

Cultural Note

Chicken lasagna demonstrates that Italian baked pasta has been fully absorbed into the Kenyan restaurant and home cooking repertoire — adapted with local spices and ingredients into something uniquely Kenyan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C covered for 20 minutes.

Serve with: Caesar Salad (KEN267), Garlic Bread (KEN279)
