🍲 Zuppa Gallurese

Suppa Cuata

Sardinia's misleadingly-named 'soup,' actually a rich, baked casserole layering stale bread and cheese soaked in meat broth until it melts into something closer to a savory bread pudding.

Despite the name, zuppa gallurese contains no liquid to spoon by the time it's served - the bread is meant to fully absorb the broth during baking, resulting in a dense, cheese-laden dish from the Gallura region in Sardinia's northeast.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking dish

  • 400 g (14 oz) stale rustic bread, sliced
  • 400 g (14 oz) pecorino sardo or a mix of pecorino and mild melting cheese, sliced
  • 1.5 L (6 cups) meat broth (beef or lamb), hot
  • Fresh mint, chopped
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Layer half the bread slices in the bottom of a baking dish.
  2. Scatter with half the garlic, mint, and parsley, then top with half the sliced cheese.
  3. Repeat with the remaining bread, herbs, garlic, and cheese.
  4. Pour the hot broth evenly over the layers until well saturated but not swimming in liquid.
  5. Season with black pepper.
  6. Bake at 200C (400F) for 40-45 minutes until the top is golden and the broth has been fully absorbed.
  7. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • A good, flavorful meat broth is essential, since it is the primary source of flavor throughout the dish.
  • Let the dish rest after baking, which helps it set enough to hold together when served.
  • Genuine pecorino sardo gives the most authentic result, though a mix with a milder melting cheese works well too.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using a weak, underseasoned broth, which leaves the whole dish tasting flat.
  • Adding too much broth, resulting in a soggy rather than properly set casserole.
  • Serving immediately without letting it rest, when the structure needs time to firm up.

Cultural Note

Zuppa gallurese takes its name from the Gallura region in northeastern Sardinia, its misleading name belying a dense, hearty baked dish rather than a spoonable soup.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat covered in the oven until warmed through.

Serve with: A robust Sardinian red wine
