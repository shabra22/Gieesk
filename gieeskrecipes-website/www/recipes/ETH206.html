⭐ Ethiopian Christmas Sweet (Genna)

ገና ጣፋጭ — Genna Tafach

Ethiopian Christmas Cookies — spiced honey cookies made specifically for Genna (Ethiopian Christmas, January 7th) after the 43-day Advent fast. Shaped as stars and crosses using the symbolism of the Ethiopian Orthodox tradition, they are the first sweets eaten after Advent.

Genna cookies are the first sweet after 43 days of fasting — the breaking of Advent fast with honey cookies in Christmas shapes (stars, crosses) is a moment of pure joy. The Ethiopian Christmas sweet.

          Cultural Note

          Genna cookies are the taste of Ethiopian Christmas morning — after the overnight church service, after the Christmas dawn celebration, the first sweet thing eaten after 43 days of fasting. A star-shaped honey cookie from a grandmother is one of the most emotionally significant foods in Ethiopian Orthodox life.

          Storage & Serving

          Reheating: Not needed.

          Serve with: After the Genna midnight mass, With the Christmas morning feast
