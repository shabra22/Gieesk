🍖 Slow-Roasted Lamb Shoulder (Mechoui, Oven Version)

Mechoui — roasted lamb (مشوي)

A bone-in lamb shoulder rubbed with allspice, cumin, chilli, turmeric, garlic and butter, sealed in foil and roasted 8 hours at 140°C until meltingly tender; served with cumin salt, bread and orange wedges.

The spices, garlic and butter are rubbed all over a 2.5 kg bone-in shoulder, which is seasoned with salt, completely enclosed in two large sheets of foil in a deep roasting pan and roasted 8 hours at 140°C, or until meltingly tender and coming away from the bone. The meat is shredded into large pieces and served with toasted ground cumin seeds mixed with sea salt.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep roasting pan, Foil

  • 1 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 100 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 2.5 kg lamb shoulder, bone in
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds, toasted, roughly ground
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes
  • bread and orange wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 140°C. Combine the spices, garlic and butter in a bowl.
  2. Rub the mixture all over the lamb and season with salt.
  3. Completely enclose the lamb in two large sheets of foil and set in a deep roasting pan.
  4. Roast for 8 hours, or until the meat is meltingly tender and comes away easily from the bone.
  5. Combine the toasted cumin seeds with the sea salt. Shred the lamb into large pieces and serve with the cumin salt, bread and orange wedges.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: meltingly tender and comes away easily from the bone.
  • The page lists one tablespoon of salt flakes without splitting it between the rub and the cumin salt.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Opening the foil early so the steam escapes.
  • Using a boneless cut, which cooks differently.

Cultural Note

The headnote describes mechoui as a whole lamb or sheep spit-roasted or roasted underground for a festive occasion, traditionally eaten whole including the organs, with liver, kidney and eyes reserved for guests of honour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate leftovers up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat covered in a moderate oven with a splash of liquid.

Serve with: Bread, Orange wedges and cumin salt
