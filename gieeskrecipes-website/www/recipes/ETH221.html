🥑 Ethiopian Avocado Juice

አቮካዶ ጭማቂ — Avocado Chumaki

Ethiopian Avocado Juice — ripe avocado blended with milk, a little honey, and cardamom into a thick, velvety green drink. One of the most distinctive features of Ethiopia's juice bar culture — blended avocado as a drink is unusual globally but beloved in Ethiopia.

Ethiopian avocado juice is one of the things that most surprises international visitors to Addis Ababa's juice bars — the ripe avocado blended with cold milk and honey into a thick, smooth, rich green drink that is unlike anything else. Ethiopia's highlands grow some of Africa's finest avocados.

Ingredients

Equipment: High-powered blender

  • 2 ripe avocados — peeled, stoned
  • 1 cup cold whole milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Add avocado flesh, milk, honey, and cardamom to blender.
  2. Blend until completely smooth and velvety.
  3. Add ice. Blend briefly.
  4. Taste — adjust honey.
  5. Pour into glasses. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The avocado must be perfectly ripe — unripe avocado produces a bitter, starchy drink.
  • Serve immediately — avocado oxidises and turns brown quickly.
  • Ethiopian avocados are larger and richer than imported varieties — the flavour difference is significant.
  • Often layered over mango juice in Addis Ababa juice bars — the green over orange is visually stunning.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unripe avocado.
  • Delayed service — browns quickly.

The Science

Avocado's flesh is primarily monounsaturated fat (oleic acid) — when blended with cold milk, the fat creates a stable emulsion with the milk proteins, producing the characteristically velvety, thick texture. The fat also carries the avocado's subtle aromatic compounds, distributing them through every sip.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian avocado juice is the drink that makes international visitors to Addis Ababa's juice bars stop in their tracks — the idea of blended avocado as a drink is unusual elsewhere, but in Ethiopia it is completely natural and completely delicious. The thick, velvety green juice layered over bright orange mango juice in a tall glass is one of the most distinctive images of Addis Ababa's modern food culture.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: At Addis Ababa juice bars, Layered with mango juice, As a breakfast drink
