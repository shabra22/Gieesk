Baci di Alassio

Delicate hazelnut and chocolate meringue-style cookies from the Ligurian coastal town of Alassio, two domed halves sandwiched together with a rich chocolate ganache.

Similar in spirit to Piedmont's baci di dama but built on a lighter, meringue-based hazelnut cookie rather than a buttery shortbread, these cookies have been a signature of Alassio's pasticcerie since the early 20th century.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Baking sheet, Piping bag

  • 200 g (1 1/3 cups) hazelnuts, toasted and finely ground
  • 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
  • 2 egg whites
  • 30 g (1/4 cup) cocoa powder
  • 120 g (4.2 oz) dark chocolate, melted, for the filling
  • 40 g (3 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened, for the filling

Method

  1. Combine the ground hazelnuts, sugar, egg whites, and cocoa powder into a smooth, thick paste.
  2. Rest the mixture for 20 minutes.
  3. Pipe or spoon small, even domes of the mixture onto a lined baking sheet, spacing them apart.
  4. Bake at 150C (300F) for 12-15 minutes until just set but still soft inside.
  5. Cool completely on the tray, as the cookies are delicate while warm.
  6. Combine the melted chocolate and softened butter into a smooth ganache.
  7. Spread a small amount of ganache on the flat side of one cookie and press a matching cookie on top to sandwich.
  8. Let the ganache set before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Grind the hazelnuts as finely as possible for the smoothest cookie texture.
  • Keep the domes small and evenly sized so they pair up neatly once sandwiched.
  • Let the cookies cool completely before handling, as they are quite delicate while still warm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-baking, which dries out the cookies and loses their intended soft, slightly chewy center.
  • Handling the warm cookies too soon, causing them to break.
  • Using too much filling, which can make the delicate cookies difficult to bite through neatly.

Cultural Note

Baci di Alassio have been a signature specialty of the Ligurian coastal town of Alassio since the early 20th century, distinct from Piedmont's similar baci di dama in their lighter, meringue-based texture.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Espresso
