🍋 Yassa au Poulet

Yassa au poulet — ma recette inratable

Her no-fail yassa — the seasoning pounded and split two-thirds to one-third, the chicken stuffed with roff, grilled at 220 degrees for forty minutes.

A Senegalese cook's own version, and the structure is the lesson: the pounded seasoning is divided, a third reserved for the sauce and two-thirds mixed with lemon juice, salt, pepper and oil for the chicken. The chicken is cut into medium pieces, incisions made and stuffed with roff, coated with the marinade and refrigerated at least two hours. Then a tablespoon of mustard is brushed on and it grills at 220 degrees for forty minutes, turned halfway. The sauce is onions softened until translucent with the reserved third, cooked until you get a good caramel colour. Her mother's line: making a yassa is easy, but making a good yassa is not so simple.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar and pestle, Oven or grill, Pan, for the sauce, Basting brush

    Marinade, as printed

    • 2 lemons
    • 6 cloves garlic
    • 2 spring onions, or 1 onion
    • 1 red bell pepper
    • 2 vegetarian chillies (optional)
    • 1 good handful of coriander (optional)
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • Salt and pepper
    • 1 tbsp mustard — appears in the method, not the printed list

    Not quantified in the source

    • Chicken, cut into medium pieces
    • Onions, for the sauce
    • Roff, for stuffing the incisions
    • Bay leaf

    Method

    1. Pound the garlic, spring onions, bell pepper, chillies and coriander into a seasoning paste.
    2. Reserve 1/3 of the pounded seasoning for the sauce.
    3. Add the lemon juice, salt, pepper and olive oil to the other 2/3.
    4. Cut the chicken into medium pieces, make incisions and stuff them with the roff.
    5. Coat the chicken with the 2/3 marinade and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
    6. Brush the chicken with 1 tablespoon of mustard.
    7. Grill at 220 degrees for 40 minutes, turning halfway.

    The sauce

    1. Heat oil and add the onions with a bay leaf, stirring often.
    2. Once the onions turn translucent, add the reserved third of the seasoning, salt and pepper.
    3. Cook over medium heat until you get a good caramel colour.
    4. Serve the chicken with the sauce.

    Chef Tips

    • Split the seasoning before the lemon goes in. A third stays dry for the sauce; two-thirds get the acid and the oil for the chicken. Mixing it all together loses you the sauce's backbone.
    • Until you get a good caramel colour — her sauce endpoint, and it is a colour not a clock.
    • Stuff the incisions with roff as well as coating the outside. Two layers of seasoning, which is the Senegalese approach to any protein.
    • Two hours minimum in the fridge. She does not offer a shortcut and there is not one.
    • Two hundred and twenty degrees for forty minutes, turned halfway. That is hot and fast, which is what gives colour without drying the pieces.
    • Her mother's line is worth keeping in mind: making a yassa is easy, but making a good yassa is not so simple. The difference is in the onions.
    • Note an internal contradiction in the source — the introduction says there is no mustard in the sauce while step six brushes the chicken with a tablespoon of it. Both appear on the page and neither is corrected here.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Mixing all the seasoning with the lemon, leaving nothing for the sauce.
    • Only coating the chicken rather than stuffing the incisions.
    • A short marinade.
    • Pulling the sauce before it reaches caramel colour.
    • Not turning the chicken halfway.

    • Gluten-free and dairy-free.

    Cultural Note

    Calling a recipe inratable — no-fail — and then quoting your mother saying a good yassa is not so simple is a very Senegalese kind of confidence. The Casamance attribution she gives matters: yassa is a southern dish that conquered the country, and the wood fire it was grilled over is what the 220-degree oven is standing in for.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: 4 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen.

    Reheating: Oven for the chicken; warm the sauce separately.

    Serve with: White rice, Kaani, Attaya
