🕊️ Colomba Pasquale

Colomba di Pasqua

Panettone's dove-shaped Easter cousin, its enriched dough baked in a distinctive bird silhouette and topped with a crackling almond glaze and pearl sugar.

Colomba shares nearly the same base dough and slow fermentation process as panettone, but is shaped into the silhouette of a dove, a symbol of peace, and finished with a glossy almond and sugar topping unique to this Easter tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Dove-shaped colomba paper mold, Stand mixer, Skewers for cooling

  • 500 g (4 cups) bread flour
  • 130 g (⅔ cup) granulated sugar
  • 4 eggs, plus 3 egg yolks
  • 150 g (⅔ cup) unsalted butter, softened
  • 120 ml (½ cup) warm milk
  • 10 g (3 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 80 g (½ cup) candied orange peel, chopped
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 5 g (1 tsp) salt
  • 2 egg whites, for the glaze
  • 80 g (⅔ cup) whole almonds
  • 60 g (½ cup) granulated sugar, for the glaze
  • 20 g (2½ tbsp) cornstarch, for the glaze
  • Pearl sugar, for topping

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk with a pinch of sugar, and let sit for 10 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, remaining sugar, and salt in a stand mixer, then add the eggs, yolks, and yeast mixture, kneading into a smooth dough.
  3. Gradually knead in the softened butter until fully incorporated and glossy.
  4. Add the orange zest, then let rise, covered, for 3-4 hours until doubled.
  5. Fold in the candied orange peel.
  6. Shape and place the dough into a dove-shaped colomba mold, then let rise again for 4-6 hours.
  7. For the glaze, whisk the egg whites with the sugar and cornstarch until smooth, then gently spread over the risen dough and scatter with whole almonds and pearl sugar.
  8. Bake at 170C (340F) for 35-40 minutes, tenting if needed, until a skewer comes out clean.
  9. Cool upside down on skewers, as with panettone, to prevent collapsing.

Chef Tips

  • The almond glaze should be spread gently, as the risen dough is very delicate at this stage.
  • Like panettone, cooling upside down is essential to prevent the tall, airy structure from collapsing.
  • This bread shares its base dough with panettone, so the same patience with the long rises applies here too.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Pressing too hard while spreading the glaze, which can deflate the delicate risen dough.
  • Skipping the upside-down cooling method, resulting in a collapsed loaf.
  • Underproofing, which leaves a denser crumb than the traditional light, pull-apart texture.

Health Benefits

  • Almonds provide healthy fats and vitamin E; best enjoyed as a festive treat.

Cultural Note

Colomba Pasquale is Italy's essential Easter bread, its dove shape symbolizing peace, and it remains as central to Italian Easter as panettone is to Christmas.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Mascarpone cream, A glass of Moscato or Prosecco
