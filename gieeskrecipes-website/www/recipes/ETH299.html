      Method

      1. Day before: marinate chicken (ETH282). Make cheesecake.
      2. Day of: all fasting dishes for vegetarian platter.
      3. Dinner service: grill chicken. Make tibs bowl fresh.
      4. Coffee ceremony during dessert service.

      Chef Tips

      • The coffee ceremony is the heart of the meal — plan it as the finale.
      • The vegetarian platter is assembled first and held warm while the meat dishes are prepared fresh.
      • This is a 2-hour dining experience — plan accordingly.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Rushing.
      • Serving the coffee ceremony before the meal.
