🫘 Loubia (Moroccan Stewed White Beans)

Loubia — stewed white beans (لوبيا)

White beans stewed with grated tomato, onion, garlic, cumin, paprika and olive oil in a thick sauce, eaten by dipping bread.

9h 40mTotal time
10mPrep
1h 30mCook
6Servings
420Calories
EasyDifficulty

Everything goes in one pot with about 2 litres of water: the soaked beans, optional meat on the bone, tomatoes, onion, garlic, herbs and spices. A pressure cooker takes about 40 minutes until the beans are tender, then it simmers uncovered until the sauce is thick but still ample. In a conventional pot the page says about 1½ hours. The author says it is comfort food for many Moroccans, and that turmeric is not traditional.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker or heavy pot

  • 1 lb. beef or lamb, on the bone; or 1 cup dried meat (optional)
  • 1 lb. dry white haricot (navy) or Cannellini beans, soaked overnight then drained
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, grated
  • 1 medium onion, grated
  • 5 cloves of garlic, finely chopped or pressed
  • 3 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 2 teaspoons ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric (optional)
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper or fresh chili peppers, to taste (optional)
  • 1/2 cup olive oil

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight and drain.
  2. Put everything in a pot with about 2 litres (2 quarts) of water.
  3. Pressure cooker: cook 40 minutes under pressure until the beans are tender, then simmer uncovered until the sauce is thick and not watery but still quite ample.
  4. Conventional pot: cook about 1½ hours to the desired tenderness, until the sauce is thick and no longer watery.
  5. Serve hot with bread for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: beans tender, sauce thick but still ample.
  • The page says turmeric is not traditional, though it lists it as optional.
  • Maroc Mama adds that loubia is most typically found in Morocco with pieces of sheep stomach.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking the beans without the overnight soak.
  • Leaving the sauce watery.
  • Skipping the soak.

Cultural Note

The author calls loubia a comfort food favourite for many Moroccans, eaten by dipping bread into the beans.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: Khobz
