🥛 Ethiopian Cinnamon Milk

ቋርቋሮ ወተት — Qerqero Wetet

Ethiopian Spiced Warm Milk — whole milk warmed with cinnamon, cardamom, and a generous spoonful of Ethiopian honey. The bedtime drink that Ethiopian parents make for children and the comfort drink for adults on cold highland evenings.

Ethiopian spiced warm milk is the bedtime ritual of Ethiopian households — the highland evenings are cold, and a warm cup of honey-cinnamon milk is the most comforting ending to the day.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small saucepan

  • 400ml whole milk
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 cardamom pods — bruised
  • 2 tbsp Ethiopian honey

Method

  1. Warm milk with cinnamon stick and cardamom pods over medium-low heat.
  2. Bring to just below boiling — steaming but not boiling.
  3. Steep off the heat 3 minutes.
  4. Strain into cups.
  5. Add honey. Stir.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Never boil milk — scalds and produces a skin.
  • Add honey off the heat — preserves enzyme activity.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling milk.
  • Adding honey to very hot milk.

The Science

Cinnamon's cinnamaldehyde dissolves into warm milk fat — distributing the aroma and flavour uniformly. The honey's glucose oxidase (enzyme) is denatured above 40°C — adding it to very hot milk destroys its antimicrobial activity.

Health Benefits

  • Calcium from milk.
  • Cinnamon blood sugar support.
  • Honey antimicrobial.
  • Warm milk promotes sleep (tryptophan).

Cultural Note

Ethiopian spiced warm milk is the language of parental care — the cup brought to a child on a cold evening, the drink prepared for an elderly parent, the self-care ritual of adults who know how to be gentle with themselves. It is warmth in liquid form.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: At bedtime, On cold highland evenings, For children and the elderly
