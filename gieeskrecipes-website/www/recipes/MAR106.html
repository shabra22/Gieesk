🥗 Beet and Orange Salad with Pomegranate

Shlada dyal lbetrav w lbortokal — beet and orange salad

Diced cooked beets tossed with mint, red onion, pomegranate seeds and walnuts in a cumin, orange zest and orange juice dressing.

An olive oil, orange zest, orange juice, cumin and salt dressing is whisked in a bowl, then tossed with cooked diced beets, fresh mint, sliced red onion, pomegranate arils and chopped walnuts, with extra mint on top. The page gives no cooking time or cue for the beets.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Serving bowl

  • 2 cups of diced cooked beets
  • 2 tbsp Mint, fresh packed leaves
  • 1 red onion sliced
  • 1 tbsp orange zest
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/4 cup orange juice, freshly squeezed
  • 1/4 cup arils from pomegranates
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts

Method

  1. In a bowl, mix the olive oil, orange zest, orange juice, cumin and salt.
  2. In a serving bowl, combine the cooked beets, mint, sliced onion, pomegranate arils and chopped walnuts.
  3. Add the dressing and toss until everything is mixed. Top with a little more mint and serve.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no beet cooking time or cue; see Moroccan Beetroot Salad for a boiled-beet method with a fork test.
  • The page mentions cilantro in the method but not in the ingredient list.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-dressing so the salad turns watery.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate; the page says 2 days in one place and 2 to 3 days in another.

Reheating: Serve cold or at room temperature.
