🫕 Meru Millet Porridge

Uji wa Wimbi wa Ameru

Meru Millet Porridge — thick, nourishing finger millet porridge prepared the traditional Meru way — slightly thicker than standard uji and sweetened with local Meru honey. The daily breakfast of the Mount Kenya slopes.

The Meru version of millet porridge is notably thicker than standard Kenyan uji. It is sweetened with the honey from Meru's renowned honey trees and eaten with a spoon.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Meru County — Eastern Kenya, slopes of Mount Kenya

The Meru people inhabit the fertile slopes of Mount Kenya. Their millet porridge has a slightly thicker consistency and the traditional addition of honey from the region's renowned honey-producing communities. The Meru have cultivated finger millet on Mount Kenya's slopes for generations.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Wooden spoon

  • 1 cup finger millet flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 2—3 tbsp Meru honey or any raw honey
  • ½ cup milk — optional for creaminess
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix millet flour with 1 cup cold water to a smooth slurry.
  2. Bring remaining 2 cups of water to a boil.
  3. Pour slurry into boiling water, stirring continuously.
  4. Stir over medium heat for 8—10 minutes until thick — Meru uji is notably thicker than standard.
  5. Add milk if using. Stir.
  6. Remove from heat. Add honey — honey added off the heat preserves its live enzymes.
  7. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Adding honey after removing from heat preserves its natural enzymes.
  • The Meru version is thicker than standard uji.
  • Raw, unprocessed Meru honey is incomparably better here.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding honey to boiling porridge.
  • Too thin — the Meru tradition produces thick, spoonable porridge.

The Science

Raw honey contains enzymes denatured above 40°C. Adding honey to warm but not boiling porridge preserves these beneficial enzymes.

Cultural Note

Meru millet porridge reflects the deep agricultural and apicultural traditions of the Mount Kenya slopes — a region producing the world's finest finger millet and extraordinary honey.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best consumed fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast, With roasted groundnuts, With fresh fruit
