🥚 Ceebu Yapp, Beef and Rice with Eggs

Ceebu yapp

Stewing beef marinated in the roff, browned, simmered with tomato paste, then joined by vegetables and 8–10 hard-boiled eggs before the ceebu jen rice method.

4h 25mTotal time
1h 10mPrep
3h 15mCook
4Servings
820Calories
MediumDifficulty

A variation on Ceebu Jen, Rice with Fish and Vegetables: 2 pounds of stewing beef marinate in the roff for at least an hour, brown and are set aside; onions and roff cook, tomato paste goes in, stock or water follows, and the beef returns without vegetables for a long simmer — the page prints the time as ‘1 to 1/2 hours’, an apparent misprint kept as printed. Vegetables and 8 to 10 shelled hard-boiled eggs follow for 45 minutes, and the rice finishes in the strained broth as in the fish version.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large deep pot, Strainer

  • 2 pounds stewing beef
  • The roff of Ceebu Jen, Rice with Fish and Vegetables: 1/4 cup parsley, 2 or 3 chiles, 2 or 3 garlic cloves
  • 8 to 10 hard-boiled eggs, shelled
  • Onions, tomato paste, stock or water, vegetables and rice as in Ceebu Jen, Rice with Fish and Vegetables — quantities as listed there
  • Oil as in Ceebu Jen, Rice with Fish and Vegetables

Method

  1. Marinate the beef in the roff for at least 1 hour.
  2. Brown the beef and set it aside. Brown the onions and the remaining roff, add the tomato paste, then the stock or water. Return the beef — without the vegetables.
  3. Simmer the beef ‘1 to 1/2 hours’ as printed (probably 1 to 1 1/2 hours; judge by tenderness).
  4. Add the vegetables and the hard-boiled eggs and simmer 45 minutes.
  5. Continue with the rice method of Ceebu Jen, Rice with Fish and Vegetables: lift out the meat, eggs and vegetables, strain and measure the broth, cook the rice in it, and serve.

Chef Tips

  • The printed time ‘1 to 1/2 hours’ is a descending range, almost certainly a misprint; cook the beef until tender.
  • Add the eggs with the vegetables, not earlier, so they don’t go rubbery.
  • Marinating at least an hour is the page’s only prep rule for the beef.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Taking ‘1 to 1/2’ literally and under-cooking the beef.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

‘Yapp’ is the Wolof word for meat, parallel to ‘jen’ for fish; the etymology of ‘yapp’ itself is not given in the sources used here.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Steam gently.

Serve with: Lemon wedges
