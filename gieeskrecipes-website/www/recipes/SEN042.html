🌿 Etodjey, Cassava-Leaf Sauce with Groundnuts

Étodié — etodjey

420 g of cassava leaves cooked down with 260 g of groundnut powder, a poached fish, dried fish and a nokoss — a Casamance sauce for rice.

95 minTotal time
30 minPrep
65 minCook
9Servings
330Calories
MediumDifficulty

The cassava-leaf sauce of the Casamance, in a version by a friend of the site’s author, Milcos. A yaboye fish is poached about 20 minutes in lightly seasoned water and set aside. Chopped or pounded cassava leaves boil in a litre of water; squash goes in; five to seven minutes later a piece of dried fish; a quarter hour later the groundnut powder, the whole chile, the nokoss; and finally the flaked fish, simmered a final ten minutes. The sauce should reach a particular consistency and the water should evaporate slightly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar or blender for the nokoss, Smaller pot to poach the fish

  • 420 g cassava leaves, washed, then chopped or pounded
  • 260 g groundnut powder
  • 1 small chile (or a little chile powder)
  • 1 onion
  • A few green onion stalks
  • A few parsley sprigs
  • 3 garlic cloves, or more to taste
  • 1/4 squash, or more to taste
  • 1 yaboye fish
  • 1 piece of dried fish
  • Salt and pepper
  • About 1 litre water
  • Rice, to serve

Method

  1. Poach the fish about 20 minutes in lightly salted, peppered water. Set it aside.
  2. Boil water in a large pot, add the cassava leaves, and top up with water as needed. Add the squash.
  3. After 5 to 7 minutes, add the dried fish and cook over medium-high heat.
  4. About a quarter of an hour later, add the groundnut powder, then the whole chile.
  5. Make the nokoss from the aromatics in the list (onion, green-onion stalks, parsley, garlic) — the extract does not itemise it — and add it. Cook 5 to 7 minutes.
  6. Flake the poached fish, add it, lower the heat and simmer a further ten minutes or so.
  7. The sauce is ready when it reaches the right consistency and the water has evaporated slightly. Serve with rice.

Chef Tips

  • The source’s three cues are ‘la sauce doit avoir cette consistance’, ‘l’eau doit s’évaporer légèrement’, and ‘c’est prêt’ — look for a thick, spoon-coating sauce.
  • The source’s yield is ‘pour environ 8/10 personnes’.
  • The poaching liquid for the fish can be used to top up the pot.
  • No Jola attribution is made on this page: it says only that the Casamance is a natural and historic region.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the groundnut powder too early and scorching.
  • Letting the water boil away before the fish goes in.

Health Benefits

  • Cassava leaves are rich in protein, vitamin C and iron.
  • Groundnuts add protein and fat.
  • Gluten-free.

Cultural Note

Several au-senegal.com pages call étodié a traditional Diola dish and ‘le plat préféré des Diolas’. The page used here makes no ethnic attribution — it says only that the Casamance is ‘une région naturelle et historique du Sénégal’, so it is labelled by region.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 3 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Gentle heat with a little water.

Serve with: Steamed white rice
