🍟 Kenyan Masala Fries

Masala Fries ya Kenya

Kenyan Masala Fries — thick-cut potato chips deep-fried until golden and crispy, immediately tossed in a fragrant spice blend of curry powder, paprika, and cumin, served with garlic mayo. The most ordered side dish in every Nairobi café and restaurant.

Masala fries at a Nairobi café are an institution — the side that automatically accompanies every burger, sandwich, and chicken dish, and the snack that people order specifically rather than as an accompaniment. The café version is usually thicker-cut, crispier, and served with garlic mayo rather than the kachumbari of the street version.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Large bowl, Paper towels

  • 800g large potatoes — cut into thick chips
  • Oil for deep frying
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper — optional
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped

Garlic mayo

  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Make garlic mayo: combine mayo, garlic, and lemon juice. Refrigerate.
  2. Soak cut chips in cold water for 30 minutes. Drain and dry completely.
  3. Mix all spices and salt in a large bowl.
  4. Heat oil to 170°C.
  5. Fry chips in batches for 5—7 minutes until golden.
  6. Immediately toss in spice mix while hot.
  7. Scatter with coriander.
  8. Serve with garlic mayo on the side.

Chef Tips

  • The spice goes on immediately out of the fryer — the heat is what makes it bloom.
  • Soak and dry chips before frying for maximum crispiness.
  • Garlic mayo is the café signature — non-negotiable at the restaurant level.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold chips before seasoning.
  • Wet chips in the fryer.
  • Skipping the garlic mayo.

The Science

See KEN113 for the cooking science of masala chips. At café standard, a slightly higher oil temperature (175°C) produces a crispier exterior while the slightly thicker cut ensures a fluffy interior.

Cultural Note

Masala fries are the universal language of Nairobi café culture — the side dish that transcends age, class, and taste preference. There is no one in Nairobi who does not love masala fries.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Oven at 200°C for 5 minutes.

Serve with: Kenyan Beef Burger, Club Sandwich, Chicken Wrap, Grilled Chicken
