🍳 Egga (Egyptian Baked Frittata)

Egga — عجة

A puffed, flour-and-baking-powder savoury cake of eggs with caramelized onions, tomato, potatoes and herbs, baked until set in the centre.

1h 45mTotal time
30mPrep
1h 15mCook
4Servings
380Calories
MediumDifficulty

Onions are cooked in butter on medium-low for 10 to 15 minutes until a medium amber colour and sweet smelling, tomato is cooked about 5 minutes until its juices evaporate, and diced potatoes cook 10 to 12 minutes until light golden brown. Eggs are whisked at least 5 minutes until frothy and pale yellow, flour and baking powder folded in, then vegetables and herbs. It bakes at 375°F (190°C) until the top is set and golden and a knife comes out clean.

Ingredients

Equipment: Skillet, Mixing bowl, Whisk, Baking dish

  • 5-7 2.5–3.5 oz (70–100 g) tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 cups diced yellow onions, almost 3 medium onions
  • 1 (Approx. 4 oz) (115 g) tomato, seeded and diced
  • 2 sun dried tomatoes, finely chopped, optional
  • 1 cup diced potatoes
  • ¼ cup (2 oz) (60 ml) sliced pickled olives, optional
  • 6 (12 oz) (340 g) large eggs
  • ¾ cup (3.2 oz) (95 g) flour
  • 1 tablespoon (15 g) baking powder
  • 1 cup (1 oz) (30 g) mixed greens (cilantro-parsely-dill)
  • 1 teaspoon (approx. 2.5 g) ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon (2 g) ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon (2 g) ground turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon (1 g) sweet paprika, 1 g, optional
  • ½ teaspoon (1 g) red pepper flakes or Aleppo pepper, optional
  • ¼ teaspoon (0.5 g) ground nutmeg
  • (Milk is used in the method but no amount is printed)

Method

  1. Cook the onions in 2 tablespoons of butter over medium-low until a medium amber colour and sweet smelling, about 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the tomato in 1 tablespoon of butter until its juices evaporate completely and the pieces darken slightly, about 5 minutes.
  3. Cook the potatoes in 2 to 3 tablespoons of butter until light golden brown and tender, about 10 to 12 minutes.
  4. Whisk the eggs, milk and spices vigorously for at least 5 minutes until frothy and pale yellow. Fold in the flour and baking powder, then the herbs and vegetables.
  5. Bake in a 375°F (190°C) oven until the top is set, lightly golden, and a toothpick or knife in the centre comes out clean. The card lists 35 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with aish baladi and pickles.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: onions amber and sweet-smelling; tomato juices evaporated; eggs frothy and pale; centre set, knife clean. The card says 2 servings, but 6 eggs suggest 4.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes are not a traditional ingredient in Egyptian egga, says the author.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-whisking the eggs, which gives a dense result.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour calls egga 'the equivalent of a Friday leisure brunch' from her Alexandria childhood, and says it was her mother's trick to avoid making individual omelettes.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 2 days.

Reheating: Warm in a moderate oven.

Serve with: Aish baladi
