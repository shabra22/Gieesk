🍯 Ethiopian Honey Butter

ቅቤ ማር — Qibe Mar

Ethiopian Honey Butter — kibbeh softened and mixed with raw Ethiopian mountain honey and a pinch of salt. The simplest and most beloved sweet condiment — spread on Ambasha and Difo Dabo at celebrations, on Kita for breakfast, and offered to honoured guests.

Ethiopian honey butter is the simplest luxury — the spiced clarified butter mixed with the finest mountain honey. Ethiopia is one of Africa's largest honey producers, and the mountain honey of the highlands is of extraordinary quality. Together with kibbeh, it produces a spread of remarkable depth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small bowl

  • 3 tbsp kibbeh — at room temperature (soft)
  • 2 tbsp raw Ethiopian mountain honey
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. If kibbeh is solid, warm very gently until just soft — not melted.
  2. Mix kibbeh with honey and salt.
  3. The mixture should be creamy and spreadable.
  4. Serve at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Room-temperature kibbeh mixes with honey seamlessly — melted kibbeh separates from the honey.
  • Raw honey is essential — the delicate floral notes are what make this special.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Melted kibbeh — separates from honey.
  • Pasteurised honey — loses the floral complexity.

The Science

Raw honey contains enzymes (glucose oxidase, invertase), pollen, and numerous volatile aromatic compounds that are destroyed by pasteurisation. The fat of the kibbeh dissolves the fat-soluble aromatic compounds from the honey, while the honey's water-soluble compounds are suspended in the mixture — creating a complex flavour that neither provides alone.

Health Benefits

  • Raw honey antimicrobial and antioxidant.
  • Kibbeh fat-soluble vitamins.
  • Natural sweetener with complex flavour.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian honey butter is offered to guests at the moment of greeting — the bread and honey butter set before a visitor is the Ethiopian statement of welcome. The quality of the honey and the kibbeh tells the guest exactly what the host thinks of them.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Covered at room temperature 1 week. Refrigerate 1 month.

Reheating: Bring to room temperature.

Serve with: Spread on Ambasha (ETH005), On Difo Dabo (ETH004), On Kita (ETH007) for breakfast
