🍚 Swahili Coconut Rice Pudding

Wali wa Tui wa Nazi

Tanzanian Wali wa Tui wa Nazi — sweet coconut rice pudding cooked slowly in coconut milk with cardamom, rose water, and a little sugar. The Swahili coast's most comforting sweet — eaten for breakfast, as a dessert, or as a treat at any time.

Sweet coconut rice is the gentle, comforting sweet of the Swahili coast — not an elaborate dessert but a simple, deeply satisfying preparation that can be breakfast, dessert, or an afternoon treat. The cardamom and rose water are the Zanzibar signatures.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan

  • 1.5 cups short-grain rice or sushi rice
  • 500ml thick coconut milk
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • Pinch of salt

Optional garnish: toasted coconut, fresh mango slices

    Method

    1. Combine rice, coconut milk, water, sugar, and salt.
    2. Bring to a boil, stirring.
    3. Reduce to lowest heat. Stir regularly.
    4. Cook 25—30 minutes until rice is completely soft and the mixture is thick and creamy.
    5. Add cardamom and rose water. Stir.
    6. Serve warm, garnished with toasted coconut and fresh mango.

    Chef Tips

    • Stir regularly — coconut milk rice sticks to the bottom.
    • Short-grain rice is creamier than basmati for pudding.
    • Rose water is added at the end — it is volatile and loses fragrance if cooked too long.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not stirring — sticks and burns.
    • Adding rose water at the start.

    The Science

    Short-grain rice has a higher amylopectin-to-amylose ratio than long-grain — the amylopectin dissolves readily during cooking, producing the characteristically creamy, thick pudding consistency.

    Cultural Note

    Sweet coconut rice is the taste of the Swahili coast's gentlest moments — the breakfast in the cool morning, the dessert after the feast, the bowl given to children as a treat.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

    Reheating: Gently with splash of coconut milk.

    Serve with: Fresh Mango, Chai ya Tangawizi, As a standalone
