🌰 Mlewza (Almond Macaroons, Ghrieba d'Ellouz)

Mlewza / ghrieba d'ellouz / ghriebat el a'kda (غريبة باللوز)

Chewy almond macaroons from a paste of whole blanched almonds and sugar, shaped into balls, rolled in icing sugar and baked until the tops crack.

48mTotal time

Whole blanched, skinned and well-dried almonds (not almond powder) are ground with caster sugar into a paste or nearly there, then worked with strained apricot jam, egg and yolk, melted butter, mastic, baking powder and lemon zest. Balls of about 0.7 oz (1.2 inch diameter) are baked at 350°F (180°C); on the conventional setting with the grill on for about 5 minutes the tops crack and the cookies bake in 15 to 18 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Baking sheets, Bowl for the icing sugar

  • 1.13 lbs almonds - blanched, skinned and well dried (do not use almond powder)
  • 7 oz caster sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp apricot jam, - measured after straining
  • 1 egg, - medium-size
  • 1 egg yolk, - medium-size
  • 2 tbsp butter, - melted and cooled
  • 3 to 4 grain of mastic gum
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp lemon zest
  • 4 drops almond extract - (optional, if you suspect that the almonds lack flavor)
  • 8 oz icing sugar

Method

  1. Grind the almonds with the caster sugar into a paste, or nearly one.
  2. Work in the remaining ingredients (salt, jam, egg and yolk, butter, mastic, baking powder, zest and almond extract if using) into a dough. The page order of mixing was not relayed in detail.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Shape small smooth balls of about 0.7 oz, 1.2 inch in diameter, roll them in the icing sugar and set them at least 3 inches apart.
  4. Conventional bake: with the grill on for about 5 minutes, until the tops of the cookies crack; they bake in 15 to 18 minutes in all.
  5. Fan oven: bake at least 12 minutes and then a few more minutes until the cookies are ready (the page gives no further cue for this setting).

Chef Tips

  • Cue: the tops of the cookies crack.
  • Use whole blanched almonds ground at home; the page says not to use almond powder.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using almond powder.
  • Placing the balls too close together.

Cultural Note

The author writes that she has yet to attend a wedding or happy event in Morocco where they are not served.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store airtight; the page gives no storage time that we relayed.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Mint tea
