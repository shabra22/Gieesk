🥒 Kousa Mahshi (Stuffed Zucchini in Tomato Sauce)

Kousa mahshi — كوسة محشي

Cored summer squash filled with short-grain rice and minced meat, simmered on a bed of tomato and mint sauce for 1 hour 10 minutes.

2h 5mTotal time
35mPrep
1h 10mCook
8Servings
280Calories
MediumDifficulty

Eight medium kousa are cored and filled about three-quarters full with a mixture of soaked Egyptian rice, minced lamb or beef, coriander, cinnamon and cumin. A sauce of garlic, tomato puree, paste, dried mint, boiling water and a chicken bouillon cube is boiled for 3 minutes, the squash goes in, and everything cooks on medium-low heat for 1 hour 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Zucchini corer, Wide pot with lid, Mixing bowl

  • 8 medium kousa Summer Squash
  • 150 grams minced meat lamb or beef
  • ½ cup short grain rice Egyptian rice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon salt or to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper powder
  • 1 teaspoon heaped coriander powder
  • ⅛ teaspoon cinnamon powder
  • ¼ teaspoon cumin powder you can add ½ teaspoon if you like cumin

Sauce

  • 2 tablespoon olive oil or ghee
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 2 medium tomato puree
  • 2 tablespoon heaped tomato paste
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dry mint
  • 3 cups boiling water
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon

Method

  1. Wash the rice and soak it 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Mix the rice with the minced meat, oil, salt, pepper, coriander, cinnamon and cumin.
  3. Core the squash and fill each about three-quarters full, leaving room for the rice to swell (the card says 'until the ¾ of the kousa'; the body says to leave about a quarter empty).
  4. Sauce: cook the garlic in oil until you can smell it, add the puree, then the paste, pepper and mint, then the boiling water and bouillon, and boil 3 minutes.
  5. Lay the stuffed squash in the sauce and cook on medium-low heat for 1 hour 10 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with some of the sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: the garlic is cooked when you can smell it. The rice soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (20 minutes).
  • A quarter teaspoon of sugar is suggested if the tomato paste tastes bitter; leftovers keep 3 days (page notes).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overfilling, which makes the squash burst as the rice swells.
  • Cooking too fast so the squash breaks down.

Cultural Note

The page gives the dish as an Egyptian home classic without further cultural remarks.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (page note).

Reheating: Reheat gently in the sauce.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
