🥛 Moroccan Almond Milk (Halib bil Louz)

Halib bil louz — almond milk (حليب اللوز)

Raw almonds soaked overnight, blended with water, honey and a pinch of salt, strained and flavoured with orange blossom water.

8h 10mTotal time
10mPrep
4Servings
170Calories
EasyDifficulty

One cup of raw unsalted almonds is soaked in water overnight (8 hours), drained and blended on high for 1 to 2 minutes with 4 cups of water, salt and honey until a smooth, milky texture is reached. The mixture is squeezed through a cloth or strainer to get all the milk out and kept in the fridge up to 4 days.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Fine strainer or cloth

  • 1 cup of raw unsalted almonds + water for soaking
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tablespoons honey (to taste)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon orange blossom water (optional)

Method

  1. Soak the almonds in water overnight (8 hours) and drain.
  2. Blend on high for 1 to 2 minutes with the water, salt and honey, until you get a smooth, milky texture.
  3. Squeeze through a fine cloth or strainer until all the milk is out.
  4. Stir in the orange blossom water if using and chill.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: smooth milky texture.
  • The soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (8 hours).
  • The page says it keeps in the fridge up to 4 days.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not squeezing the pulp well.
  • Skipping the soak.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep in the fridge for up to 4 days, per the page.

Reheating: Serve cold.
