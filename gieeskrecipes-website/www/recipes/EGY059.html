🍯 Konafa with Mastic Mahalabiya Cream

Konafa — كنافة

Shredded konafa dough tossed in melted butter, layered around a hot mastic-scented milk cream and baked until golden, then soaked in cool syrup.

3hTotal time
30mPrep
30mCook
7Servings
560Calories
MediumDifficulty

Half of 500 g of konafa dough mixed with melted butter is pressed into a 24 to 26 cm tray, the hot mahalabiya (milk, cream, sugar and cornflour thickened with mastic and vanilla sugar) is poured over, leaving a 2 cm border, and the rest of the dough is spread on top. At 160°C (320°F) it bakes 25 to 30 minutes until the top is golden brown, and the cooled syrup is poured over the hot konafa.

Ingredients

Equipment: 24 to 26 cm round tray, Saucepans

  • 500 grams konafa dough (sometimes sold as kataifi pastry)
  • 250 grams butter (melted)
  • Mastic Mahalabiya (see recipe below)

Syrup

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • ½ lemon

Mahalabiya

  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ cup thickened or whipping cream
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla sugar and two crushed mastics

Method

  1. Syrup: boil the sugar, water and lemon on medium heat until the syrup slightly thickens. Cool, then refrigerate 1 to 2 hours.
  2. Mahalabiya: bring the milk and sugar to a boil. Dissolve the cornflour in ¼ cup of milk or water and stir it in. Add the cream, vanilla and mastic and stir until the mixture thickens.
  3. Heat the oven to 160°C (320°F). Mix the konafa dough with the melted butter by hand.
  4. Press half of the dough into the tray, pour the hot mahalabiya over leaving a 2 cm border, and cover with the remaining dough.
  5. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, or until the top is golden brown.
  6. Pour the cooled syrup over the hot konafa and serve hot in triangles; flip onto a tray if you like.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: syrup slightly thickened; mahalabiya thickened; top golden brown. The syrup chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. Extra mahalabiya chilled about two hours is eaten as a pudding.
  • Mastic and vanilla sugar are one combined ingredient line on the page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Pouring hot syrup on hot konafa, which makes it soggy; the page uses cooled syrup on hot konafa.

Cultural Note

The article notes konafa traditionally holds nuts, clotted cream or cheese; this version uses a mahalabiya layer.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; cover and refrigerate leftovers up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm in a moderate oven to re-crisp.

Serve with: Arabic coffee (page suggestion)
