🍬 Caramel de sésame (Sesame Brittle)

Sésames caramélisés

Sugar dissolved on low heat in half a glass of water, sesame added and cooked on medium heat, stirred constantly once thick and sticky until dry, then poured out and cooled.

p. 53 of the October 2023 recipe book: 1 kg unhulled sesame, 1 kg sugar and ½ glass of water. The sugar is dissolved in the water on low heat; the sesame is added and cooked on medium heat. ‘When the mixture becomes thick and viscous, stir continuously until the whole is dry’; it is poured out and cooled. SUSPECTED INCONSISTENCY (not corrected): 1 kg sugar with only ½ glass of water, while the page says quantities are ‘proportional’ and mixes kg with ‘glass’ in a sister recipe. The page notes that to make the grains stick together, the sugar quantity should be increased. No time or temperature is printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot, Spoon, Tray

  • 1 kg unhulled sesame seeds
  • 1 kg sugar
  • 1/2 glass water (glass size not printed)

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the water on low heat.
  2. Add the sesame and cook on medium heat.
  3. When the mixture becomes thick and viscous, stir continuously until it is dry.
  4. Pour out and cool.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cues are ‘thick and viscous’ and then ‘dry’.
  • The book’s tip: increase the sugar if you want the grains to stick together.
  • No time is printed; judge by the cues.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Walking away once it thickens.
  • Cooking on high heat so the sugar burns.
  • Pouring onto an ungreased surface.

Health Benefits

  • Sesame is rich in minerals; this is mostly sugar by weight.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The page says sesame is very rich in vitamins and minerals.

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Served as a sweet snack
