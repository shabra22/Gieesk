🧊 Tanzanian Mango Sorbet

Barafu ya Embe

Tanzanian Mango Sorbet — Zanzibar Dodo mango purée frozen with lime juice and a light cardamom syrup. Pure mango, pure Zanzibar — the most refreshing dessert the island produces. No dairy, no fat, just the extraordinary flavour of peak-season mango.

Mango sorbet from Zanzibar's own Dodo mango is one of the finest frozen desserts in the world — the mango's extraordinary fragrance and natural sweetness need minimal intervention. Just lime for brightness, cardamom for the Zanzibar signature, and a simple syrup to produce the right texture.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Loaf tin or freezer-safe container

  • 4 large ripe Zanzibar mangoes — peeled and diced (about 600g flesh)

Cardamom Syrup

  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Make syrup: dissolve sugar in water with cardamom. Simmer 2 minutes. Cool completely.
  2. Blend mango flesh until completely smooth.
  3. Combine mango purée, cooled syrup, lime juice, and salt.
  4. Taste — adjust lime and sweetness.
  5. Pour into a shallow freezer container.
  6. Freeze 4 hours, whisking vigorously every hour (or churn in an ice cream machine for 20 minutes).
  7. Serve in chilled glasses or scooped into bowls.

Chef Tips

  • The ripest, most fragrant mango produces the finest sorbet.
  • Whisking every hour during freezing breaks up ice crystals — producing a smoother result without a machine.
  • Serve at -12°C — slightly softer than ice cream.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-ripe mango.
  • Not whisking during freezing — large ice crystals.

The Science

Sorbet texture depends on small ice crystal size. The sugar syrup lowers the freezing point — allowing the sorbet to be scoopable at -12 to -15°C rather than rock-hard. Whisking during freezing mechanically breaks up forming ice crystals, maintaining a smooth texture. The natural acids in mango and lime also slightly inhibit large crystal formation.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar mango sorbet is the island's most honest frozen dessert — the Dodo mango is extraordinary enough to need nothing but a clean technique. To eat it in Zanzibar during mango season is to taste the island at its peak.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Freeze up to 3 weeks. Soften 5 minutes before scooping.

Reheating: Not applicable.
