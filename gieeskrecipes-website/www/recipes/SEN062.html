🥭 Mango Fonio Salad

Salade de fonio à la mangue

Cooked fonio with mango, cucumber, grape tomatoes, red onion, parsley and mint, in a vinaigrette of two lemons and a full cup of olive oil.

20mTotal time
20mPrep
4Servings
540Calories
EasyDifficulty

The WBUR printing of a salad from Thiam’s book ‘Senegal’. A vinaigrette of the juice of two lemons, salt and pepper is whisked while a cup of olive oil is added slowly to emulsify. Two cups of cooked fonio (or quinoa) are combined with chopped parsley and mint, diced mango, red onion, halved grape tomatoes and diced cucumber; vinaigrette is added to taste and the salad is topped with spiced cashews if you have them. The page notes some vinaigrette may be left over.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl, Whisk

  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 cup olive oil
  • 2 cups cooked fonio (or quinoa)
  • 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch mint, finely chopped
  • 1 ripe mango, peeled, pitted, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cup red and yellow grape tomatoes, halved
  • 1 small cucumber, seeded and diced
  • 1/2 cup spiced cashews (optional; the sub-recipe is not printed)

Method

  1. Whisk the lemon juice, salt and pepper. Slowly whisk in the oil to emulsify.
  2. In a large bowl combine the fonio, parsley, mint, mango, red onion, tomatoes and cucumber.
  3. Toss, adding vinaigrette to taste. Top with cashews if using and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The oil-to-lemon ratio (1 cup to the juice of 2 lemons) is the page’s; some vinaigrette will be left over.
  • Add the dressing to taste rather than all at once.
  • Cook the fonio ahead and let it cool.
  • Quinoa substitutes if you cannot find fonio.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dressing the salad too early so the mango softens.
  • Pouring the oil in too fast.

Health Benefits

  • Mango and tomato give vitamin C and carotenoids.
  • Olive oil supplies monounsaturated fat.

Cultural Note

Compare the Yolélé company’s ‘Fonio Mango Salad’: 1/3 cup olive oil against this cup, one lemon against two, grated ginger, 4 cups of fonio against 2, six servings against four. It is a related but distinct formula and carries no book credit; it is not entered here.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 1 day refrigerated.

Reheating: Serve cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Grilled chicken or fish
