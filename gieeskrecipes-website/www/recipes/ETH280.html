The All Ethiopia Platter is the ultimate expression of Ethiopian food diversity — 80+ ethnic groups, five major religion traditions, twelve ecoregions, and thousands of years of culinary history assembled on one grand celebration table.

Method

  1. 3 days before: Tej, Tella, Key Wat.
  2. 2 days before: Oromo Marqa, Harari Fah-Fah.
  3. 1 day before: Tigrayan Zigni, all fasting dishes.
  4. Day of: Tihlo, Kitfo, Amhara Tibs, Anuak Fish — all fresh.
  5. Assemble the grand regional table.
  6. Begin with the coffee ceremony.

Chef Tips

  • This is the feast of Ethiopia — 3 days of preparation minimum.
  • The diversity is the message — serve all dishes simultaneously so guests can explore.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not starting 3 days ahead.

Cultural Note

The All Ethiopia Platter is the statement that Ethiopia is not one food culture but many — that the berbere-and-injera tradition of the highlands is only one chapter in a food story that includes enset from the south, camel from the east, river fish from the west, and barley beer from ancient Tigray. Ethiopia is one of the most food-culturally diverse nations on earth.
