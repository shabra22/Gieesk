🥔 Ethiopian Potato Alicha

ድንች አልጫ — Dinich Alicha

Ethiopian Potato Alicha — potatoes and onion cooked with turmeric, ginger, and garlic in a mild golden sauce without berbere. The filling, mild potato stew of Ethiopian fasting meals — turmeric-golden and fragrant with ginger.

Potato Alicha is the fasting-period filling staple — the potatoes absorb the turmeric and ginger and become deeply flavourful, providing sustained energy and fullness during long fasting days.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g potatoes — peeled and cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, and pepper. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add potatoes. Stir to coat.
  4. Add water. Cover. Cook 20—25 minutes.
  5. Potatoes should be very tender.
  6. Some should have partially dissolved — natural thickening.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • The potatoes that dissolve thicken the sauce naturally.
  • No berbere — the turmeric colour and flavour is the point.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking potatoes.

Cultural Note

Potato Alicha is fasting sustenance — the filling, energising stew that keeps the Orthodox faithful strong through the 200+ days of abstinence from meat and dairy.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently with added water.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter, As a filling side dish
