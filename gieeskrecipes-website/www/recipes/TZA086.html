🍲 Tanzanian Fish Tamarind Stew

Mchuzi wa Samaki wa Ukwaju

Tanzanian Mchuzi wa Samaki wa Ukwaju — fish stew with tamarind, coconut milk, and Zanzibar spices. The sweet-sour-spiced fish stew of the coastal kitchen — tamarind and coconut milk creating a uniquely complex sauce.

This fish stew combines tamarind's tartness with coconut milk richness and Zanzibar spices for extraordinary complexity. It uses the coastal Tanzanian pantry most completely.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan with lid

  • 700g firm fish fillets
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Season fish with salt and turmeric.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
  4. Add coconut milk and tamarind. Stir to combine.
  5. Bring to gentle simmer. Add fish. Cover and simmer gently 10—12 minutes.
  6. Adjust sweet-sour balance with sugar. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Balance tamarind tartness with sugar to taste.
  • Never boil coconut milk hard.
  • Firm fish only.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-souring without adjusting sugar.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

The Science

Tamarind's tartaric acid and coconut milk fats create an emulsified sauce — acid prevents fat separation while fat rounds the acidity's sharpness.

Cultural Note

The combination of tamarind and coconut milk in a fish stew is the signature of the Swahili coast — Arab-introduced tamarind and the African coconut palm creating something that belongs entirely to Tanzania.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Very gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
