🥣 Chorba Fassia (Fes-Style Diced Vegetable Soup)

Chorba fassia (شوربة فاسية)

A finely diced vegetable and vermicelli soup in a light turmeric broth, the way Fassi families like it: small pieces, not chunky.

1h 20mTotal time
20mPrep
1hCook
6Servings
250Calories
EasyDifficulty

Vegetables are cut small and braised with a little meat and smen to render their liquid and intensify flavour; stock goes in and simmers 30 minutes. Just before serving more water is added and the vermicelli cooks al dente, with herbs and pepper stirred in at the end. The pasta keeps absorbing liquid, so thin as you go. The author says relatives from Fez won't touch their bowls if the vegetables are too chunky.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep pot with lid

  • 1 cup cubed lamb, beef or chicken (cut less than 1″ thick)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium-size onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium-size carrots, peeled and finally diced (printed "finally")
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste (or 2 grated pulpy tomatoes, seeds and skin discarded)
  • 1 celery stalk, finely diced (optional)
  • 1 small turnip, finely diced
  • 2 small courgettes (or 1 Italian courgette), finely diced
  • 2 medium-size potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 cup sliced leeks (optional)
  • 3 tbsp finely chopped parsley and coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smen (or ghee or blue cheese)
  • 1 cup broken vermicelli (or orzo or small macaroni)
  • salt to taste
  • 2 qt water (or ready made stock)

Method

  1. Place a deep pot over medium-low heat. Add the oil, smen, meat and/or bones, onions, vegetables, tomatoes or paste, salt and turmeric, and stir to combine.
  2. Cover and braise, stirring every few minutes to let the vegetables render their liquid and intensify in flavour. This makes the base.
  3. Once the liquid has reduced, add the stock or water and simmer for 30 minutes. At this point the cooking can be interrupted until nearly serving time.
  4. Shortly before serving, top up with more water and bring to a simmer. Add the broken vermicelli and continue cooking a few minutes.
  5. Add the herbs and black pepper and simmer until the pasta is just about cooked (al dente).
  6. Adjust the water and seasoning; thin as you go to your liking.

Chef Tips

  • The pasta absorbs water quickly, so for leftovers add vermicelli only to the portion you will eat that day.
  • The page prints no braising time; the cue is that the liquid has reduced.
  • Cut vegetables very small; Fassi taste favours fine dice.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking the vermicelli long before serving.
  • Cutting vegetables chunky.
  • Skipping the braise, which builds the flavour.

Health Benefits

  • Lots of vegetables in a light broth.
  • Small amount of meat keeps it low in fat.

Cultural Note

The author notes that 'chorba' means soup in Arabic, 'fassia' means from Fez, and that the smen is traditionally kept in a clay jar (khabia). Some of her Fassi relatives won't touch their bowls if the vegetables are too chunky.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep soup and pasta apart if making ahead; 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat the base and add fresh pasta.

Serve with: Khobz
