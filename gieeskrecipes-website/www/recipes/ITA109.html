'Nduja e Peperoni

Calabria's fiery, spreadable spiced pork sausage melted into a pan of sweet peppers and tomatoes, creating a bold, richly spicy dish typical of the region's love for heat.

'Nduja is a soft, spreadable salami made from pork and an abundance of Calabrian chili, so intensely flavored that a small amount transforms an entire pan of vegetables into something deeply savory and properly fiery.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large sauté pan

  • 150 g (5.3 oz) nduja
  • 4 bell peppers, mixed colors, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 60 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh basil or parsley, for garnish
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion and peppers over medium heat until softened and starting to caramelize, about 12–15 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  3. Crumble in the nduja and stir, letting it melt into the peppers and release its spiced fat.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10–15 minutes until the sauce thickens and everything is well combined.
  5. Season with salt, keeping in mind the nduja itself is quite salty.
  6. Finish with fresh basil or parsley and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Let the peppers cook long enough to properly soften and sweeten before adding the nduja, building a good flavor base.
  • 'Nduja varies significantly in heat between brands — taste as you go and adjust the quantity to your spice tolerance.
  • This dish makes an excellent topping for bruschetta or a filling for a simple sandwich, beyond serving as a main.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding too much 'nduja without tasting first, resulting in an overwhelmingly spicy dish.
  • Rushing the peppers, which need real time to soften and develop sweetness.
  • Oversalting before tasting, given how much salt the 'nduja itself already contributes.

Health Benefits

  • Peppers provide vitamin C; a good source of protein from the pork, best enjoyed in moderation given its richness and spice.

Cultural Note

'Nduja is a defining product of Calabria, a region known throughout Italy for its love of chili heat, and this dish showcases the spread in one of its simplest, most direct applications.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop.

Serve with: Crusty bread, Pasta, tossed through as a quick sauce, Polenta
