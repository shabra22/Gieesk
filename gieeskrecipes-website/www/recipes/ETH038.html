🥘 Ethiopian Beef with Vegetables

ሥጋ ከአትክልት — Siga ke-Atkilit

Ethiopian Beef and Vegetables — beef stewed with potatoes, carrots, tomatoes, and onions in a mild Ethiopian spice sauce. The everyday balanced meal — the vegetables moderate the heat and provide variety to the typical all-meat wat.

Beef with vegetables is the everyday family meal of urban Ethiopia — nutritionally complete, more affordable than a pure meat wat, and slightly milder in spice than the traditional wats. It reflects the modern Ethiopian kitchen adapting to urban life.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 500g beef — stewing cuts, cubed
  • 2 medium potatoes — cubed
  • 2 carrots — sliced
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Brown beef in oil. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, berbere, tomatoes.
  3. Return beef. Add water. Simmer covered 30 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer 20 more minutes.
  5. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add potatoes and carrots after the beef is nearly tender.
  • Lighter spicing than Key Wat — this is an everyday dish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding vegetables too early — they overcook.

The Science

Potato starch dissolves into the sauce during cooking — naturally thickening without any added starch.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.
