🦐 Tanzanian Prawn Pilau

Pilau ya Kamba

Tanzanian Prawn Pilau — basmati rice cooked in a rich prawn and coconut stock with Zanzibar spices, crowned with butter-sautéed prawns. The luxury seafood pilau of Zanzibar — the dish that celebrates the Indian Ocean's greatest gift.

Prawn pilau is Zanzibar's most luxurious rice dish — the prawn shells make an intensely flavoured stock that cooks the rice, and the best prawns are saved for the final sauté and crowning of the dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot for stock, Large sufuria, Spice grinder

  • 600g large prawns — peeled, shells reserved
  • 3 cups basmati rice — soaked
  • 2 onions — sliced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Prawn Shell Stock: reserved shells, 4 cups water, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp salt

    Zanzibar Spices: 2 tsp cumin seeds, 6 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick, 6 cloves

      Method

      1. Make prawn stock: simmer shells with water, onion, garlic, salt 20 minutes. Strain. Measure 5 cups.
      2. Toast spices. Grind half. Keep half whole.
      3. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
      4. Add coconut milk and prawn stock. Bring to boil.
      5. Add drained rice. Stir. Reduce heat. Cover tightly. Cook 18 minutes.
      6. Rest 8 minutes.
      7. Sauté prawns in butter with garlic and salt — 3—4 minutes.
      8. Crown pilau with sautéed prawns. Garnish with coriander.

      Chef Tips

      • Prawn shell stock is the foundation — do not skip.
      • Prawn shells contain intense glutamate flavour.
      • Add prawns at the end — they cook in minutes.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Discarding prawn shells.
      • Adding raw prawns to the rice.

      The Science

      Prawn shells contain chitin-bound glutamate — amino acids that produce intense umami when extracted in hot water, giving the rice incomparable depth.

      Cultural Note

      Prawn pilau is Zanzibar's most luxurious expression of the pilau tradition — taking the Indian Ocean's finest protein and the island's finest spices in the great rice dish of East Africa.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: Best eaten immediately.

      Reheating: Covered with splash of water.

      Serve with: Kachumbari, Coconut Chutney, Raita
