🍞 Zanzibar Cardamom Bread

Mkate wa Iliki

Zanzibar Cardamom Bread — a soft, sweet enriched loaf perfumed heavily with Zanzibar cardamom, topped with sesame seeds and a honey glaze. The island's most fragrant morning bread — the smell of cardamom from the oven is one of Zanzibar's great morning experiences.

Zanzibar cardamom bread is the most aromatic bread on the island — the generous amount of locally grown cardamom produces a loaf whose fragrance fills the whole building. Made for Eid and for the most honoured occasions, it is the bread that signals something special is happening.

Ingredients

Equipment: Loaf tin or round tin, Oven at 175°C

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp yeast
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1.5 tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 200ml warm coconut milk
  • ½ cup warm water
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp butter — softened

Topping: 1 egg beaten + sesame seeds + 2 tbsp honey mixed with 1 tbsp water

    Method

    1. See TZA128 (Mkate wa Sinia) for the full technique — cardamom bread uses the same enriched dough method.
    2. Use 1.5 tsp cardamom (double) and add nutmeg.
    3. Shape into loaf tin.
    4. Rest 30—40 minutes.
    5. Brush with egg wash. Scatter sesame seeds generously.
    6. Bake 30—35 minutes until golden.
    7. Brush with honey glaze while still hot from the oven.

    Chef Tips

    • Double the cardamom of a normal bread — this is Zanzibar.
    • The honey glaze while hot creates a shiny, fragrant crust.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Insufficient cardamom.
    • Cold honey glaze — does not adhere.

    Cultural Note

    Zanzibar cardamom bread is the island's most fragrant food — its smell from the oven is the smell of celebration, of Eid morning, of the most important days in the Zanzibari year.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Room temperature 3 days. Freeze 2 months.

    Reheating: Warm briefly in oven.

    Serve with: Chai ya Viungo (TZA144), Butter and honey, Mbaazi wa Nazi (TZA131)
