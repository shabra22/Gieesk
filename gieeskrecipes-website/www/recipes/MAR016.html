🍑 Beef Tagine with Apricots and Almonds

Tajine de boeuf aux abricots (طاجين اللحم بالمشمش)

Beef braised with onion, saffron and ginger in a tagine, finished with dried apricots cooked in honey and cinnamon until syrupy, and fried almonds.

2h 50mTotal time
20mPrep
2h 30mCook
4Servings
590Calories
MediumDifficulty

This is the apricot twin of the prune tagine. The beef is cooked slowly with onion, garlic, ginger, saffron and cinnamon, with a splash of water in the bottom of the tagine. The apricots are cooked apart with honey and cinnamon until they sit in a thick syrup.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine or heavy pot with lid, Small pan for the apricots

  • 1-2lbs. beef bone in or bone out– cut into 3-4" chunks
  • 1/2 lb. onions finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 2 teaspoon ginger
  • 5-10 saffron threads
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup vegetable or olive oil (less if the meat is fattier)
  • 1 palmful of chopped cilantro

Apricots and almonds

  • 1/2 lb. dried apricots
  • 1-2 tablespoon honey (fresh, organic if possible)
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup blanched, fried almonds

Method

  1. Put the meat, onions, garlic, salt, pepper, ginger, saffron, cinnamon, oil and cilantro in the tagine; add 1/4-1/2 cup of water to the bottom of the tagine.
  2. Cover and cook over medium heat. Check after about 45 minutes.
  3. The meat will take approximately 2 hours to cook, depending on the cuts used; the source says simmer 2 1/2 to 3 hours until very tender and falling from the bone.
  4. Cook the apricots with the honey and the 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon until they are sitting in a thick syrup.
  5. Fry the almonds, spoon the apricots over the meat and scatter the almonds.

Chef Tips

  • Total time depends on the cut; test for tenderness instead of watching the clock.
  • The method mentions parsley but the ingredient list prints only cilantro; the page is inconsistent.
  • Keep the water to a minimum; the onions release more.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much water.
  • Cooking the apricots to a jam in the meat pot.
  • Rushing the beef.

Cultural Note

A sweet-and-savoury tagine of the type Moroccans reserve for guests and celebrations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Reheat covered over low heat.

Serve with: Khobz, Mint tea
