🥜 Nyamwezi Groundnut Soup

Supu ya Karanga — Wanyamwezi

Nyamwezi Groundnut Soup — freshly roasted groundnuts ground and simmered with onion, tomato, and leafy greens into a thick, nourishing soup. The protein-rich staple of Tanzania's central plateau — sustaining traders and farmers alike for centuries.

Groundnut soup is the Nyamwezi's most important source of plant protein — the freshly roasted and ground groundnuts produce a soup of extraordinary richness and body that sustained the great caravan traders on their journeys.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar or blender

  • 1 cup raw groundnuts — roasted and ground
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water

Method

  1. Roast groundnuts. Cool. Grind to a coarse paste.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  3. Mix ground groundnuts with 1 cup warm water.
  4. Add to the tomato base. Add remaining water.
  5. Simmer 15—18 minutes until thick.
  6. Add greens. Cook 3 more minutes until wilted.
  7. Season with salt.

Chef Tips

  • Fresh-roasted groundnuts are essential — commercial peanut butter lacks the roasted complexity.
  • The soup should be thick — not pourable.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Commercial peanut butter substitution.
  • Too thin — reduce further.

Cultural Note

Nyamwezi groundnut soup is the food of the long road — nourishing enough to sustain traders walking hundreds of kilometres, made from a single drought-resistant crop that the central plateau provides in abundance.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.
