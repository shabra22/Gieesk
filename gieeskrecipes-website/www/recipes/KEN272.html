🍗 Kenyan BBQ Chicken Wings

Mbawa za Kuku za BBQ

Kenyan BBQ Chicken Wings — marinated chicken wings baked until crispy and lacquered with a Kenyan honey-chilli-tamarind glaze. The most ordered starter in Nairobi's restaurants — sweet, sticky, and deeply addictive.

BBQ chicken wings are the restaurant starter that tables fight over — crispy-skinned, deeply glazed, and eaten with hands regardless of the restaurant's formality. The Kenyan version uses a honey-tamarind glaze that is simultaneously sweet, tart, and warming.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking tray with rack, Oven, Basting brush

  • 1 kg chicken wings — tips removed, split at joint

Marinade

  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil

Kenyan Honey-Tamarind Glaze

  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Marinate wings for 2 hours minimum.
  2. Preheat oven to 200°C. Place wings on a rack over a baking tray.
  3. Bake for 20 minutes. Flip. Bake 10 more minutes.
  4. Make glaze: simmer all glaze ingredients for 3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Brush wings generously with glaze. Bake 5 minutes.
  6. Flip, glaze again. Bake final 5 minutes.
  7. The wings should be deeply lacquered and sticky.
  8. Serve with extra glaze for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • The rack is essential — it allows air circulation for crispier skin.
  • Multiple glaze applications build the lacquer.
  • Serve immediately — wings lose crispiness quickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • No rack — wings sit in fat and steam instead of crisping.
  • Single glaze application.

The Science

The rack allows hot air to circulate underneath the wings, creating convective cooking on all surfaces simultaneously. Without a rack, the bottom surface sits in the rendered fat and produces a flabby, steamed result. Multiple glaze applications build successive caramelised layers.

Cultural Note

BBQ chicken wings have become one of Kenya's most beloved restaurant starters — the dish that every table orders regardless of what else is coming. The Kenyan honey-tamarind glaze transforms a universal dish into something unmistakably local.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Oven at 200°C for 8 minutes.

Serve with: As a starter, With coleslaw and garlic dip, Caesar Salad (KEN267)
