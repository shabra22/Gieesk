🍢 Grilled Kefta Skewers (Salima's Kitchen)

Kefta mechwia — grilled kefta

Ground beef or lamb mixed with minced red onion, cilantro, parsley, cumin and paprika, chilled, wrapped on skewers and grilled until browned all round.

45mTotal time
20mPrep
10mCook
4Servings
330Calories
EasyDifficulty

The meat and seasoning are mixed by hand for at least five minutes, chilled from 15 minutes to 2 hours, and wrapped around skewers. The skewers are grilled over medium-high heat, turned every few minutes, until they develop grill marks and brown significantly all around.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill, Skewers, Mixing bowl

  • 1 lb ground beef or lamb, 10% or more fat
  • 1 small red onion, minced finely
  • 2 tbsp cilantro, minced
  • 2 tbsp parsley, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl and mix well with your hands for at least 5 minutes.
  2. Cover and chill in the fridge for 15 minutes to 2 hours.
  3. Take a ball of the mixture and wrap it around a skewer, one at a time, until all the meat is on skewers. Heat the grill to medium-high.
  4. Grill the skewers, turning every few minutes to prevent burning, until they develop grill marks and brown significantly all around.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: grill marks and brown significantly all around.
  • The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (15 minutes to 2 hours).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mixing too briefly, so the kefta does not hold on the skewer.
  • Leaving the skewers on one side too long.
