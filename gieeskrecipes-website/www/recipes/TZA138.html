🫓 Tanzanian Zanzibar Pizza

Pizza ya Zanzibar

Tanzanian Zanzibar Pizza — thin wheat dough rolled out, filled with spiced minced beef, egg, onion, and coriander, folded into a parcel and pan-fried until golden and crispy. Nothing to do with Italian pizza — Zanzibar's own remarkable street food invention.

Zanzibar Pizza is one of the most creative street food inventions in East Africa — thin dough filled with spiced meat, egg, and vegetables, folded into a parcel and fried in a skillet with a little oil. It has nothing to do with Italian pizza beyond the name; it is entirely Zanzibari. The vendors at Forodhani assemble them to order, pressing the filled dough flat in the pan.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large flat pan or skillet

    Dough

    • 2 cups flour
    • ½ tsp salt
    • ¾ cup warm water
    • 1 tbsp oil

    Filling (per pizza)

    • 80g minced beef — seasoned
    • 1 egg
    • ¼ onion — finely diced
    • 1 tbsp fresh coriander
    • 1 green chilli — minced
    • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
    • 1 tsp curry powder
    • Salt and pepper
    • 2 tbsp oil for frying

    Method

    1. Make dough: combine flour, salt, water, oil. Knead 5 minutes. Rest 20 minutes.
    2. Divide into balls. Roll each very thin — almost translucent.
    3. Season minced beef with curry powder, salt, and pepper.
    4. Spread minced beef across half the dough. Add onion and coriander.
    5. Break an egg over the filling. Add a drizzle of mayonnaise and chilli.
    6. Fold dough over to enclose the filling — press edges to seal.
    7. Heat oil in a flat pan over medium heat.
    8. Cook 3—4 minutes per side, pressing flat, until golden and crispy.
    9. Cut into quarters. Serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • The dough must be rolled very thin — thick dough = doughy result.
    • Press firmly in the pan — this is not a gentle operation.
    • The egg inside is what makes it special — it cooks inside the parcel.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Thick dough.
    • Not pressing firmly.
    • Overcrowded filling — leaks.

    The Science

    The egg inside the sealed dough parcel poaches in its own steam as the dough fries — reaching a soft-set consistency while the dough outside simultaneously crisps through Maillard browning. The sealing prevents steam from escaping, creating a self-basting internal environment.

    Cultural Note

    Zanzibar Pizza is Zanzibar's most inventive modern street food — a completely original creation that has nothing to do with Italy but everything to do with Zanzibar's creativity in combining its available ingredients into something entirely new. It is now one of the most requested foods at Forodhani Gardens.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.
