🌿 Tanzanian Lemongrass Tea

Chai ya Mchaichai

Tanzanian Lemongrass Tea — fresh lemongrass stalks bruised and simmered in water until the tea is fragrant, pale golden, and distinctly citrusy. Sweetened with honey. The herbal tea of the Tanzanian coast — calming, aromatic, and completely caffeine-free.

Lemongrass tea is the herbal alternative to chai on the Tanzanian coast — the same fragrance that goes into the fish soups and coconut curries, here brewed on its own into a delicate, pale golden tea of genuine therapeutic value. Grown throughout coastal Tanzania, lemongrass tea is the afternoon drink of choice for those who want fragrance without caffeine.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Sieve

  • 4 stalks fresh lemongrass — bottom 15cm trimmed, bruised
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tbsp honey

Optional: 2cm fresh ginger for extra warmth

    Method

    1. Bruise lemongrass stalks by bending and crushing them with your hands or a rolling pin.
    2. Combine water, lemongrass (and ginger if using) in a saucepan.
    3. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat. Simmer 6—7 minutes.
    4. The water should be pale golden and fragrant.
    5. Strain into cups.
    6. Add honey. Stir.
    7. Serve hot or pour over ice for iced lemongrass tea.

    Chef Tips

    • Bruising is the key step — it ruptures the cells and releases the volatile oils.
    • Do not boil vigorously — simmering preserves the delicate citral aroma.
    • Excellent served iced with lime — the Zanzibar beach version.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not bruising the lemongrass — weak flavour.
    • Hard boiling — loses delicate citral aromatics.

    The Science

    Lemongrass contains citral (geranial + neral) — the primary aromatic compound that gives it its distinctive lemon-lime fragrance. Citral is a volatile compound that evaporates rapidly at high temperatures. Gentle simmering rather than boiling maximises extraction while minimising evaporative loss of the aromatics.

    Health Benefits

    • Citral is antifungal and antimicrobial.
    • Lemongrass reduces anxiety — used in traditional medicine.
    • Anti-inflammatory.
    • Caffeine-free.
    • Aids digestion.

    Cultural Note

    Lemongrass tea is the afternoon tea of the Tanzanian coast — lighter than ginger chai, more fragrant than plain tea, and completely calming. It is the drink that Zanzibar's grandmothers have been making for generations, and the one that the island's wellness community has recently rediscovered.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Brew fresh. Refrigerate as iced tea 2 days.

    Reheating: Warm gently.

    Serve with: As a standalone, With maandazi, As an after-dinner digestive tea
