🌿 Rof Gremolata

Rof — gremolata de persil

Finely chopped parsley, scallions, garlic, a seeded half Scotch bonnet and lemon zest — a raw finishing condiment made in a bowl, kept a week in the fridge.

10mTotal time
10mPrep
8Servings
8Calories
EasyDifficulty

Thiam’s rof gremolata is the finishing condiment for his lamb shank mafé, distinct from rof, the herb-and-chile stuffing used inside the fish for ceebu jën. A bunch of parsley, three scallions, two garlic cloves and half a seeded Scotch bonnet are finely chopped and combined in a bowl with the grated zest of one lemon, seasoned with salt and pepper. It makes 1 cup and keeps up to a week, airtight in the fridge. No cooking, no time or heat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small bowl, Sharp knife

  • 1 bunch parsley, leaves finely chopped — the bunch is not weighed or measured in the source
  • 3 scallions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and finely chopped
  • Grated zest of 1 lemon
  • Fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Gently combine the parsley, scallions, garlic, Scotch bonnet and lemon zest in a small bowl.
  2. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Store airtight in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

Chef Tips

  • The page says to combine ‘gently’.
  • Wear gloves for the Scotch bonnet.
  • ‘1 bunch parsley’ is the source’s own measure; use a bunch of normal supermarket size.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Blending to a paste: gremolata is chopped.
  • Zesting the pith of the lemon.
  • Making it long ahead — the herbs lose colour.

Health Benefits

  • Parsley and lemon zest are rich in vitamin C and K.

Cultural Note

Not the same preparation as rof, the Wolof herb stuffing; this one is a finishing condiment from Thiam’s later work. The Yolélé ‘Senegalese Rof Dip’ page (not published here) cools Thiam’s ‘fiery, herbaceous sauce’ with Greek yoghurt and has a list/method mismatch (habanero in the method only).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Up to 1 week, airtight, refrigerated.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Lamb shank mafé, Grilled meat and fish
