🥦 Tanzanian Mixed Vegetables

Mboga Mchanganyiko

Tanzanian Mboga Mchanganyiko — a colourful medley of seasonal Tanzanian vegetables blanched and sautéed with garlic and herbs. The restaurant and hotel vegetable side that showcases Tanzania's excellent highland vegetable production.

Tanzania's central highlands — particularly around Arusha, Kilimanjaro, and the Southern Highlands — produce excellent vegetables. The mixed vegetable preparation at Tanzanian restaurants takes these quality ingredients and treats them correctly: blanched briefly to preserve colour, finished in garlic butter.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot for blanching, Bowl of ice water, Wide pan

  • 200g broccoli florets
  • 150g green beans — trimmed
  • 2 medium carrots — julienned
  • 1 courgette — sliced
  • 100g baby corn
  • 3 tbsp butter or oil
  • 3 garlic cloves — sliced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander or parsley

Method

  1. Blanch each vegetable separately in heavily salted boiling water: carrots 3 min, beans and broccoli 2 min, courgette and corn 1 min.
  2. Immediately transfer each to ice water. Drain well.
  3. Heat butter or oil in a wide pan over medium-high.
  4. Add garlic. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add all blanched vegetables. Toss to coat.
  6. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  7. Toss with coriander. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The ice bath is what keeps the vegetables brilliantly coloured.
  • Each vegetable needs different blanching times — work in batches.
  • Serve immediately after the final toss — vegetables lose colour and crispness quickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • No ice bath — dull, overcooked colours.
  • Single blanching time for all vegetables.

Cultural Note

Mixed vegetables at a Tanzanian restaurant or hotel signal a kitchen that cares about every part of the meal — the green vegetables served alongside the pilau or grilled fish are as important as the main event.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Blanched vegetables keep 2 days. Reheat in pan.

Reheating: Toss briefly in hot pan.

Serve with: Any main course, Alongside pilau or rice, With grilled fish or meat
