Genna (Ethiopian Christmas) Doro is the Doro Wat prepared for Christmas morning — made after the 43-day Advent fast, served as the breaking of the fast alongside lamb Key Wat. The combination of chicken and lamb on the Christmas mesob is the most complete Ethiopian celebration feast of the year.

          Cultural Note

          Ethiopian Christmas (Genna) falls on January 7th and is celebrated with the overnight Genna football game (a traditional stick-and-ball game played at dawn), the church service, and the breaking of the fast with Doro Wat and lamb. The Christmas mesob table is the most complete and most beautiful Ethiopian food experience of the year.
