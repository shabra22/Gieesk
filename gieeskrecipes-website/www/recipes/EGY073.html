🍇 Khoshaf (Dried Fruit Compote)

Khoshaf — خشاف

Dried apricots, figs, cranberries and prunes soaked in hot water with sugar and orange blossom water, chilled and topped with nuts.

3h 40mTotal time
10mPrep
6Servings
220Calories
EasyDifficulty

The dried fruits are covered with hot water, sweetened with a tablespoon of sugar and a little orange blossom water, and chilled in the fridge for 3 to 4 hours before serving, topped with almonds and pine nuts. The notes offer a stove method: cook the fruit for 20 minutes until it softens.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl or jar

  • ½ cup dried apricot.
  • ½ cup dried figs.
  • ½ cup dried cranberries or raisins.
  • 1 cup of dried pitted prunes plums.
  • 5 cup hot water or to cover.
  • 1 Tablespoon sugar.
  • ½ teaspoon orange blossom water.
  • Nuts of your choice preferably almonds and pine nuts.

Method

  1. Put the dried fruit in a bowl, cover with hot water and stir in the sugar and orange blossom water.
  2. Chill in the fridge for 3 to 4 hours before serving (the article body says 2 to 4).
  3. Top with nuts. Alternatively cook the dried fruits on the stove for 20 minutes until softened.

Chef Tips

  • The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The ingredient line says '5 cup' of water, a probable misprint; use enough to cover.
  • Traditional soaking liquids also include vanilla, qamar el din juice, milk or sobia (page note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-sweetening; the dried fruit is already sweet.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim says khoshaf is served at holidays and Ramadan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Nuts
