🌭 Ciauscolo Spalmabile

Ciauscolo

The Marche's uniquely spreadable cured salami, finely ground pork smoked gently and cured until soft enough to spread directly onto warm, toasted bread like a rich pate.

10 minPrep

EasyDifficulty

Unlike most Italian salami, which are meant to be thinly sliced, ciauscolo is deliberately ground extremely fine and cured to a genuinely soft, spreadable consistency, a texture that sets it apart from virtually every other cured meat in Italian charcuterie.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small knife or spreader, Serving platter

  • 200 g (7 oz) ciauscolo (or substitute a soft, spreadable Italian salami)
  • Crusty bread or bruschetta, toasted
  • A drizzle of honey (optional)
  • Fresh rosemary, for garnish

Method

  1. Bring the ciauscolo to room temperature, which makes it easier to spread.
  2. Toast slices of crusty bread lightly.
  3. Spread the ciauscolo generously over the warm toasted bread, similar to a pate.
  4. Add a light drizzle of honey if desired, for a sweet contrast.
  5. Garnish with a little fresh rosemary and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Bringing the ciauscolo to room temperature is essential for easy, even spreading.
  • Warm, freshly toasted bread makes for the most pleasant contrast against the soft, rich meat.
  • A light drizzle of honey is a traditional, welcome addition that balances the meat richness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving straight from the refrigerator, when it is too firm to spread easily.
  • Overloading the bread, when a moderate, even layer is more traditional and enjoyable.
  • Substituting a firm, slice-only salami, which will not replicate the intended spreadable texture.

Cultural Note

Ciauscolo holds a protected geographic status in the Marche and Umbria, its uniquely spreadable texture setting it apart from virtually all other Italian cured meats.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 1 week once opened.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A glass of red wine from the Marche
