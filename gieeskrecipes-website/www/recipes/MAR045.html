🍗 Chicken Chorba

Chorba dyal djej (شوربة بالدجاج)

A light, clear chicken soup with chickpeas, potatoes, grated tomato and broken spaghetti, golden with turmeric and saffron.

1h 30mTotal time
15mPrep
1h 15mCook
6Servings
390Calories
EasyDifficulty

Benkabbou browns chicken wings with onions and spices, simmers them with chickpeas in 1.5 litres of water for 40 minutes, adds grated tomatoes, paste and diced potatoes for 15 more minutes, then cooks broken spaghetti and parsley in the pot. She says every Moroccan family has its own chorba recipe.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large casserole with lid

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 1kg chicken wings
  • 2 teaspoons turmeric
  • Generous pinch saffron threads
  • 1 teaspoon salt or more to taste
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 150g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight and drained (or 300 gr canned chickpeas, drained)
  • 2 medium sized tomatoes, grated
  • 1 ½ tablespoon tomato paste
  • 2 large white potatoes (500 gr) diced into 2 cm pieces
  • 100g spaghetti broken into 3 cm long pieces
  • 3 tablespoons chopped parsley and more for serving

Method

  1. In a large casserole warm the olive oil over medium-high heat and add the onions, chicken, spices, salt and pepper. Leave 7 to 10 minutes to lightly brown the chicken and sweat the onions, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add the chickpeas and 1.5 litres of water and bring to a boil. Reduce to medium-low, cover and simmer gently for 40 minutes.
  3. Add the grated tomatoes, tomato paste and potatoes; bring to a boil, reduce to medium-low, cover and simmer gently for 15 minutes. The chickpeas, potatoes and chicken should then be fully cooked.
  4. Add the broken spaghetti and parsley; simmer until the pasta is fully cooked, adjusting the time to the package instructions.
  5. Serve warm with more parsley.

Chef Tips

  • If you forgot to soak chickpeas, use 300 g canned and add them with the potatoes.
  • Doneness cue: chickpeas, potatoes and chicken fully cooked.
  • The finished soup is fairly watery and clear.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the pasta too early.
  • Skipping the browning.
  • Salting too heavily before tasting.

Health Benefits

  • Protein from chicken and chickpeas; potatoes make it filling.
  • Light broth, low in fat once wings are skimmed.

Cultural Note

The author calls chorba a traditional Berber soup enjoyed across North Africa and says every Moroccan family has its own chorba recipe.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge, without the pasta if possible.

Reheating: Reheat gently, adding water.

Serve with: Khobz
