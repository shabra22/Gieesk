🎃 Kenyan Roasted Pumpkin Seeds

Mbegu za Maboga

Kenyan Roasted Pumpkin Seeds — pumpkin seeds cleaned, dried, and dry-roasted with salt until toasted and crunchy. A beloved zero-waste Kenyan homestead snack — the seeds saved from cooking pumpkin and transformed into a nutritious, satisfying crunch.

Roasted pumpkin seeds are one of Kenya's most resource-conscious snacks — the seeds that might otherwise be discarded when preparing pumpkin are cleaned, dried, and roasted into something genuinely delicious. In rural Kenyan homesteads, nothing from the pumpkin is wasted: the leaves become mboga (KEN082), the flesh becomes food, and the seeds become this snack.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pan, Paper towels or clean cloth

  • 2 cups fresh pumpkin seeds — cleaned
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil — optional
  • ½ tsp paprika — optional
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder — optional

Method

  1. Scoop seeds from the pumpkin. Place in a bowl of water and separate from the stringy fibres.
  2. Rinse thoroughly.
  3. Spread on a clean cloth or paper towels and pat dry. Allow to air dry for at least 30 minutes — the drier the better.
  4. Optional: toss with oil and seasonings if using.
  5. Heat a large dry pan over medium heat.
  6. Add the seeds in a single layer.
  7. Stir continuously for 15—20 minutes until golden, toasted, and beginning to pop.
  8. Season with salt immediately while hot.
  9. Cool completely before storing.

Chef Tips

  • Completely dry seeds roast much more evenly — do not skip the drying step.
  • They are done when they turn golden and begin to pop slightly.
  • For extra flavour, toss in oil and spices before roasting for a coated version.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet seeds — steam instead of roasting, and spit dangerously.
  • Not stirring enough — uneven roasting and burning.

The Science

Pumpkin seeds contain approximately 50% fat by weight — when heated, the surface oils facilitate the Maillard reaction, creating the characteristic toasted flavour. The popping sound is the water trapped within the seed shell converting to steam and rupturing the outer husk.

Health Benefits

  • Exceptionally rich in magnesium — one serving provides over 50% of daily needs.
  • High in zinc for immune function.
  • Good source of iron, copper, and manganese.
  • Rich in antioxidants and healthy fats.

Cultural Note

Roasted pumpkin seeds represent the resourceful spirit of Kenyan homestead cooking — the instinct to find nourishment and flavour in what others might discard. They have been a traditional snack in Kenyan farming communities for generations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container for up to 3 weeks.

Reheating: Not required.

Serve with: Kenyan Masala Chai, As a standalone snack, Scattered over salads or soups
