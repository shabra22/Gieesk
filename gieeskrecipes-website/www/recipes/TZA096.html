🌿 Tanzanian Sweet Potato Leaves

Matembele

Tanzanian Matembele — sweet potato vine tips sautéed with onions, tomatoes, and garlic. The tender, slightly sweet greens from the same plant that produces Tanzania's beloved sweet potato — another zero-waste Tanzanian vegetable.

Matembele (sweet potato leaves and vine tips) are harvested from the sweet potato plant while the tubers develop underground — providing a continuous supply of nutritious greens from a plant that also produces a significant root crop. The leaves are sweeter and more tender than most African greens.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 400g sweet potato vine tips and leaves — washed and chopped
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add matembele. Stir to combine.
  5. Cover and cook 5—6 minutes until completely wilted.
  6. Season. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Only the young tender vine tips — mature stems are stringy.
  • Sweet potato leaves wilt faster than mchicha — check at 4 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using mature stems — tough and stringy.
  • Overcooking.

The Science

Sweet potato leaves have a higher beta-carotene content than the tubers themselves — the green colour from chlorophyll accompanies exceptionally high provitamin A levels.

Health Benefits

  • Exceptionally high in beta-carotene.
  • Good iron and calcium.
  • Very low in calories.

Cultural Note

Matembele is Tanzania's gift from the sweet potato plant — while the tubers provide starchy carbohydrate below ground, the leaves provide vitamins and minerals above. Nothing goes to waste.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Beans, Fish stew
