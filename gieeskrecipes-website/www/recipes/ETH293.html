🍚 Ethiopian Berbere Rice Bowl

ቤርበሬ ሩዝ — Berbere Rice Bowl

Ethiopian Berbere Rice Bowl — spiced basmati rice cooked in berbere-infused broth, topped with berbere-seared beef tibs, ayib, silsi, and avocado. The Ethiopian grain bowl — for those who want Ethiopian flavours in a non-injera format.

The Ethiopian rice bowl provides an alternative grain format for Ethiopian flavours — the berbere-infused rice carries the spice tradition into a different carbohydrate base, with tibs and the classic accompaniments on top.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan for rice, Heavy pan for tibs

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 tbsp kibbeh

Tibs (ETH022) — 300g beef, berbere-seared

  • 3 tbsp ayib
  • 3 tbsp silsi
  • ½ avocado — sliced
  • Fresh coriander

Method

  1. Heat kibbeh in saucepan. Add berbere. Stir 30 seconds.
  2. Add rice. Toast in berbere-kibbeh 1 minute.
  3. Add water. Bring to boil. Cover. Simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Make tibs per ETH022.
  5. Serve rice in bowl. Top with tibs, ayib, silsi, avocado.
  6. Scatter coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Toasting the rice in kibbeh before adding water infuses every grain with berbere flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not toasting rice in kibbeh.

The Science

Toasting rice before boiling coats each grain with kibbeh fat — this fat barrier slows water absorption slightly, producing more separate grains (less sticky) and distributing the berbere flavour uniformly.

Cultural Note

The Ethiopian rice bowl is for the Ethiopian who grew up eating both injera and rice, and who wants Ethiopian flavours in whichever grain format they choose. It is Ethiopian food philosophy — the spice tradition applied to any carbohydrate base.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Rice: with water. Tibs: briefly.

Serve with: As a standalone grain bowl
