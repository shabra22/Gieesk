🥜 Swahili Groundnut Stew

Mchuzi wa Karanga wa Kiswahili

Swahili Groundnut Stew — a rich peanut sauce stew with Zanzibar spices, coconut milk, and mixed vegetables. The coastal fusion of the inland peanut tradition with coastal coconut — richer and more aromatic than either alone.

Swahili groundnut stew is the coastal meeting point of two great Tanzanian flavour traditions — the inland peanut stew and the coastal coconut curry. Combined with the Zanzibar spice palette, it produces a vegetarian stew of extraordinary richness.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 5 tbsp peanut butter
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 200g sweet potato — cubed
  • 150g green beans
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and spices. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  3. Add sweet potato and water. Simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Add green beans. Simmer 6 more minutes.
  5. Mix peanut butter with ½ cup warm water. Add to pot.
  6. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 8 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Mix peanut paste with warm water first.
  • Add coconut milk after peanuts — prevents curdling.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding dry peanut paste — lumps.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

Cultural Note

Swahili groundnut stew is the taste of two traditions meeting — the inland peanut and the coastal coconut, unified by the Zanzibar spice palette into something that belongs entirely to the coast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently with splash of water.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002)
