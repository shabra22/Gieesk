🍵 Ethiopian Chicken Soup

ዶሮ ሾርባ — Doro Shorba

Ethiopian Doro Shorba — chicken simmered with onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, and Ethiopian aromatics into a restorative golden broth. Given to the sick, to new mothers, and as a warming meal on highland evenings. The Ethiopian healing soup.

Ethiopian chicken soup is the country's most restorative dish — the long simmering of a whole chicken with turmeric, ginger, and cardamom produces a broth of extraordinary healing quality. Given to the recovering, the new mother, and the highland farmer after a long day.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1 whole chicken or 1 kg chicken pieces — bone-in
  • 2 litres water
  • 1 onion — halved
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger — sliced
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Fresh coriander and lime to serve

Method

  1. Place chicken in a large pot. Cover with cold water.
  2. Bring to a boil. Skim foam thoroughly — 5 minutes.
  3. Add onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cardamom, and pepper.
  4. Simmer covered on lowest heat for 50—60 minutes.
  5. Remove chicken. Shred meat. Discard bones.
  6. Return meat to the golden broth.
  7. Season. Serve with coriander and lime.

Chef Tips

  • Skim the foam carefully — produces a cleaner, clearer broth.
  • The lowest possible simmer — a boil produces a cloudy broth.
  • Add turmeric generously — it is the healing and colour element.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not skimming foam.
  • Boiling instead of simmering.

The Science

See ETH034 for chicken soup cooking science. Turmeric's curcumin dissolves into the chicken fat as it renders during cooking — distributing the anti-inflammatory compound throughout the broth. This is why turmeric chicken soup has both the anti-inflammatory compound and the fat to make it bioavailable simultaneously.

Health Benefits

  • Collagen and gelatin from chicken bones.

Cultural Note

Doro Shorba is the food that Ethiopian mothers make when their children are ill — the golden broth that says 'I am taking care of you'. In the Ethiopian highlands, where evening temperatures drop sharply, a bowl of doro shorba is the most comforting thing possible.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Bring to a boil.

Serve with: As a standalone healing soup, With injera for a light meal
