🐖 Porceddu Sardo

Porceddu

Sardinia's celebrated whole roast suckling pig, traditionally spit-roasted slowly over aromatic myrtle wood embers until the skin turns deeply crackling and the meat falls-apart tender.

Porceddu is central to Sardinian celebration cooking, the young pig seasoned simply with salt and roasted for hours over myrtle wood, whose distinctive Mediterranean aroma perfumes the meat unlike any other regional roast in Italy.

Ingredients

Equipment: Spit roaster or large oven, Long skewer or spit

  • 1 whole suckling pig (about 6-8kg/13-18lb), cleaned and prepared by a butcher
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Fresh myrtle branches or rosemary, for aromatics
  • Extra virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Season the cavity and skin of the pig generously with coarse salt.
  2. Stuff the cavity with fresh myrtle branches or rosemary.
  3. If spit-roasting, secure the pig on a spit and set up over a bed of hot embers, ideally myrtle or oak wood.
  4. Roast slowly, turning regularly, for 2.5–3 hours, basting occasionally with olive oil, until the skin is deeply golden and crackling and the meat is very tender.
  5. If oven-roasting, place in a large roasting pan at 160°C (325°F) for the same duration, increasing the temperature to 220°C (425°F) for the final 20 minutes to crisp the skin.
  6. Let rest briefly before carving and serving.

Chef Tips

  • True porceddu depends on myrtle wood for its distinctive aromatic quality — source it if at all possible for an authentic result.
  • Slow, steady heat throughout the long roast is what achieves both tender meat and properly crackling skin.
  • This is genuinely a celebration dish requiring real preparation — plan for a special occasion and enough guests to enjoy the whole roast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Roasting at too high a heat throughout, which can burn the skin before the meat is properly cooked through.
  • Skipping the myrtle aromatics, missing the dish's signature Sardinian character.
  • Not resting the meat before carving, causing juices to escape.

Cultural Note

Porceddu is central to Sardinian celebration cooking, traditionally served at weddings, festivals, and other major gatherings, its myrtle-wood roasting method deeply tied to the island's landscape.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate leftover meat for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently in the oven; the skin will lose some crispness.

Serve with: Pane Carasau, A robust Sardinian red wine
