🍗 Swahili Chicken and Rice

Wali na Kuku wa Kiswahili

Swahili Chicken and Rice — chicken slow-cooked in a fragrant Zanzibar spice sauce, its rich broth used to cook basmati rice to golden perfection, the chicken placed back on top for the final steam. A simpler version of chicken pilau — all the flavour, half the work.

Swahili chicken and rice is the everyday version of chicken pilau — same flavours, same technique, less effort. It is the mid-week celebration dish for coastal Tanzanian families.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large sufuria with lid

  • 1 kg chicken — bone-in pieces
  • 3 cups basmati rice — soaked
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 6 cups water or stock
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Spices: 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cardamom, ¼ tsp cloves

    Method

    1. Brown chicken. Remove.
    2. Cook onions golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
    3. Return chicken. Add water. Simmer covered 35—40 minutes until very tender.
    4. Remove chicken. Measure broth — need 5 cups. Add rice.
    5. Boil, then cover, lowest heat 20 minutes.
    6. Place chicken atop rice. Rest 10 minutes.
    7. Garnish with coriander.

    Chef Tips

    • The broth measurement is critical.
    • Bone-in chicken is essential for the broth.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Incorrect broth ratio.
    • Boneless chicken — weak broth.

    Cultural Note

    Swahili chicken and rice is the coastal family's most reliable celebration dish — made on birthdays, homecomings, and Friday afternoons when the week has been good.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

    Reheating: Covered with splash of water.
