🥑 Kenyan Avocado Salsa

Salsa ya Parachichi

Kenyan Avocado Salsa — ripe Kenyan avocado roughly mashed with tomatoes, onion, fresh chilli, coriander, and lime juice. A chunky, vibrant condiment that sits between guacamole and kachumbari — the ideal accompaniment to grilled meats, chips, and chapati.

Kenya's extraordinary avocados — large, buttery, and deeply flavoured — make this salsa something special. It is rougher than guacamole and fresher than a dip, with visible pieces of tomato and onion throughout the avocado base. It is enormously versatile — equally at home on grilled meats, alongside fried fish, or simply scooped up with chapati.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Fork

  • 2 ripe avocados — peeled and roughly mashed
  • 2 medium tomatoes — finely diced
  • 1 small onion — finely diced
  • 1—2 fresh green chillies — finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • 2 tbsp lime or lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove — minced
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Peel avocados and roughly mash in a bowl — keep some texture, not smooth.
  2. Add lime juice immediately and mix — this prevents browning.
  3. Add tomatoes, onion, chilli, coriander, and garlic.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Mix gently to combine — do not over-work.
  6. Taste and adjust lime and salt.
  7. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Add lime juice first and mix before adding vegetables — it prevents browning from the start.
  • Keep it chunky — this is a salsa, not a puree.
  • Taste the avocado before adding lime — very creamy Kenyan avocados may need more acid.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-mashing — loses the chunky character.
  • Not adding enough lime — the salsa browns quickly without adequate acid.
  • Making too far ahead — best within 30 minutes of preparation.

The Science

Avocado browning is caused by polyphenol oxidase enzymes reacting with oxygen. Lime juice's citric acid and ascorbic acid inhibit these enzymes, keeping the salsa green. The lime must be added immediately after cutting.

Cultural Note

Avocado salsa reflects the evolution of contemporary Kenyan cooking — taking Kenya's magnificent avocados and combining them with the elements of kachumbari in a condiment that is both deeply local and universally appealing.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately. Press cling film directly on the surface and refrigerate for up to 2 hours.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

Serve with: Nyama Choma, Fried Chicken, Chips Masala, Chapati, Grilled Fish
