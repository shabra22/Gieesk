🌿 Oromo Qocho with Lamb

ቆጮ — Oromo Qocho

Southern Oromo Qocho with Lamb — enset-based Kocho bread served with a mildly spiced Oromo-style lamb stew. The southern Oromo communities that border the Wolayta and Gurage regions have adopted enset as a staple alongside their traditional dairy foods.

Southern Oromo Qocho represents the cultural meeting point between Oromo cattle culture and the enset-eating traditions of the southern Ethiopian peoples.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan

    Kocho (ETH003) or enset substitute

    • 600g lamb — cubed
    • 2 onions — diced
    • 3 garlic cloves
    • 1 tbsp ginger
    • 1 tsp berbere
    • ½ tsp turmeric
    • 2 tomatoes — diced
    • 2 tbsp oil
    • 1 tsp salt

    Method

    1. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, berbere, turmeric.
    2. Add lamb. Brown 5 minutes. Add tomatoes. Add ½ cup water.
    3. Simmer 40 minutes until tender. Season.
    4. Serve with Kocho bread.

    Chef Tips

    • Oromo version uses less berbere than Amhara.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Over-spicing.

    Cultural Note

    Southern Oromo Qocho with lamb represents the cultural mixing zone of southern Ethiopia — where the cattle-herding Oromo tradition meets the enset-farming southern highland cultures.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

    Reheating: Gently.

    Serve with: Kocho bread (ETH003), At southern Oromo celebrations
