💍 Kenyan Wedding Beef Pilau

Pilau ya Harusi

Kenyan Wedding Beef Pilau — the grandest expression of Kenyan pilau. Made for 20+ guests at weddings, the rice is richly spiced, the beef is the finest cut, and the preparation is a community effort. The meal that marks the most important day in a Kenyan family's life.

Wedding pilau is the ultimate expression of the pilau tradition — made on a scale that requires multiple cooks, enormous sufurias, and a full day of preparation. The wedding pilau cook (often a renowned local cook hired specifically for the occasion) is one of the most important wedding vendors in Kenya. This recipe scales to 20 portions — a small wedding batch.

Ingredients

Equipment: Extra-large sufuria (10L+), Spice grinder, Long wooden spoon

  • 3 kg best-quality beef — cubed
  • 10 cups basmati rice — washed and soaked
  • 4 large onions — thinly sliced
  • 12 garlic cloves — minced
  • 4 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 6 large tomatoes — diced
  • 8 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 20 cups beef stock
  • 4 tsp salt
  • ½ cup fresh coriander

Whole spices

  • 4 tsp cumin seeds
  • 16 cardamom pods
  • 4 cinnamon sticks
  • 16 whole cloves
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns
  • 4 star anise
  • 2 tsp coriander seeds

Method

  1. Toast all spices in a dry pan until deeply fragrant. Grind half, keep half whole.
  2. In the large sufuria, heat oil until very hot. Brown beef in large batches.
  3. Cook onions until deeply golden — 15 minutes for this quantity.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, spices, and tomatoes. Cook 10 minutes until very thick.
  5. Return all beef. Add stock. Simmer covered for 45—50 minutes.
  6. Drain soaked rice. Add to pot. Stir gently.
  7. Bring to a boil, reduce to lowest heat, cover tightly with lid and a cloth.
  8. Steam for 20—25 minutes.
  9. Rest 15 minutes. Fold gently. Garnish generously with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • For wedding scale, prepare the spice blend and cook the beef the day before — assemble with rice on the day.
  • The cloth under the lid absorbs excess steam and prevents the rice from becoming too wet — the wedding cook's secret.
  • Taste and adjust salt after the beef step and again after the rice is cooked.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-seasoning at scale — the spice proportions need to be maintained at larger quantities.
  • Insufficient resting — wedding pilau must rest at least 15 minutes.

The Science

At wedding scale, heat distribution becomes more challenging — the sufuria's base receives much more heat than the sides. The cloth under the lid and thorough resting help equalise temperature and moisture distribution throughout the large volume of rice.

Cultural Note

Wedding pilau is the culinary centrepiece of the most important day in a Kenyan family's life. The skill of the pilau cook is a point of family pride — and the taste and quality of the wedding pilau is discussed in the community for months afterwards.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 4 days.

Reheating: Reheat in large covered pot over low heat.
