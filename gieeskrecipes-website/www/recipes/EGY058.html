🍰 Basbousa (Egyptian Semolina and Coconut Cake)

Basbousa — بسبوسة

A buttery semolina and coconut cake baked golden, cut into pieces and soaked with hot lemon syrup.

1h 5mTotal time
20mPrep
35mCook
8Servings
470Calories
EasyDifficulty

Semolina, sugar, coconut, flour and baking powder are mixed with melted butter until the texture is sand-like and yellow, then milk is added gradually to a smooth batter. It is spread in a 26 to 28 cm tray and baked at 180°C (350°F) for 25 minutes or until the top is golden brown. Cut immediately, then pour the hot syrup, which has simmered until slightly thickened, over the hot cake and cover for 5 to 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: 26 to 28 cm baking tray, Saucepan, Foil

  • 1 cup semolina
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup desiccated coconut
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup full cream milk

Syrup (Sharbat)

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1.5 cups water
  • ½ lemon, squeezed

Method

  1. Mix the semolina, sugar, coconut, flour and baking powder. Add the melted butter and stir until the mixture has a sand-like, yellow texture.
  2. Gradually add the milk and mix to a smooth batter.
  3. Spread in the tray (28 cm for a thinner cake, 26 cm for a thicker one) and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25 minutes, or until the top is golden brown.
  4. Syrup: bring the sugar, water and lemon juice to a boil over low heat, stirring occasionally, and simmer until slightly thickened.
  5. Cut the cake into squares or triangles immediately after baking and pour the hot syrup over the hot cake.
  6. Cover with foil, or return to the turned-off oven, for 5 to 10 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: sand-like yellow mix; top golden brown; syrup slightly thickened. The cover rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • Hot syrup on hot cake is how this page does it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the cake cool before the syrup, which stops it soaking in.

Cultural Note

Omneya Negm calls basbousa one of Egypt's most beloved desserts, served at gatherings and during Ramadan, with coconut and butter marking the Egyptian version.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep covered at room temperature 2 days or refrigerate up to 5 (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Arabic coffee or mint tea (page suggestion)
