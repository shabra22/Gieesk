🍗 Kenyan Creamy Garlic Chicken

Kuku wa Cream ya Vitunguu Saumu

Kenyan Creamy Garlic Chicken — pan-seared chicken breasts in a rich cream sauce with roasted garlic, fresh herbs, and a hint of paprika. The most popular chicken dish in Nairobi's hotel restaurants — universally adored and genuinely excellent.

Creamy garlic chicken is the dish that appears on every Nairobi hotel menu for good reason — the combination of properly seared chicken and a rich, garlicky cream sauce satisfies in a way that simpler preparations do not. The Kenyan version adds paprika and fresh coriander for character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large oven-safe pan, Tongs

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 6 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Season chicken with salt, paprika, and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Sear chicken for 4—5 minutes per side until golden. Remove.
  4. Reduce heat. Add butter and garlic. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Add stock. Deglaze, scraping up any browned bits.
  6. Add cream, mustard, and paprika. Bring to a simmer.
  7. Return chicken to the pan.
  8. Simmer covered for 10—12 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
  9. Add lemon juice and coriander. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Do not move the chicken during searing — this is how the golden crust develops.
  • Deglaze thoroughly — the browned bits are the most flavourful part of the pan.
  • Add lemon juice only at the end — acid in the cream sauce too early can cause curdling.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Moving chicken during searing.
  • Boiling the cream sauce vigorously — it breaks.

The Science

The fond (browned bits) left in the pan after searing is a concentrate of Maillard-reaction products. Deglazing with stock dissolves these compounds into the sauce, providing a depth of flavour that a cream sauce made without the searing step cannot replicate.

Cultural Note

Creamy garlic chicken represents Nairobi's hotel and restaurant scene at its most crowd-pleasing — a dish that satisfies every table while showcasing the quality of Kenyan poultry and the skill of a properly trained kitchen.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop with splash of cream.
