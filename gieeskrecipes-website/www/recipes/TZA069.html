🦞 Tanzanian Grilled Lobster

Kamba Kochi wa Kuchoma

Tanzanian Grilled Lobster — Indian Ocean spiny lobster halved and grilled over charcoal with a Zanzibar garlic-butter and tamarind glaze. The pinnacle of Zanzibar seafood — the most prized catch of the Indian Ocean, simply and magnificently prepared.

Zanzibar spiny lobster grilled at the Forodhani night market for a fraction of what it would cost anywhere else in the world. The tamarind-garlic glaze is uniquely Tanzanian — the tartness of tamarind against the richness of lobster is a revelation.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp heavy knife, Charcoal grill, Basting brush

  • 2 whole spiny lobsters (500—700g each)

Zanzibar Garlic-Tamarind Glaze

  • 80g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Make Zanzibar glaze: melt butter with garlic, tamarind, honey, turmeric, chilli. Simmer 2 minutes. Add lemon and coriander. Divide in two.
  2. Split lobsters: sharp knife through head, push down to halve. Remove stomach sack.
  3. Brush cut side with glaze.
  4. Grill cut-side down over medium coals 5—6 minutes.
  5. Flip. Brush shell side. Grill 5—6 minutes. Baste every 2 minutes.
  6. Serve with remaining glaze.

Chef Tips

  • Medium coals only.
  • Do not overcook — rubbery beyond 70°C internal.
  • Cut side down first.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat.
  • Overcooking.

The Science

See KEN274 for lobster cooking science. The tamarind's tartaric acid slightly denatures surface proteins, helping the glaze adhere.

Cultural Note

Grilled lobster at Forodhani Gardens — the fish caught that morning, grilled that evening, eaten at the water's edge with dhows sailing past — is one of the greatest food experiences in Africa.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Must be eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Kachumbari, Fresh Lime
