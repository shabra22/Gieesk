🥘 Senegalese Paella with Dried Saloum Seafood

Paella sénégalaise — riz aux fruits de mer séchés du Saloum

A thiebou yapp-style rice with the Saloum dried-shellfish set — guedj, yet, dried shrimp, tuffa, yokhos and pagne — and a faint measure of nététou.

Lamine Faye’s ‘paella sénégalaise’ is a thiebou yapp (rice with meat) cooked with the dried seafood of the Saloum islands. A kilo of broken rice, 800 g of beef or lamb, two large onions, four garlic cloves, two tablespoons of tomato concentrate and three fresh tomatoes make the base, with a nokkos of onion, garlic, parsley, pepper, chile, a stock cube and a little nététou, and vegetables of choice. The dried set — a piece of guedj Yass, a piece of yet, tuffa, yokhos, a handful of dried shrimp and pagne — goes in to release its flavour. The five steps give no time or temperature.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid

  • 1 kg broken rice
  • 800 g meat (beef or lamb)
  • 2 large onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato concentrate
  • 3 fresh tomatoes
  • Nokkos: onion, garlic, parsley, pepper, chile, stock cube, plus a little nététou in small quantity — amounts not printed
  • Carrots, cabbage, cassava, aubergine, as chosen — quantities not printed
  • Oil, salt, pepper, chile — quantities not printed
  • 1 piece of guedj Yass
  • 1 piece of yet (dried Cymbium)
  • Tuffa — quantity not printed
  • Yokhos — quantity not printed
  • A handful of dried shrimp
  • Pagne — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Fry the meat and the onions. Add the tomato concentrate and fresh tomatoes.
  2. Pound the nokkos and add it with a little nététou.
  3. Add water, then the dried seafood: guedj, yet, tuffa, yokhos, pagne and dried shrimp. Simmer so that all the flavours are released.
  4. Cook the vegetables; once tender, take them out and set aside with the meat.
  5. Add the rice to the broth and cook until done; serve the meat and vegetables on top.

Chef Tips

  • The source’s two cues are ‘once tender, remove and set aside with the meat’ and ‘let it simmer so that all the flavours are released’.
  • The five printed steps contain no time or temperature; the only figures are the header times (30 minutes preparation, 1 hour cooking).
  • Use nététou sparingly — the page says ‘en faible quantité’.
  • The dried-seafood names (tuffa, yokhos, pagne) are the page’s own and are not glossed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-salting before the dried seafood has been tasted.
  • Overcooking the vegetables.

Cultural Note

The page is also a shop for the author’s dried seafood, so the product is the point as well as the recipe; it is the only source found that names the Saloum dried-shellfish set together. The ‘paella’ label is the author’s own.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 1 day refrigerated.

Reheating: Steam gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: Lemon wedges
