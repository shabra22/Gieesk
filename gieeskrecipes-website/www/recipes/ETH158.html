🫙 Ethiopian Chilli Vinegar

በርበሬ ኮምቡቻ — Berbere Xilal

Ethiopian Chilli Vinegar — white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar infused with dried bird's eye chillies, garlic, and berbere spices. The table condiment served at Ethiopian restaurants — used to add acid and heat to dishes.

Chilli vinegar is the modern Ethiopian restaurant's table condiment — the acid element that provides a different heat experience from awaze and adds brightness to any Ethiopian dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Clean glass bottle

  • 1 cup white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 6 dried bird's eye chillies — whole
  • 2 garlic cloves — halved
  • ½ tsp berbere

Method

  1. Place chillies, garlic, and berbere in a clean bottle.
  2. Pour vinegar over.
  3. Seal tightly.
  4. Infuse at room temperature for 5—7 days before use.
  5. Shake before using.

Chef Tips

  • Red wine vinegar produces a more complex flavour; white is more neutral.
  • 1 week minimum infusion — the chilli heat needs time to fully infuse.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using before infused.

The Science

Acetic acid in vinegar extracts the water-soluble capsaicin compounds from the chilli more efficiently than oil. Over 5—7 days, the capsaicin concentration in the vinegar reaches equilibrium with the chilli flesh.

Health Benefits

  • Acetic acid supports digestion.
  • Capsaicin from chillies.

Cultural Note

Chilli vinegar is the modern Ethiopian restaurant's condiment table addition — providing the acid and heat element that complements the rich, fat-based sauces.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Indefinitely at room temperature — the acidity prevents spoilage.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a table condiment on any Ethiopian dish, A dash on Shiro Wat, On injera for brightness
