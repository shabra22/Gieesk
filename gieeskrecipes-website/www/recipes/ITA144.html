🌶️ Ventricina

Ventricina

A spreadable, paprika-red cured pork salami from Abruzzo and Molise, coarsely chopped and richly spiced, traditionally served simply sliced over toasted bread.

Ventricina's distinctive coarse texture and deep red color come from generous amounts of sweet and hot Calabrian-style paprika worked through the cured pork, resulting in a salami with real character, best appreciated simply on its own.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp knife, Serving platter

  • 300 g (10.5 oz) ventricina salami, thinly sliced
  • Crusty bread or bruschetta, toasted
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh pecorino or caciocavallo cheese, for serving (optional)

Method

  1. Slice the ventricina thinly, either by hand or with a meat slicer.
  2. Arrange the slices on a serving platter.
  3. Toast the bread slices lightly and drizzle with olive oil.
  4. Serve the ventricina alongside the toasted bread, allowing guests to build their own bites.
  5. Add slices of fresh pecorino or caciocavallo alongside if desired for a fuller antipasto spread.

Chef Tips

  • Genuine ventricina, aged properly, needs nothing more than good bread and perhaps a little olive oil to shine.
  • Slightly warming the bread before serving helps release the salami's aroma when placed on top.
  • Look for ventricina from Abruzzo or Molise specifically, where the tradition is most established.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Slicing it too thick, which can make the texture unpleasantly dense rather than showing off its coarse, spreadable character.
  • Pairing it with overly assertive accompaniments that compete with, rather than complement, its spice.
  • Serving it cold straight from the refrigerator, when it's better enjoyed closer to room temperature.

Cultural Note

Ventricina is a protected specialty of Abruzzo and Molise, its coarse texture and deep paprika color distinguishing it clearly from other Italian cured meats.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Whole ventricina keeps in a cool, dry place for months; sliced portions should be refrigerated and used within a week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust red wine from Abruzzo
