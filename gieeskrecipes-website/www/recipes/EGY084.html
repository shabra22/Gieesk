🍲 Original Lamb Fatta (Simple Eid Version)

Fatta lahma — فتة لحمة

Baked crisp baladi bread, rice and browned boiled lamb layered with a garlic, tomato paste and vinegar broth: the simplest, most basic fatta.

1h 35mTotal time
20mPrep
1h 15mCook
4Servings
620Calories
MediumDifficulty

Lamb is boiled with a whole onion, and a cup of broth is kept back. Bread is cut and baked until crisp, and the lamb is fried over high heat until well browned. Garlic fried in butter until brown is cooked with tomato paste and vinegar and loosened with the broth; a dish is lined with bread, half the sauce goes over, then rice, the lamb and the rest of the sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Frying pan, Baking tray, Serving dish

  • 1/2 kg cubed lamb without bones
  • 2 cups of water
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 5 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 2 loaves of baladi bread (Egyptian pita bread)
  • 2 cups of cooked rice

Method

  1. Boil the lamb in the water with the whole onion, salt and pepper. The page gives no time.
  2. Skim the froth, remove the meat and discard the onion. Save one cup of broth for later.
  3. Cut up the bread and bake it in the oven until crisp. No temperature or time is printed.
  4. Melt two tablespoons of butter and fry the meat over high heat until well browned. Keep it warm under foil.
  5. Fry the garlic in the remaining butter until brown, add the tomato paste, deglaze with the vinegar and add the cup of broth.
  6. To serve: line a dish with the bread, pour half the sauce over it, add the rice, then the lamb, then the other half of the sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: meat well browned; garlic brown; bread crisp. The boiling time is NOT PRINTED, so cook until the lamb is tender.
  • The author suggests a yoghurt-and-mint variation and pine nuts or cashews.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the garlic.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Nevine El Shabrawy calls it perhaps the core of all fatta dishes, the simplest and most basic.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh.

Reheating: Not recommended; the bread goes soft.

Serve with: Yoghurt and mint (author suggestion)
