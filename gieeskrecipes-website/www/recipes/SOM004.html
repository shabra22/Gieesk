🌽 Muufo

Muufo

A dense, grilled cornmeal bread from Mogadishu, traditionally baked against the hot inner walls of a clay oven, its bottom crust deeply charred while the inside stays soft.

Muufo is closely associated with Mogadishu's coastal bakeries, historically cooked by slapping the flattened dough against the scorching inner wall of a clay tandoor-style oven, a technique that gives it a distinctively smoky, charred crust unlike any other Somali bread.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Heavy skillet or griddle (or clay oven if available)

  • 400 g (2 2/3 cups) fine cornmeal
  • 100 g (3/4 cup) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 350 ml (1 1/2 cups) warm water, approximately

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in a little of the warm water and let sit for 10 minutes.
  2. Combine the cornmeal, flour, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Add the yeast mixture and remaining water, kneading into a firm, slightly sticky dough.
  4. Cover and let rise for about 1 hour.
  5. Divide into portions and shape into thick, flattened rounds.
  6. Heat a heavy skillet or griddle until very hot.
  7. Cook each muufo for 10-12 minutes total, turning occasionally, pressing gently, until deeply charred in spots on the outside and cooked through within.
  8. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • A very hot cooking surface is essential to replicate the traditional charred, smoky crust.
  • The dough should be firm enough to hold a thick, substantial shape rather than spreading thin.
  • Traditional muufo gets its distinctive char from direct contact with a scorching clay oven wall - a well-heated cast iron pan is the best home approximation.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking over heat that is too gentle, missing the traditional charred, smoky crust.
  • Making the dough too wet, which prevents it from holding its thick, substantial shape.
  • Undercooking the center, leaving a doughy interior beneath the charred crust.

Cultural Note

Muufo is closely tied to Mogadishu's coastal bakery tradition, its distinctive charred crust a direct result of the clay oven technique historically used to bake it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Warm briefly in a hot dry pan.

Serve with: Maraq (stew), Tea, Ghee
