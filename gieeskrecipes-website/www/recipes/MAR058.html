🧂 Ras el Hanout (Spice Blend)

Ras el hanout (رأس الحانوت)

A fragrant blend of toasted coriander, cumin, peppercorns, cloves, caraway, anise, fennel, star anise, cardamom, cinnamon, nutmeg and dried rose petals.

25mTotal time
10mPrep
15mCook
24Servings
10Calories
EasyDifficulty

The whole spices are spread on a baking sheet at 150°C (300°F) for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, then ground with about 10 gratings of nutmeg, the cinnamon stick and dried rose petals until finely ground. Sift through a fine sieve and store in a closed jar. Benkabbou notes that the name means 'top of the shop' and that recipes differ significantly from family to family.

Ingredients

Equipment: Oven and baking sheet, Spice grinder or food processor, Fine sieve, Jar

  • 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 teaspoon white peppercorns
  • 1 teaspoon whole cloves
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
  • 1 teaspoon anise seeds
  • 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 10g star anise
  • 10g cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 whole nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon dried rose petals

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 150 C (300 F).
  2. Place all the ingredients except the cinnamon stick, whole nutmeg and dried roses on a baking sheet and put in the oven for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Transfer the roasted spices, about 10 gratings of nutmeg, the cinnamon stick and the dried rose petals to a grinder or food processor and process until finely ground.
  4. Sift the blend through a fine sieve and discard what is left. Store in a closed jar for up to 3 years.

Chef Tips

  • Quick version from the page: use pre-ground spices, one teaspoon each.
  • Cue: finely ground, then sifted.
  • Saveur's unsigned North African mix uses allspice and cayenne and keeps 2 months; blends differ.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Roasting the cinnamon, nutmeg and roses with the rest.
  • Skipping the sieve.
  • Storing it in light.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

The author says ras el hanout means 'top of the shop' in Arabic, that most of its spices do not originally come from Morocco, that today up to 60 or more spices may go in, and that recipes differ significantly from family to family.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Up to 3 years in a closed jar, per the page.

Reheating: Not heated.

Serve with: Rubs for lamb and chicken
