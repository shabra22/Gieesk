🫛 Tanzanian Okra Stew

Bamia

Tanzanian Bamia — okra cooked in a spiced tomato-onion sauce until tender. The distinctive slippery texture of okra thickens the sauce naturally — a vegetable that is simultaneously the ingredient and the thickening agent.

Okra stew is loved throughout Tanzania and the Swahili coast — the mucilage that gives okra its characteristic slipperiness dissolves into the sauce during cooking, creating a naturally thickened stew that coats ugali magnificently. Those who love okra adore its texture; this recipe converts even those who are unsure.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 400g fresh okra — washed, trimmed, sliced into 2cm pieces
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ cup water

Method

  1. Do not wash okra after cutting — excess moisture increases sliminess.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and spices. Stir 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes until thick.
  5. Add okra pieces and water.
  6. Simmer covered 15—18 minutes until okra is completely tender.
  7. The sauce will be naturally thickened by the okra mucilage.
  8. Season. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Pat okra completely dry before cooking — moisture increases the sliminess dramatically.
  • High heat initially reduces sliminess.
  • Do not stir excessively — more stirring increases mucilage release.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet okra.
  • Excessive stirring.

The Science

Okra mucilage is composed of polysaccharides (primarily pectin and glycoproteins). When heated, these dissolve into the cooking liquid, increasing viscosity. The degree of sliminess can be reduced by: initial high-heat cooking (which partially breaks down the glycoproteins), acid addition (lemon or tomato), or minimal stirring.

Health Benefits

  • Very high in fibre — particularly soluble fibre (pectin).
  • Good source of vitamin C and folate.
  • The mucilage supports digestive health.

Cultural Note

Okra stew is the dish that divides people — those who love it adore its texture and the way it coats ugali. Tanzanian cooks know that proper preparation produces a much less slimy result than careless cooking.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Rice, Any meat or fish dish
