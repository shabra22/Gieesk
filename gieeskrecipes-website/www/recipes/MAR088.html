Qahwa ma'atra — spiced coffee (قهوة معطرة)

Dark-roasted coffee ground or whole with cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cardamom, clove, black pepper and star anise, then brewed and served with warm milk and sugar.

10mTotal time
5mPrep
5mCook
2Servings
10Calories
EasyDifficulty

The coffee is mixed with a spice blend of cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cardamom, clove, black pepper and star anise and brewed however you like to make coffee. It is served with warm milk and sugar. The page gives no water amount, so the strength is yours to set.

Ingredients

Equipment: Coffee maker or pot of your choice, Cups

  • 1/2 cup dark roasted coffee, freshly ground or whole bean
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/2 tsp ground clove
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground star anise

Method

  1. Combine the coffee with the ground spices.
  2. Brew the spiced coffee however you like to make your coffee.
  3. Serve with warm milk and sugar.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no water quantity and no doneness cue; adjust strength to taste.
  • Whole beans can be ground with the spices.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-brewing so the spices turn bitter (our caution).

Health Benefits

  • Coffee contains caffeine; the spices add flavour without calories.

Cultural Note

The author writes that afternoon tea is a big part of Moroccan culture and that Moroccan coffee comes with that ritual. Tasting Table (Nikita Ephanov, 2023) adds that the spiced coffee, qahwa ma'atra, is typically reserved for the home.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink fresh.

Reheating: Make fresh.

Serve with: Warm milk and sugar
