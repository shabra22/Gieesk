🥦 Ethiopian Grilled Vegetable Bowl

አትክልት ቅቀለ — Atkilit Qiqile Bowl

Ethiopian Grilled Vegetable Bowl — berbere-marinated and grilled zucchini, eggplant, bell pepper, and sweet potato over teff injera pieces with hummus-style shiro, beetroot salad, and green chilli paste. The modern plant-based Ethiopian bowl.

The Ethiopian grilled vegetable bowl applies the berbere tradition to charcoal-grilled vegetables — each vegetable absorbs the berbere marinade and caramelises on the grill, producing a plant-based bowl of genuine depth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill pan or barbecue, Bowl

    Berbere-grilled vegetables

    • 1 zucchini — halved
    • 1 small eggplant — sliced
    • 1 bell pepper — halved
    • 1 sweet potato — cubed
    • 2 tbsp berbere
    • 2 tbsp oil

    Bowl

    • Torn injera pieces
    • Shiro Wat as hummus-style base
    • Beetroot Salad
    • Green Chilli Paste

    Method

    1. Toss vegetables in berbere and oil.
    2. Grill over high heat until charred on edges and tender.
    3. Arrange injera pieces in bowl.
    4. Spoon thick shiro in the centre.
    5. Arrange grilled vegetables.
    6. Add beetroot salad. Dot green chilli paste.

    Chef Tips

    • Each vegetable needs different grill time — sweet potato longest, zucchini shortest.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • All vegetables same time.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian grilled vegetable bowl is the modern café's commitment to showing that plant-based Ethiopian food can be as visually beautiful and gastronomically interesting as the meat dishes.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best assembled to order.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: As a standalone plant-based bowl
