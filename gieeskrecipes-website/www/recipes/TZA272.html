🥜 Haya Peanut Sauce

Ekitooke — Supu ya Karanga ya Wahaya

Haya Ekitooke — the distinctive Haya peanut sauce of Kagera, made from freshly roasted and pounded groundnuts cooked with tomatoes, onions, and smoked fish into a thick, rich sauce. Served over matoke or ugali — the sauce that defines the Kagera kitchen.

The Haya peanut sauce (ekitooke) is the defining condiment of Kagera cooking — richer and more complex than plain groundnut sauce because of the smoked fish addition, which adds a deep umami note that carries throughout the meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar and pestle, Saucepan

  • 1 cup raw groundnuts — roasted and pounded coarsely
  • 50g smoked fish — flaked finely
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1.5 cups water

Method

  1. Roast groundnuts until golden. Pound coarsely in mortar.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  3. Add smoked fish. Stir to combine.
  4. Mix pounded groundnuts with ½ cup warm water.
  5. Add to the pot. Add remaining water.
  6. Simmer 15 minutes until thick. Season.

Chef Tips

  • The smoked fish is what makes this Haya rather than generic peanut sauce.
  • Pound, do not blend — coarser texture is traditional.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using commercial peanut butter — entirely different character.
  • Omitting smoked fish — loses depth.

The Science

The smoked fish adds inosinate (IMP) — a nucleotide that acts synergistically with glutamate (from tomatoes) and guanylate (from peanuts) to produce umami intensity far greater than any single source alone. This is the umami synergy principle — the combination produces more than the sum of the parts.

Cultural Note

Haya ekitooke is the taste that Kagera-born Tanzanians miss most when they leave their region — the particular combination of roasted groundnut, smoked fish, and tomato that defines home for the people of the Great Lakes.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Haya Matoke (TZA245), Ugali (TZA001), Green bananas
