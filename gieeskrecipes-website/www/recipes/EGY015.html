🥔 Tagen Lahma bel Batates (Beef and Potato Casserole, Modern Adaptation)

Tagen lahma bil-batates — طاجن لحمة بالبطاطس

Mushroom and beef cubes braised in 7-spice stock, topped with a yoghurt-enriched mashed-potato crown and baked until bubbling.

2h 30mTotal time
30mPrep
1h 30mCook
6Servings
430Calories
MediumDifficulty

Beef cubes, mushrooms, onion and garlic stand 20 minutes with 7-spice, tomato paste and stock, then braise covered for about 1 to 1¼ hours until the beef is tender. Boiled potatoes are riced with Greek yogurt, egg, cumin and nutmeg, loosened with warm milk and piped over the filling. It bakes at 200°C/400°F for 25 to 30 minutes until the filling bubbles and the top is deeply golden, and rests 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Oven-safe casserole or tagen, Potato ricer, Piping bag (optional)

  • 400 grams beef, cut into small cubes
  • 250 grams mushrooms, quartered
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil or ghee
  • 1½–2 teaspoons Lebanese 7-spice
  • 1 teaspoons tomato paste
  • 1-1 ½ cups beef stock
  • 1–1¼ teaspoon Himalayan salt, or to taste
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

Potato topping

  • 1 kg Russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, boiled and peeled
  • ¼ cup cup Greek yogurt
  • ¼–½ cup warm milk or cream, plus more if needed
  • 1 egg
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Combine the beef, mushrooms, onion, garlic, oil, spice, paste, stock, salt and pepper and let the mixture stand for 20 minutes.
  2. Braise, covered, for about 1 to 1¼ hours, checking after about 45 minutes, until the beef is tender.
  3. Boil the potatoes until completely tender, steam dry for a minute or two, rice them, and mix in the yogurt, egg, cumin and nutmeg. Add warm milk gradually.
  4. Spoon the beef into the casserole, top with the potato, and bake at 200°C/400°F for 25 to 30 minutes, until the filling is bubbling and the top deeply golden. Broil for the last 1 to 2 minutes if wanted.
  5. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: beef tender; potatoes completely tender; filling bubbling; top golden. The 20-minute stand and the 10-minute rest are NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The braise temperature of 175°C/350°F was read once only.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet potatoes making a gluey topping; steam them dry.

Cultural Note

The author frames it as an Egyptian take on a French dish, not a classical Egyptian tagen.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat in a moderate oven until hot through.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
