🍆 Baba Ghanoush (Eggplant Tahini Dip)

Baba ghanoug — بابا غنوج

Roasted eggplant mashed with garlic, lemon, olive oil and a loosened tahini, finished with paprika and fresh herbs.

55mTotal time
10mPrep
45mCook
5Servings
130Calories
EasyDifficulty

Halved, oiled eggplant roasts at 450°F until the interior is soft and the skin peels off easily, about 35 to 45 minutes. The flesh is mashed with garlic, lemon, oil, herbs, spices and diluted tahini until creamy. The page also gives an alternate whole-foil method.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking sheet, Mixing bowl, Fork

  • 1 eggplant, Globe American eggplant
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 2-4 tablespoons tahini, diluted in 3-4 tablespoons warm water
  • 1 clove of garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon coriander, optional
  • 1 teaspoon cumin, optional
  • 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper, optional
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, parsley, or basil, chopped, (I prefer parsley)

Method

  1. Halve the eggplant, oil it and roast at 450°F until the interior is soft and the skin peels off easily, about 35 to 45 minutes.
  2. Mash the flesh and mix in the garlic, lemon, oil, herbs, spices and the diluted tahini until creamy.
  3. Serve with bread; it can be made a day or two ahead.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: interior soft and skin peels off easily. Alternate method from her notes: wrap the whole pierced eggplant in foil and roast on a rack at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes, or until too soft; the two methods disagree on time and temperature.
  • Avoid double dipping, says the page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-roasting, which leaves a bitter, stringy dip.

Health Benefits

  • Eggplant provides fibre; tahini adds calcium.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour says it is part of the mezze platter and is spelled 'Baba Ghanoug' in Egypt.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 5 days (page).

Reheating: Serve cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Aish baladi
