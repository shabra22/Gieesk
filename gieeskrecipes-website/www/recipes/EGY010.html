🥘 Torly (Egyptian Beef and Vegetable Stew)

Torly — تورلي

Seared beef cubes stewed with string beans, carrots, potatoes and tomatoes in tomato juice, served with rice.

1h 20mTotal time
20mPrep
1hCook
5Servings
310Calories
EasyDifficulty

Lean beef cubes are seared until slightly browned, onion and garlic are cooked until translucent and aromatic, and string beans, carrots, potatoes and tomatoes go in with tomato juice, tomato purée and bouillon. The pot is brought to a boil, covered and left on low heat, adding hot water if needed, until the beef is as tender as you like. The page prints no cooking time.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick stewing pot with lid

  • 500 gm lean tender beef cubes; (best use fillet cubes)
  • 1 large onion (quartered)
  • 4-5 garlic pods (peeled, whole)
  • 250 gm green string beans (trimmed)
  • 250 gm carrots (peeled, sliced)
  • 2 medium-sized potatoes (peeled, cubed)
  • 3 large ripe firm tomatoes (quartered)
  • 1 cup tomato juice
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 chicken bouillon cubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt + pepper + 7 spices + herbes de Provençe

Method

  1. Heat the pot on high, add the oil, lower to medium-high and sear the beef until slightly browned. Push it aside.
  2. Cook the onion and garlic until translucent and aromatic, and season with salt, pepper, seven spices and herbes de Provence.
  3. Add the vegetables, tomato juice, purée and bouillon. Bring to a boil, cover and lower the heat, adding hot water if the pot dries.
  4. Cook to your desired degree of tenderness of the beef.
  5. Serve with rice and a green salad.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives NO COOKING TIME; the 60 minutes here is our estimate for the cook time, so judge by the beef being tender.
  • Fillet cubes cook faster than stewing cuts (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the potatoes too early so they dissolve (our caution).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: Rice with vermicelli
