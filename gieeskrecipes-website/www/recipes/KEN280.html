🍫 Kenyan Chocolate Lava Cake

Keki ya Chokoleti Inayotiririka

Kenyan Chocolate Lava Cake — individual dark chocolate fondants baked until the exterior sets while the centre remains molten, served with passion fruit coulis and vanilla ice cream. The most theatrical restaurant dessert — and made with Kenya's excellent coffee-dark chocolate.

Chocolate lava cake (fondant) is the restaurant dessert that produces the most pleasure per bite — the moment when the fork breaks the exterior and the warm chocolate flows out is pure theatre. Made with dark chocolate and a touch of Kenyan espresso, it is a dessert of extraordinary intensity.

Ingredients

Equipment: 4 ramekins or individual cake moulds, Bowl, Oven (180°C), Electric beater

  • 150g dark chocolate (70%+) — chopped
  • 100g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 100g icing sugar
  • 50g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp strong Kenyan espresso

To serve

  • Passion fruit coulis (from 4 passion fruits blended with 1 tbsp honey)
  • Vanilla ice cream
  • Cocoa powder for dusting

Method

  1. Butter and cocoa-dust the ramekins. Refrigerate.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together over a bain-marie. Stir until smooth. Cool slightly.
  3. Beat eggs, yolks, and icing sugar until pale and ribbon-like — 4 minutes.
  4. Fold chocolate into the egg mixture.
  5. Fold in flour and espresso.
  6. Divide into ramekins. Refrigerate until needed (up to 24 hours).
  7. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  8. Bake from refrigerated for exactly 11—12 minutes — the edges should be set, the centre should wobble.
  9. Rest 1 minute. Invert onto plates. Dust with cocoa.
  10. Serve with passion fruit coulis and ice cream.

Chef Tips

  • Prepare and refrigerate the unbaked fondants up to 24 hours ahead — the make-ahead restaurant trick.
  • Timing is everything — 11 minutes from a cold ramekin, 9 from room temperature. Know your oven.
  • Test with a spare ramekin before service to confirm your oven timing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-baking — fully set, no lava.
  • Under-buttering the ramekin — sticks.
  • Not testing timing.

The Science

The outer layer of the fondant reaches full protein coagulation (set) while the centre temperature does not reach this threshold in the 11-minute window, remaining liquid. The precise timing depends on ramekin size, oven accuracy, and batter temperature — a test run is always recommended before service.

Cultural Note

Chocolate lava cake at a Nairobi hotel is the dessert that makes diners feel they are in one of the world's great restaurants — and the passion fruit coulis makes it unmistakably Kenyan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Unbaked fondants refrigerate 24 hours. Freeze for 2 weeks.

Reheating: Bake from frozen — add 2 minutes.

Serve with: Passion Fruit Coulis, Vanilla Ice Cream, Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249)
