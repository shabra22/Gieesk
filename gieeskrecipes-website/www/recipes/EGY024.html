🍚 Roz Me'ammar (Creamy Clay-Pot Baked Rice)

Roz me'ammar / maamar — الأرز المعمر

Washed short-grain rice baked in a very hot oven with boiled milk, cream, ghee, allspice and cardamom until the liquid is absorbed and the top is golden.

50mTotal time
10mPrep
40mCook
6Servings
450Calories
MediumDifficulty

Rice is washed three or four times until the water runs clear, then baked in a buttered clay or glass dish with hot milk, cream and spices. A 260 C oven is used until the liquid starts to boil (5 to 10 minutes), then 200 C until the rice absorbs the liquid, about 20 minutes, with a 2 to 3 minute broil if the top is not golden.

Ingredients

Equipment: Clay or glass baking dish, Saucepan, Oven

  • 2 cups short rice
  • 3 cups full fat milk
  • 1 cup double cream or one can of cream
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • 1/2 teaspoon all spice
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom

Method

  1. Wash the rice 3 to 4 times until the water runs clear, then drain.
  2. Boil the milk, cream and spices, then take off the heat.
  3. Preheat the oven to 260 C with the rack on the lowest position. Brush the dish with ghee.
  4. Add the rice, salt and hot milk-cream to the dish.
  5. Bake at 260 C until the liquid starts to boil, 5 to 10 minutes. Lower to 200 C and bake until the rice absorbs the liquid (about 20 minutes, the page says in brackets).
  6. If the top is not golden, broil for 2 to 3 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: water runs clear; liquid starts to boil; liquid absorbed; top golden. Total time on the page is only given as parentheticals, so we sum them.
  • Home ovens rarely reach 260 C; use your maximum and watch closely (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Opening the oven too often.
  • Skipping the lowest rack, which colours the top before the rice is cooked.

Cultural Note

Sawsan Abu Farha says 'loaded rice' began as a peasant dish baked in a clay pot so the farmer's wife could do other chores, and that a sweet version came later.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh (our note).

Reheating: Reheat covered with a splash of milk.

Serve with: Molokhia
