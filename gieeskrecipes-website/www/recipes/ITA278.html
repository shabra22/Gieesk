🌰 Vellutata di Castagne Umbra

Vellutata di Castagne

A velvety Umbrian chestnut soup, chestnuts simmered with vegetables and blended completely smooth, finished with a swirl of cream and a scattering of crisp pancetta.

Umbria's mountainous terrain has long supported chestnut cultivation, and this smooth, comforting soup showcases the nut's naturally sweet, earthy character in a form entirely different from Tuscany's dense castagnaccio cake.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender or immersion blender, Small pan for pancetta

  • 500 g (1.1 lb) cooked, peeled chestnuts
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 1 L (4 1/4 cups) vegetable stock
  • 100 ml (scant 1/2 cup) heavy cream
  • 60 g (2 oz) pancetta, diced (optional)
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh thyme
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot and saute the onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
  2. Add the chestnuts and stock, and bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook for 20-25 minutes until the vegetables and chestnuts are very tender.
  4. Blend the soup until completely smooth, using an immersion blender or transferring to a standing blender.
  5. Return to the pot, stir in the cream, and gently reheat without boiling.
  6. Meanwhile, if using, cook the pancetta in a small pan until crisp.
  7. Season the soup to taste and serve hot, topped with crisp pancetta and fresh thyme.

Chef Tips

  • Pre-cooked, vacuum-packed chestnuts save considerable time over roasting and peeling your own.
  • Blend the soup thoroughly for the silkiest, most velvety texture.
  • The crisp pancetta topping provides a nice textural contrast to the smooth soup, though it can be omitted for a vegetarian version.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not blending thoroughly enough, leaving a grainy rather than silky-smooth texture.
  • Boiling the soup after adding the cream, which can cause it to separate.
  • Using low-quality chestnuts, which can taste bland or slightly bitter.

Health Benefits

  • Chestnuts provide fiber and complex carbohydrates, with less fat than most other nuts.

Cultural Note

Vellutata di castagne reflects Umbria's mountainous chestnut-growing tradition, a comforting autumn soup found throughout the region's rural trattorie.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop without boiling.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A medium-bodied white wine
