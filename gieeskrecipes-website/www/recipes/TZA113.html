🥥 Tanzanian Coconut Lentils

Dengu ya Nazi

Tanzanian Dengu ya Nazi — red lentils simmered in coconut milk with Zanzibar spices. The coastal version of lentil curry — richer and more fragrant than the plain version, and ready in 25 minutes.

Coconut lentil curry is the quick, rich, satisfying coastal dish — the lentils need no soaking, the coconut milk goes in at the end, and within 25 minutes there is a dish of extraordinary flavour on the table.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 250g red lentils — rinsed
  • 300ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, spices. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add lentils and water. Simmer 18—20 minutes until completely soft.
  5. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 5 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Red lentils dissolve into creaminess — this is correct.
  • Add coconut milk only after lentils are fully cooked.
  • Never boil coconut milk hard.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding coconut milk too early.
  • Under-cooking lentils.

Cultural Note

Coconut lentil curry is the quick coastal meal — ready faster than any other substantial dish, yet carrying the full flavour of the Zanzibar spice tradition.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently with splash of water.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004), Ugali (TZA001)
