🥛 Maize Milk

Maize milk

Young maize pounded, the juice squeezed out and boiled — a milk made from a cereal, and the only thing like it in the Ugandan record.

Maize milk is young maize pounded in a mortar, the juice squeezed out and then boiled. It is a Lugbara preparation and it is genuinely unusual: nothing else in the Ugandan repertoire extracts a liquid from a cereal this way and drinks it. The maize has to be young — the milky stage, before the kernels harden — which is a narrow window in the growing season. The boiling is what makes it keep and what thickens it slightly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar and pestle, Cloth or fine sieve, Saucepan

  • Young maize, at the milky stage

No quantities are documented

    Method

    1. Pound the young maize.
    2. Squeeze the juice out through a cloth or sieve.
    3. Boil the juice.
    4. Serve.

    Chef Tips

    • Young maize only, at the milky stage. Press a kernel and it should burst with a white liquid — that is the window, and it is short.
    • Pound rather than blend if you can. A blender shreds the husk material into the liquid and you cannot strain it all back out.
    • Squeeze it properly through cloth. What is left in the pulp is the drink.
    • Boil it. Raw cereal juice does not keep and the boiling also thickens it a little.
    • This belongs to a set of Lugbara drinks worth knowing: ebe'de or ibe'de is unsweetened tea, named after the scooping action because it is self-service; kuruku chai is lemongrass boiled in tea; anya i'di is millet porridge with groundnut or sesame paste; mengu i'di is cooked mango with the juice squeezed while cooling and mixed with sugar.
    • No quantities are documented for this in any source. Nothing here is estimated.
    • Like nearly all fresh-maize dishes, this exists because of a glut — it is what you do when the garden gives you more young maize than you can eat off the cob.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Using mature maize, which gives almost no juice.
    • Blending instead of pounding.
    • Not squeezing the pulp out properly.
    • Drinking it unboiled.
    • Expecting it to keep like dairy milk.

    Health Benefits

    • Young maize supplies fibre, folate, thiamine and the carotenoids lutein and zeaxanthin.
    • Dairy-free and naturally free of gluten.
    • No added sugar or fat.
    • Uses maize at its most nutritious stage, before the starch sets.

    Cultural Note

    Squeezing a drink out of young maize is the sort of thing that happens where a crop arrives all at once and has to be used. It also puts the Lugbara kitchen alongside the maize-milk traditions of the Americas without any contact between them — the same plant suggesting the same idea twice.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

    Reheating: Warm gently.

    Serve with: Mukati, Akarafi, Kuruku Chai
