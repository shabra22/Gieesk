🍅 Koftet Roz (Rice Meatballs in Tomato Sauce)

Kofta bil-roz — كفتة أرز

Beef mince bound with ground rice, grated onion and a lot of parsley, coriander and dill, fried until crisp and simmered in a garlicky tomato sauce.

1h 15mTotal time
30mPrep
45mCook
4Servings
520Calories
MediumDifficulty

A herby beef mixture with ground rice is rolled into balls of about 50g in oiled hands and deep-fried in sunflower oil until golden and crisp; the oil is ready when a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. The balls then simmer in a garlic-tomato passata sauce for 15 to 20 minutes until the sauce has thickened slightly and the meatballs are cooked through. Served with white rice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Deep pan for frying, Saucepan, Slotted spoon

  • 1 large onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 50g parsley, finely chopped
  • 50g coriander, finely chopped
  • 40g dill, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp chilli powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp Arabic mixed spice, such as Arabic masala or baharat (gluten-free, if needed)
  • 500g beef mince
  • 100g ground rice
  • 500ml sunflower oil, plus extra for moulding the meatballs and 2 tbsp for frying
  • cooked white rice, to serve

Sauce

  • 1 large garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 500ml tomato passata

Method

  1. Mix the onion, garlic, herbs, chilli, spice, beef and ground rice well, then roll into balls of about 50g, using oiled hands.
  2. Heat the sunflower oil until a cube of bread dropped in browns in 30 seconds. Deep-fry the balls in batches for a few minutes until crisp and a good golden colour. Drain.
  3. Sauce: in 2 tablespoons of oil cook the garlic and tomato purée for a couple of minutes, add the passata and 500ml of water, and bring to the boil.
  4. Add the meatballs and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, until the sauce has thickened slightly and the koftas are cooked through.
  5. Serve over white rice.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: bread cube browns in 30 seconds; balls crisp and golden; sauce slightly thickened. The page says the cooked koftet roz freeze for 2 months.
  • The page lists the frying oil at 500ml; a smaller pot can use less (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Oil that is not hot enough, so the balls soak it up and break apart.
  • Crowding the pan.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate in the sauce up to 3 days (our estimate); the page says the koftas freeze for 2 months.

Reheating: Warm in the sauce over low heat.

Serve with: Rice with vermicelli, Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
