🐟 Tanzanian Dagaa Stew

Mchuzi wa Dagaa

Tanzanian Mchuzi wa Dagaa — dried dagaa (lake sardines) cooked in a spiced tomato-onion sauce. The most affordable, nutritious protein stew of Lake Victoria — eaten with ugali by millions of Tanzanians daily.

Dagaa stew is the everyday protein of Tanzania's lake regions — dried lake sardines in a simple tomato sauce, reconstituted into a nutritious stew. The meal most Tanzanian families around Lake Victoria eat every day.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium pot

  • 200g dried dagaa — rinsed and picked over
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Rinse dagaa thoroughly in cold water. Pick out any debris.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  4. Add dagaa and water.
  5. Simmer covered 12—15 minutes until dagaa are soft and well flavoured.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Rinse dagaa thoroughly — can be sandy.
  • Do not over-stir — dagaa break apart.
  • Stew should be thick, not watery.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not rinsing — sandy stew.
  • Over-stirring.
  • Too much water.

The Science

Dried dagaa absorb water during simmering — reconstituting to twice their dried volume. Rehydration also softens bones which become edible after simmering, providing significant calcium.

Health Benefits

  • Very high protein.
  • Calcium from reconstituted bones.
  • Rich in omega-3.
  • One of Africa's most nutritionally complete affordable foods.

Cultural Note

Dagaa stew is the food security of Lake Victoria's communities — the affordable protein that ensures families are nourished even in difficult times.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Sukuma Wiki, Kachumbari
