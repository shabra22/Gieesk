🌽 Creamy Polenta

Polenta Cremosa

Creamy Polenta takes the basic cornmeal porridge and enriches it with butter, cream, and Parmigiano-Reggiano for a luxuriously smooth, restaurant-style side that pairs beautifully with rich braises.

This is polenta dressed for dinner — the same slow-cooked cornmeal base finished generously with dairy for a silky, almost risotto-like texture, popular under braised short ribs, osso buco, and hearty mushroom ragùs in restaurants across Italy and beyond.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy-bottomed pot, Whisk, Wooden spoon

  • 1.2 L (5 cups) whole milk
  • 480 ml (2 cups) water
  • 250 g (1⅓ cups) coarse polenta cornmeal
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 60 g (4 tbsp) butter
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) heavy cream
  • 80 g (1 cup) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Combine the milk, water, and salt in a heavy-bottomed pot and bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Slowly whisk in the polenta in a thin stream to prevent lumps.
  3. Switch to a wooden spoon once thickened and cook over low heat, stirring frequently, for 35–40 minutes until very smooth and tender.
  4. Stir in the butter and cream, mixing until fully incorporated.
  5. Fold in the Parmigiano-Reggiano and season generously with black pepper.
  6. Taste and adjust salt as needed — the cheese adds significant saltiness.
  7. Serve immediately while hot and pourable, spooned generously under braised meats or mushrooms.

Chef Tips

  • Using milk instead of just water or stock is what gives this version its signature creaminess.
  • Add the cream and cheese off the heat or at very low heat to prevent the dairy from separating.
  • If it thickens too much before serving, loosen with a splash of warm milk.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding cheese over high heat, which can cause it to seize or become grainy.
  • Not stirring frequently enough with milk-based polenta, which scorches more easily than water-based versions.
  • Making it too far ahead — creamy polenta is best served immediately, as it firms up as it cools.

Cultural Note

Creamy, enriched polenta is a favorite in fine dining interpretations of Northern Italian cuisine, elevating a historically humble peasant dish into a celebrated component of modern Italian menus.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days — it will firm considerably; loosen with milk when reheating.

Reheating: Reheat gently over low heat with added milk or cream, stirring until smooth again.

Serve with: Osso Buco, Braised short ribs, Mushroom ragù, Braised sausages
