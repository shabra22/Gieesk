🍌 Banane malaxée (Topsi Banana)

Banane malaxée / topsi banana

A restaurant’s large-batch mashed green banana with peanuts and smoked fish, cooked about 40 minutes in red palm oil and served lukewarm or cold.

Ingredients

Equipment: Very large pot with lid, Blender

  • 5 kg green bananas
  • 4 cups peanuts (dyed/red-skinned preferred)
  • 1/2 L red palm oil
  • 6 smoked fish (cod, mackerel, hake…)
  • 5 smoked fish (mbounga, maloulou, mouloulou)
  • Well-cooked meat, to preference
  • 2 sachets of spices
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 200 g ginger
  • Chilli (optional)
  • Salt

Method

  1. Peel the bananas. Slit the skin and rub with salt or red oil to keep the sap off your hands.
  2. Sort the peanuts (soak, roast, or crush directly). Behead, detail and split the fish and rinse the smoked fish. Blend the ginger, garlic, onion and chilli.
  3. Fill the pot to about three-quarters and warm the red oil slightly.
  4. Add the bananas, fish, peanuts and salt and stir. Add water to cover the bananas.
  5. Cover and cook 40 minutes — 20 on high heat, then on medium. Serve lukewarm or cold.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cues are the times (20 high, then medium to 40 minutes).
  • The page’s tip for the hands: rub with salt or red oil.
  • The meat and the blended aromatics are listed but not placed in the steps.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overfilling the pot.
  • Leaving out the aromatics because the steps do not mention them.
  • Skipping the water to cover the bananas.
