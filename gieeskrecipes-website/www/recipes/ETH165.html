🫘 Ethiopian Lentil Fritters

ምስር ቆሎ — Misir Fried Balls

Ethiopian Lentil Fritters — cooked red lentils mashed with berbere, onion, and coriander, formed into small balls and deep-fried until golden. The Ethiopian falafel — berbere-spiced, crispy outside, tender within, eaten with silsi or green chilli paste.

Ethiopian lentil fritters are the berbere-spiced answer to falafel — the red lentils, already full of flavour from berbere and onion, are formed into small balls and deep-fried. Less prevalent than sambusa but beloved by those who know them.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep pan, Thermometer

  • 1.5 cups red lentils — cooked and very dry
  • 1 onion — very finely diced and fried golden
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ¼ cup coriander
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour (binder)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Ensure lentils are very dry — excess moisture causes fritters to break apart.
  2. Mash lentils thoroughly with all ingredients except oil.
  3. Add chickpea flour to bind. Mix well.
  4. Form into small balls or patties (4cm).
  5. Refrigerate 20 minutes — firms them up.
  6. Heat oil to 175°C. Fry in batches 3—4 minutes until golden.
  7. Drain.

Chef Tips

  • Completely dry lentils are essential — wet lentils produce fritters that break apart.
  • Refrigerating before frying is the key step for holding their shape.
  • Chickpea flour is a better binder than wheat flour — stays crispy.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet lentils — fall apart in oil.
  • Skipping refrigeration.
  • Too large — outside burns before inside cooks.

The Science

Chickpea flour contains significantly more protein than wheat flour — when used as a binder, the chickpea proteins denature and coagulate during frying at higher temperatures than wheat gluten, producing a more stable structure. This is why chickpea-bound fritters are more cohesive than wheat-bound ones.

Cultural Note

Lentil fritters are the fasting street food — during the 200+ annual fasting days, they provide fried, satisfying protein without any animal products. A street vendor frying lentil balls during Lent is doing a community service.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately. Reheat in oven. Freeze uncooked.

Reheating: Oven at 190°C.

Serve with: With Silsi (ETH146), With Green Chilli Paste (ETH149), As a street snack
