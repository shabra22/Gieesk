🍵 Ethiopian Beef Soup

ሲሮ ወጥ / የሥጋ ሾርባ — Yesiga Shorba

Ethiopian Yesiga Shorba — beef bones and meat simmered for 90 minutes with onion, garlic, ginger, and Ethiopian spices into a deep, restorative broth. The healing soup of Ethiopia — given to the sick, the recovering, and the new mother.

Ethiopian beef soup is the simplest expression of the country's deeply nourishing cooking philosophy — good bones, water, time, and a few spices produce a soup of genuine healing power. Given to new mothers, to the sick, and drunk from a cup on cold mornings in the highlands.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 800g beef bones (with some meat)
  • 2 litres water
  • 1 onion — halved
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger — sliced
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Fresh coriander to garnish
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. Place bones and meat in pot. Cover with cold water. Bring to a boil.
  2. Skim foam thoroughly — 5 minutes of skimming.
  3. Add onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, pepper, and coriander.
  4. Simmer on lowest heat for 80—90 minutes.
  5. Strain. Return broth to pot.
  6. Remove meat from bones. Return meat to broth.
  7. Season. Garnish with coriander. Serve with lime.

Chef Tips

  • Skim foam thoroughly — determines the clarity and cleanliness of the broth.
  • The lowest possible simmer produces the clearest broth.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not skimming foam.
  • Boiling instead of simmering — cloudy result.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Ethiopian beef soup is the nourishment of care — the food that Ethiopian families make when someone needs healing. The slow simmering of bones is an act of patience and love.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Bring to boil before serving.

Serve with: As a standalone healing drink, With injera for a light meal
