🍿 Ethiopian Popcorn

ፈርፋር — Ferfar (Popcorn)

Ethiopian Popcorn — popping corn dry-popped or with minimal oil in a covered pan, seasoned with salt or a pinch of berbere. An integral part of the Ethiopian coffee ceremony — popcorn is always served alongside coffee, a tradition unique to Ethiopia.

Ethiopian coffee ceremony popcorn is one of the most charming food traditions in the world — at every Ethiopian coffee ceremony, alongside the incense and the coffee, popcorn is popped and served. It is the universal snack of Ethiopian social life.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot with lid

  • ½ cup popping corn
  • 1 tsp oil (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt

Optional: pinch of berbere

    Method

    1. Heat a heavy pot over medium-high heat.
    2. Add oil if using — or pop dry.
    3. Add 3 kernels. When they pop, add remaining corn.
    4. Cover. Shake pan continuously.
    5. Pop 3—4 minutes until popping slows to 2—3 seconds between pops.
    6. Season immediately with salt (and berbere if using).

    Chef Tips

    • Shake the pan continuously — prevents burning.
    • Remove from heat when popping slows — lingering causes burnt corn.
    • Berbere popcorn is a modern Ethiopian café favourite.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not shaking — burns on the bottom.
    • Waiting too long — burnt.

    The Science

    Popcorn kernels contain a water droplet within a hard starch endosperm. At 180°C, the water vaporises rapidly, building pressure until the hull ruptures and the starch gelatinises and expands up to 40x the original volume.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian popcorn at the coffee ceremony is one of the most distinctive food traditions in the world — the unique combination of incense smoke, coffee being roasted and brewed, and popcorn being popped all simultaneously is the multi-sensory experience of Ethiopian hospitality. No other culture serves popcorn at its most important social ritual.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: During the Ethiopian coffee ceremony, At cinemas, As a social snack
