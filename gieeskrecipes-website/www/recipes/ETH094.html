🌶️ Ethiopian Spiced Chilli Paste

አዋዜ — Awaze

Ethiopian Awaze — berbere spice blended with tej (Ethiopian honey wine) or wine and kibbeh into a thick, smooth, intensely spiced paste. Ethiopia's primary table condiment and marinade — the hot sauce used on kitfo, gored gored, tibs, and as a dipping sauce.

Awaze is what happens when berbere meets a liquid — the traditional liquid is tej (Ethiopian honey wine), but red wine, beer, or water work. The result is a smooth, pourable hot sauce that is the everyday table condiment of Ethiopian restaurants and homes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Spoon

  • 4 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp tej (Ethiopian honey wine) — or red wine or water
  • 1 tbsp kibbeh — melted
  • 1 tsp awaze (existing batch) — as starter (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Combine berbere, tej (or wine), and kibbeh.
  2. Mix to a smooth, thick paste.
  3. Add salt to taste.
  4. Rest 30 minutes — the flavours meld.
  5. Use as a condiment, marinade, or dipping sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Tej is the traditional liquid — it adds a honey-wine complexity.
  • Adjust consistency with more or less liquid.
  • Awaze improves over time — make it a day ahead.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too thick — add more tej.
  • Too thin — add more berbere.

The Science

The liquid in awaze extracts the water-soluble compounds from berbere (the organic acids, some water-soluble colour compounds) while the kibbeh extracts the fat-soluble compounds (carotenoids, capsaicin). This dual extraction produces a more complete flavour expression than either liquid or fat alone.

Cultural Note

Awaze is Ethiopia's answer to hot sauce — but where commercial hot sauces are one-dimensional, awaze is complex. The combination of berbere's 15+ spice compounds with the honey sweetness of tej and the richness of kibbeh produces a condiment of extraordinary depth.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On kitfo (ETH029), On tibs (ETH022), As a marinade for fish and meat, As a table condiment for any Ethiopian dish
