🍬 Tanzanian Peanut Brittle

Simsim na Karanga

Tanzanian Simsim na Karanga — peanuts and sesame seeds bound together in caramelised sugar into a hard, brittle candy. Sold in small squares at every market across Tanzania — the most universally eaten Tanzanian sweet.

Peanut and sesame brittle is Tanzania's most widely sold market sweet — the caramelised sugar binding of roasted peanuts and sesame seeds produces a candy that is simultaneously nutty, sweet, and satisfyingly crunchy. Sold in small squares wrapped in newspaper by vendors at every market.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan, Greased baking sheet or marble slab

  • 1 cup raw groundnuts — roasted
  • ½ cup sesame seeds — toasted
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan until golden. Roast groundnuts until fragrant.
  2. Combine sugar and water in a heavy saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Cook without stirring until the sugar reaches 150°C (hard crack stage) — deep amber.
  4. Working quickly, stir in roasted groundnuts, sesame, and salt.
  5. Pour immediately onto a greased surface.
  6. Spread thin with a greased spatula.
  7. Mark into squares while still warm. Break when cool.

Chef Tips

  • The sugar must reach hard crack stage — a drop in cold water should snap, not bend.
  • Work very fast once nuts go in — the sugar sets quickly.
  • Never stir the sugar while it is cooking — causes crystallisation.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking sugar — soft brittle.
  • Over-cooking sugar — bitter.
  • Stirring the cooking sugar.

The Science

See TZA127 for candy sugar science. Hard crack stage (150—155°C) means virtually all water has evaporated from the sugar solution. At this temperature, the liquid sugar is supersaturated — when combined with the nuts and poured out, it crystallises into a rigid glass-like structure as it cools.

Cultural Note

Peanut and sesame brittle is Tanzania's most democratic sweet — bought by every Tanzanian, at every market, made by every confectioner in exactly the same way. It is the sweet that crosses every social boundary in the country.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container 3 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a standalone sweet, With chai
