Insalata di Rinforzo

A tangy Neapolitan Christmas cauliflower salad tossed with olives, capers, anchovies, and pickled vegetables, its name meaning 'reinforcement' since it's kept on the table to be replenished throughout the holidays.

Insalata di rinforzo is designed to be practical as much as delicious, its vinegar-based dressing allowing it to keep well over several days, ready to be 'reinforced' with fresh cauliflower and toppings as the Neapolitan holiday season stretches on.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot for boiling, Large mixing bowl

  • 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 100 g (½ cup) black olives, pitted
  • 3 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 4 anchovy fillets, chopped
  • 80 g (½ cup) mixed pickled vegetables (giardiniera), chopped
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 100 ml (scant ½ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the cauliflower florets until just tender, about 6–8 minutes; do not overcook, as they should retain some bite.
  2. Drain well and let cool.
  3. Combine the cauliflower with the olives, capers, anchovies, and pickled vegetables in a large bowl.
  4. Whisk together the vinegar and olive oil, season, and pour over the salad.
  5. Toss well and let sit for at least a few hours, ideally overnight, before serving to let the flavors meld.
  6. Toss again before serving and adjust seasoning to taste.

Chef Tips

  • Don't overcook the cauliflower — it should retain a slight bite rather than becoming soft and mushy.
  • This salad genuinely improves with time, so making it a day ahead is traditional and recommended.
  • The vinegar-forward dressing is what allows this dish to keep well over the extended Neapolitan holiday period.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the cauliflower, resulting in a mushy rather than pleasantly firm texture.
  • Serving immediately without letting the flavors rest and develop.
  • Being too sparing with the vinegar, which is essential both for flavor and for how well the dish keeps.

Cultural Note

Insalata di rinforzo is a fixture of the Neapolitan Christmas table, its practical, long-keeping nature reflected directly in its name, meaning 'reinforcement salad.'

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 5 days; flavor improves over the first couple of days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: As part of a Neapolitan Christmas antipasto spread
