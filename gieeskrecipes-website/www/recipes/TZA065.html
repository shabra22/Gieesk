🐙 Tanzanian Octopus Curry

Mchuzi wa Pweza

Tanzanian Mchuzi wa Pweza — Zanzibar octopus curry. Octopus slow-cooked in a richly spiced coconut milk and tomato sauce until completely tender. One of Zanzibar's most celebrated dishes — caught by Zanzibari women in the tidal flats.

Zanzibar octopus curry is one of the most remarkable dishes in East African cooking — the octopus is caught by women in the tidal flats using ancient techniques, and the curry is deeply aromatic. The trick is tenderising before the curry.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Zanzibar — the octopus harvest is one of Zanzibar's most distinctive traditions

Octopus fishing in Zanzibar is done predominantly by women — wading through tidal flats at low tide, spearing octopus with pointed sticks. One of the most ancient and visually striking fishing traditions in the world. The octopus goes directly into mchuzi wa pweza.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1 kg fresh octopus — cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 300ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1—2 red chillies
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste

Method

  1. Tenderise octopus: freeze overnight then thaw, or simmer in plain water 20 minutes. Drain.
  2. Cook onions until deeply golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
  4. Add octopus and tamarind. Simmer covered 25—30 minutes.
  5. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 10 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Tenderising is non-negotiable.
  • Freeze-thaw is the most reliable tenderising method.
  • Tamarind adds the essential tart note.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping tenderising — rubbery octopus.
  • Overcooking in curry — octopus toughens again above 85°C.

The Science

Octopus collagen converts to gelatin with slow cooking, but muscle fibres re-toughen if overcooked. Freezing ruptures cell walls, accelerating tenderisation.

Cultural Note

Mchuzi wa Pweza tells the story of Zanzibar's women — the skilled octopus catchers who have provided this dish to the island for centuries.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Very gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
