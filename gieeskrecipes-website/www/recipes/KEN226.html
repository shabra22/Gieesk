🍟 Kenyan Loaded Chips Masala

Chips Masala Zilizojaa

Kenyan Loaded Chips Masala — masala fries piled high with spiced minced beef, kachumbari, garlic mayo, cheese, and pili pili. Nairobi's most indulgent street food upgrade — completely over the top and completely irresistible.

Loaded chips masala is the peak of Nairobi's modern street food culture — the masala fries concept taken to its absolute maximum. Piled with spiced beef, fresh kachumbari, two sauces, and melted cheese, it is a meal that demands to be eaten in company and discussed for days afterwards.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Small pan for beef, Oven or grill for cheese

    Masala fries base (see KEN225)

    • 600g potatoes — chipped and fried with masala spices

    Beef topping

    • 200g minced beef
    • 1 small onion — finely diced
    • 1 tsp curry powder
    • 1 tsp paprika
    • 1 garlic clove — minced
    • 1 tsp salt

    Toppings

    • 3 tbsp kachumbari
    • 2 tbsp garlic mayo
    • 2 tbsp pili pili sauce
    • 4 tbsp grated cheese
    • 2 tbsp fresh coriander

    Method

    1. Cook minced beef with onion, garlic, curry powder, paprika, and salt until browned and dry.
    2. Make masala fries as per KEN225.
    3. Place hot fries in a serving bowl.
    4. Top with spiced minced beef.
    5. Scatter cheese over beef. Grill briefly or microwave until cheese melts.
    6. Add kachumbari, drizzle garlic mayo and pili pili sauce.
    7. Scatter fresh coriander.
    8. Serve immediately — this does not wait.

    Chef Tips

    • Everything must be hot when assembled — cold elements make the cheese solidify.
    • The kachumbari goes on after the cheese so it stays fresh.
    • Serve with napkins — this is gloriously messy.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Assembling cold fries — everything collapses.
    • Too much topping — the fries must still be accessible.

    The Science

    The cheese melt over the hot beef creates a sauce-like consistency — the fat in the cheese liquefies and coats the beef and fries beneath. Adding the fresh kachumbari after the cheese preserves its cold, acidic contrast against the hot, rich toppings.

    Loaded chips masala represents Nairobi's confident, creative street food culture — taking something already beloved and piling it higher, making it richer, and making it more unapologetically indulgent.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Must be eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: Cold Soda, Fresh Juice, As a complete meal
