🫘 Lenticchie di Castelluccio in Umido

Lenticchie di Castelluccio

Umbria's prized small, thin-skinned lentils from the high plains of Castelluccio, simmered simply with vegetables until tender — no soaking required and celebrated for holding their shape perfectly.

Grown at high altitude on the Piano Grande beneath the Sibillini Mountains, Castelluccio lentils are prized above other Italian varieties for their thin skin and consistent size, meaning they cook evenly and hold their shape rather than turning to mush.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 300 g (1½ cups) Lenticchie di Castelluccio (or other small lentils)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils; Castelluccio lentils do not require soaking.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a large pot and sauté the onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Add the lentils, crushed tomatoes, bay leaf, and enough water to cover generously.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 25–30 minutes, until the lentils are tender but still hold their shape, adding more water if needed.
  6. Season to taste and serve warm, drizzled with a little extra olive oil.

Chef Tips

  • Genuine Castelluccio lentils are prized specifically for holding their shape well through cooking — avoid overcooking, which can still turn even this variety mushy.
  • These lentils are traditionally served alongside sausages, especially around New Year, symbolizing prosperity.
  • A finishing drizzle of good olive oil at the table brings out the lentils' earthy flavor.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking, even though these lentils are more forgiving than most, which can still eventually turn them mushy.
  • Adding salt too early, which can toughen the skins of the lentils before they've softened.
  • Using a different, larger lentil variety without adjusting cooking time, since Castelluccio lentils cook faster due to their thin skin.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Lenticchie di Castelluccio hold a protected geographic status, grown on the high plains beneath Umbria's Sibillini Mountains and prized as among Italy's finest lentils.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 4 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding water if needed.

Serve with: Grilled sausages, Cotechino, Crusty bread
