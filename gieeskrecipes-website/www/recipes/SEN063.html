🌿 Fonio Salad with Lemon and Mint

Salade de fonio

Steamed fonio dressed with a lemon-oil vinaigrette and parsley, mint, cucumber and plum tomatoes — in Thiam’s own first-person account of finding fonio in Senegal.

35 minTotal time
15 minPrep
20 minCook
4Servings
520Calories
EasyDifficulty

From the face2faceafrica article ‘A Chef’s Journey: In Search of Fonio in Senegal’, byline Pierre Thiam, 1 April 2014. A cup of fonio is washed in cold running water, drained and steamed in a cheesecloth-lined steamer about 15 minutes, fluffed, sprinkled with salted water and steamed again until tender; the page also offers a microwave method, in a bowl with enough water to cover, 6–8 minutes. The vinaigrette is the juice of two lemons, salt and pepper with a cup of oil whisked in slowly to emulsify. The fonio is tossed with parsley, mint, cucumber and tomatoes and dressed enough to coat the grains.

Ingredients

Equipment: Steamer lined with cheesecloth, Whisk, Large bowl

  • 1 cup fonio
  • 1 tbsp salt dissolved in 1/4 cup water
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 cup peanut or canola oil (the method says ‘olive oil’ — see sources)
  • 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch mint, finely chopped
  • 1 cucumber, peeled and diced
  • 2 plum tomatoes, diced

Method

  1. Wash the fonio with cold running water and drain well. Place it in the top of a steamer lined with cheesecloth and steam about 15 minutes. Fluff, sprinkle with the salted water and steam again until the grains are tender. Alternatively microwave it in a bowl with enough water to cover, until tender, 6–8 minutes.
  2. Whisk the lemon juice, salt and pepper. Slowly whisk in the oil to emulsify.
  3. Combine the cooked fonio with the parsley, mint, cucumber and tomatoes. Add enough dressing to coat the grains well, toss and serve.

Chef Tips

  • ‘Steam again until the grains are tender’ is the doneness cue; the first steam is a time (about 15 minutes).
  • Add enough dressing ‘to coat the grains well’ — no more.
  • The page’s salted water is 1 tbsp in 1/4 cup.
  • The oil mismatch in the page (peanut or canola in the list, olive in the method) is recorded; use whichever you have.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overdressing.
  • Under-steaming the fonio.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 1 day refrigerated.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Grilled meats or fish
