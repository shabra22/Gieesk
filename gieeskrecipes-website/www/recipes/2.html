🍝 Truffle Carbonara

Roman classic elevated with black truffle. Silky egg and pecorino sauce, no cream ever. A lesson in restraint.

★ 4.8 (528 reviews)
25 minTotal time
5 minPrep
20 minCook
4Servings
680Calories
EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g guanciale
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 100g pecorino romano
  • Black truffle shavings
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Render guanciale in a cold pan until crispy. Remove from heat.
  3. Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, and pecorino with plenty of black pepper.
  4. Reserve 1 cup pasta water. Drain pasta and add to pan with guanciale.
  5. Off heat, pour egg mixture over pasta. Toss vigorously, adding pasta water to achieve silky sauce.
  6. Finish with truffle shavings and extra pecorino.
