🍌 Tanzanian Green Banana and Beans

Ndizi na Maharage

Tanzanian Ndizi na Maharage — green cooking bananas and beans simmered together in a spiced tomato sauce. The complete plant-based protein meal of Tanzania's highland regions — the banana provides starch, the beans provide protein, together they provide everything.

Green banana and beans is one of Tanzania's most nutritionally complete plant-based meals — the combination of starchy banana and protein-rich beans forms a naturally complete food. In the Kilimanjaro highlands where this dish originates, it has sustained communities for centuries.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 4 large green cooking bananas — peeled and halved
  • 200g kidney beans — soaked and cooked until tender
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1.5 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Peel bananas wearing gloves. Halve and submerge in salted water.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices, tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  3. Add bananas and water. Simmer covered 20—25 minutes until nearly tender.
  4. Add cooked beans. Simmer 10 more minutes.
  5. The sauce should be thick and coating.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Wear gloves when peeling bananas — the latex sap stains permanently.
  • Bananas take longer than beans — add them first.
  • The starch from the bananas thickens the sauce naturally.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not wearing gloves.
  • Adding beans and bananas simultaneously.

The Science

Green banana resistant starch dissolves partially into the cooking liquid during simmering — naturally thickening the sauce while also providing prebiotic fibre that feeds beneficial gut bacteria.

Health Benefits

  • Complete protein from banana-bean combination.
  • High resistant starch — prebiotic.
  • Rich in potassium, iron, and fibre.

Cultural Note

Green banana and beans is the Kilimanjaro highlands' most complete food — the combination that sustained the Chagga communities who farmed the fertile slopes of Africa's highest mountain for centuries.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: As a complete meal alone, With sukuma wiki, With meat stew
