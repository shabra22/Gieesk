🧇 Kenyan Waffles with Mango

Waffles na Embe ya Kenya

Kenyan Waffles with Mango — crispy Belgian-style waffles topped with fresh mango slices, passion fruit pulp, coconut whipped cream, and a drizzle of Kenyan honey. The weekend hotel brunch treat that celebrates what Kenya does best.

Waffles have taken root in Nairobi's hotel brunch culture — and the Kenyan version, topped with local mango and passion fruit rather than imported maple syrup, is genuinely superior to the standard presentation.

Ingredients

Equipment: Waffle iron, Electric beater for egg whites, Mixing bowls

    Waffle Batter

    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 2 tsp baking powder
    • 1 tbsp sugar
    • ½ tsp salt
    • 2 eggs — separated
    • 1¾ cups whole milk
    • ½ cup vegetable oil
    • 1 tsp vanilla

    Toppings

    • 2 large ripe mangoes — sliced
    • 3 passion fruits — pulp
    • 200ml coconut cream — whipped with 1 tbsp honey
    • 3 tbsp Kenyan honey
    • Fresh mint

    Method

    1. Separate eggs. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks.
    2. Mix egg yolks, milk, oil, and vanilla.
    3. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
    4. Mix wet into dry until just combined.
    5. Fold egg whites into batter — do not deflate.
    6. Heat and grease waffle iron.
    7. Cook waffles until golden and crispy — follow manufacturer timing.
    8. Whip coconut cream with honey to soft peaks.
    9. Top waffles with coconut cream, mango, passion fruit, and honey drizzle.

    Chef Tips

    • Folding beaten egg whites into the batter is the secret to crispy, light waffles.
    • Do not open the waffle iron early — it tears the waffle.
    • Serve immediately — waffles lose crispiness quickly.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Opening the iron too early.
    • Not folding egg whites — dense waffles.

    The Science

    The whipped egg whites in the batter create a foam structure that expands in the hot waffle iron — creating the characteristic light interior. The oil in the batter prevents the waffle from sticking and contributes to the crispy exterior by limiting steam retention on the surface.

    Cultural Note

    Kenyan waffles with mango represent the modernisation of the Kenyan hotel breakfast — a globally familiar format made extraordinary by the quality of Kenya's tropical fruit and the depth of its wild honey.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Toast briefly to re-crisp.

    Serve with: Fresh Tropical Juice, Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249)
