🐟 Frittelle di Baccalà

Frittelle di Baccalà

Crisp, golden salt cod fritters, a beloved Neapolitan street food and Christmas Eve tradition, the flaked fish bound simply in a light batter and fried until puffed.

These fritters are a fixture of Naples' Christmas Eve table, when tradition calls for a meatless feast built around the sea, and their crisp, savory exterior giving way to tender, flaky cod inside makes them equally popular as an everyday street food.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl for soaking, Deep frying pan

  • 800 g (1.75 lb) dried salt cod
  • 250 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour
  • 10 g (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 300 ml (1¼ cups) warm water
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Method

  1. Soak the salt cod in cold water for 24-48 hours, changing the water several times to remove excess salt.
  2. Drain, remove any skin and bones, and flake the fish into small pieces.
  3. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit for 5 minutes.
  4. Whisk the flour into the yeast mixture to form a smooth, thick batter, and let rest for 30 minutes until slightly bubbly.
  5. Fold the flaked cod, parsley, and black pepper into the batter.
  6. Heat vegetable oil to 180C (350F).
  7. Drop spoonfuls of the batter into the hot oil and fry in batches until deeply golden and puffed, about 3-4 minutes.
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with lemon wedges.

Chef Tips

  • Soaking the cod thoroughly and changing the water several times is essential to remove excess salt.
  • Letting the batter rest allows the yeast to create a lighter, airier fritter.
  • These are best served immediately while still hot and crisp.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not soaking the cod long enough, resulting in an overly salty fritter.
  • Skipping the batter's resting time, missing the light, airy texture the yeast provides.
  • Frying at too low a temperature, resulting in greasy rather than crisp fritters.

Cultural Note

Frittelle di baccalà are a beloved fixture of Naples' Christmas Eve feast, when tradition calls for a meatless meal built entirely around seafood.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh; refrigerate leftovers for up to 1 day.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot oven to help re-crisp; avoid the microwave.

Serve with: Lemon wedges, As part of a Christmas Eve seafood spread
