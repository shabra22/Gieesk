🍆 Involtini di Melanzane

Involtini di Melanzane

Thin slices of eggplant rolled around a filling of ricotta, Parmigiano, and herbs, baked in tomato sauce until tender — a lighter, roll-shaped cousin of eggplant parmigiana.

Salting the eggplant slices before cooking draws out excess moisture and bitterness, resulting in slices pliable enough to roll neatly around a creamy ricotta filling, which then bakes gently in tomato sauce until everything melds together.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking sheet, Grill pan or oven for eggplant, Baking dish

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced lengthwise about ½cm thick
  • Salt, for drawing out moisture
  • 300 g (1¼ cups) ricotta, drained
  • 60 g (¾ cup) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for topping
  • 1 egg
  • Fresh basil, chopped
  • 500 g (1 lb) tomato passata or crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 150 g (1½ cups) shredded mozzarella
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant slices generously and let them sit in a colander for 20 minutes to draw out bitterness, then pat dry.
  2. Brush the slices lightly with olive oil and grill or roast until softened and lightly golden on both sides, then set aside to cool.
  3. Combine the ricotta, Parmigiano, egg, basil, salt, and pepper into a smooth filling.
  4. Warm a little olive oil in a saucepan, sauté the garlic briefly, then add the passata and simmer for 10 minutes with a pinch of salt to make a simple sauce.
  5. Spread a thin layer of the tomato sauce over the base of a baking dish.
  6. Place a spoonful of ricotta filling at one end of each eggplant slice and roll up into a neat cylinder.
  7. Arrange the rolls seam-side down in the baking dish and cover with the remaining tomato sauce.
  8. Scatter mozzarella and a little extra Parmigiano over the top and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Chef Tips

  • Salting the eggplant is not optional — it removes bitterness and prevents the slices from being watery once baked.
  • Slice the eggplant lengthwise and evenly so each piece is easy to roll neatly around the filling.
  • Let the rolls rest for a few minutes after baking before serving, so they hold together better when plated.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the salting step, resulting in bitter, watery eggplant.
  • Slicing the eggplant too thick, making it difficult to roll neatly around the filling.
  • Overfilling the rolls, which causes the filling to spill out during baking.

Cultural Note

Involtini di melanzane is a beloved Sicilian dish, reflecting the island's deep affection for eggplant across countless regional preparations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a covered dish in the oven until warmed through.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A simple green salad
