🥜 Caramel d’arachide (Caramelised Peanuts)

Arachides caramélisées

Equal glasses of unskinned peanuts and sugar with half a glass of water: sugar dissolved on low heat, peanuts added on medium heat until thick and viscous, then stirred until dry.

p. 50 of the October 2023 recipe book: 1 glass unskinned peanuts, 1 glass sugar, ½ glass water (glass size not printed). Sugar dissolves in the water on low heat; the peanuts are added; it cooks on medium heat, stirring from time to time; when thick and viscous it is stirred continuously until it ‘dries’; poured into a dish and cooled. The peanuts end up coated with a light layer of pink caramel, the colour coming from the skins. The page says quantities are proportional for all the ingredients, and that to make the grains stick together the sugar should be increased and left to melt after caramelisation. Times are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot, Spoon, Dish

  • 1 glass unskinned peanuts
  • 1 glass sugar
  • 1/2 glass water (glass size not printed)

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the water on low heat.
  2. Add the peanuts and cook on medium heat, stirring from time to time.
  3. When the mixture is thick and viscous, stir continuously until it is dry.
  4. Pour into a dish and cool.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cues: ‘thick and viscous’, then ‘dry’, with a light pink caramel coating.
  • The pink colour comes from the peanut skins, per the page.
  • No times are printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking on high heat.
  • Stopping before the sugar dries.
  • Using peeled peanuts and expecting the pink colour.
