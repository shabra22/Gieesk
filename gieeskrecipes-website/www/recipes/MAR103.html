🥩 Express Khlii (Mqila Slaouia)

Khlii / mqila — quick preserved meat confit (خليع)

Strips of lamb or beef marinated overnight with suet, garlic, coriander and cumin, then cooked slowly in a covered pan until darkened and dried a bit and all the fat has melted.

15h 15mTotal time
15mPrep

MediumDifficulty

Lamb shoulder strips about 3/4 inch (2 cm) thick are combined with suet, grated garlic, coriander, salt, cumin, pepper and olive oil, covered and refrigerated overnight. Next day everything goes into a heavy-bottomed deep skillet or wide pot and cooks covered over low heat, stirring occasionally, about 2 1/2 to 3 hours, or until the meat has darkened and dried a bit, all of the fat has completely melted, and the liquid from the meat has evaporated. It is cooled completely, packed in a container with its oil and refrigerated.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy-bottomed deep skillet or wide pot with lid, Glass or plastic container

  • 2.2 lbs boneless lamb shoulder (or other cut of lamb or beef)
  • 7 oz lamb or beef suet, finely chopped
  • 6 large cloves garlic, grated or pressed
  • 2 tbsp ground coriander seeds
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 2/3 cup olive oil

Method

  1. Cut the meat into long strips about 3/4 inch (2 cm) thick.
  2. Combine the strips with the remaining ingredients, cover and refrigerate overnight.
  3. Next day, transfer everything, including the oil, to a heavy-bottomed deep skillet or wide pot.
  4. Cover and cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, about 2 1/2 to 3 hours, or until the meat has darkened and dried a bit, all the fat has completely melted, and the liquid has evaporated. Do not turn up the heat or add water.
  5. Remove from the heat and cool completely. Transfer the khlii and oil to a plastic or glass container, cover and refrigerate.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: darkened and dried a bit; all the fat melted; liquid evaporated. The overnight marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The page says to avoid the temptation to turn up the heat or add water.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Raising the heat, which scorches the meat.
  • Packing it before it has cooled completely.

Cultural Note

One reading says Mqila is the name of the pan, passed to the dish, and that the style comes from Salé and Rabat and is often made after Eid al-Adha, and that Benlafquih's recipe came from a friend's mother-in-law in Rabat (SINGLE-PASS).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate in its oil up to one month per the card (the article body says a couple of weeks); discard it if you see mold, bubbling or smell anything off.

Reheating: Warm in a pan with a little of the oil, or use in eggs and tagines.

Serve with: Eggs, as in the khlii and egg tagine, Bread
