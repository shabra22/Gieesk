🍯 Ethiopian Honey Bread

ማር ዳቦ — Mar Dabo

Ethiopian Honey Bread — a rich, golden loaf made with Ethiopian mountain honey, eggs, and butter, fragrant with cardamom and cinnamon. The celebration bread — served at weddings, holidays, and the most important family occasions alongside honey butter.

Ethiopian honey bread is the most indulgent of the Ethiopian breads — the mountain honey gives it a distinctive floral sweetness, the eggs and butter enrich it to a brioche-like tenderness, and the cardamom and cinnamon make it unmistakably Ethiopian.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Oven at 175°C, Loaf pan

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp yeast
  • 4 tbsp Ethiopian raw honey
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup warm water
  • 3 tbsp butter — softened
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon

Glaze: 2 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp water, brushed on after baking

    Method

    1. Dissolve yeast in warm water with 1 tbsp honey. Rest 10 minutes.
    2. Beat eggs with remaining honey.
    3. Combine flour, salt, cardamom, and cinnamon.
    4. Add yeast mixture, egg-honey mixture, and butter. Knead 10 minutes.
    5. Cover. Rise 90 minutes until doubled.
    6. Shape into a loaf. Place in greased loaf pan.
    7. Rest 20 minutes.
    8. Bake at 175°C for 30—35 minutes until deep golden.
    9. Brush with honey-water glaze immediately out of the oven.

    Chef Tips

    • Ethiopian raw honey has a floral complexity that commercial honey lacks — the bread character is different.
    • The honey glaze creates a glossy, sticky top that caramelises beautifully.
    • Lower temperature than standard bread — the honey sugars burn at high heat.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Too high temperature — honey sugars burn.
    • Insufficient kneading — dense crumb.

    The Science

    Honey's fructose content (higher than glucose) makes it more hygroscopic than sucrose — bread made with honey stays moister longer than sugar-sweetened bread. The fructose also browns at lower temperatures than sucrose, which is why honey bread requires a lower baking temperature.

    Health Benefits

    • Raw honey provides enzymes and antioxidants even after baking.
    • Eggs add complete protein.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian honey bread is the bread of abundance — made when honey is plentiful, when a celebration is happening, when the family has gathered. Its fragrance — cardamom, cinnamon, and honey — is the smell of Ethiopian celebration mornings.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Room temperature 4 days. Freeze 2 months.

    Reheating: Warm in oven.

    Serve with: With honey butter (ETH152), At celebrations, With chai, As a wedding bread
