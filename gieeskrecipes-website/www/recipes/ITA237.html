Baccalà alla Napoletana

Naples' version of salt cod, simmered in a bright tomato sauce with olives, capers, and pine nuts, a Christmas Eve tradition for families observing a meatless holiday meal.

This preparation leans into Naples' love of bold, briny flavors — olives and capers cutting through the richness of the fish — and remains one of the most anticipated dishes on the traditional Neapolitan Christmas Eve table.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl for soaking, Heavy-bottomed pan with lid

  • 800 g (1.75 lb) dried salt cod
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 500 g (1 lb) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 80 g (½ cup) black olives, pitted
  • 3 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 30 g (¼ cup) pine nuts, toasted
  • 30 g (⅓ cup) raisins, soaked in warm water
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Soak the salt cod in cold water for 24 hours, changing the water several times to remove excess salt.
  2. Drain, remove any skin and bones, and cut into large pieces.
  3. Warm the olive oil in a heavy pan and sauté the onion and garlic until softened.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the olives, capers, pine nuts, and drained raisins, and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  6. Gently add the cod pieces and simmer for 15–18 minutes until the fish is tender.
  7. Finish with fresh parsley and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Soaking the cod thoroughly and changing the water several times is essential to remove excess salt.
  • Handle the cod gently once added to the sauce, so it stays in recognizable pieces rather than breaking apart.
  • This dish is deliberately bold and briny — don't skimp on the olives and capers.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not soaking the cod long enough, resulting in an overly salty final dish.
  • Overcooking the fish, which causes it to fall apart completely into the sauce.
  • Using a thin, underseasoned sauce that doesn't stand up to the rich, briny cod.

Cultural Note

Baccalà alla Napoletana is a centerpiece of Naples' traditional Christmas Eve meatless feast, its bold, briny sauce a defining feature of the region's holiday table.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop.

Serve with: Crusty bread, As part of the Christmas Eve Feast of Seven Fishes
