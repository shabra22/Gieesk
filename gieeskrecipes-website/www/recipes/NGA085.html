🌽 Tuwo Masara

Tuwo masara — tuo masara, corn tuwo

Two cups of corn flour to three of water, stirred continuously until thick and stretchy, then a quarter cup more water and three minutes covered on the lowest heat.

Of the three tuwos this is the only one with a usable ratio, and it is a genuinely quick swallow: corn flour and water go into the pot cold together and are stirred over medium heat until the mixture is thick and stretchy. Then a quarter cup of extra water goes in, the heat drops to minimum, and the pot sits covered for three minutes before a final stir to smooth. Fifteen minutes from start to finish. Note that the extra quarter cup is in the method but not the ingredient list, so the real total is three and a quarter cups.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium pot with a lid, Wooden spatula or turning stick

  • 2 cups corn flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/4 cup water, added later — see the note

Method

  1. Combine the corn flour and the 3 cups of water in a medium pot.
  2. Place on medium heat and stir continuously until the mixture is thick and stretchy.
  3. Add the 1/4 cup of water.
  4. Reduce the heat to minimum and leave the pot covered for 3 minutes.
  5. Stir until smooth, and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Two cups of flour to three of water, plus the extra quarter cup. That is the only usable tuwo ratio in any source consulted.
  • Thick and stretchy is the cue before the extra water goes in. Stretchy is the operative word — it should pull away from the spoon.
  • Cold start, both together. Corn flour into hot water will lump immediately.
  • Stir continuously, not occasionally. The whole first stage is constant movement.
  • The covered three minutes on minimum heat is a steaming stage. Do not lift the lid.
  • The extra quarter cup is in the method but not the ingredient list, so the list's three cups understates the total. That discrepancy is in the source.
  • Tuwo masara starts from a dry flour stirred into water. Tuwo shinkafa starts from whole grains cooked and then mashed — a genuinely different technique, and the only thing distinguishing the tuwos is the grain and that.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the flour to water that is already hot.
  • Stirring intermittently.
  • Lifting the lid during the three minutes.
  • Stopping before it goes stretchy.
  • Using only the three cups listed and wondering why it is stiff.

Cultural Note

Tuwo is the northern swallow and the grain names the dish: masara for corn, shinkafa for rice, dawa for guinea corn. It goes under the miyan soups — kuka, taushe, zogale — and the pairing is as fixed in the north as amala with gbegiri is in Ibadan. That the corn version is the one with a written ratio is probably because flour is easier to measure than a pot of softened rice.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat the same day. It firms within the hour.

Reheating: Steam, or microwave wrapped in damp cling film.

Serve with: Miyan Kuka, Miyan Taushe, Miyan Zogale
