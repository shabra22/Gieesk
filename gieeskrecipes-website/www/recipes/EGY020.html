🍚 Roz bil Sha'riya (Egyptian Rice with Vermicelli)

Roz bil sha'riya — رز بالشعرية

Short-grain rice cooked with golden-toasted vermicelli, boiled until holes appear on the surface, then covered and steamed on low for fluffy, separate grains.

40mTotal time
5mPrep
30mCook
4Servings
330Calories
EasyDifficulty

Rinsed rice is stirred into butter-toasted vermicelli, then hot liquid and salt are added and boiled for about 5 minutes, or until holes appear on the surface of the rice. The pot is covered and cooked undisturbed on low for 15 to 20 minutes until the liquid is absorbed, rested 5 minutes and fluffed. It is garnished with lemon zest, herbs and toasted nuts.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium pot with a tight lid, Fine sieve

  • 1 cup short-grain Egyptian rice
  • ½ cup vermicelli
  • 1 tablespoons ghee or butter, butter, or oil
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or canola oil
  • 1½ - 1 ¾ cups hot boiling water or broth, vegetable, chicken, or beef
  • ½ teaspoon salt, or to taste

Garnish

  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest, unwaxed (unsprayed)
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley or cilantro
  • 2 tablespoons toasted pine nuts or almonds

Method

  1. Wash the rice under cold running water until the water runs clear.
  2. Heat the fat on medium and stir the vermicelli continuously until golden-brown.
  3. Add the rice and stir to coat it in the fat.
  4. Add ½ teaspoon of salt and 1½ cups of the hot liquid. Boil for 5 minutes, or until you see holes on the surface of the rice.
  5. Cover and cook on low, undisturbed, for 15 to 20 minutes, until the liquid is fully absorbed. Rest covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  6. Top with lemon zest, herbs and toasted nuts.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: vermicelli golden-brown; holes on the rice surface; liquid fully absorbed. The 5-minute rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The page contradicts itself on proportions: the card lists 1½ to 1¾ cups liquid per cup of rice with ½ cup vermicelli, her notes say a 1:1 ratio of rice to liquid, and the tips say vermicelli to rice 1:2. We keep the card quantities.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stirring once the lid is on, which breaks the grains and makes the rice sticky.
  • Burning the vermicelli; stir constantly.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour calls it a 'quintessential comfort food' tied to family gatherings and recalls her grandmother's version scented with mastic.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month (page).

Reheating: Reheat with a splash of water, covered.

Serve with: Molokhia, Torly
