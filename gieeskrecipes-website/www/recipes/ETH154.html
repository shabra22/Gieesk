🍋 Ethiopian Spiced Lemon

ሎሚ ቅቤ — Lomi Qibe

Ethiopian Lemon-Kibbeh Condiment — lemon juice mixed with melted kibbeh and a pinch of salt, served warm alongside grilled fish. The simplest of the Ethiopian condiments — the acid of lemon cutting through the fat of kibbeh into a bright, clean sauce.

This simple condiment is a lakeside restaurant staple — served warm in a small pot alongside grilled fish, the lemon-kibbeh provides an acid-fat balance that perfectly complements the charcoal-grilled berbere fish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small bowl or ramekin

  • 2 tbsp kibbeh — melted
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of black pepper

Method

  1. Warm kibbeh until just melted.
  2. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir briefly — it will not fully emulsify.
  4. Serve warm in a small ramekin.
  5. Guests dip their fish or drizzle over.

Chef Tips

  • Serve immediately — the temporary emulsion breaks quickly.
  • Warm kibbeh plus cold lemon juice creates the brief emulsion.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold kibbeh — doesn't combine with lemon.

Cultural Note

Lemon-kibbeh condiment is the lakeside restaurant's simplest and most useful sauce — drizzled over grilled tilapia at a table overlooking Lake Tana, it is one of Ethiopia's most satisfying simple pleasures.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Make to order.

Reheating: Not applicable.
