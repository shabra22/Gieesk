🫘 Tanzanian Bean and Banana Porridge

Uji wa Maharage na Ndizi

Tanzanian Uji wa Maharage na Ndizi — a thick, nourishing breakfast porridge made from blended cooked beans and ripe banana, sweetened with honey. A traditional highland Tanzanian breakfast of extraordinary nutritional completeness.

Bean and banana porridge is the traditional complete breakfast of Tanzania's banana-growing highlands — the beans provide protein, the banana provides carbohydrate and sweetness, and together they produce a breakfast that sustains for hours.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Small saucepan

  • 1 cup cooked kidney or red beans
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Blend cooked beans with 1 cup water until completely smooth.
  2. Blend banana separately until smooth.
  3. Combine both in a saucepan with remaining water.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring constantly for 8—10 minutes.
  5. Sweeten with honey. Add salt.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Blend beans completely — any lumps make the porridge unpleasant.
  • Ripe bananas only — they sweeten naturally.
  • Stir constantly — the porridge sticks.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-blending beans — lumpy porridge.
  • Not stirring — sticks and burns.

The Science

The combination of bean protein (with lysine) and banana starch (with methionine) creates a complementary protein profile — together providing all essential amino acids that neither provides alone.

Health Benefits

  • Complete protein from bean-banana combination.
  • High resistant starch from banana.
  • Rich in potassium and iron.

Cultural Note

Bean and banana porridge is the traditional breakfast wisdom of Tanzania's Chagga people — the perfect nutritional combination discovered long before modern nutrition science confirmed it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best consumed fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast, With fresh fruit
