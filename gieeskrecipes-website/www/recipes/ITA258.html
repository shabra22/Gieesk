🧀 Tomini Elettrici

Tomini Elettrici

Small, fresh Piedmontese cheese rounds marinated in a fiery chili-spiked olive oil, their playful name meaning 'electric,' a nod to the noticeable heat that builds with each bite.

Tomini are simple, mild fresh cheeses on their own, but marinating them in a punchy blend of chili, garlic, and vinegar transforms them into a lively appetizer that has become a popular modern feature on Piedmontese antipasto boards.

Ingredients

Equipment: Shallow dish for marinating

  • 8 small tomini cheeses (or substitute small rounds of fresh mild cheese)
  • 150 ml (2/3 cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 fresh red chilies, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Arrange the tomini cheeses in a single layer in a shallow dish.
  2. Whisk together the olive oil, vinegar, chopped chilies, garlic, parsley, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Pour the marinade evenly over the cheeses.
  4. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight, turning occasionally.
  5. Bring to room temperature before serving.
  6. Serve with plenty of the chili-infused oil spooned over.

Chef Tips

  • Adjust the amount of chili to your heat preference - the dish is meant to have a noticeable kick, but the intensity is flexible.
  • Letting the cheese marinate overnight allows the flavors to penetrate more deeply.
  • Bringing the cheese to room temperature before serving lets its flavor and the marinade aroma come through fully.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving straight from the refrigerator, which mutes both the cheese and marinade flavors.
  • Using overly aged or strong cheese, which can clash with the delicate fresh tomini character this dish is built around.
  • Not marinating long enough, missing the full infusion of chili and garlic flavor.

Cultural Note

Tomini elettrici is a modern, popular addition to Piedmontese antipasto spreads, its playful heat a fun contrast to the region's more traditional, gentler appetizers.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate in the marinade for up to 5 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A crisp Piedmontese white wine
