🍞 Egyptian Mahlab Bread (Street-Vendor Rounds with Black Seed)

Aish mahlab — عيش محلب

Thin yeasted rounds brushed with egg and milk, sprinkled with nigella seeds and baked until the bottoms are lightly brown.

1h 50mTotal time
15mPrep
20mCook
8Servings
170Calories
EasyDifficulty

A simple dough of flour, salt, yeast and water rises an hour or until doubled in an oiled bowl, is divided into 16 pieces and flattened to 2 mm, brushed with egg and milk and sprinkled with black seeds. After a 10 to 15 minute rest it bakes at 400°F for 15 to 20 minutes until the bottom is lightly brown, with the broiler on low for 2 to 3 minutes if needed. The bread hardens as it cools.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Baking sheets

  • 3 cup flour.
  • ½ teaspoon salt.
  • 1 ½ teaspoon dry yeast.
  • ½ teaspoon sugar - optional didn't use.
  • 1 cup warm water
  • Black seed. Nigella
  • 1 egg mixed with 1 tablespoon milk.

Method

  1. Mix the dry ingredients and slowly add water until it forms a soft, smooth dough.
  2. Place in an oiled bowl and let rise for an hour or until doubled in size.
  3. Divide into 16 parts and flatten each to a 2 mm thickness. Brush with the egg and milk and sprinkle with the black seeds.
  4. Rest 10 to 15 minutes while the oven heats to 400°F.
  5. Bake 15 to 20 minutes until the bottom is lightly brown; use the broiler on low for 2 to 3 minutes if the top is not golden. Cool to room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: dough doubled; bottom lightly brown. The rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. There is no mahlab in the ingredient list; the author says the name is unrelated to the spice and her mother never used it.
  • The card says 6 servings while the method makes 16 pieces.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rolling too thick; these are thin crisp rounds.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim recalls the vendor who came around 4:00 PM calling 'Mahlaaaaab' in her Egyptian childhood, and says her father bought it every other day.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store once cool in an airtight bag; best within a day.

Reheating: Warm briefly.

Serve with: White cheese (page suggestion)
