🍚 Zanzibar Pilau

Pilau ya Zanzibar

Zanzibar Pilau — the island's signature spiced rice dish. Beef slow-cooked with the most generous Zanzibar spice blend in East Africa — locally grown cloves, cinnamon, cardamom, and cumin — the cooking broth used to steam basmati rice to golden perfection.

Zanzibar pilau is the most fragrant rice dish in East Africa — the island where the world's finest cloves, cinnamon, and cardamom are grown produces a pilau that no other place can replicate. The spices are used in quantities that would seem excessive anywhere else; in Zanzibar they are exactly right.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy sufuria, Spice grinder, Heavy lid and cloth

  • 800g beef — cubed
  • 4 cups basmati rice — soaked
  • 3 large onions — thinly sliced
  • 5 tomatoes — diced
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 5 tbsp oil
  • 8 cups beef stock or water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ cup coriander

Zanzibar Spice Blend (toast and grind half, keep half whole)

  • 3 tsp cumin seeds
  • 12 cardamom pods
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 12 whole cloves
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns
  • 3 star anise
  • 2 tsp coriander seeds
  • 1 nutmeg — grated

Method

  1. Toast all spices very deeply — 4 minutes. Grind half to a powder. Keep half whole.
  2. Brown beef in batches in hot oil. Remove.
  3. Cook onions until very deeply golden — 15 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, ground and whole spices. Stir 2 minutes.
  5. Add tomatoes. Cook 12 minutes until completely reduced and dark.
  6. Return beef. Add stock. Simmer covered 50—55 minutes until very tender.
  7. Remove beef. Measure broth — you need exactly 7 cups. Adjust.
  8. Add drained rice to broth. Bring to a boil.
  9. Place beef back on top of rice.
  10. Cover with a cloth then the lid. Lowest heat for 22 minutes.
  11. Rest 15 minutes. Serve beef atop the fragrant golden rice.

Chef Tips

  • Zanzibar cloves have the highest eugenol content in the world — use them generously.
  • The nutmeg is a Zanzibar signature — do not omit.
  • The cloth under the lid absorbs excess steam — the wedding cook's most important trick.
  • Measuring the broth precisely ensures perfectly cooked rice.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-toasting spices.
  • Not measuring broth.
  • Rushing the onion caramelisation.

The Science

Nutmeg contains myristicin and elemicin — aromatic compounds that survive cooking partially and add a distinctive warm, slightly sweet note to the pilau that no other spice provides. It is the secret note in Zanzibar pilau.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar pilau is the dish that every Zanzibari who has left the island craves most intensely — its fragrance is the fragrance of home. Made with the island's own spices, it cannot be authentically reproduced anywhere else on earth.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Covered with splash of water.

Serve with: Kachumbari (TZA108), Chatni ya Nazi (TZA118), Raita
