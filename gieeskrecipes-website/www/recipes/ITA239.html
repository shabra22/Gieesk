🍫 Torta Caprese

Torta Caprese

A dense, naturally flourless chocolate almond cake from the island of Capri, its intensely fudgy interior and crackled top said to have been born from a baker's happy accident.

Legend holds that torta caprese was invented when a baker on Capri forgot to add flour to a chocolate almond cake for visiting guests, and the resulting dense, intensely rich, naturally gluten-free result was such a success that the mistake became the recipe.

Ingredients

Equipment: Round baking pan, Double boiler or heatproof bowl, Electric mixer

  • 200 g (7 oz) dark chocolate, chopped
  • 200 g (⅞ cup) unsalted butter
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 200 g (1 cup) granulated sugar
  • 200 g (2 cups) almond flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together gently over a double boiler, then let cool slightly.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks with half the sugar until pale and thick.
  3. Fold the melted chocolate mixture into the egg yolks, then fold in the almond flour and vanilla.
  4. Whip the egg whites with the remaining sugar to stiff peaks.
  5. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the chocolate mixture in stages, being careful not to deflate it.
  6. Pour into a well-buttered round baking pan.
  7. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30–35 minutes until the top is set and slightly cracked but the center remains fudgy.
  8. Cool completely in the pan — the cake firms up considerably as it cools.
  9. Dust with powdered sugar before slicing and serving.

Chef Tips

  • The center should remain slightly underdone when you take the cake out — it continues to set as it cools, and this is what gives torta caprese its signature fudgy texture.
  • Fold the egg whites in gently and gradually to keep as much air in the batter as possible.
  • Cooling completely before slicing is essential; the cake is very soft and delicate while still warm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overbaking, which dries out the cake and loses its intended dense, fudgy texture.
  • Slicing while still warm, when the structure hasn't yet fully set.
  • Deflating the batter by folding the egg whites in too aggressively.

Cultural Note

Torta caprese is one of Italy's most beloved cakes, its legendary accidental origin on the island of Capri now inseparable from its identity as a naturally flourless dessert.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature or slightly chilled.

Serve with: Whipped cream, Espresso
