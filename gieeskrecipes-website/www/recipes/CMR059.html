🌿 Sauce de foleré au poisson fumé (Roselle Leaf Sauce with Smoked Fish)

Sauce de foleré

Boiled and drained foleré leaves added to fried onion, smoked fish, tomatoes and spices, then water, roasted peanut paste, salt, pepper and natron, cooked 15–20 minutes.

p. 58 of the October 2023 recipe book. Fresh foleré leaves are boiled and drained. Onion is fried; the fish, tomatoes, garlic, green condiments and spices are simmered in; the leaves go in; then water, roasted peanut paste, salt, pepper and natron, and the sauce cooks 15–20 minutes. The book’s cue: natron reduces the acidity of the foleré. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot

  • Fresh foleré leaves — quantity not printed
  • Smoked fish — quantity not printed
  • Roasted peanut paste — quantity not printed
  • Water, oil, onion, garlic, tomatoes, bell pepper, leek, parsley, celery, white pepper, ginger, salt, chilli — quantities not printed
  • Rock salt (natron/kanwa) — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Boil the foleré leaves and drain them.
  2. Fry the onion. Add the fish, tomatoes, garlic, green condiments and spices and simmer.
  3. Add the leaves.
  4. Add water, the peanut paste, salt, pepper and natron.
  5. Cook 15–20 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The printed reason for the natron is to reduce the acidity of the foleré.
  • No other doneness cue is printed.
  • Quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the natron.
  • Over-boiling the leaves.
  • Adding the peanut paste without water.
