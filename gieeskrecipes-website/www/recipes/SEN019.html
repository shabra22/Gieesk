🍤 Yassa with Fish Balls and Prawns

Yassa aux boulettes de poisson et crevettes

Fish balls and prawns in an onion yassa, with a beugeudj of bissap leaves and okra pounded with lemon on the side.

1h 25mTotal time
40mPrep
45mCook
5Servings
480Calories
HardDifficulty

The most elaborate of her yassas and the only one with a sauce on the side. A seasoning farce of four garlic cloves, pepper and half a roughly cut bell pepper is pounded and reserved. Parsley, a vegetarian chilli, two more garlic cloves and a little salt are chopped, part of the farce is spread on the prawns, the fish flesh is added and mixed, and the mixture is formed into balls. The onion sauce takes the remaining farce with lemon juice and twenty centilitres of water, cooked about fifteen minutes on low heat, with the prawns and balls added at the end. The beugeudj is bissap leaves and okra cooked fifteen minutes in lightly salted water, drained, then pounded with salt and the juice of half a lemon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar and pestle, or a blender, Pan with a lid, Second pan, for the beugeudj, Slotted spoon

    • A little salt

    • 1/2 lemon, for the beugeudj
    • The juice of 1 lemon, or 2 depending on size, for the sauce

      Method

      1. Pound 4 garlic cloves, pepper and half the roughly cut bell pepper into a seasoning farce. Reserve it.
      2. Chop the parsley, the vegetarian chilli, 2 garlic cloves and a little salt together.
      3. Spread part of the farce on the prawns, add the fish flesh, and mix.
      4. Form the mixture into balls.

      The source's cooking instruction for the balls is misprinted; see the note.

      1. Cook the balls through.
      2. Soften the onions in hot oil with a chilli.
      3. Once the onions turn translucent, add the remaining farce, a little salt — not too much — pepper, the lemon juice and 20 cl of water.
      4. Cook about 15 minutes on low heat.
      5. Add the prawns and the fish balls at the end.

      The beugeudj

      1. Cook the bissap leaves and okra 15 minutes in lightly salted water, then drain.
      2. Pound or blend them with salt and the juice of 1/2 a lemon.
      3. Serve the yassa with the beugeudj alongside.

      Chef Tips

      • The beugeudj on the side is what makes this version worth the extra work — a sharp, green, slightly slippery sauce against the sweet onions.
      • Not too much salt in the sauce, by her own instruction. The prawns and the fish balls are already seasoned with the farce.
      • Split the farce: part onto the prawns and into the fish mixture, the rest into the onion sauce. Same divided-seasoning logic as all her yassas.
      • Prawns and balls in at the very end only. They need minutes, not the fifteen the sauce takes.
      • Fifteen minutes in lightly salted water for the bissap leaves and okra, then pound. Do not skip the draining or the beugeudj will be watery.
      • Be aware the source's instruction for cooking the fish balls is misprinted — it reads as a temperature with the time unit missing, so no cooking time for the balls can be stated. Cook them through by eye.
      • Her line on the dish: it is a whole art, a whole alchemy between the ingredients and the flavours. For this one, which has three separate preparations, that is fair.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Over-salting the sauce.
      • Adding the prawns with the sauce rather than at the end.
      • Not draining the beugeudj greens.
      • Putting all the farce into the fish mixture.
      • Reading the misprinted ball-cooking instruction as minutes.

      Health Benefits

      • Fish and prawns together are very high in protein and low in saturated fat.
      • Bissap leaves supply vitamin C, calcium and iron.
      • Okra adds soluble fibre.
      • Gluten-free and dairy-free.

      Cultural Note

      Fish balls are a Saint-Louis and coastal habit — a way of stretching fish and using the pieces that will not steak cleanly — and pairing them with a sour green bissap-leaf sauce is the sort of combination that only comes from a place where both are to hand and nothing is wasted.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: 2 days refrigerated. The beugeudj separately.

      Reheating: Warm the sauce; add the prawns and balls back at the end.

      Serve with: Beugeudj, from the recipe, White rice, Kaani
