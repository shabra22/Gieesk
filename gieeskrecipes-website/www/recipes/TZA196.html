🍠 Tanzanian Sweet Potato Balls

Vikurumbasi vya Viazi Vitamu

Tanzanian Sweet Potato Balls — mashed sweet potato mixed with coconut milk, cardamom, and a little flour, rolled into balls and deep-fried until golden. A naturally sweet, naturally colourful coastal street snack — the orange of the sweet potato glowing through the golden crust.

Sweet potato balls are the coastal street snack that uses Tanzania's most nutritious staple — the mashed sweet potato produces a naturally sweet, naturally orange ball that fries beautifully and is as nutritious as it is delicious.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan

  • 400g sweet potatoes — boiled and mashed
  • 3 tbsp coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

  1. Mash sweet potatoes completely smooth.
  2. Mix in coconut milk, flour, sugar, cardamom, and salt.
  3. The mixture should be firm enough to roll into balls.
  4. Roll into walnut-sized balls.
  5. Chill 15 minutes.
  6. Heat oil to 175°C.
  7. Fry in batches 3—4 minutes until golden.
  8. Drain. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Completely smooth mash — lumps create weak points that split during frying.
  • Chilling before frying firms the balls.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lumpy mash.
  • Not chilling.

Health Benefits

  • Very high beta-carotene.
  • Natural sweetness — no added sugar needed.
  • Gluten-free.

Cultural Note

Sweet potato balls are the coastal market's most colourful snack — the bright orange interior revealed when you bite through the golden crust is Tanzania's most nutritious colour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten same day.

Reheating: Briefly in hot oil.

Serve with: As a standalone snack, With honey, With chai
