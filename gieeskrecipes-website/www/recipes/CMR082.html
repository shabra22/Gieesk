☕ Café de soja (Roasted Soy ‘Coffee’ Powder)

Café de soja

A kilogram of soy sorted, wiped with a damp cloth, roasted in a pan to a dark colour, ground to remove husks, winnowed and ground in a mill — a coffee substitute made only from soy.

p. 38 of the October 2023 recipe book describes soy coffee as a product made only from soy, in ‘6 steps’. A kilogram is weighed and sorted, wiped with a damp cloth, roasted in a pan ‘until a dark colour is obtained’, cooled, ground to remove the husks, winnowed, and the roasted grains ground in a mill. THE BOOK PRINTS NO BREWING METHOD: the ratio of powder to water and how to prepare the drink are absent, as are the roasting time and temperature. The description says 6 steps but the extraction shows more fragments; not reconciled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan, Mill, Winnowing tray

  • 1 kg soy

Method

  1. Weigh 1 kg of soy and sort the grain.
  2. Wipe the grains with a damp cloth.
  3. Roast in a pan until a dark colour is obtained. Cool.
  4. Grind to remove the husks. Winnow.
  5. Grind the roasted grains in a mill to a powder.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cue is a dark colour on roasting.
  • The book gives no brewing ratio — brew the powder as you would any roasted-grain infusion and note the amounts you use.
  • Roast time and temperature are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Roasting too fast so the beans burn.
  • Skipping the winnowing and leaving husks.
  • Expecting a ratio from the book.
