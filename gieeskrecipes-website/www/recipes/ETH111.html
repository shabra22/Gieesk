🍛 Ethiopian Lentil Curry

ምስር ካሪ — Misir Kari

Ethiopian Lentil Curry — red lentils cooked with an Ethiopian-spiced curry sauce of berbere, turmeric, cardamom, and coriander with tomato and onion. The curry format applied to Ethiopian lentils — the berbere anchors it firmly in Ethiopian territory.

Ethiopian lentil curry represents the modern urban synthesis of the curry format with the berbere tradition — accessible to those who know curry but unmistakably Ethiopian in character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 1.5 cups red lentils — washed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cook onions until very golden — 10 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, berbere, turmeric, coriander, and cardamom. Stir 2 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add lentils and water. Bring to a boil.
  5. Simmer 25—30 minutes until lentils dissolve.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • The berbere gives it an unmistakably Ethiopian character.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking lentils.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian lentil curry demonstrates how Ethiopian food absorbs external influences while retaining its essential identity — the berbere makes it unmistakably Ethiopian regardless of the curry format.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ethiopian Rice (ETH013)
