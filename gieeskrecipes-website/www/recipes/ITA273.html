🥔 Tortel di Patate Trentino

Tortel di Patate

Simple, rustic Trentino potato fritters, grated potato bound with egg and flour and fried until deeply golden and crisp at the edges, often served alongside speck and cheese.

These humble fritters are a staple of Trentino's mountain kitchens, requiring nothing more elaborate than potato, egg, and flour, and their crisp edges and soft centers make them a satisfying accompaniment to the region's cured meats and cheeses.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Large skillet

  • 600 g (1.3 lb) potatoes, peeled and grated
  • 2 eggs
  • 80 g (2/3 cup) 00 flour
  • 2 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Squeeze the grated potato well to remove excess moisture.
  2. Combine the potato with the eggs, flour, chives, salt, and pepper into a thick batter.
  3. Heat a shallow layer of vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Drop spoonfuls of the potato mixture into the hot oil, flattening slightly.
  5. Fry for 3-4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crisp.
  6. Drain briefly on paper towels and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Squeezing the grated potato thoroughly is essential - excess moisture prevents the fritters from crisping properly.
  • Keep the fritters relatively thin and flat, which helps them cook through and crisp evenly.
  • These are excellent served alongside speck, cheese, or a simple green salad.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not squeezing the potato enough, resulting in soggy rather than crisp fritters.
  • Making the fritters too thick, leaving the center undercooked by the time the outside browns.
  • Frying at too low a temperature, resulting in greasy rather than crisp fritters.

Cultural Note

Tortel di patate is a humble, everyday staple of Trentino's mountain kitchens, often served alongside the region's cured meats and cheeses.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot skillet or oven to help re-crisp.

Serve with: Speck, Fresh cheese, A simple green salad
