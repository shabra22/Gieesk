🥗 Shredded Collard Green Salad with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Cashews

Salade de chou vert

Sweet potato slices roasted 40 minutes at 400° with cumin, thyme and garlic, tossed over collards shredded fine and massaged about 5 minutes with a palm-oil and lime dressing, topped with goat cheese and cashews.

A SAVEUR salad from Pierre Thiam’s Thanksgiving feature (16 November 2015). Sweet potato slices are tossed with 2 tablespoons of palm oil, cumin, thyme and garlic, seasoned and roasted at 400° about 40 minutes, flipping once, until golden brown. Lime juice and ginger stand 10 minutes to soften, then the remaining 1/4 cup of palm oil is whisked in until emulsified. The shredded collards are tossed with 1 tablespoon of the dressing and massaged about 5 minutes, then topped with the potatoes, goat cheese and cashews and served with the rest of the dressing. The recipe page carries no byline (photo credit Ryan Liebe); the parent article and credited author are Pierre Thiam. The page says West Africans cook ‘virtually every dish they make with red palm oil’.

Ingredients

Equipment: Rimmed baking sheet, Wire rack, Small bowl and whisk

  • 2 lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut crosswise into 1/2-inch-thick slices
  • 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons red palm oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
  • 1 tablespoon thyme leaves
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper — quantities not printed
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon minced ginger
  • 1 lb collard greens, stems removed, leaves thinly shredded (6 cups)
  • 2 oz goat cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup roasted, unsalted cashews, roughly chopped

Method

    Roast the potatoes

    1. Heat the oven to 400°. Toss the sweet potato slices on a rimmed baking sheet with 2 tablespoons of the palm oil, the cumin, thyme and garlic; season. Roast about 40 minutes, flipping once halfway, until golden brown. Move to a rack to cool.

    Make the dressing

    1. Combine the lime juice and ginger in a small bowl and let stand 10 minutes to soften. Whisk in the remaining 1/4 cup palm oil until emulsified. Season.

    Assemble

    1. Toss the collards with 1 tablespoon of the dressing and massage it into the greens for about 5 minutes. Transfer to a platter, top with the sweet potatoes, goat cheese and cashews. Serve the remaining dressing on the side.

    Chef Tips

    • Printed cues: potatoes ‘golden brown’; lime and ginger stand ‘to soften’; dressing ‘until emulsified’.
    • No internal temperatures; oven 400°.
    • Organic fair-trade red palm oil is sold at West African specialty grocers (the page’s note).

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Crowding the sweet potatoes so they steam.
    • Skipping the massage; the greens stay tough.
    • Dressing the salad too early.
