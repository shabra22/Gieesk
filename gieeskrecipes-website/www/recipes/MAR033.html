🫓 Msemen (Square Layered Flatbread)

Msemen / rghaif — folded Moroccan pancake (مسمن)

Paper-thin dough folded in thirds with butter and semolina, griddled until golden and crisp outside, soft and layered within.

1h 10mTotal time
40mPrep
15mCook
20Servings
190Calories
MediumDifficulty

Msemen starts as a soft, elastic dough with a little yeast, divided into plum-sized balls and left to rest for 10 to 15 minutes. Each is spread paper-thin, dotted with butter and sprinkled with semolina, then folded into a square. The squares are patted to about double their size and cooked on a hot griddle, turned several times, until cooked through, crispy on the outside and golden. A see-saw flex separates the layers.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl or stand mixer, Flat griddle or heavy skillet, Oiled work surface

    Dough

    • 3 1/2 cups white flour, all purpose or bread
    • 1/2 cup fine semolina or durum flour
    • 2 tsp sugar
    • 2 tsp salt
    • 1/4 tsp dry yeast (less in very warm weather)
    • 1 1/2 cups warm water (not hot)

    For folding

    • 1 cup vegetable oil (more if needed)
    • 1/2 cup fine semolina (can use coarser caliber if you like)
    • 1/4 cup very soft unsalted butter (more if needed)

    Method

    1. Knead the dough until very smooth, soft and elastic but not sticky.
    2. Divide into plum-sized balls and cover loosely; leave to rest for 10 to 15 minutes.
    3. Spread each ball on an oiled surface into a paper-thin, roughly shaped circle, dot with butter, sprinkle with semolina and fold into thirds twice to make a square.
    4. Heat the griddle over medium until quite hot. Pat each square to about double its size and cook, turning several times, until cooked through, crispy on the exterior and golden in colour.
    5. Flex each msemen in a see-saw motion for a few seconds to separate the layers. Reheat at 350°F (180°C) if needed.

    Chef Tips

    • Doneness cue: crisp and golden outside, cooked through.
    • The page gives no rise cue for the 1/4 tsp of yeast; the 10-15 minute rest is time only.
    • Oiled hands and surface make spreading far easier.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Dough too dry to stretch thin.
    • Griddle not hot enough, which makes it leathery.
    • Skipping the semolina, which keeps the layers apart.

    Health Benefits

    • A simple wheat bread; butter and oil make it rich, so eat in moderation.
    • Semolina adds texture.

    Cultural Note

    Taste of Maroc uses msemen as the base of rfissa and other dishes where it is shredded and steamed.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Keeps 2 days at room temperature or freezes well.

    Reheating: Heat on a griddle or at 350°F (180°C) until hot.

    Serve with: Mint tea
