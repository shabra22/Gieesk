🍲 Tanzanian Mixed Meat Stew

Mchuzi wa Nyama Mchanganyiko

Mchuzi wa Nyama Mchanganyiko — Tanzanian mixed meat stew with beef, goat, and chicken cooked together in one rich spiced sauce. The celebration stew that serves everyone — every guest gets the protein of their preference in one pot.

Mixed meat stew is the feast stew — when a large gathering requires one pot to please everyone, the mixed meat preparation ensures that goat lovers, chicken lovers, and beef lovers all find their preference in the same magnificent sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Very large pot

  • 400g beef — cubed
  • 400g goat — cubed
  • 400g chicken pieces
  • 3 onions — diced
  • 5 tomatoes — diced
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ½ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown beef and goat together first — they take longer. Remove.
  2. Brown chicken separately. Remove.
  3. Cook onions until very golden.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, all spices, tomatoes. Cook 10 minutes.
  5. Return beef and goat. Add water. Simmer 40 minutes.
  6. Add chicken. Simmer 25 more minutes.
  7. All meats should be falling-tender. Adjust seasoning.

Chef Tips

  • Add chicken later — it cooks faster than beef and goat.
  • A very large pot is essential — this is a feast preparation.
  • Season generously at the end.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding chicken too early — it overcooks.
  • Insufficient pot size.

The Science

Different meats have different collagen content and cooking times. Goat and beef (higher collagen) need 60—75 total minutes; chicken (lower collagen, smaller muscle fibres) needs only 25—30 minutes. Staggered addition ensures all are perfectly cooked simultaneously.

Cultural Note

Mixed meat stew is Tanzania's most generous celebration dish — the pot that ensures every guest is honoured regardless of their meat preference. It is the food of true Tanzanian hospitality.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
