🍘 Fekkas (Twice-Baked Almond and Raisin Biscuits)

Fekkas msseoues — fekkas (فقاص مسيوس)

Anise-scented dough rolls studded with almonds, raisins and sesame, half-baked, rested, sliced and baked again until golden and crisp.

7h 20mTotal time
45mPrep
35mCook
30Servings
160Calories
MediumDifficulty

Almonds are soaked in cold milk or water and raisins in orange blossom water, then folded into a soft, smooth dough of flour, sugar, crushed aniseed, oil and eggs. Rolls of dough are half-baked at 338°F (170°C) for 15 to 20 minutes until they form a light crust, rested at least 6 hours, sliced 1/2 cm thick and baked again until golden brown, about 12 to 15 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowls, Baking sheets, Serrated knife

  • 1.3 lbs all purpose flour, - plus extra for rolling
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 4.5 oz raisins
  • 4.5 oz almonds, skin on - washed, drained and pat-dried
  • 5 oz sugar
  • 3 tbsp aniseed, roughly crushed - (or half aniseed and half fennel seeds)
  • 5 fl oz vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs - medium-size, at room temperature
  • 2 fl oz orange blossom water - (or half milk, half orange blossom water)
  • 1/2 cup sesame seeds - unhulled, slightly toasted

Egg wash

  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • drops of white vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp instant coffee powder - (optional)

Method

  1. Soak the almonds in cold milk or water for at least 20 minutes, and the raisins in the orange blossom water for 5 minutes.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients with the sugar, aniseed, oil, eggs, soaked almonds and raisins, adding extra flour 1 tablespoon at a time to get a soft, smooth dough.
  3. Cut the dough in four and roll each piece into a fat roll, a little over 2 cm high on a base of roughly 5 to 6.5 cm (dimensions as printed), and brush with the egg wash and sesame.
  4. First bake: 338°F (170°C), about 15 to 20 minutes. The rolls should form a light crust without being completely baked: soft inside but starting to look crumbly and compact, and slightly springy outside.
  5. Rest the rolls overnight or at least 6 hours (a damp cloth for about 1 hour is the page's faster option).
  6. Cut into slices 1/2 cm thick and bake at 338°F (170°C) until golden brown, about 12 to 15 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • First-bake cues: a light crust, soft but crumbly-compact inside, slightly springy outside.
  • Second-bake cue: golden brown.
  • One reading says to turn the oven off and leave the slices inside to dry out; this is single-source.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Baking the rolls fully on the first round so they crumble when sliced.
  • Slicing too thick.

Health Benefits

  • Raisins and almonds add fibre; the biscuits are a sugar-and-oil snack.

Cultural Note

The author says broken fekkas are soaked in milk and eaten like cereal, a children's breakfast in Fez, and that 'msseoues' refers to the holes left around the seeds, almonds and raisins.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The page says fekkas keep a few months.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Mint tea, Milk, as a children's breakfast in Fez, per the author
