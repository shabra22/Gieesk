🥑 Ethiopian Avocado Salad

አቮካዶ ሰላጣ — Avocado Selata

Ethiopian Avocado Salad — ripe avocado sliced and dressed with lemon, jalapeño, onion, and fresh coriander. Ethiopia grows excellent avocados in its highland areas, and this simple salad celebrates the fruit at its best.

Ethiopia is one of Africa's major avocado producers — the highland areas produce buttery, rich avocados of outstanding quality. This simple salad showcases them with the most minimal dressing.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl

  • 3 ripe avocados — halved, stoned, sliced
  • ½ red onion — very thinly sliced
  • 1 jalapeño — sliced
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Arrange avocado slices on a plate.
  2. Scatter thinly sliced onion and jalapeño.
  3. Dress with lemon juice and oil.
  4. Season. Scatter coriander.
  5. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Serve immediately — avocado discolours quickly.
  • The lemon juice slows discolouration.
  • Ripe avocados only — they should yield to gentle pressure.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unripe avocado — hard and flavourless.
  • Preparing ahead — discolours.

The Science

Avocado browns through enzymatic oxidation — the enzyme polyphenol oxidase reacts with phenolic compounds in the presence of oxygen. Lemon juice's citric acid inhibits polyphenol oxidase activity, slowing browning for 30—60 minutes.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian avocado salad represents the modern Ethiopian café culture — the same avocados that have been grown in the highlands for generations, now served in the contemporary format that young Ethiopians enjoy.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On the fasting platter, At modern Ethiopian cafés
