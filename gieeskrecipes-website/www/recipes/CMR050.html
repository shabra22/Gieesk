🫘 Kont-niébé (Maize and White Bean Stew, Northern Cameroon)

Kont-niébé

The same method as Corn-tchap but with white (niébé) beans cooked about 20 minutes instead of 1½ hours, then 5 minutes together with the fried seasoning base.

115 minTotal time
30 minPrep

EasyDifficulty

p. 9 of the October 2023 CIFOR-ICRAF/IRAD/Université de Ngaoundéré recipe book. 1 kg of maize grains and 750 g of white (niébé) beans, with the same seasonings as Corn-tchap. The maize is cooked about an hour; the beans about 20 minutes; both go into the roasted-onion and crushed-condiment base and simmer 5 minutes. The book’s two recipes differ only in bean type and bean time, as the source shows.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar

  • 1 kg maize grains
  • 750 g white beans (niébé)
  • Oil, tomato, bell pepper, white pepper, onion, parsley, celery, basil, garlic, ginger, salt, chilli, stock cube, rock salt — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Cook the maize in water with rock salt about an hour. Cool and wash off the skins.
  2. Cook the white beans in salted water about 20 minutes. Wash them.
  3. Chop the onion and bell pepper; crush the remaining condiments.
  4. Roast the onion, add the crushed condiments and simmer. Season with salt and the stock cube.
  5. Add the maize and beans and simmer 5 minutes. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Beans take about 20 minutes here against about 1 hour 30 in Corn-tchap.
  • The printed cue for the maize is that the skins loosen and wash off.
  • Seasoning quantities are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using the Corn-tchap bean time for white beans.
  • Leaving the skins on the maize.
  • Over-salting from the rock salt and stock cube together.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe.
