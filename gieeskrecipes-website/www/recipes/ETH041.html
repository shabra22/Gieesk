🍲 Ethiopian Lamb Key Wat

የበግ ቀይ ወጥ — Ye-Beg Key Wat

Ethiopian Ye-Beg Key Wat — the celebration version of Key Wat using young lamb instead of beef. Served at Easter (Fasika), Ethiopian Christmas (Genna), and Timkat — the lamb version of the red stew carries even greater cultural weight than the beef version.

Ye-Beg Key Wat is the pinnacle of Ethiopian celebration cooking — the lamb key wat served at the most important religious celebrations. The fat of young lamb in the berbere sauce produces an even richer, more fragrant result than beef key wat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy Dutch oven

  • 1 kg young lamb — bone-in, cut into portions
  • 3 large onions — very finely diced
  • 4 tbsp kibbeh
  • 5 tbsp berbere
  • 6 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 hard-boiled eggs — pierced all over
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 20 minutes, no oil.
  2. Add kibbeh. Cook 2 minutes.
  3. Add berbere. Toast in kibbeh 3—4 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add lamb. Coat in sauce.
  6. Add water. Simmer covered 75—80 minutes.
  7. Add pierced eggs for the last 15 minutes.
  8. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Young lamb becomes more tender than beef at the same cooking time.
  • The lamb fat enriches the berbere sauce — the result is more unctuous than beef key wat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Same as ETH021 — dry-caramelise the onions without fail.

The Science

Young lamb fat (predominantly oleic acid and short-chain fatty acids) has a lower melting point than beef fat — it renders out more rapidly into the sauce, producing a more richly unctuous result at the same cooking temperature.

Cultural Note

Ye-Beg Key Wat is the Easter morning dish of Ethiopian Orthodox Christians — prepared after the 55-day Lenten fast, the lamb slaughtered at dawn, the wat cooking through the morning as the family returns from the overnight Easter service. It is the most anticipated meal of the Ethiopian year.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Tikel Gomen (ETH087), Gomen (ETH086), Ayib (ETH096)
