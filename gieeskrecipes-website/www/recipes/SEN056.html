🫘 Thiebou Nop Niébé, Black-Eyed Peas with Rice

Thiebou nop niébé

Overnight-soaked black-eyed peas simmered with a smoked ham hock until tender, then mixed with the picked meat and a cup of cooked rice.

1h 40mCook

A side dish from the Multicultural Cookbook of Life-Cycle Celebrations, reprinted by the Madison Returned Peace Corps Volunteers. A pound of dried black-eyed peas soaks overnight; the soaking water is discarded; the peas simmer covered in 6 cups of water with a half pound of smoked ham hocks, a chopped onion and a teaspoon of ground red pepper for 1 to 1 1/2 hours until tender. The hocks are skinned and the meat cut small, returned with a cup of cooked rice, and heated over medium for 5–7 minutes. The page serves it with lamb and salad.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid, Bowl for soaking

  • 1 pound (about 2 cups) dried black-eyed peas
  • Water for soaking the peas
  • 6 cups water
  • 1/2 pound smoked ham hocks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground red pepper, more or less to taste
  • 1 cup cooked rice

Method

  1. Soak the peas in water overnight. Drain and discard the soaking water.
  2. Cover the peas with 6 cups of water and add the ham hocks, onion and red pepper. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered 1 to 1 1/2 hours, until the peas and meat are tender.
  3. Remove the hocks, discard the skin and cut the meat into small pieces.
  4. Return the meat to the pot with the cooked rice and heat over medium 5 to 7 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Tender peas and tender meat are the only cue; check at an hour.
  • Discard the soaking water — the page says so.
  • The page presents this as a side, to serve with lamb and salad.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Salting early and toughening the peas (a general cook’s rule, not printed).
  • Skipping the soak.

Health Benefits

  • Black-eyed peas supply fibre, folate and plant protein.
  • A smoked ham hock adds sodium and saturated fat in small amounts per serving.

Cultural Note

The spelling ‘nop niébé’ — the ‘nop’ element appears in no other source in this collection for thiebou niébé; it is recorded as printed and not normalised. The page notes the recipe takes two days.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 4 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: Méchoui or roast lamb, Couscous
