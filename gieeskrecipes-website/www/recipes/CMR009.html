🥜 Groundnut Soup, Cameroon Style

Groundnut soup

Boiled, blended raw groundnuts and garlic simmered in a fried pepper base with beef stock, crayfish, assorted meats and smoked fish, finished with sliced ugu leaves.

The page’s title is ‘Groundnut Soup Cameroon Style’; the author says it is popular in parts of Cameroon, Togo and Benin. Raw groundnuts are soaked, peeled, boiled about 10 minutes (the page warns not to skip this or the soup tastes raw) and blended with garlic. Diced onion is fried, blitzed peppers cooked about 7 minutes on low-medium heat, then the groundnut paste, stock, crayfish, salt and seasoning cubes added with the lid on for about 10 minutes. Meats and smoked fish go in until the water reduces down, and the ugu leaves simmer 3 minutes before the heat goes off.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Pot, Frying pot

  • 2 cups raw groundnuts
  • 8–12 single cloves garlic
  • 200 g sliced ugu leaves (about two small bunches)
  • 1 cup blitzed peppers (may include tomatoes)
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • Cooked assorted meats and smoked fish — quantity not printed
  • 2 cooked rich beef stock (printed with no unit)
  • 1 tablespoon crayfish powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 or 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1.5 cups vegetable oil

Method

  1. Soak the groundnuts and peel off the skins. (The soaking time is not printed.)
  2. Boil the groundnuts about 10 minutes — the page says not to skip this or the soup will taste of raw groundnut. Blend with the garlic.
  3. Fry the onions in the oil. Add the blitzed peppers and cook about 7 minutes on low-medium heat.
  4. Add the groundnut paste, stock, crayfish, salt and seasoning cubes. Put the lid on for about 10 minutes.
  5. Add the meats and smoked fish and cook until the water reduces down.
  6. Add the sliced ugu leaves, simmer 3 minutes and turn off the heat.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue for the meats stage is ‘till the water reduces down’.
  • Boil the groundnuts first; the page is firm about this.
  • ‘2 cooked rich beef stock’ is printed with no unit — use enough to loosen the paste and note the gap.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the groundnut boil.
  • Boiling the ugu leaves too long.
  • Guessing the stock amount without watching the soup’s thickness.
