🧆 Beignets koki (Cowpea Fritters)

Beignets de niébé

Cowpeas soaked 6 hours, skinned, crushed with chilli and onion into a light, fine paste, salted and spooned into hot oil.

A page from the October 2023 northern-Cameroon recipe book. The cowpeas are covered with water and soaked 6 hours, pressed between the hands to remove the thin skin and rinsed. They are crushed finely with the chilli and onion with a little water and stirred until the paste is light and fine; salt is added. Balls are formed with a ladle or spoon and fried in hot oil. The page prints no frying time, oil temperature or colour cue.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar or grinder, Frying pot, Ladle or spoon

  • 250 g cowpeas (haricot / niébé)
  • 50 ml water
  • 10 g salt
  • 1 chilli
  • 1 medium onion
  • Oil for frying — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Cover the cowpeas with water and soak for 6 hours.
  2. Press the beans between your hands to remove the thin skin, then rinse.
  3. Crush finely with the chilli and onion, adding a little water.
  4. Stir the paste until light and fine. Add the salt.
  5. Shape balls with a ladle or spoon and fry in hot oil.

Chef Tips

  • The printed texture cue is a paste that is ‘légère et fine’ (light and fine).
  • Rubbing the soaked beans together is how the skin comes off.
  • The page prints no frying time or temperature; fry to colour and check one fritter for doneness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the skins on.
  • Making the paste too wet so the balls fall apart in the oil.
  • Oil that is not hot enough.
