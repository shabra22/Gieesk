🥣 Bouillie de maïs ou de sorgho au tamarin (Sweet Tamarind Porridge)

Bouillie au tamarin

Maize or sorghum flour poured gradually into a boiling filtrate of strained peanut paste, stirred 10 minutes to avoid lumps, sweetened and cooked 10 more minutes on low heat, then finished with tamarind juice.

p. 31 of the October 2023 recipe book. Tamarind fruit is soaked 10 minutes and the juice extracted. Roasted peanut paste is kneaded in clean water, strained through a fine sieve or cloth, and the filtrate brought to the boil. The flour is mixed with water to a smooth paste and poured gradually into the boiling filtrate while stirring for 10 minutes to avoid lumps. Sugar is added and it cooks 10 minutes on low heat; the tamarind juice goes in; serve hot. The page says it can be accompanied by beignets, cakes or bread. Water quantities are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Fine sieve or cloth

  • 500 g maize or sorghum flour
  • 100 g roasted peanut paste
  • 250 g sugar
  • 20 tamarind fruits
  • Water — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Soak the tamarind 10 minutes and extract the juice.
  2. Knead the peanut paste in clean water, strain through a fine sieve or cloth and bring the filtrate to the boil.
  3. Mix the flour with water to a smooth paste.
  4. Pour it gradually into the boiling filtrate, stirring 10 minutes to avoid lumps.
  5. Add the sugar and cook 10 minutes on low heat.
  6. Add the tamarind juice and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is stirring ‘to avoid lumps’ (the first reading of the page lacks that clause).
  • The page suggests serving with beignets, cakes or bread.
  • No water quantities are printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Pouring the flour in all at once.
  • Cooking the tamarind juice too long.
  • Skipping the straining.
