🌼 Saffron Tea

Atay bil za'faran — tea with saffron (أتاي بالزعفران)

Chinese gunpowder green tea brought to the boil several times in a teapot with sugar and a pinch of saffron.

17mTotal time
2mPrep
15mCook
2Servings
70Calories
EasyDifficulty

Water is brought to a boil with sugar, the green tea and a pinch of saffron in the teapot. The pot returns to the stove for a few minutes on medium heat until the water comes to a boil again, and this is repeated three to four times before serving immediately.

Ingredients

Equipment: Teapot, Tea glasses

  • 3-4 cups of water
  • 3 tablespoons of sugar
  • 1 tablespoon of Chinese green tea (gunpowder)
  • A pinch of saffron

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil.
  2. Add the sugar, green tea and saffron to the teapot with the hot water.
  3. Return the teapot to the stove on medium heat for a few minutes until the water comes to a boil again.
  4. Repeat this boil 3 to 4 times, then serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: the water comes back to a boil each round.
  • The order in which the saffron and sugar go in is our summary; check the live page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving it before the repeated boils are done.
