🍚 Kenyan Plain Coconut Rice

Wali wa Nazi wa Kawaida

Plain Kenyan Coconut Rice — basmati rice cooked in a simple blend of coconut milk and water until each grain is perfectly fluffy and subtly fragrant. The everyday weeknight version of the classic coastal Wali wa Nazi.

Not every meal needs the full ceremony of spiced Wali wa Nazi. Sometimes you want that gentle coconut fragrance in under 30 minutes — a simple, clean rice that pairs with anything from grilled fish to coconut chicken curry to a simple bean stew. This is that rice. Pure, fragrant, fluffy, and effortless.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan with lid, Measuring cups

  • 2 cups basmati rice — rinsed well
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Rinse the rice until water runs clear. Drain well.
  2. Combine coconut milk, water, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil.
  3. Add the rice and stir once.
  4. Reduce heat to lowest setting. Cover tightly.
  5. Cook undisturbed for 18—20 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat. Rest covered for 10 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Rinse the rice thoroughly — removes excess starch for fluffier grains.
  • Never stir after adding rice.
  • The 10-minute rest is essential — do not skip it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid during cooking.
  • Too much liquid — makes the rice mushy.
  • Stirring after adding the rice.

The Science

The ratio of coconut milk to water (roughly 1:1) provides just enough fat to coat the rice grains and prevent clumping, while the water provides the steam needed for gentle gelatinisation of the starch.

Health Benefits

  • Provides sustained energy from complex carbohydrates.
  • Coconut milk adds healthy medium-chain fats.
  • Naturally gluten-free and dairy-free.

Cultural Note

Plain coconut rice is the everyday workhorse of Swahili coastal cooking — always present alongside grilled fish, curries, and bean stews. It is one of the first dishes Kenyan coastal children learn to make.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Freeze for up to 1 month.

Reheating: Sprinkle with a little water and reheat gently or microwave covered.

Serve with: Kuku wa Nazi, Samaki wa Kupaka, Maharagwe ya Nazi, Mishkaki, Kachumbari
