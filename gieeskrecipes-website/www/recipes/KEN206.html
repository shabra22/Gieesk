🌙 Kenyan Eid Chicken Biryani

Biriani ya Kuku ya Idd

Kenyan Eid Chicken Biryani — fragrant, golden biryani prepared for Eid ul-Fitr and Eid ul-Adha celebrations. Chicken marinated in yogurt and spices layered with saffron rice, fried onions, and caramelised cashews — the celebratory dish that breaks the Ramadan fast or honours the sacrifice.

Eid biryani is one of the most anticipated meals of the Muslim Kenyan calendar — the celebratory meal that follows weeks of Ramadan fasting, or the feast that accompanies the Eid ul-Adha sacrifice. Chicken biryani for Eid is prepared with the finest spices, the best quality basmati, and the kind of loving attention that marks the most important day of the Muslim year.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy dum pot, Foil or dough for sealing, Flat griddle or heat diffuser

  • 1.5 kg chicken pieces — bone-in, skin on
  • 5 cups basmati rice — soaked 1 hour
  • 2 large onions — thinly sliced and fried until golden
  • ½ cup ghee or butter
  • ½ tsp saffron — soaked in warm milk
  • ¼ cup fried cashews
  • ¼ cup raisins

Marinade

  • 1 cup natural yogurt
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt

Rice water spices

  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in all marinade ingredients for 4 hours minimum — overnight preferred.
  2. Cook marinated chicken in ghee until 80% cooked. Remove.
  3. Parboil rice with whole spices in generously salted water until 70% cooked. Drain.
  4. In the dum pot: layer chicken, half the rice, fried onions, cashews, raisins, remaining rice, saffron milk, remaining ghee.
  5. Seal pot with foil or dough.
  6. Cook over low heat with a heat diffuser for 25—30 minutes.
  7. Serve with raita and salad.

Chef Tips

  • Parboiling rice to exactly 70% is the essential skill — practise before Eid.
  • The ghee is non-negotiable for Eid biryani — butter as a second choice only.
  • Saffron in warm milk: soak for 15 minutes before using for maximum colour and flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Fully cooking rice before dum.
  • Insufficient ghee — Eid biryani should be rich.

The Science

See KEN204 for the full science of dum cooking. Chicken reaches safe internal temperature (74°C) faster than beef — the 80% pre-cooking prevents overcooking during dum while ensuring full safety.

Cultural Note

Eid biryani represents the joy of completion — the breaking of the Ramadan fast with the most fragrant, celebratory rice dish in the Kenyan repertoire. It is also prepared for Eid ul-Adha, where the sacrifice of an animal is commemorated with a feast of generosity shared with family, neighbours, and the less fortunate.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat covered with a splash of water.

Serve with: Raita, Salad, Mango Chutney, Lemon wedges
