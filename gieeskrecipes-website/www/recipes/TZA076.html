🐟 Tanzanian Fish Balls

Kababu ya Samaki

Tanzanian Kababu ya Samaki — fish balls made from minced fish with onion, coriander, Zanzibar spices, and flour, deep-fried golden and crispy. Served with tamarind sauce and pili pili — the Zanzibar fish street food that outsells everything else.

Fish balls are the most popular street food in Zanzibar's Stone Town markets — small, golden, crispy outside and moist within, fragrant with coriander. Eaten in cones of paper with tamarind sauce, standing and talking.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor or knife, Deep frying pan

  • 500g white fish fillets — finely minced
  • 1 small onion — finely minced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 green chilli — minced
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for deep frying

Tamarind dipping: 3 tbsp tamarind paste + 1 tbsp sugar + 2 tbsp water

    Method

    1. Mix minced fish with onion, garlic, ginger, coriander, chilli, egg, flour, and spices.
    2. Form into golf ball-sized spheres.
    3. Refrigerate 15 minutes — helps them hold shape.
    4. Heat oil to 175°C.
    5. Fry in batches 4—5 minutes until golden all over.
    6. Make dipping sauce: simmer tamarind, sugar, and water 2 minutes.
    7. Serve immediately with tamarind sauce and pili pili.

    Chef Tips

    • Chill before frying.
    • Do not over-process — some texture is desirable.
    • Tamarind dipping sauce is non-negotiable.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Warm fish mixture — balls fall apart.
    • Over-processing — mushy.

    The Science

    The egg and flour create a binding matrix. Chilling firms this matrix before frying. The initial thermal shock from hot oil creates a rapid protein crust that locks the shape permanently.

    Cultural Note

    Fish balls are the food of Zanzibar's Stone Town market — bought from a vendor whose family has been frying them in the same spot for generations.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate uncooked 24 hours. Freeze 2 months.

    Reheating: Refry briefly.

    Serve with: Tamarind Sauce, Pili Pili Sauce, Fresh Lime
