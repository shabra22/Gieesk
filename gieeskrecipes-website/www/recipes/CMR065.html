🌿 Sauce de tasba à la viande (Tasba Leaf Sauce with Meat)

Sauce de tasba

Dried tasba leaves boiled with the meat 15 minutes, then peanut paste, salt and dalang added and simmered 20–30 minutes; the peanut paste can be replaced up to 50% with soaked soy paste.

70 minTotal time
20 minPrep
50 minCook
6Servings
340Calories
MediumDifficulty

p. 67 of the October 2023 recipe book. The dried tasba leaves are boiled with the meat 15 minutes; roasted peanut paste, salt and dalang are added and cooked 20–30 minutes. The book’s tips: natron softens the leaves and keeps them green; the peanut paste can be replaced up to 50% with soaked soy paste. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot

  • Dried tasba leaves — quantity not printed
  • Meat — quantity not printed
  • Roasted peanut paste — quantity not printed
  • Water, oil, onion, garlic, ginger, salt, chilli, dalang — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Boil the leaves with the meat for 15 minutes.
  2. Add the peanut paste, salt and dalang.
  3. Cook 20–30 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The book’s tip: natron softens the leaves and keeps them green.
  • Up to 50% of the peanut paste can be replaced with soaked soy paste.
  • Quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the natron when using dry leaves.
  • Adding the paste dry.
  • Not tasting for salt after the dalang.
