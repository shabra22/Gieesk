🫘 Tanzanian Pigeon Peas

Mbaazi

Tanzanian Mbaazi — pigeon peas simmered in coconut milk with Zanzibar spices and tomatoes. The coastal Swahili bean dish that is eaten for breakfast on Zanzibar with mahamri — one of the island's most iconic morning combinations.

Mbaazi with mahamri is one of the most iconic Zanzibari food combinations — the creamy pigeon peas in coconut milk alongside the cardamom-fragrant doughnuts. But mbaazi is equally eaten for lunch and dinner alongside rice. The pigeon pea's earthy, nutty flavour pairs magnificently with coconut milk.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 300g dried pigeon peas — soaked overnight
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Cook soaked pigeon peas in fresh water 50—60 minutes until tender. Reserve liquid.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onions until golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and spices. Stir 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes.
  5. Add pigeon peas and ½ cup reserved liquid.
  6. Simmer 15 minutes.
  7. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 10 minutes.
  8. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Pigeon peas take longer to cook than kidney beans.
  • Add coconut milk at the end — never boil hard.
  • The earthy flavour of pigeon peas specifically needs coconut milk to balance it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking pigeon peas.
  • Boiling coconut milk.

The Science

Pigeon peas contain more tannins than kidney beans — which contributes to their characteristic earthy, slightly astringent flavour. Coconut milk fat binds some of these tannin compounds, reducing astringency and producing a rounder, more pleasant flavour profile.

Cultural Note

Mbaazi with mahamri is one of the most celebrated food combinations in Zanzibar — the breakfast that Zanzibaris crave most when abroad. The earthy pigeon peas in coconut milk against the sweet cardamom mahamri is a contrast of extraordinary balance.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Mahamri (TZA006), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
