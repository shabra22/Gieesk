🍗 Ethiopian Roasted Chicken

ዶሮ ሮስት — Doro Rost

Ethiopian Roasted Chicken — a whole chicken marinated overnight in berbere, kibbeh, garlic, and ginger, roasted until the berbere skin caramelises into a magnificently deep red crust. The showpiece roast — Ethiopia's berbere tradition applied to a whole chicken.

Ethiopian roasted chicken applies the same berbere-kibbeh tradition to a whole roasted bird — the overnight marinade penetrates deeply, and the oven's dry heat caramelises the berbere on the skin into an extraordinary crust.

Ingredients

Equipment: Roasting pan, Oven at 200°C

  • 1 whole chicken (1.5 kg) — spatchcocked or whole

Overnight Marinade

  • 4 tbsp berbere
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 5 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients thoroughly.
  2. Score chicken skin deeply. Push marinade under the skin and into every crevice.
  3. Marinate overnight — minimum 8 hours.
  4. Bring to room temperature 1 hour before roasting.
  5. Roast at 200°C for 60—75 minutes.
  6. Baste with pan juices every 20 minutes.
  7. The berbere skin should be deeply caramelised — dark red-brown and fragrant.
  8. Rest 15 minutes before carving.

Chef Tips

  • Push marinade under the skin — it penetrates the meat rather than just colouring the exterior.
  • Spatchcocking (removing backbone) produces more even roasting.
  • The berbere crust should be dark but not burnt — watch carefully in the last 15 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Short marination.
  • Not getting marinade under the skin.
  • Not resting before carving.

The Science

The berbere marinade under the skin conducts heat more efficiently than a surface-only rub — the fat in the kibbeh acts as a heat conductor, creating a more even temperature distribution through the breast meat. The sugar content of the berbere caramelises under high heat, creating the characteristic dark crust.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian roasted chicken is the restaurant showpiece — the whole bird arriving at the table, its berbere skin crackling and fragrant, announces that something serious has been prepared in the kitchen.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C covered.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Gomen (ETH086), Tikel Gomen (ETH087)
