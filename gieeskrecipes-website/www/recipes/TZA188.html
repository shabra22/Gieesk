🍗 Tanzanian Deep Fried Chicken

Kuku wa Kukaanga

Tanzanian Kuku wa Kukaanga — chicken pieces marinated in Tanzanian spices, coated in seasoned flour, and deep-fried until magnificently golden and crispy. The Tanzanian street food version of fried chicken — spiced with curry powder and cumin rather than KFC's blend.

Tanzanian fried chicken is a completely independent development from American fried chicken — the spice blend is Tanzanian (curry powder, cumin, turmeric), the marinade is lemon-based rather than buttermilk, and the result is a distinctly East African flavoured fried chicken of great quality.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large deep frying pan or pot, Paper towels

  • 1 kg chicken pieces — bone-in

Marinade

  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt

Coating

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

  1. Marinate chicken pieces in all marinade ingredients for 1 hour minimum.
  2. Mix coating flour with spices.
  3. Dredge marinated chicken pieces in seasoned flour — coat thoroughly.
  4. Heat oil to 170°C — enough to submerge the pieces.
  5. Fry in batches — do not crowd.
  6. Fry 12—15 minutes depending on piece size, turning once.
  7. Internal temperature must reach 74°C.
  8. Drain on paper towels. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • 170°C is critical — too hot burns crust before chicken cooks; too low produces a greasy result.
  • Do not crowd the pan — oil temperature drops and chicken steams instead of fries.
  • Always check internal temperature.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong oil temperature.
  • Crowding.
  • Under-cooking — food safety risk.

Cultural Note

Tanzanian fried chicken at a street food stall is the Friday evening treat — the luxury fast food that working families buy when the week has been good. Its spice profile is unmistakably Tanzanian.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately. Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Oven at 190°C for 10 minutes.

Serve with: Chipsi Masala (TZA180), Kachumbari (TZA108), Pili Pili (TZA117)
