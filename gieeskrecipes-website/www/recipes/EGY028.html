🦐 Oven-Grilled Jumbo Shrimp with Celery and Dill

Gambari meshwi — جمبري مشوي

Jumbo shrimp baked under foil on a bed of tomato, onion, lemon, celery and dill with garlic and cumin, then uncovered for ten more minutes.

40mTotal time

Shelled jumbo shrimp are laid on sliced onion, lemon and tomato with celery, dill, garlic, lemon juice and cumin, covered and baked at 200C for 10 to 15 minutes, then uncovered for another 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking dish, Foil

  • 1/2 kg jumbo shrimp, shelled – gam-ba-ri jum-bo
  • 4 garlic cloves – tom
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice – a-seer la-moon
  • 1 tsp cumin – kam-moon
  • 2 stalks celery – ka-rafs
  • 2 stalks dill – sha-bat
  • 1 large onion, sliced – ba-sal
  • 2 lemons, sliced – la-moon
  • 2 large tomatoes, sliced – ta-ma-tem
  • salt and pepper – malh and fel-fel

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200C.
  2. Layer the onion, lemon and tomato slices with the celery and dill in a baking dish, lay the shrimp on top and season with garlic, lemon juice, cumin, salt and pepper.
  3. Cover and bake 10 to 15 minutes, then uncover and bake another 10 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no shrimp doneness cue; judge by the shrimp turning opaque and pink (our note). The method has a typo, 'sprinkle the garlic with the garlic'.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Baking uncovered from the start, which dries the shrimp.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Egyptian white rice
