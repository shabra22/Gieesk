🍋 Granita Siciliana al Limone

Granita al Limone

Sicily's icy, coarse-grained lemon dessert, its texture deliberately crystalline rather than smooth, traditionally eaten for breakfast alongside a soft brioche bun to dip into it.

Granita's signature texture comes from freezing the mixture while raking it with a fork every 30 minutes rather than churning it continuously like gelato, resulting in loose, delicate ice crystals rather than a solid block or creamy scoop.

Ingredients

Equipment: Shallow freezer-safe dish, Fork

  • 250 ml (1 cup) freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 200 g (1 cup) granulated sugar
  • 750 ml (3 cups) water
  • Zest of 2 lemons

Method

  1. Warm the water and sugar together just until the sugar dissolves, then remove from heat and let cool.
  2. Stir in the lemon juice and zest.
  3. Pour the mixture into a shallow, freezer-safe dish.
  4. Freeze for 30 minutes, then remove and rake thoroughly with a fork to break up the forming ice crystals.
  5. Return to the freezer and repeat the raking process every 30-45 minutes for about 3-4 hours total, until the mixture is entirely made up of loose, fine ice crystals.
  6. Serve immediately in chilled glasses, traditionally with a soft brioche bun alongside for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • Raking regularly is essential - skipping steps results in a solid block of ice rather than the desired loose, fluffy crystals.
  • Freshly squeezed lemon juice makes a real difference in flavor compared to bottled juice.
  • In Sicily, this is traditionally a breakfast food, especially in summer, paired with a soft brioche.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not raking often enough, which allows the mixture to freeze solid rather than forming the characteristic loose crystals.
  • Using bottled lemon juice, which lacks the bright, fresh flavor fresh juice provides.
  • Serving it too far ahead of eating, when it can begin to melt and lose its granular texture.

Cultural Note

Granita is a defining feature of Sicilian summer mornings, traditionally enjoyed with brioche for breakfast rather than as an after-dinner dessert.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store in the freezer for up to 1 week, though texture is best within the first day or two.

Reheating: Not applicable; served frozen.

Serve with: A soft brioche bun, for dipping
