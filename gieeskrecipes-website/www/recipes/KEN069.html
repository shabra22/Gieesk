🍯 Kenyan Honey Glazed Chicken

Kuku wa Asali

Kenyan Honey Glazed Chicken — chicken pieces marinated in a balance of honey, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and lemon, then grilled or roasted until the glaze caramelises into a sticky, deeply flavourful golden coating. Sweet, savoury, and completely irresistible.

Honey glazed chicken has become a fixture in Kenyan urban restaurants and home kitchens — the combination of local honey with garlic, ginger, and lemon juice creates a glaze that caramelises into something deeply flavourful during grilling. It is sweet enough to be a crowd-pleaser for all ages while remaining complex enough for sophisticated palates.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Grill or oven, Basting brush, Tongs

  • 1 kg chicken pieces — bone-in, skin-on
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp fresh coriander — for garnish

Method

  1. Score the chicken pieces deeply.
  2. Mix honey, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, lemon juice, oil, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper into a marinade.
  3. Coat the chicken thoroughly. Marinate for 1 hour minimum.
  4. Grill method: cook over medium charcoal or gas for 12—15 minutes per side. Baste with remaining marinade every 5 minutes during the last 15 minutes.
  5. Oven method: roast at 200°C for 30—35 minutes, basting every 10 minutes.
  6. The glaze is ready when it is sticky, caramelised, and deeply golden — watch carefully in the last 5 minutes to prevent burning.
  7. Rest 5 minutes, garnish with coriander, and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Add the honey glaze basting only in the last 15 minutes of cooking — honey burns quickly over high heat.
  • The higher the honey content, the faster the glaze caramelises — watch carefully.
  • A little soy sauce adds umami depth and balances the sweetness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Basting with honey glaze from the start — it burns long before the chicken is cooked.
  • Not scoring the chicken — the glaze stays on the surface.

The Science

Honey contains simple sugars (fructose and glucose) that caramelise rapidly at relatively low temperatures (around 160°C). This is why honey-glazed foods develop their characteristic deep golden colour and sticky texture during cooking, and why basting should only begin in the final cooking phase.

Cultural Note

Honey glazed chicken represents the modern Kenyan kitchen — drawing on local ingredients like Kenyan honey while embracing flavour combinations that have become part of the urban culinary identity.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat in oven at 180°C for 8—10 minutes.

Serve with: Roasted Sweet Potatoes, Coleslaw, Kachumbari, Chapati, Steamed Greens
