🍚 Sombi, Milk Rice with Fromage Blanc

Sombi — riz au lait

200 g of round rice cooked in 40 cl of water for about 20 minutes until the water is absorbed, then enriched with butter, evaporated milk, fromage blanc, sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon and orange blossom — no coconut.

The one sombi printing with no coconut at all. Round rice is cooked in 40 cl of water over medium heat for about 20 minutes ‘until the water is completely absorbed’, then a knob of butter and a pinch of salt are added; the pan is removed from the heat and stirred with the evaporated milk, the fromage blanc caillé, sugar, vanilla sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon and orange blossom. ‘Le riz au lait est prêt.’ The page adds that another variant of sombi is made with the local red rice, mostly for wedding ceremonies.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 200 g rice (round rice preferred)
  • 40 cl water
  • A pinch of salt
  • 10 tbsp sugar
  • 5 sachets vanilla sugar
  • 20 cl unsweetened evaporated milk
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  • A knob of butter
  • A pinch of nutmeg
  • A pinch of cinnamon
  • 100 g fromage blanc caillé

Method

  1. Put 40 cl of water in a saucepan. Add the rice.
  2. Cook over medium heat for about 20 minutes, until the water is completely absorbed.
  3. Add the knob of butter and a small pinch of salt. Mix and remove the pan from the heat.
  4. Add the evaporated milk, fromage blanc, sugar, vanilla sugar, a pinch of nutmeg and of cinnamon, and the orange blossom. Mix well. The rice pudding is ready.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cue: ‘until the water is completely absorbed’, paired with about 20 minutes.
  • Add the dairy and sugar off the heat.
  • The page names a red-rice wedding variant but gives no recipe for it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting from cooked rice — this version starts from raw round rice.
  • Adding the sweet milk while the pan is still on the heat.
  • Using long-grain rice and getting a looser pudding.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

‘Une autre variante du sombi est préparée à base de riz rouge local’ — the page says another variant uses local red rice and is mostly made for wedding ceremonies. Three sombi printings disagree fundamentally on the base: see Roasted Mango Sombi and Sombi, Senegalese Coconut Rice Pudding.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Serve cool or warm gently.

Serve with: Sweet tea
