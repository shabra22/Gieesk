🌿 Gurage Enset Bulla

ቡላ — Bulla

Gurage Bulla — the liquid extracted from the enset (false banana) root starch, cooked into a smooth, white porridge with kibbeh and honey. The most distinctive Gurage food after kitfo — the enset starch produces a porridge unlike any grain-based alternative.

Bulla is the purest expression of the enset food tradition — the white starch extracted from the enset root, cooked into a porridge that is simultaneously filling and digestible. Given to sick people, children, and pregnant women in the Gurage tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 1 cup enset starch (bulla) — or substitute arrowroot starch
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tbsp honey

Method

  1. Whisk starch with 1 cup cold water.
  2. Bring remaining water to a simmer.
  3. Add starch mixture, whisking.
  4. Cook stirring 12—15 minutes until smooth and thick.
  5. Season.
  6. Serve with kibbeh and honey in the centre.

Chef Tips

  • Authentic bulla starch from the enset plant is white and very fine — unlike any grain starch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lumps from poor mixing.

The Science

Enset starch gelatinises at a lower temperature than most grain starches — producing a smoother, more delicate porridge.

Health Benefits

  • Highly digestible.
  • Gentle on the stomach.
  • Enset starch is naturally gluten-free.

Cultural Note

Bulla is the food of Gurage care — given to newborn babies when solid food is introduced, to sick family members, and to pregnant women. The white, smooth porridge of enset starch is the Gurage version of chicken soup.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: With kitfo (ETH237), As a stand-alone breakfast, Given to the sick
