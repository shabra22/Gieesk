🍪 Ghorayeba (Egyptian Melt-in-the-Mouth Butter Cookies)

Ghorayebah — غريبة

Three-ingredient ghee, flour and powdered sugar cookies beaten to a creamy dough, firmed in the freezer and baked pale yellow.

2h 55mTotal time
20mPrep
15mCook
35Servings
80Calories
EasyDifficulty

Cold ghee, powdered sugar and flour mix on low for 4 to 5 minutes and then on medium-low for another 10 minutes; the dough starts like wet sand and becomes very creamy, like hummus. It freezes 15 to 20 minutes until firm to handle, is scooped into 11 to 12 gram pieces topped with pistachio, and bakes at 320°F for 15 minutes without changing colour, remaining pale yellow.

Ingredients

Equipment: Stand mixer, Baking sheet

  • 250 g all purpose flour 2 cups
  • 150 g cold ghee ⅔ cup. You can use clarified butter too.
  • 85 g powdered sugar ¾ cup
  • Pistachios.

Method

  1. Mix the ghee, sugar and flour on low for 4 to 5 minutes, then on medium-low for another 10 minutes, until very creamy.
  2. Freeze the dough 15 to 20 minutes until firm enough to handle.
  3. Heat the oven to 320°F with the rack in the middle. Scoop 11 to 12 gram pieces and press a pistachio on each.
  4. Bake 15 minutes. Do not wait for them to change colour; they need to be pale yellow.
  5. Cool on the sheet 10 to 15 minutes, then on a rack, and leave to set completely for at least 2 hours.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: dough very creamy; cookies pale yellow, not coloured. The freeze and the final set are NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The author says ghee is a must; butter will give a crunchy cookie instead of a soft one.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Baking until browned.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight at room temperature up to 5 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Not needed.

Serve with: Tea or coffee
