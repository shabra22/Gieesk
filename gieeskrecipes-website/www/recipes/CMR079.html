🥭 Nectar de mangue (Mango Drink)

Nectar de mangue

Ripe mangoes soaked 5 minutes in water with a little bleach, peeled, cut, pressed and filtered; the juice boiled 10 minutes with water and sugar, cooled and bottled in sterilised bottles.

p. 77 of the October 2023 recipe book: 2 kg ripe mangoes, 1 kg sugar and 5 litres of water, with an optional preservative. The mangoes are soaked in drinking water containing ‘un peu de javel’ (a little bleach) for 5 minutes, peeled, cut, pressed and filtered; water and sugar are added and the mix boiled 10 minutes; cooled; the preservative added; bottled in sterilised bottles; kept cool. The page states the bleach soak removes microbes and the boil destroys them. The bleach and preservative quantities are not printed, and the diagram shows 70 °C for 5 minutes against 10 minutes of boiling in the text.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Press, Cloth, Sterilised bottles

  • 2 kg ripe mangoes
  • 1 kg sugar
  • 5 litres water
  • A little bleach (javel) for the soak — quantity not printed
  • Preservative (optional) — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Soak the mangoes in drinking water with a little bleach for 5 minutes. (Rinse as you would for any bleach-washed produce — the page prints no rinse step.)
  2. Peel, cut, press and filter.
  3. Add the water and sugar. Boil 10 minutes.
  4. Cool. Add the optional preservative.
  5. Bottle in sterilised bottles. Keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The page says the bleach soak removes microbes and the boil destroys them.
  • The bleach quantity is not printed — use food-safe produce-wash practice instead of guessing.
  • The diagram shows 70 °C for 5 minutes; the text says 10 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Guessing the bleach dose.
  • Skipping the boil.
  • Bottling hot in unsterilised bottles.

Storage & Serving

Reheating: Serve cold.
