🥗 Ethiopian Garden Salad

ሰላጣ — Ye'atkilit Selata

Ethiopian Garden Salad — a mixed salad of lettuce, tomato, cucumber, carrot, and onion dressed with lemon, olive oil, and a touch of jalapeño. The modern Ethiopian restaurant salad — a familiar format with Ethiopian dressing.

The Ethiopian garden salad is the modern restaurant's nod to international dining expectations — a recognisable mixed salad format but dressed with the bright lemon and jalapeño that characterise Ethiopian fresh preparations.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl

  • 1 head lettuce — roughly torn
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 1 cucumber — diced
  • 2 carrots — grated
  • ½ onion — thinly sliced
  • 1 jalapeño — sliced
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Combine all vegetables in a bowl.
  2. Dress with lemon juice, oil, and salt.
  3. Add jalapeño. Toss gently.
  4. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Dress at the last moment — lettuce wilts quickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dressing too early.

The Science

The jalapeño's capsaicin dissolves partially into the oil component of the dressing — distributing a mild heat through the salad rather than concentrating it in specific pieces.

Cultural Note

The Ethiopian garden salad is modern Ethiopia's international greeting — a salad that any international visitor recognises immediately, dressed in the way that is unmistakably Ethiopian.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a starter at Ethiopian restaurants, Alongside grilled fish
