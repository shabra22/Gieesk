🫓 Kenyan Stuffed Chapati

Chapati ya Kujaza

Kenyan Stuffed Chapati — a chapati dough encasing a spiced filling of egg, vegetables, or minced meat, then rolled thin and pan-fried until golden. Kenya's most satisfying street breakfast — a complete meal in a flatbread.

Stuffed chapati takes the already beloved Kenyan chapati and transforms it into a complete meal. The dough is rolled thin, topped with a spiced filling — usually egg, onions, and vegetables — then sealed and cooked on a flat pan. The result is something between a chapati and an omelette, crispy on the outside and rich with filling within. At Nairobi street stalls, it is one of the most popular breakfast choices.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Large flat pan, Spatula

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 3 large eggs — beaten
  • 1 medium onion — finely diced
  • 1 medium tomato — finely diced
  • 1 green chilli — finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp curry powder

Method

  1. Make chapati dough: combine flour, salt, sugar, and 2 tbsp oil. Add warm water and knead for 8—10 minutes. Rest covered for 1 hour.
  2. Mix all filling ingredients together. Set aside.
  3. Divide dough into 6 balls. Roll each ball very thin on a floured surface.
  4. Heat a flat pan over medium heat. Lightly brush with oil.
  5. Place a rolled chapati on the pan. Cook for 30 seconds until small bubbles appear.
  6. Quickly pour 2—3 tbsp of the egg filling onto the chapati surface. Spread evenly.
  7. Use a spatula to fold the chapati over the filling.
  8. Press down gently. Cook for 2—3 minutes until golden.
  9. Flip carefully. Cook the other side for 2 minutes until golden.
  10. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The egg filling goes on while the chapati is on the pan — this cooks the bottom while the filling sets.
  • Pour the filling while the chapati is still slightly soft — before it sets completely.
  • A meat filling variation: replace the egg with spiced minced beef cooked and cooled.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much filling — the chapati cannot be folded without splitting.
  • Filling too wet — the chapati becomes soggy rather than crispy.

The Science

The egg mixture is added while the chapati is on the pan so the base heat cooks the bottom of the egg immediately, helping it adhere to the chapati surface before folding. This prevents the filling from sliding out when the chapati is folded.

Cultural Note

Stuffed chapati is one of the great Kenyan street breakfast foods — found at kiosks and food stalls across Nairobi and other urban centres, where it is prepared fresh to order and eaten standing up.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately. Refrigerate for up to 1 day.

Reheating: Warm in a dry pan for 2 minutes per side.

Serve with: Kachumbari, Pili Pili Sauce, Kenyan Masala Chai, Avocado
