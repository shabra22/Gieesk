🥩 Ethiopian Gurage Spiced Beef

ጉራጌ ሥጋ — Gurage Siga

Gurage Spiced Beef — beef cooked in the Gurage style that gave rise to kitfo, with mekelesha (cardamom, rue, and coriander) as the primary seasoning alongside kibbeh. Less raw than kitfo, more fully cooked, but carrying the distinctive Gurage aromatic profile.

Gurage spiced beef is what happens between kitfo and a fully cooked tibs — the Gurage cooking style applies their mekelesha spice tradition to a more cooked beef preparation. The rue and cardamom make it immediately distinctive.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pan

  • 500g beef — finely sliced
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 garlic cloves

Mekelesha: ½ tsp cardamom, ¼ tsp rue (or thyme), ¼ tsp coriander

  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp ayib — stirred in at the end

Method

  1. Heat kibbeh until hot.
  2. Cook onion until golden. Add garlic.
  3. Add sliced beef. Cook 5—7 minutes.
  4. Add berbere and mekelesha. Toss.
  5. Cook 2 more minutes.
  6. Stir in ayib at the very end — off the heat.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Mekelesha is the Gurage signature — do not omit rue.
  • Adding ayib at the end is the Gurage touch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Omitting mekelesha — not Gurage without it.

The Science

Rue (tena adam) contains alkaloids including rutin — which have mild antispasmodic properties. The Gurage use of rue in mekelesha reflects generations of observational knowledge about the herb's digestive benefits.

Cultural Note

Gurage spiced beef is the everyday expression of the cooking tradition that gave Ethiopia kitfo — the same mekelesha spices, the same quality beef and kibbeh, applied to a more accessible preparation.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Gomen (ETH086), Additional Ayib (ETH096)
