🫀 Tanzanian Beef Kidney Stew

Mchuzi wa Figo

Mchuzi wa Figo — Tanzanian beef kidney stew. Kidney cleaned, soaked, and cooked quickly in a spiced tomato-onion sauce with lemon and coriander. Intensely nutritious and fast to prepare — Tanzania's most economical organ meat dish.

Kidney stew is prepared similarly to liver in Tanzania — soaked first to remove bitterness, then cooked quickly in a spiced sauce. The lemon juice at the end is the essential brightening note that makes it enjoyable even for those unfamiliar with organ meats.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide frying pan

  • 500g beef kidney — cleaned and chopped
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Remove fat and core from kidney. Slice into 2cm pieces.
  2. Soak in cold salted water with lemon juice for 20 minutes. Drain.
  3. Blanch in boiling water 3 minutes. Drain and rinse.
  4. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  5. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  6. Add kidney pieces. Cook 8—10 minutes — no longer.
  7. Add lemon juice and coriander. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Soaking removes bitterness.
  • Blanching removes residual impurities.
  • Do not overcook kidney — becomes rubbery.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping soaking/blanching.
  • Overcooking.

Health Benefits

  • Exceptionally rich in iron, B12, and folate.
  • Very high protein.
  • Low cost.

Cultural Note

Kidney stew is Tanzania's most nutritious affordable dish — eaten by communities that recognise the extraordinary nutritional value of organ meats and refuse to waste any part of the animal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Very briefly.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004)
