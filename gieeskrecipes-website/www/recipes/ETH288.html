🥞 Ethiopian Teff Pancakes

ጤፍ ፓንኬክ — Teff Pancakes

Ethiopian Teff Pancakes — teff flour pancakes with cardamom and honey, light, slightly earthy, naturally gluten-free, served with Ethiopian honey and fresh fruit. The health-conscious modern Ethiopian breakfast — teff's extraordinary nutrition in the globally familiar pancake format.

Teff pancakes are the modern Ethiopian café's response to the global wellness trend — taking teff (the world's most nutritionally dense grain) and presenting it in the internationally familiar pancake format. Gluten-free, high-iron, and completely delicious.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick pan

  • 1.5 cups teff flour
  • 1 cup water or oat milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp oil

Serving: Ethiopian honey, fresh fruit, yogurt

    Method

    1. Whisk teff flour, baking powder, cardamom, and salt.
    2. Mix egg, honey, water, and oil.
    3. Combine wet and dry. Rest 30 minutes — teff absorbs moisture slowly.
    4. Heat a lightly oiled non-stick pan over medium heat.
    5. Pour ¼ cup batter. Cook 3—4 minutes until bubbles form.
    6. Flip. Cook 2 minutes.
    7. Serve with honey and fresh fruit.

    Chef Tips

    • Teff batter needs 30 minutes resting — the grain absorbs moisture and the batter thickens correctly.
    • Teff pancakes are slightly denser than wheat — this is natural.
    • Cardamom is the Ethiopian signature.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not resting batter.
    • Over-cooking.

    The Science

    Teff's bran-to-endosperm ratio is higher than wheat — meaning more water absorption is needed and batter resting time is important. The natural sour flavour of teff comes from its wild yeast content — even without fermentation, teff has a mild tanginess.

    Cultural Note

    Teff pancakes are the modern Ethiopian café's gift to the global wellness community — the world's most nutritious grain, in its most accessible format. They are appearing on international restaurant menus as the Ethiopian contribution to the gluten-free grain revolution.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Briefly in dry pan.

    Serve with: With Ethiopian honey, With fresh tropical fruit, With yogurt
