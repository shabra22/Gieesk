Moretta Fanese

A warming layered coffee cocktail from Fano, combining rum, brandy, and anise liqueur with hot espresso and a float of lemon zest, historically enjoyed by local fishermen against the cold.

Moretta was created for the fishermen of Fano, needing something both warming and energizing before heading out onto the cold Adriatic waters, and the drink's layered construction — spirits, sugar, coffee, and a twist of lemon — remains carefully preserved by local tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small heatproof glasses

  • 4 shots freshly brewed espresso, hot
  • 4 tbsp dark rum
  • 2 tbsp brandy
  • 2 tbsp anise liqueur
  • 4 tsp granulated sugar
  • Lemon peel, for garnish

Method

  1. Combine the rum, brandy, and anise liqueur in a small pot and warm gently over low heat, without boiling.
  2. Add the sugar to each glass.
  3. Pour the warmed spirit mixture over the sugar in each glass.
  4. Top with hot espresso, pouring gently to create a slight layered effect.
  5. Garnish each glass with a twist of lemon peel.
  6. Serve immediately, hot.

Chef Tips

  • Warming the spirits gently before combining with the coffee helps meld the flavors and keeps the drink properly hot.
  • A twist of lemon peel is a traditional, essential garnish, adding a bright aromatic note.
  • This drink is traditionally enjoyed slowly, often after a meal on a cold day.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling the spirits, which can burn off too much of their flavor and alcohol.
  • Skipping the lemon peel garnish, which is a genuinely important part of the traditional presentation.
  • Using low-quality spirits, which affects the overall balance of this simple drink.

Cultural Note

Moretta Fanese originated among the fishermen of Fano on the Adriatic coast, historically enjoyed as a warming, energizing drink before heading out to sea in cold weather.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Not applicable; made and consumed immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Enjoyed on its own as a warming after-dinner drink
