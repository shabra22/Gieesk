🍛 Tanzanian Beef Coconut Curry

Mchuzi wa Nyama ya Nazi

Mchuzi wa Nyama ya Nazi — Tanzanian beef and coconut curry. Tender beef in a rich coconut milk and tomato sauce with the full coastal spice array. The coastal version of beef stew — richer, more aromatic, and more deeply satisfying.

The coconut milk in this curry does two things: it makes the sauce richer and creamier, and it slightly sweetens the beef stew, producing a depth of flavour that plain tomato-based stew cannot match. It is the coastal version of mchuzi wa nyama.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 800g beef — cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown beef. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes until thick.
  4. Return beef. Add water. Simmer covered 55—60 minutes until very tender.
  5. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 10 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Never add coconut milk before the beef is tender.
  • Simmer gently after adding coconut milk.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Early coconut milk addition.
  • Hard boiling after coconut milk.

Cultural Note

Beef coconut curry is how the Swahili coast transforms the everyday beef stew into something extraordinary — the addition of coconut milk is the coastal signature that makes everything better.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004)
