🌿 Bench Mechi

ሜጪ — Mechi

Bench Mechi — a coarse maize and bean combination cooked together with wild herbs and leafy greens from the Bench Maji forest. The food of the coffee forest communities — simple, nutritious, and connected to the forest ecosystem.

The Bench people live in the Bench Maji Zone of southwestern Ethiopia — forested highland country that is part of the wild coffee biome. Their food reflects their forest environment: maize, beans, and wild forest herbs.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 1 cup maize flour (coarse)
  • ½ cup cooked beans
  • 200g leafy greens — collard or wild greens
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups water

Method

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil until golden.
  2. Add maize flour and water. Cook 15 minutes stirring.
  3. Add beans. Cook 10 more minutes.
  4. Add leafy greens. Cook 8 minutes.
  5. Season with black pepper and salt.

Chef Tips

  • Wild forest herbs are the Bench signature — use whatever leafy greens are available.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Refined maize flour — use coarse for texture.

The Science

Coarse maize flour retains more of the pericarp (outer layer) — higher fibre, more nutrients, more complex texture than fine maize meal.

Health Benefits

  • Combined maize and beans — complete protein.

Cultural Note

Bench Mechi is the food of the coffee forest — the people who live among wild coffee trees eat the simplest food their environment provides.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: At Bench community meals, As an everyday staple
