🍢 Kofta al Hatee (Egyptian Grilled Kofta Skewers)

Kofta al-Hatee — كفتة الحاتي

Beef and lamb kneaded with onion puree, garlic and parsley until it forms threads, shaped onto skewers, brushed with an onion-juice glaze and grilled.

2hTotal time
30mPrep
30mCook
6Servings
390Calories
MediumDifficulty

A beef and lamb blend is kneaded with pureed onion solids, garlic, parsley and tomato paste until thin threads of meat pull away, then chilled, shaped around skewers and brushed with a glaze made from the onion juice. The skewers are grilled on medium-high heat for 10 to 15 minutes, until browned all over and cooked through, then rested.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or food processor, Fine sieve, Wooden or metal skewers, Grill or broiler

  • 600 grams ground beef, 85% lean and 15% fat
  • 400 grams ground lamb, 85% lean and 15% fat
  • 1 onion puree, only solids, as we will use the juice in the glaze (read directions)
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup parsley leaves, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste or pepper paste
  • 1 chili pepper, red or green, finely chopped, optional
  • 2 teaspoon pink salt
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 ½ teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder

Glaze

  • The juice of 1 medium onion
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • ¼ teaspoon fine salt
  • Pinch of ground pepper

Smoke (optional)

  • 1 piece of natural charcoal
  • 2 tablespoons hot vegetable oil

Method

  1. Puree the onion and sieve it, keeping the juice for the glaze and the solids for the meat.
  2. Combine both meats with the onion solids, garlic, baking powder, tomato paste, parsley, chili and spices. Knead until thin threads of meat begin to come away. The page does not print a kneading time.
  3. Chill the mixture 30 minutes, or overnight. Soak wooden skewers at least 30 minutes.
  4. Shape golf-ball portions onto the skewers into 4 to 5 inch lengths and chill at least 30 minutes more.
  5. Mix the glaze, brush the kofta, and grill over medium-high heat for about 10 to 15 minutes, until browned on all sides and cooked through. Rest a few minutes.
  6. Optional smoke: put a piece of charcoal in a small bowl, pour hot oil on it and cover the kofta with it; the page does not print a smoking time.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: knead until thin threads come out of the meat. Chill times are NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The page offers a broiler alternative but prints no unit for its temperature, so check your oven.
  • The page gives no yield; servings are our estimate.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using lean meat; the 15% fat is part of the texture.
  • Skipping the chill, so the kofta slip off the skewers (our caution).

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour describes El Dahan, a grill house in Old Cairo said to date to the late 19th century, as a reference for this style (single reading).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked kofta up to 3 days (our estimate; the page gives none).

Reheating: Warm in a skillet or oven until hot through.

Serve with: Egyptian tahini sauce, Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad), Aish baladi
