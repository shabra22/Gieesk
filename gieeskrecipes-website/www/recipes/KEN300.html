Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple sufurias and pots, Charcoal jiko for nyama choma, Large communal platters, Long table for the feast

    The Core — Traditional Kenya

    • Ugali — 2 large pots
    • Sukuma Wiki — large pan
    • Pilau — 1 large sufuria
    • Beef Stew — 1 pot
    • Bean Stew — 1 pot
    • Kachumbari — 2 large bowls

    The Grill — Kenya Over Fire

    • Nyama Choma — 2 kg goat and beef
    • Mishkaki — 30 skewers

    The Coast — Swahili Kenya

    • Wali wa Nazi — 1 pot
    • Mchuzi wa Samaki — 1 large pot
    • Bhajia — 2 large bowls
    • Mahamri — 30 pieces

    The Condiments

    • Pili Pili Sauce — 4 small bowls
    • Coconut Chutney — 2 bowls
    • Avocado Salsa — 2 bowls

    The Finale

    • Fresh Kenyan Masala Chai — for 12
    • Festival Fruit Punch — 2 jugs

    Method

    1. Day 1 — Prepare: make pilau sauce base, marinate nyama choma, prepare bhajia batter, make pili pili sauce and chutneys.
    2. Day 2 Morning — Cook: start pilau, simmer bean stew, cook beef stew.
    3. Day 2 Midday — Grill: light the charcoal. Cook the nyama choma slowly — this is the heart of the feast.
    4. Day 2 — As guests arrive: fry bhajia, make mahamri, cook mishkaki on the fire, steam ugali and sukuma wiki.
    5. Day 2 — Final: cook wali wa Nazi, warm mchuzi wa samaki, prepare all fresh elements (kachumbari, avocado salsa).
    6. Set the table with everything simultaneously — all dishes on the table at once, family style.
    7. Brew masala chai. Fill punch jugs.
    8. Eat together. Share. Celebrate Kenya.

    Chef Tips

    • The staging is everything — this feast requires 2 days and organised cooking.
    • The nyama choma fire is the heart of the occasion — start it first and let guests gather around it.
    • Every dish should be served simultaneously — family style, everything on the table at once.
    • More food than you think you need. Always. This is Kenya.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Trying to cook everything on the day — impossible.
    • Serving individually plated — the GieesK Recipes platter is communal.
    • Not enough kachumbari — it always runs out first.
