🍲 Zuppa di Ceci e Castagne

Zuppa di Ceci e Castagne

A warming Molisan soup combining chickpeas with sweet roasted chestnuts, a pairing that reflects the region's mountainous terrain and reliance on both legumes and forest foods.

Molise, one of Italy's smallest and least internationally known regions, has a cuisine built around its rugged, mountainous landscape, and this soup's pairing of humble chickpeas with chestnuts gathered from surrounding forests reflects that resourceful, foraged tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 300 g (1½ cups) dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) cooked, peeled chestnuts
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 1.5 L (6 cups) vegetable stock
  • 60 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Drain the soaked chickpeas and simmer in fresh water until nearly tender, about 45 minutes, then drain.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a large pot and sauté the onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and rosemary and cook briefly.
  4. Add the chickpeas and stock, and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the chestnuts, roughly broken up, and simmer for another 10–15 minutes until everything is tender and the flavors have melded.
  6. For a thicker texture, mash a portion of the chickpeas and chestnuts directly in the pot.
  7. Season to taste and serve hot with a generous drizzle of olive oil.

Chef Tips

  • Pre-cooked, vacuum-packed chestnuts work well and save considerable time over roasting and peeling your own.
  • Mashing a portion of the soup directly in the pot gives it a satisfying, hearty texture without needing a blender.
  • This soup keeps and reheats well, making it an excellent make-ahead option.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using canned chickpeas without developing enough of their own cooking liquid for flavor, if starting from dried isn't possible.
  • Adding the chestnuts too early, causing them to break down completely rather than retaining some texture.
  • Under-seasoning, given the mild base flavors of chickpeas and chestnuts need a confident hand with salt and herbs.

Cultural Note

This soup reflects Molise's rustic mountain cuisine, a lesser-known Italian region whose food traditions center heavily on legumes, chestnuts, and other foraged ingredients.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 4 days; flavor improves the next day.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop, adding water or stock to loosen as needed.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust red wine
