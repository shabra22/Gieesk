🫕 Hadiya Mich

ሚጭ — Mich

Hadiya Mich — a thick barley and vegetable porridge from the Hadiya people of central southern Ethiopia. Spiced with black pepper and served with buttermilk. The everyday meal that sustained the agricultural Hadiya community.

The Hadiya people cultivate barley and sorghum in the highlands south of Hosanna. Mich is their traditional thick barley porridge, enriched with vegetables and served with fresh buttermilk — a complete meal from the agricultural tradition.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan

  • 1 cup barley flour
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 carrot — diced
  • 1 small potato — diced
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt

Serving: fresh buttermilk or yogurt

    Method

    1. Cook carrot and potato in water until soft.
    2. Whisk in barley flour gradually.
    3. Cook stirring 15—20 minutes until thick.
    4. Season with pepper and salt.
    5. Serve with buttermilk alongside.

    Chef Tips

    • The buttermilk is not optional — it is the Hadiya tradition.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Omitting the buttermilk.
