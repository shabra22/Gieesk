🌿 Sauce de woulahada (Zom Leaf Sauce with Smoked Fish)

Sauce de woulahada / zom

Fresh woulahada (zom) leaves boiled 10 minutes in natron water to keep them green, added to a fried smoked-fish base with peanut paste, and simmered 15–20 minutes.

p. 60 of the October 2023 recipe book. The method follows the foleré sauce, with fresh woulahada (zom) leaves boiled 10 minutes in water with natron — the book says natron keeps the sauce green — then added to the fried onion, smoked-fish, tomato and spice base with roasted peanut paste, and simmered 15–20 minutes. No quantities are printed. ‘Zom’ is also the nightshade leaf in the Afrik sanga page; the book calls it woulahada (zom) and neither page states its botanical identity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot

  • Fresh woulahada (zom) leaves — quantity not printed
  • Smoked fish — quantity not printed
  • Roasted peanut paste — quantity not printed
  • Rock salt (natron/kanwa) — quantity not printed
  • Water, oil, onion, garlic, tomatoes, bell pepper, leek, parsley, celery, white pepper, ginger, salt, chilli — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Boil the leaves 10 minutes in water with natron. Drain.
  2. Fry the onion. Add the fish, tomatoes, garlic, green condiments and spices and simmer.
  3. Add the leaves.
  4. Add water, the peanut paste and salt.
  5. Simmer 15–20 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The printed reason for the natron is to keep the sauce green.
  • No other doneness cue is printed.
  • The book prints no quantities.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the natron and losing the colour.
  • Over-cooking the leaves.
  • Assuming the botanical identity of zom.
