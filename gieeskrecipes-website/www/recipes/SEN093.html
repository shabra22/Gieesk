🌶️ Kanni, Hot Sauce for Accara

Kanni / kani

Onions cooked in olive oil, tomato paste, water, habaneros, a splash of white vinegar and cayenne simmered about 20 minutes, then finished with a spoonful and a half of nokkos garlic paste — the hot sauce served beside accara.

Chef Marie-Claude Mendy’s kanni is made in two parts. The nokkos — Senegalese garlic paste — is a puree of garlic, bell pepper, green onions, yellow onion, salt, pepper and cayenne. The sauce is built in a pan: green and yellow onions cooked in olive oil about 5 minutes, tomato paste added and cooked 3 to 4 minutes over medium heat, then water, black pepper, chopped habaneros, white vinegar and cayenne added and the heat lowered for about 20 minutes. One and a half tablespoons of the nokkos are stirred in at the end. Served beside accara, or spread on baguette slices as sandwiches. The page’s stated total time of 10 minutes cannot be squared with its method (about 29 minutes of stated cooking); both are recorded and not reconciled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Medium pan

    For the nokkos (Senegalese garlic paste)

    • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
    • 1 bell pepper, cubed — size not printed
    • 1 bunch green onions — size not printed
    • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
    • Salt — quantity not printed
    • Pepper — quantity and type not printed
    • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

    For the sauce

    • 2 tablespoons olive oil
    • 1/2 bunch green onions
    • 2 medium yellow onions, diced
    • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
    • 1 cup water
    • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper (printed ‘¼ teaspoons’)
    • 2 habanero peppers, finely chopped (or another hot pepper)
    • 1 teaspoon white vinegar (printed ‘1 teaspoons’)
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons cayenne pepper
    • Salt, to taste — quantity not printed

    Method

      Make the nokkos

      1. Process the garlic, bell pepper, green onions and yellow onion in a food processor with the salt, pepper and cayenne until pureed. No time and no yield are printed for the nokkos; you will use only 1 1/2 tablespoons of it.

      Make the sauce

      1. Heat the olive oil in a medium pan. Cook the green and yellow onions, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes. Add the tomato paste.
      2. Keep cooking over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring constantly.
      3. Add the water, black pepper, habaneros, white vinegar and cayenne. Lower the heat and cook about 20 minutes. Stir in 1 1/2 tablespoons of the nokkos.
      4. Season with salt to taste. Serve beside accara, or spread on baguette slices to make sandwiches.

      Chef Tips

      • The only printed cue for the nokkos is ‘until pureed’; the sauce has times, not cues.
      • The page prints a total time of 10 minutes; the steps add up to about 29, so plan for the longer figure.
      • The page uses the spelling ‘nokkos’ in the ingredient heading and ‘Nokoss’ in step 4; both are the same garlic paste.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Trusting the 10-minute total on the page.
      • Adding the whole batch of nokkos instead of the 1 1/2 tablespoons the recipe calls for.
      • Confusing this nokkos with nokoss, the onion-and-nététou base.

      Cultural Note

      The nokkos is made here as a stand-alone paste, then used by the spoonful in the sauce. Spelling is unsettled across sources — nokkos (Mendy, Ba), nokos (Thiam), nokoss — and the preparations differ; they are recorded as separate entries.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: No storage time is printed.

      Reheating: Serve warm or at room temperature.

      Serve with: Accara (Accara, Black-Eyed Pea Fritters, Accara, Black-Eyed Pea Fritters from West Africa), Baguette sandwiches, Sosu kaani
