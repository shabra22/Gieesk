🎉 Zanzibar New Year Feast

Sherehe ya Mwaka Kogwa

Zanzibar Mwaka Kogwa Feast — the menu of Zanzibar's most ancient celebration: Zanzibar Pilau, Biriani, Nyama Choma, Halwa, Kashata, and the full array of Swahili coast dishes. A guide to assembling the complete Zanzibar celebration feast for the most important occasions.

The Mwaka Kogwa feast represents the complete Zanzibar food tradition assembled for the most important occasion of the year. This recipe is really a menu guide — showing how to combine the individual dishes into a coherent celebration feast. The centrepiece is always the pilau or biriani; the accompaniments include grilled meats, coastal salads, sweet coconut confections, and the universal pili pili.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Makunduchi, southern Zanzibar — the Shirazi Persian New Year celebration

Mwaka Kogwa is Zanzibar's most ancient celebration — the Shirazi Persian New Year observed in the village of Makunduchi each July. Men engage in symbolic battles with banana stalks to settle the year's grievances; women parade through the village in their finest kanga cloth. The feast that follows is the greatest meal of the Zanzibar year — pilau, biriani, grilled meats, coconut sweets, and the full expression of the island's food culture.

Ingredients

Equipment: Everything — this is a full day's cooking

    The Mwaka Kogwa Feast Menu:

    • Zanzibar Pilau — the centrepiece rice
    • Zanzibar Biryani — for the grandest tables
    • Nyama Choma / Goat Roast — grilled celebration meat
    • Samaki wa Kupaka — coconut basted fish
    • Kachumbari — the fresh salad
    • Chatni ya Nazi — coconut chutney
    • Pili Pili — the hot sauce
    • Halwa ya Zanzibar — the celebration sweet
    • Kashata — coconut candy
    • Kaimati — doughnut balls
    • Kahawa ya Zanzibar — spiced coffee
    • Sherbet ya Ukwaju — tamarind drink

    Method

    1. Day before: marinate meats for pilau and biriani. Make halwa. Fry onions for layering.
    2. Morning of feast: begin the pilau or biriani base. Prepare goat for roasting.
    3. Midday: assemble and dum the biriani/pilau. Begin goat roast over charcoal.
    4. Two hours before serving: prepare kachumbari, chatni, pili pili, and kashata.
    5. One hour before: begin samaki wa kupaka grilling.
    6. Final hour: complete all components. Assemble the feast table.
    7. Break the biriani dum seal at the table — the ceremony that signals the feast begins.
