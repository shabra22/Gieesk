🥘 Ethiopian Chicken with Vegetables

ዶሮ ከአትክልት — Doro ke-Atkilit

Ethiopian Chicken and Vegetables — chicken pieces simmered in a mild Ethiopian spice sauce with potatoes, carrots, and tomatoes. The balanced family meal — Ethiopian spices applied to the classic chicken-and-vegetable one-pot, suitable for children and those preferring mild food.

Doro ke-Atkilit is the family-friendly chicken dish — the Ethiopian spice tradition applied to a mild, vegetable-rich stew that everyone at the table can enjoy. Less ceremonial than Doro Wat, more practical for everyday family meals.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 700g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 3 medium potatoes — cubed
  • 2 carrots — sliced
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp berbere
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Brown chicken in oil. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, berbere, tomatoes.
  3. Return chicken. Add water. Simmer covered 30 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer 20 more minutes.
  5. Vegetables should be tender; chicken falling-tender.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add potatoes and carrots after the chicken has been cooking 30 minutes.
  • Very light berbere — this is intentionally mild for family eating.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding vegetables too early.

Cultural Note

Doro ke-Atkilit is the Ethiopian family dinner — the dish that includes everyone at the table, from the youngest child to the eldest. Ethiopian cooking's remarkable capacity to be both ceremonially significant (Doro Wat) and practically family-friendly (this dish) is one of its greatest strengths.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), As a standalone meal for children
