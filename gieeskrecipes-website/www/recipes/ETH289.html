🥑 Ethiopian Avocado Smoothie

አቮካዶ ስሙዝ — Avocado Smoothie

Ethiopian Avocado Smoothie — ripe Ethiopian highland avocado blended with banana, oat milk, honey, and cardamom. The modern health café version of the avocado juice tradition — thicker, more nutritious, and completely satisfying as a meal replacement.

The avocado smoothie is the evolution of the Ethiopian avocado juice tradition (ETH221) — with banana for sweetness, oat milk for creaminess, and cardamom for the Ethiopian character. The health café breakfast staple of Addis Ababa.

Ingredients

Equipment: High-powered blender

  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup oat milk or regular milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ¼ tsp cardamom
  • Ice

Method

  1. Combine avocado, banana, milk, honey, and cardamom in blender.
  2. Blend until completely smooth.
  3. Add ice. Blend briefly.
  4. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Very ripe avocado and banana are essential.
  • Cardamom is the Ethiopian signature — do not omit.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unripe avocado — bitter.

The Science

The banana's natural sugars and the avocado's fat create a self-emulsifying smoothie — no added oil or emulsifier needed.

Cultural Note

The Ethiopian avocado smoothie is Addis Ababa's health café contribution to the global smoothie culture — Ethiopian highland avocado (larger and richer than imported varieties) produces a smoothie of extraordinary creaminess that has no global equivalent.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a breakfast, Post-exercise recovery drink
