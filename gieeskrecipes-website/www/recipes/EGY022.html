🍚 Sayadiya (Egyptian Fisherman’s Rice)

Sayadiya / Ruz sayadeya — صيادية

Rice sautéed, then cooked in a deeply caramelized onion broth with cumin, cinnamon, tomato paste and peppers; the classic accompaniment to fried fish or seafood.

40mTotal time
5mPrep
35mCook
4Servings

MediumDifficulty

Rinsed, drained rice is sautéed 2 minutes and set aside. Onion is caramelized on medium-low until evenly browned, then boiling water, spices, tomato paste, peppers, chili, garlic and bouillon are added and simmered 1 minute. The rice goes in, cooks uncovered on medium-high for 3 to 5 minutes until most liquid evaporates, then 15 to 20 minutes covered on low. The page gives the rice only; serve it with fish or seafood.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pot with lid

  • 2 cups Egyptian rice
  • 1 big yellow onion, or 2 mediums ones
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1 green bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili pepper
  • 1 tablespoon Tomato Paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 bay leave
  • 1 vegetable Bouillon
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • (Olive oil and boiling water; amounts not printed)

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and drain it completely until dry. Sauté it in a little oil for 2 minutes and set aside. Never soak Egyptian rice, says the page.
  2. In olive oil on medium-low, caramelize the finely chopped onion, stirring, until evenly caramelized to the colour in the page's photo. Onions burn easily.
  3. Add boiling water, the spices, tomato paste, peppers, chili, garlic and bouillon. Let the broth simmer 1 minute.
  4. Add the rice and cook uncovered on medium-high for 3 to 5 minutes until most of the liquid evaporates.
  5. Reduce to low, cover and cook 15 to 20 minutes. Serve with fried fish or seafood.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: rice dry after draining; onion evenly caramelized; most of the liquid evaporated. Amounts of oil and boiling water are NOT PRINTED on the card.
  • The page contains no fish recipe; its tip about sautéing onions contradicts the method, which sautés the rice (single reading).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the onions, which taints the whole pot.
  • Soaking the rice.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour describes it as 'a world of nostalgia in every fragrant bite'; her mother cooked it simply and her late father chose the best fish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days; freezer up to 3 weeks (page).

Reheating: Reheat covered with a splash of water.

Serve with: Fried fish, Bran-coated fish, Fried shrimp
