🦐 Gambari Iskandarani (Alexandrian Garlic-Butter Broiled Shrimp)

Gambari Eskandarani — جمبري إسكندراني

Head-on shrimp tossed in garlic-spice butter, topped with onion, lemon and bay, and broiled hot until pink, then rested under foil.

1h 5mTotal time

EasyDifficulty

Large shrimp are sprinkled with white vinegar and left briefly, then rinsed and dried. Cold butter is melted until foamy and cooked with garlic and spices until fragrant, tossed with the shrimp, and topped with garlic, onion, lemon and bay leaves. The page sets the broiler to 425°F for 20 to 30 minutes, until all the shrimp turn pink.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking dish, Saucepan, Foil

  • 1 kg large shrimp skin and head-on
  • ½ cup butter, cold
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon coriander
  • 1 teaspoon smoked or sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dry oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or cayenne pepper

Assembly

  • 5 bay leaves
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • White vinegar for the drizzle (amount not printed)

Method

  1. Drizzle the shrimp with white vinegar and let sit 10 minutes (the card; the body says 15). Rinse and dry.
  2. Melt the butter on medium-low; when foamy add the garlic and spices. Turn the heat off when fragrant.
  3. Toss the shrimp in the butter and top with garlic, sliced onion, lemon and bay leaves.
  4. Set the broiler to 425°F and broil for 20 to 30 minutes, or until all the shrimp turn pink (the page prints 'oil' for 'all').
  5. Rest under foil 5 minutes (card).

Chef Tips

  • Cues: butter foamy then spices fragrant; shrimp pink. The vinegar sit is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The 20 to 30 minute broil is long for shrimp, as printed; start checking early (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking, which toughens the shrimp.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour ate it growing up at home and at seafood stalls on the Mediterranean coast and recalls the restaurant Adoura in Alexandria.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked shrimp up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently; shrimp toughen quickly.

Serve with: Sayadiya rice, Aish baladi
