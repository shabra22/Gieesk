The modern Ethiopian dessert platter bridges the traditional (sesame candy, honey banana) and the contemporary (coffee cheesecake, coffee truffles) in one presentation that tells the complete story of Ethiopian sweet culture.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple preparations, Large serving board

  • Coffee Cheesecake — sliced
  • Coffee Truffles
  • Honey Banana
  • Sesame Candy
  • Mini Teff Pancakes
  • Milk Pudding

Method

  1. Days before: cheesecake, sesame candy.
  2. Day before: coffee truffles.
  3. Day of: pancakes, honey banana, milk pudding.
  4. Arrange on a large board: ancient sweets one side, modern creations other side.

Chef Tips

  • The ancient-to-modern arrangement tells the story visually.
  • Honey banana must be served warm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold honey banana.
