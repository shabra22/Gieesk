🍲 Maraket Ferakh (Egyptian Mastic-Scented Chicken Broth)

Maraket ferakh — مرقة فراخ

A whole cut-up chicken seared with garlic, ginger, mastic and whole spices and simmered on low for two hours into a clear, fragrant broth.

3h 50mTotal time

A seasoned chicken is seared in ghee with whole spices, onion and garlic until the skin is crisp and golden, covered with boiling water and simmered undisturbed on low for two hours, until fork-tender. It cools 30 minutes and is strained. The base for many Egyptian dishes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot with lid, Fine sieve

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 whole chicken cut in pieces, sprinkled with salt and pepper
  • 1 big onion, halved
  • 10 whole garlic cloves
  • 1 piece ginger, 1 X inch, whole and peeled
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 3 cardamom pods, bruised
  • 2 mastic resins
  • 5 whole allspice berries
  • 10 whole black peppercorn
  • 1 teaspoon 7 spices
  • A sprig of rosemary or time, optional
  • Stalks of fresh parsley
  • 2-4 cups of leftovers of uncooked peppers, tomatoes, celery, parsley, cilantro
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 Liters of boiling water

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper at least 1 hour ahead, preferably overnight in the fridge.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee and stir the whole spices for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Sear the onion and garlic, then the chicken until the skin is crisp and golden.
  4. Add the boiling water, vegetable scraps and herbs, cover, and simmer undisturbed on low for two hours.
  5. Check that the chicken is fork-tender; if not, cook 30 minutes more. Cool 30 minutes and strain.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: spices fragrant; skin crisp and golden; chicken fork-tender. The pre-seasoning is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The ingredient list says butter and the method says ghee. The page says avoid adding cold water at any cost; this is a flavour claim, not a food-safety rule.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding cold water, which the author says dulls flavour.
  • Boiling hard instead of a low simmer.
