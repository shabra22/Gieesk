🥔 Artichoke, Baby Potato and Preserved Lemon Tagine

Tajine de artichauts, pommes de terre et citron confit

A vegan tagine in which artichoke bottoms and baby potatoes simmer in an onion-turmeric broth with chopped preserved lemon, peas and olives.

2h 15mTotal time
45mPrep
1h 30mCook
4Servings
300Calories
MediumDifficulty

Benkabbou says most tagines she grew up with contained meat, and this one is so good no one notices. Onions soften in olive oil with garlic, turmeric, ginger, salt and pepper; stock goes in and simmers 30 minutes to make a broth; baby potatoes and artichoke bottoms (with lemon to keep them from browning) cook 25-30 minutes; peas finish for about 3 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide casserole or tagine with lid

  • 10 fresh or ready-prepared frozen globe artichoke bottoms (600g)
  • ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • ¾ tsp salt, or more to taste
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper
  • 300ml vegetable stock
  • 400g baby potatoes, scrubbed and halved
  • 2 small preserved lemons (60g), flesh and rind finely chopped
  • 130g frozen peas
  • 100g drained red-brown olives, such as Kalamata
  • Roughly chopped fresh coriander, to garnish
  • Crusty bread or couscous, to serve

Method

  1. If using fresh artichokes, trim to the bottoms and rub with the cut lemon; frozen ones can go straight in.
  2. Heat the oil, add the onions, cover and cook about 10 minutes until soft and translucent, stirring occasionally.
  3. Add garlic, turmeric, ginger, salt and pepper; stir in the stock and simmer gently for 30 minutes to obtain a broth.
  4. Add the potatoes, artichokes and chopped preserved lemon; simmer gently 25-30 minutes until the artichoke bottoms and potatoes are cooked through and soft.
  5. Add the peas and olives and simmer about 3 minutes until tender. Garnish with coriander and serve with bread or couscous.

Chef Tips

  • Frozen artichoke bottoms save a lot of prep time and work well here.
  • Check salt late: preserved lemon and olives bring plenty.
  • Do not overcook the peas; 3 minutes is enough.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting fresh artichokes brown; rub with lemon immediately.
  • Cutting the potatoes too small so they dissolve.
  • Boiling too hard, which breaks up the artichokes.

Health Benefits

  • Artichokes and peas add fibre and plant protein; the dish is vegan with no meat or dairy.
  • Olive oil and olives provide unsaturated fats.

Cultural Note

The author notes that most tagines she grew up eating contained meat, and presents this vegan version as one nobody misses meat in.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stove; add water if the sauce has thickened.

Serve with: Khobz, Couscous
