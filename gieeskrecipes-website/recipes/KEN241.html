🥤 Kenyan Coffee Milkshake

Milkshake ya Kahawa

Kenyan Coffee Milkshake — rich Kenyan single-origin coffee blended with vanilla ice cream, whole milk, and a drizzle of honey. A celebration of Kenya's world-class coffee in its most indulgent liquid form.

A Kenyan coffee milkshake made with Kenya AA or Nyeri single-origin coffee is something extraordinary — the fruit-forward brightness and complex flavour of Kenyan coffee creates a milkshake with more character than any other. Blended with vanilla ice cream and a touch of honey, it is the perfect afternoon treat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Tall glasses

  • 2 shots strong brewed Kenyan coffee — chilled
  • 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Ice — 6 cubes
  • Whipped cream — for topping

Method

  1. Brew strong Kenyan coffee and cool completely — or use cold brew.
  2. Combine coffee, ice cream, milk, honey, vanilla, and ice in a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth and thick.
  4. Pour into tall glasses.
  5. Top with whipped cream.
  6. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Chilled coffee is essential — hot coffee melts the ice cream and ruins the texture.
  • The coffee flavour should be pronounced — do not dilute with too much milk.
  • Kenyan single-origin coffee is dramatically better than blended coffee here.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Warm coffee — melts ice cream too fast.
  • Too much milk — loses the coffee intensity.

The Science

Blending ice cream with cold liquid creates a temporary emulsion of air, fat, and liquid — the air incorporated during blending gives the milkshake its characteristic thick but pourable texture. Over-blending collapses the air bubbles, producing a denser result.

A Kenyan coffee milkshake made with Kenya's legendary single-origin beans is a global first-class experience — the fruit-forward brightness of Nyeri AA or Kirinyaga beans transforms a simple milkshake into something genuinely world-class.
