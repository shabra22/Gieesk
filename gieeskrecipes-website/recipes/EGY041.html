🥗 Oriental Salad (Ahram Online Ramadan Recipe)

Salata sharqi — سلطة شرقي

Lettuce, cucumbers, arugula, tomatoes, onion, parsley and radish in a lemon, white vinegar and olive oil dressing.

15mTotal time
15mPrep
6Servings
70Calories
EasyDifficulty

A big mixed salad from a Ramadan column: a head of lettuce, four cucumbers, two packs of arugula, three tomatoes, an onion, parsley and a radish, dressed with the juice of a lemon, a tablespoon each of white vinegar and olive oil, salt and pepper. The page gives no times.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl

  • 1 head lettuce
  • 4 cucumbers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 packs arugula
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 pack parsley
  • 1 radish

Dressing

  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Chop the lettuce, cucumbers, onion, arugula, tomatoes, parsley and radish and combine in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the lemon juice, vinegar, olive oil, salt and pepper together.
  3. Toss the dressing with the salad just before serving.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no pack sizes or cutting instructions; adjust to taste (our note).
  • Dress at the last moment to keep the leaves crisp.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dressing early so the leaves wilt.
