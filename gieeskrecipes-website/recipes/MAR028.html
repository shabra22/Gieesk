🍅 Hssoua Belboula Hamra (Cracked Barley Soup with Tomatoes)

Hssoua belboula hamra — red barley soup (حسوة بلبولة حمراء)

A tomato-red soup of cracked barley with a handful of diced beef or lamb, grated onion, parsley and ginger.

1hTotal time
15mPrep
45mCook
4Servings
300Calories
EasyDifficulty

Barley grits are rinsed until the water is no longer cloudy. Onion and meat are cooked in oil until the onion is translucent and the meat browned, then the tomatoes, parsley, spices and water are added and simmered 15 to 20 minutes. Tomato paste and barley go in for about 15 minutes more until the barley and meat are tender and the broth slightly thickened.

Ingredients

Equipment: 3-quart pot

  • 1 cup cracked barley or barley grits (medium or large caliber)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 oz. beef or lamb, diced (one handful)
  • 1 onion, grated (medium)
  • 2 or 3 tomatoes, grated (seeds and skin discarded)
  • 3 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1.5 tsp salt, or to taste (less if using stock)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric (optional)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste, or to taste (optional)
  • 8.5 cups water (can use stock or broth instead)

Method

  1. Rinse the grits repeatedly until the water is no longer cloudy when swishing the barley around.
  2. In a 3-quart pot, heat the oil over medium heat. Cook the onion and meat until the onion is translucent and the meat browned.
  3. Add the tomatoes, parsley, spices and water. Boil on high, then simmer 15 to 20 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato paste and barley and simmer about 15 minutes more, until the barley and meat are tender and the broth slightly thickened.

Chef Tips

  • Large grits (dchicha) give the most chew.
  • Best served immediately: the barley keeps swelling and thickening.
  • Add a splash of water if reheating.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the rinse.
  • Leaving it to sit before serving, so it turns to paste.
  • Using too fine a grit.

Health Benefits

  • Whole-grain barley and tomatoes; low in fat.
  • A handful of meat flavours the pot without making it heavy.

Cultural Note

The author writes that large barley grits, called dchicha, are best for a chewy bite.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Reheating: Loosen with water and warm gently.

Serve with: Khobz
