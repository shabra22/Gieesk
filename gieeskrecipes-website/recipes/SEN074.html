🍢 Dibi Hausa, Senegalese Grilled Beef Kebabs

Dibi Hausa

Strips of round steak marinated 10 to 15 minutes in onion, tomato paste, ginger and white pepper, threaded on skewers, rolled in tankora (spiced peanut powder) and grilled 12 to 14 minutes.

A quick West African barbecue. A cup of roasted unsalted peanuts is crushed to a fine powder and mixed with ground ginger, salt, white pepper and cayenne — the tankora. A pound of round steak cut into strips about 1½ by 2 by ⅜ inches is tossed with grated onion, tomato paste, garlic, ginger, an optional bouillon cube, oil and seasonings and marinated 10 to 15 minutes, then threaded on eight 6-inch skewers, rolled in the tankora, brushed with extra marinade and grilled, turning and basting, until seared and browned all over and cooked through, 12 to 14 minutes. The recipe was adapted from Thiam’s beef kebabs and appeared in Adrian Miller’s book Black Smoke.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grill (charcoal or gas), Eight 6-inch skewers, Wide plate, Pastry brush

    Tankora powder

    • 1 cup roasted, unsalted peanuts, crushed to a fine powder
    • 1 tsp ground ginger
    • 1 tsp kosher salt
    • 1 tsp freshly ground white pepper
    • 1/2 tsp ground cayenne pepper

    Kebabs

    • 1 lb round steak, sliced into strips about 1 1/2 by 2 by 3/8 inches
    • 2 tbsp plus 1 1/2 tsp finely grated white or yellow onion
    • 1 tbsp tomato paste
    • 1/2 tsp finely chopped garlic
    • 1/4 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
    • 1 chicken bouillon cube, crumbled (optional)
    • 1 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil
    • 1 tsp freshly ground white pepper
    • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
    • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

    Method

    1. Stir the peanut powder, ginger, salt, white pepper and cayenne together in a small bowl and set aside.
    2. Heat the grill to high (no temperature printed). In a large bowl, combine the steak, onion, tomato paste, garlic, grated ginger and bouillon cube if using. Add the oil, white pepper, cayenne and salt, toss to coat and marinate 10 to 15 minutes.
    3. Thread a few pieces of meat on each of the eight skewers. Roll each skewer in the tankora spread on a wide plate and shake off the excess. Brush with a little reserved marinade.
    4. Grill, turning occasionally and basting, for 12 to 14 minutes, until the meat is seared and browned all over and cooked through. Serve hot or warm.

    Chef Tips

    • If using bamboo skewers, soak them in water for an hour first (printed).
    • The tankora spice mix keeps up to two months airtight at room temperature.
    • The doneness cue is visual: seared and browned all over and cooked through.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Marinating for hours — the strips are thin and the page prints 10 to 15 minutes.
    • Skipping the tankora: it is what makes the kebab dibi hausa.
    • Cutting the strips thicker than the printed ⅜ inch.

    Health Benefits

    • Round steak is lean; peanuts add protein and healthy fat.
    • Contains peanuts — a common allergen.

    Cultural Note

    The page calls this ‘a great way to get a taste of West African barbecue in a short period of time’ and ties it to Adrian Miller’s Black Smoke. The page makes no mention of the dibiterie tradition; for that see Dibi, Grilled Lamb and Onions with Mustard.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

    Reheating: Warm briefly on a grill pan or in a hot oven.

    Serve with: Sliced onion, Mustard, Baguette, Tamarind kani mustard sauce
