🌾 Fereek (Egyptian Freekeh Pilaf)

Fereek — فريك

Caramelized onion, garlic and smoky freekeh grains cooked in vegetable broth at about two cups of liquid to one of grain.

50mTotal time
10mPrep
35mCook
4Servings
290Calories
EasyDifficulty

Chopped onions are lightly caramelized in hot olive oil, the freekeh is stirred in for 3 minutes until coated, garlic goes in for a minute until fragrant, then the broth is added and brought to a gentle boil. The grain is simmered covered on low for 20 to 25 minutes, rested covered about 5 minutes and fluffed. The page gives no quantities on its ingredient list, only a ratio of about 2 cups of liquid per 1 cup of freekeh.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan with lid

  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Yellow Onion, finely chopped
  • Minced garlic
  • Freekeh grain
  • Vegetable broth
  • Salt and pepper
  • (Quantities NOT PRINTED; the tips give about 2 cups of liquid for every 1 cup of freekeh)

Method

  1. Lightly caramelize the chopped onions in hot olive oil.
  2. Add the freekeh and stir for 3 minutes until well coated with the oil.
  3. Add the minced garlic and stir for 1 minute or until it is fragrant.
  4. Stir in the broth, about 2 cups for every 1 cup of freekeh, and bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Simmer, covered, on low heat for 20 to 25 minutes, then let it sit covered about 5 minutes and fluff.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: onions lightly caramelized; garlic fragrant. There is no soak; the tips say to rinse only.
  • The step captions say to let the liquid evaporate uncovered on medium-high, while the FAQ says to simmer covered on low 20 to 25 minutes; we follow the FAQ.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using too much liquid; the grain should stay a little chewy.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour presents the proverb 'Ana zay el fereek maḥebesh shareek' as something Egyptians jokingly say, glossed as being like freekeh pilaf: I don't like partners.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat with a splash of water.

Serve with: Grilled pigeon
