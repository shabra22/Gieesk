🦑 Tanzanian Grilled Squid

Ngisi wa Kuchoma

Tanzanian Grilled Squid — whole squid scored and marinated in lemon, garlic, and Zanzibar spices, grilled over charcoal in 6—8 minutes. The beach restaurant favourite — simple, fast, and magnificent when the squid is this fresh.

Grilled squid is the Zanzibar beach restaurant starter everyone orders — whole squid, marinated briefly, grilled quickly over high charcoal. When fresh from the morning's catch and coals at peak, it needs only lemon and pili pili.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill or griddle pan, Knife for scoring

  • 600g whole fresh squid — cleaned, tentacles separated
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Pili pili sauce and lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Score squid tubes in a crosshatch pattern — cuts about 5mm deep.
  2. Combine marinade. Coat squid. Marinate 30 minutes.
  3. Grill over very high heat for 3—4 minutes per side.
  4. The squid curls and chars slightly — this is correct.
  5. Serve immediately with pili pili and lemon.

Chef Tips

  • High heat is essential.
  • Scoring allows even cooking and decorative curl.
  • Fresh squid is sweet; frozen is rubbery — enormous difference.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Low heat — rubbery steamed squid.
  • Overcooking — see the 3 minute rule.

The Science

Scored squid tubes curl towards the scored side when hit with high heat — scored muscle fibres contract faster, creating the characteristic curl. Increases surface area for Maillard browning.

Cultural Note

Grilled squid at a Zanzibar beach restaurant — fresh from the sea, cooked in minutes, eaten with cold drinks and the sound of the ocean.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Pili Pili Sauce, Lemon, Cold Beer or Fresh Juice
