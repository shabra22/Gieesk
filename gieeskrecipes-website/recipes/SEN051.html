🍽️ Senegalese-Style Rice and Fish

Thieboudienne — ceebu jen

A 50-minute home version: six stuffed fillets browned and set on a sweet-potato-and-eggplant tomato sauce, with the rice cooked separately and moistened with a cup of the liquid.

The Whole Foods rendering, ‘inspired by Whole Planet Foundation microcredit client recipes’, is the quickest in the set. Rice simmers separately and rests; each fillet is slit twice and stuffed with a parsley-garlic-cayenne mix and browned about five minutes a side. Onion, carrot and red pepper cook ten minutes; tomato paste cooks a minute; sweet potato, eggplant, broth and tinned tomatoes simmer about 20 minutes; a cup of the liquid is stirred into the rice and the fish finish on the vegetables for three to five minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan with lid, Large deep skillet with lid

  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice, thoroughly rinsed
  • 3 cups water, for the rice — in the method, not the list
  • 3 tbsp finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1/8 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 6 (3-ounce) portions halibut, sea bass or cod fillets
  • 3 tbsp peanut oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, seeded and diced
  • 1/3 cup tomato paste
  • 1 sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1 1/2 cups diced eggplant
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 can (14.5-ounce) diced tomatoes

Method

  1. Put the rice and 3 cups of water in a saucepan, bring to a boil over medium-high, reduce to medium-low, cover and simmer 15 to 20 minutes, until the liquid is completely absorbed and the rice is just tender. Rest covered off the heat 10 minutes and fluff.
  2. Mix the parsley, green onion, garlic, salt and cayenne. Cut 2 deep slits in each fillet and stuff them.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium until hot. Brown the fish about 5 minutes per side. Move to a plate and cover.
  4. In the same skillet cook the onion, carrots and red pepper, stirring occasionally, about 10 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1 minute.
  5. Add the sweet potato, eggplant, broth and tomatoes and bring to a boil over medium-high. Reduce to medium and simmer about 20 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Remove 1 cup of the liquid and stir it into the rice.
  7. Set the fish on top of the vegetables, cover, and cook 3 to 5 minutes more, until the fish is cooked through and very hot.

Chef Tips

  • The 3 cups of rice water appear only in step 1, never in the ingredient list.
  • Rice here cooks separately and is moistened with a cup of the sauce — it is a gentler, drier rice than the one-pot versions.
  • Fish is browned only; the last 3 to 5 minutes on the vegetables finish it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking the fish until it dries out before the final steam.
  • Forgetting the cup of liquid for the rice.
