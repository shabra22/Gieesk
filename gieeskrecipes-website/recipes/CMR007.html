🍽️ Mbongo Tjobi (Afrik)

Mbongo tjobi / Ongo tjobi

Machoiron (or catfish, carp, eel) steaks in a paste of crushed onions, garlic, tomato and djansan with mbongo spices, oil poured on top and cooked about 30 minutes on low heat.

50mTotal time

The Afrik printing describes a plat traditionnel particularly popular among the Bassa people of the Littoral province. Onions, garlic, tomato and djansan are crushed. The fish is washed, gutted, cut into steaks and salted, put in a terrine with the crushed mix, the mbongo spices and a little water, and mixed; it is then moved to a pot, oil poured on top, covered and cooked on low heat about 30 minutes. The only sauce cue is to check its smoothness (onctuosité), thinning with a little water if too thick. Served very hot.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mortar or food processor, Terrine or bowl, Pot with lid

  • 2 kg machoiron (or silure/catfish, carp or eel)
  • 200 g onions
  • 100 g djansan
  • 1 tomato
  • 10 cl oil
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 50 g mbongo spices
  • Salt and stock cubes — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Crush the onions, garlic, tomato and djansan.
  2. Wash, gut and cut the fish into steaks. Salt them.
  3. Put the fish in a terrine with the crushed mix, the mbongo spices and a little water. Taste for seasoning and mix.
  4. Move everything to a pot, pour the oil on top, cover and cook on low heat about 30 minutes.
  5. Check the sauce’s smoothness; thin with a little water if it is too thick. Serve very hot.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cue is the sauce’s smoothness (‘onctuosité’).
  • Thin with a little water if the sauce is too thick.
  • The page does not give a doneness cue for the fish beyond the 30 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the pot dry out — the page cooks it covered on low heat.
  • Using too little salt on the fish steaks before the mix goes on.
  • Forgetting to thin an over-thick sauce.
