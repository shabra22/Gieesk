🍚 Couscous à base de farine de riz (Rice Flour Couscous)

Couscous de riz

Rice soaked about an hour, dried about 2 hours and ground to flour, then cooked as the maize couscous: about 15 minutes after the boil, flour added, 10 more minutes; served hot with a sauce.

220 minTotal time
15 minPrep

EasyDifficulty

p. 12 of the October 2023 recipe book. The rice is soaked in cold water about an hour, dried about 2 hours and ground. The couscous follows the maize method: a flour ‘milk’ in boiling water, about 15 minutes after the boil resumes, then the rice flour is added and cooked 10 more minutes. Served hot with sauce. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Mill or mortar

  • Rice — quantity not printed
  • Water — quantity not printed
  • Cold water for soaking and for the ‘milk’ — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Soak the rice in cold water about an hour. Dry about 2 hours. Grind to flour.
  2. Boil water. Mix a little flour with cold water into a ‘milk’ and pour it in, stirring.
  3. Cook about 15 minutes after the boil resumes.
  4. Add the rice flour and cook 10 more minutes.
  5. Serve hot with a sauce.

Chef Tips

  • The printed note is to serve hot with a sauce.
  • No quantities are printed.
  • The drying time is printed as about 2 hours.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Grinding the rice while still wet.
  • Skipping the drying.
  • Serving cold.
