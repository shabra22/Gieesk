🌽 Gâteaux de maïs (Steamed Maize and Peanut Cakes)

Gâteaux de maïs

Uncrushed, unsoaked maize flour toasted in a very hot pot until golden, mixed with peanut paste, oil, salt, chilli, optional shrimp and a little water, wrapped as balls in banana leaves and steamed about an hour.

p. 6 of the October 2023 recipe book describes a traditional dish of maize flour ‘sweetened, salted and steamed’. The ingredient list and steps, however, contain no sugar. The flour is toasted in a very hot pot to a golden colour and moved to another pot; peanut paste, oil, salt, crushed chilli, optional shrimp and a small amount of water are stirred in to a slightly heavy, homogeneous paste. Small portions are wrapped as balls in banana leaves and steamed about an hour. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Hot pot, Steamer, Banana leaves

  • Maize flour (uncrushed, unsoaked) — quantity not printed
  • Peanut paste — quantity not printed
  • Refined oil — quantity not printed
  • Salt — quantity not printed
  • Crushed chilli — quantity not printed
  • Shrimp (optional) — quantity not printed
  • A small amount of water
  • Banana leaves

Method

  1. Toast the maize flour in a very hot pot until golden. Move it to another pot.
  2. Add the peanut paste, oil, salt, chilli, shrimp and a small amount of water.
  3. Stir to a slightly heavy, homogeneous paste.
  4. Wrap small portions as balls in banana leaves.
  5. Steam about an hour.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: golden colour of the toasted flour; ‘slightly heavy and homogeneous’ paste.
  • The book describes the cake as sweetened but prints no sugar.
  • All quantities are absent — start with a small batch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the flour while toasting.
  • Adding too much water so the balls will not hold.
  • Adding sugar on the strength of the description alone.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The description says ‘sucrée’ and the flow diagram lists sugar and palm oil; the ingredient list prints neither (refined oil only). Reported unreconciled.
