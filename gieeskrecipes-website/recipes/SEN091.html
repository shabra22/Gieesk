🧄 Nokos, Green Seasoning Paste

Nokos / nokkos

Bay leaves, jalapeños, green pepper, black peppercorns, a head of garlic and ginger blended to a thick, wet paste — Thiam’s pounded seasoning, not to be confused with the Wolof onion-and-nététou base nokoss.

10mTotal time

EasyDifficulty

Thiam’s nokos goes into the marinade for his chicken yassa and other dishes. Two bay leaves, two green jalapeños or other chiles, a small green bell pepper, a quarter cup of whole black peppercorns, a head of peeled garlic and a tablespoon of grated ginger are combined in a blender and processed until a thick, wet paste forms. It makes 1 cup. The name is spelled ‘nokos’ by Thiam, ‘nokkos’ by Marie-Claude Mendy and Aissatou Ba and ‘nokoss’ in other printings; all three spellings are preserved. It is not the same preparation as nokoss, the Wolof base of onion and nététou.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 green jalapeño or other chiles, stemmed and chopped
  • 1 small green bell pepper, stemmed, seeded and chopped
  • 1/4 cup whole black peppercorns
  • 1 head garlic, separated and peeled
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger

Method

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a blender.
  2. Process until a thick, wet paste forms.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is ‘until a thick, wet paste forms’ — no time or speed is given.
  • Makes 1 cup; the yassa recipe uses it by the tablespoon.
  • A pestle and mortar gives a coarser paste if you prefer.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding water, which thins the paste.
  • Confusing it with nokoss — different ingredients, different use.
  • Leaving the bay leaves in whole pieces that will not blend.

Health Benefits

  • Garlic and ginger are traditional digestive seasonings.
  • Black pepper adds piperine.

Cultural Note

Spelling is not settled across the research: Thiam ‘nokos’, Mendy ‘nokkos’ in the heading and ‘Nokoss’ in step 4, Ba ‘nokkos’. The three printings are not the same recipe.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The page prints no storage time; refrigerate airtight.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Chicken yassa, Marinades
