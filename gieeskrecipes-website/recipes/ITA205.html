🥩 Bresaola con Rucola e Grana

Bresaola della Valtellina

Delicate, air-dried beef from the Valtellina valley, sliced paper-thin and dressed simply with peppery arugula, shaved Parmigiano, olive oil, and lemon.

Bresaola is beef that's salted and air-cured for months in the cool alpine air of Valtellina, resulting in a lean, deep red cured meat with a delicate flavor best allowed to shine through minimal, bright accompaniments.

Ingredients

Equipment: Serving platter

  • 200 g (7 oz) bresaola, thinly sliced
  • 80 g (3 cups) arugula
  • 60 g (¾ cup) Parmigiano-Reggiano, shaved
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Freshly cracked black pepper

Method

  1. Arrange the bresaola slices in a single layer on a serving platter.
  2. Scatter the arugula evenly over the bresaola.
  3. Top with shaved Parmigiano.
  4. Drizzle generously with olive oil and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  5. Finish with a crack of black pepper and serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Bring the bresaola to room temperature briefly before serving for the best flavor.
  • A good vegetable peeler works well for creating thin, even Parmigiano shavings.
  • Dress the dish only just before serving, so the arugula stays crisp rather than wilting.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Slicing the bresaola too thick, which makes it harder to eat and less delicate.
  • Over-dressing with too much oil or lemon, which can overwhelm the meat's subtle flavor.
  • Using pre-shredded, low-quality Parmigiano instead of a good wedge, shaved fresh.

Health Benefits

  • Bresaola is a lean, low-fat source of protein and iron.

Cultural Note

Bresaola is a protected specialty of the Valtellina valley in Lombardy, its cool alpine climate ideal for the slow air-curing process that gives the beef its delicate character.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best assembled and eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A light red or white wine
