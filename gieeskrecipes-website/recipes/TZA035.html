🐐 Tanzanian Spiced Goat Roast

Mbuzi wa Kukaanga na Viungo

Mbuzi wa Kukaanga na Viungo — whole sections of young goat marinated overnight in a paste of Zanzibar spices, garlic, and ginger, then slow-roasted over charcoal for 2 hours until magnificently charred outside and falling-tender within. The celebration roast of Tanzania.

The spiced goat roast is Tanzania's celebration centrepiece — when the occasion demands the highest expression of hospitality, a whole or half goat is marinated overnight in Zanzibar spices and slow-roasted over charcoal. The combination of the spice paste and the low, slow charcoal heat produces a roast of extraordinary complexity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large charcoal grill or pit, Basting brush, Tongs

  • 1 half young goat (2—3 kg sections)

Overnight Spice Marinade

  • 6 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 3 tsp salt
  • ½ cup fresh coriander — blended in

Method

  1. Score goat sections deeply all over.
  2. Blend all marinade ingredients into a smooth paste.
  3. Rub paste deep into every score and all over the surface.
  4. Marinate overnight (8 hours minimum).
  5. Prepare large charcoal fire. Allow to reach glowing coals.
  6. Roast over medium-low indirect heat for 90—120 minutes, turning every 20—30 minutes.
  7. Move to direct heat in the final 15 minutes for a magnificent char.
  8. Rest 10 minutes. Carve communally.

Chef Tips

  • Overnight marination allows the spice paste to penetrate deep through the scores.
  • Low and slow — the goat must be completely tender before the final char.
  • The final direct heat is what creates the magnificent caramelised crust.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Short marination.
  • Too high heat throughout — burns outside before inside cooks.

The Science

The overnight marinade with its acidic lemon juice partially denatures surface proteins, allowing the spice compounds to penetrate deeper. The extended low-heat roasting converts connective tissue collagen to gelatin, producing falling-tender meat beneath the caramelised spice crust.

Cultural Note

The spiced goat roast is Tanzania's highest expression of celebratory cooking — the dish that marks the most important occasions and demonstrates the greatest hospitality. The overnight preparation and hours of patient roasting signal how much the occasion matters.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Briefly on grill.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Kachumbari, Pili Pili Sauce
