🍊 Orange and Cinnamon Dessert Salad

Shlada dyal limoun — oranges with cinnamon and orange flower water

Sliced sweet oranges chilled with orange flower water and cinnamon: the usual end to a Moroccan meal.

1h 10mTotal time
10mPrep
2Servings
100Calories
EasyDifficulty

Peel navel oranges and slice them 5 mm to 1 cm thick, toss with fresh orange juice, pure orange blossom water, a pinch of sugar if needed and cinnamon, cover and chill at least an hour. Serve cold with a last sprinkle of cinnamon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Cling film

  • 2 sweet oranges, preferably Navel
  • 2 to 4 tbsp orange juice, freshly squeezed
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water (pure, not imitation)
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional; no need if the oranges are very sweet)
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch ground cinnamon for garnish

Method

  1. Peel the oranges and remove the pith. Slice anywhere between 0.2″ (5 mm) and 0.4″ (1 cm) thick.
  2. Add the juice, orange blossom water, sugar and 1/4 tsp cinnamon and toss the oranges delicately.
  3. Cover with cling film and chill to macerate for at least an hour.
  4. Arrange on dishes, sprinkle with cinnamon and serve cold.

Chef Tips

  • The chill is time only (at least an hour).
  • Use pure orange blossom water, not imitation.
  • Skip sugar when oranges are very sweet.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the pith.
  • Too much orange flower water, which tastes soapy.
  • Slicing too thin.

Cultural Note

The page says it is usually served as a dessert salad at the end of a meal, especially when navel oranges are in season.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best within a day.

Reheating: Not reheated; serve cold.

Serve with: Mint tea
