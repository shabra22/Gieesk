🍍 Banana and Pineapple Layers (Cameroonian Dessert)

Banana and pineapple layers

Coconut cream boiled and cooled, then bananas, tomatoes, pineapple and avocado layered in bowls, topped with the thickened cream and chopped peanuts and chilled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small pan, 4 bowls

  • 2 firm ripe bananas, peeled and sliced
  • 2 firm ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 small pineapple, peeled and sliced
  • 1 avocado, peeled, pitted and sliced
  • 1 tablespoon roasted peanuts, coarsely chopped
  • 1 cup coconut cream

Method

  1. Bring the coconut cream to the boil in a pan. Set aside to cool.
  2. Meanwhile, pile the bananas, tomatoes, pineapple and avocado in alternating layers in individual bowls.
  3. Top with the thickened coconut cream and sprinkle with the chopped peanuts.
  4. Chill in the fridge and serve cold.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is ‘thickened coconut cream’.
  • Boil, cooling and chilling times are not printed.
  • The author found the flavour combination interesting.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using unripe bananas or tomatoes.
  • Topping with warm cream.
  • Skipping the chill.
