🌿 Kaffa Honey and Wild Herb Stew

ካፋ ወጥ — Kaffa Wat

Kaffa Forest Honey Stew — beef slow-cooked in a sauce finished with Kaffa forest honey and wild forest herbs unique to the Kaffa biosphere reserve. The distinctively sweet-savoury Kaffa cooking tradition — where the world's finest honey and forest herbs create stews unlike any other.

Kaffa cooking uses the extraordinary forest honey of the Kaffa biosphere as a cooking ingredient — the honey adds a dimension of sweetness and aromatic complexity to savoury stews that is unique in Ethiopian cooking.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 600g beef — cubed
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1.5 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tbsp Kaffa forest honey (or raw mountain honey)
  • 2 tbsp wild herbs (rosemary, thyme, or local herbs)
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions 18 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Fry 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
  4. Add beef. Stir to coat.
  5. Add water. Simmer covered 40 minutes.
  6. Add honey and wild herbs in the last 5 minutes.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Add honey at the end — it caramelises and adds complexity without bitterness.
  • Wild forest herbs replace the standard Ethiopian herb palette.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding honey early — becomes too sweet.

The Science

Honey added at the end of cooking undergoes Maillard browning without having time to caramelise fully — producing a complex, slightly burnt-honey note that enriches the sauce without sweetness dominating.

Health Benefits

  • Honey adds antimicrobial and antioxidant properties.
  • Wild herbs provide unique phytochemicals.

Cultural Note

Kaffa honey stew is the cooking tradition of the birthplace of coffee — the same forest that gave the world coffee also gives Kaffa its extraordinary honey, and the Kaffa people use both in their cooking. To eat Kaffa honey stew is to eat from the world's most biodiverse food forest.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: With injera (ETH001)
