🥬 Kenyan Spinach with Cream

Mchicha na Maziwa

Kenyan Spinach with Cream — fresh spinach wilted with garlic and onions then finished with cream or milk for a rich, silky, deeply satisfying vegetable dish. The Kenyan kitchen's most indulgent way with greens — simple, luxurious, and universally loved.

Spinach with cream is one of those dishes that proves vegetables do not need to be ascetic to be good. A generous pour of cream transforms simple wilted spinach into something restaurant-worthy — silky, rich, and deeply satisfying. In Nairobi restaurants and at home celebrations, creamed spinach is the vegetable side that everyone quietly fights over.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan, Wooden spoon

  • 500g fresh spinach leaves — washed
  • 2 medium onions — finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 100ml fresh cream or full-fat milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg — optional

Method

  1. Wash the spinach thoroughly. Drain but do not dry completely — a little water helps it wilt.
  2. Melt butter in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook onions until soft and translucent — 5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic. Cook 30 seconds.
  5. Add the spinach in batches — it wilts dramatically. Stir each batch until wilted before adding more.
  6. Once all the spinach is wilted, cook for 2—3 minutes to release excess water.
  7. Pour in the cream. Stir gently.
  8. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg if using.
  9. Simmer for 2—3 minutes until the cream is incorporated and slightly thickened.
  10. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Do not overcook after adding cream — it should coat the spinach, not reduce to nothing.
  • Squeeze out excess water from the wilted spinach before adding cream — prevents a watery result.
  • A pinch of nutmeg is the classic partner to creamed spinach — do not skip it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too much water in the spinach — the cream becomes diluted and watery.
  • Overcooking — the spinach loses its bright colour and the cream breaks.

The Science

Spinach contains approximately 92% water. When heated, this water is released — which is why spinach shrinks so dramatically. Squeezing out excess water before adding cream prevents dilution of the sauce and allows the cream to coat the leaves properly.

Health Benefits

  • Iron and calcium from spinach.
  • Cream adds fat-soluble vitamins and makes the iron more absorbable.
  • Garlic provides antimicrobial allicin.

Cultural Note

Creamed spinach occupies a special place in Kenyan restaurant menus — a touch of indulgence that elevates a simple vegetable into something celebratory. It appears at everything from hotel buffets to family Sunday lunches.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh. Refrigerate for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Warm gently over low heat.

Serve with: Ugali, Steamed Rice, Grilled Chicken, Beef Stew, Chapati
