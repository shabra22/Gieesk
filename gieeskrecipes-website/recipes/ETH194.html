🍚 Ethiopian Spiced Rice Pudding

ሩዝ ፑዲንግ — Ruz Puding

Ethiopian Spiced Rice Pudding — short-grain rice slowly cooked in milk with Ethiopian honey, cardamom, cinnamon, and rose water. The Ethiopian version of rice pudding — warmer-spiced than European versions, sweetened with mountain honey rather than sugar.

Ethiopian rice pudding is the warm comfort dessert of urban Ethiopia — the familiar rice-in-milk preparation transformed by the Ethiopian spice tradition into something distinctly its own. The cardamom and rose water speak to the Arab culinary influence on Ethiopian Muslim communities.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan

  • 1 cup short-grain or arborio rice
  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 3 tbsp Ethiopian honey
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp rose water (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Garnish: pinch of ground cinnamon, chopped pistachios (optional)

    Method

    1. Rinse rice. Combine with water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil.
    2. Reduce heat. Simmer 10 minutes until water is absorbed.
    3. Add milk, honey, sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
    4. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, for 20—25 minutes.
    5. The pudding should be thick and creamy — the rice very soft.
    6. Add rose water. Stir.
    7. Serve warm or chilled.
    8. Garnish with a pinch of cinnamon.

    Chef Tips

    • Stir frequently — rice pudding sticks easily.
    • The pudding thickens significantly on cooling — remove from heat slightly looser than desired.
    • Rose water is the finishing touch that gives it its distinctive Middle-Eastern-Ethiopian character.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not stirring — sticks and burns.
    • Removing from heat too thick — becomes solid when cold.

    The Science

    Short-grain rice releases much more amylopectin (a highly branched starch) than long-grain rice during cooking — this starch thickens the milk into the characteristic pudding consistency. The milk proteins (casein) also partially denature during the long cooking, contributing to the creamy texture.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian spiced rice pudding is the sweet that Ethiopian mothers make for sick children — warm, gentle, and easy to eat. The cardamom and honey make it comforting in the same way that chicken soup is comforting in other cultures.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

    Reheating: Gently with a splash of milk.

    Serve with: As a dessert warm or cold, At celebration after-dinner sweet, For children as a nutritious dessert
