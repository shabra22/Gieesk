🟢 Ethiopian Green Chilli Paste

አረንጓዴ በርበሬ — Ye'arenguade Berbere

Ethiopian Green Chilli Paste — fresh green jalapeños and serrano chillies blended with garlic, ginger, coriander, and a squeeze of lemon into a vibrant green condiment. The bright, fresh alternative to red awaze — used as a table condiment alongside grilled meats and fish.

Green chilli paste is the fresh counterpart to the dried-chilli-based awaze — made from raw green jalapeños, it is brighter, more herbal, and has a different heat character than red awaze. Popular as a dipping sauce at Ethiopian restaurants.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or food processor

  • 6 fresh green jalapeños — roughly chopped
  • 2 serrano chillies (for extra heat)
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
  2. Blend until very smooth.
  3. Taste and adjust salt and lemon.
  4. Rest 15 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • More jalapeños = more flavour (milder heat); more serranos = more heat.
  • The coriander adds fresh herbal brightness — do not omit.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not blending smooth enough — chunky is not right for this preparation.

The Science

Raw green chillies contain capsaicin in different chemical forms than dried red chillies — fresh chillies contain dihydrocapsaicin in higher proportions, which produces a slightly different heat sensation (broader, less pointy) than the capsaicin-dominant dried chilli heat.

Health Benefits

  • Fresh capsaicin.
  • Vitamin C from raw chillies (much higher than dried).
  • Coriander antioxidants.

Cultural Note

Green chilli paste is the vibrant, fresh face of Ethiopian condiment culture — where awaze and berbere are the deep, complex, dried-chilli tradition, green paste represents the fresh-harvest season's brightness.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 1 week. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On tibs (ETH022), On grilled fish (ETH085), As a table dipping sauce
