🐟 Tanzanian Grilled Sea Bass

Chewa wa Kuchoma

Tanzanian Grilled Sea Bass — Indian Ocean sea bass fillets pan-seared skin-crispy with a Zanzibar spice crust, served with a passion fruit-coconut beurre blanc. Zanzibar's most elegant restaurant fish.

Indian Ocean sea bass is among the finest fish in the world — firm, white, and clean-flavoured. When cooked correctly (skin side down, high heat, patience) it produces a magnificently crispy skin. The passion fruit beurre blanc is uniquely Tanzanian.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick or cast iron pan, Small saucepan for sauce

  • 2 sea bass fillets (200g each) — skin on
  • 1 tsp Zanzibar spice blend (cumin, coriander, cardamom, cloves — ground)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Passion Fruit-Coconut Beurre Blanc

  • 3 passion fruits — juice
  • 50ml coconut cream
  • 1 shallot — minced
  • 100g cold butter — cubed
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Make beurre blanc: reduce passion fruit, coconut cream, and shallot by two-thirds. Remove from heat. Whisk in cold butter piece by piece. Add honey. Season.
  2. Pat fish skin completely dry. Season with spice blend and salt.
  3. Heat oil until shimmering. Place fish skin-side down.
  4. Press lightly for 30 seconds. Cook without moving 4—5 minutes until golden and crispy.
  5. Flip. Cook 2—3 minutes on flesh side. Rest 2 minutes.
  6. Serve with beurre blanc.

Chef Tips

  • Completely dry skin is non-negotiable.
  • Press gently then do not touch until ready to flip.
  • Beurre blanc must be made off direct heat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Moving fish before skin crisps.
  • Beurre blanc over direct heat — breaks.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar sea bass at a fine dining restaurant — the fish caught that morning, the passion fruit grown on the island — is the most complete expression of what the island's food culture can achieve.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Herb Rice, Buttered Vegetables, Kachumbari
