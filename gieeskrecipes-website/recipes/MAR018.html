🥕 Lamb and Vegetable Tagine

Tajine d'agneau aux légumes (طاجين اللحم بالخضر)

A simple everyday tagine of lamb or beef with carrots, potatoes, zucchini and peas, flavoured with ginger, turmeric, saffron, coriander and parsley.

2h 5mTotal time
25mPrep
1h 40mCook
5Servings
440Calories
EasyDifficulty

This Morocco World News recipe cooks meat with onions on low heat for ten minutes, stirring often, then adds the vegetables and spices and lets the tagine cook about 1 hour 30 minutes. No liquid is added; the vegetables and onion release their own.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine (or heavy pot with lid)

  • 1 kg of red meat, beef, or lamb
  • 1 diced onion
  • 1 peeled, diced tomato
  • 3 peeled carrots, cut into wedges
  • 2 potatoes, peeled and cut into wedges
  • 1 zucchini, cut in half lengthwise
  • 150 grams of peas
  • A generous bunch of chopped coriander and parsley
  • 1 teaspoon of ginger
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • ½ teaspoon of turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon of ground black pepper
  • Few saffron stamens, soaked in cold water
  • 3 cloves of minced garlic
  • 3 tablespoons of oil (the page also prints 2 tablespoons of olive oil; see sources)

Method

  1. Add the oil, onions and meat to the tagine, place it on the stove and set the fire to low heat.
  2. Cover the tagine and heat for 10 minutes, stirring frequently.
  3. Add the tomato, garlic, spices, saffron and herbs, then arrange the vegetables over the meat.
  4. Let the tagine cook for about 1 hour and 30 minutes.
  5. Serve from the tagine with bread.

Chef Tips

  • The source suggests adding some argan oil to lamb tagines for taste.
  • No doneness cue is printed beyond the time, so test that the meat is tender with a fork.
  • Keep the heat low; no extra liquid is called for.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Raising the heat to hurry it.
  • Adding water; the vegetables release enough.
  • Cutting the vegetables too small.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Reheat covered over low heat.

Serve with: Khobz, Mint tea
