🍨 Kenyan Ice Cream Platter

Sahani ya Aiskrimu

Kenyan Ice Cream Platter — a generous hotel-style selection of vanilla, mango, coconut, and passion fruit ice cream scoops, served with fresh tropical fruit coulis, toasted coconut, Kenyan honey, wafer biscuits, and a sprig of mint. The dessert that pleases every table.

The Kenyan ice cream platter at a hotel is not an afterthought — it is a considered, beautifully presented spread of multiple flavours with local coulis that makes the familiar extraordinary.

Ingredients

Equipment: Ice cream scoop, Large chilled platter, Small sauce bottles for coulis

    Ice Cream Scoops (2 of each)

    • Vanilla
    • Mango sorbet
    • Coconut ice cream
    • Passion fruit sorbet

    Toppings

    • 2 tbsp mango coulis
    • 2 tbsp passion fruit coulis
    • 3 tbsp toasted desiccated coconut
    • 2 tbsp Kenyan honey
    • 8 wafer biscuits or tuile cookies
    • Fresh mint
    • Sliced fresh mango and strawberry

    Method

    1. Pre-chill the serving platter in the freezer for 10 minutes.
    2. Scoop all ice creams and sorbets onto the chilled platter.
    3. Drizzle mango coulis over vanilla and coconut scoops.
    4. Drizzle passion fruit coulis over mango sorbet.
    5. Scatter toasted coconut over all scoops.
    6. Drizzle Kenyan honey.
    7. Arrange wafer biscuits and fresh fruit around the scoops.
    8. Add mint sprigs.
    9. Serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • A pre-chilled platter gives you more time before the ice cream melts.
    • Scoop ice cream in advance and freeze on a tray — perfect presentation without rushing.
    • Drizzle sauces in a zigzag pattern for restaurant visual impact.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not chilling the platter.
    • Sauces poured too heavily — overwhelms the ice cream.

    The Science

    A chilled platter slows the rate of heat transfer from the room-temperature plate to the cold ice cream — the temperature differential between the pre-chilled plate and the ice cream is smaller, reducing the melting rate.

    Cultural Note

    The Kenyan ice cream platter demonstrates the hotel kitchen's understanding that dessert is about joy and sharing — a platter that arrives at the table and produces immediate smiles, in any restaurant in the world.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Assemble immediately before serving.

    Reheating: Not applicable.

    Serve with: After any hotel main course, As a shared dessert for the table
