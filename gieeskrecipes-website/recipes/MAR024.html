🐓 Chicken Couscous with Caramelised Onion and Raisin Tfaya (Oven-Steamed)

Couscous au poulet et tfaya

A weeknight-friendly chicken couscous: the grains are hydrated and baked in a pan instead of steamed, and the chicken is served under a 'brown and jammy' red onion tfaya.

2h 15mTotal time
40mPrep
1h 15mCook
6Servings
800Calories
MediumDifficulty

Koehler adapts the dish for a home kitchen: the stew simmers 45 minutes with ginger, white pepper, cloves, cinnamon and saffron, the tfaya is cooked in a skillet with raisins and honey, and the couscous is soaked in salted lukewarm water for 15-20 minutes then baked at 350°F for about 12 minutes. Fried almonds and hard-boiled egg wedges garnish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large skillet, 9 x 13 in. baking pan, Oven

    Tfaya

    • ½ cup raisins
    • 3 Tbsp. unsalted butter or olive oil
    • 1 tsp. ground ginger
    • ½ tsp. ground cinnamon
    • 3 large red onions (2¼ lb.), thinly sliced
    • ¼ cup honey

    Stew

    • 2½ lb. bone-in chicken thighs or legs, skin removed
    • 1 Tbsp. kosher salt
    • 1 Tbsp. olive oil
    • ½ tsp. ground ginger
    • ½ tsp. ground white pepper
    • 6 whole cloves
    • 1 small cinnamon stick
    • Pinch saffron threads, crumbled
    • ¾ cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
    • 1 Tbsp. unsalted butter
    • 10 cilantro sprigs, tied into a bundle with twine
    • 10 parsley sprigs, tied into a bundle with twine

    Couscous

    • 3 cups fine semolina couscous (1 lb.)
    • 1 tsp. fine salt
    • 1 Tbsp. olive oil
    • 2 Tbsp. unsalted butter, at room temperature

    Garnish

    • Vegetable oil, for frying
    • ½ cup whole blanched almonds
    • 3 large hard-boiled eggs, peeled and cut into wedges (optional)

    Method

    1. Tfaya: soak the raisins in lukewarm water for about 10 minutes and drain. Cook the raisins, butter, ginger, cinnamon, onions and ½ cup water in a large skillet over medium heat, covered, for about 30 minutes, until the onions are soft and deep golden brown. Stir in the honey, lower the heat and cook about 15 minutes more, until brown and jammy.
    2. Stew: coat the chicken with the oil, salt and spices. Add the chickpeas, butter, herb bundles and 4¼ cups water. Bring to a boil, reduce to medium-low and simmer, partly covered, for about 45 minutes, until the chicken is very tender.
    3. Couscous: spread it in the baking pan. Dissolve the salt in 3 cups lukewarm water and pour it over. Let stand 15 to 20 minutes.
    4. Heat the oven to 350°F with the rack in the centre. Fluff the couscous, drizzle with the oil and rub the grains. Bake about 12 minutes, until the grains are expanded, tender and begin to steam.
    5. Fry the almonds in about ½ inch of hot, shimmering oil for 2 to 4 minutes until just golden.
    6. Toss the couscous with the butter and mound it. Put the chicken and chickpeas in a well, ladle in about half the broth, spread the tfaya over and top with almonds and egg wedges.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues from the source: chicken very tender; couscous expanded and tender; almonds just golden; oil hot and shimmering.
    • This is an oven adaptation, not traditional triple steaming.
    • Fry almonds in small batches so they stay golden.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Baking the couscous without fluffing and rubbing first.
    • Overcooking almonds, which turn bitter fast.
    • Leaving the skin on the chicken; the source removes it.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge, with tfaya and stew stored apart from the couscous.

    Reheating: Warm the stew and tfaya gently; fluff the couscous with a splash of hot water.

    Serve with: Mint tea
