🍲 Egyptian Trotter Fatta (Kaware)

Fatta kawarea — فتة كوارع

Beef trotters simmered with spices, roasted until golden, and laid over baladi bread squares, rice and broth with a hot garlic-vinegar tomato sauce.

3hTotal time
30mPrep
2h 30mCook
5Servings
620Calories
HardDifficulty

A beef trotter is chopped into big chunks and boiled with a whole onion; the froth is skimmed, spices are added, and it cooks over medium-high heat until done. The trotters go into an oiled ovenproof dish, covered, and roast at 200°C; the uncovered finish is until golden and slightly crunched. Bread squares are toasted, rice is cooked, and a sauce of butter, garlic, vinegar and fresh tomato juice is simmered to thicken. Everything is layered in a wide dish with hot broth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Ovenproof dish with cover, Saucepan

  • 1 beef trotter (chopped into big chunks)
  • 1 onion (whole)
  • 2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 cup Egyptian rice (washed, drained)
  • 1 cup fresh tomato juice
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 baladi loaves (squared, drizzled with some olive oil)
  • Olive oil (to drizzle)
  • 30 gm butter
  • Salt + pepper + small cinnamon stick + 4 cardamom pods + 2 bay leaves

Method

  1. Boil the whole onion, add the trotter chunks and skim the froth. Add salt, pepper and the spices and cook over medium-high heat until done. The page gives no time.
  2. Lift the trotters into an oiled ovenproof dish, cover and roast at 200°C. Uncover for the final ten minutes, until golden and slightly crunched on top; the page's total is approximately 45 minutes.
  3. Toast the bread squares until golden and cook the rice the usual fluffy way; strain the broth.
  4. Sauce: melt the butter, fry the garlic until slightly golden, then add the vinegar and tomato juice. Boil, then simmer to thicken and reduce.
  5. Layer half the bread, half the rice, hot broth and half the sauce in a wide dish. Repeat, then top with the trotters.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: trotters cooked until done (no time printed); golden and slightly crunched; garlic slightly golden; sauce thickened. The roast time is ambiguous on the page: whether 45 minutes is the total or the covered portion is unclear.
  • The bread toasting time and temperature are NOT PRINTED.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking the trotters, which stay tough (our caution).

Reheating: Warm gently with broth.

Serve with: Pickles
