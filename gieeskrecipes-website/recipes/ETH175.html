🌻 Ethiopian Roasted Sunflower Seeds

ሱፍ ቆሎ — Suf Qolo

Ethiopian Roasted Sunflower Seeds — raw sunflower seeds dry-roasted with salt. The most affordable market snack — bought in paper cones from vendors, eaten while shopping or travelling. Ethiopia grows sunflowers widely for oil, and roasted seeds are a natural byproduct.

Roasted sunflower seeds are the humble, everyday snack that fills the gap when chickpeas and peanuts are too expensive — always available, always affordable, always satisfying. Sold by street vendors in small paper cones.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy dry pan

  • 2 cups raw sunflower seeds — in shell or shelled
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat a heavy dry pan over medium heat.
  2. Add sunflower seeds.
  3. Roast stirring constantly 12—15 minutes until golden and fragrant.
  4. Season with salt while hot.
  5. Cool completely before eating.

Chef Tips

  • Stir constantly — sunflower seeds burn quickly once browning begins.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not stirring constantly.

The Science

Sunflower seeds contain high concentrations of vitamin E (tocopherols) — dry roasting actually concentrates these compounds as the moisture evaporates, and some studies suggest roasting may make the vitamin E more bioavailable by breaking down cell walls.

Cultural Note

Roasted sunflower seeds are the snack of Ethiopia's working poor — affordable, filling, and always available. The vendor with the large tray of roasting seeds, shaking them with a practised circular motion, is a fixture of every Ethiopian market.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight container — 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not needed.

Serve with: As a market snack, With tea, As a travel snack
