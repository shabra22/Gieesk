🥤 Tamr Hindi (Sweet Tamarind Drink)

Tamr hindi — تمر هندي

A block of tamarind soaked in water for 8 hours, strained, sweetened to taste, blended and served over ice; never boiled.

8h 10mTotal time
10mPrep
7Servings
90Calories
EasyDifficulty

A 350 g block of tamarind is broken into chunks and covered with 8 cups of water for 8 hours, stirring a couple of times. It is strained through a fine mesh strainer, sugar is added to taste, blended, and served over ice. Warm water cuts the soaking to 4 hours.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl, Fine mesh strainer, Blender

  • 1 Block 350g tamarind
  • 2 liter of tap water 8 cups
  • Sugar to your preference

Method

  1. Break the tamarind block into chunks and cover with 8 cups of water.
  2. Cover and soak for 8 hours, stirring a couple of times in between (warm water shortens this to 4 hours).
  3. Strain through a fine mesh strainer, add sugar to taste and blend.
  4. Serve over ice.

Chef Tips

  • The soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (8 hours; the body says overnight). This is a plain soak, not a ferment.
  • The author has never boiled tamarind and has heard it reduces the health benefits.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not straining well; the seeds and fibre are bitter.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim says street vendors in Egypt carry a copper pot and clang copper discs, though they are now found mainly near tourist sites; the drink is popular at Ramadan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 5 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Ice
