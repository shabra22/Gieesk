🫕 Tanzanian Maize Porridge Sweet

Uji wa Mahindi Tamu

Tanzanian Sweet Uji — fine maize flour simmered in coconut milk with sugar, cardamom, and vanilla until silky smooth. The sweetened version of Tanzania's staple porridge — elevated with coconut milk and spice into a dessert that is as nourishing as it is comforting.

Sweetened uji with coconut milk is the dessert version of Tanzania's most fundamental food — when uji is made with coconut milk, sweetened generously, and perfumed with cardamom, it transcends its everyday role and becomes something genuinely special. Served at celebrations for children, as a dessert at coastal meals, and as a healing food for the sick.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Whisk

  • 1 cup fine maize flour (or millet flour)
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Garnish: toasted coconut, a drizzle of honey

    Method

    1. Whisk maize flour with 1 cup cold water until completely lump-free.
    2. Bring coconut milk and remaining water to a gentle simmer.
    3. Pour the flour mixture slowly into the simmering liquid, whisking constantly.
    4. Cook over medium heat, whisking frequently, for 15—18 minutes until thick and smooth.
    5. Add sugar, cardamom, vanilla, and salt. Stir.
    6. Simmer 3 more minutes.
    7. Serve warm or at room temperature, garnished with toasted coconut and honey.

    Chef Tips

    • Whisking the flour into cold water first prevents all lumps.
    • Whisk constantly as it thickens — the base thickens fast and sticks easily.
    • The consistency should be like a thick, pourable custard.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Adding flour directly to hot liquid — lumps.
    • Not whisking constantly during thickening.

    The Science

    Maize starch gelatinises between 62—80°C — this is the temperature range during which the starch granules absorb water, swell, and burst, releasing amylose into the liquid and thickening it. The coconut milk fat coats the starch granules, producing a smoother, glossier, more tender result than water alone.

    Cultural Note

    Sweet uji is the food of Tanzania's gentlest moments — given to recovering children, eaten by new mothers, served as a dessert at coastal celebrations. Its simplicity is its virtue: maize and coconut, the two most Tanzanian ingredients, together making something warming and kind.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 3 days. Reheat gently with added coconut milk.

    Reheating: Gently with a splash of coconut milk.

    Serve with: As a warm dessert, As a breakfast, With fresh fruit alongside
