Yirgacheffe Regional Meal — Tibs with the highland herbs of Yirgacheffe, and a cup of the world's most celebrated coffee (Yirgacheffe natural process, floral and fruity). The experience of eating in the place where the world's finest coffee is grown.

Yirgacheffe produces coffee considered by many coffee experts to be the finest arabica on earth — natural process Yirgacheffe with its blueberry, jasmine, and lemon notes. The regional food pairs with this extraordinary coffee.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pan, Jebena for coffee

  • Tibs with Yirgacheffe highland herbs (rosemary, thyme)
  • Injera

The coffee: Yirgacheffe natural process arabica — brewed in jebena

    Method

    1. Make Tibs per ETH022 with highland herbs.
    2. Brew Yirgacheffe coffee per ETH212.
    3. Serve coffee after the meal — the Yirgacheffe tradition.

    Chef Tips

    • The Yirgacheffe coffee should be brewed medium and served without sugar to appreciate its natural sweetness.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Over-roasting Yirgacheffe — its light-roast character is the point.

    The Science

    See ETH212 for coffee science. Yirgacheffe natural process coffees retain fruit sugars from the cherry that dry on the bean — producing the characteristic blueberry and jasmine notes that make Yirgacheffe the world's most celebrated coffee origin.

    Health Benefits

    • Yirgacheffe coffee has among the highest antioxidant levels of any coffee.

    Cultural Note

    Yirgacheffe is a pilgrimage destination for coffee lovers — the place that produces the world's most celebrated single-origin coffee. To eat Tibs and drink Yirgacheffe in its origin is to experience the complete sensory context of the world's most extraordinary coffee.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Coffee: drink immediately. Food: as per recipes.

    Reheating: Standard.

    Serve with: As a complete regional dining experience
