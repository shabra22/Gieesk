🍩 Zalabya (Luqmat el Qadi, Egyptian Doughnut Bites)

Zalabia / Luqmat el-qadi — زلابية

Yeasted batter with orange zest dropped into warm oil, fried golden and drizzled with simple syrup, honey or chocolate.

1h 40mTotal time
15mPrep
15mCook
6Servings
290Calories
MediumDifficulty

Flour, cornstarch, milk powder, salt, yeast, sugar and orange zest are mixed with warm water; yeast proofs five minutes until frothy and foamy. The batter rises for no more than an hour, and spoonfuls are fried in oil that is warm to the touch (not too hot) until golden brown, drained 5 minutes and dipped in syrup, honey, powdered sugar or melted chocolate.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Deep pan, Slotted spoon

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons powdered milk
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 ½ cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon yeast
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon orange zest
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, optional
  • ⅛ teaspoon cinnamon, optional
  • 1 cup oil
  • 1 cup simple syrup
  • Honey
  • Powdered sugar
  • Melted white chocolate
  • Melted milk chocolate

Method

  1. Proof the yeast with warm water and sugar for 5 minutes, until frothy and foamy.
  2. Mix with the flour, cornstarch, milk powder, salt, zest and optional vanilla and cinnamon into a batter and let it rise for no more than an hour.
  3. Heat the oil until warm to the touch; do not let it get too hot.
  4. Drop spoonfuls into the oil and fry once they are golden brown. Drain 5 minutes.
  5. Dip in simple syrup and finish with honey, powdered sugar or melted chocolate.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: yeast frothy; oil warm to touch; fritters golden brown. The rise of no more than an hour is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The batter can be made a day ahead.
  • The syrup is listed as '1 cup simple syrup' with no method; see the sharbat record.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Oil too hot, browning the outside before the inside cooks.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour says zalabia recalls summers in Alexandria and credits the recipe to 'tante Nadia' (Mrs. Nadia Rizk); her diaspora kids suggested the orange zest and vanilla.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh.

Reheating: Not recommended; fry fresh.

Serve with: Simple syrup
