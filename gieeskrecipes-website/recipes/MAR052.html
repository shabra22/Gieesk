🍅 Moroccan Tomato and Onion Salad

Shlada dyal tomatim wa besla

Seeded tomatoes and paper-thin onion, both finely diced and dressed with olive oil and white vinegar.

15mTotal time
15mPrep
4Servings
170Calories
EasyDifficulty

Only the exterior flesh of the tomatoes is used: seed them, slice into strips and dice very fine. The onion is cut into the thinnest strips possible, then diced. A dressing of olive oil, vinegar, salt and pepper goes over the top, and the salad is served cold or at room temperature.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp knife, Bowl

  • 2 large, fresh tomatoes
  • 1/2 Vidalia or other sweet onion
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp white vinegar
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil (good quality)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper

Method

  1. Wash the tomatoes and remove the seeds. You only want the exterior flesh. Slice into strips and then very finely dice.
  2. Cut the onion into very thin strips, as thin as you possibly can, and dice.
  3. Mix the onion and tomato together in a bowl.
  4. Whisk together the olive oil, vinegar, salt and pepper, drizzle over, adjust the seasoning, and serve cold or at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Cut as thin as you possibly can.
  • The author notes the salad exists in many cultures and has been called a Portuguese tomato and onion salad.
  • Vidalia is an American substitution; any sweet onion works.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving seeds in, which waters the salad.
  • Cutting the onion thick.
  • Dressing too early.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; 1 day.

Reheating: Not reheated.

Serve with: Khobz
