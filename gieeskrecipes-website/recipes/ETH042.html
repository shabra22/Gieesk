🍲 Ethiopian Lamb Alicha

የበግ አልጫ — Ye-Beg Alicha

Ethiopian Ye-Beg Alicha — young lamb in a mild, golden-yellow stew without berbere, seasoned with turmeric, ginger, and cardamom. The mild celebration counterpart to Ye-Beg Key Wat — served on the same platter for those who prefer no heat.

Ye-Beg Alicha is the gentle side of the Ethiopian celebration feast — while the Key Wat burns with berbere, the Alicha provides the golden, mild, cardamom-fragrant counterpoint. Made with the same quality lamb, it is equally prestigious.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 1 kg young lamb — bone-in
  • 3 onions — finely diced
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions — 20 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh. Add turmeric, ground ginger, cardamom. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add garlic, fresh ginger, pepper. Cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add lamb. Stir to coat.
  5. Add water. Simmer covered 75—80 minutes until very tender.
  6. Season.

Chef Tips

  • No berbere at all — this is genuinely mild.
  • Cardamom is the distinguishing spice.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding berbere — destroys the alicha character.

Cultural Note

Ye-Beg Alicha is the thoughtful inclusion on the Ethiopian celebration mesob — the host who serves both a red and a yellow lamb wat has thought of all their guests.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Alongside Ye-Beg Key Wat on the celebration platter
