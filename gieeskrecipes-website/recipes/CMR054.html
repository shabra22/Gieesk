🍚 Couscous de sorgho (Nyri digari, Red or White Sorghum Couscous)

Nyri digari

Sorghum flour couscous made like the maize one: a flour ‘milk’ cooked about 10 minutes after the boil returns, then the flour added and cooked 10 more minutes; served hot with a sauce.

35 minTotal time
15 minPrep
20 minCook
4Servings
210Calories
EasyDifficulty

p. 19 of the October 2023 recipe book. Sorghum flour (red or white; plain, soaked or not soaked); quantities not printed. The method follows the maize couscous with about 10 minutes after the boil returns, then the flour is stirred in and cooked a further 10 minutes. The page notes the meal must be served hot with a sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Wooden spatula

  • Sorghum flour (red or white) — quantity not printed
  • Water — quantity not printed
  • Cold water for the ‘milk’ — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Boil water. Mix a little flour with cold water into a ‘milk’.
  2. Pour the ‘milk’ into the boiling water, stirring. Cook about 10 minutes after the boil returns.
  3. Add the sorghum flour, stirring.
  4. Cook 10 more minutes.
  5. Serve hot with a sauce.

Chef Tips

  • The printed note is that this meal must be served hot with a sauce.
  • No flour-to-water ratio is printed.
  • Soaked and unsoaked flour are both given as routes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting it cool before serving.
  • Not stirring while the flour goes in.
  • Treating the times as a ratio guide.
