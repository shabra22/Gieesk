🍯 Assida (Semolina Porridge with Honey and Butter)

Assida / l'assida — semolina porridge (عصيدة)

A smooth, melting porridge of coarse semolina, poured into a dish and eaten with honey and butter.

35mTotal time
5mPrep
30mCook
4Servings
420Calories
EasyDifficulty

Boil the water, stir in the semolina and simmer about 20 minutes, tasting until it has a 'tender melting bite'. It thickens and becomes compact as it cools. Pour it into a deep dish and scatter honey and cubes of butter over it. The author calls it an easy, frugal and bland dish with a Bedouin background, traditionally served for breakfast on Eid al Mawlid.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep, thick-based saucepan, Deep serving dish

  • 1/2 lb coarse semolina (durum wheat)
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 4 cups water

For serving

  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup butter (in cubes, can replace with olive oil)

Method

  1. Use a deep, thick-based saucepan and bring the water to a boil.
  2. Add the semolina and salt, stirring, over medium heat.
  3. Simmer about 20 minutes, stirring and tasting until the semolina has a tender, melting bite.
  4. Pour into a deep dish and scatter honey and butter cubes over it.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: a tender melting bite.
  • It thickens and becomes compact as it cools, so serve warm.
  • Olive oil can replace the butter.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dumping in the semolina at once, which lumps.
  • Under-cooking so the grains are chalky.
  • Using fine semolina.

Health Benefits

  • Simple whole grain with little fat other than the topping.
  • Honey and butter are added at the table, so portion to taste.

Cultural Note

The author writes that it is traditionally served for breakfast on Eid al Mawlid and calls it an easy, frugal dish with a Bedouin background.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Leftovers set firm; the author notes squares of cold semolina pudding with sugar and cinnamon are sold in Fez.

Reheating: Warm with a splash of water if wanted soft.

Serve with: Mint tea
