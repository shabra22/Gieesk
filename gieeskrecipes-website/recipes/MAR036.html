🥣 Split Pea Bessara

Bessara bil jelban — split pea puree (بصارة بالجلبانة)

Yellow split peas simmered with onion, garlic, paprika and cumin until soft and pureed into a thick, warming soup.

10mPrep
1hCook

EasyDifficulty

Onion and garlic are cooked in olive oil until fragrant and softened, then everything else goes in. After a boil, the pot simmers partly covered for 50 to 60 minutes until the peas are tender, then it is pureed. The author notes water is traditionally the only liquid, though she likes to replace some with chicken broth, and that the thicker version is likely served in the north as an accompaniment to fish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Immersion blender

  • 1 lb. dried split peas (about 2 cups)
  • 4 cups broth (chicken, beef, or vegetable)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 4 to 5 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped (optional)
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper, or to taste
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper, or to taste (optional)
  • Garnish: cumin, salt, cayenne pepper, olive oil

Method

  1. Cook the onion and garlic in the oil until fragrant and softened.
  2. Add everything else, bring to a boil, then simmer partly covered 50 to 60 minutes, until the peas are tender.
  3. Puree until smooth.
  4. Serve with cumin, salt, cayenne and olive oil.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: peas tender.
  • Water is traditionally the only liquid; broth is the author's own richer variation.
  • Thin with hot water to your preferred consistency.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding salt late and getting a bland puree.
  • Boiling hard, which catches on the base.
  • Skipping the garnish.

Health Benefits

  • Split peas are high in protein and fibre.
  • Low in fat apart from the olive oil.

Cultural Note

The author writes that the thicker version is likely served in the north of Morocco as a popular accompaniment to fish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Thin with water and warm gently.

Serve with: Khobz
