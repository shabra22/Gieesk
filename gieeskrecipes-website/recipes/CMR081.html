🍋 Nectar de citron (Lemon Drink)

Nectar de citron

Two kilograms of lemons soaked 5 minutes in water with a little bleach, pressed and filtered; the juice boiled 10 minutes with water and 2 kg of sugar, cooled and bottled.

p. 79 of the October 2023 recipe book: 2 kg lemons, 2 kg sugar and 5 litres of water. The lemons are soaked 5 minutes in water with a little bleach, pressed by hand or press, filtered; water and sugar are added and boiled 10 minutes; cooled; ‘add the preservative if possible’ (the preservative is not in the ingredient list and its quantity is not printed); bottled; kept cool. The diagram shows 65 °C for 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Press, Cloth, Sterilised bottles

  • 2 kg lemons
  • 2 kg sugar
  • 5 litres water
  • A little bleach (javel) for the soak — quantity not printed
  • Preservative (named in the method, not in the list) — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Soak the lemons in drinking water with a little bleach for 5 minutes.
  2. Press by hand or press. Filter.
  3. Add the water and sugar. Boil 10 minutes.
  4. Cool. Add the preservative if possible.
  5. Bottle. Keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The page prints no doneness cue beyond the boil time.
  • The diagram shows 65 °C for 10 minutes — the time matches, the temperature does not say ‘boil’.
  • The bleach and preservative quantities are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Guessing the bleach dose.
  • Adding less water than printed and getting a syrup.
  • Bottling hot.
