🍇 Chicken Couscous with Tfaya

Couscous tfaya — Fes-style (كسكس بالتفاية)

Chicken legs simmered with chickpeas, served over couscous with a sweet, glossy topping of slow-cooked onions, raisins, honey and cinnamon.

2h 20mTotal time
25mPrep
1h 45mCook
6Servings
780Calories

Benkabbou's family comes from Fes, where she says tfaya originates, and this couscous follows that tradition. The chicken simmers 45 minutes in stock with onions, garlic, turmeric, ginger and saffron, chickpeas join for 15 more, and the tfaya is cooked separately: onions for 20 minutes covered, raisins, honey and cinnamon for 10, then stock for about 25 minutes until most of the liquid has evaporated into a thick golden-brown sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid, Wide pan with lid for the tfaya

    Main dish

    • 3 tbsp olive oil
    • 2 large onions, sliced
    • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
    • 2 tsp ground turmeric
    • 2 tsp ground ginger
    • 3/4 tsp salt
    • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
    • 1 pinch saffron threads
    • 6 large chicken legs
    • 400ml chicken stock
    • 1 tin chickpeas (400g), rinsed and drained
    • 50g flaked almonds, toasted, to garnish (optional)
    • 360g couscous, cooked according to the packet instructions, then seasoned with a little salt and olive oil

    Tfaya sauce

    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 4 large onions, sliced
    • 300g raisins
    • 3 tbsp clear honey
    • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
    • 1/2 tsp salt, or more to taste
    • 100ml chicken stock

    Method

    1. Heat the oil over medium heat; add the onions, garlic and spices and cook 3 minutes.
    2. Add the chicken legs and cook 5 minutes, turning. Pour in the stock and boil over high heat. Cover, reduce to medium-low and simmer 45 minutes.
    3. Stir in the chickpeas and simmer a further 15 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through.
    4. For the tfaya, heat the oil over medium-low, add the onions, cover and cook about 20 minutes until soft and translucent. Soak the raisins in hot water for 10 minutes, then drain.
    5. Add the raisins, honey, cinnamon and salt and cook uncovered for about 10 minutes. Add the stock, bring to the boil, then simmer on medium-low for about 25 minutes, until most of the liquid has evaporated and the sauce is thick and golden brown.
    6. Serve the warm couscous topped with the chicken, broth and chickpeas, then the tfaya, then toasted almonds.

    Chef Tips

    • Doneness cues: onions soft and translucent, then lightly golden; tfaya sauce thick and golden brown with most of the liquid gone.
    • This version uses packet couscous, not hand-steamed grains.
    • Taste the tfaya for salt after it reduces.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Cooking the onions over high heat, which browns without softening.
    • Skipping the raisin soak.
    • Reducing the sauce too far so it turns to a sticky jam.

    Health Benefits

    • Chicken and chickpeas give protein and fibre.
    • The tfaya is sweet from fruit and honey; serve it as a topping, not a base.

    Cultural Note

    The author writes: 'This chicken couscous dish originates in the city of Fes' and that both her parents are from Fes, where tfaya originates.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Store tfaya separately; both keep 3 days refrigerated.

    Reheating: Warm gently; add a splash of water to loosen the tfaya.

    Serve with: Mint tea
