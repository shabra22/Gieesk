🍑 Qamar el Din (Dried Apricot Paste)

Amar el-din — قمر الدين

Dried apricots stewed very tender with sugar and lemon, blended to a spreadable paste, spread ¼ to ⅓ inch thick and dried in a very low oven for 4 to 5 hours.

5h 20mTotal time

MediumDifficulty

One pound of dried apricots cooks in water, sugar and lemon until very tender, is blended into a spreadable paste and spread about ¼ to ⅓ inch thick on a tray. It dries in the lowest possible oven, usually 200°F, for 4 to 5 hours until dry but still a bit sticky to the touch. The sheet is cut and rolled and used for the Ramadan drink.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Blender, Baking sheet lined with parchment

  • 1 pound dried apricot
  • 1 ¼ cups water
  • ¼ - ⅓ cup sugar or to your liking
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice

Method

  1. Cook the apricots in the water with the sugar and lemon until very tender.
  2. Blend to a spreadable paste.
  3. Spread about ¼ to ⅓ inch thick on a lined tray.
  4. Dry in the lowest possible oven, usually 200°F, for 4 to 5 hours, until dry but still a bit sticky to the touch.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: apricots very tender; paste spreadable; sheet dry but a bit sticky. This is oven drying, not fermenting.
  • The page's own typos ('bled', 'Bale') were read as 'blend' and 'bake'; check the live page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Drying until brittle; it should stay slightly sticky.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim says qamar el din juice is the first thing people think of during Ramadan, and that the finest paste is usually made in Syria.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Airtight at room temperature for weeks (our estimate).

Reheating: Not needed.

Serve with: Soaked in water as qamar el din juice (page tips)
