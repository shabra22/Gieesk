🥘 Kebda Eskandarani (Alexandrian-Style Spicy Liver)

Kebda Iskandarani — كبدة اسكندراني

Thin strips of beef or veal liver marinated in garlic, coriander, cumin and vinegar, fried hot in batches with chili and green pepper and tucked into warm Feeno bread with tahini sauce and pickles.

8h 45mTotal time
30mPrep
15mCook
6Servings
480Calories
MediumDifficulty

Liver is frozen 1 to 2 hours, sliced into thin strips, soaked in milk in the fridge overnight, and marinated 2 to 6 hours in a garlic, coriander, cumin and vinegar mix. A tablespoon of marinade sizzles in hot oil, then the liver goes in batches, left untouched for 2 minutes and then stirred; the page says it takes no more than 7 minutes in all and stays soft and red from the inside. Julienned mild green pepper goes in as the heat is turned off, then lime juice, and it is served in warm Feeno bread with tahini and pickles.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large skillet, Mixing bowls

  • 1 Kg / 2.2 Pounds Beef or Veal Liver
  • Liver marinade, recipe follows
  • ½ cup vegetable oil, such as canola
  • 1 whole green chili pepper
  • 1 whole red chili pepper
  • 1 -2 mild green pepper, julienned
  • 1 cup tahini Sauce
  • Egyptian "Feeno" bread, or Bread Rolls

Marinade

  • 1/8-1/4 cup minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ground coriander
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon Himalayan salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon allspice, optional
  • ½-1 teaspoon chili flakes, optional
  • ¼ cup white distilled vinegar
  • Juice of one lime
  • 1 teaspoon harissa, optional

Tahini sauce

  • ½ cup Tahini paste
  • ¼ cup yogurt, optional
  • ¼ cup water, at room temperature
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder, or 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon Himalayan salt

Also

  • ½ cup full fat milk (for the soak)

Method

  1. Freeze the liver 1 to 2 hours, then slice it into thin strips.
  2. Soak in about ½ cup of full-fat milk in the fridge overnight, then drain.
  3. Mix the marinade and marinate the liver at least 2 to 6 hours.
  4. Mix the tahini sauce until smooth.
  5. Heat the oil, add 1 tablespoon of the marinade and cook until fragrant but not browned or burnt.
  6. Add the liver in batches. Leave it untouched for 2 minutes, then stir. The page says it takes no more than 7 minutes to cook the liver through and that it should stay soft and red from the inside.
  7. Add the julienned green pepper, turn off the heat immediately and finish with lime juice. Serve in warm Feeno bread with tahini and pickles.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: fragrant (not browned) marinade; liver soft and red inside. The page prints no internal temperature or food-safety statement, so check your own comfort level with the red centre. The freezer, milk soak and marinade are NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The card adds the hot chili after the marinade turns fragrant and omits the 'soft and red' cue; the body includes it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking, which makes liver rubbery.
  • Crowding the pan.

Health Benefits

  • Liver is a rich source of iron and vitamin A.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour explains that kebda means liver and Iskandarani refers to Alexandria; she recalls a childhood street cart on the Bahary corniche and suggests melting lamb tail fat (liya) before adding the oil.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten at once.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Tahini sauce, Pickles, Feeno bread
