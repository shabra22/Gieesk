🥥 Tanzanian Chicken Coconut Cream

Kuku wa Cream ya Nazi

Kuku wa Cream ya Nazi — Zanzibar chicken simmered in pure thick coconut cream with garlic, ginger, cardamom, and coriander. The most luxurious, richest chicken preparation on the Tanzanian coast — the coconut cream creates a sauce of extraordinary richness.

Chicken in coconut cream is the Zanzibar kitchen at its most indulgent — the pure thick coconut cream (not milk) creates a sauce that is richer and creamier than any dairy-based preparation. The cardamom and coriander perfume the coconut cream into something ethereal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan with lid

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 400ml thick coconut cream
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 cardamom pods — bruised
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Brown chicken pieces. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, cardamom, and turmeric.
  3. Return chicken.
  4. Pour in coconut cream.
  5. Simmer very gently (never boil) for 30—35 minutes until chicken is cooked and sauce is thick.
  6. Add lemon juice and coriander. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Coconut cream — not coconut milk. The difference in richness is dramatic.
  • Gentle simmer throughout — boiling coconut cream splits it.
  • Lemon at the end brightens the richness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling — coconut cream splits.
  • Using coconut milk instead of cream.

The Science

Coconut cream (25% fat) produces a naturally thickening sauce as the water evaporates during gentle simmering — concentrating the fat into a creamy coating. Hard boiling breaks the emulsion, producing a greasy, separated sauce.

Cultural Note

Kuku wa Cream ya Nazi is the Zanzibar kitchen's most indulgent preparation — a dish that demonstrates what coconut cream can do when treated with the respect it deserves. It is the chicken dish that makes guests close their eyes when they taste it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Very gently — never boil.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004), Kachumbari
