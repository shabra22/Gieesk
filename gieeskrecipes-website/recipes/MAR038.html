🥣 Moroccan Lentils (Addis)

Addis — brown lentils with cumin (العدس)

Brown lentils pressure-cooked with onion, tomatoes, cumin and turmeric into a thick, savoury stew, a staple for its affordability.

40mTotal time
10mPrep

4Servings

Onion is sautéed about 5 minutes in olive oil, spices go in, then the lentils, tomatoes, carrots, water and a whole hot pepper. A pressure cook of 20 minutes plus a 5-minute rest leaves the lentils very soft with a thick sauce. The author warns the recipe does not work with red lentils.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker or Instant Pot

  • 1/4 c chopped onion
  • ¼ c olive oil
  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder or 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 3-4 grated medium tomatoes or 200 mL crushed canned tomatoes
  • ¼ - ½ cup diced carrots (optional)
  • Whole hot pepper (optional)
  • 1 cup water (the method adds water and saffron that are not listed; see sources)

Method

  1. Sauté the onion in the oil about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the spices, then the drained lentils, tomatoes, carrots, water and hot pepper.
  3. Cook under pressure for 20 minutes, then rest for 5 minutes.
  4. The lentils should be very soft with a thick sauce remaining.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: lentils very soft and a thick sauce left.
  • The author warns: this recipe DOES NOT work with red lentils.
  • Moroccan lentils are a staple meal thanks to their affordability.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using red lentils, which collapse.
  • Opening the cooker before the rest.
  • Adding too much water.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Warm with a splash of water.

Serve with: Khobz
