🍢 Brochettes de soja (Soy Skewers)

Brochettes de soja

Soy soaked 12 hours, ground and filtered into soy milk, boiled, curdled with tamarind juice added gradually, pressed 20 minutes, cubed, skewered with onion and fried.

p. 39 of the October 2023 recipe book: 300 g soy, ½ litre tamarind juice, 4 onions and ½ litre of water, plus green condiments, bell pepper, garlic, salt, refined oil and skewers. The soy is soaked 12 hours (the page prints prep 1 hour, cook 1 hour), crushed and winnowed, ground wet and filtered; the soy milk is boiled; tamarind juice is added gradually and ‘causes the proteins to precipitate into flakes’; filtered to a curd; seasoned; pressed 20 minutes; cut in cubes, skewered with onion and fried. This is not a fermentation — tamarind acts as the coagulant. Seasoning amounts and the frying time are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grinder, Pot, Cloth, Press, Skewers, Frying pan

  • 300 g soy
  • 1/2 litre tamarind juice
  • 4 onions
  • 1/2 litre water
  • Green condiments, bell pepper, garlic, salt — quantities not printed
  • Refined oil for frying — quantity not printed
  • Skewers

Method

  1. Soak the soy 12 hours. Crush and winnow.
  2. Grind wet and filter. Boil the soy milk.
  3. Add the tamarind juice gradually until the proteins precipitate into flakes.
  4. Filter to a curd and season.
  5. Press 20 minutes. Cut in cubes.
  6. Skewer the cubes with onion and fry.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is that the proteins ‘precipitate into flakes’ when the tamarind juice goes in.
  • The frying time is not printed.
  • Seasoning quantities are absent.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the tamarind juice all at once.
  • Skipping the pressing.
  • Frying at too high a heat so the cubes burn.
