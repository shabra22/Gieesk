🦆 Stuffed Roast Duck (Egyptian Family Recipe)

Bataa mahshi — بط محشي

A whole duck stuffed with an onion, cumin and lime mixture, roasted slowly in an oven bag, then browned under the broiler for crisp skin.

3h 40mTotal time
30mPrep
3hCook
8Servings
560Calories
MediumDifficulty

A 5 to 6 pound duck is patted dry, stuffed with chopped onion mixed with salt, pepper, cumin and oil and a squeeze of lime, closed with toothpicks and its legs tied. It roasts at 350°F in an oven bag for 2½ to 3 hours, then is browned under the broiler or in a pot with oil and rested 5 to 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Roasting pan, Oven bag, Toothpicks, Kitchen string

  • 2 onion (450g) roughly chopped.
  • 2 teaspoons salt or to taste.
  • ½ - 1 Tablespoon black pepper.
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin.
  • ½ a lime.
  • 1 Tablespoon olive oil.
  • 5-6 pounds whole duck cleaned.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and pat the duck dry.
  2. Mix the onion with salt, pepper, cumin and oil, and squeeze the lime over it.
  3. Stuff the cavity, leaving 2 to 3 tablespoons aside, and close with 3 to 4 toothpicks.
  4. Rub the duck with the squeezed lime and then the reserved mixture, and tie the legs.
  5. Roast in an oven bag for 2½ to 3 hours. The card says to reach at least 165°F; the instructions section says the breast reaches 155°F.
  6. Brown under the broiler or in oil in a pot on the stovetop, then rest 5 to 10 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: internal temperature at least 165°F (card). The two pages' temperatures differ (165°F versus 155°F in the instructions). The oven-bag step was read once only.
  • The author says stuffing with oranges or apples is not authentically Middle Eastern.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking; check the thickest part with a thermometer.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim writes it is a family recipe from her father's side, that Muscovy ducks are the most popular in Egypt, and that her family serves it with baked rice. Her grandmother cleaned the duck with kosher salt and flour and soaked it in vinegared water.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate carved meat up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat covered in a moderate oven.

Serve with: Egyptian rice with vermicelli
