🦐 Spicy Shrimp Tagine (Shrimp Pil-Pil)

Gambas pil-pil — spicy shrimp tagine

Large shrimp laid on a pulpy grated-tomato, garlic and herb base with paprika and cayenne, then covered and cooked with lemon slices for 10 minutes.

40mTotal time
15mPrep
25mCook
4Servings

EasyDifficulty

Olive oil, grated tomatoes, garlic, parsley, cilantro, paprika, cayenne, cumin, a bay leaf, salt and pepper are cooked uncovered over medium heat in a tagine or skillet until the tomatoes are a deeper red and pulpy, about 12 minutes. The shrimp go on top for 1 minute, are turned, lemon slices are placed around the edge with 2 tablespoons water at the side, and the dish is covered and cooked for 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine or wide skillet with lid

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 ripe medium tomatoes, halved, seeded, and grated (see Note)
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Tbsp finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 Tbsp finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper or more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 1/2 lb/680 g large shrimp, peeled, tails left on (about 3/4 lb/340 g peeled)
  • 3 slices lemon, halved

Method

  1. Combine the oil, tomatoes, garlic, herbs, spices, bay leaf, salt and pepper in the tagine or skillet.
  2. Cook uncovered over medium heat until the tomatoes are a deeper red and pulpy, about 12 minutes.
  3. Place the shrimp on top and cook for 1 minute, then turn.
  4. Place the lemon slices around the edges, dribble 2 Tbsp water in at the side, cover with the lid and cook for 10 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: tomato base a deeper red and pulpy. The shrimp cooking (1 minute, then 10 covered) is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • Grating halved, seeded tomatoes gives the base its pulpy texture.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the shrimp so they turn rubbery.
  • Skipping the water, so the base scorches.

Cultural Note

Koehler calls the dish a favourite of northern Morocco, commonly named shrimp al pil-pil.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten right away.

Reheating: Warm gently; do not boil.

Serve with: Bread
