🍽️ Ethiopian Dessert Platter

ጣፋጭ ሳህን — Tafach Sahin

Ethiopian Dessert Platter — the complete Ethiopian sweet experience. Honey Cookies, Sesame Candy, Coconut Balls, Coffee Truffles, Honey Banana, Date Cake, and the Ethiopian Fruit Platter on one celebration table.

The Ethiopian Dessert Platter is the sweet conclusion to the great feast — assembling the finest Ethiopian sweets in one presentation that moves from ancient (sesame candy, honey cookies) to modern (coffee truffles) and from simple (coconut balls) to elaborate (date cake).

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple baking sheets and pans, Large serving boards

    Assemble from individual recipes:

    • Honey Cookies
    • Sesame Honey Candy
    • Coconut Balls
    • Ethiopian Coffee Truffles
    • Honey Banana — served warm
    • Date Honey Cake — sliced
    • Ethiopian Fruit Platter

    Method

    1. Days before: make honey cookies, sesame candy, coconut balls, date cake — all keep well.
    2. Day before: make coffee truffles.
    3. Day of: prepare fruit platter. Make honey bananas to order.
    4. Assemble on a large board: sweets in centre, fruit at edges, warm bananas in a dish.

    Chef Tips

    • The dry sweets (cookies, candy, coconut balls) can all be made well ahead.
    • Coffee truffles are the sophisticated centrepiece.
    • Honey bananas should be served warm — make last.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Attempting the honey bananas ahead of time.

    Health Benefits

    • Ethiopian sweets use honey rather than refined sugar as the primary sweetener — providing antioxidants, antimicrobial compounds, and a lower glycaemic impact than pure sugar confections.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian Dessert Platter is the sweet proof that Ethiopian food culture extends to dessert with the same depth and variety as its main courses — ancient and modern, simple and complex, honey and chocolate, sesame and coffee. It is Ethiopia's sweet soul on one table.
