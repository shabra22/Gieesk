🍮 Mahalabia (Egyptian Milk Pudding)

Mahalabiya — مهلبية

Cold milk whisked with cornstarch and sugar, heated until it bubbles and thickens, flavoured with vanilla and chilled to set.

1h 50mTotal time
10mPrep
10mCook
5Servings
210Calories
EasyDifficulty

Milk, sugar and cornstarch are whisked cold, then heated on medium, stirring constantly, until the mixture bubbles and thickens. The flavouring goes in off the heat; the pudding cools about 30 minutes and then chills for an hour to set. Traditional flavourings are orange blossom, rose water, vanilla and cardamom.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Whisk, Serving cups

  • 4 cups milk
  • ½ cup granulated sugar 100g or to taste
  • 6 Tablespoons cornstarch 60g
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • For garnishing: dried fruits, nuts, edible flowers or ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Whisk the milk, sugar and cornstarch together cold until smooth.
  2. Heat on medium, stirring constantly, until it bubbles and thickens.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the vanilla.
  4. Pour into cups, cool about 30 minutes, then refrigerate for an hour to set.
  5. Garnish with dried fruit, nuts or cinnamon.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: bubbles and thickens. The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The page misprints 'abbot' for 'about'.
  • Orange blossom, rose water and cardamom are traditional flavourings.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stopping stirring, which gives lumps.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim grew up making the cornstarch version, and lists orange blossom, rose water, vanilla and cardamom as traditional flavours and rose petals and pistachios as the traditional garnish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Rose petals and pistachios (traditional garnish)
