🥥 Roasted Mango Sombi

Sombi — coconut rice pudding

Coconut milk simmered with shredded coconut, sugar and vanilla until slightly thickened, cooked rice stirred in, finished with lime and served with honey-glazed mango slices and toasted coconut.

Thiam’s sombi, adapted from his 2008 book Yolélé!. Honey is cooked in a skillet until bubbly and mango slices are dipped in it and cooked about five minutes until golden-brown. In a pot, coconut milk, 6 tablespoons of shredded coconut, sugar and vanilla cook about 10 minutes, stirring, until slightly thickened; ½ cup of this sauce is reserved. The remaining coconut toasts in a skillet about 5 minutes. The rice goes into the pot and cooks about 5 minutes until all the liquid is absorbed; lime and salt go in and the vanilla pod is discarded. Bowls are drizzled with the reserved sauce, topped with the mango and toasted coconut, and served warm.

Ingredients

Equipment: Skillet, Medium pot, Tongs

  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 2 mangoes, peeled and sliced lengthwise
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened shredded coconut, divided
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise, seeds scraped, or 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup cooked white rice, preferably long-grain
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch kosher salt

Method

  1. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the honey until bubbly. Using tongs, dip the mango slices in the honey to coat and cook until golden-brown, about 5 minutes. Set aside on a plate.
  2. In a medium pot over medium heat, add the coconut milk, 6 tablespoons of the shredded coconut, the sugar and the vanilla and cook, stirring frequently, until slightly thickened, about 10 minutes. Reserve 1/2 cup of the sauce.
  3. In a skillet over low heat, cook the remaining shredded coconut, stirring occasionally, until lightly golden-brown, about 5 minutes. Remove from the heat.
  4. Put the pot over medium-low heat, add the rice and cook, stirring frequently, until all the liquid is absorbed, about 5 minutes. Stir in the lime juice and salt and discard the vanilla pod.
  5. Divide the pudding among bowls, drizzle the reserved sauce around the edges, lay the mango slices on top and garnish with the toasted coconut. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Five printed cues: honey ‘bubbly’; mango ‘golden-brown’; sauce ‘thickened slightly’; coconut ‘lightly golden-brown’; rice ‘until all the liquid is absorbed’.
  • The coconut arithmetic is consistent: ½ cup divided as 6 tablespoons and the remaining 2.
  • Start from cooked rice — the page does.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Scorching the honey.
  • Walking away from the coconut toasting.
  • Cooking the rice to a stiff paste instead of ‘liquid absorbed’.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

From the page: rice cultivation is the lifeblood of Casamance, from which sombi originates; ‘At sunset, one feels a special silence around the paddies as the rice shoots sway’. He finishes the classic Senegalese dessert with caramelised mango for brightness and toasted coconut for texture.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of coconut milk.

Serve with: Sweet tea
