🍰 Kenyan Passion Fruit Cheesecake

Cheesecake ya Passion ya Kenya

Kenyan Passion Fruit Cheesecake — a no-bake cream cheese cheesecake on a biscuit base, topped with a vivid, tart passion fruit curd that glistens beautifully. The definitive Nairobi bakery dessert — fresh, tropical, and completely irresistible.

Passion fruit cheesecake has become the most iconic dessert in Nairobi's thriving bakery scene. The no-bake cream cheese filling is rich and smooth, the biscuit base is buttery and crunchy, and the passion fruit curd topping is brilliantly tart and fragrant. Together they create a dessert that showcases Kenya's most distinctive fruit at its finest.

Ingredients

Equipment: 20cm springform tin, Electric beater, Small saucepan

    Base

    • 250g digestive biscuits — crushed
    • 120g butter — melted

    Filling

    • 500g cream cheese — room temperature
    • 200ml whipping cream
    • 4 tbsp sugar
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    • 2 tsp gelatin dissolved in 3 tbsp warm water — or 2 tsp agar agar

    Passion Fruit Topping

    • ½ cup passion fruit pulp — from about 8 passion fruits
    • 3 egg yolks
    • 4 tbsp sugar
    • 60g butter

    Method

    1. Make base: mix crushed biscuits with melted butter. Press firmly into the tin base. Refrigerate 20 minutes.
    2. Make passion fruit curd: whisk passion fruit, egg yolks, and sugar in a saucepan over low heat until thick — 8—10 minutes. Remove. Whisk in butter. Cool completely.
    3. Make filling: beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Add vanilla and dissolved gelatin.
    4. In a separate bowl, whip cream to soft peaks. Fold into cream cheese mixture.
    5. Pour filling over biscuit base. Smooth the top.
    6. Refrigerate for 4 hours until fully set.
    7. Spread passion fruit curd over the set cheesecake.
    8. Refrigerate 1 more hour. Unmould and serve.

    Chef Tips

    • The gelatin or agar agar is essential for a no-bake cheesecake that holds its shape when sliced.
    • Cool the curd completely before spreading — warm curd melts the cream cheese layer.
    • The cheesecake can be made 2 days ahead — it improves overnight.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Omitting gelatin — collapses when sliced.
    • Warm curd — melts the filling.

    The Science

    See KEN217 for passion fruit curd science. In the cheesecake, the gelatin creates a continuous protein-gel network throughout the cream cheese-cream mixture, allowing it to set firm enough to slice while remaining creamy — without baking.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate for up to 4 days.

    Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

    Serve with: Iced Kenyan Coffee, Festival Fruit Punch, Fresh Passion Fruit
