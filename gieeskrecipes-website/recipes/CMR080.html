🍉 Nectar de pastèque (Watermelon Drink)

Nectar de pastèque

Two large watermelons soaked 5 minutes in water with a little bleach, peeled, cut, crushed, pressed and filtered; water and sugar added and boiled 10 minutes, cooled and bottled.

p. 78 of the October 2023 recipe book: 2 large watermelons, 500 g sugar and 3 litres of water, with an optional preservative. Method as the mango drink: bleach soak 5 minutes, peel and cut, crush and press, filter, add water and sugar and boil 10 minutes, cool, add the preservative, bottle, keep cool. The size of the ‘large’ watermelons is not quantified; the diagram shows 70 °C for 5 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Press, Cloth, Sterilised bottles

  • 2 large watermelons (size not quantified)
  • 500 g sugar
  • 3 litres water
  • A little bleach (javel) for the soak — quantity not printed
  • Preservative (optional) — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Soak in drinking water with a little bleach for 5 minutes.
  2. Peel and cut. Crush and press. Filter.
  3. Add the water and sugar. Boil 10 minutes.
  4. Cool. Add the optional preservative.
  5. Bottle. Keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The page prints no doneness cue beyond the boil time.
  • The diagram shows 70 °C for 5 minutes against 10 minutes in the text.
  • The bleach quantity is not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Guessing the bleach dose.
  • Skipping the boil.
  • Leaving the seeds in the press.
