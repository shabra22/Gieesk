🌾 Tanzanian Nutritious Porridge

Uji wa Lishe

Uji wa Lishe — Tanzanian nutritious porridge made from a blend of finger millet, sorghum, and maize flours with groundnut paste, sweetened with honey or sugar. The health porridge given to children, new mothers, and those recovering from illness — packed with nutrients.

Uji wa Lishe (nutritious porridge) is Tanzania's answer to the question of how to pack the most nutrition into a single bowl. The combination of multiple grain flours with groundnut paste creates a porridge that is more nutritionally complete than any single grain alone. It is given to weaning children, nursing mothers, and anyone who needs nourishment.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan, Wooden spoon

  • ½ cup finger millet flour
  • ¼ cup sorghum flour
  • ¼ cup maize flour
  • 2 tbsp groundnut paste (peanut butter)
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tbsp honey or sugar
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix all flours and groundnut paste with 1 cup cold water to a smooth slurry.
  2. Bring remaining 3 cups water to a boil.
  3. Pour slurry into boiling water, stirring constantly.
  4. Cook over medium heat for 8—10 minutes until thick.
  5. Add honey and salt. Stir.
  6. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • The groundnut paste adds protein and healthy fats — do not omit.
  • Stir constantly to prevent lumping.
  • Add honey off the heat to preserve its natural enzymes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not making a slurry first — lumpy porridge.
  • Stopping stirring — sticks to the pot.

The Science

The combination of finger millet (high calcium and iron), sorghum (high antioxidants), maize (energy), and groundnuts (protein and fats) creates a nutritionally synergistic porridge where each ingredient contributes what the others lack.

Health Benefits

  • Complete nutrition from multiple grain sources.
  • Groundnuts add protein and healthy fats.
  • Finger millet provides exceptional calcium.
  • Naturally gluten-free.

Cultural Note

Uji wa Lishe reflects Tanzania's traditional nutritional wisdom — the understanding that combining multiple foods produces something more nourishing than any single ingredient. It is the porridge that Tanzanian grandmothers make for their grandchildren and daughters.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best consumed fresh.

Reheating: Warm gently with a splash of water.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast, For children and nursing mothers, With additional groundnuts for protein
