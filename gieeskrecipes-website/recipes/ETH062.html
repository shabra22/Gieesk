🍗 Ethiopian Mild Chicken Stew

አልጫ ዶሮ — Alicha Doro

Ethiopian Alicha Doro — the mild, golden-yellow chicken stew without berbere. Chicken slow-simmered in a sauce built on dry-caramelised onions, kibbeh, turmeric, cardamom, and ginger — with whole boiled eggs added at the end. The gentle counterpart to Doro Wat, served alongside on the same mesob platter.

Alicha Doro is the Doro Wat for those who prefer no heat — the same technique, the same ceremony, the same reverence, but with turmeric and cardamom rather than berbere producing a golden, aromatic, mild result. Served on every celebration platter alongside Doro Wat, providing balance.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 1 whole chicken — cut into 12 pieces
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp salt (marinade)
  • 4 onions — finely diced
  • 4 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1.5 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp ground coriander
  • 5 garlic cloves — minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 6 hard-boiled eggs — pierced all over

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in lemon and salt for 30 minutes. Rinse.
  2. Dry caramelise onions — 25 minutes, no oil.
  3. Add kibbeh. Cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add turmeric, ground ginger, cardamom, and coriander. Toast in kibbeh 1 minute.
  5. Add garlic and fresh ginger. Cook 2 minutes.
  6. Add chicken. Coat in the golden sauce.
  7. Add water. Simmer covered 75—80 minutes.
  8. Add pierced eggs. Simmer 20 more minutes.
  9. The sauce should be golden and thick.

Chef Tips

  • No berbere at all — this is genuinely mild.
  • The cardamom and coriander create the alicha aromatic signature.
  • Same dry-caramelise technique as Doro Wat — the foundation never changes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding berbere — destroys the alicha character.
  • Skipping dry-caramelisation.

The Science

See ETH027 for alicha science. In Alicha Doro, the turmeric's curcumin dissolves into the kibbeh fat, producing the golden colour. Without berbere's competing red carotenoids, the golden-yellow colour dominates the finished stew.

Health Benefits

  • Curcumin from turmeric — powerful anti-inflammatory.
  • Cardamom aids digestion.
  • High protein from chicken and eggs.
  • Suitable for those who cannot eat chilli.

Cultural Note

Alicha Doro demonstrates Ethiopian cooking's most profound quality — every great dish has its mild counterpart. The Doro Wat and Alicha Doro on the same platter ensure that every guest is welcomed, regardless of their tolerance for heat.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Alongside Doro Wat on the celebration platter, Ayib (ETH096)
