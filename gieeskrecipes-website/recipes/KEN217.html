🎂 Kenyan Celebration Cake

Keki ya Sherehe

Kenyan Celebration Cake — a moist, vanilla-scented butter cake layered with whipped cream and fresh passion fruit curd, decorated with tropical fruit. The birthday and graduation cake of Kenya — light, fragrant, and brilliantly tropical in its flavour profile.

The Kenyan celebration cake has a distinct identity — lighter than a classic British fruit cake, more fragrant with vanilla, and finished with the bright acidity of passion fruit. Whether it is a birthday, a graduation, a homecoming, or an engagement, this cake signals celebration with its soft crumb, generous cream layers, and the unmistakably Kenyan flavour of fresh passion fruit.

Ingredients

Equipment: Two 20cm round cake tins, Stand or hand mixer, Small saucepan for curd

  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 200g butter — softened
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk or natural yogurt
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Passion Fruit Curd

  • ½ cup passion fruit pulp — strained
  • 3 egg yolks
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 60g butter

Whipped Cream

  • 400ml whipping cream
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar

Decoration

  • Fresh tropical fruit — mango, kiwi, passion fruit

Method

  1. Make curd: combine passion fruit, egg yolks, and sugar in a saucepan over low heat. Stir until thickened — 8—10 minutes. Remove. Whisk in butter. Cool completely.
  2. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease and line tins.
  3. Beat butter and sugar until pale and fluffy — 5 minutes.
  4. Add eggs one at a time, beating well.
  5. Add vanilla. Alternate flour and buttermilk in three additions.
  6. Divide between tins. Bake 35—40 minutes until skewer comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely.
  8. Whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks.
  9. Fill: spread passion fruit curd on the bottom layer, then whipped cream.
  10. Place second layer. Frost top and sides with remaining cream.
  11. Decorate with fresh tropical fruit.

Chef Tips

  • The passion fruit curd can be made 3 days ahead — refrigerate until needed.
  • Completely cool layers before assembling — warm cake melts the cream.
  • Fresh tropical fruit on top is the Kenyan signature.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Assembling a warm cake.
  • Over-beating after adding flour — tough cake.

The Science

Passion fruit curd sets through the same mechanism as custard — egg proteins denature and coagulate during gentle heating, creating a thick, stable gel. The butter whisked in while hot creates an emulsion that gives the curd its characteristic glossy, smooth texture.

Cultural Note

The Kenyan celebration cake with passion fruit curd and tropical fruit decoration is a modern Kenyan classic — born from the combination of baking traditions and Kenya's extraordinary tropical fruit abundance. It is the cake that marks every important milestone in Kenyan family life.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate frosted cake for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Festival Fruit Punch, Kenyan Masala Chai, Fresh Mango Juice
