🫘 Ethiopian Roasted Chickpea Snack

ቡናማ ሽምብራ — Qolo Shimbra

Ethiopian Roasted Chickpea Snack — chickpeas soaked, dried, and dry-roasted until crunchy and golden. Part of the Qolo tradition (ETH018) — the dry-roasted snack eaten throughout the day, at church, at markets, and during coffee ceremonies.

Roasted chickpeas are the legume component of the Ethiopian Qolo snack tradition — eaten alongside roasted barley, they form the complete snack that sustains Ethiopian daily life. Crunchy, nutty, and satisfying.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy dry pan

  • 2 cups dried chickpeas — soaked overnight
  • 1 tsp salt

Optional: berbere, mitmita, or plain

    Method

    1. Soak chickpeas overnight. Drain. Spread on a cloth. Dry thoroughly.
    2. Heat a heavy dry pan over medium heat.
    3. Add chickpeas. Roast, stirring constantly, for 20—25 minutes.
    4. They should be golden, crunchy throughout, and nutty-smelling.
    5. Season with salt while hot.
    6. Cool completely before eating.

    Chef Tips

    • Must be completely dry before roasting — wet chickpeas steam instead of roast.
    • Cool before eating — they crisp fully as they cool.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Wet chickpeas — steam and become tough rather than crunchy.

    The Science

    See ETH018 for roasted grain and legume science. Chickpeas contain more protein than barley — producing more Maillard browning during dry roasting, which is why roasted chickpeas develop a deeper colour and more complex nutty flavour than roasted barley.

    Cultural Note

    Roasted chickpeas are shared at Ethiopian coffee ceremonies, at churches, at market stalls, and between friends — they are the social snack of Ethiopian community life, always available, always welcome.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Airtight container — 2 weeks.

    Reheating: Not needed.

    Serve with: As part of Qolo (ETH018), During the Ethiopian coffee ceremony, As a snack
