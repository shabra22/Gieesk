Torta Barozzi

A dense, dark, naturally flourless chocolate and hazelnut cake from Vignola, its exact original recipe kept a closely guarded secret since the 1900s, though close home versions abound.

The original torta Barozzi recipe, created by a Vignola pastry shop over a century ago, remains a trade secret to this day, but the cake's intensely fudgy, coffee-and-rum-scented character has inspired countless home versions that capture its spirit closely.

Ingredients

Equipment: Round baking pan, Double boiler or heatproof bowl, Electric mixer

  • 200 g (7 oz) dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150 g (2/3 cup) unsalted butter
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
  • 100 g (1 cup) hazelnuts, toasted and ground
  • 40 g (1/3 cup) cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp strong brewed espresso
  • 2 tbsp dark rum
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together gently over a double boiler, then let cool slightly.
  2. Whisk the egg yolks with half the sugar until pale and thick.
  3. Fold in the melted chocolate mixture, ground hazelnuts, cornstarch, espresso, and rum.
  4. Whip the egg whites with the remaining sugar to stiff peaks.
  5. Gently fold the egg whites into the chocolate mixture in stages, taking care not to deflate it.
  6. Pour into a well-buttered round baking pan.
  7. Bake at 170C (340F) for 35-40 minutes until the top is set but the center remains slightly fudgy.
  8. Cool completely before dusting with powdered sugar and slicing.

Chef Tips

  • Like torta caprese, the center should remain slightly underdone when removed from the oven, firming up as it cools into a dense, fudgy texture.
  • The espresso and rum are essential to the cake traditional flavor profile - do not omit them.
  • Cooling completely before slicing is important, as the cake is quite soft and delicate while warm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overbaking, which dries out the cake and loses its intended dense, fudgy character.
  • Slicing too soon, before the structure has had time to fully set while cooling.
  • Skipping the coffee and rum, which are key to the cake distinctive traditional flavor.

Cultural Note

Torta Barozzi originated in Vignola over a century ago, and the original recipe remains a closely guarded trade secret to this day, inspiring countless close home adaptations across Emilia-Romagna.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store covered at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature or slightly chilled.

Serve with: Whipped cream, Espresso
