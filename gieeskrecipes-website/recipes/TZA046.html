🥔 Tanzanian Beef and Potato Stew

Mchuzi wa Nyama na Viazi

Mchuzi wa Nyama na Viazi — Tanzanian beef and potato stew. Tender beef and potatoes simmered together in a rich spiced tomato sauce until both are falling-soft and the sauce is thick and glossy. The complete, filling, everyday Tanzanian meal in one pot.

Beef and potato stew is the one-pot meal that Tanzania's families rely on — the potatoes bulk out the beef, thicken the sauce, and create a complete, satisfying meal that stretches further than beef alone. It is eaten with ugali, chapati, or rice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g beef — cubed
  • 400g potatoes — peeled and cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown beef. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes.
  4. Return beef. Add water. Simmer 35 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes. Simmer 20—25 more minutes until potatoes are tender.
  6. The potatoes naturally thicken the sauce.
  7. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Add potatoes after the beef is halfway cooked — they cook much faster.
  • The potatoes thicken the sauce naturally — no flour needed.
  • Do not stir too much once potatoes are added — they break apart.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding potatoes at the start — they disintegrate.
  • Stirring too much with potatoes in.

The Science

Potato starch leaches into the sauce during cooking, naturally thickening it without the need for flour or cornstarch. This starch thickening also creates a slightly glossy, silky consistency.

Cultural Note

Beef and potato stew is Tanzania's most practical everyday dish — the meal that feeds a family on a modest budget without compromising on flavour or satisfaction.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently — potatoes can break apart.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004), Rice
