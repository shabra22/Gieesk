🐟 Scapece di Vasto

Scapece Vastese

Small fish lightly fried and then marinated in saffron-tinted vinegar for days, an Adriatic coastal specialty from Vasto designed originally to preserve the day's catch.

Scapece is a preservation technique with roots stretching back to ancient Rome, and this Vasto version's distinctive saffron-yellow color comes from steeping the spice directly in the vinegar marinade the fried fish rests in for several days before eating.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan, Large glass or ceramic container for marinating

  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) small whole fish (such as anchovies or smelts), cleaned
  • Flour, for dredging
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • 500 ml (2 cups) white wine vinegar
  • Pinch of saffron threads
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Dredge the cleaned fish lightly in flour, shaking off the excess.
  2. Heat vegetable oil and fry the fish in batches until golden and crisp, then drain on paper towels.
  3. Warm the vinegar gently with the saffron and garlic, letting the saffron infuse and color the vinegar a deep yellow.
  4. Let the vinegar mixture cool slightly.
  5. Layer the fried fish in a glass or ceramic container and pour the warm vinegar mixture over, ensuring the fish is well covered.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 days, ideally longer, allowing the fish to fully absorb the marinade.
  7. Serve cold or at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Small, whole fish work best for this preparation, as they fully absorb the vinegar marinade.
  • Letting the saffron properly infuse the warm vinegar is what gives scapece its distinctive golden color.
  • This dish genuinely needs time — the longer marinating period is what develops its characteristic tangy flavor.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing the marinating time, missing the full flavor development this preservation method depends on.
  • Using fish that are too large, which don't absorb the marinade as effectively.
  • Adding the fish to hot vinegar, which can cause it to break apart.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Scapece is one of the oldest known Italian food preservation techniques, tracing back to Roman times, and the Vasto version remains a specialty of Abruzzo's Adriatic coast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate in its marinade for up to 1 week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served cold or at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread
