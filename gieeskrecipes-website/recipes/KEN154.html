🍟 Kenyan Sweet Cassava Chips

Chips za Muhogo Tamu

Kenyan Sweet Cassava Chips — thin cassava slices boiled briefly then deep-fried until golden and crispy, tossed in cinnamon sugar while hot. A sweet-savoury Kenyan snack with a naturally creamy interior and wonderfully crunchy exterior.

Sweet cassava chips take the cassava chip concept in a dessert direction — the cinnamon sugar coating applied while the chips are hot creates a lightly caramelised, fragrant surface that makes them completely addictive. They sit happily between snack and dessert, equally at home with tea or as an after-school treat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large saucepan, Mandoline or sharp knife, Deep frying pan, Paper towels

  • 500g fresh cassava — peeled
  • Oil for deep frying
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of nutmeg — optional

Method

  1. Peel cassava, remove the woody central fibre, and slice into thin rounds (3—4mm).
  2. Pre-boil the cassava slices in salted water for 8—10 minutes until just partially tender. Drain and dry thoroughly.
  3. Mix sugar, cinnamon, salt, and nutmeg in a bowl. Set aside.
  4. Heat oil to 175°C.
  5. Fry cassava slices in batches for 3—4 minutes until golden and crispy.
  6. Remove immediately and drain briefly.
  7. While still hot, toss in the cinnamon sugar mixture.
  8. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Pre-boiling is essential for cassava chips — raw cassava will not cook through in frying time.
  • Dry thoroughly after boiling — wet cassava spatters dangerously in hot oil.
  • Season immediately while hot — the sugar adheres and lightly caramelises on the warm surface.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the pre-boil — raw inside.
  • Wet cassava in the fryer — dangerous splatter.
  • Not seasoning while hot — the cinnamon sugar does not adhere.

The Science

Pre-boiling gelatinises the cassava starch, creating the characteristically creamy interior. The subsequent frying dehydrates the exterior and creates the crunchy shell through Maillard browning.

Cultural Note

Sweet cassava chips are a beloved coastal and increasingly national snack that showcases the versatility of cassava — Kenya's most underrated root vegetable.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately — loses crispiness within an hour.

Reheating: Oven at 190°C for 5 minutes.

Serve with: Kenyan Masala Chai, As a standalone snack, With fresh fruit
