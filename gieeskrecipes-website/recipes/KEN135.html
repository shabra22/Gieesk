🥭 Kenyan Mango Juice

Juisi ya Embe

Kenyan Mango Juice — ripe Kenyan mangoes blended smooth, diluted to the perfect consistency, and lightly sweetened. Kenya grows some of the finest mangoes in the world — when they are in season, this juice is extraordinary.

Kenya produces over 20 commercial mango varieties, from the Kent and Apple mangoes of the coast to the Ngowe of the Rift Valley and the Tommy Atkins of Central Kenya. When mangoes are in season, every roadside stall overflows with them, and fresh mango juice — thick, sweet, and intensely fruity — is available on every corner. It is arguably the finest fruit juice Kenya produces.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Fine strainer, Jug

  • 4 large ripe mangoes — peeled and diced
  • 2—3 cups cold water
  • 1—2 tbsp sugar — if mangoes need it
  • 1 tbsp lime or lemon juice — optional
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ice — to serve

Method

  1. Peel and dice the mangoes, removing all flesh from the stone.
  2. Blend the mango flesh with 1 cup of water until completely smooth.
  3. Add remaining water to reach desired consistency — thicker is better for fresh mango juice.
  4. Strain through a fine mesh strainer for a smooth juice (or skip straining for a mango nectar).
  5. Add sugar only if needed — ripe mangoes may need none.
  6. Add lime juice and pinch of salt.
  7. Serve over ice immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Use the ripest, most fragrant mangoes available — the quality of the mango is everything.
  • Keep it thick — Kenyan mango juice should have body, not be watery.
  • A squeeze of lime balances the sweetness and sharpens the tropical flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-ripe mangoes — the juice is tart and lacks the characteristic sweetness.
  • Too much water — dilutes the glorious mango flavour.

The Science

Ripe mango flesh contains primarily sucrose, fructose, and glucose, plus over 300 identified aroma compounds including terpenes that give mango its characteristic tropical fragrance. The fully ripe fruit has the highest sugar content and most developed aroma profile — which is why ripeness is the most important factor in mango juice.

Health Benefits

  • Rich in vitamin C and vitamin A (from beta-carotene).
  • Good source of folate and vitamin B6.
  • High in antioxidants.
  • Naturally energising from natural fruit sugars.

Cultural Note

Mango season in Kenya is a celebration in itself — the markets overflow with mangoes at every price point, and fresh mango juice becomes one of the most anticipated seasonal drinks. The variety and quality of Kenyan mangoes is genuinely world-class.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 2 days. Drink fresh for the best flavour.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Mandazi, Any Kenyan breakfast, Fried Chicken, Chips Masala
