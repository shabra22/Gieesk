🥥 Ghoriba with Evaporated Milk (Optional Coconut Coating)

Ghoriba au lait concentré — soft cake-like cookies

Soft, cake-like cookies of eggs, oil, evaporated milk and lemon zest, baked pale golden and optionally rolled in orange flower water and coconut.

40mTotal time

Eggs and oil are beaten until frothy, then the evaporated milk, sugar, baking powder, vanilla, zest and salt go in, followed by just enough flour (starting with 3.5 cups) to make a shapeable dough. Walnut-sized balls are flattened, optionally dipped in orange flower water and then desiccated coconut, and baked at 350°F (180°C) about 15 minutes until lightly coloured or pale golden.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowls, Three baking sheets lined with parchment paper

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened evaporated milk - (Purisima)
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 1 to 2 tsp vanilla - (or 1 to 2 envelopes vanilla-flavored sugar)
  • zest from 1 or 2 lemons
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 4 cups flour - (approx.; use more or less as needed)

To finish

  • powdered sugar
  • orange flower water
  • desiccated coconut

Method

  1. Beat the eggs and oil until frothy. Mix in the evaporated milk, sugar, baking powder, vanilla, zest and salt.
  2. Add just enough flour to make a dough you can shape, starting with 3.5 cups and adding more as needed.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and line three baking sheets with parchment.
  4. Shape walnut-sized balls and flatten slightly. For the coconut version, dip each in orange flower water and then in desiccated coconut. (Shaping is from a single reading of the page.)
  5. Bake one pan at a time, about 15 minutes, until lightly coloured or pale golden. Cool before dusting with powdered sugar if you like.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: lightly coloured or pale golden.
  • Add flour gradually; the page gives an approximate amount.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all the flour at once.
  • Baking until deep brown.

Cultural Note

The page names a Spanish evaporated-milk brand (Purisima) in the title and describes a soft, cake-like cookie rather than the crumbly sesame type.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The page says up to a week at room temperature, or up to two months frozen.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Mint tea
