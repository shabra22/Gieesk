🍲 Ndolé with Beef, Prawns and Peanuts (Afro Fusion style)

Ndolé

Bone-in beef simmered with onion and seasoning, peanuts blended with ginger, celery, leek and garlic, bitter leaf cooked to a thick paste, and 30 prawns fried with garlic and onion as the topping.

Parboiled fresh peanuts are blended with ginger, onion, habanero, leek, celery and three garlic cloves; oil is heated, a diced onion sautéed, and the beef and peanut paste added and simmered 25 minutes, stirring every 5 to 10 minutes so it does not scorch. Crayfish and the bitter leaf go in and cook 10 to 15 minutes until a thick paste forms. The garnish is 30 prawns fried about 3 minutes with minced garlic until pink, then a minced onion for 3 to 5 minutes until translucent, spooned over about 5 minutes before serving. Served with boiled sweet plantain or corn fufu.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender, Frying pan

  • 1.5 lbs bitter leaf
  • 1.5 lbs beef with bones, cut into pieces
  • 4 cups large yellow onions, finely chopped or sliced (divided; reserve 1 for garnish)
  • 1/2 cup celery (1 branch)
  • 20 g fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons Afro Fusion All Purpose Seasoning (brand mix — contents not printed)
  • 6 garlic cloves (divided)
  • 3 cups fresh peanuts, de-pulped and parboiled 5 minutes
  • 1/2 cup leeks, scallions or green onions
  • 1/4 habanero or bird’s eye chilli (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 Maggi bouillon cubes (divided)
  • 1/2 cup peanut oil (divided), or to taste
  • 3 cups beef stock (listed; no step uses it)
  • 30 medium prawns
  • 1 cup crayfish (majanga)

Method

  1. Simmer the beef with half an onion, the seasoning, bouillon and salt over medium heat until tender. (The time is not printed.)
  2. Blend the peanuts with the ginger, half an onion, habanero, leek, celery and 3 garlic cloves.
  3. Heat the oil, sauté one diced onion, then add the beef and the peanut paste. Simmer 25 minutes, stirring every 5 to 10 minutes so it does not scorch.
  4. Adjust salt and bouillon and stir in the crayfish. Add the bitter leaf and cook 10 to 15 minutes until it forms a thick paste.

Garnish

  1. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil and fry the prawns with 3 minced garlic cloves for about 3 minutes, until pink. Add one minced onion and cook 3 to 5 minutes, until translucent.
  2. Spoon the garnish over the ndolé about 5 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: ‘until tender’ (beef), ‘thick paste’ (bitter leaf), ‘until pink’ (prawns), ‘until translucent’ (onion).
  • Stir every 5 to 10 minutes during the 25-minute simmer.
  • The seasoning blend is a brand product; its contents are not printed, so substitute your own seasoning knowingly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Looking for where the 3 cups of beef stock go — the method never uses them.
  • Adding the garnish too early; the page spoons it on about 5 minutes before serving.
  • Skipping bitter leaf preparation: the page gives none (ABSENT).
