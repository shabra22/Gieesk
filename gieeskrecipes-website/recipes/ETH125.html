🍆 Ethiopian Eggplant Stew

ሐምሊ ወጥ — Hamli Wat

Ethiopian Eggplant Stew — eggplant cooked in a berbere-tomato sauce with onion and garlic. The eggplant absorbs the berbere sauce into its flesh completely, producing a rich, deeply flavoured stew that eats like a meat dish.

Ethiopian eggplant stew is the great deception of the fasting platter — the eggplant's spongy flesh absorbs the berbere sauce so completely that it produces a deeply rich, almost meaty stew. Non-vegetarians who eat it often cannot believe it contains no meat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan

  • 2 large eggplants — cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Salt eggplant cubes. Rest 15 minutes. Rinse and pat dry.
  2. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  3. Add garlic and berbere. Stir 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  5. Add eggplant. Stir to coat.
  6. Cover and cook 15—18 minutes until completely tender.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Salting and rinsing removes excess moisture — eggplant fries rather than steams.
  • The eggplant will absorb the sauce completely — do not add more water.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not salting first — watery result.

The Science

Eggplant's spongy flesh (air-filled cell structure) absorbs oil and water-soluble flavour compounds with exceptional efficiency — it acts as a flavour sponge. Salting before cooking draws out moisture via osmosis, partially collapsing the air cells and reducing the vegetable's capacity for absorbing excess oil during cooking.

Health Benefits

  • Eggplant is very low in calories despite its filling texture.
  • Rich in nasunin — an antioxidant in the skin.
  • Good source of fibre.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian eggplant stew is the fasting platter's most satisfying surprise — the dish that demonstrates how Ethiopian vegetarian cooking achieves the depth and richness of meat dishes entirely through plant-based means.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter
