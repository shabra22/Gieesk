🥟 Panzerotti Pugliesi

Panzerotti

Fried half-moon pockets of pizza dough stuffed with molten mozzarella and tomato, Puglia's beloved street food answer to a hand-held pizza.

Panzerotti are essentially small, fried calzones, their pizza-like dough sealed tightly around a simple mozzarella and tomato filling and fried until the outside turns crisp and golden while the cheese inside stays gloriously molten.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Deep pot for frying

  • 500 g (4 cups) 00 flour
  • 7 g (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 300 ml (1¼ cups) warm water
  • 10 g (1¾ tsp) salt
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) mozzarella, diced and well drained
  • 200 g (7 oz) tomato passata
  • Fresh basil
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, salt, olive oil, and yeast mixture into a smooth dough, kneading for 8-10 minutes.
  3. Let rise, covered, in a warm place for about 1.5 hours until doubled.
  4. Combine the diced mozzarella with the passata, torn basil, salt, and pepper for the filling.
  5. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each into a thin round, about 12cm across.
  6. Place a spoonful of filling on one half of each round, fold over into a half-moon, and press the edges firmly to seal, crimping with a fork if needed.
  7. Heat vegetable oil to 180C (350F) and fry the panzerotti in batches until deeply golden, about 3-4 minutes per side.
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Draining the mozzarella well before filling prevents the panzerotti from becoming watery inside.
  • Seal the edges very thoroughly, pressing out any air, to prevent the filling from leaking into the frying oil.
  • Fry at a steady, moderate-high temperature -- too hot and the outside burns before the inside cooks through.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not sealing the edges well enough, causing the cheese filling to leak out during frying.
  • Using wet, undrained mozzarella, which can make the filling soggy.
  • Frying at too high a temperature, browning the outside before the dough cooks through.

Health Benefits

  • A good source of calcium and protein from the cheese, best enjoyed as an occasional treat given the frying.

Cultural Note

Panzerotti are a beloved street food throughout Puglia, particularly in Bari, sold from dedicated kiosks and enjoyed as a quick, satisfying snack.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh; refrigerate leftovers for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot oven to help re-crisp; avoid the microwave.

Serve with: A simple side salad
