🍗 Roast Chicken with Preserved Lemon, Olives and Onion Sauce

Djej m'qualli du four — roast chicken with daghmira

A whole chicken marinated overnight in chermoula, slow-roasted until the drumstick moves freely, and served over a daghmira of melted onions finished with olives and preserved lemon.

This is a roast chicken finished in sauce rather than a tagine. A whole bird is rubbed with salt, spices, saffron, garlic, cilantro, olive oil and lemon juice and refrigerated at least 8 hours. Meanwhile the onions for the daghmira cook down with ginger, turmeric, pepper and cilantro until they can be mashed with a spoon and look pureed. The chicken roasts at 325°F (160°C) for about 2 1/2 to 3 hours; the roasting juices and saffron go into the onions, then olives and preserved lemon wedges.

Ingredients

Equipment: Roasting pan, Heavy pot for the onions, Potato masher or spoon

    Chicken and marinade (chermoula)

    • 1 whole roasting chicken
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp pepper
    • 1/2 tsp ginger
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
    • 1/4 tsp saffron threads, crumbled
    • 1 small clove garlic, pressed or grated
    • 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (or finely chopped pulp from a wedge of preserved lemon)

    Onion sauce (daghmira)

    • 2 large yellow or white onions (preferably sweet)
    • 2 cloves garlic, pressed or finely chopped
    • 3 to 4 tbsp vegetable oil
    • 1 tsp ginger
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper
    • 1/4 tsp white pepper
    • 1/4 tsp salt
    • 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
    • 1 cinnamon stick (optional)
    • 1 tsp smen (optional)

    For finishing the sauce

    • 1 chicken liver (optional)
    • 1/2 cup juices from roasting pan (or more if needed)
    • 1/4 tsp saffron threads, crumbled
    • 1/2 cup red or green olives (or to taste)
    • 1 preserved lemon, cut into wedges and seeds removed

    Method

    1. Mix the marinade ingredients and rub them over and inside the chicken. Refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight.
    2. Cook the onion sauce: combine the onions, garlic, oil, spices, cilantro, cinnamon stick and smen in a pot, cover and cook over medium heat for 1 to 2 hours, until the onions are very soft and can be mashed with a spoon or vegetable masher.
    3. Take the chicken out of the fridge 3 to 4 hours before serving and bring it to room temperature.
    4. Roast in a preheated 325°F (160°C) oven, usually about 2 1/2 to 3 hours, until the chicken is well browned and the drumstick moves easily on its joint; juices should run clear when pierced.
    5. Rest the chicken 15 to 20 minutes while you finish the sauce.
    6. Mash the onions until reduced to a thick blended mass sitting in oil. Add the roasting juices, saffron, chicken liver if using, olives and preserved lemon wedges and simmer 5 to 10 minutes.
    7. Serve the chicken with the onion sauce spooned over.

    Chef Tips

    • Doneness cues quoted by the source: the drumstick moves easily on its joint, and juices run clear when pierced with a sharp knife.
    • The onions are done when they no longer look like onions and the mixture looks as though it has been pureed.
    • Moroccan tradition, says the source, is one chicken for every three people.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Roasting straight from the fridge. The source calls for bringing the bird to room temperature first.
    • Pulling the onions off the heat too soon. They should mash with a spoon.
    • Over-salting the sauce: preserved lemon and olives are already salty.

    Health Benefits

    • Lean protein from the chicken, with onions as the sauce rather than cream or flour.
    • Olives add monounsaturated fat; preserved lemon adds flavour with little energy.

    Cultural Note

    The page quotes a Moroccan tradition of one chicken per three people, and names the onion sauce by its Moroccan name, daghmira. It points readers to m'qualli-style cooking: garlic, turmeric, ginger, olives and preserved lemon.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Chicken and sauce keep 3 days in the fridge, stored separately.

    Reheating: Reheat the chicken in a 325°F oven, covered; warm the onion sauce gently on the stove.

    Serve with: Khobz, Preserved lemons
