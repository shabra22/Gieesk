🥭 Mango and Pumpkin Spice Cake

Gâteau mangue-potiron

A pumpkin-spice batter of flour, cinnamon, ginger, sugar, pumpkin puree, oil and four eggs with diced mango folded in, topped with fanned mango slices and baked about 2 hours at 325° in a 9-inch springform until a toothpick comes out clean.

Pierre Thiam adapted his wife’s classic pumpkin cake for his Thanksgiving table, adding mango both in the batter and fanned over the top. Published by SAVEUR on 16 November 2015: serves 8–10; total time 2 hours 20 minutes. The oven is heated to 325°; a 9-inch springform is greased and floured; flour, cinnamon, ginger, baking soda and salt are whisked in one bowl and the sugar, pumpkin puree, oil, vanilla and eggs in another until smooth; wet is added to dry and whisked just until smooth; half a mango is diced into 1/2-inch cubes and folded in; the batter is poured into the pan and smoothed. The remaining 1 1/2 mangoes are sliced lengthwise into 1/4-inch-thick slices and arranged in an overlapping circle on top, and the cake baked about 2 hours until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool completely on a wire rack, unmould onto a platter and dust with confectioners’ sugar.

Ingredients

Equipment: 9-inch springform cake pan, Two large bowls, Wire rack

  • Unsalted butter, for greasing — quantity not printed
  • 2 3/4 cups (12 3/8 oz) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 2 teaspoons ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 cups (14 oz) sugar
  • 1 cup canned or homemade pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 mangoes, peeled and pitted
  • Confectioners’ sugar, for dusting — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 325°. Grease and flour a 9-inch springform cake pan. Whisk the flour, cinnamon, ginger, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl whisk the sugar, pumpkin puree, oil, vanilla and eggs until smooth. Add the wet to the dry and whisk just until smooth.
  3. Dice half of one mango into 1/2-inch cubes and fold into the batter. Pour into the pan and smooth the top. No time is printed for this step.
  4. Slice the remaining 1 1/2 mangoes lengthwise into 1/4-inch-thick slices and arrange in an overlapping circle on top.
  5. Bake about 2 hours, until a toothpick inserted in the center of the cake comes out clean.
  6. Cool completely on a wire rack. Unmould onto a platter and dust with confectioners’ sugar.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: ‘until smooth’, ‘just until smooth’, ‘a toothpick inserted in the center of the cake comes out clean’.
  • Only the oven setting 325° is printed; the scale is not named.
  • Fan the slices evenly so the top bakes level.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-whisking after adding wet to dry.
  • Opening the oven early.
  • Unmoulding before the cake is completely cool.
