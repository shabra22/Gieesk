🫚 Nectar de gingembre (Ginger Drink)

Nectar de gingembre

Two kilograms of ginger washed, cut, crushed and pressed, the juice filtered and boiled 10 minutes with 2 kg of sugar and 6 litres of water, cooled and bottled in sterilised containers.

p. 80 of the October 2023 recipe book: 2 kg ginger, 2 kg sugar and 6 litres of water. The ginger is washed, cut small, crushed and pressed; the juice is filtered; water and sugar are added and it boils 10 minutes; cool; bottle in sterilised containers; keep cool. The page says the drink is much liked by adults and is generally ginger juice, sugar and water. The diagram says 75 °C, 10 min and shows ‘tourteau’; the text calls the ginger ‘le fruit’. Recorded as printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Press or cloth, Sterilised bottles

  • 2 kg ginger
  • 2 kg sugar
  • 6 litres water

Method

  1. Wash the ginger and cut it small.
  2. Crush and press it. Filter the juice.
  3. Add the water and sugar. Boil 10 minutes.
  4. Cool. Bottle in sterilised containers.
  5. Keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cues are the 10-minute boil and sterilised bottles.
  • The diagram shows 75 °C for 10 minutes against ‘boil’ in the text — not reconciled.
  • No shelf life is printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Bottling in unsterilised containers.
  • Skipping the filtering.
  • Assuming a shelf life.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep cool. No shelf life is printed.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Served cold
