🍩 Balah el Sham (Egyptian Churro-Style Fritters)

Balah el-Sham — بلح الشام

Choux dough made with water and corn oil, piped through a star tip into warm oil, fried until puffy and golden and soaked in cinnamon-clove syrup.

1h 20mTotal time
25mPrep
25mCook

MediumDifficulty

Water, corn oil, sugar, salt and vinegar are boiled and the flour is added; the heat goes off and the dough cools completely, then eggs are beaten in until a sticky dough forms. It is piped with a star tip into oil that is warm to the touch but not too hot, fried until puffy and golden, and soaked in the simple syrup while still warm. The page lists the syrup ingredients but gives no syrup method or any times.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Piping bag with star tip, Deep pan

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup corn oil
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar
  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Corn oil for frying

Syrup

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 stick cinnamon
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 teaspoon orange blossom or rose water

Method

  1. Boil the water, oil, sugar, salt and vinegar, add the flour and stir to a dough. Turn off the heat and let the dough cool completely.
  2. Beat in the eggs and vanilla until a sticky dough forms.
  3. Pipe through a star tip into oil that is warm to the touch but not too hot. Fry until puffy and golden.
  4. Make the syrup by simmering the syrup ingredients (the page gives no method; see the sharbat record for one). Soak the fritters in the syrup while still warm.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: dough cooled completely; sticky dough; puffy and golden. No frying times or temperatures are printed.
  • The page was adapted from Manal Al Alem's Bala7 El Sham recipe (single reading).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding eggs to hot dough, which cooks them.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour recalls her first encounter with balah el sham: a plate from a Muslim neighbour on a Ramadan night when she was under 7.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best the same day.

Reheating: Re-crisp in a hot oven.

Serve with: Simple syrup
