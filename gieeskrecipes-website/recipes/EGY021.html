🍚 Egyptian White Rice (Ahram Online Ramadan Recipe)

Roz abyad — رز أبيض

Rice stirred in butter for five minutes, boiled in hot water or broth, then cooked slowly over very low heat for ten minutes.

30mTotal time
5mPrep
25mCook
4Servings
300Calories
EasyDifficulty

This plain rice comes from an Ahram Online article of classic Ramadan recipes. The rice is stirred in butter or oil for five minutes before the hot liquid goes in; it is brought to a boil on medium heat, covered and cooked slowly over very low heat for ten minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium pot with a lid

  • 2 cups rice
  • 3 cups hot water or hot chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil

Method

  1. Heat the butter or oil and stir the rice in it for five minutes.
  2. Add the hot water or broth and bring to a boil over medium heat.
  3. Cover and cook slowly over very low heat for ten minutes.

Chef Tips

  • The page prints no salt amount and no doneness cue; season to taste and check that the water is absorbed (our note).
  • Rice type is not named; short or medium grain is typical in Egypt (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid while it steams.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat with a splash of water.
