🍲 Supukanj, Soupe Kandia (Au Sénégal)

Supukanj — soupe kandia

A Casamance classic as printed by the Au Sénégal portal: yet and meat browned, fish, dried fish, crabs and shrimp, puréed okra, nokoss and palm oil, over rice.

The Au Sénégal page prints the same ingredient list and method as the Afrik version — word for word in places — and adds a comment thread worth reading: the webmaster explains that nététou is the fermented seed of the néré pod, called soumbala in Guinea and Mali, and a reader says her mother never browned certain ingredients first. The page calls it a ‘Casamance dish much loved by Senegalese’ but harder for foreigners to appreciate. It prints one time only (3 to 4 minutes for the tomato); the rest is cue-governed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mortar, Blender or mortar for the okra

  • 1 kg rice
  • 1/4 litre palm oil
  • 1 kg okra
  • 1 good slice of fish
  • 250 g meat, a little shrimp, 4 crabs
  • 1 slice of smoked fish, 1 piece of yet, 1 piece of dried fish
  • 1 fresh chile, dried chile, nététou, 3 garlic cloves, green onion, onion — quantities not printed except the garlic
  • 1 tbsp tomato concentrate
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Heat the 2 tablespoons of oil, add the yet and the meat, and after a few moments the chopped onion; let it turn pink.
  2. Add the tomato and cook 3 to 4 minutes. Add water to the pot with salt and boil to cook the meat.
  3. Add the fresh fish, dried fish, cleaned and opened crabs, shrimp and fresh chile, then let simmer.
  4. Wash the okra, cut it and reduce it to a purée.
  5. Make the nokoss: pound the dried chile, nététou, garlic and green onion, and add it to the pot.
  6. Add the okra and simmer, stirring from time to time so the sauce does not stick.
  7. Add the palm oil and cook; check seasoning and finish over low heat.
  8. Serve with white rice.

Chef Tips

  • The only sensory cue on the page is the onion turning pink — everything after that is judgement.
  • A reader’s comment says her family never browned some ingredients first. The recipe is one household’s, not a rule.
  • The webmaster’s note: nététou is the fermented seed of the néré tree — the same ingredient the Mande call soumbala.
  • Cook the rice separately.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Treating the single printed time as the whole recipe.
  • Leaving out the nokoss.
  • Not stirring the okra.

Cultural Note

Presented on the site as a ‘plat casamançais très apprécié des Sénégalais’ — a Casamance dish, much loved by Senegalese. The site is a general Senegal portal run under a site-name byline, not a named cook; its text for this recipe matches the earlier Afrik page, which is itself recorded at Soupe Kandia.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 3 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Low heat, covered.

Serve with: Steamed white rice
