🥘 Kabab Hala bel Batates (Beef and Potato Pot Stew)

Kebab halla bil-batates — كباب حلة بالبطاطس

Beef cubes browned with onions, slowly cooked until the onions melt into a velvety sauce, with golden fried potatoes added at the end.

3h 10mTotal time
20mPrep
2h 50mCook
6Servings

EasyDifficulty

Meat cubes are dried, coated in salt and pepper, browned on medium until they have a nice brown crust, then cooked with onions and garlic until they sweat and begin to caramelize. Hot water or stock just to cover goes in and the stew slow-cooks 2 to 3 hours until the onion collapses into a velvety sauce and the meat is fork-tender; fried potato cubes go in for the last 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot or slow cooker, Frying pan

  • 1/2 kg meat, cut into cubes
  • 1/2 kg chopped onions
  • 3 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 teaspoons of seven spices
  • Salt and pepper
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 2 cups boiled water
  • (Potato cubes for frying; quantity not printed)

Method

  1. Dry the meat cubes and coat with salt and pepper.
  2. Brown on medium in the butter and oil until the cubes have a nice brown crust. Add the onions and garlic and cook until they sweat and start to caramelize.
  3. Add the seven spices, bay leaves and hot water or stock to barely cover. Cook in a slow cooker for two to three hours, until the onion collapses into a velvety sauce and the meat is fork-tender.
  4. Fry the potato cubes until they have a golden crust and add them for the last 20 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: nice brown crust; onions sweat and caramelize; onion collapses into sauce; meat fork-tender. Potato quantity is NOT PRINTED.
  • Her page uses a slow cooker; a heavy covered pot on very low heat is the stove equivalent (our note).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Washing the meat and not drying it, which stops browning.

Health Benefits

  • Protein-rich; onions add flavour without added sauces.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour writes that her grandmother called it 'Potatoes and Meat Fondant'.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat gently, adding a splash of water.

Serve with: Rice with vermicelli
