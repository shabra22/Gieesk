🥪 Ethiopian Fried Fish Sandwich

አሣ ሳምቡሳ — Asa Sandwich

Ethiopian Fried Fish Sandwich — berbere-marinated fried Nile perch in a baguette-style roll with silsi, berbere mayo, pickled jalapeños, and fresh tomato. The modern street food of Hawassa and Bahir Dar lakeside towns — the fish sandwich that makes the most of the freshest catch.

The Ethiopian fried fish sandwich is the lakeside town's modern street food — combining the berbere fried Nile perch tradition with the sandwich format popular in Ethiopian urban culture.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep pan

    Berbere Fried Fish

    • 4 Nile perch fillets (150g each)
    • 2 tbsp berbere
    • 1 tbsp lemon juice
    • 2 garlic cloves
    • 1 tsp salt
    • Oil for frying

    Sandwich Build

    • 4 baguette rolls or bread rolls
    • 4 tbsp berbere mayo

    Silsi (ETH146)

    • Sliced tomato
    • 4 pickled jalapeño slices per sandwich
    • Fresh coriander or lettuce

    Method

    1. Marinate fish in berbere, lemon, garlic, and salt for 15 minutes.
    2. Fry as per ETH082 method until golden.
    3. Split rolls. Spread berbere mayo on both sides.
    4. Add silsi. Place fried fish.
    5. Top with sliced tomato, pickled jalapeño, and coriander.
    6. Serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • The berbere mayo is the element that makes this distinctly Ethiopian.
    • Serve immediately — the hot fish wilts the bread quickly.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Assembling too far ahead.

    Cultural Note

    The Ethiopian fried fish sandwich is lakeside Ethiopia's contemporary street food — combining the country's freshwater fish tradition with the urban sandwich culture that has become part of Ethiopian food life. Eating one while watching hippos in Lake Hawassa is a quintessentially modern Ethiopian experience.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Eat immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: With cold water or lemon-water, As a complete lakeside lunch
