🎃 Ethiopian Pumpkin Stew

ዱባ ወጥ — Duba Wat

Ethiopian Pumpkin Stew — pumpkin chunks simmered in a berbere and tomato sauce. The pumpkin absorbs the berbere deeply and partially dissolves, creating a thick, rich sauce of surprising complexity. An underappreciated Ethiopian fasting gem.

Ethiopian pumpkin stew is the fasting platter's sweet note — the pumpkin's natural sweetness balancing the heat of the berbere in a combination that surprises with its depth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g pumpkin — peeled and cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cook onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic and berbere. Fry 2 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes. Cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add pumpkin. Stir to coat.
  5. Add water. Cover. Cook 20—22 minutes.
  6. Some pumpkin dissolves — natural thickening.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Pumpkin dissolves partially — the dissolved portions thicken the sauce beautifully.
  • Orange-fleshed pumpkin for maximum beta-carotene and colour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-cooking to complete mush — some whole pieces are better.

The Science

Pumpkin's high beta-carotene content makes it the most vibrant orange vegetable in Ethiopian cooking. The dissolved pumpkin starch thickens the berbere sauce, which then turns a vivid orange-red — one of the most visually striking Ethiopian dishes.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian pumpkin stew is the harvest season's gift to the fasting platter — the sweetness of pumpkin and the heat of berbere in a combination that captures the spirit of abundance even in the strictest fasting period.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter
