🍆 Stuffed Aubergine with Yoghurt and Fried Bread ("Makdoos")

Makdoos (as titled on the page) — مكدوس

Tiny aubergines stuffed with spiced minced beef and pine nuts, simmered in tomato sauce, layered with fried bread and a thickened yoghurt sauce.

Small cored aubergines are filled with cooked minced beef moistened with a seasoned red sauce, pine nuts and parsley, then cooked covered in more of the sauce with bouillon and hot water for about 15 to 20 minutes. Pita squares are deep-fried, a cornstarch-thickened yoghurt sauce is cooked until it bubbles, and everything is layered with roasted almonds and parsley.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep fryer or pot, Wide covered pan, Saucepan

  • 1 kg tiny long black stuffing aubergine (cored)
  • 250 gm cooked minced beef
  • 1.5 tbsp finely chopped onion
  • 1 tsp crushed garlic
  • 3 pita bread loaves (separated, squared)
  • 1 cup tomato juice
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 kg plain yoghurt
  • 2 tsp corn starch
  • 100 gm pine nuts (roasted)
  • 150 gm blanched, halved almonds (roasted)
  • 2 heaped tbsp parsley leaves (chopped), divided
  • 2 bouillon cubes
  • Juice of one lemon
  • 30 gm butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Sunflower or corn oil for deep frying bread
  • Salt + pepper + 7 spices + grated nutmeg

Method

  1. Deep-fry the pita squares until crisp and drain.
  2. Red sauce: cook the onion until translucent, add the seasonings until fragrant, then the tomato juice and paste. Simmer on low until thickened and reduced, then add the lemon juice.
  3. Moisten the cooked mince with 3 to 4 tablespoons of the sauce, add the pine nuts and half the parsley, and stuff the aubergines.
  4. Cook the stuffed aubergines in the rest of the sauce with the bouillon and hot water, covered, on medium heat for about 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. Yoghurt sauce: dissolve the cornstarch in 50 ml of water, stir into the yoghurt and heat on medium, stirring, until the mixture thickens and bubbles. Add the garlic at the end.
  6. Layer the aubergines, red sauce, fried bread and yoghurt sauce, and finish with almonds and parsley.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: onion translucent; sauce thickened; yoghurt sauce bubbling. The page gives no frying time for the bread.
  • The title is printed as Makdoos, but the dish is a stuffed fresh aubergine dish, not the pickled Levantine makdous.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not stirring the yoghurt as it heats, which can make it split.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate components separately up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm the aubergines in their sauce; assemble fresh.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
