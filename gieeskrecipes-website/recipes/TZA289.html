🍖 Tanzanian Lamb Chops Chermoula

Mbavu za Kondoo na Chermoula ya Tanzania

Tanzanian Lamb Chops with Zanzibar Chermoula — rack of lamb chops marinated in a Tanzanian chermoula of coriander, cumin, cloves, cardamom, garlic, and olive oil, grilled to a perfect medium rare and served with coconut mashed potato and a tamarind reduction. Fine dining Tanzania at its most accomplished.

Tanzanian chermoula is the spice tradition of the Zanzibar coast applied to the North African herb paste format — the result is a marinade of extraordinary fragrance that forms a magnificent crust on the lamb as it grills. This is the dish that Tanzania's best hotel chefs serve to demonstrate the country's culinary maturity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender for chermoula, Cast iron grill pan or charcoal grill, Saucepan for mash

  • 8 lamb chops — French trimmed

Zanzibar Chermoula

  • 1 cup fresh coriander
  • ½ cup fresh parsley
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Coconut Mashed Potato

  • 600g potatoes — boiled
  • 100ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and white pepper

Tamarind Reduction

  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Blend all chermoula ingredients until smooth.
  2. Coat lamb chops generously in chermoula. Marinate 2 hours minimum.
  3. Make coconut mash: beat boiled potatoes with warm coconut milk, butter, salt, and white pepper until silky.
  4. Make tamarind reduction: simmer tamarind, honey, and water until slightly syrupy — 3—4 minutes.
  5. Grill chops over high heat: 3 minutes per side for medium rare. Rest 4 minutes.
  6. Plate: swoosh of coconut mash → 2 chops crossed elegantly → tamarind reduction drizzled → microgreens.

Chef Tips

  • French trimmed chops for fine dining presentation.
  • High heat and speed — lamb chops are thin and cook fast.
  • Rest is mandatory — lamb chops, being thin, redistribute their juices quickly.
  • The chermoula crust should char very lightly — creating the flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking — lamb chops become tough at well-done.
  • Not resting.
  • Under-seasoning the mash.

The Science

The chermoula contains both water-soluble (from herbs and lemon) and fat-soluble (from spices and oil) flavour compounds. During grilling, the water evaporates, concentrating the fat-soluble compounds and the Maillard reaction creates hundreds of additional flavour compounds on the crust surface — producing a flavour intensity far beyond the raw marinade.

Cultural Note

Tanzanian lamb chops with chermoula is the dish that Tanzania's fine dining hotels have developed to serve international guests — it speaks a global fine dining language while being unmistakably Tanzanian in every flavour. It is Tanzania's culinary confidence on a plate.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Marinate up to 24 hours. Cook to order.

Reheating: Not recommended for rare/medium rare chops.

Serve with: Coconut mashed potato, Tamarind reduction, Sautéed local greens, Passion fruit juice (TZA226)
