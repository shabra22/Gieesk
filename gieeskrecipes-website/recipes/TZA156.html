🌿 Zanzibar Spice Tour Soup

Supu ya Viungo vya Zanzibar

Zanzibar Spice Tour Soup — a fragrant vegetable and coconut milk broth built on every spice the island grows: cloves, cinnamon, cardamom, nutmeg, lemon grass, ginger, and turmeric. A soup that tastes like a walk through a Zanzibar spice garden.

This soup was inspired by Zanzibar's famous spice tours — the tours of the island's spice farms where visitors discover cloves, cinnamon, cardamom, nutmeg, lemon grass, and more growing in profusion. The soup attempts to capture all of them in one bowl.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Blender (optional)

  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 sweet potatoes — cubed
  • 2 carrots — sliced
  • 1 stalk lemon grass — bruised
  • 3 cardamom pods
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and all whole spices and turmeric. Stir 1—2 minutes.
  3. Add sweet potato, carrot, and stock.
  4. Simmer 20—25 minutes until vegetables are tender.
  5. Remove whole spices and lemon grass.
  6. Blend half the soup smooth. Return to pot.
  7. Add coconut milk. Simmer gently 8 minutes.
  8. Season. Garnish with coriander. Serve with lime.

Chef Tips

  • Remove whole spices before blending.
  • Partial blending — some texture is important.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not removing whole spices — woody texture.

The Science

Each Zanzibar spice contributes distinct volatile aromatic compounds: cloves (eugenol), cinnamon (cinnamaldehyde), cardamom (1,8-cineole), nutmeg (myristicin). Together in one soup they create an aromatic complexity that mirrors the island's spice garden experience.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar Spice Tour Soup is the island's most evocative dish — eating it is walking through the spice farms of Kizimbani, the fragrance of cloves and cinnamon surrounding you, the Indian Ocean visible through the trees.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Chapati ya Nazi (TZA135), As a standalone starter
