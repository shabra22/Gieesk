🐟 Bahir Dar Lakeside Feast

ባህር ዳር ምግብ — Bahir Dar Mgib

Bahir Dar Lakeside Feast — the feast of Lake Tana's capital city: Lake Tana Fish (ETH087), Asa Wat (ETH081), Fried Nile Perch (ETH082), Amhara Tibs (ETH231), Injera, and the first cup from the Ethiopian coffee ceremony. The feast that defines life on the banks of the Blue Nile source.

Bahir Dar on Lake Tana is one of Ethiopia's great food cities — the lakeside restaurants, the fish markets, and the Blue Nile waterfalls nearby create a food experience unique to this city.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple pans and grill

  • Lake Tana Fish
  • Asa Wat
  • Fried Nile Perch
  • Amhara Tibs
  • Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony

Method

  1. Make Asa Wat 30 minutes ahead.
  2. Fry Nile perch in batches.
  3. Grill Lake Tana fish over charcoal.
  4. Sear Amhara Tibs fresh.
  5. Assemble on injera.

Chef Tips

  • Fresh Lake Tana fish is the centrepiece — everything else supports it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Old fish — freshness is the entire point.

Cultural Note

Bahir Dar lakeside dining is one of Ethiopia's greatest food experiences — the view of Lake Tana with its ancient island monasteries, the fresh fish from the lake, and the Ethiopian coffee ceremony completing the meal. It is a complete sensory and cultural experience.
