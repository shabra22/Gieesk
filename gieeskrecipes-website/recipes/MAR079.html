🌙 Kaab el Ghazal (Gazelle Horns, Small-Batch Version)

Kaab el ghazal — gazelle's heel (كعب الغزال)

A 10-piece batch of white, unbrowned gazelle horns: a thin orange-blossom dough around an almond-cinnamon filling, dried overnight and baked at 170°C.

13h 14mTotal time
1hPrep
14mCook
10Servings
210Calories
HardDifficulty

A small dough of flour, powdered sugar, butter and orange blossom water is kneaded until the gluten has almost developed and rested 30 minutes, then divided into 10 pieces of about 20 g and rolled thin. The ground-almond filling is wrapped inside, the horns are shaped and left to dry overnight, then baked at 170°C for 12 to 14 minutes. They should stay white, not brown.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin or pasta machine, Baking sheet

    Dough

    • 125 g wheat flour (9% protein)
    • 15 g powdered sugar
    • 30 g unsalted butter at room temperature
    • 40 g orange blossom water
    • 5 almonds with peel (optional)

    Filling

    • 125 g ground almonds
    • 63 g powdered sugar
    • 10 g unsalted butter at room temperature
    • cinnamon to taste, about a little less than 1/2 teaspoon
    • 17-20 g orange blossom water
    • 2 pebbles (about 1 g) of gum arabic, pulverized or crushed or, failing that, we can use 5 g glucose or 5 g of honey

    Method

    1. Knead the dough ingredients for a few minutes, until the gluten has almost developed. Form a ball and rest it 30 minutes.
    2. Mix the filling ingredients into a paste.
    3. Divide the dough into 10 pieces of about 20 g each and roll thin (the author used level 2 on a pasta machine).
    4. Fill, shape into horns and let them dry overnight at room temperature.
    5. Preheat the oven to 170°C (340°F), top and bottom heat. Bake 12 to 14 minutes at medium-high. The horns will be white and should not brown.

    Chef Tips

    • Cue: white, not browned.
    • The page says the flavour improves after resting 1 to 2 days.
    • The overnight drying is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Browning or toasting them.
    • Skipping the overnight drying.

    Cultural Note

    The author notes that the name is often mistranslated as 'gazelle horn' and that 'gazelle's heel' is closer, and, per one reading, that the same pastry is sold in Algeria as tcharek.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: The page says the flavour is best after 1 to 2 days and gives a longer keeping time of 15 to 20 days (as relayed).

    Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

    Serve with: Mint tea
