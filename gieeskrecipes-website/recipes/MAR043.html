🥣 Moroccan Lentil Soup (Adas)

Adas — brown lentil soup (العدس على الطريقة المغربية)

A simple brown-lentil soup with tomato, onion, parsley and paprika, eaten with a piece of bread.

8h 50mTotal time
10mPrep
40mCook
4Servings
330Calories
EasyDifficulty

Brown lentils are soaked overnight, then cooked in olive oil with onion, grated tomato, parsley and paprika for about 10 minutes before boiling water is added and the pot is covered for 30 minutes, stirring from time to time. The page says to taste and adjust the spices at the end.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 250 grams (1 1/4 cups) brown lentils, soaked in water overnight
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 large tomato
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped parsley
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 liter (4 cups) water

Method

  1. Soak the lentils overnight and drain. Grate the tomato pulp and discard the skin. Finely chop the parsley and onion.
  2. On medium heat, heat the olive oil in a large pot; add the onion, grated tomato and lentils and mix. Add the parsley, then paprika, salt and pepper.
  3. Mix and cook on medium heat for around 10 minutes.
  4. Add boiling water, cover the pan and cook for 30 minutes, stirring from time to time.
  5. Taste and adjust the salt, pepper and paprika. Serve hot with a piece of bread.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no doneness test; time (30 minutes) and tasting are the guide.
  • Soaking helps the lentils soften and cook easily.
  • Add a squeeze of lemon if you like; the page does not.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the soak.
  • Adding cold water, which slows cooking.
  • Not tasting for seasoning.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Add water to loosen and reheat.

Serve with: Khobz
