🍯 Ghoriba with Honey (Ghoriba Dyal 'Asal)

Ghoriba dyal 'asal (غريبة بالعسل)

Small golden cookies from beaten eggs, honey and lemon zest, brushed with egg yolk and baked until a rich golden colour.

40mTotal time
25mPrep
15mCook

Whole eggs, an egg white and sugar are beaten until thick, then oil or melted butter, vanilla, warmed honey, flour, baking powder, salt and zest make a soft dough. Small balls are brushed with egg yolk and baked at 350°F (180°C) for 12 to 15 minutes, or until a rich golden colour. The author uses a tea glass as the traditional measure.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowls, Baking sheets, Pastry brush

  • 2 whole eggs
  • 1 egg white - (reserve the yolk)
  • 1/3 cup sugar - (1/2 tea glass)
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil or melted butter - (1/2 tea glass)
  • 1 tsp vanilla - (or 1 sachet vanilla sugar)
  • 3 tbsp honey, - warmed but not hot
  • 2 2/3 c flour, plus additional if necessary - (4 tea glasses
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • zest of 1 or 2 lemons - (or 1 orange)
  • 1 egg yolk, lightly beaten - (for egg wash)

Method

  1. Beat the whole eggs, egg white and sugar until thick.
  2. Mix in the oil or melted butter, vanilla and warmed honey, then the flour, baking powder, salt and zest, adding extra flour only if needed for a soft, workable dough.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Roll small balls of dough (the page gives a ball size of about 1 1/4 inches in one reading; see the sources note) and brush with the beaten yolk.
  4. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, or until a rich golden colour.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: rich golden colour.
  • Warm the honey just until runny; the page says warmed but not hot.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hot honey cooking the eggs.
  • Too much extra flour, which makes the cookies dry.

Cultural Note

The author notes a tea glass is the traditional measure for ingredients and compares the texture to madeleines.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The page says they stay fresh at room temperature for several days, or two months frozen.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Mint tea
