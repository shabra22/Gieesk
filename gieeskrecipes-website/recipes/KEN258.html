🍝 Kenyan Chicken Alfredo

Alfredo ya Kuku ya Kenya

Kenyan Chicken Alfredo — grilled chicken strips over fettuccine in a rich, creamy parmesan sauce with garlic, paprika, and fresh coriander. The most popular pasta dish in Nairobi's restaurants — Italian technique with a gentle Kenyan touch.

Chicken Alfredo is the pasta dish that Nairobi's restaurants have made their own — the parmesan cream sauce is made properly (with pasta water emulsification), the chicken is grilled rather than boiled, and fresh coriander replaces plain parsley for a subtle Kenyan character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot for pasta, Large wide pan, Tongs

  • 400g fettuccine or tagliatelle
  • 400g chicken breast — grilled with paprika and garlic
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 100g parmesan — finely grated
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup pasta cooking water — reserved
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander

Method

  1. Grill chicken with paprika, garlic, and salt. Slice thinly.
  2. Cook pasta al dente. Reserve 1 cup cooking water.
  3. In a wide pan, melt butter. Cook garlic 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add cream. Simmer 2 minutes.
  5. Add pasta and a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously.
  6. Add parmesan gradually, tossing constantly. Add pasta water as needed for silky sauce.
  7. Season with paprika, salt, and pepper.
  8. Plate and lay chicken strips over pasta.
  9. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • The pasta water is the secret — its starch emulsifies the cream and parmesan into a silky sauce.
  • Add parmesan off the heat — high heat makes it clump.
  • Toss vigorously and continuously — the motion creates the emulsion.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Discarding pasta water.
  • Adding parmesan over high heat — clumps.
  • Not enough water — sauce is too thick.

The Science

Pasta cooking water contains dissolved starch that acts as an emulsifier — it bridges the fat (cream, butter) and water phases, creating a stable sauce that clings to the pasta rather than pooling at the bottom of the bowl.

Cultural Note

Chicken Alfredo has become one of Nairobi's most ordered restaurant dishes — testimony to how completely Kenya's food service industry has absorbed global cuisine while making it accessible and excellent.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop with a splash of cream.
