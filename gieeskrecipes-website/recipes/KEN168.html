🥭 Kenyan Mango Kachumbari

Kachumbari ya Embe

Kachumbari ya Embe — Mango Kachumbari. The beloved classic kachumbari elevated with diced ripe mango — adding tropical sweetness to the sharp, fresh salad. The perfect condiment for grilled fish and coastal dishes.

Mango kachumbari takes Kenya's most beloved condiment and adds the country's most celebrated tropical fruit. The mango's sweetness perfectly balances the sharpness of the onion and lemon, creating a condiment that is simultaneously refreshing, fruity, and bright. It is especially popular alongside grilled fish and coastal seafood dishes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Sharp knife

  • 1 large ripe mango — peeled and finely diced
  • 2 medium tomatoes — finely diced
  • 1 medium onion — finely diced and rinsed in cold water
  • 1 fresh green chilli — finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Rinse diced onion in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain — this removes harshness.
  2. Combine mango, tomatoes, drained onion, chilli, and coriander in a bowl.
  3. Add lime juice, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss gently to combine.
  5. Taste and adjust — more lime for tartness, more mango for sweetness.
  6. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Rinsing the onion in cold water removes the sharp harshness while keeping the crunch.
  • Use a mango that is ripe but still firm — it should hold its shape when diced.
  • The combination of lime and lemon adds complexity that either alone does not provide.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overripe, mushy mango — the salsa becomes watery.
  • Not rinsing the onion — too aggressive and sharp.

The Science

Rinsing raw onion in cold water dissolves the sulphur compounds responsible for eye-watering sharpness. The compounds are water-soluble and leach out in 5 minutes of soaking — the onion retains its texture and crunch but loses its harsh edge.

Cultural Note

Mango kachumbari is the celebration upgrade of Kenya's most beloved condiment — served at coastal restaurants, holiday gatherings, and any occasion when the finest Kenyan mangoes are in season.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten within 2 hours — the mango releases juice over time.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

Serve with: Grilled Fish, Nyama Choma, Pilau, Chapati, Coastal Seafood
