🥧 Chicken Pastilla

Pastilla au poulet / bastilla (بسطيلة)

A sweet-savoury pie of chicken, scrambled eggs and almonds between crisp brick sheets, dusted with icing sugar and cinnamon.

1h 50mTotal time
40mPrep
1h 10mCook

700Calories
HardDifficulty

The French recipe on Afrik.com is credited to the book La table du ramadan. The chicken is browned 3 minutes per side, covered by a third with water and cooked about 40 minutes until done; eggs are scrambled into the sauce; almonds are boiled 10 minutes, then butter and sugar are added; layers of brick sheets are brushed with melted butter, and the pie is baked at 180°C for 10 to 15 minutes until well browned. The page uses curry alongside paprika and cinnamon, an unusual choice reported as printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Pie dish, Pastry brush, Oven

  • 1 poulet de 1,5 kg (1 chicken, 1.5 kg)
  • 500 g d'amandes (500 g almonds)
  • 18 feuilles de brick (18 brick sheets)
  • 2 oignons (2 onions)
  • 1/2 citron (1/2 lemon)
  • 10 oeufs (10 eggs)
  • 150 g de beurre (150 g butter)
  • 1 botte de coriandre (1 bunch coriander)
  • 1 c à s de paprika rouge doux (1 tbsp sweet red paprika)
  • 1 c à s de curry (1 tbsp curry)
  • 3 c à s de cannelle + un peu pour la décoration (3 tbsp cinnamon + a little for decoration)
  • 70 g de sucre en poudre (70 g sugar)
  • 2 c à s de sucre glace (2 tbsp icing sugar)
  • 3 c à s d'huile d'olive + un filet (3 tbsp olive oil + a drizzle)

Method

  1. Brown the chicken 3 minutes on each side. Cover it by a third with water and cook about 40 minutes, until it is well cooked.
  2. Add the onions, herbs and spices to the sauce, then scramble the eggs into it with 20 g of the sugar and 2 tbsp of the cinnamon.
  3. Plunge the almonds in a pan of boiling water for 10 minutes, then mix them with the sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Melt 100 g of the butter. Layer brick sheets brushed with butter in the pie dish, fill with the almonds and the chicken-egg mixture, and fold the sheets over.
  5. Brush the top with 50 g of butter and bake at 180°C (gas 6) for 10 to 15 minutes, until well golden.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and cinnamon and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Cues quoted: chicken well cooked; pie well golden.
  • The assembly order here is our summary; the research tool returned only number-bearing sentences from the page.
  • Fry the almonds briefly if you want a crunchier layer.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Soggy filling: reduce the sauce first.
  • Not brushing each sheet with butter.
  • Baking too long, which burns the sugar.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten warm on the day; keeps 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a moderate oven to re-crisp.

Serve with: Mint tea
