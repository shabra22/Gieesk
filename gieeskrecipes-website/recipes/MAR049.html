🍅 Matbucha (Cooked Tomato and Pepper Salad)

Matbucha / maticha (מטבוחה)

Charred green peppers and ripe tomatoes simmered slowly with garlic, paprika and a pinch of sugar into a thick, sauce-like salad.

1h 20mTotal time
20mPrep
1hCook
8Servings
130Calories
EasyDifficulty

Joan Nathan chars four green peppers on all sides over a flame or under the broiler (about 20 minutes), steams them in a closed bag for 10 minutes to loosen the skins, then simmers them with tomatoes and garlic over a low flame, mashing, until the liquid begins to evaporate and the salad is thick and sauce-like. She finishes with argan or olive oil and serves it at room temperature.

Ingredients

Equipment: Broiler or gas burner, Heavy skillet, Plastic bag

  • 4 green peppers
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 pounds very ripe fresh tomatoes (about 8), peeled, seeded, and diced or one 32-ounce can diced tomatoes, drained
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons sweet paprika
  • 2 teaspoons salt, or to taste
  • 1 tablespoon argan or extra virgin olive oil for drizzling
  • Freshly ground pepper to taste

Method

  1. Put the whole peppers under the broiler or directly on the gas burner, turning frequently with tongs, until charred on all sides, about 20 minutes.
  2. Place them in a tightly closed plastic bag for 10 minutes to loosen the skins. Alternatively roast in a 450-degree oven, turning every 10 or so minutes for 20 minutes, until soft and charred.
  3. Peel the charred skin, discard the seeds and cut the peppers into 1-inch squares.
  4. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet and add the peppers, tomatoes, garlic, sugar, paprika, salt and pepper.
  5. Simmer over a low flame, stirring occasionally and mashing with a fork or potato masher, until the liquid begins to evaporate; keep cooking until the salad has a thick, sauce-like consistency.
  6. Adjust seasoning, drizzle with argan or olive oil and serve at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • No total simmer time is printed; use the thick, sauce-like cue.
  • Canned tomatoes are fine, drained.
  • It keeps well and improves overnight.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the peppers unpeeled.
  • Cooking over high heat and scorching.
  • Stopping while still watery.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 5 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature or warmed lightly.

Serve with: Khobz
