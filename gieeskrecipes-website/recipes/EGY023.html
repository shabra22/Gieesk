🍗 Roz Mo'ammar bel Ferakh (Baked Creamy Rice with Chicken)

Ruz mo'ammar bil-ferakh — رز معمر بالفراخ

A spiced whole chicken is simmered for broth and golden-seared, then baked over rice cooked in hot milk and broth with yogurt, topped with butter.

3h 40mTotal time
40mPrep
2hCook

HardDifficulty

A dry-brined chicken is seared in ghee and whole spices and simmered 30 to 35 minutes in boiling water, then cooled 20 minutes, cut up and the broth strained. Rinsed rice is seasoned with yogurt and oil in a clay or Pyrex dish; hot milk and broth are poured over, the chicken and diced butter go on top, and it bakes until the crust turns golden and the inside creamy.

Ingredients

Equipment: 4-quart pot, Clay or Pyrex baking dish, Oven

    Rice

    • 2 cups Egyptian rice, rinsed
    • 2 tablespoons canola oil
    • 1/4 cup whole plain yogurt
    • 1 teaspoon salt
    • 1 teaspoon pepper
    • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom, optional
    • 2 bay leaves, optional
    • 1 stick of cinnamon, optional
    • 2 1/2 cups hot whole milk
    • 1 cup chicken broth
    • 1 whole cooked chicken (see recipe below)
    • 4 tablespoons butter, diced

    Chicken and broth

    • 3 tablespoons ghee
    • 10 allspice berries
    • 10 whole cloves
    • 5 bay leaves
    • 7 cardamom pods
    • 3 mastic resins
    • I medium whole raw chicken, dry brined overnight (see notes)
    • 1 onion, quartered
    • 5 garlic cloves, whole
    • 2 celery ribs with leaves
    • 2 branches of sage stalks and leaves
    • 1 sprig of thyme
    • 1 liter of boiling water, more if needed
    • Salt and pepper, to taste

    Method

    1. Dry-brine the raw chicken overnight in the fridge (the notes describe a salt, pepper, lemon zest and herb rub, and rinsing before cooking).
    2. Warm the ghee in the pot and fry the whole spices until fragrant. Add the onion and garlic until slightly brown, then sear the chicken until the skin is crispy and golden.
    3. Add the boiling water and simmer on low-medium for 30 to 35 minutes, until no blood or raw flesh shows. Cool the chicken 20 minutes, cut it up and strain the broth.
    4. Put the rice, salt, pepper, cardamom, yogurt and oil in the baking dish. Pour in the hot milk and the broth.
    5. Arrange the chicken pieces and diced butter on top and bake. The recipe card says 400 for 30 minutes (no unit printed); the page text says 380°F for 30 to 40 minutes, then broil the last 5 to 7 minutes to torch the top.
    6. Serve next to molokhia.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: chicken skin crispy and golden; no blood or raw flesh; golden crust and creamy interior. The overnight brine is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
    • The page says to use only boiling water, never cold, for the broth. The baking instructions contradict each other (card versus text); treat 380°F with a short broil as the fuller reading and watch the rice.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Adding cold water to the broth, which the page says makes it cloudy.
    • Under-baking so the rice stays loose.

    Cultural Note

    Nermine Mansour calls it traditional, for special occasions, family gatherings and Ramadan, and says it originated in the countryside (unsourced).

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Page: boil the chicken and broth 1 to 2 days ahead and refrigerate; the baked dish is best fresh.

    Reheating: Reheat covered in a moderate oven.

    Serve with: Molokhia
