🫛 Swahili Okra Curry

Mchuzi wa Bamia wa Nazi

Swahili Okra Curry — fresh okra cooked in a fragrant coconut-tomato sauce with coastal spices. The coconut milk tames the okra's natural sliminess into a silky, creamy sauce that is distinctly Swahili and utterly delicious.

The combination of okra and coconut milk in Swahili cooking is a revelation — the coconut milk integrates with the okra's natural mucilage to create a silky, cohesive sauce rather than the separate sliminess that puts many people off plain okra stew. It is one of the coastal kitchen's most elegant ways with a humble vegetable.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan, Wooden spoon

  • 400g fresh okra — trimmed and sliced
  • 400ml thick coconut milk
  • 2 medium onions — finely chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tsp ginger — grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander — chopped

Method

  1. Dry okra completely. Fry in hot oil for 3—4 minutes until lightly browned. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden — 6 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 30 seconds.
  4. Add tomatoes, curry powder, and turmeric. Cook for 6—8 minutes until thick.
  5. Pour in coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Return fried okra. Simmer gently for 6—8 minutes.
  7. Season and scatter coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Frying okra first dramatically reduces sliminess.
  • Coconut milk further integrates with okra mucilage — resulting in a silky rather than slimy sauce.
  • Lemon juice at the end brightens the dish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wet okra — becomes very slimy.
  • Boiling coconut milk hard.

The Science

Coconut milk fat droplets interact with okra mucilage polysaccharides, creating a more homogeneous emulsion rather than separate slimy strands — producing a smooth, silky sauce texture.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh. Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Very gently over low heat.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi, Chapati, Grilled Fish, Ugali
