🐟 Hout Quari (Sardine Patty Sandwich)

Hout quari — sardine kefta sandwich (حوت قاري)

Sardines pulsed with ras el hanout, smen and cilantro into a paste, grilled as small patties and served in bread with onion, tomato sauce and harissa.

20mTotal time

The author says this sandwich is not what people associate with Moroccan food, but her Moroccan husband insisted it is among the most authentic local foods; she found it on a Marrakech food tour. Sardine flesh (or tinned sardines) is pulsed with ras el hanout, a little smen or ghee and cilantro to a thick paste, shaped into patties and grilled 5 minutes per side until cooked through.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Grill pan

  • 1/4 kg sardine flesh cleaned and removed from bones OR 2 tins of sardines (appx 250grams)
  • 10-15 grams ras al hanout (add more or less to your liking)
  • 10-15 grams smen (moroccan butter) can use Indian ghee or salted butter at room temperature - just enough to hold together
  • small handful chopped cilantro
  • Bread (pocket bread or sturdy roll)
  • Toppings (amounts not printed): chopped raw onion, raw tomato sauce, harissa, sliced green olives, smen

Method

  1. Pulse the sardines with the ras el hanout, smen and cilantro to a thick paste.
  2. Shape into small patties.
  3. Grill 5 minutes on each side or until the fish is cooked through.
  4. Serve in the bread with onion, tomato sauce, harissa and olives.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: cooked through.
  • Add smen or ghee only until the mix holds together.
  • Batbout or pocket bread makes a good case.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overprocessing to a puree.
  • Leaving out the binder.
  • Grilling on too high a heat.

Cultural Note

The author writes that this sandwich is not what people associate with Moroccan food, but that her Moroccan husband insisted it is among the most authentic local foods; she found it on a Marrakech food tour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh.

Reheating: Warm briefly in a pan.

Serve with: Batbout
