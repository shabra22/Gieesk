🍖 Mazzarelle Abruzzesi

Mazzarelle

Small bundles of lamb offal wrapped in lamb intestine casing with wild herbs, slow-braised in tomato sauce, a traditional springtime dish tied to Abruzzo's Easter lamb slaughter.

Mazzarelle makes thoughtful use of lamb offal - heart, liver, lungs - wrapped with fresh herbs inside intestine casing, a technique reflecting Abruzzo's shepherding heritage and its practical nose-to-tail approach to spring lamb.

Ingredients

Equipment: Kitchen twine, Heavy-bottomed pan with lid

  • 600 g (1.3 lb) mixed lamb offal (heart, liver, lungs), cleaned and sliced into strips
  • Lamb intestine casing, cleaned (from a butcher)
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Fresh wild fennel or fennel fronds, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 100 ml (scant 1/2 cup) dry white wine
  • 80 ml (1/3 cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and chili flakes, to taste

Method

  1. Lay out strips of the cleaned offal and sprinkle with parsley, wild fennel, garlic, and a little salt and chili.
  2. Roll each portion up into a small bundle and wrap with a piece of the intestine casing, tying securely with kitchen twine.
  3. Warm the olive oil in a heavy pan and saute the onion until softened.
  4. Add the wrapped bundles and brown lightly on all sides.
  5. Add the wine and let it reduce, then add the crushed tomatoes.
  6. Cover and simmer gently for 1.5 hours, until the bundles are tender.
  7. Season to taste and serve hot with plenty of sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Ask your butcher to help clean and prepare the offal and casing if you are not experienced working with them.
  • Tying the bundles securely is important so they hold together through the braise.
  • This is a traditional springtime dish, timed to when fresh lamb offal is most available.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not securing the bundles tightly enough, causing them to unravel during cooking.
  • Rushing the braise, which leaves the offal with an unpleasantly tough texture.
  • Skipping the fresh herbs, which are essential to balancing the offal rich flavor.

Health Benefits

  • Offal is notably rich in iron, vitamin B12, and other micronutrients.

Cultural Note

Mazzarelle reflects Abruzzo's shepherding heritage and traditional nose-to-tail use of spring lamb, historically prepared around Easter when lamb offal was most available.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A robust red wine from Abruzzo
