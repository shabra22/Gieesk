🍪 Almond Ghriba (Crinkle Cookies, an American Cook's Version)

Ghriba bil louz — almond tea cookies

Almond-flour cookies with orange blossom water, rolled in powdered sugar and baked until crinkled and set — a non-Moroccan blogger's version of ghriba.

Almond flour, sugar, orange blossom water (or rose water or lemon zest), butter and eggs form a dough with a little all-purpose flour, baking powder and salt. The cookies are coated in powdered sugar and baked at 350°F for 12 to 14 minutes until crinkled and set when pressed lightly. The author is an American who first tried them while teaching English in Morocco.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowls, Baking sheet, Cooling rack

  • 2 cups (224 g) almond flour or ground blanched almonds
  • 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon (1 teaspoon) orange blossom water or rose water or zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons (28 g) unsalted butter room temperature
  • 2 (2) large eggs room temperature
  • 2/3 cup (83.33 g) all purpose flour
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons (1 1/4 teaspoons) baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon (1/2 teaspoon) salt
  • 1/2 cup (60 g) powdered sugar for coating

Method

  1. Mix the almond flour, sugar and flavouring with the butter and eggs, then stir in the all-purpose flour, baking powder and salt (the exact order was not relayed).
  2. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Shape the dough into balls and roll them in the powdered sugar.
  3. Bake 12 to 14 minutes, until the cookies have crinkled and are set when you press them lightly.
  4. Cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes before moving.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: crinkled, and set when lightly pressed.
  • Orange blossom water, rose water or lemon zest are the printed alternatives.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-baking so the cookies go dry.
  • Moving them while still soft.

Health Benefits

  • Gluten is low but not absent (it includes wheat flour); almonds add protein.

Cultural Note

The author writes that recipes and flavours vary across the country and that these are among the most common flavours.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store airtight at room temperature.

Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

Serve with: Mint tea
