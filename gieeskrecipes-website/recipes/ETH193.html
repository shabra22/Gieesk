🍞 Ethiopian Sweet Ambasha

ጣፋጭ አምባሻ — Tafach Ambasha

Ethiopian Sweet Ambasha — the celebration version of Ambasha (ETH005) made with additional honey, more sugar, and enriched with egg. Deeper golden, more fragrant with cardamom, and decorated with the intricate geometric pattern — served as the dessert bread at the most important celebrations.

Sweet Ambasha is the dessert version of the everyday Ambasha — made more indulgent with egg, more honey, and a more generous hand with cardamom. It occupies the space between bread and cake, eaten as dessert at celebrations.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Large flat pan with lid or oven at 175°C

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp ground fenugreek
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 3 tbsp oil

Honey glaze: 2 tbsp honey + 1 tsp water

    Method

    1. Dissolve yeast in warm water with 1 tsp honey. Rest 10 minutes.
    2. Beat egg with remaining honey, sugar, and oil.
    3. Combine flour, salt, cardamom, fenugreek, and cinnamon.
    4. Add yeast mixture and egg mixture to flour. Knead 8 minutes.
    5. Cover. Rise 90 minutes until doubled.
    6. Press into a large round 2cm thick.
    7. Brush with egg wash.
    8. Press deep geometric patterns into the surface.
    9. Pan method: cook covered on medium-low 20 minutes per side.
    10. Or oven: bake at 175°C for 35—40 minutes.
    11. Brush with honey glaze immediately out of the pan/oven.

    Chef Tips

    • Egg enrichment makes the crumb more tender than standard Ambasha.
    • More honey than the standard version — the bread should taste noticeably sweet.
    • The honey glaze gives a beautiful glossy finish.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Too high heat — the honey content burns easily.

    The Science

    See ETH170 for honey bread baking science. The additional egg in Sweet Ambasha provides lecithin — an emulsifier that creates a more tender crumb by inhibiting gluten development.

    Cultural Note

    Sweet Ambasha is the northern Ethiopian celebration bread elevated to dessert — the same geometric pattern, the same fenugreek fragrance, but sweeter, richer, and glazed with honey. At Tigrinya celebrations, it is the sweet conclusion to the meal.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Room temperature 4 days. Freeze 2 months.

    Reheating: Warm in oven.

    Serve with: With honey butter (ETH152), As a celebration dessert, With chai
