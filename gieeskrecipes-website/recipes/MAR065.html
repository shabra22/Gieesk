🍩 Sfenj (Moroccan Yeasted Doughnuts)

Sfenj (سفنج)

Wet, yeasted ring doughnuts fried in hot oil until golden and chewy, dusted with sugar or served with honey and pistachios.

3hTotal time
1h 30mPrep
30mCook
9Servings
290Calories
MediumDifficulty

The dough is made of flour, orange zest, sugar and salt with yeast bloomed to a froth in warm water, kneaded about 15 minutes until very smooth, and left to roughly double in size (about an hour). Balls about the size of a small egg are stretched to rings roughly 4 inches across and fried in oil at 350°F, 2 minutes on one side and 1 1/2 on the other, then cooled about 4 minutes and finished with honey and ground pistachios.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot, Thermometer, Slotted spoon

  • 1 tablespoon dry active yeast
  • 3 tablespoons sugar plus ¼ cup for dusting
  • 4 tablespoons plus ¾ cup warm water
  • 2 ½ cups plus two tablespoons flour
  • Zest from 1 orange
  • ½ teaspoon Kosher Salt
  • 2 cups canola oil
  • 1 cup honey
  • ½ cup ground pistachios

Method

  1. Combine the yeast, 1 tablespoon sugar and 4 tablespoons warm water and let stand about 15 minutes, until frothy and blooming.
  2. Mix in the flour, zest, remaining sugar, salt and water and knead about 15 minutes, until the dough is very smooth.
  3. Cover and let rise until roughly double in size, approximately 1 hour.
  4. Heat the oil to 350°F. Take pieces about the size of a small egg, stretch each into a ring about 4 inches across, and fry 2 minutes on one side and 1 1/2 minutes on the other.
  5. Cool about 4 minutes. Dust with sugar, or drizzle with warm honey and sprinkle with ground pistachios.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: yeast frothy and blooming; dough doubled in size.
  • A food blog version uses 3 cups flour and 2 to 4 hours rise with no cue, and adds cinnamon sugar, which it calls inauthentic.
  • Keep the oil at temperature; wet dough soaks up oil if it is too cool.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Oil too cool.
  • Under-kneading.
  • Using dry hands on the sticky dough; wet or oil them.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten warm the same day.

Reheating: Not recommended; refresh briefly in a hot oven.

Serve with: Mint tea
