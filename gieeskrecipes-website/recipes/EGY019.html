🌯 Egyptian-Style Beef Shawarma

Shawarma masri — شاورما مصري

Thinly sliced flank steak marinated in yogurt, vinegar and a home-ground shawarma blend, seared in batches and served in warm bread with tahini sauce.

4hTotal time
15mPrep
45mCook
6Servings
470Calories
MediumDifficulty

Whole spices are toasted about a minute and ground into a shawarma blend, then mixed with onion, yogurt, vinegar, lime, oil and garlic as the marinade for sliced flank steak, left at least 3 hours or overnight. The beef rests 20 minutes at room temperature before being seared in batches over medium-high heat to caramelized edges, then tossed with tomato and onion for 2 more minutes. An optional charcoal finish smokes the meat 10 to 15 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Spice grinder, Large skillet or griddle, Blender

  • 5 green cardamom pods
  • 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
  • 1¾ tablespoons allspice berries
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon ground sumac
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon

Marinade

  • 1 medium yellow onion, quartered
  • 1 cup whole-milk plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar or white vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons shawarma spice blend
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 2 1/4 pounds flank steak or top round, sliced across the grain into thin strips

Tahini sauce

  • 1 cup 1 cup tahini
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Warm water, as needed

Cooking

  • 35 g lamb fat, liyeh, beef tallow, or 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 1 Roma tomato, diced
  • 1 medium red onion, diced
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • 1 charcoal briquette, optional for a smoky finish

To serve

  • Pita, shrak, or lavash bread
  • Harissa
  • Pickled cucumbers
  • Pickled turnips
  • French fries

Method

  1. Toast the whole spices for about 1 minute until fragrant and grind with the other spices.
  2. Blend the onion, yogurt, vinegar, lime, oil, garlic, 2 tablespoons of the blend and salt; coat the sliced beef until every piece is well covered and marinate at least 3 hours or overnight.
  3. Take the beef out of the fridge about 20 minutes before cooking. Sear in batches over medium-high heat to caramelized edges, just cooked through. Add the tomato and red onion for another 2 minutes.
  4. Optional: smoke with a charcoal briquette for 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Whisk the tahini sauce ingredients with warm water to a pourable consistency; serve the meat in warm bread with sauce, harissa, pickles and fries.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: spices fragrant; meat coated; caramelized edges. The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least 3 hours or overnight) and is not a cure or ferment.
  • The tahini line is misprinted '1 cup 1 cup tahini' on the page.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Crowding the pan so the beef steams.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour calls it one of Egypt's most beloved street foods.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked meat up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat in a hot skillet.

Serve with: Egyptian tahini sauce, Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
