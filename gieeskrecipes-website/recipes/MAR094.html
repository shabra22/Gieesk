🍢 Heart and Kidney Kebabs

Qlayeb / kebab dyal lqalb w lkola — heart and kidney skewers

Cubes of lamb or beef heart and kidney, marinated in paprika and cumin, threaded with fat and grilled until firm outside and soft inside.

1h 37mTotal time
25mPrep
12mCook
6Servings
340Calories
MediumDifficulty

Kidney and heart are cut into 1/2-inch cubes and marinated with suet or caul fat and spices for at least an hour or overnight. They are threaded onto skewers alternating each couple of pieces with fat, about 6 cubes of kidney and fat per skewer, and grilled over hot coals without letting the flame reach the skewers, rotating to cook evenly. The cue is firm outside, soft inside; overcooking makes it rubbery.

Ingredients

Equipment: Metal skewers, Charcoal grill

  • 2.2 lbs Mix of kidney and heart, - lamb or beef
  • 12 oz caul fat or kidney fat
  • 1 1/2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne, - optional

For serving

  • cayenne, - to taste
  • cumin, - to taste
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Remove the transparent membrane from the kidney and cut it into 1/2 inch cubes. Cut the heart the same way.
  2. Dice the kidney fat into 1/2 inch cubes; if using caul fat, cut it into squares as wide as the cubes.
  3. Toss everything with the paprika, cumin, salt and cayenne and marinate at least an hour, up to overnight.
  4. Thread the heart and kidney cubes onto skewers, alternating each couple of pieces with fat; about 6 cubes of kidney and fat per skewer.
  5. Grill over hot coals, making sure the flame does not reach the skewers, rotating so they cook evenly on all sides, until firm outside and soft inside. Serve with cumin, cayenne and salt.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: firm outside but soft inside; avoid overcooking, which makes the kebab rubbery.
  • The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting flames reach the skewers.
  • Overcooking until rubbery.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat the same day.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Bread
