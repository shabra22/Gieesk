🥕 Ethiopian Potato and Carrot Alicha

ድንች ካሮት አልጫ — Dinich Karot Alicha

Ethiopian Potato and Carrot Alicha — potatoes and carrots cooked in a mild turmeric and ginger sauce without berbere. The golden-yellow mild root vegetable stew that children and heat-sensitive guests can enjoy fully.

Potato and carrot alicha is the mild filling element of the fasting platter — providing sustained energy from root vegetables with gentle turmeric and ginger spicing.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 400g potatoes — cubed
  • 3 carrots — sliced
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup water
  • 1.5 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and turmeric. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add potatoes and carrots. Stir.
  4. Add water. Cover. Cook 22—25 minutes until very tender.
  5. Season.

Chef Tips

  • No berbere — the mildness is intentional.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding berbere.

Cultural Note

The mild potato and carrot alicha ensures that the fasting platter welcomes every guest — from the youngest child to the oldest elder — regardless of their tolerance for heat.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), On the fasting platter as the mild element
