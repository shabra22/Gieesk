🍑 Beef (or Lamb) Tagine with Prunes

Tajine de boeuf aux pruneaux (طاجين اللحم بالبرقوق)

Bone-in beef or lamb simmered with onion, saffron, ginger and cinnamon until it falls from the bone, topped with prunes cooked down to a thick syrup and fried almonds.

MarocMama's classic wedding tagine builds a short, savoury sauce first. Meat, onion, garlic, salt, pepper, ginger, saffron and cinnamon are simmered in oil with cilantro; once the meat is cooked the remaining liquid is reduced to a thick sauce. The prunes are cooked separately with honey and a second helping of cinnamon until they sit in a thick syrup, and fried blanched almonds go on top.

Ingredients

Equipment: Tagine or heavy pot with lid, Small pan for the prunes, Pressure cooker (optional)

  • 1-2 lb. beef or lamb bone in – cut into 3-4" chunks
  • 1/2 lb. onions finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 2 teaspoon ginger
  • 5-10 saffron threads (optional)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup vegetable or olive oil (decrease if the meat has more fat)
  • palmful of chopped cilantro

Prunes and almonds

  • 1/2 lb. prunes
  • 1-2 tablespoon honey
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup blanched, fried almonds

Method

  1. Put the meat, onions, garlic, salt, pepper, ginger, saffron, cinnamon, oil and cilantro in the pot.
  2. Cover and simmer on medium heat for 2 1/2 to 3 hours, until the meat is very tender and falls away from the bone. In a pressure cooker, cook on medium heat for 45-50 minutes.
  3. Remove the meat and let the remaining liquid reduce to a thick sauce.
  4. Cook the prunes with the honey and the 1 1/2 teaspoons of cinnamon until they are sitting in a thick syrup.
  5. Fry the blanched almonds in a little oil; this only takes a few minutes once the oil is hot.
  6. Return the meat to the sauce, top with the prunes and almonds, and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Cinnamon appears twice by design: once in the meat, once with the prunes.
  • Doneness cues from the source: very tender, falling from the bone; sauce reduced thick; prunes in thick syrup.
  • A reader noted a mention of turmeric in the instructions; the author confirmed no turmeric is needed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the sauce thin; reduce it after removing the meat.
  • Cooking the prunes in the meat pot for hours.
  • Adding turmeric because of the stray mention; it is not part of the dish.

Health Benefits

  • Prunes bring fibre and potassium; almonds add healthy fat.
  • Honey is used in small amounts, only for the prune syrup.

Cultural Note

The author writes that the dish is served at births and weddings, and that she served it at her own wedding.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Reheat covered over low heat.

Serve with: Khobz, Mint tea
