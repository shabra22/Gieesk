🫐 Creamy Fonio Cereal with Blueberries, Pomegranate and Brown Butter

Bouillie de fonio

Fonio simmered five minutes in salted water, stirred with warm milk, topped with blueberries, pomegranate and a spoonful of foaming brown butter.

A breakfast porridge from ‘The Fonio Cookbook’. A cup of fonio, 2 cups of water and a teaspoon of salt boil on high, then simmer gently, covered, for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the water has been absorbed. The heat goes off and 2 cups of warmed milk (or full-fat coconut milk) are stirred in with a minute’s standing, then the optional brown sugar and cinnamon. Each bowl is topped with blueberries and pomegranate seeds, and the butter is cooked in a small pan until slightly brown and foamy and drizzled over.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Small pan for the butter

  • 1 cup raw fonio
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups milk or full-fat coconut milk, warmed
  • 2 tsp brown sugar (optional)
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter

Method

  1. Combine the fonio, water and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat, cover and simmer gently 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the water has been absorbed. Turn off the heat.
  2. Stir in the warmed milk and let stand 1 minute. Stir in the brown sugar and cinnamon if using.
  3. Divide among four bowls and top each with blueberries and pomegranate seeds.
  4. In a small pan cook the butter until slightly brown and foamy. Drizzle over the fonio and serve.

Chef Tips

  • ‘Until the water has been absorbed’ is the porridge cue.
  • ‘Slightly brown and foamy’ is the butter cue — a minute too long and it burns.
  • Vegan version: coconut milk and no brown butter, per the blogger.
  • Warm the milk first so the fonio doesn’t cool and seize.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Burning the butter.
  • Adding cold milk to hot fonio.

Health Benefits

  • Fonio is gluten-free and easy to digest.
  • Blueberries and pomegranate add antioxidants.

Cultural Note

The blogger’s framing, drawn from the book: fonio is ‘an ancient grain that’s cultivated by small farms in West Africa’, with a delicate, nutty taste, low on the glycemic scale. It is not a Senegalese traditional breakfast as printed; the recipe is a modern one from the cookbook.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated; the cereal thickens.

Reheating: Loosen with warm milk.

Serve with: Fresh fruit
