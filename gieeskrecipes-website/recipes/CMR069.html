🥤 Nectar de baobab (Baobab Fruit Drink)

Nectar de baobab

A kilogram of baobab pulp soaked 10 minutes, macerated, filtered and boiled 20 minutes in 5 litres of water to rid it of micro-organisms, then sugar, milk or flavouring added and the drink bottled and kept cool.

p. 76 of the October 2023 recipe book: 1 kg of baobab pulp, 120 g of sugar, flavouring or milk (as chosen), 5 litres of water and an optional preservative. The fruit is sorted, soaked 10 minutes, macerated and filtered; the filtered juice is boiled 20 minutes ‘to rid it of all micro-organisms’, cooled, mixed with the sugar, milk or flavouring and preservative, bottled and kept cool. The diagram shows 65 degrees and 12% sugar; text and diagram disagree and are not reconciled. No shelf life is printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Fine cloth or sieve, Bottles

  • 1 kg baobab pulp
  • 120 g sugar
  • Flavouring or milk, as chosen — quantity not printed
  • 5 litres water
  • Preservative (optional) — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Sort the fruit. Soak it 10 minutes.
  2. Macerate and filter.
  3. Boil the filtered juice 20 minutes.
  4. Cool. Mix in the sugar, milk or flavouring and the optional preservative.
  5. Bottle and keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The book’s stated aim of the 20-minute boil is to rid the juice of micro-organisms.
  • The diagram shows 65 degrees and 12% sugar; the text says 20 minutes of boiling. Not reconciled.
  • Keep cool; no shelf life is printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the boil.
  • Bottling hot.
  • Assuming a shelf life that the book does not give.

Health Benefits

  • Baobab pulp is a tart fruit; the drink is sweetened.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The ingredient list says pulp while the method begins from whole fruit.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep cool (‘conserver au frais’). No shelf life is printed.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Served cold
