🍝 Macarona Bechamel (Egyptian Baked Pasta)

Makarona bechamel — مكرونة بالبشاميل

Penne tossed in a cinnamon-spiced beef and tomato sauce, layered with a nutmeg béchamel and baked until the top is crusty and golden.

2h 20mTotal time
30mPrep
1h 40mCook
6Servings
620Calories
MediumDifficulty

An onion and ground beef sauce is spiced with cinnamon and cumin, mixed with grated carrot and tomato purée and simmered at least 30 minutes. Penne is cooked 3 minutes less than the box says, tossed with a little white sauce and layered with the meat. A béchamel from butter, flour, hot milk and nutmeg covers the top, and the dish bakes at 180°C for 40 to 50 minutes until the top is crusty and golden, then cools for at least 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Saucepan, Baking dish, Oven

  • 2 tablespoons Sunflower oil
  • 1 Onion
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 teaspoon Cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon Cumin
  • 500 g Mince meat (Ground beef)
  • Salt
  • 400 g Tomato puree (1 can)
  • 2 Carrots
  • 400 g Penne Pasta
  • Salt

Béchamel

  • 100 g All-purpose flour
  • 100 g Butter
  • 4½ cups Milk
  • ½ teaspoon Nutmeg
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook the onion in the oil until translucent, then the garlic and spices for 30 seconds. Add the mince and cook until the meat turns brown.
  2. Grate in the carrots, add the tomato puree and salt, and simmer for at least 30 minutes until reduced.
  3. Boil the penne 3 minutes less than the packet time and drain.
  4. Béchamel: melt the butter on low, stir in the flour, then off the heat whisk in at least three-quarters of the hot milk. Add salt and nutmeg and return to the heat to thicken.
  5. Toss the pasta with about ½ cup of the white sauce, layer it with the meat sauce in a baking dish, and cover with the remaining béchamel.
  6. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40 to 50 minutes, until the top is crusty with a golden brown colour. Let it cool at least 10 minutes before cutting.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: meat browned; sauce reduced; top crusty and golden. The cooling rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least 10 minutes).
  • Total time here follows the page card (1 hour 40 minutes) with our split into prep, rest and cook.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the pasta before baking.
  • A lumpy béchamel from adding cold milk to a hot roux.

Cultural Note

The page says the dish is thought to have Greek origins and compares it to pastitsio or lasagna; it also notes it suits sahur.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat covered in a 180°C oven until hot.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
