🥖 Kenyan Chicken Panini

Panini ya Kuku

Kenyan Chicken Panini — a pressed, toasted panini filled with spiced chicken, melted cheese, caramelised onions, and a hint of pili pili. Nairobi's café staple — the global sandwich format with Kenyan character.

The chicken panini arrived in Nairobi's café scene and was immediately improved — spiced chicken, pili pili mayo, and caramelised onions replace the standard fillings. Pressed until the bread is golden and the cheese is fully melted, it is the lunchtime sandwich of choice for Nairobi's office workers.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sandwich press or heavy pan, Knife

  • 2 panini rolls or ciabatta
  • 300g chicken breast — grilled with curry spices
  • 4 slices mozzarella or cheddar
  • 1 small onion — caramelised
  • 2 tbsp pili pili mayo (mayo + pili pili sauce)
  • 2 slices tomato
  • Butter for pressing

Method

  1. Grill chicken breast with curry powder, paprika, and salt until cooked. Slice thinly.
  2. Caramelise onion: cook slowly in butter until golden and sweet — 15 minutes.
  3. Mix mayo with pili pili sauce.
  4. Split panini rolls. Spread pili pili mayo on both sides.
  5. Layer: chicken, cheese, caramelised onion, tomato.
  6. Press in sandwich press for 4—5 minutes until golden and cheese is melted.
  7. Or: brush outside with butter and press in a heavy pan, weighted, for 3 minutes per side.

Chef Tips

  • Caramelised onion takes time — do not rush it.
  • Butter the outside of the bread for the best golden crust.
  • Press firmly and consistently for even contact.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing the onion.
  • Not enough pressure during pressing.

The Science

Pressing the sandwich creates uniform contact between the bread and the heated surface, ensuring even Maillard browning. The pressure also compresses the filling, melding the flavours together more effectively than an unpressed sandwich.

Cultural Note

The Kenyan chicken panini demonstrates how Nairobi's café culture absorbs global food trends and makes them local — the pili pili mayo and spiced chicken are the Kenyan signature on a universally recognisable format.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Press briefly in a hot pan.

Serve with: Masala Fries, Fresh Salad, Fresh Juice
