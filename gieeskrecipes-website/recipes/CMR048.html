🌽 Ekomba (Steamed Corn and Peanut Cake)

Ekomba

Fresh or soaked dry corn with toasted peanuts ground to a thick-soup consistency, wrapped in banana leaves and boiled about 2 hours.

630 minTotal time
30 minPrep
120 minCook

A camer.be printing of a dish the page says originates from the Centre and South regions, often served at festive ceremonies and dot (dowry) ceremonies. Dry corn is soaked the night before. The peanuts are toasted, stopping before they are fully browned. The corn and peanuts are ground, water added until the mix reaches the consistency of a thick soup, and it is wrapped in softened banana leaves and boiled about 2 hours, adding water only beside the packets in small amounts. The page gives no cue beyond the time.

Ingredients

Equipment: Grinder or blender, Large pot, Banana leaves

  • 8 fresh corn ears, or 600 g dry corn
  • 200 g peanuts
  • Salt — quantity not printed
  • Banana leaves — quantity not printed

Method

  1. If using dry corn, soak it the night before.
  2. Toast the peanuts, stopping before they are fully browned.
  3. Grind the corn and peanuts, adding water until the mix is the thickness of a thick soup. Add salt.
  4. Soften the banana leaves, wrap the mix and tie the packets.
  5. Boil about 2 hours, adding water only beside the packets, in small amounts.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cues are the thick-soup consistency and the 2-hour time.
  • Add water only around the packets, not onto them.
  • Stop toasting the peanuts before they brown fully.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-toasting the peanuts.
  • Pouring water over the packets.
  • Thinning the mix too far.
