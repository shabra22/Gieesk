🥗 Kenyan Grilled Chicken Salad

Saladi ya Kuku wa Kukaanga

Kenyan Grilled Chicken Salad — spiced grilled chicken strips over mixed greens with sliced mango, avocado, cherry tomatoes, and a passion fruit vinaigrette. The healthy option on every Nairobi café menu — made extraordinary by the quality of Kenya's tropical ingredients.

The Kenyan grilled chicken salad has its own identity — the tropical fruit, the avocado, and above all the passion fruit vinaigrette make it something that no imported salad concept can match. The passion fruit vinaigrette is tart, sweet, and fragrant in a way that simple lemon vinaigrette is not.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan or grill, Bowl, Whisk

  • 300g chicken breast
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt

Salad

  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 ripe mango — sliced
  • 1 ripe avocado — sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes — halved
  • ¼ red onion — thinly sliced

Passion Fruit Vinaigrette

  • 3 tbsp passion fruit juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Season chicken with curry powder, paprika, and salt.
  2. Grill over medium-high heat for 5—6 minutes per side until cooked through.
  3. Rest 3 minutes. Slice thinly.
  4. Make vinaigrette: whisk passion fruit juice, olive oil, honey, lemon, and salt.
  5. Arrange greens on plates.
  6. Top with mango, avocado, tomatoes, and onion.
  7. Lay chicken strips over salad.
  8. Drizzle vinaigrette generously.

Chef Tips

  • Let the chicken rest before slicing — it stays juicier.
  • The passion fruit vinaigrette is the star — make extra.
  • Dress the salad immediately before serving.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-cooking chicken — dry strips.
  • Dressing too early — wilted greens.

The Science

Passion fruit juice's complex acidity (citric, malic, and tartaric acids) creates a vinaigrette with greater depth than lemon alone. The honey emulsifies partially with the olive oil, creating a more cohesive dressing.

Health Benefits

  • High protein from chicken.
  • Mango and passion fruit provide vitamins A and C.

  • Very low in carbohydrates.

Cultural Note

The Kenyan grilled chicken salad demonstrates that healthy café food can celebrate rather than apologise for local ingredients — the passion fruit vinaigrette and tropical fruit make this salad something genuinely distinctive.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best assembled immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Iced Kenyan Coffee, Fresh Juice, Sourdough roll
