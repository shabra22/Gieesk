🌽 Cornchaff (Adalu): Corn and Beans in Palm Oil

Cornchaff / corn tchap

Dried beans and boiled corn simmered with smoked turkey or fish, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, onion, crayfish, smoked paprika and palm oil — a one-pot corn and bean porridge.

180 minTotal time
90 minPrep
90 minCook
7Servings
560Calories
MediumDifficulty

Adamu describes cornchaff as a one-pot corn and beans porridge enjoyed throughout West Africa and especially popular in Nigeria and Cameroon. Dried beans are soaked overnight or 6 to 8 hours, then pressure-cooked roughly 8 to 12 minutes or cooked about 1 to 1½ hours until tender. Corn, smoked turkey or fish, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, onion, crayfish, palm oil, paprika and seasoning go in with 2–3 cups or more of water or stock and simmer about 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot or pressure cooker

  • 1 pound dried beans, any variety (or three 16-ounce cans of beans)
  • 1 pound corn (sweet boiled corn or any kind)
  • 1–2 cups smoked turkey, fish or chicken
  • 2 medium tomatoes, diced
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger (printed ‘teaspoons’)
  • 1/2 medium onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup crayfish, smoked/dried shrimp (optional)
  • 1/2–3/4 cup palm oil
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to season
  • 1–2 tablespoons chicken bouillon (optional)

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight or 6 to 8 hours.
  2. Pressure-cook the beans roughly 8 to 12 minutes, or cook in a pot about 1 to 1½ hours until tender.
  3. Add the corn, smoked turkey or fish, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, onion, crayfish, palm oil, paprika, salt, pepper and bouillon with 2–3 cups or more of water or stock.
  4. Simmer about 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cue is beans ‘until tender’.
  • Use the pressure cooker for the beans if you have one — the page gives both times.
  • The page says ‘any variety’ of bean but its method mentions red and black beans.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the bean soak.
  • Letting the pot catch while it simmers.
  • Adding the spices before the beans are tender — the exact order is not printed on the page.

Cultural Note

The page says cornchaff is especially popular in Nigeria and Cameroon and names no region or community. The name appears as ‘cornchaff’, ‘corn tchap’ and ‘adalu’ in different sources.
