🫀 Ethiopian Beef Liver Wat

ጉበት ወጥ — Gubet Wat

Ethiopian Gubet Wat — beef liver sliced and cooked quickly in kibbeh with onion, jalapeño, and berbere. The liver must be cooked quickly — over-cooked liver is rubbery. Done right, it is one of the most nutritious and most flavourful Ethiopian preparations.

Liver wat is the fastest Ethiopian main dish — the liver must be cooked quickly to remain tender. The kibbeh and berbere provide the Ethiopian character; the jalapeño provides freshness and heat. Served with injera, it is one of the most nutritionally dense meals possible.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pan

  • 500g beef or calf liver — sliced 1cm thick
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 jalapeños — sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Slice liver. Remove membrane and any tubes.
  2. Heat kibbeh until smoking hot.
  3. Sear liver in a single layer — 1.5 minutes per side only.
  4. Remove liver.
  5. In the same pan: cook onion 2 minutes. Add jalapeño, garlic, berbere.
  6. Return liver. Toss. Season. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Liver is done at 1.5 minutes per side — over-cooking is the only mistake.
  • Smoking hot pan is essential.
  • Serve the instant it is done — waiting makes it tough.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-cooking — tough and dry.
  • Cool pan.

The Science

Liver has very low fat content and high protein — it goes from tender to tough very quickly as the proteins denature. At 70°C internal temperature, the liver is perfectly cooked; at 80°C it becomes unpleasantly tough. The total cooking time from a hot pan to 70°C is approximately 3 minutes.

Health Benefits

  • Liver is the world's most nutrient-dense food.
  • Exceptional B12, iron, vitamin A, folate.
  • Complete protein.

Cultural Note

Gubet Wat is the Ethiopian dish of maximum nutrition — the liver, properly cooked in one minute per side in kibbeh, provides more vitamins and minerals per gram than any other food preparation.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Very briefly — will over-cook.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ayib (ETH096)
