🍤 Grilled Prawns

Grilled prawns, grilled shrimp

Threaded tail to head so they do not curl, marinated about ten minutes, and cooked in five to eight.

The companion to the grilled tilapia, and its one real piece of technique is worth the entry on its own: thread the prawns from tail to head, because this prevents them curling up as they cook. About two pounds of prawns take a quarter cup of grated shallots, a heaping teaspoon each of ginger and garlic, a teaspoon of salt, two teaspoons of dried ground red pepper, a heaping teaspoon of fish spice and four tablespoons of white vegetable oil, with one or two lemons. Ten minutes in the marinade, five to eight minutes to cook.

Ingredients

Equipment: Skewers, Charcoal grill, Grater, Bowl

  • About 2 lb prawns or shrimp
  • 1/4 cup grated shallots or onion
  • 1 heaping tsp fresh ground or grated ginger
  • 1 tsp fresh garlic, ground, grated or crushed
  • 1 tsp salt or seasoning salt
  • 2 tsp dried ground red pepper
  • 1 heaping tsp fish spice
  • 4 tbsp white vegetable oil
  • 1-2 lemons

Method

  1. Peel and devein the prawns if needed.
  2. Mix the grated shallots, ginger, garlic, salt, red pepper, fish spice and oil.
  3. Leave the prawns in the marinade about 10 minutes.
  4. Thread them onto skewers from tail to head — this prevents them curling up as they cook.
  5. Grill over burnt-down charcoal.
  6. They should cook in about 5 to 8 minutes.
  7. Finish with lemon juice and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Thread from tail to head. Her own reasoning is that it prevents the prawns curling as they cook, and it is the single most useful prawn-grilling tip anywhere in this collection.
  • Ten minutes in the marinade, no longer. Prawns take salt and acid very fast and go rubbery if left.
  • Five to eight minutes total. Prawns are done the moment they turn opaque, so err short.
  • Fish spice is a Ghanaian market blend rather than a single ingredient and no source lists its composition. Any seafood seasoning will stand in.
  • Four tablespoons of oil for two pounds of prawns keeps them from sticking to the grill bars.
  • The lemons are not in the method, only the ingredient list — squeeze them over at the end.
  • Be aware there is a yield contradiction in the source: one fetch gives six prawns alongside the two-pound weight, which cannot both be right.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Threading head to tail, so they curl.
  • Leaving them in the marinade.
  • Overcooking past eight minutes.
  • Grilling over flames rather than coals.
  • Forgetting the lemon, which is only in the ingredient list.

Health Benefits

  • Prawns are very high in protein and low in fat.
  • A good source of selenium, iodine and vitamin B12.
  • Grilled rather than fried.
  • Very low in carbohydrate.

Cultural Note

Ghana's coast supplies prawns the length of it, and grilled over the same coals as the tilapia they are the quicker half of a beach lunch. Threading tail to head so they stay straight is the kind of small, practical knowledge that distinguishes someone who has grilled a lot of prawns from someone who has read about it.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 1 day refrigerated. Grilled prawns do not keep.

Reheating: Not recommended — they toughen. Eat cold.

Serve with: Banku, Shito, Jollof Rice
