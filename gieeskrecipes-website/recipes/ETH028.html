🥩 Ethiopian Raw Beef Cubes

ጎረድ ጎረድ — Gored Gored

Ethiopian Gored Gored — prime raw beef cut into cubes and dressed with kibbeh, mitmita (bird's eye chilli powder), awaze (spiced chilli paste), and fresh jalapeño. Ethiopia's raw beef tradition — requires the freshest, highest quality beef and is eaten immediately. An act of cultural confidence and culinary daring.

Gored Gored is the raw version of kitfo — where kitfo is minced, gored gored is cubed. The beef must be of exceptional quality and absolute freshness. Dressed with kibbeh and mitmita, eaten with injera, it is one of Ethiopia's most ancient and most prized dishes. It requires trust in the source of the meat.

Heritage & Origin

Origin: Ethiopia — the raw beef tradition that predates written history

Gored Gored is Ethiopia's most culturally significant raw meat dish — whole cubes of the freshest possible beef dressed with kibbeh, mitmita, and awaze, eaten as they are. The tradition of eating raw beef in Ethiopia is ancient, connected to the cultural practice of communal cattle slaughter where the freshest meat is consumed immediately. It requires the highest quality, freshest beef — and a cultural understanding that Ethiopian cattle-raising practices have historically produced beef of extraordinary quality.

Ingredients

Equipment: Very sharp knife, Chilled cutting board

  • 500g beef tenderloin or sirloin — very fresh, highest quality
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh — warmed
  • 1 tsp mitmita
  • 1 tsp awaze
  • 1 jalapeño — finely sliced
  • Salt to taste

Serve with: injera and ayib

    Method

    1. Use only the freshest, highest-quality beef — butcher-sourced on the day.
    2. Cut beef into 2cm cubes. Work quickly on a chilled board.
    3. Dress immediately with warmed kibbeh — toss to coat.
    4. Add mitmita, awaze, jalapeño, and salt. Toss gently.
    5. Serve immediately on injera. Eat within minutes.

    Chef Tips

    • Food safety is the absolute priority — use only the freshest, highest-grade beef from a trusted source.
    • The beef should be cold from refrigeration when cut but brought to room temperature for eating.
    • Eat immediately — do not leave at room temperature.
    • Not recommended for pregnant women, elderly, immunocompromised individuals.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Using beef of uncertain freshness or source.
    • Not eating immediately.

    The Science

    Raw beef safety depends entirely on the beef's microbial load — which is primarily a function of how recently the animal was slaughtered and how hygienically the meat was handled. The acidity of awaze (which contains vinegar) and the fat of kibbeh provide some antimicrobial protection on the surface, though these do not sterilise the meat.

    Cultural Note

    Gored Gored is Ethiopia's ultimate act of food confidence — eating the very freshest beef, at its most natural, dressed with the country's finest spices. It is associated with celebration, with courage, and with the deep trust between the Ethiopian cattle farmer and the cook.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Eat immediately — no storage.

    Reheating: Not applicable.

    Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ayib (ETH096), Tej (honey wine)
