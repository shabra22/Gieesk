The Regional Journey Platter is the most ambitious Ethiopian food experience — a tasting of every major regional tradition assembled at one table, connecting the diner to 3,000 years of Ethiopian food culture across 80+ ethnic groups.

Equipment: All equipment from individual recipes, Large tasting boards

    Method

    1. Prepare all long-cooked dishes over 2 days.
    2. Arrange on tasting boards: small portions of each dish.
    3. Label each with its region and people.
    4. Begin with the Kaffa coffee ceremony.
    5. Proceed through the regions geographically.

    Chef Tips

    • Label everything — the cultural journey is the experience.
    • Begin with kitfo and end with coffee.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Unlabelled dishes — loses the educational value.
