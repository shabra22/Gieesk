🧈 Ethiopian Butter Chicken

ቅቤ ዶሮ — Qibe Doro

Ethiopian Qibe Doro — chicken slowly simmered in abundant kibbeh with onion, garlic, ginger, and mild spices including cardamom and turmeric. Less berbere, more kibbeh — the richest, most indulgent chicken preparation in Ethiopian cooking, and the mildest.

Qibe Doro is the Ethiopian answer to a butter chicken — not spicy with tomato cream, but rich and golden with kibbeh, fragrant with cardamom and ginger. The chicken is simmered in a generous quantity of kibbeh, producing an extraordinarily rich, unctuous sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 800g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 4 tbsp kibbeh — generous
  • 2 onions — finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup water or chicken stock
  • 2 tsp salt

Optional: 2 tbsp yogurt or ayib stirred in at the end

    Method

    1. Dry caramelise onions — 15 minutes.
    2. Add kibbeh — all 4 tablespoons. Melt and coat onions.
    3. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, cardamom, and pepper. Cook 2 minutes.
    4. Add chicken. Coat in the kibbeh sauce.
    5. Add water or stock. Simmer covered 40—45 minutes.
    6. The sauce should be rich, golden, and slightly oily — this is correct.
    7. Stir in yogurt or ayib at the very end if using.

    Chef Tips

    • 4 tablespoons of kibbeh is correct — the richness is the point.
    • The resulting sauce will look oily — this is traditional and correct.
    • Cardamom is the key aromatic here — use a generous amount.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Too little kibbeh — loses the characteristic richness.
    • Adding water too early.

    The Science

    Kibbeh's spice infusion (see ETH092) means every tablespoon of kibbeh carries the flavours of its infused spices — when 4 tablespoons are used, the sauce becomes a concentrated expression of these aromatics, producing the characteristic Qibe Doro fragrance.

    Health Benefits

    • Kibbeh provides vitamins A, D, E from the spice infusion.
    • Cardamom aids digestion.
    • High protein.

    Cultural Note

    Qibe Doro is the chicken of abundance — when kibbeh is available in plenty, this is what Ethiopian cooks make to demonstrate their generosity. The golden richness of the sauce is the richness of the host's hospitality.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

    Reheating: Gently — the kibbeh will re-melt.

    Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ayib (ETH096), During celebrations alongside Doro Wat
