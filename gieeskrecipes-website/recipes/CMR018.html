🥔 Kwacoco (Steamed Grated Cocoyam)

Kwacoco

Peeled cocoyam pulsed with a little water, wrapped in foil-lined plastic wrap or banana leaves and steamed about an hour — served with mbanga or palm-nut soup.

75 minTotal time
15 minPrep
60 minCook
4Servings
280Calories
EasyDifficulty

The plain kwacoco: three pounds of cocoyam are peeled, bruised spots cut out, cut into large chunks and rinsed immediately to prevent discolouration. They are pulsed in a food processor until pureed with just enough water, about a cup per package is wrapped in heat-resistant plastic wrap inside foil (or banana leaves), the sides folded in twice to seal, and the packages are steamed about an hour. The page serves it with mbanga or palm-nut soup.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Steamer or lined pot

  • 3 pounds (1.5 kg) cocoyam (malanga, taro, yautia)
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
  • 1–2 teaspoons (5–10 g) salt (author used garlic salt)
  • Banana leaves, or foil lined with heat-resistant plastic wrap

Method

  1. Peel the cocoyam and remove any bruising and discolouration.
  2. Cut into large chunks and rinse immediately to prevent discolouration.
  3. Pulse in a food processor until pureed, adding just enough water.
  4. Wrap about a cup of the puree per package. Fold the sides in twice to seal.
  5. Steam for about an hour. Serve with mbanga or palm-nut soup.

Chef Tips

  • No doneness cue is printed beyond ‘about an hour’.
  • Use heat-resistant wrap that will not melt (page advice).
  • Rinse the cut chunks right away.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the peeled cocoyam to discolour.
  • Using ordinary plastic wrap that can melt.
  • Over-processing with too much water.

Cultural Note

The page describes kwacoco as a traditional Cameroonian dish and does not name a community. The ‘kwacoco bible’ is described by the same author as a Bakweri staple.
