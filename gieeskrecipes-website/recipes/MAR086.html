🥑 Avocado Milkshake (Moroccan Juice-Bar Style)

Avocat — avocado milkshake (عصير الأفوكا)

A ripe avocado blended with milk, sugar or honey and ice until smooth and chilled.

5mTotal time
5mPrep
3Servings
260Calories
EasyDifficulty

One cup to a cup and a half of milk goes in the blender first with a ripe avocado, sugar or honey and a handful of ice, and more milk is added in increments until the milkshake reaches the texture you prefer. It is best served chilled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 2 to 3 cups milk - or non-dairy milk
  • 1 medium ripe avocado
  • 2 tablespoons sugar or honey - or to taste
  • handful of ice cubes (optional)

Method

  1. Pour 1 to 1 1/2 cups of the milk into a blender.
  2. Add the avocado, sugar or honey and ice. Blend until smooth.
  3. Add milk in increments until the milkshake is the texture you prefer.
  4. Serve chilled.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: blended until smooth; texture to taste.
  • The page gives no blending time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • An under-ripe avocado, which will not blend smooth.

Health Benefits

  • Avocado adds fibre and unsaturated fat; milk adds calcium.

Cultural Note

The author writes that avocado milkshakes are enjoyed throughout Morocco, especially during the nights of Ramadan.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink fresh; it browns on standing.

Reheating: Serve cold.
