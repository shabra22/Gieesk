🍖 Kenyan Beef Ribs

Mbavu za Ng'ombe

Kenyan Beef Ribs — a full rack of beef ribs slow-roasted for 3 hours until falling-tender, finished over charcoal with a Kenyan spice rub and honey glaze. The ultimate restaurant BBQ statement — bones sticking up from a magnificent rack.

Kenyan beef ribs — properly done — are one of the most impressive dishes in the restaurant repertoire. The three-hour slow roast followed by charcoal finishing creates meat that falls from the bone while still holding a magnificent charred exterior.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large roasting tin, Oven, Charcoal grill or broiler, Basting brush

  • 1 full rack beef ribs (1.5—2 kg)

Dry Rub

  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp cayenne

Honey Glaze

  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove — minced

Method

  1. Apply dry rub all over the ribs. Refrigerate 4 hours minimum.
  2. Preheat oven to 150°C. Wrap ribs tightly in foil.
  3. Slow roast for 2.5—3 hours until meat pulls back from bones and is very tender.
  4. Mix honey glaze ingredients.
  5. Remove ribs from foil. Brush with honey glaze.
  6. Grill over charcoal or under broiler for 8—10 minutes, basting every 3 minutes.
  7. The glaze should caramelise magnificently.
  8. Rest 5 minutes. Cut between bones to serve.

Chef Tips

  • The two-stage cooking is the secret — slow oven makes them tender; grill makes them magnificent.
  • Wrap tightly in foil — the steam inside creates falling-off-the-bone tenderness.
  • Apply glaze in multiple layers during the final grilling.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the slow roast stage.
  • Single glaze application — insufficient caramelisation.

The Science

At 150°C over 3 hours, collagen in the rib connective tissue slowly converts to gelatin — this is the low-and-slow BBQ principle. The resulting meat is simultaneously tender and moist because the collagen conversion creates a self-basting effect. The subsequent high-heat grill creates Maillard browning impossible at low temperature.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Oven at 160°C wrapped in foil.

Serve with: Creamy Mashed Potatoes (KEN277), Grilled Vegetable Platter (KEN270), Coleslaw
