🍩 Beignets (Yeast-Raised Wheat Doughnuts, Northern Cameroon)

Beignets

Sifted flour mixed by hand with yeast, sugar and warm water into a smooth dough, rested 2–3 hours until doubled or tripled in volume, then fried 5–10 minutes, turning constantly.

p. 55 of the October 2023 recipe book. The page calls beignets ‘the number-one dish of the Cameroonian diet’, eaten hot with sautéed beans and/or porridge. The yeast is dissolved with the salt in part of the warm water and the sugar in the rest; a well is made in the sifted flour and mixed by hand to a smooth, homogeneous dough. It is covered and left 2–3 hours; after the rest the dough ‘will have doubled, even tripled, in volume’. The beignets are fried 5–10 minutes, turned constantly, and drained. The rise cue qualifies this yeast dough under the fermentation rule. Frying temperature, oil quantity and how the 250 ml of water is split are not printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Frying pan

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 100 g sugar
  • 8 g baker’s yeast
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast with the salt in part of the warm water, and the sugar in the rest.
  2. Make a well in the sifted flour and mix by hand to a smooth, homogeneous dough.
  3. Cover and leave 2–3 hours. The dough should have doubled, even tripled, in volume.
  4. Fry the beignets 5–10 minutes, turning constantly.
  5. Drain and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is that the dough has doubled, even tripled, in volume.
  • Printed accompaniments: sautéed beans and/or porridge.
  • Frying temperature and oil quantity are not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying before the dough has risen.
  • Not turning the beignets.
  • Using cold water for the yeast.

Cultural Note
