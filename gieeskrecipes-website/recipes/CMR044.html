🐟 Poisson braisé à la camerounaise (with Sauce Essaho)

Poisson braisé

Per-person recipe: a bream scored and brushed with a fried paste of chilli, djanssan, ginger, onion, tomato and celery, grilled about 15 minutes a side, with a soy-and-sesame sauce essaho.

Quantities are given per person. The fish is scaled, gutted, gills and fins removed, head and tail left on, and lightly scored and salted. The chilli, djanssan, ginger (djidja), celery, onion and tomato are crushed with salt and fried about 15 minutes in the oil; the fish is brushed with the paste and left about ten minutes. It is grilled over a barbecue or under the oven grill about 15 minutes per side, brushed again, and garnished with parsley. The sauce essaho (bouillon, onions and garlic, hot sauce, black soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, salt, white pepper) is listed, but the page stops at ‘Portez à ébullition… Baissez le feu, laissez frémir’ — its method is incomplete.

Ingredients

Equipment: Barbecue or oven grill, Mortar, Frying pan

    Sauce essaho

    • 1 chicken stock cube
    • Onions and garlic — quantities not printed
    • 1 tablespoon hot sauce or small chillies
    • 2 teaspoons black soy sauce
    • 2 teaspoons sugar
    • 2 teaspoons sesame oil
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
    • A pinch of freshly ground white pepper

    Per person

    • 1 medium sea bream (about 300 g) or 1 sea bass
    • 5 cl peanut oil
    • 1 red chilli
    • 5 djanssan seeds
    • 5 g ginger (djidja)
    • 1 small onion
    • 2 cloves garlic
    • 1 small tomato
    • 1 small stalk celery
    • 2 sprigs parsley
    • Salt and pepper

    Method

    1. Scale and gut the fish, remove the gills and fins, leaving the head and tail. Score lightly, salt and pepper.
    2. Crush the chilli, djanssan, ginger, celery, onion and tomato with salt. Fry in the oil about 15 minutes.
    3. Brush the fish with the paste and leave about ten minutes.
    4. Grill over a barbecue or under the oven grill about 15 minutes per side, brushing again. Garnish with parsley.
    5. Sauce essaho: the page lists the ingredients and says to bring to the boil and lower the heat to simmer, but gives no further method.

    Chef Tips

    • The page prints no doneness cue; check the thickest part for flesh that flakes.
    • Quantities are per person — scale up for more fish.
    • The sauce essaho method is incomplete as printed.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not leaving head and tail on as the page says.
    • Skipping the scoring.
    • Expecting a complete sauce method — the page stops short.
