🦑 Tagen Seafood we Freekeh (One-Pot Seafood and Freekeh Bake)

Tagen seafood wi freek — طاجن سي فود وفريك

Freekeh baked with olives, tomatoes, celery and stock, topped with quick-seared shrimp and calamari.

1hTotal time
20mPrep
40mCook
7Servings
470Calories
MediumDifficulty

Vegetables are stir-fried 2 to 4 minutes until fragrant, then freekeh, and moved to a baking dish. The shrimp and calamari are stir-fried until the shrimp start to turn pink and the calamari edges curl, then set aside. Stock, spices and herbs go in and the dish roasts at 375° for 20 to 30 minutes, until the freekeh swells, the liquid reduces and the vegetables are tender.

Ingredients

Equipment: Skillet, Baking dish, Oven

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, whole
  • 2 stalks celery, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 200 grams calamari
  • 200 grams shrimps, tails on, shelled, and deveined
  • 2 cups freekeh
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 4 cups of vegetable stock
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 5 sprigs thyme
  • Juice of one lemon
  • salt and pepper
  • 2 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°.
  2. Stir-fry the onion, garlic, celery and tomatoes in the oil for 2 to 4 minutes until fragrant, add the freekeh and stir, then move to the baking dish.
  3. Stir-fry the shrimp and calamari and remove them once the shrimp begin to turn pink and the calamari edges curl up.
  4. Add the stock, tomato paste, spices, thyme, olives and lemon juice to the dish and roast 20 to 30 minutes, until the freekeh swells, the liquid is reduced and the vegetables are tender. Top with the seafood.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: shrimp pink; calamari edges curl; freekeh swollen with liquid reduced. When the seafood goes back in is not stated; add it at the end so it stays tender (our note).
  • The page calls the vessel a 'heat resistant dish' in one step and a 'roasting pan' in others.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the seafood too early so it turns rubbery.

Health Benefits

  • Freekeh and seafood make a high-fibre, high-protein meal.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour says it was inspired by her hometown Alexandria, is usually cooked in a clay pot, and that freekeh is a great alternative to rice.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Salad
