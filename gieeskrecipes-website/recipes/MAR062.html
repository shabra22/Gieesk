🫓 Harcha (Semolina Griddle Bread)

Harcha (حرشة)

A dense, crumbly semolina galette with butter or olive oil, leavened with baking powder and cooked in a pan 3 to 4 minutes a side.

50mTotal time
15mPrep
25mCook
6Servings
360Calories
EasyDifficulty

The French recipe on Afrik.com mixes fine and medium semolina with salt, baking powder and butter or olive oil into a sandy dough, adds warm milk, rests it 10 minutes, rolls it to 1 cm and cuts small rounds. They are cooked in a non-stick pan 3 to 4 minutes on each side. It is traditionally one large family galette served with olive oil; the author prefers mini galettes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick pan, Rolling pin, Round cutter

  • 200 g d'un mélange de semoule fine et moyenne (200 g mixture of fine and medium semolina)
  • 1/2 cc de sel (1/2 tsp salt)
  • 1 sachet de levure chimique (1 sachet baking powder)
  • 150 g de beurre ou d'huile d'olive (150 g butter or olive oil)
  • 1 petit verre de lait tiédi (1 small glass warm milk)
  • eau (water; no quantity given)

Method

  1. Mix the semolina, salt, baking powder and butter or oil to a sandy dough.
  2. Add the warm milk, and water only if needed. Do not knead; work until the dough is smooth, even and malleable.
  3. Leave to rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Roll to a thickness of 1 cm and cut small rounds.
  5. Cook in a non-stick pan three to four minutes on each side. The page gives no heat level.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no doneness cue for the cooking; look for golden colour.
  • There is no yeast, so no rise: baking powder only.
  • Serve with honey, olive oil, butter or black olives.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-working the dough.
  • Rolling thinner than 1 cm.
  • Cooking over high heat.

Health Benefits

  • Semolina is a slow-digesting grain.
  • The butter makes it rich; olive oil is a lighter option.

Cultural Note

The page says harcha is traditionally one large galette for the family, served with olive oil; the author prefers mini galettes.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best the day it is made.

Reheating: Warm in a dry pan.

Serve with: Honey, olive oil, butter or black olives
