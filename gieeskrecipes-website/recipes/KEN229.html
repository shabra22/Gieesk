🥑 Kenyan Avocado Toast

Toast ya Parachichi

Kenyan Avocado Toast — thick-sliced toasted sourdough topped with smashed Kenyan avocado, kachumbari, chilli flakes, a squeeze of lemon, fresh coriander, and a fried egg. The global breakfast trend made better with Kenya's world-class avocados.

Kenya produces some of the world's finest avocados — the large, buttery Hass and Fuerte varieties that grow in the highlands have an extraordinary richness and flavour. On good sourdough with kachumbari and a fried egg, Kenyan avocado toast surpasses versions made with avocados imported from anywhere else.

Ingredients

Equipment: Toaster, Pan for eggs, Fork for mashing

  • 4 thick slices sourdough or good bread
  • 2 large ripe Kenyan avocados
  • 2 tbsp kachumbari — drained
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 eggs — fried or poached
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander — chopped
  • Flaky sea salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Toast bread until golden and crispy.
  2. Mash avocados with lime juice, salt, and pepper. Keep some texture.
  3. Spread avocado generously on each toast.
  4. Fry or poach eggs to preference.
  5. Spoon kachumbari on top of the avocado.
  6. Place egg on top.
  7. Scatter chilli flakes, coriander, and a drizzle of olive oil.
  8. Finish with flaky salt. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • The avocado must be perfectly ripe — Kenyan avocados are at their best when the skin gives slightly under gentle pressure.
  • Add lime juice immediately after mashing — prevents browning.
  • Drained kachumbari is key — excess liquid makes the toast soggy.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-ripe avocado.
  • Not draining kachumbari — soggy toast.
  • Over-mashing — loses the creamy texture.

The Science

See KEN138 for avocado browning science. Draining kachumbari removes tomato water that would otherwise saturate the toast — keeping the base crispy under the avocado layer.

Cultural Note

Kenyan avocado toast represents Nairobi's thriving modern café scene claiming this global breakfast trend with confidence — Kenya's avocados make a genuinely superior version of this dish that locals and visitors alike recognise.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Assemble immediately before eating.

Reheating: Not applicable.
