🥤 Almond Date Milk

Halib bil louz w tmar — date and almond milk

Milk blended with soft dates and almonds until smooth and creamy, served cold.

5mTotal time
5mPrep
2Servings
230Calories
EasyDifficulty

Six to eight ounces of milk or milk substitute are blended for 2 to 3 minutes with four large soft dates and ten to twelve almonds until smooth and creamy, and poured into a glass cold.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender

  • 6-8 oz of milk or milk substitute (almond milk/soy milk etc)
  • 4 large soft dates
  • 10-12 almonds

Method

  1. Put the milk, pitted dates and almonds in a blender.
  2. Blend for 2 to 3 minutes until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a glass and enjoy cold.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: smooth and creamy.
  • The page says to use soft dates.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using dry dates that do not blend smooth.

Health Benefits

  • Dates and almonds add fibre, potassium and healthy fats; dates are high in natural sugar.

Cultural Note

The author writes that dates and almonds go together in Morocco like peanut butter and jelly in America, and that sweet milk is served at weddings and special celebrations as a wish for a sweet life.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Drink fresh.

Reheating: Serve cold.
