🍅 Ethiopian Tomato Salad

ቲማቲም ሰላጣ — Timatim Selata

Ethiopian Tomato Salad — ripe tomatoes sliced with onion, jalapeño, coriander, and lemon dressing. The simplest, most refreshing element on the Ethiopian table — the raw tomato's acidity cutting through the richness of the wats.

Ethiopian tomato salad is the table's refreshing note — the raw tomatoes and lemon providing the bright acid counterpoint to the rich berbere wats. Simple, immediate, and essential.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl

  • 4 ripe tomatoes — sliced
  • ½ onion — thinly sliced
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ¼ cup coriander
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Slice tomatoes. Arrange on a plate.
  2. Scatter onion and jalapeño.
  3. Dress with lemon and oil.
  4. Season. Scatter coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Very ripe tomatoes only — unripe tomatoes lack the sweetness and acidity.
  • Rest 5 minutes — salt draws out the tomato juices into the dressing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unripe tomatoes.

The Science

Ripe tomatoes contain lycopene — a fat-soluble antioxidant. The small amount of oil in the dressing increases lycopene bioavailability dramatically compared to eating them with no fat.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian tomato salad is the simplest expression of what the Ethiopian table understands — that raw freshness and acidity are as important as richness and spice. Together they create the complete meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: On the fasting platter, Alongside grilled fish, With any Ethiopian meal
