🍮 Om Ali (Egypt’s Iconic Bread Pudding)

Om Ali — أم علي

Baked puff pastry layered with a spice- and mastic-infused milk and cream, nuts and raisins, topped with whipped cream and baked until bubbling.

1h 50mTotal time
30mPrep
50mCook
6Servings
620Calories
MediumDifficulty

The milk and cream warm until they bubble at the edges and a spice bag is added; they simmer very gently for no more than a minute and steep at least 30 minutes or until the milk reaches room temperature. Puff pastry is baked until puffed and golden and layered with nuts, raisins and the milk mixture in an 8 to 9 inch dish, topped with whipped cream and cinnamon, and baked at 360°F (180°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until it starts to bubble, then broiled or torched.

Ingredients

Equipment: 8 to 9 inch deep baking dish, Saucepan, Cheesecloth or tea ball, Torch or broiler

  • 4 cups infused milk-cream mixture (recipe follows)
  • 4 puff pastry sheets or 5 good quality baked croissants
  • 1 cup mixed nuts of your choice, toasted
  • 3 tablespoons raisins
  • 1 cup whipped cream (recipe follows)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Milk mixture

  • 3 1/2 cups full fat milk
  • 1/2 cup whipping cream
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 mastic resins, crushed with a spoonful of sugar
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 5 whole cloves
  • 5 allspice whole berries
  • 3 pods of bruised cardamom
  • 1 teaspoon of rose water or orange blossom, optional

Whipped cream

  • ½ cup whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons superfine or icing sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla

Method

  1. Warm the milk and cream. Once it starts to bubble on the edges, add the spices in an infuser, simmer very gently for no more than 1 minute, then steep at least 30 minutes or until the milk comes to room temperature.
  2. Bake the puff pastry until puffed and golden (the page says 375°C for 20 to 25 minutes, a misprint for °F), then break it into pieces.
  3. Whip the cream with the sugar and vanilla.
  4. Layer pastry, infused milk, nuts and raisins in the dish, then top with whipped cream and cinnamon.
  5. Bake at 360°F (180°C) for 20 to 25 minutes, or until it starts to bubble, then broil 3 minutes or use a torch.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: milk bubbles at the edges; pastry puffed and golden; the dish begins to bubble. The steeping is time plus a temperature cue (room temperature).
  • The page prints 375°C for the pastry, which cannot be right; use about 375°F (190°C).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling the milk, which can curdle it with the mastic.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour retells the legend of Om Ali and Shajar al-Durr, but reports that the Egyptian food archaeologist Mennat-Allah El Dorry has found no historical evidence for it and attributes its popularity to a column by the author Anis Mansour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; refrigerate leftovers up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Tea or coffee
