🫛 Insalaaddo Digir

Insalaaddo Digir

Cooked fava beans and chickpeas tossed with chopped tomato, cucumber, green onion and parsley, dressed with lemon, olive oil and black pepper, and left an hour to come together.

This is the cold, no-cook end of Somali bean cooking and it is a useful thing to have: a side that doubles as lunch, made from whatever beans are already cooked. The hour of marinating is the only instruction that matters — served straight away it tastes like a pile of ingredients, and after an hour it tastes like a dish. Tinned beans are entirely acceptable, though many households cook a big batch and freeze it in portions.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl

  • 90 g (1/2 cup) cooked fava beans
  • 90 g (1/2 cup) cooked chickpeas
  • 1 medium firm tomato, chopped
  • 70 g (1/2 cup) English cucumber, chopped
  • 2 stalks green onion, chopped
  • 40 g (1/4 cup) fresh parsley, chopped
  • 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
  • 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
  • 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
  • 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) ground black pepper

Optional

  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Method

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Stir well.
  3. Marinate for 1 hour for the flavours to blend, or serve right away if you are in a hurry.

Chef Tips

  • Give it the hour. It is a genuinely different dish before and after, and this is the whole of the technique.
  • Tinned fava beans and chickpeas are fine and are what most people use. Rinse them well.
  • Use a firm tomato. A soft one waters the dressing down over the hour it sits.
  • Cooking a large batch of beans and freezing them in portions makes this a five-minute dish for the rest of the month.
  • The garlic is optional and it is assertive raw — one clove for four servings is plenty.
  • Chop everything to roughly the size of a chickpea so it eats evenly off a spoon.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Serving it immediately.
  • Not rinsing tinned beans, so it tastes of the can.
  • Using a soft, ripe tomato.
  • Overdressing it — a tablespoon of lemon and two of oil is the balance.

Health Benefits

  • Fava beans and chickpeas together are high in plant protein and fibre.
  • A good source of folate, iron and manganese.
  • No cooking at all, so nothing is lost to heat.
  • Entirely plant-based and genuinely filling for the calories.

Cultural Note

Digir covers a wide family of beans and pulses in Somali, and the word turns up across the cuisine — in cambuulo, in maraq digir, in the okra-and-bean stew. Beans are the backbone of Somali eating in a way the meat-and-rice reputation obscures, and a cold bean salad is a reminder that not all of it is a long stew.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days. It improves on the second day.

Reheating: Not applicable — it is a cold dish.

Serve with: Sabaayad, Bariis Iskukaris, Kalluun la Dubay
