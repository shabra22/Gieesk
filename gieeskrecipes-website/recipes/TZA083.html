🍜 Tanzanian Coconut Fish Soup

Supu ya Samaki wa Nazi

Tanzanian Coconut Fish Soup — a fragrant, rich soup of flaked fish in a coconut milk broth with ginger, lemon grass, and Zanzibar spices. The coastal version — richer, more fragrant, and deeply restorative.

Coconut fish soup is the richer, more celebratory version of plain fish soup — the coconut milk adds depth and MCTs, lemon grass adds fragrance, and the result heals and satisfies simultaneously.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 600g fish — bone-in pieces
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 stalk lemon grass — bruised
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 1 onion — chopped
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. Simmer fish with water, onion, garlic, ginger, lemon grass, turmeric for 20 minutes.
  2. Remove fish. Flake meat. Discard bones and lemon grass.
  3. Add coconut milk and tomatoes to broth. Simmer gently 8 minutes.
  4. Return flaked fish. Add curry powder. Season.
  5. Garnish with coriander. Serve with lime.

Chef Tips

  • Fish frames make the richest base.
  • Never boil after adding coconut milk.
  • Lime at table is essential.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling coconut milk.
  • Discarding fish frames.

Cultural Note

Coconut fish soup is the healing food of Zanzibar's coastal communities — the soup mothers make when someone is unwell, that fishermen eat to restore strength.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Very gently.

Serve with: Chapati (TZA004), As a standalone, With Rice
