🐟 Stuffed Fried Sardines with Chermoula

Sardines mchermla — mzeouej (married) sardines (سردين محشي بالشرمولة)

Butterflied sardines sandwiched in pairs with chermoula paste, marinated, coated in white and durum flour and fried until done; served hot or at room temperature over parsley.

3hTotal time
40mPrep
20mCook
6Servings
480Calories
MediumDifficulty

Small fresh sardines are scaled, boned and butterflied. Each is rubbed with a third of the chermoula, laid skin side down, spread generously with more chermoula and topped skin side up with a second sardine. The stuffed pairs marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours, are drained and coated with a mix of white and durum flour, and fried in hot oil about 3 minutes per side. The author says the stuffed pairs are called mzeouej, machrouk or ashiq we ma'ashuk.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep skillet or fryer, Shallow dish, Paper towels

  • 2.2 lbs fresh sardines (ideally small), - scaled, boned and butterflied
  • 1 cup chermoula paste
  • 1 tsp salt, - or to taste
  • 1 cup frying oil
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup durum flour
  • lemon wedges
  • fresh parsley

Method

  1. Rub the sardines with 1/3 of the chermoula and add salt to taste.
  2. Lay a sardine skin side down, spread generously with chermoula and top symmetrically with another sardine, skin side up.
  3. Cover and marinate the stuffed sardines in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  4. Drain them and coat with a mix of the white and durum flours.
  5. Shallow or deep fry in hot oil for about 3 minutes per side.
  6. Serve hot, warm or at room temperature over a bed of parsley or salad, with lemon wedges.

Chef Tips

  • The frying time (about 3 minutes per side) and the marinade (at least 2 hours) are NO CUE, TIME ONLY; the page relays no colour cue.
  • The author says the stuffed pairs are meant to look like a couple.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Letting the pairs slide apart; press them together firmly.
  • Crowding the pan so the oil cools.

Health Benefits

  • Sardines are rich in omega-3 fatty acids and calcium; frying adds fat.

Cultural Note

The author explains that the stuffed pairs are named for being coupled; SBS likewise translates 'mzeouejim' as 'married', for the fillets paired like a couple.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten the day they are fried.

Reheating: Re-crisp in a hot oven.

Serve with: Salad, Bread
