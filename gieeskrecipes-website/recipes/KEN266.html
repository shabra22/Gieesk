🍗 Kenyan Cream of Chicken Soup

Supu ya Kuku wa Cream

Kenyan Cream of Chicken Soup — a silky, deeply flavoured soup of slowly simmered chicken, vegetables, and herbs, finished with cream and a hint of Kenyan paprika. The hotel starter that comforts and satisfies without heaviness.

Cream of chicken soup made properly — with a real chicken stock base, simmered slowly and finished with cream — bears no resemblance to its tinned counterpart. At Nairobi's finest hotels, this is the soup course that signals the kitchen knows what it is doing.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Saucepan, Blender

  • 600g chicken pieces — bone-in
  • 2 litres water
  • 2 medium onions — diced
  • 2 medium carrots — diced
  • 2 celery stalks — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Method

  1. Simmer chicken with water, one onion, carrot, celery, garlic, thyme, and salt for 45 minutes.
  2. Remove chicken. Shred finely. Strain and reserve the stock.
  3. Melt butter in a saucepan. Cook remaining diced onion until soft.
  4. Add flour. Cook 1 minute to form a roux.
  5. Whisk in reserved chicken stock gradually until smooth.
  6. Simmer 10 minutes until thickened.
  7. Add cream and paprika. Simmer 5 minutes.
  8. Add shredded chicken.
  9. Season and garnish with parsley.

Chef Tips

  • The chicken stock is the soul of this soup — do not rush the simmering.
  • The roux thickens the stock into the characteristic cream-of-soup consistency.
  • Reserve some shredded chicken to be visibly added at service — diners want to see the chicken.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Short simmer — weak stock.
  • Lumpy roux — whisk consistently when adding stock.

The Science

A roux (butter and flour cooked together) contains starch granules coated in fat. When stock is whisked in, the fat coating slows the initial starch absorption, preventing lumps. As the soup simmers, the starch gelatinises fully, creating a smooth, thickened sauce.

Cream of chicken soup at a Nairobi hotel is a comfort food touchstone — the soup course that families remember from special occasion dinners, school graduation lunches, and first hotel experiences.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days — add cream when reheating.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Garlic Bread (KEN279), As a starter course
