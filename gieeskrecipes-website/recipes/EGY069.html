🥛 Sobia (Egyptian Coconut and Rice Cold Drink)

Sobia — سوبيا

Ground rice soaked in water in the fridge, then blended with coconut milk, powdered milk, sugar and vanilla and strained; served ice-cold.

6h 10mTotal time
10mPrep
3Servings
380Calories
EasyDifficulty

Two tablespoons of dry rice are ground to a fine powder and mixed with a cup of water in the fridge for at least 6 hours. The mixture is blended 3 to 4 minutes with sugar, powdered milk, coconut milk and vanilla powder until well combined, strained through cheesecloth, a coffee filter or tea strainer, and chilled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or grinder, Fine strainer or cheesecloth, Jar

  • 2 Tablespoons dry uncooked rice.
  • 1 Cup water.
  • ¼ Cup sugar- more or less depending on your liking.
  • ¼ Cup powdered milk.
  • 2 Cups coconut milk.
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla powder.

Method

  1. Grind the rice to a fine powder, stir it into the water and let it sit for at least 6 hours in the fridge.
  2. Blend with the sugar, powdered milk, coconut milk and vanilla for 3 to 4 minutes, until well combined.
  3. Strain through cheesecloth, a coffee filter or a tea strainer.
  4. Chill and serve cold.

Chef Tips

  • The rice soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least 6 hours in the fridge; another line says overnight). This version is a plain soak, not a ferment.
  • The page compares it to horchata.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the straining, which leaves it gritty.

Cultural Note

Amira Ibrahim says sobia is popular at Ramadan and sold in plastic bottles by street vendors; she compares it to horchata. Nermine Mansour recalls the juice shops on Ramses Street in Cairo.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Ice
