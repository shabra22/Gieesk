🍳 Frittata alle Erbe Friulana

Frittata alle Erbe

A simple Friulian frittata built on a generous mix of foraged spring herbs and wild greens, reflecting the region's deep tradition of gathering edible plants from the countryside.

Friuli's mountainous and rural landscape has long supported a strong foraging tradition, and this frittata showcases that heritage, using whatever mix of wild herbs and greens - nettles, wild garlic, dandelion - happens to be in season.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large non-stick skillet, Plate for flipping

  • 8 eggs
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) mixed wild greens and herbs (spinach, nettles, wild garlic, parsley), blanched and chopped
  • 60 g (3/4 cup) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Squeeze the blanched greens thoroughly to remove excess water.
  2. Beat the eggs with the Parmigiano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir the chopped greens into the beaten eggs.
  4. Warm the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour in the egg mixture, spreading evenly, and cook for 5-6 minutes until the bottom is set.
  6. Carefully flip using a plate, then slide back into the pan to cook the other side for another 4-5 minutes.
  7. Slide onto a board, slice into wedges, and serve warm or at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Squeeze the greens thoroughly to avoid excess moisture making the frittata soggy.
  • A varied mix of greens gives the most authentic, complex flavor, reflecting the traditional foraged approach.
  • This frittata is equally good warm, at room temperature, or cold, making it versatile for picnics.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not squeezing the greens enough, resulting in a watery frittata.
  • Cooking over heat that is too high, browning the outside before the center sets.
  • Using only one type of green, missing the more complex flavor a varied mix provides.

Cultural Note

This frittata reflects Friuli's strong foraging tradition, using the mix of wild herbs and greens gathered from the region's mountainous countryside each spring.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days; excellent eaten cold.

Reheating: Reheat gently in a dry skillet, or enjoy at room temperature.

Serve with: Crusty bread, A simple side salad
