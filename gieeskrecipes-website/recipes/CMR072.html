🌺 Nectar d’oseille ou de foléré (Roselle Calyx Drink)

Nectar d’oseille / simi / oworo

A kilogram of roselle calyces soaked 10 minutes, boiled 20 minutes, cooled, filtered and sweetened with sugar to taste — also called simi or oworo.

p. 71 of the October 2023 recipe book. Calyces are sorted to remove impurities, soaked in 5 l of drinking water 10 minutes to soften, boiled 20 minutes, removed from the heat and the juice left to cool; filtered through a fine white cloth or sieve; sugar added to the desired taste; the juice bottled and kept cool. The page says the drink, also called simi or oworo, is much liked by young and old. The ingredient list prints 4 litres of water while the method prints 5 l — a SUSPECTED MISPRINT/CONFLICT, not corrected; the diagram also says soaking in 5 l and shows 65 degrees.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Fine cloth or sieve, Bottles

  • 1 kg roselle calyces
  • 4 litres drinking water (the method says 5 l)
  • 2 kg sugar (the method says ‘to taste’)

Method

  1. Sort the calyces and remove impurities.
  2. Soak in 5 l of drinking water for 10 minutes to soften them.
  3. Boil 20 minutes. Remove from the heat and let the juice cool.
  4. Filter through a fine white cloth or sieve.
  5. Add sugar to the desired taste and mix.
  6. Bottle and keep cool.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cues are ‘to soften’ for the soak and sugar ‘to taste’.
  • Water: 4 l in the list and 5 l in the method — pick one and note it.
  • No colour cue is printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding all 2 kg sugar without tasting.
  • Skipping the filtering.
  • Not cooling before bottling.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The page title also offers the name ‘nectar de foléré’ and names simi or oworo.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keep cool (‘conserver au frais’). No shelf life is printed.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Served cold
