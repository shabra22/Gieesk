🥙 Hawawshi (Crispy Meat-Stuffed Pita)

Hawawshi — حواوشي

Beef and lamb mixed with a blended paste of onion, tomato, peppers and parsley, stuffed into baladi bread, seared on a griddle and baked until crusty.

1hTotal time
30mPrep
30mCook
10Servings
520Calories
MediumDifficulty

Onions, tomatoes, a mild pepper, hot chilies and parsley are blended to a fine paste and squeezed to remove excess water, then mixed with ground beef and lamb and spices. About ¾ to 1 cup of filling goes into each pita, which is brushed with oil, seared on a griddle until crusty and golden, and baked at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes until the juices run clear.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender or food processor, Cheesecloth or strainer, Griddle or skillet, Baking sheet, Oven

  • 1 pound ground beef, at least 25% fat
  • 2 pounds ground lamb
  • 2 medium red onions, coarsely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, diced
  • 2 cups fresh parsley leaves
  • 1 mild green bell pepper, coarsely chopped
  • 2-3 green chili peppers, coarsely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon fine salt
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 2 teaspoons ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes, optional
  • ½ teaspoons ground cardamom
  • 10 whole Aish Baladi, Egyptian whole wheat pita bread, or pita
  • 2-3 tablespoons vegetable oil

To serve

  • 1 cup pickles
  • 1 cup tahini sauce

Method

  1. Blend the onions, tomatoes, bell pepper, chilies and parsley to a fine paste and squeeze out excess water. The page prints no blending time.
  2. Mix the paste with the beef, lamb and spices until evenly combined.
  3. Snip the edge of each pita to open a pocket and fill with about ¾ to 1 cup of the meat mixture, spreading it evenly.
  4. Brush the bread with oil and sear on a griddle until the bread is crusty and golden; no time is printed.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes, until the stuffing is cooked through and the juices run clear.
  6. Serve with tahini sauce, pickles and salata baladi.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: bread crusty and golden; juices run clear. Searing and squeezing times are NOT PRINTED.
  • Squeezing the paste matters; wet filling steams the bread soft.
  • The method step reads '1 pounds ground beef' (single reading); the ingredient list says 1 pound beef and 2 pounds lamb.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the filling wet.
  • Overfilling so the pita splits.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour writes, 'If America has the hamburger, Egypt has hawawshi,' and notes popular lore crediting a butcher, Ahmed Al-Hawawshi, in 1970s Cairo (single reading; lore, not settled history).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked hawawshi up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Re-crisp in a hot oven or dry skillet.

Serve with: Egyptian tahini sauce, Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
