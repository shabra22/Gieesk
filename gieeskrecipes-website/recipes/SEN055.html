🍅 Vegetarian Jollof Rice

Jollof — Thiam

Vegetables and tomato paste cooked low until the oil separates and rises, stock simmered in, basmati stirred through and covered for 20 minutes, peas folded in.

70 minTotal time
15 minPrep
55 minCook
5Servings

EasyDifficulty

Thiam’s WBUR printing of jollof is the plain red-rice backbone of the Wolof tradition, without fish. Onion, red pepper, carrots, plum tomatoes and a very large measure of tomato paste cook slowly with water about 15 minutes until the oil separates and rises. Vegetable stock and a bay leaf simmer 10 more minutes; rinsed basmati (rinse until the water runs clear) goes in and cooks tightly covered and undisturbed 20 minutes; peas are folded in at the end. The page prints ‘2 cups tomato paste’ against 3 cups of stock — a suspected misprint kept as printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot with a tight lid

  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1/2 yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 carrots, peeled and diced
  • 3 plum tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups tomato paste (as printed — see sources)
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 dried bay leaf
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper — quantities not printed
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen green peas, thawed

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high. Add the onion, bell pepper, carrots, tomatoes, tomato paste and water. Reduce to low and cook, stirring often, about 15 minutes, until the oil separates and rises.
  2. Add the stock and bay leaf, season and bring to a boil. Reduce and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Drain.
  4. Add the rice, season again, bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover tightly and cook undisturbed 20 minutes.
  5. Fold in the peas, remove the bay leaf and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • ‘Until the oil separates and rises’ is the doneness cue for the base — the same cue as in the fish dishes.
  • Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  • The tomato paste quantity is a suspected misprint: if 2 cups looks wrong against 3 cups of stock, you can start with 2 tablespoons as in Thiam’s fonio jollof, but that is a cook’s adjustment, not a printed one.
  • Do not lift the lid during the 20 minutes.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid.
  • Cutting short the 15-minute paste stage so the oil never rises.

Cultural Note

Thiam says jollof has ‘Wolof origins’ — that Jollof was an ancient Wolof kingdom in Senegal — and that the dish spread and evolved across West Africa. This is his claim, recorded as such; Oldways states the same Wolof-empire etymology, but no source in this set documents it beyond the assertion.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 4 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Steam with a splash of water.

Serve with: A green salad
