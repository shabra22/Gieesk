🍗 Poulet DG (Afrik, Amina Savoura)

Poulet DG / PDG

Chicken browned with onions, then simmered with leek, carrots, green beans, peppers, tomatoes, ginger, curry, djansan and pèpè, with yellow plantain added after 10 minutes and simmered a further 50.

95 minTotal time
35 minPrep
60 minCook
6Servings
610Calories
MediumDifficulty

The French-language version: the chicken is cut up and browned in 3 tablespoons of oil in a sauté pan, the onions added until golden, then the oil is thrown out and the chicken set aside. In a cocotte: add 1/2 litre of water, salt, pepper, curry and the stock cube; add the leeks, carrots, green beans, peppers and tomatoes in pieces, then garlic, crushed ginger, parsley and celery. Bring to a boil. The plantain goes in after 10 minutes and the pot simmers 50 minutes more on low heat, stirring from time to time, adding water if needed. The plantains can alternatively be fried separately and added at the end ‘so they absorb the sauce’.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sauté pan, Cocotte or large pot

  • 1 chicken
  • 4 yellow plantains
  • 4 carrots
  • 100 g green beans
  • 1 leek
  • 2 onions
  • 1/2 red bell pepper and 1/2 green bell pepper
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 small stalk celery
  • 2 sprigs parsley
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 stock cube
  • Ginger, to taste
  • 2 teaspoons curry
  • 30 djansan seeds
  • 4 pépé seeds
  • Salt and pepper
  • Peanut or sunflower oil

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into pieces and brown them in a sauté pan with 3 tablespoons of oil.
  2. Add the onions and cook until golden. Pour off the oil and set aside the chicken.
  3. In a cocotte add 1/2 litre of water, salt and pepper, sprinkle with curry and add the stock cube.
  4. Add the leeks, carrots, green beans, peppers and tomatoes in pieces, then the garlic, crushed ginger, parsley and celery. Bring to a boil.
  5. Add the plantain after 10 minutes, then simmer a further 50 minutes on low heat, stirring from time to time and adding water if needed.
  6. Optional: fry the plantains separately and add them at the end so that they absorb the sauce.

Chef Tips

  • The page times the dish (10 + 50 minutes) rather than giving a doneness cue.
  • Djansan and pépé are listed but the page never says when to add them — add them with the aromatics.
  • The page notes the plantains can be fried separately for a firmer result.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the frying oil in the pot.
  • Putting the plantain in at the start.
  • Letting the pot run dry over the 50-minute simmer.
