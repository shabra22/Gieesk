🍖 Tarb (Kofta Wrapped in Lamb Fat)

Tarb — طرب

Minced lamb mixed with onion and parsley, shaped into fingers, wrapped in lamb fat and baked with a little water for an hour and a half.

2h 50mTotal time
20mPrep
1h 30mCook
6Servings

MediumDifficulty

One kilo of minced lamb is mixed with a chopped onion, salt, pepper, an unspecified spice and parsley and left at least an hour. It is shaped into fingers and wrapped in lamb fat, arranged on a tray with seasoning and half a cup of water, and baked in an oven on medium heat for one and a half hours until cooked.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking tray, Mixing bowl

  • One medium onion
  • 1 kg minced lamb
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon spices
  • 1 tablespoon parsley
  • Tarb (lamb fat)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • 1/2 cup water

Method

  1. Chop the onion and mix it into the lamb with the salt, pepper, spices and parsley. Leave for at least one hour.
  2. Shape into fingers and wrap each in lamb fat.
  3. Arrange on a tray, season with the extra salt and pepper and add the water.
  4. Place the tray in an oven on medium heat for one and a half hours, until cooked.

Chef Tips

  • No oven temperature is printed (NOT PRINTED); medium heat is the page's wording. The one-hour rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The "spices" line does not say which spices; use your usual lamb blend.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving gaps in the fat wrap, which dries the kofta out (our caution).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm in a moderate oven.

Serve with: White rice, Salata sharqi
