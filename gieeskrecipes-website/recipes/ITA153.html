Baci di Dama

Delicate Piedmontese hazelnut cookies, two small domes sandwiched together with dark chocolate — 'lady's kisses,' named for their gentle, paired shape.

Baci di dama demand a light hand and finely ground hazelnuts to achieve their characteristic delicate crumble, with just a thin layer of melted chocolate holding the two halves together in the shape their name evokes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Mixing bowl, Baking sheet

  • 150 g (1 cup) hazelnuts, toasted and finely ground
  • 150 g (⅔ cup) unsalted butter, softened
  • 150 g (¾ cup) granulated sugar
  • 150 g (1¼ cups) 00 flour
  • Pinch of salt
  • 120 g (4.2 oz) dark chocolate, melted

Method

  1. Grind the toasted hazelnuts as finely as possible in a food processor, being careful not to over-process into paste.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together until light.
  3. Add the ground hazelnuts, flour, and salt, mixing just until a smooth dough forms.
  4. Rest the dough, wrapped, for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll small portions of dough into balls, about 2cm across, using consistent sizing so pairs match evenly.
  6. Arrange on a lined baking sheet and bake at 160°C (325°F) for 12–15 minutes until just set but not browned.
  7. Cool completely on the tray, as the cookies are very delicate while warm.
  8. Spread a small amount of melted chocolate on the flat side of one cookie and press a matching cookie on top to sandwich.
  9. Let the chocolate set completely before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Grind the hazelnuts as finely as possible, as any large pieces will affect the cookies' delicate texture.
  • Keep the cookies small and uniform in size so they pair up neatly once sandwiched.
  • Let the baked cookies cool completely before handling — they are quite fragile while still warm.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-processing the hazelnuts into a paste, which changes the dough's texture.
  • Handling the warm cookies too soon, causing them to crumble.
  • Using too much chocolate filling, which can make the delicate cookies fall apart when bitten.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

Baci di dama are a beloved specialty of Piedmont, particularly the Alba and Tortona areas, prized for their delicate texture and the region's excellent local hazelnuts.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Espresso
