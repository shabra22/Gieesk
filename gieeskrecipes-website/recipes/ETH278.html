📅 Ethiopian Fasting Calendar Food

ጾም ቀን ምግብ — Tsom Qen Mgib

Ethiopian Fasting Calendar — the complete 200+ day fasting food tradition. The Ethiopian Orthodox calendar includes 55-day Lent, 40-day Advent, Apostles' Fast, Wednesday and Friday weekly fasts, and many others. The beyaynetu (ETH121), Shiro Firfir (ETH123), and all plant-based dishes are the food of this extraordinary commitment.
