🦐 Kenyan Luxury Seafood Breakfast

Kifungua Kinywa cha Samaki cha Kifahari

Kenyan Luxury Seafood Breakfast — butter garlic prawns, smoked fish, soft scrambled eggs with coriander, avocado slices, and a glass of freshly squeezed mango juice. The ultimate Mombasa beachside hotel breakfast — the Indian Ocean's gifts for the morning.

The luxury seafood breakfast at a Kenyan coastal hotel is one of the world's great breakfast experiences — fresh prawns sautéed at sunrise, smoked fish from the morning's catch, perfectly scrambled eggs with fresh coriander, and the warm Indian Ocean visible from the breakfast terrace.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan for prawns, Non-stick pan for eggs, Warm plates

  • 8 large prawns — peeled and deveined
  • 100g smoked fish — mackerel or snapper
  • 4 eggs — softly scrambled
  • 1 ripe avocado — sliced

Prawn Butter

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Scrambled Eggs

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp cream
  • Salt and white pepper

Accompaniments

  • 1 glass fresh mango juice
  • 2 slices sourdough — toasted
  • Lemon wedges and pili pili sauce

Method

  1. Make garlic butter: melt butter with garlic. Cook 1 minute.
  2. Add prawns. Cook 2—3 minutes until pink. Add coriander and lemon. Remove.
  3. Soft scramble eggs: melt butter in non-stick pan. Add beaten eggs, cream, salt, and pepper. Stir constantly over low heat until just set but still glossy. Remove from heat — the residual heat finishes them.
  4. Slice smoked fish.
  5. Slice avocado.
  6. Arrange everything on a warm plate.
  7. Serve with mango juice and toasted sourdough.

Chef Tips

  • Soft scrambled eggs are removed from heat slightly before they look done — residual heat finishes them.
  • Prawns take 3 minutes — watch them like a hawk.
  • Warm plates are essential for this breakfast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooked scrambled eggs — dry and rubbery.
  • Overcooked prawns.
  • Cold plates.

The Science

Soft scrambled eggs require the pan to be removed from heat before the eggs are fully set. The residual thermal energy in the pan and eggs continues to cook them for 20—30 seconds after removal. Stopping on the stove when "just right" produces overcooked eggs; stopping 20 seconds before produces perfect eggs.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Prepare and serve immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Fresh Mango Juice (KEN135), Sourdough toast, Pili Pili Sauce (KEN161)
