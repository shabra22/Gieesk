🥖 Egyptian Fino Bread (Soft Rolls)

Aish fino — عيش فينو

Soft finger-shaped rolls made from a milk-and-butter enriched dough, risen twice and baked 10 minutes at 400°F until golden.

4h 35mTotal time
25mPrep
10mCook
6Servings
240Calories
MediumDifficulty

Flour, warm milk, water, yeast, sugar, salt and oil are mixed and left aside 10 minutes, then kneaded 10 minutes until soft, a little sticky but smooth. Chilled butter is added and kneading continues 5 more minutes. After rising 1½ to 2 hours, the dough is divided into six, rolled to 6 by 9 inches, shaped into logs, risen again an hour and baked at 400°F (200°C) for 10 minutes or until golden.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl or stand mixer, Baking tray

  • 2 cup + 3 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup warm milk
  • ¼ cup warm water
  • 1 ½ teaspoon yeast
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoon oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter chilled

Method

  1. Mix the ingredients except the butter and leave aside for 10 minutes.
  2. Knead for 10 minutes until the dough is soft, a little sticky, but smooth. Add the butter and knead 5 more minutes.
  3. Let it rise for one and a half to two hours.
  4. Divide into 6 balls, roll each to 6 inches wide and 9 inches long, then into logs.
  5. Cover the logs and let them rise again for one hour.
  6. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and bake the rolls 10 minutes, or until golden.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: dough soft, a little sticky but smooth; rolls golden. Both rises are NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The author notes the fino roll is called Samoli in Saudi Arabia and Samoon in the UAE.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing the second rise, which makes dense rolls.

Cultural Note

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best the same day; keep in a bag 2 days.

Reheating: Warm briefly in an oven.

Serve with: Ful medames, Hawawshi
