🐟 Tanzanian Tamarind Fish

Samaki wa Ukwaju

Tanzanian Samaki wa Ukwaju — fish marinated in tamarind and spices, pan-seared golden, finished in a tamarind-onion sauce. The sweet-sour-spiced fish dish of the Swahili coast.

Tamarind and fish is one of the great Swahili coast flavour combinations — the tartness of tamarind perfectly counterpoints Indian Ocean fish. Found at every coastal food stall.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 800g fish fillets — snapper or kingfish

Marinade

  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt

Sauce

  • 2 onions — sliced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Marinate fish in tamarind, garlic, turmeric, cumin, salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Sear fish 3—4 minutes per side until golden. Remove.
  3. Cook onions in same pan until golden.
  4. Add tomatoes, tamarind, sugar. Cook 5 minutes.
  5. Add water. Return fish. Simmer gently 5—8 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Sear first — the crust is structural.
  • Tamarind is naturally salty.
  • Sugar balances tartness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the sear — fish breaks in sauce.
  • Over-souring.

The Science

The initial sear creates a protein crust that structurally reinforces the fish fillet — allowing it to survive sauce simmering without breaking apart.

Cultural Note

Samaki wa Ukwaju is the taste of the Swahili coast — the tamarind that arrived on Arab dhows paired with Indian Ocean fish that was always here.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Very gently.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Chapati (TZA004)
