🐠 Firire, Fried Fish with Onion Sauce and Salad

Firire

A whole fish slashed, rubbed with garlic, mustard, vinegar and chile, fried, and served beside its own fried onion sauce, a mustard-vinegar salad and plantain.

70 minTotal time
25 minPrep
30 minCook
4Servings
560Calories
EasyDifficulty

Firire is fried fish and onion sauce. The fish is slashed along the body and rubbed with a marinade of garlic, mustard, vinegar, chile powder, red pepper and chopped onion for 15 minutes; the onions are scraped off and set aside. The fish fries until browned, the marinated onions go into the same pan and fry until browned, stirring every two or three minutes, then the green pepper cooks about seven minutes to make the sauce. Lettuce and cucumber are dressed with vinegar, oil and mustard, and plantain is fried and salted. The fish sits in the middle of the salad, garnished with tomatoes and plantain, the sauce served alongside.

Ingredients

Equipment: Frying pan, Bowl for the marinade, Serving dish

  • 1 whole fish, cleaned — size and species not printed
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Chile powder — quantity not printed
  • Ground red pepper, to taste
  • 1 medium (250 g) onion, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Oil for frying — quantity not printed
  • 1/2 green pepper, sliced along its length

Salad and garnish

  • A dash of vinegar, oil and mustard for the dressing
  • Lettuce — quantity not printed
  • 1 medium cucumber, peeled and sliced
  • Plantain, peeled and sliced — quantity not printed
  • 2 medium cherry tomatoes (as printed)

Method

  1. Wash the fish very well and make slashes along the body.
  2. Combine the garlic, mustard, vinegar, chile powder, red pepper, onions and a pinch of salt. Rub on the fish and leave to marinate 15 minutes.
  3. Scrape the onions off the fish and reserve them.
  4. Heat the oil in a pan over moderate heat. Fry the fish on both sides until browned. Remove and set aside.
  5. Add the marinated onions to the pan and fry until browned, stirring every 2–3 minutes.
  6. Stir in the green pepper, reduce the heat to medium, and cook about 7 minutes. Set this sauce aside.
  7. Combine a dash of vinegar, oil and mustard for a dressing. Toss with the lettuce and cucumber.
  8. Fry the plantain and season with salt.
  9. Arrange the salad in a dish and place the fish in the centre. Garnish with the tomatoes and plantain. Serve with the reserved sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Scrape the marinade onions off before frying the fish — they burn in the first pan and become the sauce in the second.
  • ‘Fry until browned’ is the only fish cue; the page gives no time for the fish.
  • Bread works in place of plantain, and the page says it also pairs with mayonnaise.
  • Firire differs from yassa in structure: the fish is fried and the onion sauce is served alongside; in yassa the fish finishes in the onion sauce.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the onions on the fish in the first pan.
  • Skipping the vinegar-mustard dressing — it is what cuts the oil.

Reheating: Reheat the fish in a hot oven or pan; serve the salad fresh.
