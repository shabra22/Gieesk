🥬 Mahshi Koronb (Vegan Stuffed Cabbage Leaves)

Mahshi koronb — محشي كرنب

Blanched cabbage leaves rolled around a herb, tomato and rice filling, packed over onion, tomato and garlic and simmered in tomato broth.

3hTotal time
2hPrep
1hCook
6Servings
220Calories
HardDifficulty

Cabbage leaves are blanched about 2 to 3 minutes until soft and pliable. The onions sweat in a tomato sauce, cool, and are mixed with rice and herbs. The rolls, loose, with the sides not tucked in, go seam-down over sliced onion, tomato and garlic, are covered with broth and weighed down with a plate, and simmer on low 45 minutes to 1 hour until cooked through and the rice is tender; an oven option runs 375°F for 50 to 60 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid, Heat-resistant plate, Slotted spoon

  • 1 large head of cabbage or 2 small heads
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • ½ teaspoon Himalayan salt

Stuffing

  • 1 cup Egyptian rice, rinsed and drained
  • 3 onions, finely chopped or pureed
  • ½ cup passata
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon dry mint
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika, optional
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne, optional
  • 1 cup Mixed of parsley, dill and cilantro, finely chopped
  • salt & pepper, to taste

Broth

  • 2 cups vegetable broth, or any broth of your choice
  • ¼ cup Passata or 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon Vegeta powder
  • 1-2 tablespoons lime juice
  • salt & pepper, to taste

Pot

  • 1 medium tomato, sliced
  • 1 medium yellow or purple onion, sliced to 1/4" thick
  • 3 garlic cloves, slivered
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or canola oil

Method

  1. Blanch the cabbage leaves about 2 to 3 minutes, or until soft and pliable (the tips say 2 to 5 minutes, translucent and pliable). Trim the thick ribs.
  2. Sweat the onions 1 to 2 minutes, add the sauce ingredients and cook 7 to 10 minutes (the body says 5 to 7). Cool completely, then mix with the rice and herbs. The rice is not soaked.
  3. Roll the filling in the leaves loosely, without tucking in the sides.
  4. Layer the sliced onion, tomato and garlic in the pot with the oil, then pack in the rolls seam-down. Pour over the broth and set a heat-resistant plate on top.
  5. Bring to a boil on medium-high, then cook on low 45 minutes to 1 hour, until cooked through and the rice is tender. Or bake at 375°F for 50 to 60 minutes.
  6. Invert onto a plate and serve.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: leaves soft and pliable; rice tender. Do not tuck in the sides because the rice swells.
  • The page's own typos ('Mohshi', 'heart resistant dish') were read as intended; check the live page. The page says the liquid should be topped up if it runs low.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Packing the rolls tightly or overfilling them.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour's page is a tribute to her late grandmother, Teta Aida.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm gently in the pot with a splash of broth.

Serve with: Tahini sauce, Pickles, Salata sharqi
