🥞 Atayef (Ramadan Pancakes in Syrup)

Qatayef — قطايف

Small yeasted pancakes cooked on one side, folded around nuts or cream, then fried and dipped in orange-blossom syrup.

1h 30mTotal time
20mPrep
40mCook
10Servings
290Calories
MediumDifficulty

A batter of flour, semolina, yeast, baking powder and mahlab rests 30 minutes and is ladled onto a hot pan, cooked on one side only until the surface is covered in dry bubbles and the back is golden. The pancakes are filled with crushed nuts and sugar, clotted cream or cheese, sealed, fried at 350 to 360°F (175 to 182°C) until golden brown and dipped in a clove-cardamom syrup.

Ingredients

Equipment: Non-stick pan, Deep frying pan, Saucepan, Thermometer

    Syrup

    • 1 cup cup white sugar
    • 1 cup cup water
    • 3 Whole cloves
    • 3 cardamom pods, bruised
    • 1 stick cinnamon
    • 1 teaspoon orange blossom or rose water

    Batter

    • 1 ¼ cup warm water plus 2-4 tablespoons
    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
    • ¼ cup fine semolina flour
    • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
    • ½ teaspoon instant yeast
    • ½ teaspoon baking powder
    • ¼ teaspoon teaspoon baking soda
    • ¼ teaspoon orange blossom water or rose water, optional
    • ¼ teaspoon mahlab

    Fillings

    • ½ cup crushed nuts + 1 tablespoon sugar + 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
    • ½ cup clotted Cream
    • ½ cup mozzarella or Akawi cheese

    Frying

    • 1 cup vegetable oil

    Method

    1. Syrup: simmer the syrup ingredients for 10 minutes, then turn off the heat.
    2. Whisk the batter ingredients and rest 30 minutes.
    3. Cook small pancakes on one side only; once bubbles appear on the surface lower the heat and remove when the face has dry bubbles and the back is golden.
    4. Fill with nuts, cream or cheese, fold and seal the edges.
    5. Fry at 350 to 360°F (175 to 182°C) until golden brown, then dip in the syrup.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: bubbles on the surface, dry; back golden; fried golden brown. The 30-minute batter rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The page says they freeze up to one month before frying.
    • Misprints kept: duplicated "cup" in the syrup lines.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Flipping the pancakes; they are cooked on one side only.

    Cultural Note

    Nermine Mansour calls atayef 'the crown jewel of Ramadan nights in Egypt'. She notes Egypt serves nut-filled fried atayef in syrup while the Levant favours Akawi cheese, and that her family made a savoury feta and black-olive version.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Page: freeze up to one month; defrost before frying.

    Reheating: Serve soon after dipping; re-crisp briefly in a hot oven.

    Serve with: Syrup, nuts (included)
