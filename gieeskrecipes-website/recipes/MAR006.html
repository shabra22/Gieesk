🫘 Hergma (Trotters with Chickpeas and Wheat)

El hergma — kouraine, calf or lamb trotters with wheat berries and chickpeas

Calves' or lamb trotters cooked for hours with chickpeas, wheat berries and raisins until the sauce is thick and the meat falls from the bone. A hearty Eid dish.

Hergma is a trotter stew with chickpeas and a topping of wheat berries tied in cheesecloth, cooked slowly (or in a pressure cooker) until the liquid reduces to a very thick sauce. Benlafquih's version soaks chickpeas and wheat overnight, cooks the trotters under medium pressure for 1 1/2 hours (or simmers 3), then adds wheat and cooks 2 1/2 hours more under pressure (or 5 simmering). Nada Kiffa's version on the same page is a leaner pressure-cooker pot with 3 lbs of trotters and 6 oz of chickpeas.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker or large heavy pot, Cheesecloth for the wheat, Large bowl for soaking

    Benlafquih's version

    • 2 calves' feet, cut into several pieces by the butcher (or 8 lamb feet, cut in half)
    • 3/4 cup dried chickpeas
    • 1 1/2 cups wheat berries (wheat kernels)
    • 1/4 cup dark golden raisins
    • 2 large onions, chopped
    • 1 large onion, sliced
    • 6 to 8 cloves garlic, finely chopped
    • 2 or 3 small pieces of cinnamon stick or bark
    • 4 tsp salt
    • 3 tsp ground ginger
    • 2 tsp black pepper
    • 1 1/2 tsp paprika
    • 1 tsp saffron threads, crumbled
    • 1 tsp turmeric
    • 1 tsp smen, or to taste
    • 1/2 cup olive oil

    Method

    1. Soak the chickpeas and wheat berries overnight, or for at least 8 hours.
    2. Put the trotters in the pot with the onions, garlic, spices, smen, oil and water to cover. Cover and cook the trotters with medium pressure for 1 1/2 hours, or simmer over medium-low heat for 3 hours.
    3. Add the drained chickpeas and raisins; tie the wheat in cheesecloth and add it. Cover and cook with medium pressure for 2 1/2 hours, or simmer for 5 hours, until the wheat is tender.
    4. Continue simmering without pressure and uncovered until the liquids have reduced to a very thick sauce.
    5. Serve the trotters with the wheat and chickpeas and the thick sauce.

    Chef Tips

    • Taste a wheat kernel to check it is soft before the final reduction.
    • Moroccans always prefer the flavor and texture of dried chickpeas over canned, says the source.
    • Nada Kiffa's variant on the page: cook about 1 hour in a pressure cooker, then 30 minutes more or until the chickpeas are tender and the trotters are easily falling off the bones.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Under-cooking the wheat. It needs the full time.
    • Stopping before the sauce is very thick.
    • Skipping the butcher: ask them to cut the feet into pieces and clean them.

    Health Benefits

    • Chickpeas and wheat add fibre and plant protein.
    • Trotters give collagen-rich gelatin; the dish is rich, so serve modest portions.

    Cultural Note

    Taste of Maroc calls hergma a national favourite and street food, eaten for breakfast in some Marrakesh eateries, and made at Eid when trotters are available. The page calls it a cousin of the Sephardic Shabbat dish dafina/skhina and notes it is sometimes cooked in ashes in a tangia in Marrakesh.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge; the wheat thickens, so loosen with water on reheating.

    Reheating: Reheat covered with a splash of water, stirring gently.

    Serve with: Khobz
