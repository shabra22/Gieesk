🍢 Lamb or Beef Brochettes (Qotban / Kebab)

Qodban / kebab / sfafed — skewered meat cubes (قضبان)

One-inch cubes of lamb leg or beef alternated with suet on skewers, marinated in onion, parsley and olive oil, and grilled over ash-covered coals; served with a cumin-salt-cayenne mix.

2h 50mTotal time
30mPrep
20mCook

MediumDifficulty

Meat is cut into 1-inch cubes with suet, marinated at least 2 and up to 8 hours with onion, parsley, pepper, salt and olive oil, then threaded about 6 cubes to a skewer with fat every few pieces, starting and ending with meat. The skewers go over coals covered with a grey-silver ash and are turned once. The meat should be well coloured but still juicy by Moroccan standards.

Ingredients

Equipment: Metal skewers, Charcoal grill

  • 2.2 lbs boneless leg of lamb (or filet of beef), trimmed and cut into 1 inch cubes
  • 8 oz beef or lamb fat, preferably suet (kidney fat) cut into 1 inch cubes
  • 2 medium yellow or white onions, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup flat leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt or to taste
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Serving mix

  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cayenne or to taste

Garnish

  • 1/2 cup flat leaf parsley, leaves only finely chopped
  • 1 medium-size onion, finely chopped

Method

  1. Toss the meat, fat, onions, parsley, pepper, salt and oil. Cover and marinate at least 2 hours and up to 8.
  2. Thread around 6 cubes of meat onto each skewer, alternating every few pieces with a cube of fat. Start and end with meat.
  3. Start grilling when the charcoal is covered with a layer of grey-silver ash.
  4. Cook a few minutes, then turn to cook the other side; the page's relayed total is about 20 minutes.
  5. Serve with the cumin-salt-cayenne mix, parsley and onion. You can keep the fat nuggets, but it is better to discard them as they have done their job of keeping the meat moist.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: well coloured but still juicy, by Moroccan standards; some like it well done with burnt edges, some medium.
  • The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (2 to 8 hours).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting to grill over flames instead of ash-covered coals.
  • Beginning or ending a skewer with fat, which burns.

Health Benefits

Cultural Note

The author explains that in Morocco 'kebab' means skewered meat cubes rather than ground meat, and notes a charcoal brazier is called a kanoun or mejmar.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten right off the grill.

Reheating: Reheat briefly in a hot pan.

Serve with: Bread, Salad
