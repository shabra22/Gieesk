🍤 Bouchées de plantains aux crevettes (Plantain and Prawn Rolls)

Plantain and prawn rolls

Raw peeled prawns wrapped in thin lengthwise strips of plantain, secured with toothpicks and fried until golden and cooked through, served with a chilled tomato, spring-onion and lemon sauce.

An appetiser from the International Cuisine site, labelled Cameroon. Tomatoes, spring onions, yellow bell pepper, lemon juice, ginger, salt and pepper are mixed and chilled. The prawns are salted. The plantains are sliced lengthwise into long thin strips and each prawn wrapped and secured with a toothpick, then fried until golden brown all over and cooked through. Drained and served with the chilled sauce. The author notes the plantain wraps tended to break and that the result ‘did not end up as rolls’.

Ingredients

Equipment: Frying pan, Toothpicks

  • 1 lb raw peeled prawns
  • 2 plantains
  • 3 tomatoes, finely diced (printed ‘3 to matoes’)
  • 1 bunch of spring onions, finely chopped
  • 1 small yellow bell pepper, de-seeded and finely diced
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon freshly grated ginger
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the tomatoes, spring onions, bell pepper, lemon juice, ginger, salt and pepper and chill.
  2. Salt the prawns.
  3. Slice the plantains lengthwise into long, thin strips.
  4. Wrap each prawn in a strip and secure with a toothpick.
  5. Fry until golden brown all over and cooked through. Drain and serve with the chilled sauce.

Chef Tips

  • The printed doneness cue is ‘golden brown all over and cooked through’.
  • The author found the plantain wraps tended to break.
  • The ripeness of the plantain is not printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cutting the plantain strips too thick or thin.
  • Frying in oil that is not hot enough.
  • Overcooking the prawns.
