🥬 Amarante à la queue de bœuf (Folong or Endeng with Oxtail)

Folong / endeng

Steamed amaranth leaves, pressure-cooked oxtail with aromatics, and a tomato stew reduced uncovered — the dish the page says is known as endeng or folong in Central Africa.

Afrik’s recipe for amaranth with oxtail: the leaves are steamed in a couscoussier, the oxtail is pressure-cooked with the aromatics, the leaves are chopped, stewed with tomato, and the sauce reduced uncovered. The page does not give times. The leaves are known as endeng or folong in Central Africa, with Gabon, Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea named.

Ingredients

Equipment: Couscoussier or steamer, Pressure cooker, Pot

  • 1 kg amaranth leaves, in bunches
  • 1 kg oxtail
  • 2 large onions
  • 1 large tomato or 2 ordinary tomatoes
  • 1 large piece of ginger
  • 2 stalks celery
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • Pepper and salt — amounts not printed
  • 2 beef stock cubes
  • 1/2 glass oil
  • 1 large glass of water

Method

  1. Steam the amaranth leaves in a couscoussier.
  2. Pressure-cook the oxtail with the aromatics until tender.
  3. Chop the steamed leaves.
  4. Stew the leaves with the tomato, adding the oxtail.
  5. Reduce the sauce uncovered.

Chef Tips

  • The only printed cues are ‘une fois tendre’ for the oxtail and a reduced sauce.
  • No times are printed — judge by tenderness.
  • Wash the leaves well before steaming.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the sauce thin instead of reducing it.
  • Under-cooking the oxtail.
  • Over-steaming the leaves.
