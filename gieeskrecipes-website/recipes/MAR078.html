🌙 Kaab el Ghazal (Gazelle Horns, Almond Crescents)

Kaab el ghazal / kaab lghzal — gazelle's ankle (كعب الغزال)

Thin pastry wrapped around a fragrant almond paste and shaped into crescents, baked just until very light golden — not brown.

3h 10mTotal time
1h 30mPrep
10mCook
20Servings
170Calories
HardDifficulty

Blanched almonds are ground with sugar, melted butter, orange flower water, cinnamon and optional gum arabic into a paste that is rolled into small rods. A dough of flour, butter, orange flower water and warm water is rested half an hour, rolled into a long strip, wrapped around the rods, shaped into crescents, rested at least an hour, pricked three times and baked 10 minutes until very light golden, not brown. The page prints no oven temperature.

Ingredients

Equipment: Food processor, Rolling pin, Baking sheet, Knife, Skewer or fork for pricking

    Almond paste

    • 500g almonds.
    • 250g granulated sugar.
    • 60g butter, melted.
    • 1/4 cup orange flower water.
    • 1/2 tsp Arabic gum (optional).
    • 1/2 tsp cinnamon.

    Pastry

    • 500g flour.
    • 80g butter, melted.
    • 1/2 cup orange flower water.
    • A pinch of salt.
    • 1/2 cup warm water.

    Method

    1. Boil the almonds 1 to 2 minutes, drain and peel them, then grind them with the sugar, butter, orange flower water, cinnamon and gum arabic into a paste. Roll the paste into small rods.
    2. Mix the flour, melted butter, orange flower water, salt and warm water into a dough. Divide into 5 or 6 balls, cover with plastic and rest half an hour.
    3. Roll each ball into a long strip and lay a rod of almond paste along it, leaving about 5 cm between pieces.
    4. Fold the dough over the paste, pinch the ends firmly closed, cut the pieces apart, and gently shape each roll into a crescent.
    5. Rest the crescents at least an hour before baking.
    6. Prick three holes in the top of each and bake 10 minutes, until the colour is very light golden, not brown.

    Chef Tips

    • Cue: very light golden, not brown.
    • The page prints NO oven temperature; the Bake-Street version bakes at 170°C, but that is a different recipe.
    • The rest before baking is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least an hour).

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Browning them: the page insists they stay very light.
    • Leaving the pinched ends open so the paste leaks.

    Cultural Note

    The author describes it as an authentic Moroccan almond cookie named for its crescent shape and served at tea time and at every ceremony or special event.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Store airtight; the page gives no storage time that we relayed.

    Reheating: Serve at room temperature.

    Serve with: Mint tea
