🥕 Seven-Vegetable Tagine (Chickpea, Low-Sodium Adaptation)

Tajine bil khodra — vegetable tagine

Turnips, carrots, butternut squash, zucchini, green beans and chickpeas simmered in a cumin, paprika, ginger and turmeric broth and finished with tomatoes and herbs.

1h 10mTotal time

Turnips, carrots, onion and spices simmer in vegetable broth; the squash, zucchini, string beans and chickpeas follow, and everything cooks covered until tender. Plum tomato slices go on top for 5 minutes until just soft, then cilantro and parsley are added and the tagine sits covered for 10 minutes to let the flavours meld. This is a nutrition-institute adaptation using reduced-sodium broth.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large wide pot or tagine with lid

  • 2 white turnips, peeled and quartered
  • 1 cup sliced carrots, in 3/4-inch slices
  • 1 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground sweet paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups reduced-sodium vegetable broth, divided
  • 2 1/2 cups butternut squash, in 1-inch cubes
  • 1 cup chopped zucchini, in 3/4-inch pieces
  • 1/4 pound string beans, trimmed and cut in 1 1/2-inch lengths
  • 1 (15 ounce) can no salt added chickpeas, drained
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 3 plum tomatoes, cut crosswise into 3/4-inch slices
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/4 cup chopped flat-leaf (Italian) parsley

Method

  1. Combine the turnips, carrots, onion, spices and part of the broth in the pot, and bring to a simmer (the page's opening steps are summarised from the ingredient list).
  2. Cover and simmer over medium heat for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the squash, zucchini, string beans, chickpeas, salt and pepper with the remaining broth. Cover and cook until the vegetables are tender, 20 minutes.
  4. Arrange the tomato slices on top, cover, and cook until the tomatoes are just soft, 5 minutes.
  5. Add the cilantro and parsley and let the tagine sit, covered, for 10 minutes to let the flavours meld.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: vegetables tender; tomatoes just soft. The final 10-minute stand is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cutting the vegetables unevenly so some turn to mush.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently with a splash of broth.

Serve with: Couscous, Bread
