🍫 Kenyan Chocolate Mousse

Mousse ya Chokoleti

Kenyan Chocolate Mousse — dark chocolate and Kenyan espresso mousse, light and airy from whipped cream and egg whites, rich and complex from the chocolate and coffee. Served in individual glasses with passion fruit coulis.

Chocolate mousse is one of those desserts that appears effortless but requires precision — the right chocolate temperature, the right fold, the right refrigeration time. Made with Kenyan espresso-enhanced dark chocolate and topped with passion fruit coulis, it is the complete hotel restaurant dessert experience.

Ingredients

Equipment: Electric beater, Large bowl, Serving glasses

  • 200g dark chocolate (70%) — melted and cooled to 40°C
  • 1 tsp strong Kenyan espresso
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 4 egg whites
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 200ml whipping cream

Passion fruit coulis

  • 3 passion fruits
  • 1 tbsp honey

Method

  1. Melt chocolate with espresso. Cool to 40°C.
  2. Whisk egg yolks into the cooled chocolate.
  3. Whip cream to soft peaks. Fold into chocolate.
  4. Whisk egg whites to stiff peaks with sugar.
  5. Fold egg whites into chocolate mixture in three additions — gently, maintaining volume.
  6. Spoon into glasses. Refrigerate 3 hours minimum.
  7. Make coulis: blend passion fruit pulp with honey. Strain.
  8. Spoon coulis over the set mousse before serving.

Chef Tips

  • The chocolate must be at 40°C before adding eggs — too hot scrambles them.
  • Fold, never stir — stirring deflates the mousse.
  • Egg whites in three additions maintains maximum volume.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Chocolate too hot — scrambled eggs in chocolate.
  • Stirring rather than folding.
  • Insufficient refrigeration — loose mousse.

The Science

Chocolate mousse is a three-phase foam stabilised by fat (from cream and chocolate). The egg white foam provides the air structure; the cream fat stabilises the air bubbles; the chocolate fat creates a matrix that firms during refrigeration. The espresso provides small amounts of caffeine that slightly inhibit fat crystallisation, keeping the mousse softer.

Cultural Note

Chocolate mousse with passion fruit coulis is one of Nairobi's most beloved restaurant desserts — the marriage of dark chocolate intensity and passion fruit's tropical brightness is as natural as Kenyan coffee and milk.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Passion fruit coulis, Thin chocolate wafer, Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249)
