🍚 Couscous de maïs (Nery massardi, Maize Flour Couscous)

Nery massardi

A little maize flour mixed with cold water into a ‘milk’, poured into boiling water and cooked about 15 minutes after the boil returns; more flour is stirred in and cooked about 10 minutes more.

p. 4 of the October 2023 recipe book. Three flour routes are given: simple, crushed unsoaked, and crushed and soaked (about 8 hours, dried about 2 hours, then ground). Water is boiled; a little flour is mixed with cold water into a ‘milk’ (the bran), poured into the boiling water with stirring, and cooked about 15 minutes after the boil returns. The maize flour is then added, stirred with a wooden pestle or spatula, and cooked about 10 minutes more. No quantities are printed.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Wooden spatula or pestle

  • Maize flour — quantity not printed
  • Water — quantity not printed
  • Cold water for the ‘milk’ — quantity not printed

Method

  1. Prepare the flour: clean and grind the grains and sieve; or moisten, remove the skins, winnow, crush and grind; or crush, soak about 8 hours, dry about 2 hours and grind.
  2. Boil water. Mix a little flour with cold water into a ‘milk’.
  3. Pour the ‘milk’ into the boiling water, stirring. Cook about 15 minutes after the boil returns.
  4. Add the maize flour, stirring with a wooden pestle or spatula to prevent lumps.
  5. Cook about 10 more minutes. Serve with a sauce.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: boiling water and stirring to prevent lumps.
  • The book gives no flour-to-water ratio.
  • Serve with a sauce, as the book pairs it throughout.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the flour without stirring.
  • Using too little water, which gives a hard mass.
  • Not waiting for the boil to return before timing.
