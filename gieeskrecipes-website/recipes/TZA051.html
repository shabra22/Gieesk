🍵 Tanzanian Goat Soup

Supu ya Mbuzi

Supu ya Mbuzi — Tanzanian goat soup. Goat bones and meat simmered for 2 hours with vegetables, ginger, and spices into a deeply rich, golden, nourishing broth. Tanzania's most restorative soup — believed to cure anything that ails you.

Supu ya Mbuzi is Tanzania's most restorative food — the goat soup that is given to new mothers, the sick, and those recovering from hardship. The goat bones produce a gelatinous, golden broth of extraordinary nourishment. Every traditional healer recommends it.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 800g goat bones and pieces
  • 3 litres water
  • 2 onions — halved
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp ginger — sliced
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Blanch goat pieces in boiling water 5 minutes. Drain and rinse.
  2. Place in pot with all vegetables, garlic, ginger, and spices.
  3. Cover with 3 litres cold water.
  4. Bring to a boil. Skim foam.
  5. Lowest simmer for 2 hours.
  6. Strain. Shred any meat. Return to broth.
  7. Garnish with coriander. Serve with lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Blanching produces a much clearer, cleaner broth.
  • Lowest possible simmer — never boil hard.
  • Lemon at the table is essential.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hard boiling — cloudy broth.
  • Skipping blanching.

The Science

See TZA034 for bone broth cooking science. Goat bones are particularly collagen-rich around the joints — producing an especially gelatinous, nourishing broth.

Health Benefits

  • Rich in collagen, gelatin, and bone minerals.
  • Deeply nourishing.
  • Anti-inflammatory from ginger and spices.

Cultural Note

Supu ya Mbuzi is Tanzania's healing food — the soup that new mothers receive after birth, that the sick are given when medicine has not worked, and that is believed to restore strength when all else has failed.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Bring to boil before serving.

Serve with: As a standalone soup, With chapati, With ugali
