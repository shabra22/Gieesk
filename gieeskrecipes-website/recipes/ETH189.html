🍗 Ethiopian Hot Pepper Chicken Wings

ዶሮ ክንፍ — Doro Kinf

Ethiopian Hot Berbere Chicken Wings — chicken wings marinated in berbere, kibbeh, garlic, and lemon for 2 hours, then grilled or fried until the berbere caramelises magnificently. The Ethiopian version of hot wings — fiercer, more complex, and completely its own.

Ethiopian berbere chicken wings are what happens when the American wing format meets Ethiopian berbere — the result is a wing of extraordinary complexity, where 15+ spice compounds caramelise on the surface during grilling.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl for marinating, Grill or oven at 200°C

  • 1 kg chicken wings — separated at joints
  • 3 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp mitmita
  • 1.5 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix berbere, kibbeh, garlic, lemon, mitmita, and salt into a paste.
  2. Coat wings thoroughly. Marinate 2 hours minimum.
  3. Grill over medium charcoal: 12—15 minutes per side.
  4. Or oven: 200°C for 25—30 minutes, turning once.
  5. Serve with silsi (ETH146) for dipping.

Chef Tips

  • The berbere caramelises magnificently on the skin.
  • The 2-hour marination is the minimum — overnight is better.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Short marination — berbere doesn't penetrate.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian berbere wings are the modern Ethiopian bar and restaurant scene's most popular snack — the intense berbere heat combined with the fat of the chicken skin makes them one of the most satisfying snacks in the Ethiopian food repertoire.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately. Marinate up to 24 hours.

Reheating: Oven at 200°C.

Serve with: With silsi (ETH146), With awaze (ETH144), At Ethiopian bar culture
