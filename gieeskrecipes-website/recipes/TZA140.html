🍖 Tanzanian Lamb Ribs Zanzibar Style

Mbavu za Kondoo za Zanzibar

Zanzibar Lamb Ribs — lamb ribs marinated in a Zanzibar spice paste of cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, and tamarind, slow-roasted over charcoal until the meat is falling off the bone and the exterior is magnificently caramelised. Zanzibar grilling at its most spectacular.

Zanzibar lamb ribs are the celebration grill — the lamb ribs marinated overnight in the island's spice paste and slow-roasted over charcoal. The cloves and cinnamon caramelise on the exterior while the meat becomes falling-tender within.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill, Basting brush

  • 1 rack lamb ribs — separated into individual ribs

Zanzibar Spice Paste

  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Blend all spice paste ingredients to a smooth paste.
  2. Coat lamb ribs thoroughly. Marinate minimum 4 hours — overnight best.
  3. Prepare charcoal to medium coals.
  4. Grill over medium-low indirect heat 40—50 minutes, turning every 10—15 minutes.
  5. Move to direct heat last 10 minutes for the caramelised exterior.
  6. Rest 8 minutes. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Low and slow for the ribs.
  • Cloves caramelise beautifully over charcoal.
  • The tamarind in the paste aids caramelisation.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat — burns exterior before interior cooks.

Cultural Note

Zanzibar lamb ribs are the celebration grill of the island — when the spice paste caramelises over charcoal, the smell is the smell of every Eid celebration the island has ever had.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Briefly on grill.

Serve with: Kachumbari (TZA108), Wali wa Nazi (TZA002), Pili Pili (TZA117)
