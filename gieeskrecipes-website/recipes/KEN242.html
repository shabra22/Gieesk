🌿 Kenyan Baobab Smoothie

Smoothie ya Mbuyu

Kenyan Baobab Smoothie — tangy baobab powder blended with mango, banana, coconut milk, and honey. Africa's original superfood fruit in its most drinkable form — tart, fragrant, and extraordinarily nutritious.

Baobab — the fruit of the ancient tree of Africa — is one of the most nutritious foods on the planet. Its tangy, slightly sweet powder blends beautifully with mango and banana, softened by coconut milk. In Kenya's growing health café scene, baobab smoothies have become the superfood drink of choice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Glasses

  • 2 tbsp baobab powder
  • 1 large ripe mango — frozen chunks
  • 1 ripe banana — frozen
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Ice — 4 cubes

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
  2. Blend on high speed until completely smooth.
  3. Taste and adjust honey.
  4. Pour into glasses.
  5. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Frozen mango and banana give the best thick consistency.
  • Baobab powder varies in tartness — taste and adjust honey accordingly.
  • The coconut milk's fat carries the fat-soluble vitamins in the baobab.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-sweetening — baobab is quite tart.

The Science

Baobab contains both water-soluble (vitamin C) and fat-soluble (beta-carotene) nutrients. The coconut milk fat in this smoothie significantly enhances the absorption of the fat-soluble compounds compared to a water-based baobab drink.

Health Benefits

  • Baobab has one of the highest vitamin C contents of any food.
  • Rich in calcium, magnesium, and potassium.
  • Very high in dietary fibre — prebiotic.
  • Mango adds beta-carotene.

Cultural Note

The baobab smoothie represents the meeting of Kenya's ancient food heritage and modern health café culture — an ancient African superfood fruit now served in glass cups at Nairobi's hippest cafés.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Consume immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: As a standalone morning drink, With Kenyan Avocado Toast
