🥩 Tanzanian Beef Stir Fry

Nyama ya Kukaanga Kwa Haraka

Tanzanian Beef Stir Fry — tender beef strips stir-fried at maximum heat with mixed vegetables, garlic, ginger, and Tanzanian spices in a quickly made bold sauce. Fast, vibrant, and full of flavour — the quick dinner of urban Tanzania.

Beef stir fry is the dish that Dar es Salaam's quick-service restaurants do best — thin beef strips at screaming-hot heat, a few vegetables, and a bold quick sauce. In and out of the pan in 15 minutes but with flavour that takes much longer to build in a slow dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wok or large wide pan

  • 500g beef sirloin — thinly sliced
  • 1 red pepper — sliced
  • 1 green pepper — sliced
  • 1 carrot — julienned
  • 2 onions — sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat wok to smoking over highest heat.
  2. Add oil. Sear beef in two batches — 1.5—2 minutes. Remove.
  3. Stir-fry onions and garlic 1 minute.
  4. Add carrots and peppers. Stir-fry 2 minutes.
  5. Return beef. Add ginger, soy, oyster sauce, cumin, paprika.
  6. Toss everything together on high heat for 2 minutes.
  7. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Maximum heat throughout — this is a stir-fry.
  • Small batches for beef — crowding produces steaming.
  • Everything prepped before starting — it moves fast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Low heat.
  • Crowding the pan.
  • Not prepping everything before starting.

The Science

See TZA031 for stir-fry science. This version uses even higher heat (wok) and less sauce, producing a drier, more charred result called "wok hei" — the characteristic breath of the wok.

Cultural Note

Beef stir fry reflects Tanzania's openness to cooking techniques from the Indian Ocean trading world — Chinese cooking methods arrived on the East African coast centuries ago and have become completely naturalised.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly in a hot pan.

Serve with: Wali (TZA017), Ugali (TZA001), Chips
