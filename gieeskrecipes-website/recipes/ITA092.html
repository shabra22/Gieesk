Bistecca alla Fiorentina

A thick-cut T-bone steak from Tuscany's prized Chianina cattle, grilled simply over high heat and seasoned only with salt, olive oil, and a touch of rosemary at the end.

True bistecca alla Fiorentina is defined by strict tradition: a steak at least 4–5cm thick from Chianina beef, seared quickly over very high heat to a rare or medium-rare finish, and served whole at the table before being carved — anything more elaborate is considered unnecessary interference with excellent meat.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal or very hot grill, Tongs, Meat thermometer (optional)

  • 1 T-bone or porterhouse steak, 800g–1kg (1.75–2.2 lb), at least 4cm thick
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Method

  1. Remove the steak from the refrigerator at least 1 hour before cooking to come to room temperature.
  2. Pat the steak thoroughly dry and season generously with coarse salt just before grilling.
  3. Prepare a very hot charcoal grill or heat a heavy grill pan until smoking hot.
  4. Sear the steak for 4–5 minutes per side without moving it, to develop a deep crust.
  5. Stand the steak on its bone edge for a couple of minutes to render the fat along the bone.
  6. Check for doneness — traditionally served rare to medium-rare, with an internal temperature around 48–52°C (118–125°F) for rare.
  7. Rub the hot steak with the crushed garlic and rosemary sprig, and drizzle with olive oil.
  8. Rest for 5–10 minutes before carving the meat off the bone and slicing against the grain.
  9. Season with a little more salt and pepper, and serve with lemon wedges.

Chef Tips

  • The thickness of the cut matters enormously — a thin steak cannot achieve the same deep sear without overcooking the interior.
  • Resist the urge to move the steak while searing; leaving it undisturbed is what builds a proper crust.
  • Resting the meat after cooking is essential to let the juices redistribute before carving.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking a steak that's too thin for this style, resulting in an overcooked interior before a good crust develops.
  • Skipping the resting period, causing the juices to run out onto the board when carved.
  • Over-seasoning with marinades or sauces, which goes against the dish's whole ethos of showcasing excellent beef simply.

Cultural Note

Bistecca alla Fiorentina is fiercely protected by Tuscan tradition, with strict expectations around the cut, thickness, breed of cattle, and simple preparation that locals consider non-negotiable.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately; leftover steak keeps refrigerated for up to 2 days.

Reheating: Best served cold or at room temperature if leftover, sliced for a salad; reheating tends to overcook it further.

Serve with: White cannellini beans dressed in olive oil, A robust Chianti wine
