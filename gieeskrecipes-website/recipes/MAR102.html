🐟 Fish Chraime (Spicy Fish in Tomato and Paprika Sauce)

Chraime / hot fish — Sephardic Shabbat dish

White fish poached in a sauce of onion, garlic, tomato paste, diced tomatoes, paprika and cayenne, finished with parsley.

45mTotal time
15mPrep
30mCook
4Servings
260Calories
EasyDifficulty

Onion and garlic are sautéed in olive oil on medium heat until softened, about 3 to 4 minutes; tomato paste, tomatoes, water and the spices make the sauce, which cooks 10 minutes on low with the lid on. The fish is slid in, the pot covered and left to cook for 10 more minutes. The author says that this is not a recipe she has eaten in Morocco; she presents it as a Sephardic Shabbat dish.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan with lid

  • 1 lb white fish, like cod or halibut
  • 1/2 of a white onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp garlic, minced
  • 1/2 jalapeno or other spicy pepper (optional)
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2-3 large tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp paprika
  • 1/2 Tbsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 palmful parsley to garnish

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic (and pepper, if using) in the oil on medium heat until softened, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato paste, tomatoes, water and spices and bring to a simmer (our summary of the ingredient list).
  3. Continue cooking the sauce on low heat with the cover on for 10 minutes.
  4. Slide the fish gently into the sauce, cover the pot and leave it alone to cook for 10 more minutes.
  5. Garnish with parsley.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: onion softened. The fish cooking (10 minutes covered) is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • Cut back the cayenne if you like less heat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stirring after the fish is in, which breaks it up.
  • Overcooking thin fillets.

Health Benefits

  • Lean white fish in a vegetable sauce; the cayenne adds heat without calories.

Cultural Note

The author writes that, like dafina, fish chraime is prepared for Shabbat by Sephardic Jews, and adds that she has not eaten this version in Morocco.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate and eat within 2 days.

Reheating: Warm gently.

Serve with: Bread
