🥑 Avocado–Mango Salad

Saladu awooka àk mango

Cubed ripe mango and chunks of avocado tossed in a parsley, lime, orange and jalapeño dressing with peanut oil, chilled about an hour and topped with orange segments and shredded coconut.

75 minTotal time
15 minPrep
5Servings
260Calories
EasyDifficulty

A cold salad printed in the SAVEUR Senegal feature of May 2012 and published online on 20 August 2014. Six tablespoons of the half cup of parsley are whisked with peanut or canola oil, lime and orange juice, a minced jalapeño, salt and pepper; the mangoes and avocados are tossed in gently; the bowl is covered and refrigerated about 1 hour. The salad is transferred to a serving bowl, topped with halved orange segments, the remaining parsley and the optional coconut, and served chilled.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl, Whisk, Serving bowl

  • 1/2 cup finely chopped parsley (6 tablespoons for the dressing, the rest to finish)
  • 1/4 cup peanut or canola oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice
  • 1 jalapeño, stemmed, seeded and minced
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste — quantities not printed
  • 2 ripe mangoes, peeled, pitted and cut into 1/4-inch cubes
  • 2 ripe avocados, pitted, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 small navel orange, peeled and cut into segments
  • 2 teaspoons unsweetened shredded coconut (optional)

Method

  1. In a large bowl whisk 6 tablespoons of the parsley with the oil, lime juice, orange juice, jalapeño, salt and pepper.
  2. Add the mangoes and avocados and toss gently. Cover and refrigerate about 1 hour.
  3. Transfer to a serving bowl. Halve the orange segments crosswise and arrange over the top. Sprinkle with the remaining parsley and the coconut, if using. Serve chilled.

Chef Tips

  • The one timing is ‘about 1 hour’ of chilling; the page gives no ripeness test beyond ‘ripe’.
  • Toss gently so the avocado holds its chunks.
  • Cut the mango into smaller cubes (1/4 inch) than the avocado (1 inch), as printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using under-ripe fruit.
  • Chilling much longer than an hour with the lime on the avocado.
  • Leaving seeds in the jalapeño.

Cultural Note

The local name is printed ‘Saladu Awooka àk Mango’ on the recipe page and ‘Saladu Awooka ak Mango’ in the gallery index — a spelling discrepancy recorded, not resolved. The page names no region.

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed; eat the day it is made.

Reheating: Served cold.

Serve with: Grilled fish, Fried fish, Rice
