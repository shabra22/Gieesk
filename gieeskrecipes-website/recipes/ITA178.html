🍖 Zampone Modena con Lenticchie

Zampone Modena con Lenticchie

A spiced pork sausage traditionally stuffed into a pig's trotter, slow-simmered until tender and sliced thick, served alongside braised lentils as a New Year's Eve tradition symbolizing prosperity.

Zampone's distinctive casing — the skin of the pig's trotter — gives the sausage inside a particularly rich, gelatinous texture once slowly simmered, and its pairing with lentils, whose coin-like shape symbolizes wealth, has made it a fixture of Italian New Year celebrations.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot for the zampone, Separate pot for lentils

  • 1 whole zampone (about 1kg/2.2lb), pre-cooked variety available from good butchers
  • 300 g (1½ cups) dried lentils (Castelluccio if available)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 60 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Follow the package instructions for the zampone, typically involving pricking the skin all over and simmering gently in water for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Meanwhile, rinse the lentils (no soaking needed for most varieties).
  3. Warm the olive oil in a separate pot and sauté the onion, carrot, and celery until softened, then add the garlic.
  4. Add the lentils, crushed tomatoes, bay leaf, and enough water to cover generously.
  5. Simmer gently for 30–40 minutes until the lentils are tender, adding more water if needed.
  6. Season the lentils to taste.
  7. Once the zampone is tender, remove and let rest briefly, then slice thickly.
  8. Serve the sliced zampone alongside a generous portion of the lentils.

Chef Tips

  • Most zampone sold today comes pre-cooked and vacuum-sealed, significantly simplifying preparation — always follow the specific package instructions.
  • Pricking the skin before simmering helps prevent it from bursting during cooking.
  • Castelluccio lentils, if available, hold their shape particularly well and are the traditional choice.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the zampone until the skin bursts, which can be avoided by pricking it beforehand.
  • Overcooking the lentils until they turn to mush, rather than retaining a slight bite.
  • Skipping the traditional lentil pairing, which carries genuine symbolic importance for New Year celebrations.

Cultural Note

Zampone con lenticchie is a beloved New Year's Eve tradition throughout Italy, the lentils' coin-like shape symbolizing hopes for prosperity in the year ahead.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate cooked zampone and lentils separately for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop or in a covered dish in the oven.

Serve with: Mashed potatoes, A robust red wine from Emilia-Romagna
