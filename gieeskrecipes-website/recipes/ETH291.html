🥪 Ethiopian Café Sandwich

ካፍቴሪያ ሳንድዊች — Cafeteria Sandwich

Ethiopian Café Sandwich — roasted chicken or berbere beef in toasted bread with berbere mayo, ayib, silsi, shredded cabbage, and jalapeños. The Ethiopian café's signature lunch sandwich — completely Ethiopian in flavour despite the globally familiar format.

The Ethiopian café sandwich is the modern urban lunch — the bread, the mayonnaise, and the sandwich format are international; the berbere mayo, the ayib, and the silsi make it unmistakably Ethiopian.

Ingredients

Equipment: Toaster or grill pan for bread

  • 2 sandwich rolls or baguette sections
  • 200g roasted chicken or berbere beef slices
  • 3 tbsp berbere mayo
  • 3 tbsp ayib
  • 2 tbsp silsi
  • Shredded cabbage
  • 2 jalapeños — sliced
  • Tomato slices

Method

  1. Toast rolls.
  2. Spread berbere mayo on both cut faces.
  3. Layer cabbage, chicken or beef, tomato, jalapeños.
  4. Add ayib in a spoonful.
  5. Spoon silsi over.
  6. Close sandwich. Press briefly.

Chef Tips

  • Toast the bread — warmth activates the berbere mayo.
  • Ayib inside the sandwich is the Ethiopian upgrade to cheese.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold bread.

The Science

Heat from toasted bread melts the ayib slightly and activates the volatile aromatics in the berbere mayo.

Cultural Note

The Ethiopian café sandwich is the lunch of Addis Ababa's professional class — the sandwich eaten at the desk, at the café counter, and in the car between meetings. Its Ethiopianness is expressed not in form but in flavour.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.
