🦐 Spaghetti al Cartoccio

Spaghetti al Cartoccio

Seafood spaghetti sealed and finished inside a parchment paper parcel, trapping all the aromatic steam so the dish arrives at the table still puffed, to be torn open tableside.

Cooking the pasta's final moments inside a sealed paper parcel concentrates all the briny seafood aroma that would otherwise escape into the kitchen, creating a genuinely theatrical moment when the parcel is torn open at the table.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large sauté pan, Parchment paper

  • 400 g (14 oz) spaghetti
  • 500 g (1.1 lb) mixed shellfish (mussels, clams, shrimp)
  • 200 g (7 oz) squid, sliced into rings
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 dried chili, crumbled
  • 150 ml (⅔ cup) dry white wine
  • 300 g (10.5 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Warm half the olive oil in a large pan and sauté the garlic and chili briefly.
  2. Add the mussels and clams with the wine, cover, and cook until they open, about 4-5 minutes; discard any that stay closed.
  3. Remove the shellfish, strain and reserve the cooking liquid.
  4. In the same pan, add the cherry tomatoes and cook briefly, then add the squid and shrimp, cooking for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until very al dente, about 2 minutes less than the package instructions.
  6. Drain the pasta and toss briefly with the seafood mixture and reserved cooking liquid.
  7. Divide the pasta and seafood among large sheets of parchment paper, folding and sealing each into a sealed parcel.
  8. Bake at 220C (425F) for 6-8 minutes until the parcels puff up.
  9. Serve immediately, tearing each parcel open at the table to release the aroma.

Chef Tips

  • Undercook the pasta slightly before sealing it in the parcel, as it continues cooking from the trapped steam in the oven.
  • Seal the parchment parcels well to trap the steam, which is what infuses the pasta with the seafood aroma.
  • This dish is best served immediately from the oven, torn open right at the table for the full sensory effect.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the pasta before sealing, resulting in mushy noodles by the time it finishes in the parcel.
  • Not sealing the parchment tightly enough, letting steam escape rather than infusing the dish.
  • Overcooking the seafood, particularly the delicate shrimp and squid, before assembling the parcels.

Cultural Note

Spaghetti al cartoccio is a signature coastal Campanian dish, its theatrical paper-parcel presentation a favorite for special dinners and celebrations along the Amalfi Coast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately; seafood pasta does not keep or reheat well.

Reheating: Not recommended; best enjoyed fresh from the oven.

Serve with: A chilled Italian white wine, Crusty bread
