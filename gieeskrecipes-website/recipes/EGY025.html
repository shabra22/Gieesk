🍤 Gambari Maklee (Egyptian Crispy Fried Shrimp)

Gambari maqli — جمبري مقلي

Shrimp marinated in a blended tomato, pepper, garlic and cumin paste, coated twice in seasoned flour and fried two minutes per side.

1h 27mTotal time
40mPrep
17mCook
4Servings
470Calories
MediumDifficulty

A smooth marinade of tomato, red pepper, garlic, spices, cilantro and a little vinegar coats the shrimp for 30 minutes, then they are drained and shaken in seasoned flour and corn flour. The card says to sprinkle with water and coat again. They are fried in small batches, 2 minutes per side or until golden, and served with tahini, rustic salad and pickles.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Deep frying pan, Sieve, Container with lid

  • 1 kg Shrimp, deveined, shelled, and tail off
  • 1 tomato, seeded and quartered
  • 1 red pepper, seeded and quartered
  • 5 garlic clove, whole
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2-1 teaspoon cayenne, optional
  • ½ cup cilantro leaves and stalks
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon white distilled vinegar

Coating

  • 2 ½ cup all purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoons corn flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin

Frying and serving

  • 2 cups vegetable oil
  • 1 cup tahini Sauce
  • 3 cups Egyptian rustic salad (salata Baladi)
  • ½ cup pickles

Method

  1. Defrost frozen shrimp overnight in the fridge and dry well.
  2. Blend the tomato, pepper, garlic, spices, cilantro, salt, pepper and vinegar to a smooth marinade. Toss with the shrimp and refrigerate 30 minutes, no more.
  3. Drain in a sieve, then shake the shrimp in the seasoned flour mixture in a covered container. The card says to sprinkle with a few drops of water and coat again.
  4. Heat the oil (the page text gives 350 to 375°F). Fry in small batches 2 minutes per side or until golden.
  5. Serve with tahini sauce, salad and pickles.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: marinade smooth; shrimp golden. The marinade rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The page gives no internal-temperature cue.
  • The page body says 15 to 30 minutes marinating while the card says 30 minutes 'no more'; it also lists 'tail off' but tells you to keep the tails on.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Marinating too long; the acid and salt toughen shrimp.
  • Crowding the pan.

Cultural Note

Nermine Mansour says it is sold in Cairo and Alexandria seafood restaurants in warm pita, and recalls eating it at Zaki Al Samak in Cairo.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Page: reheat leftovers 5 minutes in a toaster oven at 375 °F; freeze only if the shrimp was bought fresh.

Reheating: A toaster oven for 5 minutes at 375°F (page).

Serve with: Egyptian tahini sauce, Sayadiya rice
