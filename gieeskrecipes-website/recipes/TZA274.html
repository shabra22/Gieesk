🌾 Pare Irrigation Farmer's Lunch

Chakula cha Mkulima wa Mifereji — Wapare

Pare Farmer's Lunch — the midday meal of the Pare mountain irrigation farmers. Yam stew with beans, greens, and pumpkin — a complete, nutritious, entirely plant-based meal that fuels the physical work of mountain terrace farming.

The Pare people developed sophisticated irrigation systems (mifereji) on their mountain slopes long before European contact — channelling mountain water through carved channels to terraced fields. The farmers who maintained these systems needed a complete, nourishing midday meal. This simple yam, bean, and vegetable stew is that meal.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 400g yam — cubed
  • 1 cup cooked beans
  • 200g pumpkin — cubed
  • 2 cups spinach or greens
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 tomatoes — diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1.5 cups water

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden. Add tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes.
  2. Add yam and pumpkin. Add water. Cover and cook 15 minutes.
  3. Add cooked beans. Cook 8 more minutes.
  4. Add greens on top. Cover 3 minutes.
  5. Season with salt.

Chef Tips

  • Add greens last — they wilt in 3 minutes and lose colour if overcooked.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding greens too early.

Cultural Note

The Pare mountain farmer's lunch is the food of one of Tanzania's most innovative agricultural traditions — the people who carved water channels into mountain rock to irrigate their terraced fields. The meal is as practical and ingenious as the irrigation system itself.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.
