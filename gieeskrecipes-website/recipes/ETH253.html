🍳 Silte Dabo Firfir

ሲልጤ ዳቦ ፍርፍር — Silte Dabo Firfir

Silte Dabo Firfir — torn day-old Silte flatbread tossed with eggs, berbere, kibbeh, and onion. The Silte version of firfir — using their distinctive wheat flatbread rather than injera. The Silte Zone of central Ethiopia is known for its unique flatbread tradition.

The Silte are a Muslim people closely related to the Gurage, living in the Silte Zone south of Addis Ababa. Their flatbread tradition differs from both injera and standard kita — the Silte Dabo Firfir uses this bread torn and tossed with eggs and berbere.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 3 pieces day-old Silte flatbread or kita — torn
  • 3 eggs — beaten
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat kibbeh. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add berbere. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add torn bread. Toss to coat.
  4. Add beaten eggs. Scramble with the bread — 3—4 minutes.
  5. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Day-old bread holds together better than fresh.
  • The eggs are added at the end and scrambled through.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Fresh bread — too soft, becomes mushy.

Cultural Note

Silte Dabo Firfir is the breakfast that uses yesterday's bread — the Silte people's version of the universal Ethiopian firfir tradition, adapted to their specific flatbread culture.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.
