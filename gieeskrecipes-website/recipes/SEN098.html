🍖 Lamb Chops with Bissap Sauce and Riz Wolof

Côtelettes d’agneau au bissap / riz wolof

Lamb chops grilled 4 to 5 minutes a side, served with a sauce of shallot sweated in butter, infused bissap and veal stock until it coats the spoon, and a quantified riz wolof of tomato paste, peas and carrot.

Published 13 June 2020 on Aïssatou Mbaye’s Aistou Cuisine, this pairs grilled lamb chops with a hibiscus-based sauce and a full riz wolof. The sauce: a knob of butter melted over medium heat, one finely chopped shallot sweated 2 minutes without colouring, pepper added and cooked 2 more minutes, 15 cl of leftover infused bissap poured in to deglaze and reduce, a tablespoon of powdered veal stock diluted in 50 ml of water added, and the sauce thickened over medium heat a few minutes until it coats the spoon. The chops are grilled on a barbecue or very hot pan 4 to 5 minutes per side, or 15 minutes under the oven grill turning halfway. The rice is riz wolof with 500 g rice, 100 g peas, a carrot, 3 tablespoons tomato paste, 2 tablespoons oil, an onion, a pepper and a garlic clove. WHAT THE PAGE DOES NOT PRINT: the weight or number of lamb chops, any ingredient list for the hibiscus marinade, how the leftover bissap infusion was made, and the method for cooking the rice.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pan, Barbecue, very hot pan or oven grill

    Sauce

    • A knob of butter — quantity not printed
    • 1 shallot, finely chopped
    • Pepper — quantity not printed
    • 15 cl leftover infused bissap (the strength of the infusion is not printed)
    • 1 tablespoon powdered veal stock
    • 50 ml water
    • Salt: ‘a pinch if necessary’

    Riz wolof

    • 500 g rice
    • 100 g peas
    • 1 carrot
    • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
    • 2 tablespoons neutral oil (peanut or sunflower)
    • 1 onion
    • 1 pepper (red, green or yellow)
    • 1 clove garlic
    • Salt and pepper — quantities not printed

    Lamb

    • Lamb chops — quantity not printed
    • Hibiscus marinade — ingredients not printed

    Method

      Sauce

      1. Melt the knob of butter in a pan over medium heat. Sweat the finely chopped shallot 2 minutes without colouring. Add pepper and cook 2 more minutes.
      2. Deglaze with the 15 cl of infused bissap and reduce. Add the veal stock diluted in the 50 ml of water and salt if needed. Thicken over medium heat a few minutes, until the sauce coats the spoon.

      Lamb

      1. Marinate the chops in bissap (the page prints neither the marinade nor the quantity of lamb). Grill 4 to 5 minutes per side on a barbecue or very hot pan, or 15 minutes on the oven’s grill mode, turning halfway. No internal temperature or doneness cue is printed.

      Rice

      1. Cook the riz wolof with the ingredients listed. The page’s rice method is not recorded in the research notes.
      2. Serve the chops with the riz wolof and the bissap sauce.

      Chef Tips

      • Printed cues: the sauce should coat the spoon (« la sauce doit napper la cuillère ») and the shallot should be sweated « sans coloration ».
      • There is no doneness cue for the lamb, only times: 4 to 5 minutes per side, or 15 minutes under the grill.
      • The page notes bissap enters several Senegalese dishes for its sourness, and that the white variety is the one most used in sauces and broths.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Colouring the shallot.
      • Over-reducing the sauce past a coat.
      • Relying on the times alone for the lamb.

      Cultural Note

      ‘Riz wolof’ is the page’s name for the tomato rice; ‘thiéboudiene blanc’ is also used on the site. This is a contemporary Senegalese-French plate rather than a traditional one.
