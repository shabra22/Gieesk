🧅 Tfaya (Caramelised Onion and Raisin Topping)

Tfaya — onions and raisins for couscous (تفاية)

Sweet, jammy onions cooked down with raisins, cinnamon and ginger, spooned over chicken or lamb couscous.

55mTotal time
10mPrep
45mCook
5Servings
210Calories
EasyDifficulty

Tfaya is the sweet-savory topping that finishes many couscous dishes. This version softens thinly sliced onions in oil, lowers the heat, adds salt, turmeric, ginger and cinnamon, and cooks until very soft and lightly caramelised. After about 20 minutes the sugar and soaked raisins go in for another 10 to 15.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large non-stick pan

  • 8-10 medium onions sliced thinly
  • 1 cup raisins (yellow work best)
  • 1 cinnamon stick broke into several pieces
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 4 Tbsp vegetable or unflavored oil

Method

  1. Slice the onions. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat and add the onions.
  2. Soak the raisins in just enough warm water to cover.
  3. When the onions have started to soften, lower the heat and add the salt, turmeric, ginger and cinnamon. Cook on low until the onions are very soft and begin to caramelise slightly.
  4. After about 20 minutes, add the sugar and the drained raisins and cook another 10 to 15 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Yellow raisins work best, says the author.
  • Cue: onions very soft and slightly caramelised, not dark.
  • Use as a garnish for couscous, not as a side dish.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • High heat that browns the onions before they soften.
  • Adding sugar too early.
  • Forgetting to drain the raisins.

Health Benefits

  • Onions and raisins bring fibre and natural sweetness.
  • A small spoonful goes a long way.

Cultural Note

The author notes people sometimes call this 'Moroccan couscous with raisins'.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Warm in a small pan with a spoonful of water.

Serve with: Couscous
