🥬 Ethiopian Spiced Cabbage

ጥቅል ጎመን — Tikil Gomen

Ethiopian Tikil Gomen — cabbage cooked with carrots, potatoes, onion, turmeric, and cumin. A staple element of the Ethiopian celebration and fasting platter — simpler and milder than Gomen, providing a gentle, familiar vegetable note.

Tikil Gomen (literally 'spiced cabbage') is the most mild-mannered of Ethiopian vegetable dishes — the cabbage's natural sweetness, the carrots' beta-carotene, and the gentle spicing of turmeric and cumin make it a universally loved side dish. It appears on virtually every Ethiopian mesob.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • ½ head cabbage — roughly chopped
  • 2 medium potatoes — cubed
  • 1 carrot — sliced
  • 1 onion — diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup water

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic, turmeric, and cumin. Stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add potatoes and carrot. Stir. Add water.
  4. Cover. Cook 10 minutes.
  5. Add cabbage. Stir through.
  6. Cover. Cook 8 more minutes until everything is tender.
  7. Season.

Chef Tips

  • The cabbage wilts significantly — start with what seems like too much.
  • Add potatoes before cabbage — they take longer.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding cabbage at the same time as potatoes.

Health Benefits

  • Sulforaphane from cabbage.
  • Beta-carotene from carrots.
  • Very filling despite low calories.

Cultural Note

Tikil Gomen is the comfort vegetable of Ethiopian cooking — mild enough for everyone, familiar enough to welcome new guests, and present on virtually every Ethiopian table at every meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: On every Ethiopian mesob platter, With Doro Wat (ETH061), As a fasting side
