🥒 Koussa bel Bechamel (Zucchini Béchamel Casserole)

Kousa bil-bechamel — كوسة بالبشاميل

Sliced zucchini layered with a baharat beef sauce and a cream-cheese béchamel, baked and finished under the broiler.

2hTotal time
30mPrep
1h 10mCook
6Servings
430Calories
MediumDifficulty

Ground beef is cooked on medium-low until deep brown and dry, then simmered with onion, garlic, baharat, marinara, paste and a parmesan rind until thick. A roux is cooked about 5 minutes into a golden paste, warm milk and cream cheese are added, and zucchini, sauce and white sauce are layered and baked at 375°F for 30 to 40 minutes, then broiled for 5 minutes and rested 15 to 20.

Ingredients

Equipment: Baking dish, Skillet, Saucepan, Broiler

  • 1 kg Zucchini, preferable black beauty zucchini
  • meat Sauce, recipe follows
  • white sauce

Meat sauce

  • 600 grams ground beef, preferably grass fed
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon meat baharat
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • rind of parmesan cheese, 2 inches x 2 inches

White sauce

  • 2 tablespoons butter, 30 grams
  • 2 cups full fat milk
  • 2 tablespoons all purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg, freshly grated
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • ¼ cup grated mozzarella and/or cheddar cheese

Method

  1. Brown the beef on medium-low until it turns deep brown and absorbs all its liquid. Add the onion, garlic, baharat, marinara, water, paste and parmesan rind and simmer until it thickens; the body of the page says 20 to 30 minutes.
  2. White sauce: cook the butter and flour for about 5 minutes, stirring constantly, until it resembles a golden paste. Add warm milk, then the cream cheese and nutmeg.
  3. Slice the zucchini about ½ inch thick. Layer sauce, zucchini, sauce and white sauce, and top with cheese.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 30 to 40 minutes, until golden and the zucchini is soft, then broil for 5 minutes.
  5. Rest 15 to 20 minutes before cutting.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: meat deep brown and dry; roux a golden paste; casserole golden with zucchini soft. The rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • The card says butter 2 tablespoons (30 grams); the body says 2 tablespoons of 15 grams each (a small discrepancy on the page).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not cooking the beef dry, which makes the casserole watery.

Health Benefits

  • Zucchini adds fibre and vitamin C; beef adds iron and protein.

Cultural Note

The page presents it as an Egyptian family casserole in the béchamel tradition; no further cultural remarks were relayed.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat in a moderate oven.

Serve with: Salata baladi (tomato-cucumber salad)
