🍖 Mechoui: Slow-Roast Lamb Shoulder, Moroccan-Style

Mechoui — shoulder of lamb (مشوي)

A home-oven version of Morocco's festive roast: a whole lamb shoulder rubbed with spiced butter, roasted gently for five hours, then crisped hot and rested.

Mechoui is traditionally a whole lamb roasted on a spit or in a pit. Linda Duffin, a British food writer, scales it to a 2.5 kg shoulder in a home oven: rubbed with a spice butter, started six hours before eating at 150°C/300°F, covered and roasted for five hours, then returned uncovered at 220°C/425°F for 30 to 45 minutes until well browned. A 15 to 20 minute rest finishes it, and the author's cue is gently spiced and falling-off-the-bone tender.

Ingredients

Equipment: Roasting tin with lid or foil, Oven

  • 1 whole shoulder of lamb, about 2.5 kg
  • 3 tbsp butter, at room temperature
  • 1 heaped tspn cumin powder
  • 1 tspn coriander powder
  • 1/2 tspn fennel seeds, ground
  • 1/2 tspn cinnamon
  • 1/4 tspn cayenne
  • 1 tspn smoked sweet paprika
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1/4 tspn freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tspn salt
  • 150ml water
  • Seeds and juice of about 1/2 pomegranate
  • A handful of fresh coriander, roughly torn

Method

  1. Six hours before you want to eat, pre-heat the oven to 150C/300F/Gas Mark 2.
  2. Mix the butter with all the spices, garlic, pepper and salt and rub it all over the lamb.
  3. Put the lamb in a roasting tin with the water, cover, and roast for five hours.
  4. Increase the heat to 220C/425F/Gas Mark 7 and put the lamb back in, uncovered, for 30-45 minutes until well browned.
  5. Allow to rest for 15-20 minutes. Serve with pomegranate seeds and juice and torn coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue quoted: gently spiced and falling-off-the-bone tender.
  • This is a British writer's Moroccan-style adaptation; the traditional method is a whole animal in an earth oven.
  • Do not skip the rest; the juices settle.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting too late; the five hours are essential.
  • Skipping the final blast, which gives the crust.
  • Carving immediately.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Pull the meat and keep it 3 days in the fridge in its juices.

Reheating: Warm covered in a low oven with the juices.

Serve with: Khobz, Zaalouk
