🍚 Moroccan Rice Pudding (Rozz bil Halib)

Rozz bil halib — rice with milk (الأرز باللبن)

Rice simmered in water, then in milk with a cinnamon stick and lemon peel, finished with sugar, butter and orange blossom water.

50mTotal time
10mPrep
40mCook
4Servings
290Calories
EasyDifficulty

Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, cook it in water over medium heat for about 15 minutes until the water has gone, add the milk and cook another 15 minutes or so, adding more milk if the rice is not yet really soft. Sugar and butter go in at the end with orange blossom water and ground cinnamon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan

  • 1 cup rice (any size of cup)
  • 2 cups of water (same cup used for rice)
  • 2 cups of milk (may need 1 extra cup)
  • 1 small piece of lemon peel
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/3 cup of sugar (or to taste) - same cup used for the rice
  • 1 teaspoon of orange blossom water (or to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • Ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Wash the rice with water, changing it a couple of times until the water becomes clear.
  2. Cook the rice with the 2 cups of water, lemon peel and cinnamon stick over medium heat. When the water has evaporated, after around 15 minutes, add the milk.
  3. When the milk has evaporated, after another 15 minutes or so, taste the rice. It needs to be really soft; if not, add a little more milk and keep cooking.
  4. Add the sugar and butter, turn off the heat and finish with the orange blossom water. Serve with ground cinnamon on top.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: rice really soft; water, then milk, absorbed.
  • The page mentions needing up to 1 extra cup of milk.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not rinsing the rice until clear.
  • Adding the milk before the water has gone.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store covered in the fridge; the page gives no storage time that we relayed.

Reheating: Warm with a splash of milk or serve cold.

Serve with: Mint tea
