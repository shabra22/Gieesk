🍖 Tanjia with Cumin and Preserved Lemon (Oven Method)

Tanjia marrakchia — viande confite au citron et au cumin

A slow oven version of Marrakech's tanjia: beef shank or lamb shoulder with a whole head of garlic, cumin, ras el hanout and preserved lemon, cooked at 150°C for about 4½ to 5 hours.

This French-language recipe cooks the meat in a sealed pot in a 150°C oven for 4½ to 5 hours without opening it during the first 3 hours, with water only halfway up the meat. There are no onions or tomatoes. The sauce is short, intense and lemony; if too liquid, it is poured into a pan and reduced 5 to 10 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy lidded pot or clay tangia, Oven, Saucepan for reducing the sauce

  • 1,5 kg de jarret de bœuf ou d’épaule d’agneau, en gros morceaux avec os (1.5 kg beef shank or lamb shoulder, bone-in)
  • 1 tête d’ail (8 à 10 gousses), pelée et écrasée (1 head of garlic, 8-10 cloves, peeled and crushed)
  • 1 citron confit, écorce en lamelles (1 preserved lemon, rind in strips)
  • 2 c. à café de cumin moulu (2 tsp ground cumin)
  • 1 c. à café de ras el hanout (1 tsp)
  • 1 c. à café de gingembre en poudre (1 tsp ground ginger)
  • ½ c. à café de poivre noir (1/2 tsp black pepper)
  • 1 pincée de safran (1 pinch saffron)
  • 1 ½ c. à café de sel (1 1/2 tsp salt)
  • 1 c. à soupe de smen (facultatif) (1 tbsp smen, optional)
  • 60 ml d’huile d’olive (60 ml olive oil)
  • 250 ml d’eau (250 ml water)

Method

  1. Combine the meat with all the other ingredients except the water. Marinate 1 hour (or overnight in a cool place).
  2. Add the water: it should come only halfway up the meat, no more.
  3. Cover tightly and bake at 150°C for 4h30 to 5h, without opening during the first 3 hours.
  4. The meat should come off the bone with a spoon. If not, continue 30 minutes.
  5. If the sauce is too liquid, pour it into a saucepan and reduce 5 to 10 minutes, then return it to the meat.

Chef Tips

  • Cue from the source: the meat should come away from the bone with a spoon.
  • Slow cooker version in the source: 8 hours on low with 150 ml water; pressure cooker: about 1 h 15.
  • Keep the lid on; the seal is the point.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Opening the pot early and losing the steam.
  • Too much water, which makes a stew rather than a confit.
  • Skipping the reduction if the sauce is thin.

Cultural Note

The French source frames tanjia as a Marrakech dish and says the recipe uses neither onions nor tomatoes.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

Reheating: Reheat covered at low heat with a spoon of water.

Serve with: Khobz
