🥣 Fondé, Millet Porridge

Fondé / kharaw

Millet flour (kharaw) stirred into boiling water and simmered 20 to 30 minutes over low heat, cooled a few minutes, then sweetened and flavoured with vanilla sugar, nutmeg and milk or lait caillé.

Fondé is the thin millet porridge of Senegal, eaten at breakfast or as an evening meal. This version, published by the blogger ‘fabbydiaw’ (Les Saveurs de Bintou) on 24 January 2019 and updated 21 November 2020, boils water in a pot and adds the kharaw — millet flour ‘transformed by the women’ — gradually, then boils it 20 to 30 minutes over a low heat stirring from time to time. It is left a few minutes to cool before the sugar goes in (the sugar may also be added before taking the pot off the heat), followed by vanilla sugar, nutmeg and milk and/or lait caillé. The page says only that the fondé is ‘prêt’. THE PAGE PRINTS 500 g OF MILLET TO 1/2 L OF WATER. This ratio cannot make a pourable porridge; it is a suspected misprint and is reported as printed, not corrected, and the page gives no thickness cue, so the dish is not reproducible from this source without a judgement the page does not supply.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Wooden spoon

  • 500 g millet (the ingredient list says ‘mil’; the method says ‘kharaw’, millet flour) — see the misprint flag
  • 1/2 L water (printed ‘1\2L’)
  • Sugar — quantity not printed
  • Vanilla sugar — quantity not printed
  • Nutmeg — quantity not printed
  • Milk and/or lait caillé — quantity not printed (printed ‘Lait et\ou lait caillé’)

Method

  1. Bring the water to the boil in a pot. Add the kharaw (millet flour) little by little.
  2. Boil 20 to 30 minutes over a low heat, stirring from time to time.
  3. Let cool a few minutes, then add the sugar (it can also go in before the pot comes off the heat), the vanilla sugar, the nutmeg and the milk and/or lait caillé.
  4. The page says the fondé is then ready.

Chef Tips

  • The page prints NO thickness cue — only a time, 20 to 30 minutes at low heat.
  • The 500 g to 1/2 L ratio is reported exactly as printed and is almost certainly wrong; it is not corrected here.
  • Add the flour gradually to avoid lumps; the page says ‘peu à peu’.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Following the 500 g: 1/2 L ratio literally.
  • Adding the flour all at once.
  • Judging doneness by the clock alone.

Cultural Note

The author calls fondé ‘un plat typiquement africain, originaire du peuple bambara’, found across West Africa, ‘particulièrement au Mali et dans le sud du Sénégal’ — a Bambara-origin claim attributed to her, not a Wolof one. A separate report in the Dakar press says it is traditionally eaten by the Socé as dessert or breakfast and is now a popular evening meal in Dakar.

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed.

Reheating: No instructions are printed.

Serve with: Milk or lait caillé, Sugar and nutmeg
