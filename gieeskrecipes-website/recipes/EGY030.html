🐟 Samak Makli (Egyptian-Style Crispy Fried Fish Sandwich)

Samak maqli — سمك مقلي

Spiced cod or haddock fried in a flour-cornstarch dredge and packed into pita with tahini, tomato, cucumber, arugula and optional roasted eggplant.

1h 30mTotal time
30mPrep
30mCook

MediumDifficulty

Fish fillets are seasoned with coriander, cumin, paprika, Aleppo pepper, garlic and lemon juice for 10 to 20 minutes in the fridge, dredged in flour, cornstarch and baking soda, and fried in about 2 inches of oil, about 3 minutes on the first side until golden, then 3 to 4 minutes more until crispy. It is served in pita with tahini, vegetables and pickles; roasted eggplant rounds are optional.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep skillet, Baking sheet, Wire rack

    Eggplant (optional)

    • 1 large eggplant
    • Extra virgin olive oil
    • Kosher salt

    Fish

    • 11/2 pounds fish fillet, preferably cod or haddock, sliced into 4 to 6 portions
    • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
    • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
    • 1 teaspoon paprika
    • 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes
    • Kosher salt
    • Black pepper
    • 3 large garlic cloves, pressed or minced
    • 3 large lemons, halved
    • Oil, for frying (see note)

    Dredge

    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
    • 1/2 cup cornstarch
    • 1 teaspoon baking soda

    To serve

    • Tahini sauce
    • 1 large tomato
    • 2 to 3 Persian cucumbers
    • 2 to 3 cups baby arugula
    • Pita bread, halved to make 4 to 6 pita pockets
    • Olives and/or pickles

    Method

    1. Optional eggplant: salt the rounds and rest 30 minutes, then roast at 400°F for 20 minutes, flip and roast 10 to 15 minutes more until golden brown all over.
    2. Season the fish with the spices, garlic and the juice of 2 lemons and refrigerate about 10 to 20 minutes.
    3. Dredge in the flour, cornstarch and baking soda mix.
    4. Heat the oil about 2 inches deep on medium until it bubbles when a pinch of flour goes in. Fry about 3 minutes on the first side until golden brown, then 3 to 4 minutes more until crispy and golden.
    5. Squeeze lemon over the fish and serve in pita with tahini, vegetables and pickles.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: oil bubbles at a pinch of flour; fish golden and crispy. The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (10 to 20 minutes).
    • The page prints the yield as 6 to 4 servings; servings here follow our midpoint.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Frying in oil that is not hot enough.

    Cultural Note

    Suzy Karadsheh writes that it is inspired by a fried fish from her childhood, which her mother often made as whole fried fish.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten fresh.

    Reheating: Re-crisp in a hot oven.

    Serve with: Egyptian tahini sauce, Sayadiya rice
