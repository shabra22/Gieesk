🫓 Ethiopian Chicken Firfir

ዶሮ ፍርፍር — Doro Firfir

Ethiopian Doro Firfir — torn day-old injera tossed with Doro Wat sauce and shredded chicken, kibbeh, and berbere. The most celebrated firfir — made with yesterday's Doro Wat, it transforms the greatest Ethiopian dish into a brilliant next-day breakfast.

Doro Firfir is the morning after Doro Wat — the leftover berbere sauce and shredded chicken tossed with torn day-old injera and additional kibbeh produces a firfir of extraordinary depth. Ethiopian families look forward to Doro Firfir almost as much as Doro Wat itself.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 3 pieces day-old injera — torn into pieces
  • 1 cup Doro Wat sauce — warmed, with shredded chicken
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 small onion — diced
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Heat kibbeh in a wide pan.
  2. Cook onion until golden — 5 minutes.
  3. Add berbere. Stir 30 seconds.
  4. Add warmed Doro Wat sauce with its chicken.
  5. Add torn injera. Toss to coat every piece.
  6. Cook 3—4 minutes, tossing, until injera has absorbed the sauce.
  7. Season. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Day-old injera absorbs better than fresh.
  • The Doro Wat sauce must be warmed — cold sauce does not penetrate the injera.
  • Serve the instant it is done — Doro Firfir becomes heavy if it sits.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold sauce — does not penetrate.
  • Fresh injera — absorbs too fast, becomes mushy.

The Science

See ETH032 for firfir cooking science. Doro Firfir has a particularly complex flavour because the Doro Wat sauce already contains 25—30 minutes of Maillard-reaction onion compounds, berbere carotenoids, and chicken gelatin — all of which are reheated and further concentrated when tossed with the injera.

Cultural Note

Doro Firfir is proof that Ethiopian cooking produces no waste and creates no lesser dish by recycling — the morning-after firfir made from Doro Wat is celebrated in its own right, anticipated by the family almost as eagerly as the original feast.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Ayib (ETH096), A soft-boiled egg on top
