🍖 Tanzanian Oxtail Stew

Mchuzi wa Mkia wa Ng'ombe

Tanzanian Oxtail Stew — oxtail slow-cooked for 2.5 hours in a rich spiced tomato and onion sauce until the meat falls from the bone and the sauce is gloriously thick and gelatinous. A restaurant favourite and a special occasion dish that rewards patience with extraordinary richness.

Oxtail stew is Tanzania's most gelatinous, richest, and most patient meat dish. The 2.5-hour cook is non-negotiable — oxtail contains the most collagen of any beef cut, and only extended cooking converts it entirely to gelatin, producing the characteristic sticky, incredibly rich sauce.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large heavy pot with lid

  • 1 kg oxtail — jointed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown oxtail pieces well. Remove.
  2. Cook onions deeply golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, all spices, tomatoes. Cook 10 minutes until very thick.
  4. Return oxtail. Add water.
  5. Simmer covered on the lowest heat for 2—2.5 hours until meat falls from bone.
  6. The sauce will be very thick and glossy.
  7. Adjust seasoning. Scatter coriander.

Chef Tips

  • The longer the simmer the better — 3 hours is not too long.
  • Pressure cooker reduces time to 50—55 minutes.
  • The sauce should be so thick it coats the back of a spoon thickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking — tough, chewy oxtail.
  • High heat — toughens the meat.

The Science

Oxtail contains 40—50% collagen by dry weight — the highest of any beef cut. This makes it the richest source of gelatin when slow-cooked, producing the characteristically sticky, thick, naturally thickened sauce that cannot be replicated by any other cut.

Cultural Note

Oxtail stew is Tanzania's most patient dish — a food that rewards those who are willing to wait. At Tanzanian restaurants, it is the dish that the serious meat-eater orders when they want the best.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Chapati (TZA004), Rice
