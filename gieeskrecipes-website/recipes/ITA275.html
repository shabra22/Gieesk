🥩 Mocetta Valdostana

Mocetta

Valle d'Aosta's prized air-dried cured meat, traditionally made from chamois or ibex and now often beef, sliced paper-thin and dressed simply with olive oil, lemon, and mountain herbs.

Mocetta reflects Valle d'Aosta's Alpine hunting heritage, historically made from wild chamois to preserve meat through the harsh mountain winters, and its intensely savory, slightly gamey character remains a defining regional specialty.

Ingredients

Equipment: Serving platter

  • 150 g (5.3 oz) mocetta (or substitute bresaola), very thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 30 g (1/4 cup) shaved aged mountain cheese (such as Fontina)
  • Fresh thyme or mountain herbs
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Arrange the thinly sliced mocetta over a serving platter.
  2. Drizzle generously with olive oil and lemon juice.
  3. Scatter with shaved mountain cheese.
  4. Finish with fresh thyme and a generous crack of black pepper.
  5. Serve immediately at room temperature.

Chef Tips

  • Slice the mocetta as thinly as possible for the best texture, similar to bresaola.
  • A good, peppery olive oil and fresh lemon are essential to balance the meat intense, savory character.
  • If genuine mocetta is not available, bresaola is a reasonable substitute, though with a milder flavor.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Slicing too thick, which changes the delicate eating experience this dish depends on.
  • Skipping the lemon, which is essential for brightening the rich, savory meat.
  • Serving straight from the refrigerator, which mutes the flavor.

Cultural Note

Mocetta reflects Valle d'Aosta's Alpine hunting heritage, historically preserved from wild chamois or ibex to sustain families through the region's long, harsh mountain winters.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Whole mocetta keeps in a cool, dry place for months; sliced portions should be used within a week.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Rye bread, A robust red wine from Valle d'Aosta
