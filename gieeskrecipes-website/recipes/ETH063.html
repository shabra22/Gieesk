🍗 Ethiopian Chicken Tibs

ዶሮ ጥብስ — Doro Tibs

Ethiopian Doro Tibs — boneless chicken pieces sautéed fast and hot in kibbeh with onions, jalapeños, tomatoes, rosemary, and berbere. The chicken version of the beloved Ethiopian tibs — faster than Doro Wat, equally satisfying, and irresistibly fragrant.

Doro Tibs is the everyday chicken dish for when Doro Wat's 2.5 hours are not available — the same kibbeh, berbere, jalapeño, and rosemary combination that makes beef tibs great, applied to chicken pieces. Done in 20 minutes, it delivers maximum Ethiopian flavour in minimum time.

Ingredients

Equipment: Cast iron pan or heavy skillet

  • 600g boneless chicken — breast or thigh, cut into pieces
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 large onion — diced
  • 2 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 2 jalapeños — sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1.5 tsp berbere
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Pat chicken pieces dry.
  2. Heat kibbeh in a heavy pan until smoking hot.
  3. Sear chicken in a single layer — 3 minutes per side.
  4. Add onion. Stir and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add garlic, jalapeño, and rosemary. Stir 1 minute.
  6. Add tomatoes, berbere, black pepper, and salt.
  7. Toss everything together for 2 more minutes.
  8. Chicken should be cooked through — internal temperature 74°C.
  9. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Chicken thighs are more forgiving than breast — they stay juicier under high heat.
  • Hot pan is critical — the sear is what develops the flavour.
  • Check internal temperature — 74°C for food safety.
  • Serve immediately — Doro Tibs waits for nobody.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Crowding the pan — chicken steams rather than sears.
  • Under-cooking — food safety risk.
  • Delaying service.

The Science

Chicken proteins denature at lower temperatures than beef — the myosin in chicken begins denaturing at 50°C, which is why chicken achieves its Maillard crust faster than beef. The rosemary's rosmarinic acid and carnosic acid are antioxidants that protect the chicken's own fats from oxidising during high-heat cooking, maintaining flavour quality.

Cultural Note

Doro Tibs is the everyday miracle of Ethiopian cooking — taking the same ingredients that make the grandest dish (Doro Wat) and applying them in a completely different way to produce something fast, satisfying, and completely its own.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly in hot kibbeh.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ayib (ETH096), As part of a combination platter
