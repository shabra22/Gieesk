🥣 Thiakry with Whole Millet, Yoghurt and Condensed Milk

Thiakry / chakery

Whole millet boiled 30 minutes in lightly salted water, cooled flat so it does not clump, whisked into sweetened condensed milk, yoghurt, milk, vanilla and cinnamon, chilled at least an hour and topped with pistachios.

Yummy Medley’s thiakry (published 14 April 2017, last updated 23 December 2019) uses whole millet grains because the author could not buy thiakry couscous and says she was still learning to make it. 1 cup of millet is rinsed and boiled in 2 cups of water and 1/4 teaspoon of salt for 30 minutes over low to medium heat; the millet is cooled completely on a flat tray to keep it from clumping; the sweetened condensed milk, yoghurt, milk, vanilla and cinnamon are whisked in a separate bowl; the millet is whisked in and allowed to cool in the refrigerator at least an hour; it is served topped with chopped pistachios. The page prints ‘14 oz about 1 cup’ for the sweetened condensed milk — a can size and a volume that disagree — and the line is reproduced as printed. There is no doneness cue for the 30-minute boil. A commenter says the dish is called ‘fura da nono’ in northern Nigeria.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Flat tray or plate, Whisk, Mixing bowl

  • 1 cup millet
  • 2 cups water to boil the millet
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 14 oz about 1 cup sweetened condensed milk (as printed)
  • 1 cup yoghurt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Chopped pistachios (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse and boil the millet in 2 cups of water and 1/4 teaspoon of salt for 30 minutes on low to medium heat.
  2. Allow the millet to cool completely on a flat tray or plate to keep it from clumping.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the sweetened condensed milk, yoghurt, milk, vanilla and cinnamon.
  4. Whisk in the millet and allow it to cool in the refrigerator for at least one hour. Serve topped with chopped pistachios.

Chef Tips

  • The page prints no doneness cue for the millet — only the 30 minutes.
  • Spread the millet thin to cool; the page says this keeps it from clumping.
  • Suggested toppings: mango, raisins, coconut, chopped nuts.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Reading ‘14 oz about 1 cup’ as exact.
  • Mixing hot millet into the dairy.
  • Serving before the hour of chilling.

Cultural Note

Described as a sweet, creamy, mildly tangy dessert mostly native to Senegal and Gambia; the author notes that traditional thiakry uses millet couscous and compares this version to tapioca pudding, ‘significantly better’.

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed.

Reheating: Served cold.

Serve with: Mango, Coconut, Sweet tea
