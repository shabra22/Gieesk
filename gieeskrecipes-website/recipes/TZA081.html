🦑 Tanzanian Coconut Squid

Ngisi wa Nazi

Tanzanian Ngisi wa Nazi — squid rings quickly sautéed in a fragrant coconut milk, garlic, and Zanzibar spice sauce. Light, aromatic, and very fast — the coastal squid curry that takes 15 minutes from start to finish.

Coconut squid is the quick seafood dish of coastal Tanzania — squid from the morning's catch in a light coconut sauce in under 15 minutes. The key is speed — the squid goes in last and comes out quickly.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 500g cleaned squid — rings and tentacles
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 red chilli
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Cook onions until golden. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  2. Add tomatoes. Cook 6 minutes until thick.
  3. Add coconut milk. Bring to gentle simmer.
  4. Add squid rings. Cook for exactly 3 minutes.
  5. Add lemon juice and coriander. Remove from heat immediately.
  6. Serve at once.

Chef Tips

  • 3 minutes for squid — not a second more.
  • Have everything ready before squid goes in.
  • Remove from heat immediately.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooked squid — rubbery beyond recovery.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Not recommended.
