🍽️ Ethiopian Modern Breakfast Platter

ዘመናዊ ቁርስ — Zemanawi Qurs

Ethiopian Modern Breakfast Platter — Teff Pancakes (ETH288), Chechebsa (ETH010), fried eggs with berbere, Avocado Smoothie (ETH289), Honey Butter (ETH152), and Ethiopian Coffee (ETH212). The complete modern Ethiopian morning — traditional and contemporary on one table.

The modern Ethiopian breakfast platter is the Addis Ababa hotel and café's version of a full breakfast — combining traditional Ethiopian breakfast dishes with contemporary formats.

Ingredients

Equipment: Multiple pans, Blender

  • Teff Pancakes — 4 pancakes
  • Chechebsa — small portion
  • 2 eggs — fried with berbere and kibbeh
  • Avocado Smoothie
  • Honey Butter
  • Ethiopian Coffee

Method

  1. Begin with coffee — the ceremony takes time.
  2. Make chechebsa.
  3. Make teff pancakes.
  4. Fry eggs with berbere butter.
  5. Blend smoothie last.
  6. Serve simultaneously on a large platter.

Chef Tips

  • The coffee ceremony anchors the modern Ethiopian breakfast — everything else is served around it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing — the breakfast is a morning ritual, not a quick meal.

Cultural Note

The modern Ethiopian breakfast platter is the experience that international visitors to Addis Ababa hotels discover and remember forever — the combination of the Ethiopian coffee ceremony, teff pancakes, and chechebsa with berbere eggs creates a morning that is impossible to replicate outside Ethiopia.
