      Method

      1. Two days before: make kashata. Marinate goat.
      2. Day before: prepare all sauces (pili pili, chatni). Marinate fish. Prepare kaimati dough.
      3. Morning of feast: begin pilau (90 minute process). Begin ugali croquettes preparation.
      4. 3 hours before: start nyama choma over charcoal.
      5. 2 hours before: cook mbaazi wa Nazi, sukuma wiki.
      6. 1 hour before: deep fry bhajia and ugali croquettes. Begin samaki wa kupaka.
      7. 30 minutes before: make vitumbua and mahamri.
      8. Final 15 minutes: fry kaimati. Brew chai. Mix hibiscus and Dawa.
      9. Break the pilau — the signal that the GieesK feast has begun.

      Chef Tips

      • The pilau is the centrepiece — give it the most attention.
      • The kaimati must be served immediately — they are the finale.
      • Label all dishes for guests unfamiliar with Tanzanian cuisine.
      • Have one person manage drinks and another the main dishes — this feast needs a team.
      • The nyama choma must be carved table-side — the theatre is part of the experience.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Attempting without a team.
      • Not staging over 2 days.
      • Starting the pilau too late.
