🥟 Pastels, Fried Fish Pies

Pastels

An egg-and-oil dough rolled to an eighth of an inch, cut into three-inch rounds, filled with white fish, parsley, basil, cilantro and habanero, and fried at 350°.

The Saveur printing makes about two dozen pies. A shaggy dough of flour, one egg and six tablespoons of water is kneaded about six minutes and rested an hour. Garlic, onion and a minced habanero are cooked about five minutes, then mixed with eight ounces of white fish — mackerel, trout or dorado — and chopped parsley, cilantro and basil. Rounds are cut with a three-inch cutter, a teaspoon of filling goes in each, and the pies are folded to half-moons, crimped with a fork and fried in two inches of oil. Note that the raw fish is cooked only by the six-minute fry.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large bowl, 2-quart saucepan, 3-inch round cutter, Fork, 6-quart Dutch oven, Deep-fry thermometer

  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 2 tbsp canola oil, plus oil for frying (frying quantity: oil to a depth of 2 inches)
  • 6 tbsp water — appears only in the method
  • 2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1 habanero or Scotch bonnet chile, stemmed, seeded, minced
  • 8 oz white fish, such as mackerel, trout or dorado
  • 4 sprigs parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 leaves basil, finely chopped
  • 2 sprigs cilantro, finely chopped
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper — quantities not printed

Method

    Dough

    1. Stir the flour, egg and 6 tablespoons of water until a shaggy dough forms. Knead on a work surface about 6 minutes, until smooth. Wrap in plastic and rest 1 hour.

    Filling

    1. Heat the 2 tablespoons of oil in a 2-quart saucepan over medium-high. Cook the garlic, onion and chile, stirring, about 5 minutes, until soft. Transfer to a bowl and stir in the fish, parsley, cilantro, basil, salt and pepper.

    Shaping

    1. Roll the dough to an 1/8-inch-thick circle. Cut about 24 rounds with a 3-inch cutter. Put about 1 teaspoon of filling in the centre of each, wet half of the edge, fold into a half-moon, press to seal and crimp with a fork.

    Frying

    1. Pour oil to a depth of 2 inches into a 6-quart Dutch oven and heat over medium-high until a deep-fry thermometer reads 350° (unit not printed). Fry in batches about 6 minutes, until golden brown and the filling is cooked through. Drain on parchment and serve hot with kaani sauce.

    Chef Tips

    • The printed cue is ‘golden brown and the filling is cooked through’ — the fish goes in uncooked, so do not pull the pies early.
    • Fry in batches so the oil does not drop in temperature.
    • The page does not include a kaani sauce; use Sosu Kaani, Habanero Chile Sauce.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Overfilling: a teaspoon is what the page prints.
    • Letting the fish be fully raw in a too-brief fry.
    • Skipping the fork crimp and losing filling in the oil.

    Cultural Note

    SAVEUR’s May 2012 Senegal feature was written by John O’Connor (‘A Feast for All’); the recipes themselves carry no personal byline. This one first appeared in the May 2012 issue and was republished online on 30 September 2013.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Fry the day you eat them; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

    Reheating: Recrisp in a hot oven 8 to 10 minutes.

    Serve with: Sosu kaani, Kanni
