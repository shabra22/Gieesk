🍛 Ethiopian Beef Curry

የሥጋ ካሪ — Yesiga Kari

Ethiopian Beef Curry — beef stewing cuts simmered with an Ethiopian curry sauce of berbere, turmeric, cardamom, and tomato. The beef version of the Ethiopian curry — same spice tradition as ETH039 applied to more affordable stewing beef.

Ethiopian beef curry is the accessible cousin of the lamb curry — stewing beef rather than lamb, slightly more affordable, equally deeply spiced. The berbere gives it a distinctly Ethiopian character.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 700g beef stewing cuts — cubed
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 4 tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp cardamom
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. See ETH039 — substitute beef for lamb. Beef takes slightly longer — 75—80 minutes.

Chef Tips

  • Stewing beef needs longer than lamb — patient simmering is essential.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-cooking — tough beef.

Cultural Note

Ethiopian beef curry is the weekday interpretation of the Ethiopian spice tradition — the curry format making Ethiopian spices accessible to those more familiar with that cooking style.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), Ethiopian Rice (ETH013)
