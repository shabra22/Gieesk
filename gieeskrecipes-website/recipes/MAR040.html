🍅 Harira (Tomato Soup with Chickpeas and Lentils)

Harira (حريرة)

Morocco's Ramadan soup: lamb, tomatoes, lentils, chickpeas, celery and herbs, thickened with a flour-and-water slurry to a silky consistency.

2h 10mTotal time
30mPrep
1h 40mCook
6Servings
330Calories
MediumDifficulty

Many Moroccans consider a Ramadan meal incomplete without harira. This version browns diced meat, adds bones, chickpeas, tomato puree, onion and spices, pressure-cooks, then adds the lentils, tomato paste, celery, herbs and more water. Rice or vermicelli are added, and a flour-and-water thickener is stirred in at the end and simmered 5 to 10 minutes until the soup is silky and cream-like. The author says the word harira derives from the Arabic word for silk.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pressure cooker or large pot, Blender (for the tomatoes)

  • 8 oz lamb, beef, or chicken, diced
  • 3 tbsp vegetable or olive oil
  • Soup bones: several (optional)
  • 2 lb ripe tomatoes (about 6 large)
  • 1 handful dry chickpeas, soaked and peeled
  • 2 handfuls dry green or brown lentils
  • 1 large onion, grated
  • 1 rib celery with leaves, chopped (or 1 small bunch Chinese celery)
  • 1 small bunch flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 small bunch cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp smen (optional)
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1.5 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste, mixed with 1 or 2 cups water
  • 3 tbsp uncooked rice or broken vermicelli
  • 1 cup flour, mixed with 2 cups water (the thickener)
  • 3 cups (710 ml) water for the first stage and 8.5 cups (2 L) for the second
  • Lemon wedges and cilantro, optional garnish

Method

  1. Brown the meat in the oil. Add the bones, chickpeas, tomato puree, onion, spices, smen and 3 cups water; cook 25 minutes under pressure (or simmer 50 to 60).
  2. Add the lentils, tomato paste, celery, herbs and 8.5 cups water.
  3. Rice option: pressure 30 minutes (or 60 simmered), add the rice and cook 15 minutes more (or 30 simmered). Vermicelli option: pressure 45 minutes (or 90 simmered), then add the vermicelli and simmer until tender.
  4. Stir in the flour-and-water thickener, using only as much as is needed, and simmer 5 to 10 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with lemon wedges and cilantro.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: thickened to a silky, cream-like consistency.
  • The page times assume a pressure cooker and say to double them for a conventional pot.
  • Peel the soaked chickpeas for a smoother soup.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dumping the thickener in at once so it lumps; stir it in gradually.
  • Over-thickening; use only as much as needed.
  • Overcooking the vermicelli.

Health Benefits

  • Legumes and tomatoes supply protein, fibre and lycopene.
  • A traditional fast-breaking soup, hearty and warming.

Cultural Note

The author writes that many Moroccans consider a meal during Ramadan incomplete if harira isn't on the table, and that the word is derived from the Arabic word for silk.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days in the fridge; it thickens further.

Reheating: Loosen with water and reheat, stirring.

Serve with: Lemon wedges and cilantro (garnish)
