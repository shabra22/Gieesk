🍗 Chicken Rfissa (Trid with Chicken and Lentils)

Rfissa / trid — chicken, lentils and fenugreek over shredded msemen (رفيسة)

Free-range chicken and lentils cooked with fenugreek, saffron and onions in a golden broth, poured over shredded, steamed msemen.

9hTotal time
1hPrep

HardDifficulty

Rfissa is built in layers: msemen torn into shreds, steamed until tender, then topped with the chicken, lentils, onions and plenty of broth. The chicken marinates with onions, oil and spices overnight, the fenugreek and lentils are soaked, and everything simmers until the chicken and lentils are quite tender. The author traces it to tharid, a centuries-old Arab dish of stew and broth served over bread, and says it is traditionally served on the third day following the birth of a baby.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot with lid, Couscoussier or steamer, Large platter

  • 1 large chicken, quartered or left whole
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp pepper
  • 1 1/2 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tbsp Ras el Hanout (or 1 1/2 tbsp msakhen)
  • 1 1/2 tsp turmeric

For the lentils

  • 1/2 cup uncooked lentils, green or brown
  • 4 tbsp fenugreek seeds, soaked overnight and drained
  • 1 1/2 tsp saffron threads, heated gently and then crumbled
  • 1 handful fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1 handful fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 4 1/2 cups water
  • 1 1/2 tsp smen (Moroccan preserved butter)

Msemen or trid

  • 1 1/2 batches msemen, shredded (or trid pastry)

Method

  1. Make the msemen and tear it into pieces while still hot. Store cooled pieces in a bag; freeze if made more than a day ahead.
  2. Soak the fenugreek and the lentils separately the night before (at least six hours). A quick soak with boiling water takes 1 to 2 hours.
  3. Marinate the chicken with the onions, oil and spices in a heavy pot, covered, in the fridge overnight or at least 6 hours.
  4. Cook the covered pot over medium heat 15 to 20 minutes, stirring, until a rich sauce has formed.
  5. Free-range (beldi) chicken: add the fenugreek, herbs and 4½ cups water; simmer covered over medium-low to medium about 1 hour; add the lentils and cook covered another hour, until the chicken and lentils are quite tender.
  6. Factory-raised (roumi) chicken: add everything together and simmer about 1 hour, until the lentils are tender and the chicken is well-cooked.
  7. Taste for salt and swirl in the smen. Optionally broil the chicken briefly to crisp the skin.
  8. Steam the shredded msemen in a couscoussier about 10 minutes, until steaming hot and tender. Pile it on a platter, add the chicken, then the lentils, onions and most of the broth.

Chef Tips

  • Doneness cue: chicken and lentils quite tender.
  • A whole chicken can be removed when nearly cooked and finished at 450°F (230°C), per the page.
  • The marinade is a chilled rest; the source gives time only (at least 6 hours) and no other cue.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the soak of the fenugreek.
  • Putting the lentils in too early with a factory-raised bird.
  • Steaming the msemen too long so it turns to paste.

Health Benefits

  • Lentils and fenugreek add protein and fibre.
  • Fenugreek is traditionally linked with nursing mothers.

Cultural Note

The author writes that rfissa traces back to tharid and is regarded by many as Moroccan comfort food at its best, traditionally served on the third day following the birth of a baby.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; store the msemen separately.

Reheating: Warm the chicken and broth gently, then re-steam the msemen.

Serve with: Mint tea
