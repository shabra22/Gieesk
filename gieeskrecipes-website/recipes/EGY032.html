🕊️ Hamam Mahshi bel Freek (Pigeons Stuffed with Green Wheat)

Hamam mahshi bil-freek — حمام محشي بالفريك

Pigeons stuffed with freekeh, onions, giblets and mint, braised in chicken broth for about 90 minutes, then served on freekeh cooked in the braising liquid.

HardDifficulty

Cracked green wheat is soaked 30 minutes, drained and squeezed, then mixed with sautéed onion and chopped giblets and mint. About ¼ cup stuffs each pigeon, and the birds are sewn, tied and braised covered in 2 cups of stock at 175C for about 90 minutes, basted once or twice. About 30 minutes before they finish the rest of the stock and the reserved freekeh are simmered about 20 minutes, rested 10 minutes, and the birds served on top.

Ingredients

Equipment: Roasting pan with lid, Needle and string, Saucepan

  • 4 pigeons with giblets – ha-mam with ki-bid wi ki-la-wi
  • 1 lemon, juiced – la-moon-a
  • 3 tbsp butter or oil – zib-da or zeit
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped – ba-sal
  • 2 cups cracked green wheat – freek
  • 1 tbs fresh mint, chopped – na-na
  • 4 cups chicken broth – ma-ra-a
  • salt and pepper – malh and fil-fil

Method

  1. Soak the freek in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 175C. Rub the pigeons with lemon juice and season.
  3. Fry the onions and chopped giblets for seven or eight minutes, until the onions are cooked through and translucent.
  4. Drain and squeeze the freek and mix with the fried onions, mint, salt and pepper. Stuff about ¼ cup into each bird, sew and tie the legs.
  5. Pour 2 cups of stock over the birds, cover and braise about 90 minutes, basting once or twice.
  6. About 30 minutes before they finish, boil the remaining stock and add the reserved freek; simmer about 20 minutes and rest about 10 minutes. Serve the birds on the freek with the braising juices.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: onions translucent and cooked through. The freekeh soak is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (30 minutes). The braise time of about 90 minutes is as printed.
  • Compare with the grilled pigeon, which cooks in minutes; the braise is for a more tender, moist result.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overstuffing, since the freekeh swells.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Reheat covered in broth.

Serve with: Freekeh pilaf
