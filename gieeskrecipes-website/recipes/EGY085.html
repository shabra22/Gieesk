🌯 Ashengoot (Siwan Goose and Egg Wraps)

Ashengoot — أشنجوت

Goose legs simmered with mastic and spices, shredded into thin flatbreads with a boiled egg and spring onion, and dressed with date syrup, olive oil and lemon.

3h 15mTotal time
45mPrep
2h 10mCook

Goose legs simmer with onion, mastic, bay, pepper, cinnamon and cardamom for about 1½ to 2 hours, or until the meat is tender. A dough of flour, salt and water is kneaded about 5 minutes, rested 20 minutes, divided into eight balls and cooked 1 minute on each side. The eggs cook exactly 8 minutes and go into an ice bath; assembled with goose, egg and spring onions and a date-syrup and olive-oil dressing, the wraps are rolled like a cigar.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Rolling pin, Flat pan or griddle, Bowl of ice water

  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 3 mastic resin pieces
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 2 goose legs
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 spring onions (scallions)
  • 200g (7oz/1½ cups) plain (all-purpose) flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Olive oil, for brushing
  • 4 tbsp date syrup
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • A pinch of chilli flakes
  • Juice of ½ a lemon
  • Sea salt and ground black pepper
  • 150ml (5fl oz/½ cup plus 2 tbsp) water (for the dough)

Method

  1. Cover the goose legs with water in a pot with the onion, mastic, bay, pepper, cinnamon and cardamom. Cover and simmer for about 1½ to 2 hours, or until the meat is tender. Shred the meat.
  2. Dough: mix the flour, salt and water and knead until smooth and elastic, about 5 minutes. Cover with a cloth and rest 20 minutes.
  3. Divide the dough into eight equal balls, roll thin and cook on a hot pan for 1 minute on each side, brushing with oil.
  4. Eggs: boil exactly 8 minutes, then plunge into an ice bath, peel and slice.
  5. Mix the date syrup, olive oil, chilli and lemon juice into a dressing.
  6. Spread dressing on a flatbread, add goose, egg and spring onions, and roll up like a cigar.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: meat tender; dough smooth and elastic. The dough rest is NO CUE, TIME ONLY. The page says chicken or duck can replace goose.
  • The thin 20 cm circle for the flatbread is a single-pass figure.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the flatbreads, which crack when rolled (our caution).

Cultural Note

The authors call it a Siwan wedding staple that marks communal celebration; they adapt it with a date and olive oil dressing.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best fresh; goose keeps refrigerated 3 days (our estimate).

Reheating: Warm the goose; assemble fresh.

Serve with: Date syrup dressing
