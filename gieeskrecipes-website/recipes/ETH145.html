🫙 Ethiopian Ayib with Herbs

አይብ ከቅጠላ — Ayib ke-Qitela

Ethiopian Ayib with Herbs — freshly made ayib (ETH096) mixed with finely chopped green jalapeño, coriander, and spring onion. The herb-enriched version of the essential Ethiopian fresh cheese — more complex than plain ayib, this version serves as both a condiment and a standalone dip.

Ayib with herbs takes the plain fresh cheese of ETH096 and elevates it into a condiment of its own character — the green jalapeño provides mild heat, the coriander adds freshness, and the spring onion adds sharpness. Served in a larger mound on the celebration platter.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Muslin cloth, Bowl

    Fresh Ayib (make per ETH096)

    • 2 litres whole milk
    • 4 tbsp lemon juice
    • Pinch of salt

    Herb Mixture

    • 2 green jalapeños — very finely diced
    • ¼ cup fresh coriander — finely chopped
    • 3 spring onions — finely sliced
    • 1 tsp lemon zest
    • Salt to taste

    Method

    1. Make Ayib per ETH096.
    2. Drain for 1.5 hours until firm but still moist.
    3. Place in a bowl.
    4. Add finely diced jalapeño, coriander, spring onion, and lemon zest.
    5. Mix gently — do not over-mix or the texture becomes paste-like.
    6. Season with salt.
    7. Shape into a mound or serve in a ramekin.

    Chef Tips

    • Mix gently — ayib should stay crumbly, not become a paste.
    • The green jalapeño is milder than berbere — this is intentionally mild heat.
    • Lemon zest brightens the herb flavours.
    • Serve at room temperature — cold ayib loses its flavour.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Over-mixing — paste instead of crumbles.
    • Serving cold.

    The Science

    See ETH096 for cheese-making science. The addition of jalapeño (capsaicin dissolved in the cheese fat) creates a mild heat that persists longer than water-soluble capsaicin sources — the fat slows the release of capsaicin onto capsaicin receptors.

    Cultural Note

    Ayib with herbs is the celebration version of the essential cheese — the additional herbs signal that this is a special occasion, that the cook has taken extra care, that the guest deserves more than the plain version.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

    Reheating: Bring to room temperature.

    Serve with: With kitfo (ETH029), On the celebration platter, As a standalone dip with injera
