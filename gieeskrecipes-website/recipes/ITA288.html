🥧 Torta Verde Ligure

Torta Verde

A savory Ligurian vegetable pie, its thin pastry shell enclosing a filling of Swiss chard, rice, and cheese, sliced into wedges and enjoyed warm or at room temperature.

Torta verde is a versatile, everyday staple across Liguria, its filling varying slightly by household but always centered on greens, and its simple oil-based pastry making it a lighter alternative to the region's more elaborate torta pasqualina.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Round baking pan

  • 400 g (3 cups) 00 flour
  • 150 ml (⅔ cup) water, approximately
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for brushing
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 kg (2.2 lb) Swiss chard, stemmed
  • 100 g (½ cup) rice, cooked
  • 250 g (1 cup) ricotta
  • 80 g (1 cup) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 2 eggs
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Combine the flour, water, olive oil, and salt into a smooth, elastic dough. Divide into two portions, cover, and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Blanch the chard briefly, then squeeze out excess water thoroughly and chop.
  3. Combine the chopped chard with the cooked rice, ricotta, Parmigiano, eggs, and nutmeg into a well-mixed filling.
  4. Roll out one portion of dough thin and line an oiled round baking pan, letting the edges overhang.
  5. Spread the filling evenly over the base.
  6. Roll out the second portion and lay over the top, sealing by folding and crimping the edges.
  7. Brush lightly with olive oil and prick the top a few times.
  8. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 40–45 minutes until deeply golden.
  9. Let cool slightly before slicing into wedges.

Chef Tips

  • Squeeze the chard very thoroughly to prevent the filling from becoming watery.
  • The rice adds substance and helps bind the filling, giving the pie a satisfying texture.
  • This pie is excellent served warm, at room temperature, or cold, making it versatile for picnics.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not squeezing the chard enough, resulting in a watery filling that seeps into the pastry.
  • Rolling the pastry too thick, overwhelming the delicate filling.
  • Skipping the venting pricks on top, which can cause uneven puffing during baking.

Health Benefits

  • Swiss chard provides fiber and vitamins A and K; a good source of calcium from the cheese.

Cultural Note

Torta verde is an everyday staple across Liguria, a simpler, lighter cousin to the more elaborate torta pasqualina reserved for Easter.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days; excellent eaten cold.

Reheating: Reheat gently in the oven, or enjoy at room temperature.

Serve with: A simple green salad, A crisp Ligurian white wine
