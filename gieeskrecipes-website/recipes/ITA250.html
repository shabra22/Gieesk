Gramigna alla Salsiccia

Short, curly pasta tubes tossed in a rich, creamy sausage and tomato sauce, a beloved everyday classic from Bologna's home kitchens and neighborhood trattorie alike.

Gramigna, named for its resemblance to a weedy grass, is a shape built specifically to catch onto a chunky, meaty sauce, and its pairing with sausage in a light cream-tomato sauce has made it one of Emilia-Romagna's most reliably comforting weeknight dishes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large saute pan

  • 400 g (14 oz) gramigna pasta (or substitute cavatappi or fusilli)
  • 400 g (14 oz) Italian sausage, casings removed and crumbled
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 100 ml (scant 1/2 cup) dry white wine
  • 200 g (7 oz) canned crushed tomatoes
  • 150 ml (2/3 cup) heavy cream
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • Grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, for serving
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pan and saute the onion until softened.
  2. Add the crumbled sausage and cook until browned, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add the wine and let it reduce.
  4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente.
  6. Stir the cream into the sausage sauce and simmer for a few more minutes until slightly thickened.
  7. Drain the pasta, reserving some cooking water, and toss into the sauce with a splash of the water.
  8. Season to taste and serve with grated Parmigiano.

Chef Tips

  • Break the sausage into small, even crumbles for the best texture throughout the sauce.
  • A short simmer after adding the cream helps the sauce properly thicken and cling to the pasta.
  • Gramigna curly shape is specifically designed to catch onto chunky sauces - a similar tubular, curled pasta works well as a substitute.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking the cream sauce, which can cause it to separate rather than stay smooth.
  • Using bland sausage, which is the primary flavor driver of this simple dish.
  • Substituting a very smooth, long pasta shape, which won't hold the chunky sauce as well.

Cultural Note

Gramigna alla salsiccia is a beloved everyday classic in Bologna and throughout Emilia-Romagna, a reliable comfort dish found in countless neighborhood trattorie.

Storage & Serving

Storage: The sauce keeps refrigerated for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat the sauce gently on the stovetop; cook fresh pasta just before serving.

Serve with: A medium-bodied red wine from Emilia
