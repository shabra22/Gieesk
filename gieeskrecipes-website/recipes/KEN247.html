🥑 Kenyan Avocado Shake

Shake ya Parachichi

Kenyan Avocado Shake — two whole ripe Kenyan avocados blended with cold milk, honey, and a squeeze of lime into a rich, impossibly creamy, pale green shake. The shake that astonishes newcomers to Kenyan café culture — richer than any dairy milkshake, lighter than any dessert.

The avocado shake is one of the most distinctive things you can drink in a Kenyan café. Thick, pale green, and unbelievably creamy, it is a shake made entirely from fruit fat rather than dairy fat — and the result is something the world outside Kenya has not yet fully discovered. For Kenyans, it is completely normal. For visitors, it is a revelation.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Tall glasses

  • 2 large ripe Kenyan avocados
  • 2 cups cold whole milk
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ice — 6 cubes

Method

  1. Scoop avocado flesh into the blender.
  2. Add cold milk, honey, lime juice, vanilla, salt, and ice.
  3. Blend on high speed for 45—60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
  4. Taste and adjust honey and lime.
  5. Pour into tall glasses.
  6. Serve immediately — avocado discolours quickly.

Chef Tips

  • Drink immediately — avocado oxidises and browns quickly after blending.
  • The lime juice slows browning slightly and adds essential acidity.
  • Kenyan avocados — large, buttery, and rich — make this incomparably better than imported varieties.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not drinking immediately — turns brown.
  • Under-ripe avocado — lacking creaminess.

The Science

See KEN138 for avocado fat composition science. In shake form, the avocado fat droplets are emulsified by high-speed blending with milk, creating a stable, ultra-fine emulsion with exceptional mouthfeel — the same mechanism that makes avocado-based dressings and dips so creamy.

Health Benefits

  • Rich in healthy monounsaturated fats.
  • High in potassium, vitamins K, C, and B6.
  • High in folate.
  • Very filling — the fat and fibre combination provides prolonged satiety.

Cultural Note

The avocado shake is one of the most surprising and delightful discoveries in Kenyan café culture for international visitors — a completely normal Kenyan drink that astonishes people from countries where avocado is expensive and treated with reverence. In Kenya, avocados are so abundant that blending two of them into a shake is perfectly natural.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Consume immediately.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Kenyan Avocado Toast, Club Sandwich, As a standalone breakfast shake
