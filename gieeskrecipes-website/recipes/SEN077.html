🍲 Thiéré Bassi Salté, Millet Couscous with Meat and Vegetables

Thiéré bassi salté

Steamed millet couscous mixed with white beans, chickpeas, raisins, fried meatballs and baobab-leaf powder, served over a beef-and-chicken sauce with squash, cassava, carrot, turnip and sweet potato.

A celebration dish tied by the page to Tamkharit, the Senegalese name for Ashura, when it is traditionally eaten by the country’s Muslims. Beef and chicken thighs are marinated 30 minutes with crushed garlic, salt and pepper, browned in a large pot, and simmered about an hour with tomato paste, grated tomatoes, a paste of onion, spring onion, garlic and chilli, and chunks of squash, cassava, carrot, turnip and sweet potato, then reduced until concentrated and fragrant. Millet couscous is soaked in boiling water, rubbed apart, steamed 20 minutes; lalo (baobab leaf powder) is dissolved in 12 cl water and beaten into a thick white mixture, stirred into the couscous, and steamed another 20 minutes. Fried minced-meat balls, chickpeas, white beans and raisins are mixed in; a ladle of sauce moistens the couscous, and the meat, vegetables and sauce go alongside.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Couscoussier or steamer, Frying pan, Whisk

    Couscous

    • 250 g millet couscous
    • 100 g minced meat
    • 50 g cooked white beans
    • 50 g cooked chickpeas
    • 20 g raisins
    • 10 g lalo (baobab leaf powder)
    • 12 cl water for the lalo

    Sauce

    • 300 g beef
    • 2 chicken thighs
    • 5 cl vegetable oil
    • 1 medium onion, grated
    • 1 small green bell pepper, coarsely chopped
    • 100 g tomato paste
    • 10 cl grated tomatoes
    • 1 piece of squash, cut into large chunks — weight not printed
    • 4 pieces of cassava — weight not printed
    • 1 carrot, peeled and cut into 4
    • 1 turnip, peeled and cut into 4
    • 1 sweet potato, peeled and cut into pieces
    • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
    • 1 red chilli pepper
    • 1/2 bunch spring onions
    • Salt, pepper — quantities not printed

    Method

      Sauce

      1. Marinate the beef and chicken about 30 minutes with some of the crushed garlic, salt and pepper. Brown them in the hot oil in a large pot.
      2. Dissolve the tomato paste in a little water and add to the pot. Add the squash, cassava, carrot, turnip and sweet potato and cover with water.
      3. Blend the onion, spring onions, remaining garlic, pepper, chilli, salt and pepper to a paste and add to the sauce with the grated tomatoes. Simmer about 1 hour, until the meats and vegetables are cooked.
      4. Remove the meat and vegetables, then reduce the sauce over low heat until it is concentrated and fragrant.

      Couscous

      1. Soak the millet couscous in boiling water, rub the grains apart and let them absorb. Steam 20 minutes.
      2. Dissolve the lalo in 12 cl of water and beat until you get a thick white mixture. Stir it into the couscous and steam a further 20 minutes.
      3. Shape the minced meat into small balls and fry them in a little hot oil. Mix the chickpeas, white beans, raisins and meatballs into the couscous.

      To serve

      1. Moisten the couscous with a ladle of sauce and mix gently. Serve with the meat, the vegetables and the remaining sauce.

      Chef Tips

      • Printed cues: simmer ‘until the meats and vegetables are cooked’; reduce the sauce ‘until it is concentrated and fragrant’; beat the lalo ‘until you get a thick white mixture’.
      • Rub the soaked couscous apart between your hands so the steam can pass through.
      • Keep the squash and cassava in large pieces — the page cuts them large.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Adding the vegetables so small they dissolve.
      • Skipping the lalo-and-steam stage, which gives the couscous its characteristic texture.
      • Pouring all the sauce over the couscous instead of a ladle.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: Couscous and sauce 3 days refrigerated, separately.

      Reheating: Steam the couscous; warm the sauce.

      Serve with: Remaining sauce, Hot pepper
