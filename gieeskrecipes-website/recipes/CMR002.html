🥬 Ndolé, Cameroonian Bitter Leaf Stew (Eat Well Abi)

Ndolé

Blended peanuts, ginger, garlic, onion and Scotch bonnet simmered with ground crayfish, finished with spinach and bitter leaf and topped with fried prawns and onions.

A lighter, shorter ndolé. Blanched peanuts, ginger, garlic, Scotch bonnet and onions are blended with 2 cups of water, then poured into a pot with ground crayfish and another cup of water, covered, and simmered about 15 minutes with regular stirring so it does not burn. Sliced spinach and washed bitter leaf go in with salt, covered on low heat and stirred until the spinach has wilted properly. Prawns are fried on both sides and sliced onions fried separately, and both top the pot. The page links to a separate post for washing out the bitterness; that advice is not reproduced here.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Pot with lid, Frying pan

  • 1 cup blanched peanuts
  • 1 inch ginger (thumb size)
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 cup frozen shrimp
  • 1 cup washed bitter leaf
  • 3 cups spinach
  • 2 tablespoons ground crayfish
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Peel the onions and garlic. Remove the stalk from the Scotch bonnet. Slice half of one onion and set it aside.
  2. Blend the remaining onions with the peanuts, ginger, garlic and Scotch bonnet and 2 cups of water.
  3. Fry the prawns on both sides until cooked, then fry the sliced onions and remove from the heat. (Frying times are not printed.)
  4. Pour the blend into a pot, add the ground crayfish and a cup of water, cover and simmer about 15 minutes, stirring regularly to prevent burning.
  5. Wash and slice the spinach and bitter leaf. Add them with salt, cover and cook on low heat, stirring until the spinach has wilted properly.
  6. Top with the fried prawns and onions.

Chef Tips

  • The printed doneness cues are ‘cooked’ for the prawns and ‘wilted properly’ for the spinach.
  • Stir regularly during the 15-minute simmer.
  • Wash the bitter leaf as the author’s linked post advises if it is too bitter (the page does not give the method here).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping the stirring and letting the blended peanuts catch.
  • Adding the prawns to the pot before they are fried, as the method fries them separately.
  • Leaving the Scotch bonnet stalk on.

Cultural Note

This printing uses spinach and bitter leaf together and only 1 cup of peanuts. It is one of five ndolé printings in this collection; the ratios differ widely and are recorded unreconciled.
