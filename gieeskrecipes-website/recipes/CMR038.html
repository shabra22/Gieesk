🍃 Pudding Egusi (Steamed Egusi and Meat Cake)

Pudding egousi

Finely ground egusi mixed with simmered beef, ground smoked fish, crayfish, onion and pepper to a pasty consistency, wrapped in warmed plantain leaves and steamed about an hour.

A steamed egusi cake from Afrik, labelled ‘Recette du Cameroun’. Smoked fish is cut, ground and soaked in hot water. The beef is simmered with half an onion; the fish goes in when the beef is almost cooked. The meat and fish are mixed into the egusi to a pasty consistency, with water added if it is too pasty. Pepper, onion and crayfish are ground and added. Portions are wrapped in warmed plantain leaves and steamed about an hour, with water added from time to time. The method on the page is summarised in prose with few quantities.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Steamer, Plantain leaves

  • 500 g beef
  • 200 g dry smoked fish
  • 1 kg egusi seeds, finely ground
  • 150 g crayfish
  • 1 large onion
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 beef stock cubes
  • 8 plantain leaves (printed ‘8 feuille’)
  • Chillies (optional, to taste)

Method

  1. Cut, grind and soak the smoked fish in hot water.
  2. Simmer the beef with half the onion. When it is almost cooked, add the fish.
  3. Mix the meat and fish into the ground egusi to a pasty consistency. Add water if it is too pasty.
  4. Grind the pepper, the remaining onion and the crayfish and add them.
  5. Wrap portions in warmed plantain leaves. Steam about an hour, adding water from time to time.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cues are ‘presque cuite’ for the beef and a ‘pasty’ consistency for the mixture.
  • Add water to the steamer from time to time.
  • The method on the page is not broken into times and amounts beyond these.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding too much water to the mix so the cake will not hold.
  • Skipping the warming of the leaves.
  • Letting the steamer dry out.

Cultural Note

The page says only ‘Recette du Cameroun’ and gives no community. It is not called mets de pistache on the page, though it is a similar seed-and-meat cake.
