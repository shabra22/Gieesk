🌿 Kenyan Herb Rice

Wali wa Mimea

Kenyan Herb Rice — fluffy basmati rice cooked by the absorption method, finished with a generous herb oil of fresh coriander, parsley, garlic, and lemon. The restaurant rice side that transforms every main course it accompanies.

Herb rice is the unsung hero of the restaurant table — perfectly cooked fluffy rice, finished with a vibrant herb oil, that elevates any main course it accompanies. The key is the absorption method and the quality of the herb oil applied at the end.

Ingredients

Equipment: Medium saucepan with tight lid, Small bowl for herb oil

  • 3 cups basmati rice — washed
  • 4.5 cups water or light stock
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Herb Oil

  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves — minced
  • ½ cup fresh coriander — finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley — finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Combine herb oil ingredients. Set aside.
  2. Bring water and salt to a boil in a saucepan.
  3. Add rinsed rice. Stir once. Return to a boil.
  4. Reduce to lowest heat. Cover tightly.
  5. Cook for 18 minutes — do not lift the lid.
  6. Remove from heat. Rest covered for 5 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork.
  8. Fold herb oil through the rice gently.
  9. Taste and adjust salt and lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Never lift the lid during cooking — the steam is what cooks the rice.
  • The herb oil must be added hot rice — it wilts the herbs slightly and blooms the garlic.
  • Fold gently — do not stir or the rice becomes gluey.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Lifting the lid — steam escapes and rice is undercooked.
  • Stirring after adding herbs — gluey texture.

The Science

The absorption method traps steam inside the covered pot — the rice cooks through a combination of absorbed water and steam condensation. The starches gelatinise evenly when undisturbed, producing the fluffy, separate-grain texture. Stirring after cooking ruptures the swollen starch granules, releasing amylose — creating stickiness.

Health Benefits

  • Basmati rice has a lower GI than regular white rice.
  • Fresh herbs add antioxidants.

Cultural Note

Herb rice demonstrates that the side dish matters — at Kenya's finest hotels, no main course arrives with plain boiled rice. The herb oil and perfect cooking technique make it a dish in its own right.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days. Add a splash of water when reheating.

Reheating: Microwave with a damp cloth on top, or steam briefly.
