🍪 Ethiopian Honey Cookies

ማር ኩኪ — Mar Kuki

Ethiopian Honey Cookies — crispy, fragrant cookies made with raw Ethiopian mountain honey, spiced with cardamom, cinnamon, and fenugreek. The celebration cookie — served at Ethiopian Orthodox Christian holidays, coffee ceremonies, and as a gift.

Ethiopian honey cookies carry the entire Ethiopian spice tradition into a sweet — the cardamom, cinnamon, and fenugreek that perfume Difo Dabo and celebration breads appear here in a crispy cookie that keeps for weeks. The raw mountain honey gives them a floral complexity that commercial honey cannot replicate.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl, Rolling pin, Baking sheet, Oven at 170°C

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp raw Ethiopian honey
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp oil or softened butter
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground fenugreek
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp water (if needed for dough consistency)

Glaze: 1 tbsp honey + 1 tsp water

    Method

    1. Whisk egg with honey, sugar, and oil until combined.
    2. Combine flour, baking powder, salt, cardamom, cinnamon, fenugreek, and cloves.
    3. Add wet ingredients to dry. Mix to a firm dough — add water if too dry.
    4. Rest covered 30 minutes.
    5. Roll to 4mm thickness.
    6. Cut with a round or decorative cutter.
    7. Place on lined baking sheet.
    8. Brush lightly with honey glaze.
    9. Bake at 170°C for 14—18 minutes until golden.
    10. Cool completely on a rack.

    Chef Tips

    • Lower temperature than standard cookies — honey sugars burn at high heat.
    • The fenugreek is the Ethiopian signature — do not substitute.
    • Cool completely before storing — they crisp as they cool.
    • Ethiopian mountain honey with its complex floral notes is dramatically superior to commercial honey here.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Too high temperature — burns.
    • Storing warm — becomes soft.

    The Science

    Honey's high fructose content produces Maillard browning and caramelisation at lower temperatures than sugar — which is why honey cookies bake at 170°C rather than the standard 180°C. The fenugreek's sotolone compound (caramel-curry note) develops beautifully in the oven, contributing to the cookie's signature aroma.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian honey cookies are the celebration gift — wrapped in cloth and given between families at Ethiopian Orthodox holidays, at weddings, and at New Year (Enkutatash). Their long shelf life means they can travel with travellers between highland towns and arrive still perfectly crispy.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Airtight container — 3 weeks at room temperature.

    Reheating: Not needed.

    Serve with: During Ethiopian coffee ceremonies, As celebration gifts, With chai
