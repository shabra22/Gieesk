The GieesK Signature Meal is the modern Ethiopian dining experience at its peak — the best of traditional Ethiopian cooking presented with contemporary precision, alongside the modern café innovations that represent Ethiopia's culinary future.

Ingredients

Equipment: Full kitchen setup

  • Berbere Grilled Chicken
  • Modern Tibs Bowl
  • Modern Vegetarian Platter
  • Coffee Cheesecake
  • Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony
  • Berbere Fries as a side
  • Honey Latte as the café drink

Method

  1. Day before: marinate chicken. Make cheesecake.
  2. Day of: all fasting dishes for vegetarian platter.
  3. Dinner service: grill chicken. Make tibs bowl fresh.
  4. Coffee ceremony during dessert service.

Chef Tips

  • The coffee ceremony is the heart of the meal — plan it as the finale.
  • The vegetarian platter is assembled first and held warm while the meat dishes are prepared fresh.
  • This is a 2-hour dining experience — plan accordingly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing.
  • Serving the coffee ceremony before the meal.

Cultural Note

The GieesK Signature Ethiopian Café Meal is our vision of modern Ethiopian dining — honouring every tradition (the coffee ceremony, the berbere, the injera) while embracing the contemporary café culture that young Ethiopians have built. It is the old and the new together, as they should be.
