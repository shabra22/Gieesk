🍢 Liver Kebabs (Kouah)

Kouah / kebda mechwia — grilled liver (كبدة مشوية)

Cubes of fresh calf or lamb liver threaded with suet, seasoned with paprika and cumin, and grilled until lightly seared but still a bit spongy when squeezed.

1h 30mTotal time
20mPrep
10mCook
4Servings
290Calories
MediumDifficulty

Young liver, ideally less than 48 hours old, is trimmed of its membrane and cut into 3/4-inch cubes, tossed with small cubes of suet, salt, paprika, cumin and optional cayenne, and marinated at least an hour. The cubes are threaded onto skewers, alternating every few pieces with fat, and grilled once the coals have gone grey. The cue is cooked through and lightly seared but still a bit spongy when squeezed; serve immediately.

Ingredients

Equipment: Metal skewers, Charcoal grill

  • 1.1 lbs calf or lamb liver less than 48 hrs old (young liver is better)
  • 4 oz kidney fat (suet), optional but recommended
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp ground sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper optional

For serving

  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Remove the transparent membrane around the liver and cut it into cubes about 3/4 inch (2 cm).
  2. Cut the suet into small cubes. Combine the liver and fat with the spices, cover and marinate at least an hour (or overnight) in the fridge.
  3. Light the charcoal. The grill is ready when the flames have gone out and the coals have a thin grey cover.
  4. Thread the liver onto skewers, alternating every few pieces with fat and packing them neither loose nor tight; about 6 to 8 cubes of liver to a skewer.
  5. Grill, turning to cook on all sides, until the liver is cooked through and lightly seared but still a bit spongy when squeezed. Serve immediately with cumin and salt.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: cooked through, lightly seared, a bit spongy when squeezed.
  • The page stresses fresh, young liver (less than 48 hours old).
  • The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY (at least an hour).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking, which makes liver tough.
  • Using old liver.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Eat immediately; liver does not keep well once cooked.

Reheating: Not recommended.

Serve with: Bread, Cumin and salt mix
