🥞 Baghrir (Thousand-Hole Semolina Pancakes)

Baghrir / beghrir (بغرير)

Spongy, yeast-raised semolina pancakes cooked on one side only, so the top is covered with tiny bubbles that become holes. Soaked in warm honey-butter.

40mTotal time
5mPrep
20mCook
16Servings
90Calories
EasyDifficulty

The batter of fine semolina or durum flour, all-purpose flour, yeast, baking powder, sugar, salt and lukewarm water is blended for about a minute, rested 10 to 15 minutes until light and foamy, and poured into a medium-hot non-stick crepe pan without swirling. Each pancake cooks on one side only, about 1 to 2 minutes, until set with no wet spots. They are cooled in a single layer before stacking.

Ingredients

Equipment: Blender, Non-stick crepe pan

  • 1 1/2 cups fine semolina or durum flour
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp dry yeast
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 cups + 2 tbsp lukewarm water
  • Butter and honey, to serve (no quantity printed)

Method

  1. Blend everything for a solid minute until smooth and creamy.
  2. Rest about 10 to 15 minutes. The batter should be light and foamy with tiny bubbles.
  3. Heat a non-stick crepe pan over medium heat. Pour in batter without swirling and cook one side only, about 1 to 2 minutes, until set with no wet spots and the surface is covered with holes.
  4. Cool in a single layer before stacking. Serve with warm honey and butter.

Chef Tips

  • Cues: batter light and foamy; surface holes; no wet spots left.
  • If the holes stay small, thin the batter with a tablespoon or two of water (relayed from the page; unverified).
  • A very bright yellow batter may contain colouring, a sign of an inferior batter, says the author.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Swirling the batter.
  • Flipping the pancake.
  • Stacking while hot.

Cultural Note

The author notes that the flipped version is called khringos in some regions, and that Moroccans reserve a pan for beghrir.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Keeps 2 days; stack with paper between.

Reheating: Warm in a low oven, or serve at room temperature with warm honey-butter.

Serve with: Honey and butter, Mint tea
