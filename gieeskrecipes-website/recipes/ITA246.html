🍖 Mondeghili

Mondeghili

Milan's own flattened, pan-fried meatballs, traditionally made from leftover boiled or roasted meat, seasoned with garlic, lemon zest, and nutmeg, and fried in butter until deeply golden.

Mondeghili exemplify Milanese resourcefulness, historically born from the practical need to use up leftover meat from a Sunday roast or boiled dinner, transformed into something genuinely craveable with a good crisp crust from frying in butter.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Large skillet

  • 400 g (14 oz) leftover cooked meat (beef, veal, or a mix), finely chopped
  • 100 g (1 cup) stale bread, soaked in milk and squeezed
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g (3/4 cup) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 100 g (1 cup) breadcrumbs, for coating
  • 80 g (1/3 cup) unsalted butter
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Combine the finely chopped meat with the soaked bread, egg, Parmigiano, garlic, lemon zest, nutmeg, and parsley.
  2. Season well and mix until cohesive, then rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Shape the mixture into small, flattened oval patties.
  4. Coat each patty in breadcrumbs, pressing gently to adhere.
  5. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Fry the mondeghili for 3-4 minutes per side, until deeply golden and heated through.
  7. Drain briefly on paper towels and serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • Using leftover roasted or boiled meat gives these their traditional flavor and is a great way to use up Sunday dinner leftovers.
  • Shape them flattened rather than round, which is traditional for mondeghili and helps them cook through quickly.
  • Frying in butter rather than oil gives the characteristic rich, golden crust.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Shaping them too thick, which can leave the center underheated by the time the outside browns.
  • Skipping the lemon zest and nutmeg, which are essential to the traditional Milanese flavor profile.
  • Overcrowding the pan, which can cause them to steam rather than fry to a proper crust.

Cultural Note

Mondeghili are a beloved Milanese specialty born from resourceful home cooking, traditionally a clever way to transform leftover meat into a genuinely craveable dish.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 2 days.

Reheating: Reheat in a hot skillet or oven to help re-crisp; avoid the microwave.

Serve with: A simple green salad, Mashed potatoes
