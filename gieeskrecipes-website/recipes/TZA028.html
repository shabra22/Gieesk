🍗 Tanzanian Chicken Stew

Kuku wa Mchuzi

Kuku wa Mchuzi — everyday Tanzanian chicken stew. Bone-in chicken pieces simmered in a simple, fragrant tomato and onion sauce with garlic, ginger, and warm spices. The everyday family chicken dish of Tanzania — simple, satisfying, and endlessly made.

Kuku wa Mchuzi is different from the spiced curry version — it is lighter, simpler, and more about the natural flavour of the chicken and the quality of the tomato-onion sauce. It is the everyday chicken stew that Tanzanian mothers make on ordinary weekdays.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot with lid

  • 1 kg chicken pieces — bone-in
  • 2 onions — diced
  • 3 tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup water
  • ¼ cup coriander

Method

  1. Brown chicken. Remove.
  2. Cook onions until golden.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, curry powder. Stir 30 seconds.
  4. Add tomatoes. Cook 8 minutes until thick.
  5. Return chicken. Add water. Simmer covered 30—35 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander.

Chef Tips

  • Simple is the key — this dish is about the chicken, not the spice.
  • Bone-in chicken provides much more flavour.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boneless chicken — loses depth.
  • Over-spicing — this is a gentle, everyday stew.

Cultural Note

Kuku wa Mchuzi is the taste of an ordinary Tuesday in Tanzania — the smell that means a normal, happy family dinner is on the way. Its simplicity is its strength.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop.
