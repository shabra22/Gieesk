🍗 Poulet DG (Immaculate Bites)

Poulet DG / DG chicken

A whole chicken cut small and browned about 10 minutes, simmered in a blended onion, parsley, celery, tomato and garlic paste with carrots, green beans and peppers, then tossed with fried ripe plantain rounds.

Adamu describes poulet DG as a cross between stir-fried chicken and fricassée. The chicken is seasoned with salt and pepper and browned about 10 minutes total in a Dutch oven until golden on both sides (internal 165°F / 73°C). A paste of onion, parsley, celery, tomato and garlic is blended and poured in, scraping up the browned bits; the bouillon is added and the pot stirred occasionally 8–10 minutes. Carrots and green beans simmer 2–3 minutes, the green peppers go in, and the stove is turned off. Ripe plantain rounds about 1 inch thick are fried in oil ½ inch deep until golden brown and tossed in at serving so they do not go soggy.

Ingredients

Equipment: Dutch oven, Blender, Frying pan

  • 1 whole chicken (3 1/2–4 pounds), skin on and cut into small chunks
  • 1 tablespoon (18 g) salt
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons (10–11 g) pepper
  • 1/2 cup (118 ml) oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups (90 g) fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups (180 g) celery, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 bulb garlic, peeled
  • 2 tablespoons (20 g) Maggi or chicken bouillon
  • 1/2 cup (118 ml) water if needed
  • 1 pound (450 g) carrots, cut in 1/2-inch slices
  • 1 pound (450 g) green beans, cut
  • 2–3 green bell peppers
  • 5–6 medium ripe plantains
  • Oil for deep frying the plantains

Method

  1. Season the chicken with the salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the Dutch oven on medium-high, add 1/2 cup oil and brown the chicken, turning once, about 10 minutes total until golden on both sides. It should reach 165°F (73°C).
  3. Blend the onion, parsley, celery, tomato and garlic to a fine paste. Pour it in, scraping up the browned bits.
  4. Add the bouillon and stir occasionally 8–10 minutes, adding up to about 1/2 cup water if needed.
  5. Add the carrots and green beans and simmer 2–3 minutes. Add the green peppers, stir, and turn off the stove.
  6. Cut the plantains into rounds about 1 inch thick and fry in oil 1/2 inch deep on medium-high, hot but not smoking, until golden brown.
  7. Toss the chicken and plantains with the vegetables and serve at once.

Chef Tips

  • Printed cues: chicken golden on both sides and 165°F (73°C) inside; plantains golden brown.
  • Fry the plantains separately and toss them in at serving so they do not turn soggy.
  • The page describes the dish as a cross between stir-fried chicken and fricassée.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the plantains early so they go soft.
  • Skipping the scrape of the browned bits.
  • Crowding the plantains in the frying pan.

Cultural Note

Four printings of poulet DG appear here (Poulet DG (Immaculate Bites)–034). The plantain timing, the chicken pre-cooking and the seasoning all differ and are not reconciled. A reader comment says it was hard to find outside West Cameroon; the author gives no community.

Storage & Serving

Storage: No storage time is printed.

Reheating: Best served fresh; the page advises holding the plantains until serving.

Serve with: Rice (optional), Pepper sauce
