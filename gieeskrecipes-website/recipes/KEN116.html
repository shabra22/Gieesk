🥔 Kenyan Viazi Karai

Viazi Karai

Viazi Karai — Mombasa's most beloved street food. Boiled potato halves coated in spiced gram flour batter and deep-fried until golden and crispy, served with tamarind coconut chutney. See KEN017 for the full recipe with complete heritage.

Viazi Karai is the coastal version of bhajia — where bhajia uses thin potato slices, viazi karai uses boiled potato halves, creating a more substantial snack with a soft, yielding interior beneath the crispy batter. The tamarind coconut chutney served alongside is as important as the potato itself.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Mixing bowl, Deep frying pan, Blender (for chutney)

  • 6 medium potatoes — peeled and halved
  • 2 cups gram flour (besan)
  • ¼ cup rice flour
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1½ cups water
  • Oil for deep frying
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • ½ cup fresh grated coconut
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • salt to taste

Method

  1. Boil potato halves in salted water for 12—15 minutes until just tender. Drain and cool completely.
  2. Make batter: combine gram flour, rice flour, spices, and salt. Whisk in water to a thick, coating batter.
  3. Make chutney: blend tamarind, coconut, sugar, garlic, coriander, and water until smooth.
  4. Heat oil to 175°C.
  5. Dip each potato half in batter. Fry for 3—4 minutes until deep golden.
  6. Drain and serve immediately with chutney.

Chef Tips

  • Cool potatoes completely before battering — warm potatoes cause batter to slide off.
  • The batter must be thick enough to coat and cling.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Warm potatoes — batter slides off.
  • Thin batter — does not create a proper crust.

Storage & Serving

Reheating: Oven at 180°C for 8 minutes.
