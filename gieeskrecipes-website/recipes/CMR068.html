🍋 Bouillie simple au citron (Sweet Lemon Porridge)

Bouillie au citron

Peanut paste and flour mixed with water, sieved and poured into boiling water until homogeneous and slightly viscous, then sugar and strained lemon juice added and cooked 10 minutes; served hot with beignets, cakes or bread.

p. 33 of the October 2023 recipe book: 500 g maize or sorghum flour, 100 g roasted peanut paste, 250 g sugar and 5 large lemons. The paste and flour are mixed with water, sieved and poured into boiling water, stirring to a homogeneous, slightly viscous product. Sugar and filtered lemon juice are added and it cooks 10 minutes; serve hot. A variant on p. 34 adds half a glass of rice cooked first until fully cooked; that variant is recorded here only as a note.

Ingredients

Equipment: Pot, Sieve

  • 500 g maize or sorghum flour
  • 100 g roasted peanut paste
  • 250 g sugar
  • 5 large lemons
  • Water — quantities not printed

Method

  1. Mix the peanut paste and flour with water and sieve.
  2. Pour into boiling water, mixing until homogeneous and slightly viscous.
  3. Add the sugar and the filtered lemon juice.
  4. Cook 10 minutes. Serve hot.

Chef Tips

  • The printed cue is ‘homogeneous and slightly viscous’.
  • Serve with beignets, cakes or bread, as the page says.
  • No water quantities are printed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding the lemon juice before the porridge thickens.
  • Not sieving.
  • Over-sweetening.

Cultural Note

The book attributes its recipes to the northern part of Cameroon and names no community, lamidat or town for any recipe. The p. 34 variant (with rice) calls the juice ‘tamisé’ where p. 33 says ‘filtré’.
