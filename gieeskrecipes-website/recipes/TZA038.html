🍗 Tanzanian Grilled Chicken

Kuku wa Kuchoma

Kuku wa Kuchoma — Tanzanian grilled chicken. Chicken pieces marinated in garlic, ginger, lemon, and Tanzanian spices, grilled over charcoal until deeply golden, slightly charred, and irresistibly fragrant. Tanzania's favourite weekend and celebration chicken.

Grilled chicken is Tanzania's most beloved affordable protein over charcoal — less expensive than goat but just as satisfying when properly marinated and grilled. At every weekend nyama choma spot across Tanzania, kuku wa kuchoma is the most ordered item.

Ingredients

Equipment: Charcoal grill, Tongs

  • 1 whole chicken — jointed into 8 pieces
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Score chicken pieces 3—4 times to the bone.
  2. Mix marinade. Rub into scores and all over. Marinate 2 hours.
  3. Grill over medium coals, skin-side down, 10 minutes.
  4. Turn. Grill 8 minutes.
  5. Continue turning every 5 minutes for 25—30 total minutes.
  6. Internal temperature: 74°C.

Chef Tips

  • Score deeply — the marinade needs to reach the bone.
  • Medium heat only — chicken burns easily.
  • Check doneness — no pink at the bone.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too high heat.
  • Not checking internal temperature.

The Science

Scoring breaks the skin barrier — allowing the oil-based marinade to penetrate the muscle fibres beneath. This produces a more evenly seasoned result than surface marination alone.

Cultural Note

Kuku wa Kuchoma is Tanzania's most democratic celebration food — affordable enough for everyday but impressive enough for guests. Every Tanzanian jiko cook has their own marinade that they guard fiercely.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten fresh.

Reheating: Grill briefly.

Serve with: Ugali (TZA001), Kachumbari, Pili Pili, Chips
