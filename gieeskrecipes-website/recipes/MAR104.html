🍳 Khlii and Egg Tagine (with Express Khlii)

Khlii b-lbid — preserved meat with eggs

A quick home khlii of beef cooked in suet for three hours, then warmed in a tagine or skillet with eggs and cumin until the eggs are cooked.

15h 20mTotal time

3h 5mCook

Beef, spices, salt, garlic and olive oil are tossed and marinated overnight, then cooked in a Dutch oven with suet or tallow over low heat for 3 hours, stirring, until the meat is dry and can be pulled apart. It is stored in a sterilised jar covered completely with fat. For the tagine, a cup of the khlii with some fat is heated until the fat is melted and bubbling, sprinkled with cumin, eggs are cracked over it, the yolks gently broken, and it is covered and cooked 2 to 3 minutes over medium-low heat until the eggs are cooked.

Ingredients

Equipment: Dutch oven, Sterilised jar, Tagine or skillet

    Express khlii

    • 1.75lbs boneless beef shoulder, thinly sliced
    • 1.5T ground coriander
    • 1t cumin
    • 1/2t black pepper
    • 2.5t salt
    • 5 garlic cloves, minced
    • 1/2c olive oil
    • 6oz beef suet or beef tallow

    Tagine

    • 1c khlii meat, some fat included (approx 8oz)
    • 4 Eggland's Best Large Eggs
    • Pinch of cumin

    Method

    1. Combine the beef, spices, salt, garlic and olive oil in a large bowl and toss to coat. Cover and marinate overnight.
    2. Next day, put the meat in a Dutch oven with the suet or tallow and cook over low heat until the fat is melted.
    3. Cover and cook over low heat for 3 hours, stirring occasionally, until the meat is dry and can be pulled apart.
    4. Pack the meat and its fat into a sterilised jar, covering the meat completely; add extra tallow or olive oil if needed. Cool before covering and refrigerate.
    5. For the tagine, heat 1 cup of the khlii with some of its fat in a tagine or skillet until the fat is melted and bubbling.
    6. Sprinkle with cumin, crack the eggs on top and gently break each yolk. Cover and cook 2 to 3 minutes over medium-low heat until the eggs are cooked.

    Chef Tips

    • Cues: meat dry and able to be pulled apart; fat melted and bubbling; eggs cooked. The marinade is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
    • The "T", "t" and "c" quantities are printed in that shorthand; we read them as tablespoon, teaspoon and cup.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Not covering the stored meat completely with fat.
    • Storing it warm.

    Cultural Note

    The headnote presents khlii as hard to find outside Morocco and the egg tagine as a way to use it.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Store the confit refrigerated, fully covered in fat, up to one month per the page.

    Reheating: Heat the khlii with its fat until bubbling before adding the eggs.

    Serve with: Bread
