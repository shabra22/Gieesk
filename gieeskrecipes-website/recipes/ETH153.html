🥛 Ethiopian Spiced Yogurt

ዮጉርት — Ye'tiff Yogurt

Ethiopian Spiced Yogurt — thick natural yogurt mixed with garlic, green jalapeño, fresh mint, and lemon. The cooling condiment served alongside spicy preparations — a modern Ethiopian restaurant addition that provides dairy-based cooling for the intensely spiced dishes.

Spiced yogurt is the modern Ethiopian restaurant's answer to the traditional Ayib — a quicker, more widely available dairy condiment that provides the same cooling function alongside hot dishes. Its green jalapeño and mint make it fresh and cooling simultaneously.

Ingredients

Equipment: Bowl

  • 400ml thick natural yogurt (Greek-style)
  • 1 green jalapeño — very finely minced
  • 1 garlic clove — minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh mint — chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Mix gently.
  3. Rest 10 minutes.
  4. Serve chilled.

Chef Tips

  • Thick yogurt only — thin yogurt becomes watery with the vegetable additions.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Thin yogurt.

The Science

Yogurt's lactic acid provides the same dairy cooling effect as ayib — the casein proteins in yogurt form a physical barrier in the mouth that temporarily reduces capsaicin's contact with taste receptors, providing relief from chilli heat more effectively than water.

Cultural Note

Spiced yogurt is the modern Ethiopian restaurant's gesture toward international dining culture — the familiar yogurt condiment that any global visitor recognises, given an Ethiopian character through jalapeño and mint.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve cold.

Serve with: Alongside all spicy preparations, With kitfo as an ayib alternative
