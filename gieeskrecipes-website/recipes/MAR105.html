🥗 Moroccan Beetroot Salad

Shlada dyal lbetrav — beet salad (سلطة الشمندر)

Beets boiled with half a lemon until a fork pierces them, peeled, diced and chilled with grated onion, parsley, olive oil, vinegar and cumin.

2h 45mTotal time
15mPrep
1hCook
4Servings
130Calories
EasyDifficulty

Three beets of about 2 pounds are boiled in water with a halved lemon for 45 to 60 minutes, depending on size, until the skin comes off easily and a fork pierces them. They are peeled, trimmed, diced and mixed with the onion, parsley, oil and vinegar, covered and chilled for 1 to 2 hours, and served cold.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Mixing bowl, Plastic wrap

  • 3 beets – approximately 2 pounds
  • 1 small onion grated
  • 1/3 cup of finely chopped parsley
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon of vinegar (or lemon juice)
  • Salt, pepper, cumin to taste
  • 1 lemon sliced in half (for boiling the beets)

Method

  1. Put the beets in a pot of water with the halved lemon (to enhance colour and flavour). Boil on medium-high for 45 to 60 minutes, depending on size.
  2. Test: the skin should come off easily and you should be able to pierce the beets with a fork.
  3. Peel the beets, remove the two ends, then slice and dice them.
  4. Mix the onion, parsley, oil, vinegar, salt, pepper and cumin, then combine with the diced beets.
  5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and chill for 1 to 2 hours. Serve cold, alone, with bread or tossed in a garden salad.

Chef Tips

  • Cue: skin comes off easily; a fork pierces the beet. The chill is NO CUE, TIME ONLY.
  • Wear gloves when peeling to avoid stained hands (our tip).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-boiling large beets.
  • Dressing the beets while hot, which dulls the colour (our caution).

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate covered up to 2 days (our estimate; the page gives none).

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Bread
