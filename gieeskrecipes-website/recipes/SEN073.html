🍖 Dibi, Grilled Lamb and Onions with Mustard

Dibi — dibiterie style

Cubed leg of lamb marinated six hours with mustard, garlic, ginger and lemongrass, wrapped in foil under onion rings and cooked over wood fire — or in the oven or on an electric grill.

Dibi is the grilled mutton of the dibiterie — the small restaurants dedicated to it, where the meat hangs on the bone to prove its freshness. This is the foil-and-onion version: 500 g of lamb leg cut into 3 cm cubes is marinated at least six hours with mustard, grated garlic, stock cube, salt, pepper, olive oil, ginger and lemongrass, laid on foil, covered with onion rings and cooked over a wood fire. The page says it can also be cooked in the oven or on an electric grill, in foil to avoid drying out and finished on the grill. Printed cook time: 40 minutes.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Aluminium foil, Wood fire, grill or oven

  • 500 g leg of lamb, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp mustard (the method uses 1 tbsp — see the note)
  • 3 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1 tbsp stock cube
  • Salt, pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp lemongrass

Method

  1. In a bowl, mix the meat, 1 tablespoon of mustard, the stock cube, grated garlic, salt, pepper, olive oil, ginger and lemongrass. Leave to marinate for at least 6 hours.
  2. Arrange the marinated meat on a sheet of aluminium foil, cover with onion rings and cook over a wood fire — about 40 minutes by the page’s time.
  3. In the same way, cooking can be done in the oven or on an electric grill. In the oven, cook the meat in foil to avoid drying out and finish it on the grill.

Chef Tips

  • The page gives no doneness cue and no temperature, only 40 minutes; the finished dish is described as ‘smoky, tender and spicy’.
  • Foil keeps the meat from drying; finish under the grill for colour.
  • Serve with the onions and the mustard sauce, bread and a spicy pepper.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cooking straight from the marinade for too short a time.
  • Not covering the meat with onions, which protect it.
  • Using both tablespoons of mustard without noticing the method only uses one.

Health Benefits

  • Lamb is rich in iron and zinc.
  • Ginger and garlic are traditionally valued for digestion.

Cultural Note

From the page: ‘There are small restaurants entirely dedicated to it, where the meat is displayed on the bone to prove its freshness’, and in a country where meat isn’t eaten every day and fish is more common, the dibiteurs are a huge hit. Mbaye’s site notes that the word dibi comes from Bambara and means grill.

Storage & Serving

Storage: 2 days refrigerated.

Reheating: Reheat in foil in a hot oven.

Serve with: Baguette, Mustard-onion sauce, Tamarind kani mustard sauce
