🥟 Ethiopian Shiro Sambusa

ሽሮ ሳምቡሳ — Shiro Sambusa

Ethiopian Shiro Sambusa — the crispy sambusa pastry filled with thick shiro paste, onion, garlic, and coriander. The fasting-period sambusa — when lentils run out, shiro provides the filling. Silkier and richer than lentil sambusa.

Shiro Sambusa uses the chickpea flour stew (shiro) as its filling — the thick shiro sets firmly when cooled, making it an ideal sambusa filling. The resulting sambusa is silkier than lentil-filled versions.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan for shiro, Rolling pin, Deep pan

    Sambusa Pastry (same as ETH161)

      Shiro Filling

      • ¾ cup shiro powder
      • 1 cup water
      • 1 onion — finely diced and fried
      • 2 garlic cloves
      • ¼ cup coriander
      • 1 tsp salt
      • Oil for frying

      Method

      1. Make thick shiro: cook onion, add shiro powder mixed with water. Cook until very thick. Cool completely.
      2. Make pastry as per ETH161.
      3. Shape, fill with thick cool shiro, seal, and fry as per ETH161.

      Chef Tips

      • Shiro filling must be very thick — it sets as it cools.
      • Cool completely before filling.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Thin shiro — runs out of the pastry.

      Cultural Note

      Shiro Sambusa is the fasting chef's ingenuity — taking the pantry staple (shiro powder) and using it as a sambusa filling, demonstrating that the Ethiopian fasting tradition produces creative solutions rather than limitations.

      Storage & Serving

      Storage: Best eaten immediately. Freeze uncooked.

      Reheating: Oven at 190°C.

      Serve with: With Silsi (ETH146), During fasting periods
